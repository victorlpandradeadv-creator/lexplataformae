The jurist's path

The protected goods

The protected goods are what the injury reaches when the platform downgrades, sanctions or erases the position of enjoyment of an IOC, even when formal access remains open, and they are called personal integrity, symbolic identity and biographical continuity. They are defined by public criteria and are tied to civil personality, which is why the loss of a history or a title can go beyond the loss of an item.

What is lost when access remains open?

In platform-governed ecosystems, what the consumer keeps over time is more than access to a digital performance. It is a position of enjoyment that incorporates history, memory, recognition, identity projection and continuity of presence in the environment. That is why certain losses, erasures, sanctions and unilateral reconfigurations reach beyond the economic value of the purchase, extending to dimensions of personal integrity and symbolic identity. The problem is to define these losses so that they are neither downgraded to mere displeasure nor treated as a side effect of the service.

In digital ecosystems, the value offered and enjoyed is not exhausted by instrumental utilities, because it depends on regimes of visibility, recognition, belonging and reputation that are part of the very experience of enjoyment and condition the subject's practical position in the environment.p. 456-457

PTEm ecossistemas digitais, o valor ofertado e fruído não se exaure em utilidades instrumentais, porque depende de regimes de visibilidade, reconhecimento, pertencimento e reputação que integram a própria experiência de fruição e condicionam a posição prática do sujeito no ambiente.

Where it comes from

In platform-governed ecosystems, what the consumer keeps over time is not only functional access to a digital performance, but a position of enjoyment that incorporates history, memory, recognition, identity projection and practical continuity of presence in the environment.p. 534

PTEm ecossistemas governados por plataforma, o que o consumidor mantém ao longo do tempo não é apenas acesso funcional a uma prestação digital, mas uma posição de fruição que incorpora histórico, memória, reconhecimento, projeção identitária e continuidade prática de presença no ambiente.

The corresponding legal protection, therefore, cannot be blind to the circumstance that certain losses, erasures, sanctions and unilateral reconfigurations reach not only the economic value of the acquisition, but also dimensions of personal integrity and symbolic identity that become objectified in the very course of the digital experience.p. 534

PTA tutela jurídica correspondente, por isso, não pode ser cega à circunstância de que certas perdas, apagamentos, sanções e reconfigurações unilaterais atingem não apenas o valor econômico da aquisição, mas também dimensões de integridade pessoal e de identidade simbólica que se objetivam no próprio curso da experiência digital.

The point that must now be addressed is how to define relevant losses that cannot be downgraded to “displeasure” or treated as a side effect.p. 456

PTO ponto que se impõe, agora, é definir perdas relevantes que não podem ser rebaixadas a “desagrado” nem tratadas como efeito colateral.

The difficulty lies in the shape of the harm, which does not always appear as a total interruption. Access may remain nominal while the experience is degraded by persistent changes in the conditions of participation, predictability and governability of the environment, and it is precisely these losses, which reach the enjoyment bundle without interrupting the service, that protection needs to cover. Protection thus no longer depends on the crude figure of total interruption.

Where it comes from

Protection ceases to depend on the crude figure of total interruption and extends to situations in which access remains nominal, but the experience of use is lowered by persistent changes in the conditions of participation, predictability and governability of the environment.p. 458

PTA tutela deixa de depender da figura grosseira da interrupção total e passa a alcançar situações em que o acesso permanece nominal, mas a experiência de uso é rebaixada por alterações persistentes nas condições de participação, de previsibilidade e de governabilidade do ambiente.

The problem is to make them legally treatable through objective delimitations and criteria of controllability, including so as to capture losses that reach the enjoyment bundle even when there is no total interruption, but rather a persistent lowering of the conditions of use and recognition.p. 457

PTO problema é torná-las juridicamente tratáveis com recortes objetivos e critérios de controlabilidade, inclusive para capturar perdas que atingem o feixe de fruição mesmo quando não há interrupção total, mas rebaixamento persistente das condições de uso e de reconhecimento.

Why does neither price nor feeling measure this loss?

The common reading goes wrong in two directions. On one side is the patrimonialist view, which confines the problem to the value of the good affected and turns every conflict into price and residual compensation, a view that does not hold up against Anderson Schreiber, for whom rereading private law in light of human dignity requires attention to the existential projections of the person. On the other side is the reading that treats symbolic value as a private emotional projection and therefore as legally irrelevant, and it fails too, because part of the value enjoyed in these environments is symbolic and socially structured.

Where it comes from

The rereading of private law in light of human dignity broke long ago with the narrow view that confined the legal problem to the patrimonial value of the good affected, requiring attention to the existential projections of the person in the social world Schreiber(Schreiber, 2014, p. 10; p. 17).p. 534

PTA releitura do direito privado à luz da dignidade humana já rompeu, há muito, com a visão estreita que confinava o problema jurídico ao valor patrimonial do bem atingido, exigindo atenção às projeções existenciais da pessoa no mundo social (Schreiber, 2014, p. 10; p. 17) .

Protection ceases to operate under an institutional blindness that converts every conflict into price and residual compensation and comes to admit, on the basis of the system itself, remedies of functional restoration, preservation of trails, access to records, procedural correction and restoration of the position of enjoyment whenever this is possible and legally due.p. 543

PTA tutela deixa de operar sob cegueira institucional que converte todo conflito em preço e indenização residual e passa a admitir, com base no próprio sistema, remédios de recomposição funcional, preservação de trilhas, acesso a registros, correção procedimental e restauração da posição de fruição sempre que isso for possível e juridicamente devido.

The progression of the argument then requires making explicit that part of the value enjoyed in digital ecosystems is not only instrumental, but symbolic and socially structured. To that end, it is necessary to set aside the reading that symbolic value is a mere private emotional projection, immune to control and, for that reason, legally irrelevant.p. 459

PTA progressão do argumento exige, então, explicitar que parte do valor fruído em ecossistemas digitais não é apenas instrumental, mas simbólico e socialmente estruturado. Para tanto, é necessário afastar a leitura de que o valor simbólico seja mera projeção emocional privada, imune a controle e, por isso, juridicamente irrelevante.

The way out is not to swap price for feeling. The point is not to dissolve the analysis into sentimentalism. The question is more sober, because in platform environments the consumer's personality is broken down into data, profiles, histories, metrics, reputations and participation trails. Following Schreiber, the harm is understood through the attribute of personality objectively affected, and not through the suffering subjectively experienced, which keeps protection from depending on sentimental proof or on a dramatization of the harm.

In platform environments, the consumer's personality is cut into data, profiles, histories, metrics, reputations and participation trails that condition concrete possibilities of permanence, recognition and self-determination.p. 535

PTEm ambientes de plataforma, a personalidade do consumidor é recortada em dados, perfis, históricos, métricas, reputações e trilhas de participação que condicionam possibilidades concretas de permanência, reconhecimento e autodeterminação.

Where it comes from

This does not mean dissolving the analysis into academic sentimentalism, an elegant shortcut for thinking ill of the one whose conduct is now being questioned. The question at hand is more sober.p. 535

PTIsso não significa dissolver a análise em sentimentalismo acadêmico, um atalho elegante para pensar mal daquele cuja conduta ora se questiona. A questão em pauta é mais sóbria.

When records, signs, usage trajectories, digital historicity and symbolic belongings come to structure objectifiable forms of personal continuity, the corresponding impact no longer fits comfortably in the narrow grammar of the lost price. This key becomes even clearer when the harm is understood not from the suffering subjectively experienced, but from the attribute of personality objectively affected. The advantage of this formulation lies precisely in preventing the protection of the person from depending on sentimental proof or on dramatization of the harm, concentrating instead on the legally protected projection that was violated Schreiber(Schreiber, 2014, p. 17).p. 534-535

PTQuando registros, signos, trajetórias de uso, historicidade digital e pertencimentos simbólicos passam a estruturar formas objetiváveis de continuidade pessoal, a afetação correspondente já não cabe confortavelmente na gramática estreita do preço perdido. Essa chave se torna ainda mais nítida quando a lesão é compreendida não a partir do sofrimento subjetivamente experimentado, mas do atributo da personalidade objetivamente atingido. A vantagem dessa formulação está justamente em evitar que a tutela da pessoa dependa de prova sentimental ou de dramatização do dano, concentrando-se antes na projeção juridicamente protegida que foi violada (Schreiber, 2014, p. 17) .

The methodological basis comes from Miguel Reale and Tércio Sampaio Ferraz Jr. Reale describes the norm as an integration of facts and values, and it is this integration that allows governed enjoyment to be treated as a legally modelable experience. Reale also observes that an unforeseen technical circumstance may alter the meaning and content of a legal text. Ferraz Jr. supplies the safeguard, because dogmatic delimitation works by cutting into reality to isolate the problems relevant to the decision, and dogmatics can deal with this kind of loss only if it is able to make it decidable without falling into descriptive paralysis.

Why do the protected goods appear under different names?

The protected goods do not go by a single name. At the opening of the part devoted to the regime of the IOC, two complementary dimensions appear, functional integrity, linked to performance and to the utility delivered, and symbolic integrity, linked to belonging, reputation, distinction and promises of identity. Shortly afterward, the two are gathered under the name of personal integrity, a category with two articulated faces. In its own section, personal integrity becomes an umbrella concept and symbolic identity appears as a distinct category. In the typology of digitally based business models, the pair returns as functional integrity and symbolic identity, and in the part on the constitutional order biographical continuity joins them.

The identity and symbolic face protects belonging, reputation, distinction and the promise of recognition mediated by signs of the environment.p. 395

PTA face identitária e simbólica protege pertencimento, reputação, distinção e a promessa de reconhecimento mediada por signos do ambiente.

Where it comes from

The decisive point is that, in platform ecosystems, the object does not present itself as a stabilized thing, but as a bundle of functional and symbolic enjoyment that depends on regimes of access, visibility, updating and governance. For this reason, adequate protection requires recognizing two complementary dimensions. Functional integrity, linked to performance, to the conditions of use and to the utility actually delivered. And symbolic integrity, linked to belonging, reputation, distinction and promises of identity mediated by signs and internal equivalences of the environment, when these elements are part of the performance as offered and perceived.p. 391

PTO ponto decisivo é que, em ecossistemas de plataforma, o objeto não se apresenta como coisa estabilizada, mas como feixe de fruição funcional e simbólica que depende de regimes de acesso, visibilidade, atualização e governança. Por isso, a tutela adequada exige reconhecer duas dimensões complementares. A integridade funcional, ligada ao desempenho, às condições de uso e à utilidade efetivamente entregue. E a integridade simbólica, ligada a pertencimento, reputação, distinção e promessas de identidade mediadas por signos e equivalências internas do ambiente, quando esses elementos integram a prestação tal como oferecida e percebida.

Next, personal integrity is introduced to name the legal goods whose erosion constitutes the relevant harm. It is a category with two faces that operate in articulation, and not as separate categories. The functional face protects performance, use and the utility actually delivered.p. 395

PTEm seguida, a integridade pessoal entra para nomear os bens jurídicos cuja erosão constitui a lesão relevante. Trata-se de uma categoria com duas faces que operam articuladas, e não como categorias separadas. A face funcional protege o desempenho, o uso e a utilidade efetivamente entregue.

Functional integrity and symbolic identity delimit the field of relevant losses beyond binary interruption, reaching degradations of enjoyment and disorganizations of the sign-value associated with belonging, distinction and recognition.p. 483

PTIntegridade funcional e identidade simbólica delimitam o campo das perdas relevantes para além da interrupção binária, alcançando degradações de fruição e desorganizações do valor-signo associado a pertencimento, distinção e reconhecimento.

The protected core of enjoyment comes to include personal integrity and symbolic identity, objectified in history, reputation, memory, belonging and continuity of trajectory.p. 543

PTO núcleo protegido da fruição passa a incluir integridade pessoal e identidade simbólica, objetivadas em histórico, reputação, memória, pertencimento e continuidade de trajetória.

The shifting names do not undo the content. Protection covers the functional integrity of the acquired object and the personal and symbolic integrity of the consumer. Measured against the doctrine of digital goods, the regime seeks protection of using and, in certain cases, protection of being, whenever enjoyment incorporates symbolic identity and biographical continuity. Both formulations describe a constant pair, what the object does for the consumer and who the consumer is within the environment. The second pole changes name with the angle of view and becomes biographical continuity when the focus falls on time. In the two-faces formulation, personal integrity covers both poles. In the umbrella formulation, it adds to the classic integrities a facet of agency that serves both to exercise use and to exist in the system, and symbolic identity stands beside it, linked by the two captivities.

Where it comes from

To make this phenomenon legally visible, the category of Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) is proposed as an infrastructure-concept capable of giving concrete form to the notion of immaterial product provided for in art. 3, § 1, of the Consumer Protection Code1 as a governed enjoyment bundle. Under this formulation, legal protection no longer focuses only on the formal regularity of the performance and extends to the functional integrity of the acquired object and the personal and symbolic integrity of the consumer.p. 11

PTPara tornar esse fenômeno juridicamente visível, propõe-se a categoria de Objeto Imaterial de Consumo (OIC) como conceito-infraestrutura apto a concretizar a noção de produto imaterial prevista no art. 3º, § 1º, do Código de Defesa do Consumidor enquanto feixe de fruição governada. A partir dessa formulação, a tutela jurídica deixa de se concentrar apenas na regularidade formal da prestação e passa a abranger a integridade funcional do objeto adquirido e a integridade pessoal e simbólica do consumidor.

The regime of the IOC takes on the civil-law gain, rejects the strawman of the legal nonexistence of the digital and repositions the debate on the ground that matters. It seeks the protection of using, the control of functional integrity and, in certain cases, the protection of being, whenever enjoyment incorporates the symbolic identity and biographical continuity of the consumer user.p. 573-574

PTO regime do OIC assume o ganho civilista, recusa o espantalho da inexistência jurídica do digital e reposiciona o debate no terreno que importa. Ele busca a tutela do usar, o controle da integridade funcional e, em certas hipóteses, a tutela do ser, sempre que a fruição incorporar identidade simbólica e continuidade biográfica do usuário consumidor.

The two faces need to be thought of together because the enjoyment of the IOC is not realized by separating one from the other, and because the relevant degradation of continuous consumption usually falls simultaneously on both, as happens when a unilateral reconfiguration preserves technical access but lowers the reputational or symbolic position of the consumer in the ecosystem.p. 395

PTAs duas faces precisam ser pensadas em conjunto porque a fruição do OIC não se realiza separando uma da outra, e porque a degradação relevante do consumo contínuo costuma incidir simultaneamente sobre as duas, como acontece quando uma reconfiguração unilateral preserva o acesso técnico mas rebaixa a posição reputacional ou simbólica do consumidor no ecossistema.

For anyone building a claim, the practical consequence is that no new legal good is created. Using personal integrity as an umbrella is an operation that engages with the settled dogmatics of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC), and identity captivity does not create a new good either, because its relevance lies in making a specific form of loss in the fact of consumption controllable. In either formulation, what decides is the description of the loss by verifiable criteria, not the label chosen.

Where it comes from

The use of “personal integrity” as an umbrella concept to encompass the pairing “health and safety” native to consumer protection does not propose the creation of a new legal good, but an operation that engages directly with the dogmatics already settled in the CDC.p. 465

PTA uso de “integridade pessoal” como conceito guarda-chuva para englobar o binômio “saúde e segurança” nativos da tutela consumerista não propõe a criação de um bem jurídico novo, mas uma operação que converse diretamente com a dogmática já estabilizada no CDC.

In consumer-law terms, the relevance of the concept is not to create a new good, but to make controllable a specific form of loss in the fact of consumption, in which the integrity of agency and symbolic identity are corroded by instability, opacity and manufactured risk, affecting the adequacy of the regime of enjoyment and the legitimate trust that sustains continuous platform-governed relationships.p. 225

PTEm termos jurídico-consumeristas, a relevância do conceito não é criar um bem novo, mas tornar controlável uma forma específica de perda no fato do consumo, na qual a integridade de agência e a identidade simbólica são corroídas por instabilidade, opacidade e risco fabricado, afetando a adequação do regime de fruição e a confiança legítima que sustenta vínculos contínuos governados por plataforma.

What does personal integrity protect?

Personal integrity is defined negatively and positively. It does not designate an emotional state or discomfort in the face of the ordinary frustrations of the market, but the preservation of minimum conditions of agency, continuity and practical orientation of the consumer in an environment that permanently administers the possibilities of enjoyment. In this environment, the consumer participates in an ecosystem that organizes access, priorities, visibilities and sanctions. Integrity is affected when these conditions are lowered persistently or structurally, to the point of turning use into chronic instability, functional disorientation or loss of control over one's own way of existing in the environment, even if formal access remains available.

The legal relevance, therefore, lies not in measuring internal shocks, but in identifying when the continuous administration of the environment significantly alters the level of predictability, consistency and governability of enjoyment, corroding the core of utility stabilized in the relationship.p. 458

PTA relevância jurídica, portanto, não está em medir abalos internos, mas em identificar quando a administração contínua do ambiente altera, de modo significativo, o patamar de previsibilidade, consistência e governabilidade da fruição, corroendo o núcleo de utilidade estabilizado no vínculo.

Where it comes from

Personal integrity, in the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption, does not designate an emotional state or subjective discomfort arising from ordinary frustrations of the market. It designates the preservation of minimum conditions of agency, continuity and practical orientation of the consumer within a technical-organizational environment that permanently administers the possibilities of enjoyment. When the acquired utility is realized as a position of enjoyment sustained by a platform, the subject is not only the recipient of content, but a participant in an ecosystem that organizes access, priorities, visibilities and sanctions.p. 457

PTIntegridade pessoal, no regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo, não designa estado emocional nem desconforto subjetivo decorrente de frustrações ordinárias do mercado. Designa a preservação de condições mínimas de agência, continuidade e orientação prática do consumidor dentro de um ambiente técnico-organizacional que administra, de modo permanente, as possibilidades de fruição. Quando a utilidade adquirida se realiza como posição de fruição sustentada por plataforma, o sujeito não é apenas destinatário de um conteúdo, mas participante de um ecossistema que organiza acessos, prioridades, visibilidades e sanções.

Integrity is affected when these conditions are lowered persistently or structurally, to the point of turning the experience of use into chronic instability, functional disorientation or loss of control over one's own way of existing in the environment, even if formal access remains available.p. 458

PTA integridade é atingida quando essas condições são rebaixadas de forma persistente ou estrutural, a ponto de converter a experiência de uso em instabilidade crônica, desorientação funcional ou perda de controle sobre o próprio modo de existir no ambiente, ainda que o acesso formal permaneça disponível.

The umbrella has two layers of content. The first is that of the classic integrities, physical, psychic, patrimonial and even aesthetic. The second is a facet specific to continuous consumption, integrity of the capacity for agency and predictability, understood as the minimum set of conditions that allows consumers to orient their own action, understand practical consequences, plan their continued presence and exercise use according to stabilizable expectations. Nor does this facet correspond to an emotional state or to a psychological standard of protection, because it designates a functional condition of existence in the system, observable in the governability of the environment, the stability of the rules and the legibility of consequences.

Where it comes from

For this impact to be legally assimilable, it is necessary to make explicit the content of what is understood here by personal integrity in the regime of the IOC. The term is used as an umbrella concept compatible with the theory of quality in the CDC, which already operates through categories broad enough to encompass harms arising from the fact of consumption without requiring the crude figure of total interruption or of immediate material injury.p. 465

PTPara que essa afetação seja juridicamente assimilável, é necessário explicitar o conteúdo do que aqui se compreende por integridade pessoal no regime do OIC. O termo é empregado como conceito guarda-chuva compatível com a teoria da qualidade no CDC, que já opera por categorias suficientemente amplas para abarcar danos decorrentes do fato do consumo sem exigir a figura grosseira da interrupção total ou da lesão material imediata.

Personal integrity includes, therefore, classic dimensions of physical, psychic, patrimonial and even aesthetic integrity, but now also comes to incorporate an operational facet proper to continuous consumption in an environment. It is integrity as to the capacity for agency and predictability, understood as a minimum set of conditions that allows the consumer to orient his own action, understand practical consequences, plan permanence and exercise use according to stabilizable normative expectations in an ecosystem governed by architecture and by private sanctions. This integrity of agency does not correspond to a private emotional state or to a psychological parameter of protection. It designates a functional condition of existence in the system, observable in patterns of governability of the environment, of stability of rules and of legibility of consequences.p. 465-466

PTIntegridade pessoal inclui, portanto, dimensões clássicas de integridade física, psíquica, patrimonial e até mesmo a estética, mas agora passa a incorporar também uma faceta operacional própria do consumo contínuo em ambiente. Trata-se da integridade quanto à capacidade de agência e de previsibilidade, entendida como conjunto mínimo de condições que permite ao consumidor orientar a própria ação, compreender consequências práticas, planejar permanência e exercer o uso segundo expectativas normativas estabilizáveis em um ecossistema governado por arquitetura e por sanções privadas. Essa integridade de agência não corresponde a estado emocional privado nem a parâmetro psicológico de tutela. Ela designa condição funcional de existência no sistema, observável em padrões de governabilidade do ambiente, de estabilidade de regras e de legibilidade das consequências.

The grounding in Consumer Law comes from Claudia Lima Marques. In her reconstruction, consumer protection is organized at least around the protection of physical-psychic integrity and economic integrity, and the CDC's theory of quality operates through the legal duty of quality-adequacy and quality-safety. The agency facet may thus be presented as an extension of the adequacy of the supply regime itself, and not as a normative innovation. Julie E. Cohen explains why this facet belongs to the supply, arguing that networked information technologies do not merely empower the connected subject but configure it, so that agency comes to be treated as a governable variable of the service.

Where it comes from

In Claudia Lima Marques's reconstruction, consumer protection in the market is organized, at least, by the protection of the consumer's physical-psychic integrity and economic integrity, in addition to the concern with the moment at which this protection applies in the contractual dynamics Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 13). This framework engages directly with the theory of quality in the CDC, understood as a legal duty of quality-adequacy and quality-safety, by which defects of inadequacy and of insecurity are qualified according to reasonable expectations of supply, functionally replacing the classic categories of the latent defect in the previous regime Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 1349). Within this framing, the inclusion of an operational facet linked to agency and predictability in environments of platform-governed enjoyment may be presented as an extension of the adequacy of the supply regime itself, and not as a normative innovation.p. 465

PTNa reconstrução de Claudia Lima Marques, a tutela do consumidor no mercado se organiza, ao menos, pela proteção de sua integridade físico-psíquica e de sua integridade econômica, além da preocupação com o momento em que essa tutela incide na dinâmica contratual (Marques, 2016, p. 13) . Essa moldura conversa diretamente com a teoria da qualidade no CDC, compreendida como dever legal de qualidade-adequação e qualidade-segurança, pela qual se qualificam vícios por inadequação e por insegurança segundo expectativas razoáveis de fornecimento, em substituição funcional das categorias clássicas do vício redibitório no regime anterior (Marques, 2016, p. 1349) . Nesse enquadramento, a inclusão de uma faceta operacional ligada à agência e à previsibilidade em ambientes de fruição governada por plataforma pode ser apresentada como desdobramento de adequação do próprio regime de fornecimento, e não como inovação normativa.

Personal integrity and symbolic identity become fully intelligible as dimensions of the IOC when it is recognized that, in platform-governed environments, agency and identity are not located outside the supply as mere psychological phenomena, but may be configured by architectural design and by continuous modulation. Cohen synthesizes this point by stating that technologies and artifacts mediate the embodied perception of reality and that, over time, they come to constitute and define the lived world, so that networked information technologies do not merely empower the networked self, but configure it Cohen(Cohen, 2012, [p. 36]).p. 463

PTIntegridade pessoal e identidade simbólica se tornam plenamente inteligíveis como dimensões do OIC quando se reconhece que, em ambientes governados por plataforma, agência e identidade não se situam fora do fornecimento como meros fenômenos psicológicos, mas podem ser configuradas por desenho arquitetural e por modulação contínua. Cohen sintetiza esse ponto ao afirmar que tecnologias e artefatos mediam a percepção encarnada de realidade e que, ao longo do tempo, passam a constituir e definir o mundo vivido, de modo que tecnologias informacionais em rede não apenas empoderam o self em rede, mas o configuram (Cohen, 2012, [p. 36]) .

The legal consequence is that agency comes to be treated as a governable variable of the supply and that motivational patterns may be reinforced in pre-deliberative layers by techniques of choice architecture and algorithmic anticipation Cohen(Cohen, 2019, p. 77).p. 463

PTA consequência jurídica é que a agência passa a ser tratada como variável governável do fornecimento e que padrões motivacionais podem ser reforçados em camadas pré-deliberativas por técnicas de arquitetura de escolha e de antecipação algorítmica (Cohen, 2019, p. 77) .

How is personal integrity linked to civil personality?

In the part on the constitutional order, personal integrity gains an informational and biographical dimension, because people also come to project themselves socially through their digital records, their accumulated trajectory and their recognizable position in the ecosystem. Isn't protecting a trajectory just protecting hurt feelings?

The first objection is sentimentalism, the claim that protecting trajectory and reputation would mean protecting hurt feelings. But the injury is measured by the attribute objectively affected, and symbolic identity is a public, collectively verifiable position. The second is that history and records would be a data matter, foreign to consumption, but the administration of these elements is part of the very regime of enjoyment and interferes with the consumer's informational and biographical integrity. The third is the lack of full ownership. Article 24, item VII, of the Copyright Law answers it, because it shows that the legal order already accepts minimum duties of access to preserve the memory of an immaterial object under a third party's control, without making the consumer an author.

Where it comes from

The digital experience can reach legally relevant dimensions of personal integrity precisely because data protection, informational self-determination and the free development of personality make up the existential core of the person in an informational environment Laprano(Canavez; Andrade; Laprano, 2021, p. 139; p. 148; p. 155-156). That is why the administration of these elements cannot be treated as a simple “data topic” set apart from the consumer relationship. It is part of the very regime of enjoyment and directly interferes with the consumer's informational and biographical integrity.p. 535

PTA experiência digital pode atingir dimensões de integridade pessoal juridicamente relevantes justamente porque proteção de dados, autodeterminação informacional e livre desenvolvimento da personalidade integram o núcleo existencial da pessoa em ambiente informacional (Canavez; Andrade; Laprano, 2021, p. 139; p. 148; p. 155-156) . Daí por que a administração desses elementos não pode ser tratada como simples “tema de dados” apartado da relação de consumo. Ela integra o próprio regime de fruição e interfere diretamente na integridade informativa e biográfica do consumidor.

The Copyright Law recognizes, in Article 24, item VII, the author's moral right to access a single and rare copy of the work that is legitimately in the possession of another in order to preserve its memory. The point is not to turn the consumer into an author, nor to transplant the regime of authors' moral rights into the consumer field. The point is narrower and more elegant: the Brazilian legal order already accepts, in another domain, that preserving memory linked to an immaterial object under a third party's control may justify minimum positive duties to provide access and enable recording. Does any discomfort become an injury?

Delimiting the categories keeps personal integrity, symbolic identity, symbolic captivity, identity captivity and digital citizenship from becoming expansive labels that absorb any discomfort from the ordinary frictions of the market. The aim is not to raise the consumer's experience to a psychological yardstick, nor to turn trivial frustrations, aesthetic preferences or normal service fluctuations into matters of reinforced protection. Nor is it to attribute unlawfulness to technology as such or to replace dogmatic criteria with moral disapproval of the business model. The matrix of losses, moreover, is not meant to exhaust the phenomenon but to make it decidable by criteria.

Where it comes from

Thus the protection of integrity and identity, when they operate as functional dimensions of enjoyment in private environments of public relevance, can be formulated as a protective core without abstract moralism, provided it remains tied to criteria of relevance and to standards of verifiability capable of converting this core into decidable losses in the IOC regime. This incorporation requires, however, explicit delimitation to keep personal integrity, symbolic identity, symbolic captivity, identity captivity and digital citizenship from becoming expansive labels capable of absorbing any discomfort arising from ordinary market frictions.p. 467

PTCom isso, a proteção da integridade e da identidade, quando operadas como dimensões funcionais de fruição em ambientes privados de relevância pública, pode ser formulada como núcleo protetivo sem moralismo abstrato, desde que permaneça vinculada a critérios de relevância e a padrões de verificabilidade capazes de converter esse núcleo em perdas decidíveis no regime do OIC. Essa incorporação exige, contudo, delimitação explícita para impedir que integridade pessoal, identidade simbólica, cativeiro simbólico, cativeiro identitário e cidadania digital se convertam em rótulos expansivos aptos a absorver qualquer desconforto decorrente de fricções ordinárias do mercado.

This is not about raising the consumer's experience to a psychological parameter nor about turning trivial frustrations, aesthetic preferences or normal service fluctuations into matters of reinforced protection. Nor is it about attributing unlawfulness to technology as such or replacing dogmatic criteria with moral disapproval of the business model.p. 467

PTNão se trata de elevar a experiência do consumidor a parâmetro psicológico nem de transformar frustrações triviais, preferências estéticas ou oscilações normais de serviço em matéria de tutela reforçada. Também não se trata de atribuir ilicitude à tecnologia como tal ou de substituir critérios dogmáticos por reprovação moral do modelo de negócio.

The matrix of losses relating to personal integrity and symbolic identity, as outlined here, is not meant to exhaust the phenomenon but to make it decidable by criteria, opening the way to standards of verifiability and restoration compatible with continued enjoyment.p. 468

PTA matriz de perdas relativa à integridade pessoal e à identidade simbólica, assim delineada, não pretende esgotar o fenômeno, mas torná-lo decidível por critérios, abrindo o caminho para a formulação de padrões de verificabilidade e de recomposição compatíveis com a fruição continuada.

On the side of personality, the limits are equally express. The consumer's integrity may comprise the preservation of minimum conditions of biographical continuity, built trajectory, symbolic insertion and stability of enjoyment, without dissolving the analysis into psychologism or displacing the CDC from the center of protection. The reinforcements from the LGPD do not make the consumer the absolute owner of everything the platform records, the copyright parallel does not turn the consumer into an author, restoration of the position of enjoyment applies where appropriate, and moral damages are reserved for qualified and objectifiable hypotheses, without reaching mere annoyance.

Where it comes from

The consumer's integrity, in continuous digital ecosystems, may comprise the preservation of minimum conditions of biographical continuity, built trajectory, symbolic insertion and stability of enjoyment, without this implying dissolving the analysis into psychologism or removing from the CDC its protective centrality.p. 536-537

PTA integridade do consumidor, em ecossistemas digitais contínuos, pode compreender a preservação de condições mínimas de continuidade biográfica, trajetória construída, inserção simbólica e estabilidade de fruição, sem que isso implique dissolver a análise em psicologismo nem retire do CDC sua centralidade protetiva.

These provisions do not turn the consumer into the absolute owner of everything the platform records, but they reinforce that the consumer's digital biography is not raw mass entirely unavailable to the data subject and that the administration of the memory of enjoyment needs to remain controllable, intelligible and minimally accessible.p. 542

PTEsses dispositivos não transformam o consumidor em proprietário absoluto de tudo o que a plataforma registra, mas reforçam que sua biografia digital não é massa bruta totalmente indisponível ao titular e que a administração da memória de fruição precisa permanecer controlável, inteligível e minimamente acessível.

The consequence is not to make the consumer an author, but to recognize that erasures, resets, inventory exclusions, suppressions of history and sanctions that eliminate the biography built in the environment require verifiable reasoning, minimum preservation of records and, where appropriate, restoration through reinstatement of the position of enjoyment.p. 543

PTA consequência não é autoralizar o consumidor, mas reconhecer que apagamentos, resets, exclusões de inventário, supressões de histórico e sanções que eliminem a biografia construída no ambiente exigem motivação verificável, preservação mínima de registros e, quando cabível, recomposição por restauração da posição de fruição.

Nor is the civil law taxonomy that divides digital goods into patrimonial, existential and mixed the point of entry. It remains useful for ownership, succession and circulation, but once the IOC is qualified, the pertinent question becomes the regime of enjoyment that the asset sustains. The protected goods are therefore not shown by classifying the skin, the account or the title as an existential good, but by the position of enjoyment that governance has downgraded.

Where it comes from

When this qualification asserts itself, the pertinent initial question ceases to be about the patrimonial or existential nature of the asset and becomes about the regime of enjoyment that this asset sustains within the environment that produces it.p. 571

PTQuando essa qualificação se afirma, a pergunta inicial pertinente deixa de ser sobre a natureza patrimonial ou existencial do ativo e passa a ser sobre o regime da fruição que esse ativo sustenta no interior do ambiente que o produz.

The patrimonial, existential and mixed taxonomy, considered in itself, is neither discarded nor demonized. It remains a useful instrument when the conflict genuinely turns on ownership, succession, post mortem destination or inter vivos disposition of the digital asset, cases in which the civil law floor offers a technically adequate answer.p. 571

PTA taxonomia patrimonial, existencial e mista, em si mesma considerada, não é descartada nem demonizada. Ela continua sendo instrumento útil quando o conflito se organiza efetivamente em torno de titularidade, transmissão sucessória, destinação post mortem ou disposição inter vivos do ativo digital, hipóteses em que o piso civilista oferece resposta tecnicamente adequada.
