Situation e) · 7.1.2 of the thesis

When the platform changed the rules and left no trace

e) structural opacity and open auditability

Which facts show an alteration without a trace?

A relevant change in access, performance, utility, operating rules or sanctions without preserving minimum trails for reconstruction. In practice, the answers are generic, such as claims that the information no longer exists, that it is internal-use data or that the procedure cannot be reconstructed.

Which records should be requested to reconstruct the alteration?

Essential logs, the history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements, enough to make the dispute knowable. Almost all of this is held by the supplier, so the usual path is to request disclosure. Elements attributable to the supplier and publicly retrievable are also useful, such as update records, stated reasons for changes, access policies and internal sanction criteria.

Where does the duty to leave a trace come from?

Open auditability is a parameter, and not an institution foreign to the system. It unfolds from the duty of information, objective good faith and the facilitation of defense in Article 6 of the CDC1. The regime for disclosure of documents or things in Articles 396 to 400 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC)2 is the most direct route to put it into operation. It is the minimum threshold of external verifiability that turns opaque modulations into matter for adversarial debate, proof and decision.

How do you avoid a procedural fishing expedition?

The request must be surgical, and not a procedural fishing expedition. The application identifies, as completely as possible, the document or record, its evidentiary purpose and the circumstances showing that it is in the party's possession, such as the version history of an item, the trail of purchase screens or the category and reason for the sanction. If the supplier denies having the document, the applicant may show by other means that the denial is untrue. If the refusal is unjustified, the judge may accept as true the facts the document would prove. The parameter does not reach every microscopic adjustment. Auditability neither requires full disclosure of the source code nor rules out a technically delimited examination under confidentiality protection.

How far did governance reach in the Vanguard case?

In the Vanguard case, protection against cheating came to depend on software loaded into the core of the operating system, started with the computer and kept as a condition of enjoyment. The platform's governance thus reaches the consumer's own equipment. The interest in preserving competitive integrity is legitimate, but the question is whether it is enough to authorize this expansion without reinforced control. Articles 8, 10 and 12 of the CDC3 reach the risk that the architecture transfers to the equipment, and Article 6 of Law 13,709/2018, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD)4, requires purpose, necessity and transparency in data processing. The point is not to assert the automatic unlawfulness of the software, but to refuse its legal immunization. Alleged security does not replace legally demonstrated security, and open auditability prevents the platform from hiding behind technical opacity and trade secrets.

When it applies
When relevant alterations of access, performance, utility, rule or sanction reach enjoyment without minimum trails for reconstruction.
Whenever relevant alterations of access, performance, utility, operating rule or sanction bear upon enjoyment without preservation of minimum trails for institutional reconstruction, there will be a violation of the parameter of open auditability.
Duty of supply
To maintain minimum verifiability over what changed, when, for what reason and with what functionally relevant effects.
The corresponding duty of supply is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was altered, when it was altered, for what reason it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.
Minimum evidence
Essential logs, history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements.
The minimum evidence required consists of essential logs, history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements sufficient to make the controversy knowable.
Possible legal qualification
Abusive practice through immunizing opacity, inadequacy of the regime of supply and reinforcement of the duty of evidentiary cooperation, without prejudice to other applications.
The possible legal qualification may take the form of an abusive practice through immunizing opacity, inadequacy of the regime of supply and reinforcement of the duty of evidentiary cooperation, without prejudice to other combined applications.
Institutional consequence
Orders of preservation and disclosure, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and control of the lawfulness of the alteration or the sanction.
The institutional consequence includes orders for the preservation and disclosure of records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and control of the lawfulness of the alteration or sanction on the basis of minimum institutional intelligibility.
