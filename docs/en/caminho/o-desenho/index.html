And if the loss is part of the plan?

A bad change may be just a mistake, and mistakes happen. But the picture changes when you look at everything together, instead of one thing at a time.

Staged rarity, manufactured haste, the promise of progress, the fear of losing what you have built up and the small downgrades that never stop seem trivial when seen one by one. Together, they squeeze the time you have to think, raise the cost of leaving, make it harder to go back and leave you more and more dependent on the very place that governs what you use.

That is why exploitation almost never appears in a scandalous clause or in an advertisement that lies on its own. It appears as an operating program, what the service does every day.

Where it comes from

What occurs, more often, is the combination of staged scarcity, manufactured urgency, promise of progression, diffuse threat of loss, gradual downgrades of utility and symbolic anchoring of permanencep. 670

PTO que ocorre, com mais frequência, é a combinação entre escassez encenada, urgência fabricada, promessa de progressão, ameaça difusa de perda, rebaixamentos graduais de utilidade e ancoragem simbólica da permanência

In IOC ecosystems, exploitation rarely presents itself in the clean form of a scandalous clause or of an advertisement that lies in isolation. It tends to emerge as an operating programp. 670

PTEm ecossistemas de OIC, a exploração raramente se apresenta sob a forma limpa de uma cláusula escandalosa ou de uma publicidade isoladamente mentirosa. Ela tende a emergir como programa de funcionamento

It tends to emerge as an operating program, that is, as an articulation of multiple mechanisms that compress reflection time, reorganize expectation, increase exit cost, hinder reversal and make enjoyment progressively dependent on the very environment that governs itp. 670

PTEla tende a emergir como programa de funcionamento, isto é, como articulação entre múltiplos mecanismos que comprimem tempo de reflexão, reorganizam expectativa, aumentam custo de saída, dificultam reversão e tornam a fruição progressivamente dependente do próprio ambiente que a governa

Abusive teleology

Teleology means purpose. It becomes abusive when the way the service is supplied comes to aim, in practice, at exploiting the fragility of the people who use it. Or at hiding what is lost, or at turning each change into a way of locking you in and charging you.

The most important point here is that nobody has to guess at intentions. The question is not what was going on in anyone's head at the company, and not every way of selling, persuading or running a game stands condemned. What gets examined is where the design points, judged by the pattern that repeats, the coherence among the pieces and the effect it produces again and again.

Where it comes from

Abusive teleology thus describes a situation in which the practical purpose of the supply arrangement departs from legally tolerable limits and comes to orient the environment toward structural exploitation of vulnerability, toward relevant obscuring of enjoyment or toward recurrent conversion of mutability into a technology of capture.p. 732

PTA teleologia abusiva descreve, assim, situação em que a finalidade prática do arranjo de fornecimento se afasta dos limites juridicamente toleráveis e passa a orientar o ambiente para exploração estrutural da vulnerabilidade, para obscurecimento relevante da fruição ou para conversão recorrente da mutabilidade em tecnologia de captura.

This is not about investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor about moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governance. The focus is on the functional purpose objectively inscribed in the arrangement, as revealed by its replicability, its internal coherence and its repeated impact on the consumer's experience.p. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada. O foco está na finalidade funcional objetivamente inscrita no arranjo, tal como se revela por sua replicabilidade, por sua coerência interna e por seu impacto reiterado sobre a experiência do consumidor.

The category does not depend on imputing subjective psychological intention or on locating a single act as a sufficient causep. 448

PTA categoria não depende de imputar intenção psicológica subjetiva nem de localizar um único ato como causa suficiente

Does the abusive purpose pass the five questions?

So that it does not become a catch-all label, the abusive purpose has to pass a five-question test, and every answer must be yes.

Is the purpose written into the design itself? It has to show up in screen choices, in the terms of use, in the access rules and in the way penalties are applied, and not be just a side effect that happened once.

Does it repeat as a pattern? An isolated episode, a mistake or an exception is not enough.

Does it take advantage of weaknesses that are built into the arrangement? For example, how hard it is to understand what is going on, the dependence of someone who has already invested a lot, the cost of switching games and the loss of the reputation, friends and history built there.

Does it come with a lack of clarity and little room to contest? It is not enough for the system to be complex. The way it works has to make it hard to understand and question the decisions that matter.

And does it seriously and lastingly affect what you bought, even without taking everything away at once? A mere fluctuation in the service does not count.

Where it comes from

For this qualification to remain controllable and not turn into an elastic label, it must take the form of a test composed of cumulative elementsp. 448

PTPara que essa qualificação permaneça controlável e não se converta em rótulo elástico, ela deve assumir a forma de um teste composto por elementos cumulativos

The first requirement is inscribed teleology. It must be possible to reconstruct that the exploitative purpose is not merely an occasional side effect, but appears embedded in design choices, terms of use, authorization mechanisms and enforcement strategies that structure the environment of enjoyment.p. 448

PTO primeiro requisito é a teleologia inscrita. Deve ser possível reconstruir que a finalidade exploratória não está apenas em um efeito colateral eventual, mas aparece incorporada em escolhas de design, termos de uso, mecanismos de autorização e estratégias de enforcement que estruturam o ambiente de fruição.

The second requirement is reproduction as a pattern. The deviation cannot be an isolated episode, a contingent error or an operational exception.p. 448

PTO segundo requisito é a reprodução como padrão. O desvio não pode ser episódio isolado, erro contingente ou exceção operacional.

The exploitative purpose must bear on asymmetries of intelligibility and agency that are part of the very arrangement of governed consumption, such as semiotic vulnerability, dependence through captivity, migration costs and loss of investments accumulated in reputation, networks or history.p. 449

PTA finalidade exploratória deve incidir sobre assimetrias de inteligibilidade e agência que integram o próprio arranjo do consumo governado, como vulnerabilidade semiótica, dependência por catividade, custos de migração e perda de investimentos acumulados em reputação, redes ou histórico.

The fourth requirement is opacity combined with low contestability. It is not enough for the system to be complex or to operate with metrics. The mode of operation must structurally hinder the understanding and challenging of the relevant decisionsp. 455

PTO quarto requisito é a opacidade combinada com baixa contestabilidade. Não basta que o sistema seja complexo ou opere com métricas. É necessário que o modo de operação dificulte, de forma estrutural, a compreensão e a impugnação das decisões relevantes

Abusive teleology must produce a substantial and persistent impact on the practical conditions of use and value of the acquired object, even if there is no total interruption.p. 455

PTA teleologia abusiva deve produzir impacto substancial e persistente nas condições práticas de uso e valor do objeto adquirido, ainda que não haja interrupção total.

The requirement prevents the category from being triggered by any fluctuation in the servicep. 455

PTA exigência impede que a categoria seja acionada por qualquer oscilação do serviço

Is an aggressive business already pursuing an abusive purpose?

This is not a catch-all label. Charging, persuading and governing a game are not abuses in themselves. Nor is it enough for a business to be merely aggressive, for a design to be debatable or for an arrangement to be simply efficient for the company.

And there are legitimate purposes that the platform can and should pursue, such as security, stability and game balance. The abusive purpose appears only when it can be shown, with reasons, that the design is aimed mainly at exploiting your weakness and capturing value disproportionately, and not at those ends.

Where it comes from

Abusive teleology cannot function as a moral wild card.p. 456

PTA teleologia abusiva não pode funcionar como coringa moral.

[...] abusive teleology is not to be confused with a merely harsh business choice, with debatable design or with architecture that is simply efficient for the supplier.p. 666

PT[...] a teleologia abusiva não se confunde com opção empresarial apenas severa, com design discutível ou com arquitetura simplesmente eficiente para o fornecedor.

It must depend on the rational demonstration that the architecture is oriented, predominantly, toward the exploitation of vulnerability and the disproportionate capture of value, with persistent opacity and asymmetry, and not toward legitimate purposes of the service, such as security, stability and the balance of the ecosystem.p. 456

PTEla deve depender da demonstração racional de que a arquitetura é orientada, de modo dominante, à exploração da vulnerabilidade e à captura desproporcional de valor, com opacidade e assimetria persistentes, e não a finalidades legítimas do serviço, como segurança, estabilidade e equilíbrio do ecossistema.

This formulation is no license for judicial impressionism or for abstract moralizing about the digital environment.p. 666

PTEssa formulação não é licença para impressionismo judicial nem moralização abstrata do ambiente digital.

Unlawful act of architecture

We are used to looking for abuse in a contract clause, in a sentence you can underline. But sometimes no clause on its own is the problem.

The problem is the pattern that repeats. Options that come pre-checked, friction whenever you try to do what you want, manufactured urgency and a cancellation path that seems designed to make you give up. And also equivalences that shift whenever it suits the company, penalties that are hard to understand and proof that is impossible to gather. When these pieces work together as a method, in a stable way, the problem is no longer only in the text. It is in the design.

That is why it is said that, in these cases, design is also conduct. The way the service was built is treated as something the company does, and answers for, and not as a neutral setting where things simply happen.

Where it comes from

The unlawful act does not arise, therefore, from a singular episode that can be treated as an accident. It emerges when defaults, frictions, manufactured urgencies, hostile cancellation routes, elastic equivalences, sanction regimes or proof arrangements work together as a method of exploitation.p. 666

PTO ilícito não nasce, portanto, de um episódio singular que pode ser tratado como acidente. Ele surge quando defaults, fricções, urgências fabricadas, rotas de cancelamento hostis, equivalências elásticas, regimes de sanção ou arranjos de prova funcionam em conjunto como método de exploração.

When this governance repeatedly organizes informational noise, decisional friction, evidentiary opacity, non-auditable mutation and insufficient restoration, the unlawful act is no longer only in the statement. It is in the architecture.p. 664

PTQuando essa governança organiza, de forma reiterada, ruído informacional, fricção decisória, opacidade probatória, mutação não auditável e recomposição insuficiente, o ilícito já não está apenas no enunciado. Está na arquitetura.

The core of the parameter lies in treating architecture as conduct.p. 732

PTO núcleo do parâmetro está em tratar a arquitetura como conduta.

What is called into question is not design as style, visual language or interface aestheticsp. 664

PTO que se põe em questão não é o design enquanto estilo, linguagem visual ou estética de interface

Is everything innovation, or is everything in the contract?

This avoids two common mistakes. The first is accepting that everything is innovation, as if the mere fact that the platform changes all the time left it free to downgrade anything. The second is hunting for loose clauses in the contract, as if the service could only be controlled in pieces of text and never in the way it works.

What is needed, in the end, are familiar criteria. Information you can really understand, respect for trust, equivalence of what was bought, changes that leave a trace, a real possibility of undoing what was done and at least some chance to contest. When the design starts working against these criteria, the service itself becomes an instrument of exploitation.

Where it comes from

The first would be to dissolve the problem in the vague language of “innovation” or of the platform's “dynamic nature”, as if the continuous reorganization of the environment were enough to immunize any relevant downgrading of enjoyment. The second would be to reduce the legal response to the atomized hunt for isolated clauses, as if supply could only be controlled in its textual fragments and never in its operational logic.p. 664

PTO primeiro seria dissolver o problema na linguagem vaga da “inovação” ou da “natureza dinâmica” da plataforma, como se a contínua reorganização do ambiente bastasse para imunizar qualquer rebaixamento relevante da fruição. O segundo seria reduzir a resposta jurídica à caça atomizada de cláusulas isoladas, como se o fornecimento só pudesse ser controlado em seus fragmentos textuais e nunca em sua lógica operacional.

And these criteria have already been prepared throughout the previous course: material intelligibility of information, protection of trust, functional value equivalence, open auditability, real possibility of reversal and minimum conditions of contestation.p. 664

PTE esses critérios já foram preparados ao longo do percurso anterior: inteligibilidade material da informação, proteção da confiança, equivalência de valor funcional, auditabilidade aberta, possibilidade real de reversão e condições mínimas de contestação.

Abusive teleology arises precisely when the design of supply stops respecting these parameters and comes to operate against them, converting the very structure of the service into an instrument of exploitation.p. 664

PTA teleologia abusiva surge precisamente quando o desenho do fornecimento deixa de respeitar esses parâmetros e passa a operar contra eles, convertendo a própria estrutura do serviço em instrumento de exploração.

LawDark patterns

Some screen traps already have a name known worldwide, dark patterns. Examples include the box that comes pre-checked so you sign up for an extra service, the cancel button hidden in light gray and the warning that only a few units are left when that is not true.

What matters in these cases is not an isolated sentence but the way choices are presented and steered. And Brazilian consumer protection law already allows many cases of deceptive design to be punished. Design thus stops being treated as neutral aesthetics and goes back to being what it is, a commercial practice with an economic goal, which the law can characterize.

Where it comes from

[...] the asymmetry of intelligibility and agency can be produced and exploited by the interaction architecture itself, making the design of options and avenues of reaction legally relevant [...] This point becomes clearer when interface patterns are described as a sanctionable commercial practice, since abusiveness stops depending on an isolated textual statement and comes to include the way choices are presented and inducedp. 405

PT[...] a assimetria de inteligibilidade e de agência pode ser produzida e explorada pela própria arquitetura de interação, tornando juridicamente relevante o desenho das opções e das vias de reação [...] Esse ponto se torna mais nítido quando padrões de interface são descritos como prática comercial sancionável, já que a abusividade deixa de depender de uma afirmação textual isolada e passa a incluir a forma como escolhas são apresentadas e induzidas

several of these provisions are related to dark patterns, which allows us to conclude that under the Brazilian legal framework it is possible to sanction several cases of deceptive designp. 405

PTvárias dessas disposições estão relacionadas a padrões obscuros, o que nos permite concluir que sob o quadro legal brasileiro é possível sancionar vários casos de desenho enganoso

When it is recognized that it is possible to sanction cases of deceptive design and the provisions of art. 39 of the CDC1 are linked to dark patterns, design is put back in its correct place, as an economically oriented and legally qualifiable commercial practice, and not as aesthetic neutralityp. 432

PTQuando se reconhece que é possível sancionar casos de desenho enganoso e se vinculam as disposições do art. 39 do CDC a padrões obscuros, o design é recolocado no seu lugar correto, como prática comercial economicamente orientada e juridicamente qualificável, e não como neutralidade estética

It emerges when defaults, frictions, manufactured urgencies, hostile cancellation routesp. 666

PTEle surge quando defaults, fricções, urgências fabricadas, rotas de cancelamento hostis

Misleading advertising by architectureMisleading advertising by architecture

An advertisement does not need to lie to deceive. It can tell the truth in pieces and lie as a whole, just by the way it organizes what you see first and what is left for later.

It works like this. The good side of the purchase goes to the center of the screen, big and colorful. The cost, the chance that things will change and the limits go to the footer, to a link that only appears later or to a layer nobody opens when deciding. Each piece of information is there, but taken together they lead you to the wrong understanding, and it is that whole that the law can examine.

Where it comes from

In simpler terms, communication does not need to lie to deceive. It is enough that it manages the interpretive framing in a sufficiently asymmetric way. Advertising can tell the truth in pieces and still lie as a whole.p. 626

PTEm termos mais simples, a comunicação não precisa mentir para enganar. Basta que ela administre o enquadramento interpretativo de forma suficientemente assimétrica. A publicidade pode dizer a verdade em pedaços e ainda assim mentir no conjunto.

This is the core of misleading advertising by semiotic architecture. The architecture of the advertisement becomes legally relevant when the supplier structures the consumer's understanding so as to reinforce the positive side of the experience and push the onerous, mutable or limited side out of focusp. 626

PTEsse é o núcleo da publicidade enganosa por arquitetura semiótica. A arquitetura do anúncio passa a ser juridicamente relevante quando o fornecedor estrutura a compreensão do consumidor de modo a reforçar o lado positivo da experiência e empurrar o lado oneroso, mutável ou limitado para fora do foco

It can be produced by the way the essential is ranked, by selective emphasis on advantages and by the displacement of relevant conditions to disclaimers, low-visibility layers, later links or discursive zones that do not usefully form part of the decisional momentp. 626

PTEle pode ser produzido pela forma como o essencial é hierarquizado, pela ênfase seletiva nas vantagens e pelo deslocamento das condições relevantes para disclaimers, camadas de baixa visibilidade, links posteriores ou zonas discursivas que não integram de modo útil o momento decisório

Biographical continuityBiographical continuity

When an account is deleted, you do not lose just one item. You lose the whole position, the history, the inventory, the trajectory and your place in that world.

Your story there was made of repeated choices, performance, time, purchases, cooperation with other players and signs of belonging built up over years. It was built under the platform's rules, but it is still yours, because it is the result of what you did. That is why protection can include the continuity of this biography, and not only a refund for a purchase that went wrong.

Where it comes from

The clause is valuable because it lays the problem bare: what is lost is not only an isolated item, but the global position of enjoyment, the history, the inventory, the trajectory and the practical continuity of insertion in the ecosystemp. 540-541

PTA cláusula é valiosa porque desnuda o problema: não se perde apenas item isolado, mas a posição global de fruição, o histórico, o inventário, a trajetória e a continuidade prática de inserção no ecossistema

It is a biographical result produced by repeated choices, performance, investment of time, acquisition, cooperation, accumulation of signs of belonging and permanence in the ecosystem. Even though this construction takes place under the mediation of the Lex Plataformae, it remains attributable to the consumer as an expression of biographical continuity within the supply regime.p. 537

PTEla é resultado biográfico produzido por escolhas reiteradas, desempenho, investimento de tempo, aquisição, cooperação, acúmulo de sinais de pertencimento e permanência no ecossistema. Ainda que essa construção ocorra sob mediação da Lex Plataformae, continua sendo atribuível ao consumidor como expressão de continuidade biográfica no interior do regime de fornecimento.

Thus, what is asserted is only that the consumer's integrity may include the preservation of minimum conditions of biographical continuity and stability of symbolic insertion, and not only the refund of a frustrated purchasep. 537

PTAssim, o que se afirma é apenas que a integridade do consumidor pode incluir preservação de condições mínimas de continuidade biográfica e estabilidade de inserção simbólica, e não só o reembolso de uma compra frustrada

In serious cases, losing the account, the history or the trajectory can harm your personality rights and give rise to moral damages, that is, compensation for harm that is not financial. This happens when the loss affects, in a way that can be shown, your story, your belonging or the reputation and memory you built in there.

Not every annoyance reaches this point, and so you have to show what was affected. But when everything changes and you no longer feel the same, that is not drama or a passing irritation. It is the foreseeable consequence of a regime in which the value of what you use and your place in the game depend on someone else's management.

Where it comes from

In qualified cases, the loss of account, history or trajectory may constitute moral damages for injury to personality, especially when it objectively affects biographical continuity, institutionalized belonging in the ecosystem or relevant anchors of reputation and memory, and not mere subjective displeasure.p. 541

PTEm hipóteses qualificadas, a perda de conta, histórico ou trajetória pode configurar dano moral por lesão à personalidade, especialmente quando atinge de modo objetivável continuidade biográfica, pertencimento institucionalizado no ecossistema ou lastros relevantes de reputação e memória, e não mero dissabor subjetivo.

When everything changes and the consumer does not feel the same, this is not subjective drama or mere displeasure. It is the foreseeable consequence of a regime in which use value, symbolic value and continuity of enjoyment are subject to the governance of third parties.p. 21

PTQuando tudo muda e o consumidor não se sente o mesmo, não se trata de drama subjetivo ou mero dissabor. É a consequência previsível de um regime em que o valor de uso, o valor simbólico e a continuidade da fruição ficam sujeitos à governança de terceiros.

And consumer law already has doors for this. When the design takes usefulness away from what you bought, the door is the rule on quality defects. When it pushes your choice with invented scarcity, manufactured haste and hurdles on one side only, the door is the rule on abusive practices. And when all this becomes a rule written into the terms, the door is the rule on abusive clauses.

There is no need to invent a new law for platforms. What changes is what these doors can see, which is no longer just the episode but the pattern that produces the loss.

Where it comes from

The first axis of framing falls on the quality defect, whenever the operating program of the service produces a relevant loss of adequacy, continuity or functional equivalence of enjoyment.p. 674

PTO primeiro eixo de enquadramento recai sobre o vício de qualidade, sempre que o programa de funcionamento do serviço produz perda relevante de adequação, continuidade ou equivalência funcional da fruição.

The second axis of framing falls on abusive practices, and it is here that abusive teleology appears most strongly as a repeated method of exploitation.p. 676

PTO segundo eixo de enquadramento recai sobre as práticas abusivas, e é nele que a teleologia abusiva aparece com mais força como método reiterado de exploração.

When artificial scarcity, manufactured urgency, cycles of promise, fabrication of risk, asymmetric frictions and hindered reversibility operate in a predictable and repeated wayp. 676

PTQuando escassez artificial, urgência fabricada, ciclos de promessa, fabricação de risco, fricções assimétricas e reversibilidade dificultada operam de modo previsível e repetido

The third axis is that of abusive clauses, whenever the exploitative program gains contractual institutionalization in predisposed terms, policies and acceptances.p. 677

PTO terceiro eixo é o das cláusulas abusivas, sempre que o programa exploratório ganha institucionalização contratual em termos, políticas e aceites predispostos.

It does not require leaving the CDC or inventing a parallel law of platforms.p. 679

PTEle não exige sair do CDC nem inventar um direito paralelo das plataformas.

This is how legal control stops aiming only at the episode and comes to reach the governance pattern that produces, reinforces or normalizes the injury.p. 679

PTÉ assim que o controle jurídico deixa de mirar apenas o episódio e passa a atingir o padrão de governança que produz, reforça ou normaliza a lesão.

What can you demand?

When loss is the rule, the problem is the designWhen loss is the rule, the problem is the design

When does loss become part of the game's routine?

When several small things work together to take advantage of your weaknesses or to make your experience worse as a matter of routine. They are small frictions that wear you down, losses everyone already treats as normal and manufactured urgency. They are also artificial scarcity, a lack of clarity that works in the company's favor and equivalences that shift whenever it suits the company.

You do not need to prove that someone wanted to harm you. What matters is the purpose that shows in the very functioning of the environment. It can be seen in the pattern that repeats, the coherence among the pieces and the effect produced again and again.

What must the game's design not aim at?

Not to build the experience around an exploitative purpose that goes against good faith, transparency and balance, or that disregards what you legitimately acquired.

How do you show that the problem forms a pattern?

The pattern, the fact that it repeats and the way the pieces of the environment combine. And the relevant impact this has on your freedom of choice in practice, on your understanding or on the utility of what you use.

Can you ask for more than fixing one case?

More than fixing an episode. You can ask for the game's paths to be redone, for transparency to increase and for the design to be corrected. When this design produces a pattern of injury that repeats, the response can also reach the structure that reproduces it.

Is every way of selling and persuading abusive?

Not every way of selling, persuading or running the game is abusive. What is controlled is a program that can be recognized in the functioning of the environment, and not someone's hidden will.

Where it comes from

Whenever microfrictions, normalized losses, manufactured urgencies, artificial scarcities, strategic opacity, elastic equivalences and other mechanisms operate in a coordinated way to extract value from vulnerability or degrade enjoyment as a routine of the environment, there will be a case of abusive teleology.p. 746

PTSempre que microfricções, perdas normalizadas, urgências fabricadas, escassezes artificiais, opacidade estratégica, equivalências elásticas e outros mecanismos operarem de modo coordenado para extrair valor da vulnerabilidade ou degradar a fruição como rotina do ambiente, haverá hipótese de teleologia abusiva.

This is not about investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor about moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governance. The focus is on the functional purpose objectively inscribed in the arrangement, as revealed by its replicability, its internal coherence and its repeated impact on the consumer's experience.p. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada. O foco está na finalidade funcional objetivamente inscrita no arranjo, tal como se revela por sua replicabilidade, por sua coerência interna e por seu impacto reiterado sobre a experiência do consumidor.

The corresponding supply duty is not to structure the experience on the basis of an exploitative purpose incompatible with good faith, transparency, balance and protection of the enjoyment legitimately incorporated into the bond.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de não estruturar a experiência a partir de finalidade exploratória incompatível com boa-fé, transparência, equilíbrio e tutela da fruição legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo.

The minimum required proof consists of demonstrating the pattern, its replicability, the coordination between mechanisms of the ecosystem and the relevant impact produced on practical autonomy, intelligibility or utility of the experience.p. 746-747

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste na demonstração do padrão, de sua replicabilidade, da coordenação entre mecanismos do ecossistema e do impacto relevante produzido sobre autonomia prática, inteligibilidade ou utilidade da experiência.

The institutional consequence goes beyond redress of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced duties of transparency, design correction and structural or collective measures.p. 747

PTA consequência institucional vai além da reparação de episódios isolados e pode incluir reconfiguração de fluxos, deveres reforçados de transparência, correção de desenho e medidas estruturais ou coletivas.

What is controlled is not an inner will, but a recognizable programp. 732

PTO que se controla não é uma vontade íntima, mas um programa reconhecível
