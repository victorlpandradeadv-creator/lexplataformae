Your rights · By problem

The rule that harmed me was buried in the terms of use. Now what?

The company says the rule was in the terms of use, which are the contract you accept when you create the account or make a purchase. Having it written somewhere is not enough, because what counts is whether, when you made your decision, you could understand what that rule meant.

Where it comes from

The mere accumulation of texts, links, notices, policies, terms and explanatory windows does not, by itself, satisfy the requirement of adequate information.p. 722

PTA simples acumulação de textos, links, avisos, políticas, termos e janelas explicativas não satisfaz, por si, a exigência de informação adequada.

The decisive point, therefore, is not to prove that “the text was there”, but to assess whether the legally relevant meaning was knowable in real terms.p. 601

PTO ponto decisivo, portanto, não está em provar que “o texto estava lá”, mas em aferir se o sentido juridicamente relevante era cognoscível em termos reais.

How do you know whether the rule was really hidden?

Information can fail in three ways. There can be so much text that the essentials get lost among everything else, or the rule can be scattered across documents, screens and menus that do not connect.

The third way is the screen flow itself, which highlights what attracts you and pushes what works against you to places almost no one looks. In all three cases the rule exists, but it does not inform.

The question, then, is whether the rule appeared clearly and at the moment when it could change your decision to buy, to continue or to react.

Where it comes from

Informational inadequacy may thus take the form of excess, dispersion and flow architecture. Excess, when textual overload makes it impractical to distinguish the essential from the accessory. Dispersion, when the relevant elements of the relationship are fragmented across multiple documents, screens and paths without sufficient integration.p. 722

PTA inadequação informacional, assim, pode assumir a forma de excesso, dispersão e arquitetura de fluxo. Excesso, quando a sobrecarga textual torna impraticável distinguir o essencial do acessório. Dispersão, quando os elementos relevantes do vínculo são pulverizados em múltiplos documentos, telas e caminhos sem integração suficiente.

Informational inadequacy can thus take the form of excess, dispersion and flow architecture. Excess, when textual overload makes it impracticable to distinguish the essential from the accessory. Dispersion, when the relevant elements of the bond are scattered across multiple documents, screens and paths without sufficient integration. Flow architecture, when the informational experience is designed to highlight the attractive, simplify the favorable and move the onerous, the contingent or the restrictive to zones of low visibility, lower salience or improbable reading.p. 722-723

PTA inadequação informacional, assim, pode assumir a forma de excesso, dispersão e arquitetura de fluxo. Excesso, quando a sobrecarga textual torna impraticável distinguir o essencial do acessório. Dispersão, quando os elementos relevantes do vínculo são pulverizados em múltiplos documentos, telas e caminhos sem integração suficiente. Arquitetura de fluxo, quando a experiência informacional é desenhada para valorizar o atrativo, simplificar o favorável e deslocar o oneroso, o contingente ou o restritivo para zonas de baixa visibilidade, menor saliência ou leitura improvável.

The legally relevant question is no longer whether a given piece of information was somewhere in the supplier's documentary ecosystem. It becomes whether that information was made accessible, comprehensible and relevant at the moment it could influence the legitimate formation of the consumer's expectation or reaction.p. 723

PTA pergunta juridicamente relevante deixa de ser se determinado dado estava em algum lugar do ecossistema documental do fornecedor. Passa a ser se esse dado foi tornado acessível, compreensível e relevante no momento em que poderia influenciar a formação legítima da expectativa ou a reação do consumidor.

Which screens show how the rule reached you?

It helps to keep a record of the path you took before accepting or buying, with the screens, buttons and notices and where the link to the terms was. It is also worth saving the version of the terms in force that day, because these texts change over time.

If the rule came in a patch note, which is the text the company publishes when it changes the service, it is worth saving that note and the date it was published. Publishing the note is not enough when the change affects the core of what you use.

Where it comes from

The minimum required proof calls for identification of what was shown, at what moment, with what salience, through which channels and to what extent this organization allowed the consumer to understand what was legally relevant in order to adhere, remain or react.p. 744

PTA prova mínima exigível requer identificação do que foi mostrado, em que momento, com qual saliência, por quais canais e em que medida essa organização permitia ao consumidor compreender o que era juridicamente relevante para aderir, permanecer ou reagir.

The consumer does not find just a single, centralized and stable contractual text. The consumer faces successive interface flows, scattered notices, secondary menus, help centers, versioned policies, partial explanations, promotional communications, contextual notifications and later changes that redesign the informational field without offering a material synthesis of what changed.p. 722

PTO consumidor não encontra apenas um texto contratual centralizado e estável. Enfrenta fluxos sucessivos de interface, avisos dispersos, menus secundários, centrais de ajuda, políticas versionadas, explicações parciais, comunicações promocionais, notificações contextuais e alterações posteriores que redesenham o campo informacional sem oferecer síntese material do que mudou.

In continuous digital services, it is not enough for the platform to “publish an update”, “release a patch note” or bury the modification in layers of text, links or generic notices.p. 657

PTEm serviços digitais contínuos, não basta que a plataforma “publique uma atualização”, “solte uma nota de patch” ou enterre a modificação em camadas de texto, links ou avisos genéricos.

What can you ask for when the rule was not properly disclosed?

You may ask the company to correct the way it informs, put what matters in plain view and show what it actually displayed to you. You may also ask for what you lost because of the lack of clarity to be restored.

Depending on the case, the obscure rule may cease to apply against you. Consumer law says that a contract does not bind you when you had no real chance to learn its content beforehand, or when it was written in a way that makes it hard to understand.

When the text is confusing, the confusion may weigh against the company, because it drafted the contract and you had no way to negotiate.

Where it comes from

The possible institutional consequence includes a duty to correct the communicational arrangement, reinforcement of useful transparency, orders to display and restoration of the enjoyment affected by decisional opacity.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional possível inclui dever de correção do arranjo comunicacional, reforço de transparência útil, ordens de exibição e recomposição da fruição afetada por opacidade decisória.

The CDC already contains the normative core of this problem. The offer and presentation of products or services must ensure correct, clear, precise and conspicuous information. This means that the technical complexity of the supply does not authorize the supplier to hide behind indecipherable vocabulary, documentary cascades or communicational architectures that deliver words and withhold meaning. Likewise, consumer contracts do not bind the consumer when the consumer is not given a real opportunity to learn of their content beforehand, nor when they are drafted in a way that hinders understanding of their meaning and scope.p. 601

PTO CDC já contém o núcleo normativo desse problema. A oferta e a apresentação de produtos ou serviços devem assegurar informações corretas, claras, precisas e ostensivas. Isso significa que a complexidade técnica do fornecimento não autoriza o fornecedor a se esconder atrás de vocabulário indecifrável, cascatas documentais ou arquiteturas comunicacionais que entregam palavras e retêm sentido. Do mesmo modo, contratos de consumo não obrigam o consumidor quando não lhe é dada oportunidade real de conhecimento prévio do conteúdo, nem quando são redigidos de modo a dificultar a compreensão de seu sentido e alcance.

What is asked for is correction of the decision environment, neutralization of obscurity, production of records and restoration of the affected enjoyment.p. 609

PTO que se pede é correção do ambiente decisório, neutralização da obscuridade, exibição de registros e recomposição da fruição afetada.

The legal qualification tends to apply as a defect in the informational quality of the supply, an abusive practice by functional obscuring and, depending on the case, grounds for the invalidity of clauses or limitations incompatible with the legitimately formed expectation.p. 744

PTA qualificação jurídica tende a incidir como vício de qualidade informacional do fornecimento, prática abusiva por obscurecimento funcional e, conforme o caso, suporte para invalidade de cláusulas ou de limitações incompatíveis com a expectativa legitimamente formada.

Pre-drafted clauses must be read so as to respect the legitimate interests of those who accepted them without being able to negotiate them, and obscure drafting triggers precisely the presumption that the supplier failed in its duty to organize consent on a fair basis.p. 604

PTCláusulas predispostas devem ser lidas de modo a respeitar os legítimos interesses de quem as aceitou sem poder negociá-las, e a redação obscura aciona justamente a presunção de que o fornecedor falhou no dever de organizar o consentimento em bases leais.

Where can you show that the rule was hidden?

The complaint to the company can ask it to show where and when the rule was presented to you. If the answer is only that everything was in the contract, it confuses existing with informing.

Outside the company, the case can be taken to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, routes detailed on the channels page. When the same rule affects all customers, the correction can reach everyone through action by public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

The supplier does not fulfill its role merely by throwing information into the environment; it must also demonstrate, when questioned, that it informed adequately, that it regularly exercised its power of change and that the impact produced was compatible with the promised performance.p. 660

PTO fornecedor não cumpre seu papel apenas jogando informação no ambiente; também precisa demonstrar, quando questionado, que informou adequadamente, que exerceu regularmente seu poder de alteração e que o impacto produzido foi compatível com a prestação prometida.

The centrality of verifiable adequacy also prevents the supplier's standard defense from resting on the claim that “everything was in the contract”. This formula confuses formal existence with material fulfillment of the duty.p. 723

PTA centralidade da adequação verificável também impede que a defesa padrão do fornecedor se satisfaça com a alegação de que “tudo estava no contrato”. Essa fórmula confunde existência formal com cumprimento material do dever.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets. These standards offer a concrete criterion for consent decrees, recommendations, civil investigations and minimum governance requirements: functional impact reports on relevant changes, verifiable transparency protocols, preservation of essential records, understandable contestation procedures and minimum criteria of restoration in case of relevant degradation of enjoyment.p. 662-663

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma. Esses standards oferecem critério concreto para termos de ajustamento, recomendações, investigações civis e exigências mínimas de governança: relatórios de impacto funcional em alterações relevantes, protocolos de transparência verificável, preservação de registros essenciais, procedimentos de contestação compreensíveis e critérios mínimos de recomposição em caso de degradação relevante da fruição.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is every long contract illegal?

No. The question is whether an ordinary person could read and understand what the rule meant, what rights and duties it created and what consequences it carried.

Nor is the question whether or not you read it. It is whether, given how the information was organized, it could guide your decision.

Having the rule in the contract does not save the company, but it does not condemn it on its own either, because everything depends on how the whole was organized.

Where it comes from

What matters is whether the average consumer could read and understand what those clauses meant, which rights and obligations were being assumed and which practical consequences followed from adherence.p. 604

PTO que importa é se o consumidor médio podia ler e compreender o que aquelas cláusulas significavam, quais direitos e obrigações estavam sendo assumidos e quais consequências práticas decorriam da adesão.

The point is not to assert that the consumer “did not read” or “did not understand”p. 401

PTO ponto não é afirmar que o consumidor “não leu” ou “não entendeu”

In environments of high informational complexity, the contract may even contain a nominal reference to the disputed risk, change or limitation, and still fail to inform adequately if the organization of the communicational whole produces noise, minimizes the impact of the relevant information or makes its practical connection with the consumer's decision unworkable.p. 723

PTEm ambientes de alta complexidade informacional, o contrato pode até conter referência nominal ao risco, à alteração ou à limitação controvertida, mas ainda assim deixar de informar de maneira adequada se a organização do conjunto comunicacional produz ruído, minimiza o impacto do dado relevante ou inviabiliza sua conexão prática com a decisão do consumidor.

One word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
