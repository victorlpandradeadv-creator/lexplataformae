The jurist's path

This path reconstructs, for lawyers, judges, members of the Public Prosecutor's Office and students, the legal regime of the IOC. Each chapter starts from a problem, shows why the ordinary reading falls short and arrives at what can be demonstrated and claimed.

What are the chapters?

  1. 1How to read this doctrineContinuous digital consumption confronts the jurist with a conflict that the grammar of defect and accident captures poorly, because the loss usually arrives as technical normality rather than as an event. From this follow the problem and the hypothesis that organize the chapters ahead, the normative scope in which they operate, the choice of a game as a laboratory and the weight of each kind of proof.
  2. 2Why technology changes the legal questionThe defense that the loss was simply how the software works treats as nature what, in a governed digital environment, is a design choice someone made. The philosophy of technique, of media and of design brings these choices back into the field of attribution, and each of its authors changes the question the jurist puts to the case.
  3. 3How the platform extracts valuePlatform consumption tends to be organized around a business model that extracts value from control of access, from manufactured scarcity and from the consumer's own presence, and this model explains supplier conduct that reading a clause in isolation cannot reach. Read with Varoufakis, Dowbor and Marx, the model gives the jurist conditions of attribution, and not a condemnation of the market.
  4. 4The Immaterial Object of ConsumptionThe Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) is the category that qualifies as an immaterial product, under Article 3, paragraph 1, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC) 1 , the utility that only exists as a position of enjoyment sustained by private governance. Without this qualification the case seems to have no object, and with it the jurist knows what to demonstrate in court.
  5. 5The law of the platformThe law of the platform is the private ordering through which the platform creates rules, converts them into architecture and enforces them with sanctions that reach the consumer's position of enjoyment, without being either state law or a mere contract. This order takes shape and punishes through design, reaches a consumer who lives inside it and finds its limit in state law.
  6. 6Semiotic vulnerabilitySemiotic vulnerability is the form the consumer's factual vulnerability takes when the platform controls the signs, metrics, visibility and records through which the consumer understands what they acquired, perceives what they lost and tries to prove the loss. In these environments formally informed consent is not enough, and the category fits Article 4, item I, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC) 1 without creating a new vulnerability.
  7. 7Information, advertising and anticipationOn platforms, the decision to buy is formed in an environment in which information, advertising, calendar and signs of prestige operate together, and the value of what is bought depends on criteria the supplier itself administers. For this reason the duty to inform is read as verifiable adequacy, the offer as controllable management of meaning, and the defense that everything was in the accepted terms loses force.
  8. 8Open auditabilityOpen auditability is the minimum threshold of external verifiability that makes it possible to reconstruct what the supplier changed, when, why and with what effect, whenever the change or the sanction relevantly affects the enjoyment of an IOC. The requirement arises within the CDC, has a defined scope and does not yield to secrecy or to technical complexity.
  9. 9Functional value equivalenceFunctional value equivalence is the criterion for judging whether a change to an IOC preserved, in substantial terms, what justified the purchase. The test rests on Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC 1 , it reaches the appearance of an item when that appearance is what was bought, and it leads to restoration.
  10. 10Abusive teleology and the unlawful act of architectureAbusive teleology is the category of unlawful act of architecture that makes it possible to impute to the supplier a program of exploitation built into the design of the service, when no clause or isolated episode is enough to show the injury. It operates through five cumulative requirements, separates aggressive business from exploitative purpose and enters the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC) through doors the law has already opened.
  11. 11Protected legal interestsThe protected legal interests are what the injury reaches when the platform downgrades, sanctions or erases the position of enjoyment of an IOC, even when formal access remains open, and they are called personal integrity, symbolic identity and biographical continuity. They are defined by public criteria and are tied to civil personality, which is why the loss of a history or a title can go beyond the loss of an item.
  12. 12The constitutional order and the sectoral frameworkPlatform governance is an exercise of private economic power over positions of continued enjoyment, and the jurist needs to know on what basis the legal order limits it without treating the company as the State or reducing the conflict to the contract. That basis comes from the economic Constitution, the horizontal effect of fundamental rights and the CDC, while the Legal Framework for Electronic Games and the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents help bring the sector into view without resolving the conflict of enjoyment on their own.
  13. 13Intellectual propertyIntellectual property enters the regime of the IOC as part of the infrastructure through which the platform defines what can be accessed, kept, altered, withdrawn, audited and contested, and not as a topic parallel or superior to consumer protection. Exclusivity has come to organize competition and enjoyment, and its social function, read from Article 5, item XXIX, of the Brazilian Federal Constitution 1 , makes it a field of reconciliation with the CDC.
  14. 14ProofIn platform-governed consumption, the disputed fact arises and remains within the supplier's infrastructure, so whoever controls records, versions and traces of sanction also controls the conditions for reconstructing what happened. Semiotic vulnerability then turns into allegation, proof and claim, and the CDC and the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC) provide the tools to request the right record and allocate the burden.
  15. 15The remediesRemedies close the examination of a conflict over an IOC, because they answer what to do with the injury once the object has been qualified, the change reconstructed, equivalence assessed and the evidence organized. Primacy belongs to functional restoration and regime correction, and compensation comes after them, not in their place.
