Your rights · By problem

I want to leave, but I would lose everything I built there. Does that count?

You think about quitting the game, the network or the service, but leaving means losing the rank, the reputation, the history and the ties that only exist in there. This cost of leaving counts, and it helps explain why the company has greater duties toward those who already depend on the environment.

Where it comes from

Permanence comes to depend on continuous alignment with mutable operational standards, and leaving is no longer simple substitution of a service, because it involves a discontinuity of prestige, comparability and belonging. This dependence on administered recognition is legally relevant because it makes integrity and identity governed variables of enjoyment and requires, on the dogmatic plane, reinforced duties of qualified transparency, contestability, auditability and restoration proportional to the type of power exercised by the Lex Plataformae.p. 358-359

PTA permanência passa a depender de alinhamento contínuo a padrões operacionais mutáveis, e a saída deixa de ser simples substituição de serviço, porque envolve descontinuidade de prestígio, comparabilidade e pertencimento. Essa dependência de reconhecimento administrado é juridicamente relevante porque torna integridade e identidade variáveis governadas da fruição e exige, no plano dogmático, deveres reforçados de transparência qualificada, contestabilidade, auditabilidade e recomposição proporcionais ao tipo de poder exercido pela Lex Plataformae.

How do you know whether the cost of leaving has started to trap you?

Leaving an ordinary service means switching suppliers. Here it is different, because the history, the rank, the signs of prestige and the ties you made stay with the company and are only worth something in there. Going away interrupts your recognizable existence in that world.

This cost goes beyond money and convenience, because it includes losing the continuity of your story and the positions of recognition you hold there. None of this assumes that you are truly trapped or unable to act, since what counts is dependence in practice. And the difficulty of quitting is not a weakness of yours, but a foreseeable effect of an environment that measures, compares and rewards those who stay.

The clearest sign appears when the company changes the rules and you accept only because leaving would cost more. In a long relationship like this, your yes after the change is often just a forced adherence to the new state of things.

Where it comes from

The exit barrier can be described as a form of social death within the ecosystem. The expression does not operate as moral dramatization, but as a functional description of the effect produced when the platform concentrates the records that sustain belonging and relevance, that is, history, repertoire, ranked position, signs of prestige, community ties and traces of participation. Leaving the environment, under these conditions, is not like migrating from one service to another, because it implies interrupting one's very socially recognizable existence in that world, with loss of symbolic continuity and practical erasure of marks of trajectory that have become a condition of public commensurability.p. 359

PTA barreira de saída pode ser descrita como uma forma de morte social no interior do ecossistema. A expressão não opera como dramatização moral, mas como descrição funcional do efeito produzido quando a plataforma concentra os registros que sustentam pertencimento e relevância, isto é, histórico, repertório, posição ranqueada, sinais de prestígio, vínculos comunitários e rastros de participação. Abandonar o ambiente, nessas condições, não se assemelha a migrar de um serviço para outro, porque implica interromper a própria existência socialmente reconhecível naquele mundo, com perda de continuidade simbólica e apagamento prático de marcas de trajetória que se tornaram condição de comensurabilidade pública.

In this same register, symbolic identity and personal integrity are connected by two effects of dependence that can be named symbolic captivity and identity captivity. [...] The second designates the form of dependence in which the cost of exit is not exhausted in financial or convenience dimensions, because it implies loss of narrative continuity and of positions of recognition whose existence takes place, decisively, within the ecosystem, through networks, histories, roles and internal circuits of value.p. 466

PTNesse mesmo registro, a identidade simbólica e a integridade pessoal se conectam por dois efeitos de dependência que podem ser nomeados como cativeiro simbólico e cativeiro identitário. [...] O segundo designa a forma de dependência em que o custo de saída não se esgota em dimensões financeiras ou de conveniência, porque implica perda de continuidade narrativa e de posições de reconhecimento cuja existência se dá, de maneira decisiva, dentro do ecossistema, por redes, históricos, papéis e circuitos internos de valor.

The institutionally relevant effect of this picture can be described as identity captivity. The expression does not designate physical imprisonment nor assume inability to act, but names practical dependence, by which the continuity of enjoyment and the stability of symbolic identity come to be mediated by the administered ecosystem.p. 358

PTO efeito institucionalmente relevante desse quadro pode ser descrito como cativeiro identitário. A expressão não designa aprisionamento físico nem supõe incapacidade de agir, mas nomeia dependência prática, pela qual a continuidade da fruição e a estabilidade da identidade simbólica passam a ser mediadas pelo ecossistema administrado.

The subjection described, therefore, is neither a moral trait nor an individual pathology, but a predictable effect of an environment in which performance, exposure and measurability work as a grammar of permanence.p. 361

PTA sujeição descrita, portanto, não é traço moral nem patologia individual, mas efeito previsível de um ambiente em que desempenho, exposição e mensurabilidade funcionam como gramática de permanência.

In captive contracts, the so-called supervening “consent” is often nothing more than forced adherence to the new state of affairs, especially when the consumer is already invested in the relationship and depends on the ecosystem to keep enjoying what they acquired.p. 654-655

PTEm contratos cativos, o chamado “consenso” superveniente frequentemente não passa de adesão forçada ao novo estado de coisas, especialmente quando o consumidor já está investido na relação e depende do ecossistema para continuar fruindo aquilo que adquiriu.

What is worth recording before deciding to leave?

It is worth recording your history, the rank, the badges and titles, the inventory and the rewards that only you have, with screenshots and dates. After the account disappears or is left behind, only statements, emails, old screenshots and memory remain to show what existed.

What shows the weight of what you built is not the sadness of leaving, but the value the system itself gives to it, through badges, visibility, requirements for taking part and reputation. Before leaving, it is also fair to ask the company for confirmation of and access to the data it keeps about you, as well as portability, which is the transfer of that data.

Where it comes from

Once the assets are removed or the state of the account is reverted, the user comes to depend on old statements, scattered emails, past captures and reconstructed memory to prove that a given content legitimately was part of his position of enjoyment.p. 616

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição.

The minimum sign of proof lies not in a subjective account of sadness or frustration, but in the objective demonstration of how the system itself assigns social value to the object, through internal markers, visibility, participation requirements, access restrictions and reputational effects.p. 673

PTO sinal mínimo de prova não está em relato subjetivo de tristeza ou frustração, mas na demonstração objetiva de como o próprio sistema atribui valor social ao objeto, por meio de marcadores internos, visibilidade, requisitos de participação, restrições de acesso e efeitos reputacionais.

The use of data linked to the consumer's trajectory, history and digital biography must remain compatible with the purposes disclosed and with the context of the relationship, needs to be intelligible to the data subject, and does not eliminate the rights to confirmation of existence, access, correction, deletion or portability of processed data.p. 542

PTO uso de dados vinculados à trajetória, ao histórico e à biografia digital do consumidor deve permanecer compatível com as finalidades informadas e com o contexto da relação, precisa ser inteligível ao titular, e não elimina as faculdades de confirmação da existência, acesso, correção, eliminação ou portabilidade de dados tratados.

What can you ask for when both staying and leaving are costly?

When the company changes something important in what you use, it must give notice with ample, clear and timely information. The reason is that, for those who depend on the environment, leaving, adapting or contesting is never neutral.

This dependence reinforces the company's duties to be transparent, to let you contest, to make changes checkable and to restore what you lost. The size of these duties follows the power it exercises over the environment.

The design of the environment may take advantage of the cost of leaving, together with hard cancellation paths, manufactured rush and lack of clarity. When this repeats as a method, consumer law may treat the whole as an abusive practice. Then the request can reach the correction of the design itself.

Where it comes from

When the company modifies content, quality, parameters or relevant conditions of enjoyment, it must offer ample, intelligible and timely information, precisely because the consumer is placed in a relationship of practical dependence in which the cost of exit, adaptation or contestation is not neutral Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 1319-1320).p. 658

PTQuando a empresa modifica conteúdo, qualidade, parâmetros ou condições relevantes da fruição, precisa oferecer informação farta, inteligível e em tempo útil, justamente porque o consumidor está inserido numa relação de dependência prática em que o custo de saída, adaptação ou contestação não é neutro (Marques, 2016, p. 1319-1320) .

This dependence on administered recognition is legally relevant because it makes integrity and identity governed variables of enjoyment and requires, on the dogmatic level, reinforced duties of qualified transparency, contestability, auditability and restoration proportional to the type of power exercised by the Lex Plataformae.p. 359

PTEssa dependência de reconhecimento administrado é juridicamente relevante porque torna integridade e identidade variáveis governadas da fruição e exige, no plano dogmático, deveres reforçados de transparência qualificada, contestabilidade, auditabilidade e recomposição proporcionais ao tipo de poder exercido pela Lex Plataformae.

The exploitative purpose must bear on asymmetries of intelligibility and agency that are part of the very arrangement of governed consumption, such as semiotic vulnerability, dependence through captivity, migration costs and loss of investments accumulated in reputation, networks or history.p. 449

PTA finalidade exploratória deve incidir sobre assimetrias de inteligibilidade e agência que integram o próprio arranjo do consumo governado, como vulnerabilidade semiótica, dependência por catividade, custos de migração e perda de investimentos acumulados em reputação, redes ou histórico.

The unlawful act does not arise, therefore, from a singular episode that can be treated as an accident. It emerges when defaults, frictions, manufactured urgencies, hostile cancellation routes, elastic equivalences, sanction regimes or proof arrangements work together as a method of exploitation.p. 666

PTO ilícito não nasce, portanto, de um episódio singular que pode ser tratado como acidente. Ele surge quando defaults, fricções, urgências fabricadas, rotas de cancelamento hostis, equivalências elásticas, regimes de sanção ou arranjos de prova funcionam em conjunto como método de exploração.

Who can you turn to when leaving means losing everything?

The first address is the company itself, which must respond to the request for your data and to the contesting of changes that weighed on what you had.

If it does not respond, the case can go on through Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, paths that the channels page details. Since the cost of leaving is usually the same for all users, the response can also be collective, through a class action or through public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure minimally intelligible procedures, verifiable reasoning, reasonable possibility of contestation and non-arbitrary treatment of the user in the administration of enjoyment.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar procedimentos minimamente inteligíveis, motivação verificável, possibilidade razoável de contestação e tratamento não arbitrário do usuário na administração da fruição.

This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not in a single piece.p. 662

PTIsso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets.p. 662

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma.

How far does the protection of someone who wants to leave go?

Not every intense relationship with a game is a problem, and keeping customers with challenges and rewards is not illegal in itself. What counts is when this raises, in a way that can be shown, the cost of going away.

The rank exists while the account and the service exist, and data protection does not make you the owner of everything the company records. Small frustrations and normal changes to the service are also left out, because the loss needs to be significant, lasting and describable by checkable criteria.

Where it comes from

The legal interest here is not to pathologize every intense relationship with the game, but to recognize that the dynamics of retention and reward may function as an objective mechanism for raising the cost of exit and expanding dependence on the continuity of the bond Torres(Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 20; p. 21; p. 23-24).p. 673

PTO interesse jurídico aqui não é patologizar toda relação intensa com o jogo, mas reconhecer que a dinâmica de retenção e recompensa pode funcionar como mecanismo objetivo de elevação do custo de saída e de ampliação da dependência da continuidade do vínculo (Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 20; p. 21; p. 23-24) .

Thus, the ranked ladder does not operate as a detachable asset. It operates as a grammar of recognition that exists only while the account and the service remain active, which makes leaving, slowing down or falling out of line more costly than they would seem under an ontology of static “product”.p. 355

PTAssim, a escada ranqueada não opera como patrimônio destacável. Ela opera como gramática de reconhecimento que só existe enquanto a conta e o serviço permanecem ativos, o que torna a saída, a desaceleração ou o desalinhamento mais custosos do que pareceriam sob uma ontologia de “produto” estático.

It also presupposes secure management of relevant records and trails. These provisions do not turn the consumer into the absolute owner of everything the platform recordsp. 542

PTTambém pressupõe gestão segura de registros e trilhas relevantes. Esses dispositivos não transformam o consumidor em proprietário absoluto de tudo o que a plataforma registra

For this reason, the categories only operate when the downgrading is significant and persistent, when it substantially alters the level of agency and the consumer's position in the ecosystem, and when it can be described by verifiable criteria of loss in the IOC regime, under the filters of relevance and controllability.p. 467-468

PTPor isso, as categorias só operam quando o rebaixamento é significativo e persistente, quando altera substancialmente o patamar de agência e a posição do consumidor no ecossistema, e quando pode ser descrito por critérios verificáveis de perda no regime do OIC, sob os filtros de relevância e controlabilidade.
