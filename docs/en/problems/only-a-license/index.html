Your rights · By problem

The company says I only have a license. So I didn't buy anything?

You paid, the button said buy, and the contract calls what you received a license, meaning a permission to use that can be withdrawn. The label in the contract does not by itself decide your protection, because Brazilian consumer law also treats things with no physical form as products.

Where it comes from

The Terms of Service describe the same items as “Virtual Content” offered under an access license tied to the account Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). The consequence is that what the interface presents as a “purchase”, the contractual instrument requalifies as conditional and institutionally administered access.p. 401

PTOs Termos de Serviço descrevem os mesmos itens como “Conteúdo Virtual” oferecido sob licença de acesso vinculada à conta (Riot Games, 2024e) . A consequência é que aquilo que a interface apresenta como “compra”, o instrumento contratual requalifica como acesso condicionado e institucionalmente administrado.

The law does not condition the product on corporeality, nor tie it to the image of the tangible thing delivered once and for all.p. 574

PTA lei não condiciona o produto à corporeidade, nem o prende à imagem da coisa tangível entregue de uma vez por todas.

The possible institutional consequence consists in opening the subsequent filters of the regime, preventing the controversy from being neutralized by the claim of mere provision of a service or simple formal licensing.p. 743

PTA consequência institucional possível consiste na abertura dos filtros subsequentes do regime, impedindo que a controvérsia seja neutralizada pela alegação de mera prestação de serviço ou de simples licenciamento formal.

How can you tell whether the word license is being used against you?

It happens when you complain that something disappeared or got worse and the answer is that you never had anything, because it was all a permission that could evaporate. Things with no physical form are also goods in the eyes of the law, and when real money changed hands the word license is not enough to erase what you received.

The contract itself usually helps you. The same terms of use that speak of a license acknowledge that there is content linked to your account, such as currency, skins and other items. And they admit that you may have bought it, earned it or received it as a gift.

What decides your protection is neither the fact that the item is digital nor the name the company gives it. It is how much what you use depends on an environment that the platform maintains, adjusts and can change. That dependence bears on your access, on your ability to stay and on what you are able to understand.

Where it comes from

Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda's doctrine of digital goods fulfills an important function because it resolves, with good density, a problem that cannot be circumvented by contractual rhetoric or by nostalgia for tangibility: techno-digital assets are legal goods and do not disappear from the world of law just because they are incorporeal.p. 565

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais de Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda cumpre função importante porque resolve, com boa densidade, um problema que não pode ser contornado por retórica contratual ou por nostalgia da tangibilidade: ativos tecnodigitais são bens jurídicos e não desaparecem do mundo do Direito só porque são incorpóreos.

When there is actual economic outlay or relevant incorporation of utility into the subject's sphere, the language of the license is not enough to erase the legal density of the asset, nor to neutralize the application of public policy rules Lacerda(Lacerda, 2024, p. 124; p. 193).p. 573

PTQuando há efetivo desembolso econômico ou incorporação relevante de utilidade à esfera do sujeito, a linguagem da licença não basta para apagar a densidade jurídica do ativo, nem para neutralizar a incidência de normas de ordem pública (Lacerda, 2024, p. 124; p. 193) .

Riot Games' Terms of Service expressly recognize a core of “Virtual Content” associated with the account, made up of in-game currency, skins, emotes, accessories and other items, and admit that this formation may occur through purchase, earning, gift or other internal dynamics of the ecosystem.p. 575

PTOs Termos de Serviço da Riot Games reconhecem expressamente um núcleo de “Conteúdo Virtual” associado à conta, composto por moeda do jogo, skins, emotes, acessórios e outros itens, e admitem que essa formação pode ocorrer por compra, ganho, presente ou outras dinâmicas internas do ecossistema.

The clause helps prove the existence of the object. It does not exhaust its regime.p. 575

PTA cláusula ajuda a provar a existência do objeto. Não esgota o seu regime.

Whenever the consumed utility depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and made mutable by private governance, with impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating of the experience, the field of application of the IOC will be established.p. 743

PTSempre que a utilidade consumida depender estruturalmente de ambiente administrado, modulado e mutável por governança privada, com impacto sobre acesso, permanência, inteligibilidade ou atualização da experiência, estará configurado o campo de incidência do OIC.

Which documents show what you actually received?

It is worth keeping the proof of payment and the purchase screen showing the word the button used. A screenshot of the inventory or library with what is in your account also helps. This shows that you made your decision based on the language of a purchase, even if the contract speaks of a license.

Keep the terms of use from the day of purchase as well, because that is where the company acknowledges the content linked to your account and the ways of obtaining it.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

The result is an objective inequality of decoding and agency, because the consumer decides under the grammar of “buying”, but enjoyment takes place under the grammar of the “revocable license” associated with the accountp. 401-402

PTO resultado é uma desigualdade objetiva de decodificação e de agência, porque o consumidor decide sob a gramática do “comprar”, mas a fruição se realiza sob a gramática da “licença revogável” associada à conta

The company expressly defines “Virtual Content” as a set that includes in-game currency, champions, skins, emotes, accessories and other items associated with the accountp. 566

PTA empresa define expressamente “Conteúdo Virtual” como conjunto que inclui moeda do jogo, campeões, skins, emotes, acessórios e outros itens associados à conta

What can you ask for when the answer is that it was all just a license?

Your request can start by asking for the case to be handled under consumer law, with what you bought treated as an immaterial product, that is, a product with no physical form. The service the company provides does not erase this, because it is precisely how the company maintains and updates what it sold.

Once the case is treated as the purchase of a product, you can demand that what was promised keep being delivered over time. Even the account termination clause of a major game states that losing access does not affect any rights you may have under consumer protection laws.

Where it comes from

The resulting legal qualification is that of an immaterial product subject to the regime of Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1, without the service dimension of the relationship ruling out control of enjoyment as the core of the supply.p. 743

PTA qualificação jurídica daí resultante é a de produto imaterial submetido ao regime do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC , sem que a dimensão prestacional do vínculo afaste o controle da fruição como núcleo do fornecimento.

The performance does not disappear, but appears as a mode of sustaining, updating and shaping the object of consumption itself.p. 718

PTA prestação não desaparece, mas comparece como modo de sustentação, atualização e conformação do próprio objeto de consumo.

The immaterial object is also measured by the functional correspondence between promised utility and utility effectively maintained in the course of the relationship.p. 577

PTO objeto imaterial é medido também pela correspondência funcional entre utilidade prometida e utilidade efetivamente mantida no curso da relação.

If your account is terminated you will no longer have access to your account, including the associated data or the Virtual Content (including the Game Currency) (although this does not limit or affect any rights you may have under data protection or consumer protection laws).p. 540

PTSe sua conta for encerrada você não terá mais acesso à sua conta, inclusive aos dados associados ou ao Conteúdo Virtual (inclusive a Moeda do Jogo) (embora isso não limite nem afete nenhum direito que você eventualmente tiver segundo as leis de proteção de dados ou de defesa do consumidor).

Who decides whether the license sets aside your rights?

Not the company alone. The case stays within consumer law, with no need for a new law for the digital world. That is why it follows the same paths as any consumer complaint, starting with the company and moving on, if needed, to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court.

If your question is about inheritance, about what happens to an account after someone dies or about passing the item on, that is a different conversation. These disputes are usually handled under the rules of civil law, which deal with who owns what.

Where it comes from

The construction of this key of qualification remains situated within the discipline of consumption, without purporting to inaugurate an autonomous microsystem for the digital.p. 410

PTA construção dessa chave de qualificação permanece situada no interior da disciplina do consumo, sem pretender inaugurar um microssistema autônomo para o digital.

The questions are who the holder is, whether the asset can be inherited, whether the platform can block it after death, whether contractual clauses can bar disposal inter vivos or causa mortis. These are relevant conflicts, and the civil-law framework is well equipped to face them.p. 568

PTPergunta-se quem é o titular, se o bem pode ser herdado, se a plataforma pode bloqueá-lo após a morte, se cláusulas contratuais podem barrar disposição inter vivos ou causa mortis. Esses são conflitos relevantes e a moldura civilista os enfrenta com bom equipamento.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

How far does the argument against the license go?

The word buy on the button is not illegal in itself, and you do not need to prove you fully own the item to be protected. What is required is a minimum of stability in what you bought.

Defeating the license argument does not settle everything either. It opens the door, but what happens next still depends on what changed, whether the function was preserved and whether the company kept you properly informed.

Not every digital asset falls into this category. First you need to show that what you use truly depends on an environment governed by the platform, and is not just any digital file.

Where it comes from

This does not provide grounds for claiming that the use of the word “buy” is, in itself and in isolation, unlawful.p. 402

PTIsso não serve de fundamento para afirmar que o uso da palavra “comprar” se mostra, por si só e isoladamente, ilícito.

Nor is it necessary to resort to a strong idea of consumer ownership over the digital good in order to demand minimum stability.p. 708

PTTambém não é necessário recorrer a uma ideia forte de propriedade do consumidor sobre o bem digital para exigir estabilidade mínima.

Yet even once this trick is barred, the central problem of the IOC remains: how to control, within an enjoyment that continues to exist, the ordinary mutations that erode its functional and symbolic integrity?p. 573

PTMas, mesmo depois de barrar esse truque, ainda resta o problema central do OIC: como controlar, no interior de uma fruição que continua existindo, as mutações ordinárias que corroem sua integridade funcional e simbólica?

The point, however, is that applying this taxonomy to platform consumer disputes requires a prior methodological step, and this step is verifying that the object at issue is not merely a digital asset generically considered, but an Immaterial Object of Consumption under governed enjoyment.p. 571

PTO ponto, todavia, é que a aplicação dessa taxonomia a controvérsias de consumo em plataforma exige passo metodológico prévio, e esse passo é a verificação de que o objeto em causa não é apenas ativo digital genericamente considerado, mas Objeto Imaterial de Consumo sob fruição governada.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

  1. Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 3. A supplier is any natural or legal person, public or private, national or foreign, as well as entities without legal personality, that carry out activities of production, assembly, creation, construction, transformation, import, export, distribution or commercialization of products or provision of services. § 1. A product is any good, movable or immovable, material or immaterial. § 2. A service is any activity supplied in the consumer market for remuneration, including those of a banking, financial, credit and insurance nature, except those arising from employment relationships.”

    PT“Art. 3° Fornecedor é toda pessoa física ou jurídica, pública ou privada, nacional ou estrangeira, bem como os entes despersonalizados, que desenvolvem atividade de produção, montagem, criação, construção, transformação, importação, exportação, distribuição ou comercialização de produtos ou prestação de serviços. § 1° Produto é qualquer bem, móvel ou imóvel, material ou imaterial. § 2° Serviço é qualquer atividade fornecida no mercado de consumo, mediante remuneração, inclusive as de natureza bancária, financeira, de crédito e securitária, salvo as decorrentes das relações de caráter trabalhista.”
