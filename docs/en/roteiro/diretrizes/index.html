Situations

7.1.3 · p. 748 a 752

Control guidelinesOn the guidelines for controlling architecture and governance

The ten guidelines steer the control of the platform's design and governance. Each one says what to examine and what the examination cannot accept as an answer.

Read in the full text

Where should the examination focus?

The examination does not stop at isolated clauses, terminal acts or one-off events. Its center is the regime of supply, that is, the operating program of the environment, the mechanisms by which enjoyment is administered and the legitimacy filters that make it controllable. The ten guidelines below organize this control, and together they reject both atomized examination and inflamed generalization.

Where it comes from

The center of gravity of the examination must be the regime ofp. 748

PTO ponto de gravidade do exame deve ser o regime do

Taken together, these guidelines offer a criterion of application that rejects both atomized examination and inflamed generalization.p. 752

PTEssas diretrizes, em conjunto, oferecem critério de aplicação que recusa tanto o exame atomizado quanto a generalização inflamada.

Architecture and governance are part of supply

Architecture and governance are part of supply itself and are not a neutral setting. It is through architecture that the platform defines paths, frictions, salience, reversibility and costs of understanding, and it is through governance that it administers permanence, sanction, updating, priority, visibility and contestation. Making this structure invisible reduces the debate to the final acts of an environment that has already predetermined much of the disputed experience.

Where it comes from

The first guideline, therefore, consists in treating architecture and governance not as a neutral setting of the bond, but as constitutive elements of the supply itself.p. 749

PTA primeira diretriz, portanto, consiste em tratar arquitetura e governança não como cenário neutro do vínculo, mas como elementos constitutivos do próprio fornecimento.

It is through governance that the supplier administers permanence, sanction, updating, priority, visibility and mechanisms of contestation.p. 749

PTÉ pela governança que o fornecedor administra permanência, sanção, atualização, prioridade, visibilidade e mecanismos de contestação.

Alterations are judged by auditability and equivalence

Significant alterations, rule changes and reconfigurations with material impact are not judged by the generic discourse of updating, innovation or technical convenience. They are examined through open auditability and functional value equivalence. The question is whether the alteration was made intelligible and whether it preserved the function that justified the acquisition, and not only whether the platform was allowed to make it.

Where it comes from

They must be examined in light of open auditability and functional value equivalence.p. 749

PTDevem ser examinadas à luz da auditabilidade aberta e da equivalência de valor funcional.

The relevant question is not only whether the platform could make the change.p. 749

PTA pergunta relevante não é apenas se a plataforma podia alterar.

Sanctions require reasons and real contestation

Private sanctions may affect access, permanence, visibility, reputation, relational insertion and continuity of enjoyment. That is why they require verifiable reasons, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation. The procedure must be reconstructible, and contestation cannot be an empty ritual. The examination also reaches the procedural conditions the supplier designed for adhesion, enjoyment, proof and resistance.

Where it comes from

They require verifiable reasons, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTExigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

The procedure must be institutionally reconstructable and contestation must not be a mere empty ritual.p. 750

PTÉ necessário que o procedimento seja institucionalmente reconstruível e que a contestação não seja mero ritual vazio.

The case is read along its trajectory

The controversy is reconstructed from the environment in which it arose, and not only from the moment it became visible. Judges and protection bodies consider how the platform organized, from the start, adhesion, understanding, circulation of information, access to records and contestation. The case is seen along its trajectory, and not only in its outcome.

Where it comes from

This means that judges and protection bodies need to consider how the platform organized, from the start, the conditions of adhesion, understanding, circulation of information, access to records and possibility of contestation.p. 750

PTIsso significa que julgadores e órgãos de tutela precisam considerar a forma pela qual a plataforma organizou, desde o início, as condições de adesão, compreensão, circulação de informação, acesso a registros e possibilidade de contestação.

The case, therefore, must be seen in its trajectory and not only in its outcome.p. 750

PTO caso, portanto, precisa ser visto em sua trajetória e não apenas em seu desfecho.

Opacity is not a procedural privilege

Structural opacity should not be treated as an inevitable and neutral feature of digital complexity. It is a factor that intensifies vulnerability and evidentiary asymmetry. It justifies orders to preserve and disclose minimal records, delimitation of expert examinations and dynamic redistribution of the burden whenever the relevant evidence arises and remains under the supplier's technical control.

Opacity, in other words, cannot operate as a procedural privilege self-generated by the governance model itself.p. 750

PTA opacidade, em outras palavras, não pode operar como privilégio processual autogerado pelo próprio modelo de governança.

Where it comes from

It must be recognized as a factor that intensifies vulnerability and evidentiary asymmetry.p. 750

PTDeve ser reconhecida como fator de intensificação da vulnerabilidade e da assimetria probatória.

This fact justifies orders to preserve and disclose minimal records, delimitation of expert examinations and dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof whenever the relevant evidence arises and remains under the supplier's technical control.p. 750

PTEsse dado justifica ordens de preservação e exibição de registros mínimos, delimitação de perícias e redistribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório sempre que a prova relevante nascer e permanecer sob controle técnico do fornecedor.

Neither fragment the case nor turn it into a manifesto

Control avoids both fragmentation into individual lawsuits unable to reach the source of the injury and turning each case into a manifesto against technology. It must distinguish individual inadequacy, correctable without further expansion, from a regime defect, repeatable and embedded in ordinary operation. This distinction calibrates the response.

Neither atomization nor grandiloquence.p. 750

PTNem atomização, nem grandiloquência.

Where it comes from

Control must be specific enough to respond to the concrete conflict and attentive enough to the structure to recognize when this conflict is a localized manifestation of a repeated pattern.p. 750

PTO controle precisa ser suficientemente específico para responder ao conflito concreto e suficientemente atento à estrutura para identificar quando esse conflito é manifestação localizada de um padrão reiterado.

Restore before compensating

In architectural injury, the response favors functional restoration and procedural correction. Whenever possible, restoration of access, reversal or review of sanctions, restoration of functionality, preservation of history, equivalent substitution and adjustment of governance procedures are appropriate.

Where it comes from

In cases of architectural injury, the legal response must favor functional restoration and procedural correction rather than automatically resorting to monetization.p. 751

PTEm hipóteses de lesão arquitetural, a resposta jurídica deve privilegiar recomposição funcional e correção procedimental, em vez de recorrer automaticamente à monetização.

What is required, whenever possible, is restoration of access, reversal or review of sanctions, restoration of functionality, preservation of history, equivalent replacement and adjustment of governance procedures.p. 751

PTO que se exige, sempre que possível, é restauração de acesso, reversão ou revisão de sanções, recomposição de funcionalidade, preservação de histórico, substituição equivalente e ajuste de procedimentos de governança.

A repeated pattern calls for regime correction

When the pattern of injury is repeated, replicable and embedded in the structure, protection is not limited to the single case. The way opens for injunctive relief and regime correction, with procedural duties, architecture adjustments, reinforced auditability, review of sanction flows and intelligible channels of contestation.

Where it comes from

When the pattern of injury proves repeated, replicable and embedded in the structure of the environment, protection should not be limited to the single case.p. 751

PTQuando o padrão de lesão se revelar reiterado, replicável e incorporado à estrutura do ambiente, a tutela não deve se limitar ao caso singular.

This includes procedural duties, architecture adjustments, reinforced auditability requirements, review of sanction flows, imposition of intelligible channels of contestationp. 751

PTIsso inclui deveres procedimentais, ajustes de arquitetura, exigências reforçadas de auditabilidade, revisão de fluxos de sanção, imposição de canais inteligíveis de contestação

The judge need not become an engineer

Control does not require the judge to become the system's engineer. It requires bringing the environment back under verifiable filters of legitimacy. Open auditability, functional equivalence, verifiable reasons, procedural coherence, intelligibility of contestation, preservation of records and reconciliation between exclusivity and protection are enough to make the conflict decidable.

The complexity of the digital does not eliminate Law. It only requires Law to choose more rigorously what it needs to understand in order to control.p. 751

PTA complexidade do digital não elimina o Direito. Apenas exige que ele escolha com mais rigor o que precisa compreender para controlar.

Where it comes from

Control of architecture and governance does not require the judge to become an engineer of the system nor the protection body to have full technical mastery of the ecosystem.p. 751

PTO controle de arquitetura e governança não exige que o julgador se transforme em engenheiro do sistema nem que o órgão de tutela detenha domínio técnico integral do ecossistema.

Supply, proof and remedy are one problem

Supply, proof and remedy cannot be rigidly separated. The offer shapes the perception of loss, governance conditions proof, and the distribution of access and opacity affects the adequacy of the remedy. Separating these planes produces decisions that are organized in form and blind in substance.

Where it comes from

Finally, a tenth guideline requires that institutional examination preserve unity between supply, proof and remedy.p. 751

PTPor fim, uma décima diretriz impõe que o exame institucional preserve unidade entre fornecimento, prova e remédio.

The way the architecture distributes access and opacity influences the adequacy of the remedy.p. 751

PTA forma como a arquitetura distribui acesso e opacidade influencia a adequação do remédio.
