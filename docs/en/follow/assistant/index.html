You trust an artificial intelligence assistant. Who decides what it does for you?

You use an artificial intelligence assistant to study, work, write or decide. It speaks with confidence, and you came to trust it as guidance.

1 What did you sign up for?

You did not sign up for an answer. You signed up for an assistant that guides you, and its value lies precisely in your being able to trust what it says enough to act on it.

That is why the problem is rarely a wrong sentence. The problem is the whole environment that turns what it says into something you trust.

Where it comes from

What is offered is an environment of cognitive orientation.p. 757

PTO que se oferece é ambiente de orientação cognitiva.

The service is valuable not only because it answers, but because it induces the user to treat the answer as a relevant point of orientation.p. 758

PTO serviço vale não apenas porque responde, mas porque induz o usuário a tratar a resposta como ponto relevante de orientação.

2 Is today's assistant the same as yesterday's?

Not always. The company swaps the model that answers, changes usage limits, cuts features and adjusts the assistant's behaviour behind the screen. The name stays the same, and you keep paying for the same plan.

This variation is not an accident of technology. It is the company's choice, and the lack of clarity about it must have a limit.

Where it comes from

The third is the tendency to treat the model's variability as a brute technical fact, rather than as a governed element whose opacity must be legally bearable.p. 760

PTO terceiro é a tendência de tratar a variabilidade do modelo como fato bruto da técnica, e não como elemento governado cuja opacidade precisa ser juridicamente suportável.

3 Why does it sound so sure?

Because it was designed to. The assistant states what it knows and what it makes up with the same firmness, and the way the answer appears makes it look complete and reliable.

When the appearance of certainty goes beyond what the service delivers, the cost of the error falls on you.

Where it comes from

There may be informational noise when the architecture of the answer projects an appearance of certainty, completeness or sufficiency incompatible with the real limits of performance.p. 759

PTPode haver ruído informacional quando a arquitetura da resposta projeta aparência de segurança, completude ou suficiência incompatível com os limites reais da prestação.

4 Does it serve everyone the same way?

Not necessarily. Internal filters decide what the assistant agrees to do and what it refuses. When those filters treat equivalent requests differently, some people get what they ask for and others do not, by criteria nobody sees.

There is no need to prove that anyone meant to favour one side. It is enough that the design produces the difference and that it cannot be checked.

Where it comes from

It is enough that the environment replaces legibility with a performance of confidence, shifting to the user the cost of bearing error, revision, fluctuation or functional loss without adequate institutional trails of reconstruction.p. 759

PTBasta que o ambiente substitua legibilidade por performance de confiança, deslocando para o usuário o custo de suportar erro, revisão, oscilação ou perda funcional sem trilhas institucionais adequadas de reconstrução.

5 Is the error only in the answer?

No. Capability below what was promised, bias and misinformation served with an appearance of certainty arise from the way the service is built. A wrong answer can serve as evidence, but the problem lies in the regime that produced it.

So looking only at the wrong sentence is looking in the wrong place.

Where it comes from

The first is the tendency to reduce the conflict to the sentence produced, ignoring the semiotic architecture that made it reliable or operational.p. 760

PTO primeiro é a tendência de reduzir o conflito à frase emitida, ignorando a arquitetura semiótica que a tornou confiável ou operacional.

6 Can what happened be reconstructed?

Rarely from your side. The history, the version in use, the filters and the limits stay with the company. You only have the screens you saved and your memory.

When the problem affects many people, users themselves can reconstruct how it works from outside, with repeated tests and screenshots. That is what happened in the case below.

Where it comes from

The standard of proof suited to this subtype cannot, therefore, naively reproduce the logic of the isolated event.p. 759

PTO padrão probatório compatível com esse subtipo não pode, por isso, reproduzir ingenuamente a lógica do evento isolado.

7 What can you demand?

You can demand access to the conversation history, a sufficient explanation of the limits that matter, notice before relevant changes and equal treatment for equal requests. Nobody needs to open the assistant's code for that.

When the failure arises from the design and affects all users, the response can be collective and correct how the service works, and not just your case.

Where it comes from

It is not a matter of requiring unrestricted opening of the model, code or internal architecture.p. 759

PTNão se trata de exigir abertura irrestrita de modelo, código ou arquitetura interna.

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Has this happened before?

In October 2026, WhatsApp users reported that Meta AI generated posters with "Flávio 22" and refused equivalent requests with "Lula 13". Newsrooms such as Núcleo, Fast Company Brasil and Tecnoblog repeated the tests and recorded the difference.

Meta acknowledged the failure. It said the filter blocked the candidates' names but did not always recognise a first name followed by the number, that the loophole was fixed on the 14th and that there was no political favouritism. The Superior Electoral Court ordered equal treatment of the two campaigns. What matters here is not intention, which the company denies, but that an invisible filter treated equivalent requests unequally.

In 2025, paying ChatGPT subscribers woke up without the models they used, replaced without notice. After the backlash, OpenAI brought the old model back and promised to give notice before removing models.
