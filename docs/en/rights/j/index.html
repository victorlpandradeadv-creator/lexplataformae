Your rights

Owning the work is not an excuse for everythingOwning the work is not an excuse for everything

When do the rights over the game become a shield?

When the company uses the rights it holds over the game to resist any control over the way it supplies what you bought. This includes copyright, trade secrets, licenses and control over the system itself.

It is worth understanding the role of these rights. Intellectual property does not appear here only as an obstacle coming from outside. It is what helps the company define what can be used, for how long and under what conditions. It protects creation, but it also establishes the company's authority over the way you use what you bought. That is why it is part of the very structure of what is sold.

How should the company use exclusivity?

The platform must use its exclusivity without turning it into a shield to hide everything, worsen what you bought or take away your access without reason. The balance between the company's rights and yours is struck through the social function, under which those rights also exist to serve the interest of society. And it is struck through the limits the Constitution places on whoever exercises private power.

How do you show whether the restriction protects the game or only shields the company?

It helps to show whether the restriction the company invokes is really necessary to protect the game and the work, or whether it is serving to shield the way it governs the environment.

What can you ask for when the company invokes the work?

You can ask for consumer law to keep applying. In practice, that can mean a limited examination of the records that preserves the secrets, and control over the equivalence of what you bought. It can also mean that the company respects the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration. And, if the game is shut down, that the exit comes, as far as possible, with a transition, preservation or transfer of what you had inside it.

Does the company lose its rights over the game?

The intellectual property rights over the game, the art and the code remain protected. Nobody asks the company to give up the work. What is at issue is using this exclusivity as a general veto against consumer protection.
