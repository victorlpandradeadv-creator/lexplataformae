Who decides what you see in an online store or on a social network?Infrastructure and intermediation IOCs

You look for a product in an online store, compare prices, check the reviews and pick the seller who seems most reliable. Or you open a social network and see what is trending, what others liked and what seems to matter that day.

It feels like you are looking at the market or the conversation as they are. But everything reached you already selected, ordered and highlighted by the platform, using criteria you do not control and can almost never check.

Where it comes from

Platforms of this nature do not merely connect. They select, rank, highlight, hide, bring closer, delay, prioritize and reorganize the way in which the reality of the environment becomes perceptible to the user.p. 761

PTPlataformas dessa natureza não apenas conectam. Elas selecionam, hierarquizam, destacam, ocultam, aproximam, retardam, priorizam e reorganizam a forma pela qual a realidade do ambiente se torna perceptível ao usuário.

The market, the offer and even the social relevance of what circulates within it reach the consumer and the user through private filters of visibility, salience and intelligibility.p. 761

PTO mercado, a oferta e até mesmo a relevância social do que circula em seu interior comparecem ao consumidor e ao usuário por meio de filtros privados de visibilidade, saliência e inteligibilidade.

What are you actually using?

It does not matter what was sold in the end or what sector the platform works in. What decides is how it organizes the exchange, the meeting and the comparison inside the environment it runs.

These platforms usually present themselves as a neutral square that merely brings sellers and buyers together, or those who post and those who read. That is true only on the surface.

What you get is a structure of visibility, reputation, curation, intermediation and delivery. Without it, the product or the content would not even have reached you the way it did.

Where it comes from

The incidence of the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption on infrastructure and intermediation platforms is not defined by the final object traded nor by the traditional economic classification of the sector in which the platform operates.p. 760

PTA incidência do regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo sobre plataformas de infraestrutura e intermediação não se define pelo objeto final negociado nem pela classificação econômica tradicional do setor em que a plataforma atua.

In their formal language, they frequently appear as neutral spaces of encounter between seller and buyer, between sender and receiver, between advertiser and audience, between content producers and the public.p. 761

PTEm sua linguagem formal, elas frequentemente aparecem como espaços neutros de encontro entre vendedor e comprador, entre emissor e receptor, entre anunciante e audiência, entre produtores de conteúdo e público.

This description, although partially true on a superficial level, is insufficient to capture the real structure of supply.p. 761

PTEssa descrição, embora parcialmente verdadeira em plano superficial, é insuficiente para captar a estrutura real do fornecimento.

Why is the storefront part of what you get?

You do not consume only a third party's product or the post that shows up on your screen. Before that, you consume the frame that defines what seems most relevant, most reliable, safest, most popular or most worthy of attention.

That is why rankings, reputation, featured spots, recommendations and reach are not screen details. They are part of the platform's usefulness and weigh on what you expect, on the risk you see and on the choice you make.

Where it comes from

They consume, before that, private frames that define what will be seen as more relevant, more reliable, more comparable, safer, more popular or more worthy of attention.p. 761

PTConsomem, antes disso, molduras privadas que definem o que será visto como mais relevante, mais confiável, mais comparável, mais seguro, mais popular ou mais digno de atenção.

Rankings, reputation, featuring, recommendation, prioritization and modulation of reach are not peripheral elements. They are part of the functional utility of the ecosystem and directly influence the formation of expectation, the assessment of risk and the possibility of rational choice.p. 761

PTRankings, reputação, destaque, recomendação, priorização e modulação de alcance não são elementos periféricos. Integram a utilidade funcional do ecossistema e influenciam diretamente a formação da expectativa, a avaliação do risco e a possibilidade de escolha racional.

Where does this show up in your daily life?

In online stores. The terms of use of the large marketplaces show that the platform organizes much more than listings. It handles the rules, visibility, payment, reputation, delivery and dispute resolution.

On social networks. The terms of the large networks show private rules about what stays up, who sees what, what is moderated and how conversation circulates.

Shopping and conversation are different things, but the mechanism is the same. In both cases, the platform governs the place where things appear and prepares the space in which you will decide.

Where it comes from

In both cases, the platform does not merely host listings. It administers criteria of presentation, trust, prioritization and legibility of the market accessible to the consumer.p. 762

PTEm ambos os casos, a plataforma não se limita a hospedar anúncios. Ela administra critérios de apresentação, confiança, priorização e legibilidade do mercado acessível ao consumidor.

Here too the platform does not merely “let appear” content produced by third parties. It administers the way this content enters the user's field of perception and how its relevance is socially stabilized.p. 762

PTTambém aqui a plataforma não apenas “deixa aparecer” conteúdos produzidos por terceiros. Ela administra a forma como esses conteúdos entram no campo de percepção do usuário e como sua relevância é socialmente estabilizada.

In both cases, the platform governs the environment of appearance.p. 762

PTEm ambos os casos, a plataforma governa o ambiente de aparição.

Do you really choose on your own?

You do not choose in front of a transparent market, with every offer side by side. You choose inside a field the platform has already interpreted for you.

Your choice is shaped by how prominently an item is placed, how trustworthy a seller looks, the way reviews are displayed and the difference between what is sponsored and what is not. It is also shaped by the link to payment and delivery and by the order of categories.

In the end, the platform produces the market you are able to see.

Where it comes from

Consumers do not choose in a vacuum, before a transparent and symmetrical universe of offers. They choose within a field the platform has already pre-interpreted.p. 762

PTO consumidor não escolhe no vazio, diante de universo transparente e simétrico de ofertas. Ele escolhe dentro de um campo já pré-interpretado pela plataforma.

The platform therefore produces the perceptible market itself.p. 762

PTA plataforma, portanto, produz o próprio mercado perceptível.

This has a name

Semiotic vulnerability. It is the disadvantage of someone who has to rely on signs that someone else controls. Here it grows because the platform's screen comes to manufacture what is real for you, and what it does not show almost ceases to exist.

Where it comes from

It is a semiotic vulnerability intensified by structural dependence on the interface as producer of the accessible real.p. 763

PTTrata-se de vulnerabilidade semiótica intensificada pela dependência estrutural da interface como produtora do real acessível.

The platform defines what stands out, what is hidden, what seems more trustworthy, what acquires the appearance of consensus, what emerges as comparable and what becomes practically invisible.p. 763

PTA plataforma define o que se destaca, o que se esconde, o que parece mais confiável, o que adquire aparência de consenso, o que surge como comparável e o que se torna praticamente invisível.

What does the platform promise without saying it?

The promise is not only in what the company says out loud, like neutrality, efficiency or convenience. It is also in the implicit promise that you will be able to make sense of the market or the conversation that appears on the screen.

When the criteria are hidden, when there are internal interests you do not see or when the order of things cannot be checked, what you get is hit at its core. You lose the ability to read the environment.

Where it comes from

In environments like these, the supplier's promise lies not only in the explicit discourse of neutrality, efficiency or convenience. It lies also in the implicit promise of intelligibility of the market or of social circulation.p. 763

PTEm ambientes assim, a promessa do fornecedor não está apenas no discurso explícito de neutralidade, eficiência ou conveniência. Está também na promessa implícita de inteligibilidade do mercado ou da circulação social.

When this intelligibility is governed by opaque criteria, non-transparent internal interests or non-auditable patterns of ranking, enjoyment comes to be affected in its very core of legibility.p. 763

PTQuando essa inteligibilidade é governada por critérios opacos, interesses internos não transparentes ou padrões de hierarquização não auditáveis, a fruição passa a ser afetada em seu próprio núcleo de legibilidade.

How do you prove what the shop window hid?

Looking at the product that arrived or the post that vanished is not enough. The whole experience has to be reconstructed. Depending on the case, some points come in here.

  • How the offers were presented.
  • What was a paid result and what was not.
  • The role of reviews and reputation.
  • The link between prominence and delivery.
  • The badges and trust signals on the screen.
  • The context in which the content appeared and the moderation rules.

On a social network, it also matters how the feed, recommendations and reach shaped what you saw. In an online store, what matters is the ranking, featured placement, seller rating and sponsorship. Keep dated screenshots while you still can.

Where it comes from

It is not enough to examine the final product acquired or the individual content removed, prioritized or hidden. It is necessary to reconstruct the architecture of the experience.p. 763

PTNão basta examinar o produto final adquirido ou o conteúdo individual removido, priorizado ou ocultado. É necessário reconstruir a arquitetura da experiência.

This includes, depending on the case, the form of presentation of offers, the distinction between organic and paid results, the role of ratings and reputations, the integration between visibility and logistics, the signs of reliability provided by the interface, the context in which content appears, the relevant moderation criteria and the mechanisms by which the platform induces a perception of market, consensus or priority.p. 763

PTIsso inclui, conforme o caso, a forma de apresentação das ofertas, a distinção entre resultados orgânicos e pagos, o papel de avaliações e reputações, a integração entre visibilidade e logística, os sinais de confiabilidade fornecidos pela interface, o contexto de aparição do conteúdo, os critérios de moderação relevantes e os mecanismos pelos quais a plataforma induz percepção de mercado, de consenso ou de prioridade.

Which traps make you give up too early?

Thinking the screen is just decoration. The way things are shown is part of what the platform supplies.

Thinking the platform is only liable for what it wrote. What it actually does with visibility also counts, and not only what is in the contract or the advertising.

Thinking the problem is only the last step. The failed purchase or the removed post is the end of a story. What led there was the storefront the platform set up.

These traps leave the platform with all the power over your choice, hidden behind an appearance of neutral technique.

Where it comes from

The first is the tendency to treat the interface as mere visual support, rather than as a constitutive part of the supply.p. 763

PTO primeiro é a tendência de tratar a interface como mero suporte visual, e não como parte constitutiva do fornecimento.

The second is the tendency to imagine that the platform would be legally liable only for what it expressly said in a clause or in advertising, as if the effective governance of visibility were legally irrelevant.p. 763

PTO segundo é a tendência de imaginar que a plataforma só responderia juridicamente pelo que dissesse expressamente em cláusula ou publicidade, como se a governança efetiva da visibilidade fosse juridicamente irrelevante.

The third is the tendency to reduce the dispute to the terminal act, such as the failed purchase, the removal of a piece of content or the supposed preference for a given offer, without investigating the private structure of salience that conditioned the user's entire experience.p. 763

PTO terceiro é a tendência de reduzir o litígio ao ato terminal, como a compra frustrada, a remoção de um conteúdo ou a suposta preferência por determinada oferta, sem investigar a estrutura privada de saliência que condicionou a experiência inteira do usuário.

What must the platform guarantee?

No one can demand that the platform open up all its systems. But it has to make the minimum checkable about how it presents, ranks, moderates and rates what appears.

What is asked for is enough clarity for you to understand how the environment organizes your experience. And to understand how the platform's choices weigh on what you expect, on how you compare offers and on your chance to challenge.

Where it comes from

Open auditability requires minimum institutional verifiability of the relevant criteria of presentation, prioritization, moderation, reputation and circulation, without this implying unrestricted opening of internal systems.p. 764

PTA auditabilidade aberta exige verificabilidade institucional mínima sobre os critérios relevantes de apresentação, priorização, moderação, reputação e circulação, sem que isso implique abertura irrestrita de sistemas internos.

What is demanded is sufficient intelligibility to understand how the environment organizes the user's experience and how certain choices of the platform interfere in the formation of expectation, in the comparability between alternatives and in the possibility of contestation.p. 764

PTO que se demanda é inteligibilidade suficiente para compreender como o ambiente organiza a experiência do usuário e como determinadas escolhas da plataforma interferem na formação da expectativa, na comparabilidade entre alternativas e na possibilidade de contestação.

And if the platform changes the rules of the game?

Sometimes the platform changes how it intermediates, the logic of who gets seen, the reputation criteria or the rules of circulation. If the change makes what you legitimately had much worse and nothing makes up for the loss, it may be challenged.

This applies to those who buy and sell in the online store and to those who post and seek reach on the social network.

Where it comes from

Functional equivalence, in turn, applies when changes to the intermediation regime, the visibility logic, the reputational criteria or the circulation rules significantly degrade the utility legitimately incorporated into the relationship, without compatible restoration.p. 764

PTA equivalência funcional, por sua vez, incide quando alterações do regime de intermediação, da lógica de visibilidade, dos critérios reputacionais ou das regras de circulação degradam de modo relevante a utilidade legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo, sem recomposição compatível.

This may occur both in e-commerce and in the social circulation of content, whenever the platform substantially alters the functional role of the infrastructure it offered as an environment of exchange, reach or recognition.p. 764

PTIsso pode ocorrer tanto no comércio eletrônico quanto na circulação social de conteúdo, sempre que a plataforma altere substancialmente o papel funcional da infraestrutura que oferecia como ambiente de troca, alcance ou reconhecimento.

What can you ask of whoever organizes the shop window?

The path is to demand duties about the very way things are organized, and not just to argue about the final product or about what a third party did. It is fair to ask whether it was clear what the platform was doing there and whether sponsored content could be told apart from the rest.

It is also fair to ask whether the reputation shown could be checked and whether hidden criteria got in the way of comparison. And whether the screen itself misled, confused or created an imbalance that consumer law does not accept.

In the most serious cases, when the same company referees the storefront and profits from what happens in it, the issue may reach broader abusive practices and competition among sellers.

Where it comes from

The possible dogmatic consequence in this environment tends to gravitate toward procedural duties of controllability over interface governance.p. 764

PTA consequência dogmática possível, nesse ambiente, tende a gravitar em torno de deveres procedimentais de controlabilidade da governança da interface.

It becomes necessary to examine, through the lens of the IOC, whether there was adequate information about the nature of the intermediation, whether the distinction between organic priority and sponsored priority was materially intelligible, whether the reputation displayed was functionally verifiable, whether the opacity of the criteria compromised relevant comparability and whether the interface itself produced deceptiveness, obscuring or structural asymmetry incompatible with the consumer regime.p. 764

PTTorna-se necessário examinar, em chave de OIC, se houve informação adequada sobre a natureza da intermediação, se a distinção entre prioridade orgânica e prioridade patrocinada foi materialmente inteligível, se a reputação exibida era funcionalmente verificável, se a opacidade dos critérios comprometeu comparabilidade relevante e se a própria interface produziu enganosidade, obscurecimento ou assimetria estrutural incompatível com o regime consumerista.

In more serious cases, especially when the platform acts at once as arbiter of the environment and as an economic agent with a stake in its results, the opacity of the ranking may also intersect with broader abusive practices and carry competition repercussions of its own.p. 764

PTEm hipóteses mais graves, especialmente quando a plataforma atuar simultaneamente como árbitra do ambiente e agente econômico interessado em seus resultados, a opacidade da hierarquização pode também dialogar com práticas abusivas mais amplas e com repercussões concorrenciais próprias.

How do you know if this is your case?

Answer for yourself.

  • Did your choice depend on what the platform highlighted, hid or ranked?
  • Was it unclear what was a paid ad or how reputation was calculated?
  • Did the platform change the visibility or reputation rules without explaining?
  • Are you unable to reconstruct what happened without the company's records?

The more yes answers, the more your case resembles what the law protects here. This does not guarantee the outcome, but it shows where to start.

Where it comes from

The subtype thus shows that the infrastructure and intermediation platform is not a mere neutral corridor through which previously formed wills circulate. It is an agent in the construction of the field in which these wills become possible, perceptible and operable.p. 764

PTO subtipo demonstra, assim, que a plataforma de infraestrutura e intermediação não é mero corredor neutro por onde circulam vontades previamente formadas. Ela é agente de construção do campo em que essas vontades se tornam possíveis, perceptíveis e operacionalizáveis.

Why is intermediation not neutral?

The utility does not end with the good, the content or the advertiser at the end of the chain. It is an infrastructure of visibility, reputation, curation, intermediation and logistics. The platform selects, ranks, highlights, hides and reorganizes the way the environment becomes perceptible. Rankings, reputation, featured placement, recommendation and reach modulation are part of the functional utility. On marketplaces, the consumer chooses within a field the platform has already interpreted. On social networks the same mechanism reappears, because the platform manages how content enters the field of perception.

The platform therefore produces the perceptible market itself.p. 762

PTA plataforma, portanto, produz o próprio mercado perceptível.

Where it comes from

What is offered, in legally relevant terms, is an infrastructure of visibility, reputation, curation, intermediation and logisticsp. 761

PTO que se oferece, em termos juridicamente relevantes, é uma infraestrutura de visibilidade, reputação, curadoria, intermediação e logística

They select, rank, highlight, hide, bring closer, delay, prioritize and reorganize the way the reality of the environment becomes perceptible to the user.p. 761

Rankings, reputation, featured placement, recommendation, prioritization and reach modulation are not peripheral elements.p. 761

PTRankings, reputação, destaque, recomendação, priorização e modulação de alcance não são elementos periféricos.

The consumer chooses within a field already pre-interpreted by the platform.p. 762

What vulnerability does the interface produce?

The asymmetry is not only about how much information there is. It lies in how the relevant field of the decision is constituted. The platform defines what stands out, what is hidden, what seems more trustworthy and what becomes almost invisible. The promise also lies in the implicit intelligibility of the market or of social circulation, and it suffers when opaque or non-auditable criteria govern it.

Where it comes from

The consumer faces an asymmetry in the constitution of the relevant field of the decision.p. 763

PTEnfrenta assimetria de constituição do campo relevante da decisão.

The platform defines what stands out, what is hidden, what seems more trustworthy, what acquires the appearance of consensus, what emerges as comparable and what becomes practically invisible.p. 763

PTA plataforma define o que se destaca, o que se esconde, o que parece mais confiável, o que adquire aparência de consenso, o que surge como comparável e o que se torna praticamente invisível.

It also lies in the implicit promise that the market or social circulation is intelligible.p. 763

PTEstá também na promessa implícita de inteligibilidade do mercado ou da circulação social.

How is the experience reconstructed?

Examining the final product or the removed content is not enough. The presentation of the offers has to be reconstructed, along with the distinction between organic and paid results, reviews and reputations, the integration between visibility and logistics, the signals of trustworthiness and the moderation criteria. On social networks, that means the feed, the recommendation and the reach. On marketplaces, ranking, featured placement, seller rating and sponsorship. The blind spots are treating the interface as visual support, limiting liability to what appears in a clause or in advertising and reducing the dispute to the terminal act.

It is not enough to examine the final product purchased or the individual piece of content removed, prioritized or hidden.p. 763

PTNão basta examinar o produto final adquirido ou o conteúdo individual removido, priorizado ou ocultado.

Depending on the case, this includes the way the offers are presented, the distinction between organic and paid results, the role of reviews and reputations, the integration between visibility and logisticsp. 763

What consequence is possible?

Procedural duties of controllability over interface governance. The examination checks whether there was adequate information about the intermediation, whether the distinction between organic and sponsored priority was intelligible, whether the reputation displayed was verifiable and whether opacity compromised relevant comparability. Open auditability and functional equivalence operate as cross-cutting filters.
