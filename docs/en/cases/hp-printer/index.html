I bought the printer. Who decides which cartridge it takes?

Since 2016, HP printers have been receiving updates that make them reject other brands' cartridges that worked until the day before. The company has been fined, has settled and has apologized, but it kept the system.

The case went around again in November 2026, with some facts mixed up. Here you find what the sources confirm, what is not quite so and what Brazilian law has to say.

What happened?

In September 2016, owners of HP OfficeJet printers in several countries saw the machine reject other brands' cartridges overnight. According to the EFF, the update had been sent in March and only kicked in months later, with a message saying the cartridge was damaged.

HP apologized and offered an optional update to undo the block. But it kept the system, which it calls Dynamic Security, and says it protects print quality, the integrity of the device and its intellectual property.

In Italy, in 2020, the authority in charge of competition and consumer protection fined HP 10 million euros. The boxes did not properly warn about the restriction, and the updates that renewed it arrived without the correct information.

In the United States, a first settlement, announced in 2019, created a 1.5 million dollar fund for owners of some models. The settlement approved in 2025, over a November 2020 update, paid nothing to the consumers affected.

That settlement gave 5 thousand dollars to each of the three named plaintiffs and 725 thousand dollars to the lawyers. In exchange, owners of some LaserJet models got the option to decline the updates that bring the block.

In Europe, consumer associations reached a settlement with HP in 2022, with a fund of up to 1.35 million dollars for buyers in Belgium, Italy, Spain and Portugal. Brazil was in the initial demand, but was left out of the settlement.

In January 2024, HP's CEO told CNBC that the company loses money on the printer and profits from the ink. In his words, a customer who does not buy the brand's supplies is a bad investment, and the goal is to turn printing into a subscription.

What is not quite so?

The fine India imposed on HP in July 2026 was not for blocking cartridges. The Indian competition authority penalized HP India for coordinating, with resellers, rigged bids in public tenders for computers and printing supplies.

Both Indian cases began because HP India itself disclosed the cartel in exchange for a lower fine. It is a serious case, but of another kind, and it says nothing about the cartridge in your printer.

Instant Ink subscription cartridges stop working when you cancel, even with ink inside. But on that plan you pay for the pages you print, and the cartridges come to be returned. The sensitive point there is whether this was said clearly before you signed up.

What weighs more is elsewhere. On a printer you bought, the update changes after the sale what it accepts. And with HP+, a choice made at setup ties the printer to the brand's ink for the rest of its life.

If the printer is mine, what changed in it?

When you buy a printer, you also buy the function of printing with the compatible cartridges you find for sale. If an update sent from afar starts rejecting what worked, the machine stays on your desk, but part of what you bought has left it.

And this is no accident. On the HP+ page, HP itself writes that periodic updates will keep the block effective and stop cartridges that previously worked.

Where it comes from

For this reason, enjoyment is sustained by continuous technical-operational support and, consequently, unilateral changes to parameters of availability, compatibility or maintenance may degrade, reconfigure or extinguish the functional value delivered to the consumerp. 201

PTEm razão disso, a fruição é sustentada por um suporte técnico-operacional contínuo e, por conseguinte, alterações unilaterais em parâmetros de disponibilidade, compatibilidade ou manutenção podem degradar, reconfigurar ou extinguir o valor funcional entregue ao consumidor

An update is not bad just because it exists. The problem starts when it takes away, without clear notice and with no way out, something you used on a device that is still within its expected lifespan.

Where it comes from

What is argued is whether, under the pretext of improvement, the update compromises in a relevant way the use, performance or compatibility of goods still within their expected durability, without adequate information, without effective consent and without mitigating means.p. 655

PTO que se discute é se, sob o pretexto de melhoria, a atualização compromete de modo relevante o uso, o desempenho ou a compatibilidade de bens ainda dentro da durabilidade esperada, sem informação adequada, sem consentimento efetivo e sem vias mitigatórias.

Doesn't security justify the block?

HP says other brands' cartridges can carry viruses into the printer and, through it, into the network. Experts heard by the press considered this risk remote for the great majority of people.

Security is a legitimate interest, but the word alone does not end the conversation. Whoever blocks in its name needs to show the risk in a way that can be checked, and not just announce it.

Where it comes from

What is at stake is the redefinition of the material boundaries of the user's sovereignty over their own equipment.p. 521

PTO que está em jogo é a redefinição das fronteiras materiais de soberania do usuário sobre seu próprio equipamento.

But the discourse of security does not, by itself, neutralize the legal relevance of the architecture adopted.p. 522

PTMas o discurso da segurança não neutraliza, por si só, a relevância jurídica da arquitetura adotada.

There is also a detail that says a lot. Other brands' cartridges that use an original HP chip, under license or reused, kept working. What the printer sorts, then, is the chip, and not the ink.

Where it comes from

For this reason, claims of efficiency, optimization, system balance, operational convenience or need for updating are not enough, by themselves, to neutralize control.p. 733

PTPor isso, alegações de eficiência, otimização, equilíbrio do sistema, conveniência operacional ou necessidade de atualização não bastam, por si, para neutralizar o controle.

And what about HP+, offered at setup?

HP+ shows up when you set up the printer, with free months of ink by subscription and an extra year of warranty. In exchange, the printer starts requiring an HP account, an internet connection and original ink for the rest of its life.

The imbalance lies in time. The benefit ends in months, and the restriction never ends, because once activated HP+ stays embedded in the device. When getting in is easy and getting out is impossible, that setup screen weighs much more than it seems.

Where it comes from

It makes it possible to identify cases in which microfrictions, microlosses, microopacities, discreet shifts of information, asymmetric reversibilities, manufactured urgencies, artificial scarcities, elastic equivalences and selective regimes of difficulty operate in a coordinated way, forming a program of extraction that, seen as a whole, becomes legally relevant.p. 732

PTEle permite identificar hipóteses em que microfricções, microperdas, microopacidades, deslocamentos discretos de informação, reversibilidades assimétricas, urgências fabricadas, escassezes artificiais, equivalências elásticas e regimes seletivos de dificuldade operam de maneira coordenada, formando um programa de extração que, visto em conjunto, se torna juridicamente relevante.

Does this apply in Brazil?

Brazilian law does not forbid a printer from working better with the brand's ink. It requires that you know, before buying, clearly and in Portuguese, what the printer accepts and what it may stop accepting later.

Consumer law also treats as void a change the company makes on its own to the quality of what it sold. And it forbids making one product conditional on buying another, which can happen through the design of the machine, without any written clause.

In Europe, consumers were compensated for the lack of information. Brazilians were in the same demand and were left without a settlement. This does not mean they have no rights, only that the settlement did not reach Brazil.

What can you ask for, if it happened to you?

If the printer stopped accepting a cartridge that worked after an update, it is worth keeping the error message, the date, the model and the cartridge receipt. It also helps to note the version of the printer's internal software, which appears in its settings.

You can ask for the printer to accept again what it accepted, with the previous version of the software or with the option to decline the update, which HP already offers for some models. When that is not possible, replacement, a price reduction or a refund come in.

You do not need to prove how the printer's software works inside. It is enough to show that the cartridge worked and stopped working after the update. The system records are with HP, and it is HP that has to explain what changed.

Where it comes from

In these scenarios, requiring exhaustive proof from the vulnerable party as if the conditions under which the litigation is produced were symmetrical means, in practice, rewarding private control of obscurity.p. 735

PTNesses cenários, exigir prova exaustiva da parte vulnerável como se as condições de produção do litígio fossem simétricas significa, na prática, premiar o controle privado da obscuridade.

The first request goes to HP itself. If it does not solve the problem, Procon, consumidor.gov.br and the Small Claims Court are within your reach, and the page on how to complain shows how to use each one and within what deadline.

How is the case characterized?

The printer is a tangible good and not an immaterial object of consumption. The case shows the same structure of governed enjoyment migrating to the physical good, because the utility purchased comes to depend on authentication, compatibility and updates under the supplier's continuous control.

The legal structure is the same in IOCs: update, rebalancing or rule change are not illegitimate merely because they exist, but may become unlawful when they substantially degrade the functional core of the purchase without verifiable justification and without adequate restoration.p. 655

PTA estrutura jurídica é a mesma em OICs: atualização, reequilíbrio ou mudança de regra não são ilegítimos por existirem, mas podem tornar-se ilícitos quando degradam substancialmente o núcleo funcional da aquisição sem justificativa verificável e sem recomposição adequada.

The test is functional value equivalence. The question is not whether the printer still prints, but whether it still serves, at an equivalent level, the purpose that justified the purchase, which included printing with the compatible cartridges on the market.

Where it comes from

In other words, the legally relevant question is not whether everything remained identical, but whether the object keeps serving, at a functionally equivalent level, the ends that legitimately justified contracting and maintaining the relationshipp. 649

PTEm outras palavras, a pergunta juridicamente relevante não é se tudo permaneceu idêntico, mas se o objeto continua servindo, em patamar funcionalmente equivalente, aos fins que legitimamente justificaram a contratação e a manutenção da relação

As Bruno Miragem notes, unilateral alteration is already sensitive in the consumer regime and becomes more serious when it reduces the consumer's interest in the performance. The remote rejection of cartridges that worked is that aggravation.

Where it comes from

Unilateral alteration is already, by itself, legally sensitive in the CDC; and this sensitivity is aggravated when the change is not neutral, but results in a material reduction or impairment of the consumer's interest in the performancep. 651-652

PTA alteração unilateral já é, por si, juridicamente sensível no CDC; e essa sensibilidade se agrava quando a mudança não é neutra, mas resulta em diminuição ou comprometimento material do interesse do consumidor com a prestação

The answer goes through the quality defect that makes the product unfit for its intended purpose, under art. 18 of the CDC1, and through the nullity of the clause that authorizes unilateral modification of content or quality, under art. 51, XIII, of the CDC2. The omission in the offer and on the packaging runs into the duty of information of art. 31 of the CDC3.

Is there tying without a declared obligation?

Tying does not need a clause. When the technical design leaves the brand-licensed supply as the only practical way out, the conditioning is done through the path, and not by written obligation. It is functional tying, which comes close to the practice prohibited by art. 39, I, of the CDC4.

Where it comes from

Abusive teleology appears forcefully when one observes that the system not only bars the direct purchase of the main good, but turns the acquisition into a functional tying sale with a set of items and internal currencies that, in most consumption trajectories, do not correspond to the player's interest.p. 450

PTA teleologia abusiva aparece com força quando se observa que o sistema não apenas interdita a compra direta do bem principal, mas converte a aquisição em venda casada funcional com um conjunto de itens e moedas internas que, na maior parte das trajetórias de consumo, não correspondem ao interesse do jogador.

The point is not to forbid a preference for original ink, but to prevent the consumer's choice from being replaced by a block. The fact that third-party cartridges with an original HP chip keep working indicates that the criterion for rejection is control of the aftermarket, and not the quality of the ink.

Do intellectual property and security authorize the block?

HP's CEO justified the block as protection of intellectual property. Exclusivity is legitimate on its own plane, but it does not immunize the way it is exercised within the consumer relationship.

Intellectual property protects the ecosystem, but it does not absolve its governance.p. 739

PTA propriedade intelectual protege o ecossistema, mas não absolve sua governança.

Where it comes from

The protection of intellectual property remains intact on its own level, but this does not authorize its invocation to work as a general clause of immunization against control of adequacy, transparency, loyalty, functional equivalence and protection of enjoyment.p. 737

PTA proteção da propriedade intelectual permanece íntegra em seu plano próprio, mas isso não autoriza que sua invocação funcione como cláusula geral de imunização contra controle de adequação, transparência, lealdade, equivalência funcional e tutela da fruição.

The same goes for security. The technical claim must be verifiable. Open auditability does not require HP to reveal its code, but it requires minimal intelligibility about what interferes with enjoyment, including the criterion that separates a dangerous cartridge from a merely unlicensed one.

Where it comes from

Open auditability is fully compatible with intellectual property because it does not require full disclosure of business secrets or total exposure of the technological architecture, but only minimal institutional intelligibility about what interferes in a legally relevant way with enjoyment.p. 738

PTA auditabilidade aberta é plenamente compatível com a propriedade intelectual porque não exige revelação integral de segredos empresariais ou exposição total da arquitetura tecnológica, mas apenas inteligibilidade institucional mínima sobre aquilo que interfere de modo juridicamente relevante na fruição.

What is the adequate response?

Because the problem lies in the operating program, and not in an episode, the response does not end with compensating whoever complained. It reaches regime correction, with the option to decline the update, the reversal of the block and information in the offer, through the specific relief of art. 84 of the CDC5.

If the abusiveness lies in the program, it is not enough to treat its isolated effects.p. 733

PTSe a abusividade está no programa, não basta tratar seus efeitos isolados.

The American settlement approved in 2025 shows the opposite risk. It corrected part of the regime, with the option to decline updates on some models, and left without restoration those who had already lost the function. The collective dimension of the case allows both responses to go together through collective redress.

Does the business model change the measure of the duties?

It does. Under the autometric principle, the extent of the duties is measured by the supplier's own choices. HP chose to sell the printer at a loss, according to its CEO, to recover the margin on supplies and to retain remote control over compatibility.

Whoever retains that control answers, in the same measure, for prior information, for the stability of the function sold and for restoration when it changes it. Selling the device cheap and profiting on refills is a legitimate choice, and its counterpart is read in the conditions the supplier itself created.

What does this case test, and what does it not?

The case is not one of an immaterial object of consumption. The printer is a tangible thing, and the reading uses the IOC regime by structural analogy. The 2026 text itself admits this analogy when it takes firmware updates as an example of the same problem of controlling mutability (p. 655).

The facts come from decisions, settlements and HP's official pages where they exist, and from the specialized press for the rest. The statements of HP's CEO are those the press transcribed from the January 2024 CNBC interview.

As of 25 November 2026, no specific Brazilian decision on Dynamic Security was found. The offer of Instant Ink and HP+ in Brazil was not confirmed. The printer messages in Portuguese, reported by the Brazilian press, show that the block reached devices used in Brazil.

The commentary characterizes the structure and does not state that HP committed an unlawful act in Brazil. That would depend on evidence about the Brazilian offer, the packaging, the notices and the updates actually sent to devices in Brazil.
