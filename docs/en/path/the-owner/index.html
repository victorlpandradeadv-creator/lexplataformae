Path · Part 3 of 11

Who owns the place where your skin has value?

The company that set up the store is the same one that controls the game, the rules, the updates and what other players see. The skin stays in your account, and on paper it is yours, but it is the platform that decides how useful it is, how much it shines and how long it stays worth anything.

This changes what it costs to be there. Staying does not cost only the next payment, because it also costs attention, adaptation, loss of equivalence when things change and a dependence that never ends. And the company does not profit only at the moment of purchase. It profits by managing the place every day, with invented rarity, update cycles and changes of meaning that push you into another round of spending.

Where it comes from

The platform operates as an infrastructure landlord: it controls the environment, the rules, the updates and the visibility of what acquires value there.p. 146

PTA plataforma opera como senhorio de infraestrutura: controla o ambiente, as regras, as atualizações e a visibilidade do que ali adquire valor.

“Cloud capital” appears as dominion over access, reputation and permanence, so that the consumer, although preserving formal ownership over items linked to the account, remains subject to the unilateral power to modulate utility, prestige and conditions of enjoyment. The cost is expressed not only in a new payment, but also in attention, adaptation, loss of equivalence and continuous dependence on the administered territory.p. 146

PTO “capital na nuvem” aparece como domínio sobre acesso, reputação e permanência, de modo que o consumidor, embora preserve titularidade formal sobre itens vinculados à conta, permanece submetido ao poder unilateral de modular utilidade, prestígio e condições de fruição. O custo não se expressa apenas em novo pagamento, mas também em atenção, adaptação, perda de equivalência e dependência contínua do território administrado.

Rent is extracted not only at the act of purchase, but in the continuous management of the digital territory, where artificial scarcity, update cycles and reconfiguration of signs reorganize desires and push the subject into new rounds of spending and recomposition of status.p. 146

PTA renda não se extrai apenas no ato de compra, mas na gestão contínua do território digital, onde escassez artificial, ciclos de atualização e reconfiguração de signos reorganizam desejos e empurram o sujeito para novas rodadas de gasto e recomposição de status.

Political economyTechnofeudalism

In feudal times there were lords, who owned the land, and serfs, who lived and worked on it under the lord's rules. Some say that today the land has become the cloud. The owners of the servers, the platforms and the algorithms play the part of the new lords, and those who use these places live under their rules.

You do not stop consuming because of this. The point is that, each time you take part, you help grow the same power that governs you. That is why people speak of cloud serfs. And, in the case of the game, the fief is not even a figure of speech, because it has a name and an address, which is your account.

Where it comes from

The author argues that the capitalist system would be on the way to being replaced by what he calls “technofeudalism”, in which capital stops presenting itself primarily as material property and comes to concentrate “in the cloud”, that is, in digital assets, online platforms and algorithms.p. 142

PTO autor argumenta que o sistema capitalista estaria em vias de ser substituído pelo que denomina “tecnofeudalismo”, no qual o capital deixa de se apresentar prioritariamente como propriedade material e passa a concentrar-se “na nuvem”, isto é, em ativos digitais, plataformas online e algoritmos.

The author reintroduces a feudal grammar to describe the present, in which the owners of cloud capital appear as new feudal lords, while the rest return to the condition of serfs.p. 151

PTO autor reintroduz uma gramática feudal para descrever o presente, na qual os proprietários do capital na nuvem figuram como novos senhores feudais, enquanto os demais retornam à condição de servos.

Hence the force of the feudal image: consumers and users become “cloud serfs” not because they stop consuming, but because their participation comes to reproduce the very capital that governs them.p. 146

PTDaí a força da imagem feudal: consumidores e usuários tornam-se “servos da nuvem” não porque deixam de consumir, mas porque sua participação passa a reproduzir o próprio capital que os governa.

The fief is not a metaphor for the digital environment. It is an account.p. 153

PTO feudo não é uma metáfora do ambiente digital. Ele é uma conta.

Why is the skin not like a refrigerator?

An ordinary purchase is different. When you buy a refrigerator, it goes to your home and from then on exists out of the seller's reach, at least for as long as a refrigerator can be expected to last. The store profits once and that is it.

The skin is different, because it only works inside a place you do not control. It goes through updates, balance adjustments, the end of events, catalog changes, changes in the game's currency and new policies. That is why the platform does not profit only from delivering the skin. It also profits by controlling access and time, that is, by charging for passage, for staying and for the predictability of a place that it governs itself.

Where it comes from

In the industrial economy, the consumer pays to acquire a good whose existence, once purchased, tends to stabilize out of the supplier's reach, at least within reasonable expectations of durability and of the discipline of defects.p. 210

PTNa economia industrial, o consumidor paga para adquirir um bem cuja existência, uma vez comprada, tende a se estabilizar fora do alcance do fornecedor, ao menos dentro de expectativas razoáveis de durabilidade e de disciplina dos vícios.

The consumer does not buy a “thing” and take it with them; they enter an access regime in which the object remains functional only within the infrastructure they do not control, crossed by updates, balancing, closing of events, catalog rotation, reconfigurations of internal currency and policy changesp. 210

PTO consumidor não compra uma “coisa” e a leva consigo; ele ingressa em um regime de acesso em que o objeto se mantém funcional apenas dentro da infraestrutura que não controla, atravessado por atualizações, balanceamentos, encerramentos de eventos, rotatividade de catálogo, reconfigurações de moeda interna e mudanças de política

The consequence is that the platform stops monetizing only the delivery of a good and comes to monetize control of access and time.p. 209

PTA consequência é que a plataforma deixa de monetizar apenas a entrega de um bem e passa a monetizar o controle do acesso e do tempo.

It does not sell a “thing”, it sells passage, permanence and predictability in an environment it governs itselfp. 209

PTEla não vende uma “coisa”, vende passagem, permanência e previsibilidade em um ambiente por ela mesma governado

Political economyToll economy

Some earn less from what they deliver and more because everyone is forced to go through their turnstile. The services may even be useful, but the profit comes mainly from the point of passage that nobody can avoid.

In the game, this toll is not charged just once. The platform charges for entry, charges for the hurry of anyone who wants to get there first, charges for convenience, charges for what sets you apart from others and charges for the return of an item that had gone away. And, above all, it charges for protection against a risk it created itself, such as the fear of missing the next window.

Where it comes from

Rentiers earn not so much from the services they provide as from everyone's obligation to pass through their turnstiles.p. 185

PTOs rentistas ganham não tanto pelos serviços que prestam, como pela obrigação de todos passarem pelas suas catracas.

Many services are useful, or even necessary, but generate disproportionate profits relative to the contributionp. 185

PTMuitos serviços são úteis, ou até necessários, mas geram lucros desproporcionais relativamente ao aporte

The platform monetizes access in layers: it charges for entry, charges for acceleration, charges for convenience, charges for symbolic differentiation, charges for return and, above all, charges for mitigating the risk it fabricates itself.p. 210

PTA plataforma monetiza o acesso em camadas: cobra pelo ingresso, cobra pela aceleração, cobra pela conveniência, cobra pela diferenciação simbólica, cobra pelo retorno e, sobretudo, cobra pela mitigação do risco que ela mesma fabrica.

The toll does not present itself only as an entry price. It reappears as a repeated charge for permanence, organized around time, attention and the predictability of cycles.p. 210

PTO pedágio não se apresenta apenas como preço de entrada. Ele reaparece como cobrança reiterada pela permanência, organizada em torno do tempo, da atenção e da previsibilidade de ciclos.

Who decides what the game's currency is worth?

The game's currency, the one you buy with real money and then trade for skins, shows the same power. The terms of use admit that the company may, at times, change what this currency buys, including asking for more coins for the same skin. The same company sets the currency, the catalog and the price, and that is why it can gradually raise prices inside the game without facing the competition of an open market.

The same contract goes a step further and says that the data stored on the company's servers, including the content of your account, has no value at all. The effect is direct. If on paper your collection is worth nothing, inflation inside the game looks natural, and not like a loss you suffered. And, as if that were not enough, the contract tries to limit the company's liability to what you paid in the twelve months before the problem, precisely in a place where value comes from years of accumulation.

Where it comes from

The terms admit that, “at times”, the company may alter the purchasing power of the Game Currency, including increasing the number of coins needed to acquire virtual products such as skins, and place this movement under the generic promise of notice and gradualness Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e).p. 176

PTO termo admite que, “às vezes”, a empresa pode alterar o poder de compra da Moeda do Jogo, inclusive aumentando o número de moedas necessárias para adquirir produtos virtuais como skins, e coloca esse movimento sob a promessa genérica de aviso e de progressividade (Riot Games, 2024e) .

By defining the catalog, modulating scarcity, managing windows of opportunity and operating internal currencies, bundles and conversions, the supplier comes to calibrate the general level of prices charged, gradually raising price references and shifting anchors of acceptability without facing the discipline typical of markets with open supply and demandp. 176

PTAo definir o catálogo, modular escassez, gerir janelas de oportunidade e operar moedas internas, pacotes e conversões, o fornecedor passa a calibrar o nível geral de valores praticados, elevando gradualmente referências de preço e deslocando ancoragens de aceitabilidade sem enfrentar a disciplina típica de mercados com oferta e demanda abertas

The contract expressly denies any value attributed to data residing on servers operated or controlled by Riot, including Game Content and Virtual Content associated with the account.p. 176

PTO contrato nega expressamente qualquer valor atribuído a dados residentes nos servidores operados ou controlados pela Riot, incluindo Conteúdo do Jogo e Conteúdo Virtual associado à conta.

If the collection is legally described as having no value, internal inflation and the loss of purchasing power come to be treated as a natural variation of the fief, not as deterioration of an economic position relevant to the consumerp. 176-177

PTSe o acervo é juridicamente descrito como não tendo valor, a inflação interna e a perda do poder de compra passam a ser tratadas como variação natural do feudo, não como deterioração de uma posição econômica relevante para o consumidor

The limitation clause provides that total liability may be limited to the amounts paid by the user in the 12 months prior to the cause arisingp. 177

PTA cláusula de limitação prevê que a responsabilidade total pode ser limitada aos valores pagos pelo usuário nos 12 meses anteriores ao surgimento da causa

The platform reserves the power to govern value, currency and access within the territory, but tries to downgrade the civil response for losses to a calculation that ignores precisely what makes the fief efficient, which is prolonged accumulation, dependence and exit costp. 177

PTA plataforma reserva o poder de governar valor, moeda e acesso dentro do território, mas tenta rebaixar a resposta civil por perdas a um cálculo que ignora justamente o que torna o feudo eficiente, que é o acúmulo prolongado, a dependência e o custo de saída

How do small losses become a big bill?

A dripping faucet works like this. Each drop seems like nothing, but at the end of the month the bill arrives. Many platforms do not live only on large, visible charges. They also live on small events, small decisions and small losses, spread across millions of people and almost invisible one by one.

Added up, they weigh a lot. But each one stays too small to be worth a fight, and that is where the trick lies. The profit comes from repetition at scale, always below the point at which someone would bother to complain or to punish.

Where it comes from

The engine of many digital ecosystems is not only overt charging, but drainage through microevents, microdecisions and microlosses, distributed at scale and diluted to the point of becoming almost invisible, although cumulatively relevant.p. 26

PTO motor de muitos ecossistemas digitais não é apenas a cobrança ostensiva, mas a drenagem por microeventos, microdecisões e microperdas, distribuídas em escala e diluídas a ponto de se tornarem quase invisíveis, embora cumulativamente relevantes.

[...] the boundary between lawful and unlawful stops functioning as an effective prohibition and comes to operate as an optimization parameter of the business model itself, in which profit is realized not by a high margin on few units, but by scale and repetition below the punishable thresholdp. 26

PT[...] a fronteira entre lícito e ilícito deixa de funcionar como proibição eficaz e passa a operar como parâmetro de otimização do próprio modelo de negócio, no qual o lucro se realiza não por margem alta em poucas unidades, mas por escala e repetição abaixo do limiar punível

[...] but rather pulverizes and outsources them, converting costs, frictions and setbacks into noise that is acceptable per unit and, therefore, hard to contest through individual perceptionp. 26

PT[...] mas sim os pulveriza e os terceiriza, convertendo custos, fricções e reveses em um ruído aceitável por unidade e, por isso, difícil de contestar por percepção individual

Who decides when a skin becomes rare?

How does something that can be copied for free become rare? A digital skin could be sold to everyone, forever, without running out. If it disappears, comes back only sometimes or becomes a last chance, that is not the nature of the object. It is the company's choice.

The platform creates sales windows, store rotations, rarity tiers, seasonal events, passes, countdowns and limited bundles. Then it turns this arrangement into a story, with words like rare, exclusive, last chance, collectible and special edition. That is how something that was not scarce becomes scarce, by design.

Where it comes from

In digital objects, the marginal cost of reproduction tends to fall drastically and, in theory, availability could expand without requiring productive capacity comparable to the factory, logistics and extraction of inputs.p. 209

PTEm objetos digitais, o custo marginal de reprodução tende a cair drasticamente e, em tese, a disponibilidade poderia expandir-se sem exigir capacidade produtiva comparável à fábrica, à logística e à extração de insumos.

For this reason, when a persistent regime of unavailability, exclusivity and last chance is observed in these markets, what one faces is not a natural condition of the object, but a deliberate institutional choice, so that scarcity appears not as a fact, but as a projectp. 209

PTPor isso, quando se observa nesses mercados um regime persistente de indisponibilidade, exclusividade e última chance, não se está diante de condição natural do objeto, mas de escolha institucional deliberada, de modo que a escassez não aparece como fato, mas como projeto

The platform creates windows, rotations, tiers, seasonal events, passes, countdowns, limited bundles and conditioned acquisition mechanisms. It then converts this arrangement into narrative, speaking of “rarity”, “exclusive”, “last chance”, “collectible” and “special edition”.p. 209

PTA plataforma cria janelas, rotações, tiers, eventos sazonais, passes, contagens regressivas, pacotes limitados e mecanismos de aquisição condicionada. Em seguida, converte esse arranjo em narrativa, falando em “raridade”, “exclusivo”, “última chance”, “colecionável” e “edição especial”.

The non-scarce is made scarce by designp. 209

PTO não-escasso é transformado em escasso por desenho

And when the exclusive skin comes back cheaper?

A skin is sold at a high price because it is exclusive, and exclusivity is part of the price. Months later, the same skin comes back in an alternative store, in a special currency or in an aggressive promotion, for much less. And nobody had told you, clearly, at the time of purchase, that the exclusivity had an expiry date.

In that case, the value of what you received was retroactively reshaped, and your legitimate expectation was frustrated. This can be treated as misleading advertising by omission and as a breach of the offer. The response can include refunding what was paid, reducing the price or compensating with something that serves the same function, plus losses and damages if there is further harm. This is not mere discomfort on your part. It is a loss that Brazilian consumer law knows how to handle.

Where it comes from

If, after a period, the same item reappears in an alternative store, in a special currency, in an aggressive promotion or at a significantly lower price, and if the temporal limitation of the exclusivity was not adequately and comprehensibly disclosed at the time of acquisition, there is a retroactive reconfiguration of the value delivered, with frustration of legitimate expectation and violation of transparency.p. 214

PTSe, passado um período, o mesmo item reaparece em loja alternativa, em moeda especial, em promoção agressiva ou por preço significativamente inferior, e se a limitação temporal da exclusividade não foi informada de modo adequado e compreensível no momento da aquisição, ocorre reconfiguração retroativa do valor entregue, com frustração de expectativa legítima e violação de transparência.

Scarcity, here, may operate as a device for capturing consent under informational inadequacy, with potential classification as misleading advertising by omission and breach of the offerp. 214

PTA escassez, aqui, pode operar como expediente de captação de consentimento sob inadequação informativa, com potencial enquadramento como publicidade enganosa por omissão e descumprimento da oferta

The legal consequence does not end with subjective discomfort, since the subsequent alteration of the attribute that justified the price may demand material restoration through the typical channels of consumer law, by restitution, price reduction or equivalent functional compensation, and may also open room for losses and damages when additional harm arising from the positional deception is demonstrated.p. 214

PTA consequência jurídica não se esgota em desconforto subjetivo, pois a alteração posterior do atributo que justificou o preço pode demandar recomposição material por vias típicas do consumo, por restituição, abatimento ou compensação funcional equivalente, e pode ainda abrir espaço para perdas e danos quando demonstrado prejuízo adicional decorrente do engano posicional.

And what risk do you need to protect yourself from? Not the risk of the skin breaking, like a defective fridge. It is the risk of falling behind, of not belonging, of losing status, of seeing the skin you wanted go out of circulation and of seeing the image you built in there lose value.

This risk is not natural, because the platform calibrates it. It organizes time into seasons, goals and missions, rotates the catalog, creates progression systems that demand hours of repetition and applies automatic punishments. Missions show the mechanism clearly, because they work as a simple trade where you do something and get something, and they turn your time into currency without ever calling it work. Nobody forces you to buy, and yet the pressure is there, made of dependence and fear of missing out. Consumption becomes anxiety management.

Where it comes from

In environments of continuous enjoyment, the most effective risk is not the risk of the product “not working”, which would correspond to a classic grammar of quality defect by inadequacy.p. 211

PTEm ambientes de fruição contínua, o risco mais eficaz não é o risco do produto “não funcionar”, que corresponderia a uma gramática clássica de vício de qualidade por inadequação.

The decisive risk is the risk of loss of opportunity, the risk of falling behind, the risk of not belonging, the risk of losing status, the risk of seeing the desired object go out of circulation and the risk of the symbolic identity built in the ecosystem suffering depreciation. This risk is not natural.p. 211

PTO risco decisivo é o risco de perda de oportunidade, o risco de ficar para trás, o risco de não pertencer, o risco de perder status, o risco de ver o objeto desejado sair de circulação e o risco de a identidade simbólica construída no ecossistema sofrer depreciação. Esse risco não é natural.

It is calibrated by the platform through a temporal architecture, with seasons, splits, goals and missions. It is also calibrated by an architecture of supply, with rotation, scheduled exclusions and variable catalogs. Added to this are an architecture of reward, with progression systems and grind, and a sanctioning architecture, with bans, restrictions, automated penalties and opaque policies.p. 211

PTEle é calibrado pela plataforma por meio de uma arquitetura temporal, com temporadas, splits, metas e missões. Também é calibrado por uma arquitetura de oferta, com rotatividade, exclusões programadas e catálogos variáveis. Soma-se uma arquitetura de recompensa, com sistemas de progressão e grind, e uma arquitetura sancionatória, com banimentos, restrições, penalidades automatizadas e políticas opacas.

In terms of political economy, it is the typical gesture of turning time into currency without ever calling it workp. 211

PTEm termos de economia política, é o gesto típico de transformar tempo em moeda sem jamais chamá-lo de trabalho

Coercion appears neither as violence, nor as direct prohibition, nor as an explicit imposition to buyp. 210

PTA coerção não aparece como violência, nem como proibição direta, nem como imposição explícita de compra

Consumption, in this register, becomes anxiety management.p. 210

PTO consumo, nesse registro, vira administração de ansiedade.

Architectural teleologyArchitectural teleology

Teleology means purpose, what a thing is for. Here, the word says that none of this is a marketing accident. These are design choices that point in one direction, which is to extract more value from whoever is there, and not just to efficiently organize something that was naturally limited.

This purpose is not hidden in a secret document. It shows in the way the screen organizes what you see, what you calculate and the risk you feel. And it becomes important to the law when it stops being just commercial strategy and starts to hide or frustrate the correspondence between what was promised and what was delivered.

Where it comes from

[...] the asymmetry is not limited to the supplier's “better knowledge”. It materializes in the supplier's very capacity to define what exists, for how long it exists, under what conditions it exists and under which narratives it exists [...] For this reason, artificial scarcity must be understood as part of an architectural teleology. It is part of a set of design choices oriented toward extraction and not toward the mere efficient organization of a naturally limited resourcep. 214

PT[...] a assimetria não se limita ao “melhor conhecimento” do fornecedor. Ela se materializa na própria capacidade do fornecedor de definir o que existe, por quanto tempo existe, em que condições existe e sob quais narrativas existe [...] Por isso, a escassez artificial deve ser compreendida como parte de uma teleologia arquitetural. Ela integra um conjunto de escolhas de design orientadas à extração e não à simples organização eficiente de um recurso naturalmente limitado

It is part of a set of design choices oriented toward extraction and not toward the mere efficient organization of a naturally limited resource, and it is at this threshold that it ceases to be merely commercial strategy and becomes a legally relevant category for qualifying the deviation of purpose when the equivalence between promise and delivery is made opaque or deliberately frustratedp. 214

PTEla integra um conjunto de escolhas de design orientadas à extração e não à simples organização eficiente de um recurso naturalmente limitado, e é nesse limiar que deixa de ser apenas estratégia comercial para se tornar categoria juridicamente relevante à qualificação do desvio de finalidade quando a equivalência entre promessa e entrega é tornada opaca ou deliberadamente frustrada

Architectural teleology manifests itself not only in clauses and policies, but in the way the interface organizes visibility, calculation and risk.p. 214

PTA teleologia arquitetural não se manifesta apenas em cláusulas e políticas, mas na forma como a interface organiza visibilidade, cálculo e risco.

Political economyInvisible labor

And there is the work nobody calls work. You test new versions of the game before they come out, point out bugs, send suggestions, post, comment, make memes, write guides, record videos and stream. All of this gives the game visibility, explains the catalog to others and keeps desire alive.

For the company, this is worth a lot, because it lowers the cost of testing the game and works as advertising. But the contract treats your ideas as something the company can use freely, without paying anything. And whoever does this as a content creator looks like a small independent entrepreneur, when in practice they work as the public face of the business itself, under rules the company can change or withdraw. It is production that appears as participation.

Where it comes from

Here the user is not only measured but mobilized as a distributed workforce that reduces quality control costs, widens the bug detection surface and feeds the supplier's organizational intelligence, producing a productivity that appears not as work, but as participationp. 218

PTO usuário, aqui, não é só medido, ele é mobilizado como força de trabalho distribuída que reduz custo de controle de qualidade, amplia a superfície de detecção de bugs e alimenta a inteligência organizacional do fornecedor, produzindo uma produtividade que não aparece como trabalho, mas como participação

In the Policy on unsolicited idea submissions, the supplier declares that it “values” feedback, but simultaneously structures the regime of appropriation, by reserving for itself the free use of ideas, suggestions and creative materials sent by the user, without this generating economic consideration, compensation or a duty of acknowledgmentp. 218

PTNa Política de envio de ideias não solicitadas, o fornecedor declara “valorizar” feedback, mas simultaneamente estrutura o regime de apropriação, ao reservar para si o uso livre de ideias, sugestões e materiais criativos enviados pelo usuário, sem que isso gere contrapartida econômica, indenização ou dever de reconhecimento

generated by users themselves, such as posts, comments, streams, memes, guides and reviews, which becomes an engine of visibility and recommendation and works, in practice, as invisible labor of production and marketing of the IOCp. 219

PTgerado pelo próprio usuário, como posts, comentários, transmissões, memes, guias e avaliações, que se converte em motor de visibilidade e recomendação e funciona, na prática, como trabalho invisível de produção e de marketing do OIC

Among the most conspicuous forms of this invisible labor is the work of content creators, streamers and influencers who orbit the ecosystem as if they were autonomous “microentrepreneurs”, when, in practice, they function as an intense public face of the incorporated exterior.p. 219

PTEntre as formas mais ostensivas desse trabalho invisível está o trabalho dos criadores de conteúdo, streamers e influenciadores que orbitam o ecossistema como se fossem “microempreendedores” autônomos, quando, na prática, funcionam como uma face pública intensa do exterior incorporado.

the very possibility of monetizing content depend on private, revocable and selective rulesp. 219

PTa própria possibilidade de monetizar conteúdo dependem de regras privadas, revogáveis e seletivas

Political economyDigital lumpenproletariat

Whoever produces value this way ends up in a strange place. They play a large part in production, but outside any category of work, employment or provision of services. They have no contract and no wage, and nobody recognizes them as someone who works.

At the same time, your progress, your status, your reputation and even your access depend on someone else's decisions, like those of a worker who lives under conditions set by the boss. The word lumpenproletariat names those who work on the margins, without the rights of those who work. Applied here, it describes someone who is a consumer and, at the same time, labor, without the law seeing that second part.

Where it comes from

The consumer-user of platforms is formally outside the wage relation and outside the classic categories of work that sustained industrial dogmatics, but they are materially incorporated into the valorization regime as a constant source of diffuse productivity and as a base of support for the toll. From this point of view, the consumer approaches a digital lumpenproletariat: they participate intensely in the production of value, but do so on an opaque plane, outside the traditional categories of work, employment contract or provision of services.p. 223

PTO consumidor-usuário de plataformas encontra-se formalmente fora da relação salarial e fora das categorias clássicas de trabalho que sustentaram a dogmática industrial, mas ele é materialmente incorporado ao regime de valorização como fonte constante de produtividade difusa e como base de sustentação do pedágio. Desse ponto de vista, o consumidor aproxima-se de um lumpemproletariado digital: participa intensamente da produção de valor, mas o faz em um plano opaco, fora das categorias tradicionais de trabalho, contrato de emprego ou prestação de serviços.

When progress, status, reputation and the very continuity of access to the Immaterial Object of Consumption become dependent on unilateral decisions of the platform, the consumer finds themselves in a position analogous to that of the worker subjected to living conditions defined by others, but without the law recognizing them as such.p. 223

PTQuando o progresso, o status, a reputação e a própria continuidade de acesso ao Objeto Imaterial de Consumo se tornam dependentes de decisões unilaterais da plataforma, o consumidor se vê em posição análoga à do trabalhador submetido a condições de vida definidas por outrem, mas sem que o direito o reconheça como tal.

The “cloud serf” produces without a contract, without a wage and without recognition as work.p. 223

PTO “servo da nuvem” produz sem contrato, sem salário e sem reconhecimento como trabalho.

[...] but as manifestations of a common structural arrangement, marked by platform technofeudalism, digital rentierism and a specific form of exploitation of the consumer who, contextually, is also characterized as incorporated external workforce (the “consumer-worker”)p. 143

Political economyIntellectual property as a fence

Intellectual property is the set of rights that protects creations, such as the design of a skin, the music of a game and the code that makes everything work. It exists to encourage those who create. But, in an online game, it does more than protect the work. It helps the company define who can use what, for how long and under what conditions.

When these rights are combined with licenses, accounts, copy locks, catalogs and passwords, the incentive to create can become a fence. Circulating becomes infringement and enjoying becomes a matter of permission. That is why intellectual property does not appear here as an outside detail, but as a piece of the very structure that encloses the game and your collection inside it. And competition between companies changes, because it stops being about a better product and becomes about who fences in more and holds on better.

Where it comes from

Once stabilized, the code tends to reproduce itself through institutional inertia, path dependencies and legal and economic mechanisms that make alternatives and reappropriations more expensive. In this scenario, intellectual property appears as an infrastructure of semiotic and technical governance. It not only protects creations, but contributes to delimiting uses, restricting reconfigurations and consolidating the supplier's authority over the conditions of enjoymentp. 130

PTUma vez estabilizado, o código tende a se reproduzir por inércia institucional, por dependências de trajetória e por mecanismos jurídicos e econômicos que encarecem alternativas e reapropriações. Nesse cenário, a propriedade intelectual comparece como infraestrutura de governança semiótica e técnica. Ela não apenas protege criações, mas contribui para delimitar usos, restringir reconfigurações e consolidar a autoridade do fornecedor sobre as condições de fruição

When this decision is coupled with license agreements, accounts, DRMp. 234

PTQuando essa decisão se acopla a contratos de licença, contas, DRM

catalogs and authentication mechanisms, the incentive to create gives way to a technology of blocking, so that circulation is treated as infringement and enjoyment is converted into authorization. In this context, competition ceases to be merely a dispute over products and becomes a dispute over enclosures.p. 234

PTcatálogos e mecanismos de autenticação, o incentivo à criação cede espaço a uma tecnologia de bloqueio, o que faz com que a circulação seja tratada como infração e a fruição se converta em autorização. Nesse contexto, a concorrência deixa de ser apenas disputa por produto e passa a ser disputa por cercamentos.

It is here that intellectual property rights cease to appear as a peripheral legal title and begin to operate as a material component of the architecture of closure.p. 234

PTÉ aqui que os direitos de propriedade intelectual deixam de aparecer como título jurídico periférico e passam a operar como componente material da arquitetura de fechamento.

The competitive mutation described manifests itself clearly in platform-operated interactive entertainment because competitive value is not concentrated in the “quality” of an isolated good, but in the capacity to keep the consumer bound to a proprietary grammar of belonging, progress and distinctionp. 232

PTA mutação concorrencial descrita se manifesta com nitidez no entretenimento interativo operado por plataforma porque o valor competitivo não se concentra na “qualidade” de um bem isolado, mas na capacidade de manter o consumidor preso a uma gramática proprietária de pertencimento, progresso e distinção

Identity captivityIdentity captivity

So why not leave? Because leaving costs more than money. When you leave, you lose the recognition, the reputation and the history you built there, recorded in rankings, collections, histories and signs of distinction that only exist inside that game.

This dependence is not a weakness of yours or something in your head. It comes from the way the place is set up, because it is the platform that defines what counts as value, as belonging and as progress, and that can downgrade or erase all of it. Over time, the toll stops being the price of an item and becomes the fee for keeping your place. Giving this a name does not create a new good. Its purpose is to make this form of loss visible and controllable.

Where it comes from

[...] reinforcing a circuit of permanence that converts the individual's participation into an input of valorization for the platform. This effect can be named, with operational precision, identity captivity, understood as a modality of dependence in which the cost of exit is not reduced to the patrimonial plane, but involves loss of recognition, of reputation and of narrative continuity objectified in public records of performance, collections, histories and signs of distinction produced within the ecosystem.p. 224-225

PT[...] reforçando um circuito de permanência que converte a participação do indivíduo em um insumo de valorização para a plataforma. Esse efeito pode ser nomeado, com precisão operacional, como cativeiro identitário, entendido como modalidade de dependência em que o custo de saída não se reduz ao plano patrimonial, mas envolve perda de reconhecimento, de reputação e de continuidade narrativa objetivada em registros públicos de desempenho, acervos, históricos e sinais de distinção produzidos no interior do ecossistema.

Dependence, in this register, is not psychological in the individualizing sense, but institutional and semiotic, because it derives from a regime of visibility and sanction that defines what counts as value, belonging and legitimate progress and, correlatively, defines how this can be downgraded, erased or discredited.p. 225

PTA dependência, nesse registro, não é psicológica no sentido individualizante, mas institucional e semiótica, porque decorre de um regime de visibilidade e de sanção que define o que conta como valor, pertença e progresso legítimo e, correlatamente, define como isso pode ser rebaixado, apagado ou desautorizado.

In consumer law terms, the relevance of the concept is not to create a new good, but to make a specific form of loss in the fact of consumption controllablep. 225

PTEm termos jurídico-consumeristas, a relevância do conceito não é criar um bem novo, mas tornar controlável uma forma específica de perda no fato do consumo

The result is identity captivity, in which the toll stabilizes as a fee for maintaining one's place, not as a one-off price for an item.p. 258

PTO resultado é um cativeiro identitário, no qual o pedágio se estabiliza como taxa de manutenção do lugar, não como preço pontual de um item.

So is having a store, a currency and rare items illegal?

None of this makes it illegal to have a store, a currency of its own, events or rare items. Creating scarcity is commercial strategy, and strategy, on its own, is not unlawful. It starts to matter to the law when it hides or frustrates the correspondence between what was promised and what was delivered, as in the case of the exclusivity that had a time limit nobody mentioned.

Where it comes from

For this reason, artificial scarcity must be understood as part of an architectural teleology. It is part of a set of design choices oriented toward extraction and not toward the mere efficient organization of a naturally limited resource, and it is at this threshold that it ceases to be merely commercial strategy and becomes a legally relevant category for qualifying the deviation of purpose when the equivalence between promise and delivery is made opaque or deliberately frustratedp. 214

PTPor isso, a escassez artificial deve ser compreendida como parte de uma teleologia arquitetural. Ela integra um conjunto de escolhas de design orientadas à extração e não à simples organização eficiente de um recurso naturalmente limitado, e é nesse limiar que deixa de ser apenas estratégia comercial para se tornar categoria juridicamente relevante à qualificação do desvio de finalidade quando a equivalência entre promessa e entrega é tornada opaca ou deliberadamente frustrada

This is not about investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor about moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governancep. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada

What can you demand?

Owning the work is not an excuse for everythingOwning the work is not an excuse for everything

When do the rights over the game become a shield?

When the company uses the rights it holds over the game to resist any control over the way it supplies what you bought. This includes copyright, trade secrets, licenses and control over the system itself.

It is worth understanding the role of these rights. Intellectual property does not appear here only as an obstacle coming from outside. It is what helps the company define what can be used, for how long and under what conditions. It protects creation, but it also establishes the company's authority over the way you use what you bought. That is why it is part of the very structure of what is sold.

How should the company use exclusivity?

The platform must use its exclusivity without turning it into a shield to hide everything, worsen what you bought or take away your access without reason. The balance between the company's rights and yours is struck through the social function, under which those rights also exist to serve the interest of society. And it is struck through the limits the Constitution places on whoever exercises private power.

How do you show whether the restriction protects the game or only shields the company?

It helps to show whether the restriction the company invokes is really necessary to protect the game and the work, or whether it is serving to shield the way it governs the environment.

What can you ask for when the company invokes the work?

You can ask for consumer law to keep applying. In practice, that can mean a limited examination of the records that preserves the secrets, and control over the equivalence of what you bought. It can also mean that the company respects the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration. And, if the game is shut down, that the exit comes, as far as possible, with a transition, preservation or transfer of what you had inside it.

Does the company lose its rights over the game?

The intellectual property rights over the game, the art and the code remain protected. Nobody asks the company to give up the work. What is at issue is using this exclusivity as a general veto against consumer protection.

Where it comes from

PTSempre que a plataforma invocar titularidade exclusiva, segredo empresarial, licenciamento ou estrutura proprietária do ecossistema para resistir ao controle do modo de fornecimento, a compatibilização deverá ser feita à luz da função social e dos limites constitucionais do exercício das posições privadas.
