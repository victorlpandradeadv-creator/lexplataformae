Why technology changes the legal question

The defense that the loss was simply how the software works treats as nature what, in a governed digital environment, is a design choice someone made. The philosophy of technique, of media and of design brings these choices back into the field of attribution, and each of its authors changes the question the jurist puts to the case.

How does the rule move from the clause to the interface?

Feenberg sums up the premise by saying that no technology is an island, because every technology is mediated by markets and administrations. If so, design choices are socially situated decisions, open to criticism as to the purposes they fulfill and the asymmetries they produce, and therefore they are a site of attribution and of assessment of legitimacy.

The platform should not be naturalized as a simple medium, as if it were a neutral engineering unit to which the social would be added later.p. 126

PTA plataforma não deve ser naturalizada como simples meio, como se fosse uma unidade neutra de engenharia à qual o social se adicionaria posteriormente.

Where it comes from

From there, Feenberg formulates a highly productive premise for the legal critique of platforms: no technology is an island because every technology is mediated by markets and administrations.p. 126

PTA partir daí, Feenberg formula uma premissa de alto rendimento para a crítica jurídica das plataformas: nenhuma tecnologia é uma ilha porque toda tecnologia é mediada por mercados e administrações.

If technology is always mediated by economic and administrative rationalities, then design choices must be read as socially situated decisions, open to criticism as to the purposes they fulfill and the asymmetries they produce, that is, as a locus of attribution and of assessment of legitimacy.p. 126

PTSe a tecnologia é sempre mediada por racionalidades econômicas e administrativas, então escolhas de design devem ser lidas como decisões socialmente situadas, criticáveis quanto às finalidades que realizam e quanto às assimetrias que produzem, isto é, como locus de imputação e de aferição de legitimidade.

Non-neutrality has a concrete translation in the interface. Feenberg observes that arrangements of neutral appearance may incorporate unconscious preferences, and, in digital consumption, what becomes visible, recommendable, subjected to friction, permitted or rewarded may operate as interface naturalness and mask value choices under the idiom of functionality. Defaults, the options already checked in advance, along with hierarchies of choice, urgency devices and reward regimes, guide conduct without needing to declare the value orientation that informs them. Therefore, even without a lie or omission in the text, practical normativity can induce perceptions, desires and permanence through a grammar of interaction presented as simple usability.

Where it comes from

Feenberg also describes constraints materialized in systems capable of determining individual actions more effectively than political authority, because they bear on reflexes, skills and attitudes. When conduct is administered by systems and interfaces, governance ceases to operate only by explicit command and comes to operate by architecture, and the regime in which the consumer decides in the face of statements is distinguished from the one in which they decide within an environment that organizes microstructures of choice. In this second regime, legal responses cannot be restricted to clauses and notices. When constraints and incentives are integrated into the system to produce permanence, extraction and dependence under the appearance of enjoyment, the purpose ceases to appear as a side effect and comes to compose the material form of supply.

The last link is the design code. For Feenberg, the result of struggles and collaborations is the stabilized intersection between social choice and technical specification, and it follows that the consumed object includes the set of mediations that makes the content enjoyable, monetizable and governable, and that this code can be assessed as to the purpose it fulfills. Once stabilized, the code tends to reproduce itself through institutional inertia and through legal and economic mechanisms that make alternatives more expensive, and intellectual property appears as an infrastructure of semiotic and technical governance, which delimits uses, restricts reconfigurations and consolidates the supplier's authority over the conditions of enjoyment. When the technical code defines conditions of enjoyment, permanence and loss, the asymmetry between supplier and consumer ceases to be a mere informational defect and becomes architectural structure, a field of legal relevance that opens the way to the qualification of the teleology inscribed in the architecture, the subject of the chapter on abusive teleology and the unlawful act of architecture.

If the technical code is determined by actors, it is politically situated and economically orientable and, therefore, its teleologies can be reconstructed and criticized.p. 135

PTSe o código técnico é determinado por atores, ele é politicamente situado e economicamente orientável e, por isso, suas teleologias podem ser reconstruídas e criticadas.

What place is left for law in the face of design?

So that the critique does not fall into a pessimism with no way out, Feenberg relies on a critical constructivism, according to which technology is neither value-neutral nor universal and, for that very reason, admits democratic interventions in the technosystem. He gives the name democratic rationalization to the interventions of users and subordinate actors that impose on technical development needs that had been ignored, or that run against hierarchies already stabilized in the technical form. He gives two examples, AIDS patients, who reoriented clinical research, and the users of the French Minitel, who introduced human communication into a network planned to distribute data. It follows from these examples that democratic intervention need not mean only external regulation and can be a dispute internal to the technical form and to the governance that administers it.

Where it comes from

To prevent this critique from falling into a pessimistic substantivism, Feenberg anchors himself in a critical constructivism, according to which technology is neither value-neutral nor universal and, precisely for that reason, admits an explicit theory of democratic interventions in the technosystem Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 38).p. 126

PTPara evitar que essa crítica recaia em um substantivismo pessimista, Feenberg se ancora em um construtivismo crítico, segundo o qual a tecnologia não é neutra em valor nem universal e, justamente por isso, admite uma teoria explícita de intervenções democráticas no tecnossistema (Feenberg, 2017, p. 38) .

“Democratic rationalization”, in Feenberg, is not a metaphor for “participation” in a generic sense. It is presented as a principle by which interventions of users and subordinate actors manage to impose on technical development needs that were ignored or to run against hierarchies already stabilized in the technical form.p. 126

PTA “racionalização democrática”, em Feenberg, não é uma metáfora para “participação” em sentido genérico. Ela é apresentada como um princípio pelo qual intervenções de usuários e atores subordinados conseguem impor ao desenvolvimento técnico necessidades ignoradas ou contrariar hierarquias já estabilizadas na forma técnica.

Instead of participating individually as objects of a procedure, the activists act as an organized interested party and reorient the system toward new purposes, pressing for experimentation to become a standard form of care for the incurably ill and for the design of protocols to reflect, more responsively, the needs and urgencies of those who live with the condition.p. 127

PTEm vez de participar individualmente como objetos de um procedimento, os ativistas atuam como parte interessada organizada e reorientam o sistema para novos propósitos, pressionando para que a experimentação se converta em forma padrão de cuidado para doentes incuráveis e para que o desenho dos protocolos reflita, de modo mais responsivo, as necessidades e urgências daqueles que vivem a condição.

The fact that the terminal “disguised itself” as a telephone device suggested to some users that the network could serve for interpersonal communication. From there, users “hacked” the network in which they were embedded and altered its functioning, introducing practices of online human communication, such as anonymous chat, aimed at fun, companionship and meetings, where mere data distribution had been planned.p. 135

PTO fato de o terminal “disfarçar-se” de dispositivo telefônico sugeriu a alguns usuários que a rede poderia servir para comunicação interpessoal. A partir daí, os usuários “hackearam” a rede na qual estavam inseridos e alteraram seu funcionamento, introduzindo práticas de comunicação humana online, como o bate-papo anônimo, voltadas a divertimento, companhia e encontros, onde havia sido planejada a mera distribuição de dados.

PTPrimeiro, o exemplo mostra que “intervenção democrática” não precisa significar apenas regulação externa; pode significar disputa interna à forma técnica e à governança que a administra.

It is at this point that open auditability and functional value equivalence appear for the first time. If technique is constructed and disputed, these requirements can be read as parameters for the democratization of design, minimal material conditions so that the addressee is not reduced to a mere executor of an architectural script oriented by extraction and organizational predictability. This reading lets us understand legal criteria as ways of reorienting technical rationality without assuming that law stands purely outside it, and Feenberg describes the mechanism by which an ethical demand, once it becomes legally enforceable, stabilizes as a technical requirement and comes to form part of the system's regime of compliance and functioning. The two requirements appear here only as a horizon, and the construction of each is left to its own chapter.

Elas não funcionam como adereços retóricos de transparência, mas como condições materiais mínimas para que o destinatário não seja reduzido à posição de mero executante de um roteiro arquitetural orientado por extração e previsibilidade organizacional (Feenberg, 2017, p. 36-38) .

The idea of democratic interventions in the technosystem is of objective interest because it allows us to understand legal criteria as forms of reorienting technical rationality without presupposing a “pure” externality of law.p. 127

PTA ideia de intervenções democráticas no tecnossistema é de objetivo interesse porque permite compreender critérios jurídicos como formas de reorientação da racionalidade técnica sem pressupor uma externalidade “pura” do direito.

PTUma vez materializada, a exigência deixa de circular como debate moral e passa a integrar o regime técnico de conformidade e de funcionamento.

Feenberg also offers a reading of consent. In revisiting industrial labor, he shows how the union of capital and science can reduce the workers' task to the exercise of vigilance and dexterity. In formulating the notion of operational autonomy, he turns to Karl Marx to argue that the capitalist form of organizing labor gains technical reality when machinery turns the worker into a subordinate component of a preconstituted system. The example is not digital and matters for its structural function, that of showing that technical decisions can serve purposes of control and predictability. Transposed to consumption, the asymmetry appears as the supplier's operational autonomy, and the successful architecture is the one that reduces the operator's dependence on users, converts participation into data and value and does not generate an equivalent power to define purposes. Consent then tends to operate under low contestability, because the consumer adheres to a regime whose rationality has already been decided and incorporated into the system.

Where it comes from

In revisiting the theme of industrial labor, Feenberg exposes how the union of capital and science can be oriented toward reducing the workers' task to the exercise of vigilance and dexterity, reorganizing dependencies and reinforcing management's decision-making autonomy. Although the example is not digital, its function here is structural. It shows that technical decisions can be oriented by purposes of control and predictability and that this control materializes in technical form.p. 133

PTAo recuperar o tema do trabalho industrial, Feenberg expõe como a união entre capital e ciência pode orientar-se a reduzir a tarefa dos trabalhadores ao exercício de vigilância e destreza, reorganizando dependências e reforçando a autonomia decisória da gestão. Embora o exemplo não seja digital, sua função aqui é estrutural. Ele mostra que decisões técnicas podem ser orientadas por finalidades de controle e previsibilidade e que esse controle se materializa em forma técnica.

In formulating the notion of “operational autonomy”, Feenberg (2002, p. 43) seeks to demonstrate that capitalist control of the production process is not exercised only by legal-formal command or administrative coordination, but is consolidated when the very organization of labor is technically incorporated. It is at this point that the author turns to Karl Marx (citing an edition of Capital published in 1906) to argue that the specifically capitalist form of organizing the labor process comes to acquire a palpable technical reality insofar as machinery and productive arrangements turn the worker into a subordinate component of a preconstituted material system.p. 133-134

PTAo formular a noção de “autonomia operacional”, Feenberg (2002, p. 43) procura demostrar que o controle capitalista do processo produtivo não se exerce apenas por comando jurídico-formal ou por coordenação administrativa, mas se consolida quando a própria organização do trabalho é incorporada tecnicamente. É nesse ponto que o autor recorre a Karl Marx (citando edição de O Capital publicada em 1906) para sustentar que a forma especificamente capitalista de organizar o processo de trabalho passa a adquirir uma realidade técnica palpável na medida em que a maquinaria e os arranjos produtivos transformam o trabalhador em componente subordinado de um sistema material pré-constituído.

When this logic is transposed to consumption, the typical asymmetry of the technosystem appears as the supplier's operational autonomy. A successful architecture is one that reduces the operator's dependence on the subjects who use it, converts participation into data and value and does not generate an equivalent power to define purposes. In this scenario, consent tends to operate under material conditions of low contestability, because the consumer adheres to an operating regime whose rationality has already been decided and incorporated into the system, which deepens the distance between formal choice and practical governance Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 54-55).p. 133-134

PTQuando essa lógica é transposta ao consumo, a assimetria típica do tecnossistema aparece como autonomia operacional do fornecedor. Uma arquitetura bem-sucedida é aquela que reduz a dependência do operador em relação aos sujeitos que a utilizam, converte participação em dados e em valor e não gera poder equivalente de definição de finalidades. Nesse cenário, o consentimento tende a operar sob condições materiais de baixa contestabilidade, pois o consumidor adere a um regime de funcionamento cuja racionalidade já foi decidida e incorporada ao sistema, o que aprofunda a distância entre escolha formal e governança prática (Feenberg, 2017, p. 54-55) .

What does the balancing of the champion Mel show behind a technical adjustment?

The case that shows the political rationality of design is the balancing of League of Legends. The meta, short for metagame, is the set of dominant strategies and optimal choices stabilized in a given period, produced and corrected by buffs, which deliberately increase the power of a character, item or ability, and by nerfs, which reduce it. Buffs and nerfs are no mere accessory to the game but the means by which the ecosystem defines, in regular cycles, which characters and strategies remain viable and which fall, for a period, to suboptimal choices. The patch operates there as a norm in operation, because it reconfigures the competitive utility of assets, redistributes frequency of use and alters the real cost of staying. The update makes functional obsolescence an ordinary component of enjoyment, since what was learned, mastered and chosen can be downgraded within weeks by unilateral technical decision.

Where it comes from

The term “meta” is used in the technical sense of the competitive gaming ecosystem, as short for metagame. It is the set of dominant strategies, optimal choices and performance patterns stabilized in a given time frame, resulting from the interaction between rules, updates and the aggregate behavior of the community.p. 121

PTO termo “meta” é empregado no sentido técnico do ecossistema de jogos competitivos, como abreviação de metagame. Trata-se do conjunto de estratégias dominantes, escolhas ótimas e padrões de desempenho estabilizados em determinado recorte temporal, resultantes da interação entre regras, atualizações e comportamento agregado da comunidade.

The English word “buff” designates a deliberate increase in the power and effectiveness of a game element (character, item, ability, system), with measurable impact on performance and on the metagame.p. 121

PTA palavra inglesa “buff” designa aumento deliberado de poder e efetividade de um elemento do jogo (personagem, item, habilidade, sistema), com impacto mensurável no desempenho e no metajogo.

In League of Legends, buffs and nerfs are not an ornament added to the game, but the very means by which the ecosystem defines, in regular cycles, which characters and strategies remain competitively viable and which are, for a period, downgraded to the condition of suboptimal choices. In this key, the patch operates as a norm in operation. It does not merely readjust variables, but reconfigures, in practice, the competitive utility of the enjoyed assets, redistributes frequency of use, repositions preferences and alters the real “cost” of remaining in the environment, since what is functionally strong tends to concentrate attention, learning and investment of time.p. 121

PTNo League of Legends, buffs e nerfs não são um adereço posterior ao jogo, mas o próprio meio pelo qual o ecossistema define, em ciclos regulares, quais personagens e estratégias permanecem competitivamente viáveis e quais ficam, por um período, rebaixadas à condição de escolhas subótimas. Nessa chave, o patch opera como norma em operação. Ele não apenas reajusta variáveis, mas reconfigura, na prática, a utilidade competitiva dos ativos fruídos, redistribui frequência de uso, reposiciona preferências e altera o “custo” real de permanência no ambiente, já que aquilo que é funcionalmente forte tende a concentrar atenção, aprendizado e investimento de tempo.

If the meta is, to a large extent, the set of incentivized choices, then the update is not merely maintenance. It is the mechanism that makes functional obsolescence an ordinary component of enjoyment, since what was learned, mastered and chosen as an axis of performance can be downgraded, within weeks, by unilateral technical decision.p. 122

PTSe o meta é, em grande medida, o conjunto de escolhas incentivadas, então a atualização não é apenas manutenção. Ela é o mecanismo que torna a obsolescência funcional um componente ordinário da fruição, pois aquilo que foi aprendido, dominado e escolhido como eixo de performance pode ser rebaixado, em semanas, por decisão técnica unilateral.

The company's own documentation treats balancing as a formalized procedure. In the Champion Balance Framework, Riot describes the goal of making buff and nerf decisions consistent and easier to understand, with measurable parameters, audience segmentation, combined use of win rate and ban rate and thresholds that function as triggers for intervention. The company also maintains a public chronological index of Patch Notes, which allows the regularity of the patch to be reconstructed as the normal mode of operation of the service. This practice is one of the company's most praiseworthy and one of the most functional ways for platforms to meet the requirements of open auditability and functional equivalence. Even so, the rationality that presents itself as transparency and predictability fulfills a social function, because it converts statistical performance into practical orders about what should be played, what should be avoided and what loses competitive centrality.

Where it comes from

In publicly explaining its Champion Balance Framework, Riot Games describes the goal of making buff and nerf decisions “consistent” and “easier to understand”, including through measurable parameters, audience segmentation and combined use of win rate and ban rate, as well as thresholds that function as triggers for intervention Riot Games(Riot Games, 2019a). The result is a technical rationality that presents itself as transparency and predictability, but that also fulfills a social function, insofar as it converts statistical performance into practical orders about what should be played, what should be avoided and what loses competitive centrality.p. 122

PTAo explicar publicamente seu Plano de Balanceamento de Campeões, a Riot Games descreve o objetivo de tornar “consistentes” e “mais fáceis de entender” as decisões de fortalecimento e enfraquecimento, inclusive por meio de parâmetros mensuráveis, segmentação de públicos e uso combinado de taxa de vitória e taxa de banimento, além de limiares que funcionam como gatilhos de intervenção (Riot Games, 2019a) . O resultado é uma racionalidade técnica que se apresenta como transparência e previsibilidade, mas que também cumpre função social, na medida em que converte desempenho estatístico em ordens práticas do que deve ser jogado, do que deve ser evitado e do que perde centralidade competitiva.

Riot maintains a public index of Patch Notes, in chronological sequence, which allows the regularity of the patch to be reconstructed as the normal mode of operation of the service Riot Games(Riot Games, [202-?]b).p. 122

PTA Riot mantém um índice público de Notas de Atualização, em sequência cronológica, que permite reconstruir a regularidade do patch como modo normal de operação do serviço (Riot Games, [202-?]b) .

Among the many practices consolidated by the company, the regular maintenance of the public index of patch notes is one of the most praiseworthy and was a mechanism by which the company adapted to the dialectical business model it had established with its community. The practice is, for most platforms, one of the most practical and functional ways of methodologically meeting the requirements of open auditability and maintenance of functional equivalence formulated in sections 5, 6 and 7 of this thesis.p. 122

PTDentre as muitas práticas consolidadas pela empresa, a manutenção regular do índice público de notas de atualização é uma das mais elogiáveis e se tratou de mecanismo de adaptação da empresa ao modelo de negócio dialético que estabelecera com sua comunidade. A prática é, para a maior parte das plataformas, uma das vias mais práticas e funcionais para adequação metodológica às exigências de auditabilidade aberta e manutenção de equivalência funcional formuladas nas seções 5, 6 e 7 desta tese.

The contractual basis completes the picture. The Terms of Service authorize altering, updating, suspending and applying nerfs to features and parts of the service, including virtual products, with automatic patches and with or without prior notice, and they allow altering the purchasing power of the game currency, for example by increasing the number of coins needed to buy skins. Balancing appears, in this picture, as a regular exercise of governance by design, and the ecosystem as an administered economy, in which value and access can be recalibrated by unilateral decision of the supplier.

Where it comes from

The Terms of Service state that the supplier may alter, update, suspend and even “nerf” features and parts of the services, including virtual products, and they also provide for the application of background patches and automatic updates, with or without prior notice Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). Balancing presents itself, in this picture, as a regular exercise of governance by design: a mode of administering the ecosystem in which technical choices cyclically reorganize the field of the possible, modulate competitive utility and reorder incentives for remaining and acquiring.p. 123

This point requalifies the usual language of the industry, which tends to hide the normative core under engineering vocabulary (“tweak”, “update”, “balancing”). When a platform unilaterally alters a rare cosmetic item or redesigns a character, it is determining, by code, the material conditions of enjoyment and the way the good will be recognized and compared in the social world of the game. If the alteration lowers the functional and symbolic value that motivated the acquisition, the problem is not “user nostalgia”, but an architectural redefinition of the content delivered, with a direct impact on auditability and on the attribution of abusiveness.p. 135

PTEsse ponto requalifica a linguagem usual do setor, que tende a esconder o núcleo normativo sob vocabulário de engenharia (“ajuste”, “atualização”, “balanceamento”). Quando uma plataforma altera unilateralmente um item cosmético raro ou redesenha um personagem, ela está determinando, por código, as condições materiais de fruição e o modo como o bem será reconhecido e comparado no mundo social do jogo. Se a alteração rebaixa o valor funcional e simbólico que motivou a aquisição, o problema não é “nostalgia do usuário”, mas redefinição arquitetural do conteúdo entregue, com impacto direto sobre auditabilidade e imputação de abusividade.

The second inverts Ellul's argument and holds that, if technique is autonomous, nothing would be anyone's decision. This reading fails, because the inevitability Ellul speaks of is a critical description of a system effect, the tendency of the technical to naturalize and justify itself, and not a claim that history is closed to contestation. Saying that technique acquires autonomy does not mean assuming a single, inevitable or monolithic causality, but recognizing that, in certain arrangements, incentives make the implementation of systems partly self-fueling, often without public deliberation proportional to its effects. The legal problem lies between that tendency and the possibility of democratic governance, and Ellul himself ends his work with an appeal to individual and collective responsibility in the face of technique.

Where it comes from

The Ellulian reference to “inevitability” must be taken as a critical description of a system effect, that is, of the tendency toward naturalization and self-justification of the technical, and not as a normative thesis according to which history would be closed to contestation.p. 96

PTA referência elluliana à “inevitabilidade” deve ser tomada como descrição crítica de um efeito de sistema, isto é, da tendência à naturalização e à autojustificação do técnico, e não como tese normativa segundo a qual a história estaria fechada à contestação.

Saying that technique acquires “autonomy” does not mean postulating a single, inevitable or monolithic causality, but recognizing that, in certain sociotechnical arrangements, institutional and economic incentives turn the innovation and implementation of systems into a partly self-fueling dynamic, frequently dissociated from public deliberation proportional to its effects. It is precisely in this space, between systemic tendency and the possibility of democratic governance, that the legal problem in question lies.p. 88

PTDizer que a técnica adquire “autonomia” não significa postular uma causalidade única, inevitável ou monolítica, mas reconhecer que, em certos arranjos sociotécnicos, os incentivos institucionais e econômicos tornam a inovação e a implementação de sistemas em uma dinâmica parcialmente autoalimentada, frequentemente dissociada de deliberação pública proporcional aos seus efeitos. É precisamente nesse espaço, entre tendência sistêmica e possibilidade de governança democrática, que se situa o problema jurídico em questão.

At the end of his work, Ellul questions the belief that technical progress is an objective synonym for human progress and makes an appeal to individual and collective responsibility in the face of technique Ellul(Ellul, 1968, p. 441-445).p. 96

PTAo final de sua obra, Ellul questiona a crença no progresso técnico como sinônimo objetivo de progresso humano e formula um apelo à responsabilidade individual e coletiva diante da técnica (Ellul, 1968, p. 441-445) .

The third is that everything was in the terms and the consumer was warned. The contractual text does not lose importance, but examining design has two consequences. Design must be treated as a material dimension of supply, because it is where the practical rules of access, permanence and loss are stabilized. Legal control cannot depend only on general clauses or advertising promises, since it must reach the mechanisms through which the platform produces equivalences, lowers values and applies modulations without sufficient transparency.

Consumer Law cannot confine itself to checking clauses or isolated informational duties when experience, enjoyment and loss are conditioned by technical decisions embedded in the environment.p. 137

PTO Direito do Consumidor não pode se limitar à verificação de cláusulas ou deveres informacionais isolados quando a experiência, a fruição e a perda são condicionadas por decisões técnicas incorporadas ao ambiente.

Where it comes from

The first consequence is that design must be treated as a material dimension of supply, because the practical rules of access, permanence and loss that define the enjoyment of the immaterial object are stabilized in it. The second is that, if architecture concentrates teleologies and condenses ambivalent functions, legal control cannot depend only on general clauses or advertising promises, and must reach the mechanisms by which the platform produces equivalences, lowers values and applies modulations without sufficient transparency.p. 137

PTA primeira consequência é que o design deve ser tratado como dimensão material do fornecimento, porque nele se estabilizam as regras práticas de acesso, permanência e perda que definem a fruição do objeto imaterial. A segunda é que, se a arquitetura concentra teleologias e condensa funções ambivalentes, o controle jurídico não pode depender apenas de cláusulas gerais ou de promessas publicitárias, devendo alcançar os mecanismos pelos quais a plataforma produz equivalências, rebaixa valores e aplica modulações sem transparência suficiente.

The fourth is that the critique would be moralizing or opposed to technology. The very choice of authors rules out this objection. Postman matters not as nostalgia for print culture, but as a key to understanding that each medium sets up its own ecology of perception and knowledge. The shift carried out by Feenberg, which moves rationality from the plane of an abstract ideal to that of an operating social function, serves precisely so that the consumer law critique is not confused with a moralizing refusal of technique. What is reconstructed is a regime of power whose effectiveness is produced by systems, standards and decision routines embedded in infrastructures.

Where it comes from

Postman's contribution matters not as nostalgia for print culture, but as a key to understanding that each medium sets up its own ecology of perception and knowledge.p. 98

PTA contribuição de Postman não interessa como nostalgia da cultura impressa, mas como chave para compreender que cada meio instaura uma ecologia própria de percepção e conhecimento.

By shifting rationality from the domain of an abstract ideal to that of an operating social function, Feenberg provides the bridge needed for the consumer law critique not to be confused with a moralizing refusal of technique. What is reconstructed is a regime of power whose effectiveness is produced by systems, standards and decision routines embedded in infrastructures Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 15-16).p. 123

PTAo deslocar a racionalidade do domínio de um ideal abstrato para o de uma função social operante, Feenberg fornece a passagem necessária para que a crítica jurídico-consumerista não se confunda com recusa moralizante da técnica. O que se reconstrói é um regime de poder cuja eficácia se produz por sistemas, padrões e rotinas decisórias incorporadas às infraestruturas (Feenberg, 2017, p. 15-16) .

Does the diagnosis of technique create law?

The diagnosis does not create law by itself. The reference to positive software law does not anticipate the later dogmatic construction, and the categories that depend on this background, such as semiotic vulnerability, abusive teleology, open auditability and functional value equivalence, are built only in their own chapters. Nothing said here supports the conclusion that every update is unlawful, because updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility in a competitive ecosystem, and not every improvement becomes a legal problem.

Where it comes from

For the purposes of this section, the reference does not anticipate the later dogmatic construction; it only shows that dependence on maintenance, updating and governance over time is not an accident external to the consumer relationship, but an attributable feature of supply in digital environments.p. 91

PTPara os fins desta seção, a referência não antecipa a construção dogmática posterior; apenas demonstra que a dependência de manutenção, atualização e governança no tempo não constitui acidente externo à relação de consumo, mas traço imputável do fornecimento em ambientes digitais.

This problem does not turn every improvement into an unlawful act. Updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility in a competitive ecosystem.p. 470

PTEsse problema não transforma toda melhoria em ilícito. Atualizações podem ser necessárias para compatibilidade, segurança, estabilidade e legibilidade em um ecossistema competitivo.

The authors also come with limits of use that the jurist must respect. Ellul is brought in not for a generic critique of technological modernity, but to make intelligible the technical order in which the IOC is formed. Postman matters less for his sectoral object, television, than for the mechanism. The parallel between Vanderburg and Durkheim works by analogy, comparing the functions of belonging, validation and sanction that metrics and infrastructures take on with those Durkheim describes in the symbolic regime. Feenberg's industrial example counts for its structural function, not for being digital.

Where it comes from

Ellul is not summoned to support a generic critique of technological modernity, but to make intelligible the technical order in which the IOC is formed.p. 97

PTEllul não é convocado para sustentar uma crítica genérica à modernidade tecnológica, mas para tornar inteligível a ordem técnica em que o OIC se forma.

The conversion of public space into show business matters less for its sectoral object than for its mechanism.p. 101

PTA conversão do espaço público em show business interessa menos por seu objeto setorial do que por seu mecanismo.

Technical-operational modernity compresses dense symbolic mediations without eliminating the structural need for symbolization; it shifts, however, its supports and criteria of validity to artifacts, devices and functional grammars, opening space for metrics, performances and access infrastructures to take on, in practice, functions of belonging, validation and sanction comparable, by analogy, to those Durkheim describes in the symbolic regime, converting technical governance into lived moral routine.p. 108

PTA modernidade técnico-operacional comprime mediações simbólicas densas sem eliminar a necessidade estrutural de simbolização; desloca, porém, seus suportes e critérios de validade para artefatos, dispositivos e gramáticas funcionais, abrindo espaço para que métricas, performances e infraestruturas de acesso assumam, na prática, funções de pertencimento, validação e sanção comparáveis, por analogia, às que Durkheim descreve no regime simbólico, convertendo governança técnica em rotina moral vivida.

Care is also needed with the evidentiary material. The empirical dimension is illustrative and disciplined by mechanisms, with no claim to describe the ecosystem in full or to show statistical prevalence, and the Riot Games examples serve to make institutional arrangements and legally relevant points of friction intelligible. Entries from collaborative repositories, such as the game's wiki, come in only as technical-operational support and as a lead for locating primary documentation, and never to prove institutional promises, internal decision criteria or official milestones of change.

Where it comes from

The empirical dimension of the work is mobilized for an illustrative purpose and disciplined by mechanisms, so as to make intelligible institutional arrangements and legally relevant points of friction. There is no intention to describe the ecosystem exhaustively, nor to demonstrate statistical prevalence, nor to offer a complete overview of its history or its variations.p. 49

PTA dimensão empírica do trabalho é mobilizada com finalidade ilustrativa e disciplinada por mecanismos, de modo a tornar inteligíveis arranjos institucionais e pontos de fricção juridicamente relevantes. Não se pretende descrever o ecossistema exaustivamente, nem demonstrar prevalência estatística, nem oferecer panorama completo de sua história ou de suas variações.

As to the status of the subsidiary use of collaborative repositories (such as Fandom) for naming categories and minimal operational description of mechanics, the criterion already set in Chapter 2 is adopted, according to which this material is not used to prove institutional promises, internal decision criteria or official milestones of change, operating only as technical-operational support and as a lead for locating primary documentation when this becomes necessary.p. 94

PTQuanto ao estatuto do uso subsidiário de repositórios colaborativos (como o Fandom) para nomeação de categorias e descrição operacional mínima de mecânicas, adota-se o critério já fixado no Capítulo 2, segundo o qual esse material não é empregado para provar promessas institucionais, critérios internos de decisão ou marcos oficiais de mudança, operando apenas como apoio técnico-operacional e como pista para localização de documentação primária quando esta se fizer necessária.
