Consumer-citizen

The explanation in plain words is on the path of the skin, in Are you just a customer in there?

What it is

They are a consumer because there is an acquisition and a legitimate expectation of enjoyment. They are a citizen in an analytical sense, because their presence in the environment depends on conditions of belonging and continuity set by private governance. The word has nothing to do with nationality, sovereignty or constitutional status. It refers to the practical conditions of participation and permanence in an environment where a unilateral change to those conditions reshapes the very object being enjoyed.

The consumer-citizen is the subject who acquires IOCs whose utility is realized as functional permanence in an ecosystem, and not as the isolated delivery of a transferred good.p. 379

PTConsumidor-cidadão é o sujeito que adquire OICs cuja utilidade se realiza como permanência funcional em um ecossistema, e não como entrega isolada de um bem transferido.
