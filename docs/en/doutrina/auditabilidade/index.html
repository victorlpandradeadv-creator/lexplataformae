The jurist's way

Open auditability

Open auditability is the minimum threshold of external verifiability that makes it possible to reconstruct what the supplier changed, when, why and with what effect, whenever the change or the sanction significantly affects the enjoyment of an Immaterial Object of Consumption. The requirement arises within the CDC, has a delimited scope and does not yield to secrecy or to technical complexity.

What problem does open auditability solve?

In a continuous digital service, asking whether the platform may change the environment leads to little, because changing is part of the way the service is rendered. The legal problem is to distinguish legitimate mutation from the mutation that breaks the balance of the supply, degrades the promised enjoyment or makes the very possibility of control opaque. This last hypothesis has a trait of its own, since a good part of the disputes do not arise from abrupt suppression, but from progressive changes, interface changes and visibility adjustments that seem trivial when taken one by one. The consumer keeps the account, keeps seeing the object and keeps accessing the platform, while the utility may fall and, with it, the consumer's capacity to prove the fall.

In IOCs, mutability exists, but it does not hover above the Law.p. 635

PTEm OICs, a mutabilidade existe, mas não paira acima do Direito.

Where it comes from

The legal problem, therefore, lies not in finding out whether the platform may alter the environment, but in establishing the criteria by which legitimate mutation is distinguished from the mutation that breaks the balance of the supply, degrades the promised enjoyment or makes opaque the very possibility of control.p. 635

PTO problema jurídico, portanto, não está em descobrir se a plataforma pode alterar o ambiente, mas em estabelecer os critérios pelos quais se distingue a mutação legítima da mutação que rompe o equilíbrio do fornecimento, degrada a fruição prometida ou torna opaca a própria possibilidade de controle.

The consumer remains formally “inside” the ecosystem, keeps the account, keeps seeing the object, can keep accessing the platform. Even so, the utility enjoyed may be substantially lowered, and the capacity to prove this lowering may be simultaneously eroded by the very way the environment is administered.p. 635

The difficulty lies in who keeps the information. The supplier controls the architecture, the criteria for change, the logs, which are the automatic records of what the system did, the moment of the change and the very means by which it will be perceived. With this, it concentrates the power to reconfigure and also the power to narrate alone what happened. The effect appears in two images, that of the supplier who comes to hold the practical monopoly of the real and that of the consumer reduced to a bearer of symptoms, who feels having lost something important, but cannot demonstrate what was lost, how it was lost or why the loss goes beyond the trivial annoyance of networked life.

Why do neither total transparency nor published information solve it?

The debate usually gets stuck between two positions. One holds that any transparency requirement compromises trade secrets, innovation or business autonomy, and the other imagines that there is only effective control with complete opening of the internal functioning. Both fail, and the clash is a binary theater, because the first turns technical complexity into an alibi and the second confuses legal protection with invasion of the productive environment. The refusal has a practical effect, since conditioning control on total transparency produces paralysis, and accepting mutability as a neutral given normalizes substantial degradations as mere operational variation.

Where it comes from

Open auditability rejects this binary theater.p. 727

A primeira transforma complexidade técnica em álibi para irresponsabilidade. A segunda confunde tutela jurídica com invasão total do ambiente produtivo. The supplier's usual answer is that the information is already available, in the terms, in the cross-references, in the frequently asked questions and in the update history. This is not enough, because information, under the CDC, does not fulfill its function merely by existing somewhere. What matters is its reconstructive and decisional usefulness, that is, serving to know what occurred and to decide what to do. When this is missing, there is documentary staging and performative transparency, in which the consumer knows that something changed, but cannot discern what changed, by what criterion, with what practical effect and how to demonstrate the impact.

Nor do the two customary dogmatic exits help. Is open auditability a new right?

No, and precision matters for whoever will ground a claim. Open auditability is neither an autonomous positive right nor an institute foreign to the consumer microsystem. It is an internal development of three things the system already has, the duty to inform, objective good faith and the facilitation of the consumer's defense. Called sometimes an operator, sometimes a standard, sometimes a parameter, it designates in every case a standard of requirement that the interpreter applies to the case, of technically qualified transparency usable by institutions. It is enforceable whenever the supplier makes relevant changes, sanctions or reconfigurations capable of reaching the core of enjoyment.

Where it comes from

It is for this reason that open auditability must be understood as an internal unfolding of the duty to inform and not as an institution foreign to the microsystem.p. 641

PTÉ por essa razão que auditabilidade aberta deve ser entendida como desdobramento interno do dever de informação e não como instituto estranho ao microssistema.

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and of business secrecy, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of execution; a development of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of the facilitation of the consumer's defense; a criterion for reducing evidentiary and informational asymmetry; and an institutional condition for relevant mutations of the IOC to cease being accepted as accomplished facts narrated only by the supplier itself.p. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor; um critério de redução de assimetria probatória e informacional; e uma condição institucional para que mutações relevantes do OIC deixem de ser aceitas como fatos consumados narrados apenas pelo próprio fornecedor.

Open auditability designates, in continuous digital services, a standard of technically qualified and institutionally usable transparency, enforceable whenever the supplier makes relevant changes, sanctions or reconfigurations capable of reaching the core of enjoyment of the Immaterial Object of Consumption.p. 639-640

PTA auditabilidade aberta designa, em serviços digitais contínuos, um standard de transparência tecnicamente qualificada e institucionalmente aproveitável, exigível sempre que o fornecedor promove alterações relevantes, sanções ou reconfigurações capazes de atingir o núcleo de fruição do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo.

The first support is the duty to inform. The manual by Benjamin, Marques and Bessa requires correct, clear, precise and conspicuous information, records the need for prior control of the contract by the consumer and recalls that information is part of the contractual content and, if it fails, may represent a quality defect. Ricardo Morishita Wada adds that leaving the time for performance under the supplier's exclusive definition takes away the consumer's capacity for control, and Bruno Miragem treats the duty to inform as a duty of clarification, whose fulfillment the supplier itself has the burden of demonstrating and whose breach may attract Article 46 of the CDC1. On this basis the step that grounds the category is taken. Whoever alters central elements of enjoyment without giving an intelligible basis for reconstructing what occurred does not merely communicate poorly, because that supplier prevents control and worsens the consumer's position in the relationship.

Where it comes from

The second support is objective good faith during performance. In a digital service, the performance is not exhausted in an initial delivery, because the relationship extends through updates, fixes, rebalances and sanctions, and treating this period as a neutral space would be far too crude. Claudia Lima Marques rejects the idea that there is an empty space between the birth of the obligation and the satisfaction of the performance and describes the relationship as a continuous whole, with ancillary duties to inform, to cooperate and of loyalty. Breaching them, for the author, means nonperformance, even if partial. The long-term contract, in turn, rests more on trust and on the maintenance of quality, so that the supplier cannot reserve for itself all the intelligibility of the process of change and leave the consumer only with the imposed result.

The third support is the facilitation of defense. Article 6, item VIII, of the CDC2, according to the manual by Benjamin, Marques and Bessa, is not merely procedural in nature, because it is aimed at effective protection and at reparation, and the spirit of the Code is not to impose on the vulnerable party the proof of elements that are part of the supplier's professional risk. Since the platform concentrates records, histories, enforcement criteria and versions, the facilitation of defense and the redistribution of the burden cease to be occasional instruments and become a condition of minimum effectiveness of the regime. Open auditability then works as a technique for reducing informational and evidentiary asymmetry, which puts the conflict back on reconstructible ground.

Why can the Law control without seeing everything?

Control does not depend on full transparency, and the support comes from legal theory. Humberto Ávila proposes shifting the focus from security of meaning to security of criteria, with prior parameters to face later interpretive problems, and distinguishes security from transparency. Transparency has its core in information, while security seeks argumentative rationality and the control of arbitrariness. From there, the question ceases to be opening the black box all the way down and becomes defining controllable criteria that turn modulations, that is, the continuous changes in the conditions of the service, into legally decidable alternatives.

Where it comes from

It is in this sense that Humberto Ávila formulates an explicit turn by arguing that one should shift the focus from security of meaning to security of criteria, in order to provide, in advance, prior and abstract parameters to face later and concrete interpretive problems Ávila(Ávila, 2011, p. 71).p. 472

PTÉ nesse sentido que Humberto Ávila formula uma virada explícita ao sustentar que se deve deslocar o foco da segurança de significado para a segurança de critérios, a fim de fornecer, de antemão, parâmetros prévios e abstratos para enfrentar problemas interpretativos posteriores e concretos (Ávila, 2011, p. 71) .

The question is not to open the black box all the way down, but to define prior and controllable criteria that make it possible to turn technically opaque modulations into legally decidable alternatives, without reducing control to automatic deference to complexity.p. 472

PTA questão não é abrir a caixa-preta até o fundo, mas definir critérios prévios e controláveis que permitam transformar modulações tecnicamente opacas em alternativas juridicamente decidíveis, sem reduzir o controle à deferência automática à complexidade.

Tércio Sampaio Ferraz Jr. completes the argument through the function of dogmatics. He presents it as a technology aimed at action, which carves out what is operable in the form of a decision, and recalls that the real legal decision operates with incomplete information and has as its immediate purpose to absorb insecurity, transforming undecidable incompatibilities into decidable alternatives. On the platform, opacity tends to push the dispute toward endlessness, because there always remains a technical behind-the-scenes capable of explaining the effect. A minimum threshold operates as a mechanism of finitude, since it organizes the debate around elements that can be discussed under adversarial proceedings.

The Law does not need to see everything in order to control.p. 473

PTO Direito não precisa ver tudo para controlar.

Julie E. Cohen and Eros Roberto Grau explain why this control is justified. Cohen describes dominant platforms as agents that produce a territory of their own, made of protocols, data flows and algorithms, and argues that any meaningful control of this environment depends on a baseline level of information about what such mechanisms do. Grau, discussing the formation of the rule of law from Duguit, associates the legal norm with the socially organized sanction and observes that the market establishes an order produced by it. On platforms, the sanction appears as suspension, ban, lowering of visibility and blocking of features, executed by architecture and by internal procedures. When this operates as a pattern, the demand for auditability ceases to be an aesthetic transparency agenda and becomes a minimum requirement for subjecting private coercion to rational control.

What needs to be auditable?

The trigger is the relevant impact on enjoyment, and its delimitation is rigorous so that the parameter does not slide into maximalism. Its proper field begins when the change, the unilateral modulation, the suppression, the migration, the nerf, which is the adjustment that reduces the power or usefulness of an item, the discontinuation, the reconfiguration of a rule or of the interface, or the sanction applied to the user affects enjoyment in a relevant way. Relevance may appear in access and permanence, in the functional integrity of the object, in the promised equivalences, in the symbolic position of the object in the ecosystem or in the consumer's conditions of proof and challenge. The central point is that the change must be capable of lowering enjoyment, frustrating legitimate expectation or abusively concentrating in the supplier's hands the intelligibility of what occurred.

The CDC was not designed to allow the supplier to turn the harm into a mere accounting cost and move on.p. 645

PTO CDC não foi desenhado para permitir ao fornecedor transformar a lesão em mero custo contábil e seguir adiante.

Without access to the relevant versions, to the execution records and to the elements that demonstrate the supply performed, the legal discussion runs the risk of being held hostage to the documentary fiction of the base contract, which often says little about the real life of the servicep. 646

PTSem acesso às versões relevantes, aos registros de execução e aos elementos que demonstrem o fornecimento realizado, a discussão jurídica corre o risco de ficar refém da ficção documental do contrato-base, que muitas vezes diz pouco sobre a vida real do serviço

Specific performance of the obligation prevails precisely to prevent the supplier from turning nonperformance into an advantageous economic calculation, converting harms into losses and damages as an ordinary cost of the business. For this reason, conversion into indemnification should only occur at the consumer's option or when specific relief or the equivalent practical result is impossible Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 898).p. 646

PTHá prevalência do cumprimento específico da obrigação justamente para evitar que o fornecedor transforme o inadimplemento em cálculo econômico vantajoso, convertendo lesões em perdas e danos como custo ordinário do negócio. Por isso, a conversão em indenização só deve ocorrer por opção do consumidor ou quando impossível a tutela específica ou o resultado prático equivalente (Miragem, 2020, p. 898) .

This design is articulated with preliminary measures aimed at the early production of evidence, notably actions for production of documents, especially in disputes in which the contractual instrument was not delivered to the consumer or in which the actual content of the relationship needs to be reconstructed Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 903).p. 646

PTEsse desenho se articula com providências antecedentes voltadas à produção antecipada de provas, notadamente as ações de exibição, especialmente em litígios em que o instrumento contratual não foi entregue ao consumidor ou em que o conteúdo efetivo do vínculo precisa ser reconstruído (Miragem, 2020, p. 903) .

All the mechanisms answer the same requirement: to prevent the supplier's exclusive technical control from turning into a monopoly over the proof of the very unlawful act alleged.p. 663

PTTodos os mecanismos respondem à mesma exigência: impedir que o controle técnico exclusivo do fornecedor se converta em monopólio sobre a prova do próprio ilícito alegado.

Auditability alone does not decide the material legitimacy of the change, because demonstrating what changed does not answer whether the change preserved the substance of the acquired enjoyment. This second question belongs to functional value equivalence, the subject of a chapter of its own. The two criteria presuppose each other, since without auditability equivalence is hard to demonstrate and without equivalence auditability runs the risk of becoming a mere capacity to narrate changes. In short, one controls the how of the mutation and the other, the how much and the what, and from this come two tests for the interpreter. The first is whether there was minimum institutional verifiability, with reasoning, timing, effects and reconstruction trails. The second is whether the change preserved sufficient functional equivalence or significantly lowered the practical and symbolic core of the object. The order matters, because first the change is reconstructed and then its legitimacy is measured. When one of the tests fails, the change becomes legally suspect, and when both fail, the hypothesis strongly approaches a supply defect, an abusive practice, a breach of trust or an architecture of structural opacity.

Where it comes from

On its own, open auditability does not resolve the whole dispute, because demonstrating what changed and how it changed does not exhaust the question of the material legitimacy of the change.p. 647

PTSozinha, a auditabilidade aberta não resolve toda a controvérsia, porque demonstrar o que mudou e como mudou não esgota a pergunta sobre a legitimidade material da mudança.

One standard controls the how of the mutation; the other, the how much and the what of the mutation.p. 638

PTUm standard controla o como da mutação; o outro, o quanto e o que da mutação.

First, was there minimum institutional verifiability of the change, with reasoning, timing, effects and reconstruction trails of the supply? Second, did the change preserve sufficient functional equivalence with the legitimately acquired enjoyment, or did it significantly lower the practical and symbolic core of the object?p. 639

PTPrimeiro, houve verificabilidade institucional mínima da mudança, com motivação, temporalidade, efeitos e trilhas de reconstrução do fornecimento? Segundo, a alteração preservou equivalência funcional suficiente com a fruição legitimamente adquirida, ou rebaixou de modo relevante o núcleo prático e simbólico do objeto?

First, the change is reconstructed. Then, its material legitimacy is measured. This order matters. Without it, the supplier will always have the advantage of the fog.p. 647

PTPrimeiro, reconstrói-se a mudança. Depois, mede-se sua legitimidade material. Essa ordem importa. Sem ela, o fornecedor sempre terá a vantagem da névoa.

When one of these conditions fails, the legitimacy of the change already becomes legally suspect. When both fail at the same time, the hypothesis strongly approaches a supply defect, an abusive practice, a breach of trust or even an architecture of structural opacity incompatible with the consumer regime.p. 638-639

PTQuando uma dessas condições falha, a legitimidade da mudança já se torna juridicamente suspeita. Quando ambas falham ao mesmo tempo, a hipótese se aproxima com força de vício de fornecimento, prática abusiva, quebra da confiança ou até de arquitetura de opacidade estrutural incompatível com o regime consumerista.

Isn't auditing the same as opening the trade secret?

The first is the objection of secrecy, according to which requiring auditability would amount to opening the code, handing over the trade secret and leaving the software unprotected. The answer is that the legal order already lives with limited forms of scrutiny that preserve the informational asset, such as judicial secrecy, restricted expert access, delimited functional analysis and institutional control. Denis Borges Barbosa records that judicial secrecy restricts third-party access to sensitive information, such as source code, and prevents its use by the opposing party for purposes foreign to the dispute. The reconciliation between protecting the asset and the need for verification, therefore, is nothing new.

The protection of software does not turn it into a sacred black box.p. 643

PTA proteção do software não o transforma em caixa-preta sagrada.

Where it comes from

This point is important because the business objection usually comes in predictable garb: requiring auditability would amount to requiring the opening of code, the handing over of the trade secret, the exposure of know-how or the unprotection of the software.p. 643

PTEsse ponto é importante porque a objeção empresarial costuma vir com roupagem previsível: exigir auditabilidade equivaleria a exigir abertura de código, entrega do segredo industrial, exposição de know-how ou desproteção do software.

Judicial secrecy, restricted expert access, delimited functional analysis, technically necessary use for certain purposes and institutional control are not demolitions of exclusivity.p. 643

PTSegredo de justiça, acesso pericial restrito, análise funcional delimitada, utilização tecnicamente necessária para certos fins e controle institucional não são demolições da exclusividade.

The legal discipline itself provides for judicial secrecy, which restricts third-party access to sensitive information, such as source code, and prevents its use by the opposing party for purposes foreign to the dispute Barbosa(Barbosa, 2017, p. 1940).p. 643

PTA própria disciplina jurídica prevê segredo de justiça, o que restringe o acesso de terceiros a informações sensíveis, como o código-fonte, e impede seu uso pela parte contrária para fins estranhos ao litígio (Barbosa, 2017, p. 1940) .

The secret also has to be shown in order to be protected. Generically invoking secrecy or technical sensitivity, without delimiting what needs to remain reserved, is not enough, and Barbosa observes that, for there to be judicial control, it is necessary to identify what the secret is, because the broad assertion that it relates to the company's field of activity does not allow such control. The protection remains legitimate, but what interests Consumer Law is not, as a rule, the internal ingenuity of the program, but how a certain operating regime, a certain change or a certain criterion affected enjoyment. The examination falls on the functioning for purposes of control, and not on the parasitic appropriation of the asset. When technical decisions condition permanence in the service on invasive requirements, the need for the platform not to hide behind technical opacity and trade secrets is reinforced.

The company can still protect its secret. What it cannot do is want secrecy over the very impact of the change it imposed on the consumer.p. 644

PTA empresa continua podendo proteger seu segredo. O que ela não pode é querer segredo sobre o próprio impacto da mudança que impôs ao consumidor.

Where it comes from

Generically invoking “secrecy” or “technical sensitivity” without delimiting what actually needs to remain reserved and without admitting any form of institutionally relevant reconstruction is not enough.p. 644

PTInvocar genericamente “segredo” ou “sensibilidade técnica” sem delimitar o que de fato precisa permanecer reservado e sem admitir qualquer forma de reconstrução institucionalmente relevante não basta.

For confidentiality to be legally respected, it needs to be identified and carved out, not treated as a magic fog that dissolves every requirement of accountability.p. 644

PTPara que o sigilo seja juridicamente respeitado, ele precisa ser identificado e recortado, não tratado como névoa mágica que dissolve toda exigência de prestação de contas.

For the hypothesis to be submitted to judicial control, “it is necessary to unveil and identify what secret would have been disclosed”, it not being enough to allege, broadly, that it is a secret related to the company's field of activity, because such a sweeping assertion does not allow effective judicial control of the dispute Barbosa(Barbosa, 2018, p. 392).p. 644

PTPara que a hipótese seja submetida ao controle judicial, “é necessário que se desvende e identifique qual o segredo que teria sido divulgado”, não bastando alegar, de modo amplo, que se trata de segredo relacionado à área de atuação da empresa, porque afirmação assim abrangente não permite efetivo controle judicial da controvérsia (Barbosa, 2018, p. 392) .

The legally relevant examination falls on the functioning for purposes of control, not on the parasitic appropriation of the asset.p. 644

PTO exame juridicamente relevante incide sobre o funcionamento para fins de controle, não sobre a apropriação parasitária do ativo.

When technical decisions and internal policies expand the extraterritoriality of code, conditioning permanence and participation on invasive requirements or on control mechanisms that exceed the perimeter of the contracted object, the claim to practical sovereignty over the consumer and over his material means becomes evident, which reinforces the need for open auditability as a minimum requirement so that the platform does not hide behind technical opacity and trade secrets, and so that governance by architecture is treated as imputable conduct, contestable and subject to proportionality.p. 499

PTQuando decisões técnicas e políticas internas ampliam a extraterritorialidade do código, condicionando permanência e participação a exigências invasivas ou a mecanismos de controle que excedem o perímetro do objeto contratado, evidencia-se a pretensão de soberania prática sobre o consumidor e sobre seus meios materiais, o que reforça a necessidade de auditabilidade aberta como exigência mínima para que a plataforma não se oculte atrás de opacidade técnica e de segredo industrial, e para que a governança por arquitetura seja tratada como conduta imputável, contestável e sujeita a proporcionalidade.

The second is the objection of technical complexity. Without the parameter, the supplier can always claim that it changed for technical reasons, that it informed as best it could, that the impact was small, that the change was provided for in some document and that the consumer simply did not understand or did not like it. With it, it becomes possible to ask what was changed, on what basis, with what effect and with what reconstruction trails. The generic invocation of internal policy, security, service improvement or ecosystem integrity does not work as a self-sufficient justification, and legitimate complexity is not to be confused with obscurity used as a technique of power.

In continuous consumer relationships, complexity does not revoke good faith.p. 647

PTEm relações de consumo contínuo, a complexidade não revoga a boa-fé.

Where it comes from

Without this standard, the supplier can always claim that it changed for technical reasons, that it informed as best it could, that the impact was small, that the change was provided for in some document and that the consumer simply did not understand or did not like it.p. 645

PTSem esse standard, o fornecedor pode sempre alegar que mudou por razões técnicas, que informou como pôde, que o impacto foi pequeno, que a alteração estava prevista em algum documento e que o consumidor apenas não entendeu ou não gostou.

On the other, it prevents the generic invocation of “internal policy”, “security”, “service improvement” or “ecosystem integrity” from working as a self-sufficient justification immune to control, emptying duties of information, loyalty and adequacy in continued performance.p. 473

PTDe outro, impede que a invocação genérica de “política interna”, “segurança”, “melhoria do serviço” ou “integridade do ecossistema” funcione como justificativa autossuficiente e imune a controle, esvaziando deveres de informação, lealdade e adequação na execução continuada.

In governed environments, this is what separates legitimate complexity from obscurity functionalized as a technique of power.p. 728

PTEm ambientes governados, é isso que separa a complexidade legítima da obscuridade funcionalizada como técnica de poder.

The third is that the requirement would multiply technical litigation. The opposite occurs, because the parameter has a containment function. With preserved trails, identifiable versions and statable reasons, the endless war of opaque technicalities is avoided, in which each side speaks a different language and the decision ends up handed over to intuitive deference to the more organized party. Expert examinations, production of documents and clarification orders come to fall on knowable objects, and the dispute is delimited.

Where it comes from

Open auditability also performs a function of containing litigation.p. 728

PTA auditabilidade aberta também desempenha função de contenção do litígio.

When there are preserved trails, identifiable relevant versions, minimally statable reasons for change and basic elements for reconstructing the conflict, the judicial discussion is prevented from turning into an endless war of opaque technicalities, in which each side speaks a different language and the decision ends up handed over to intuitive deference to the more organized party.p. 728

PTQuando há trilhas preservadas, versões relevantes identificáveis, motivos de alteração minimamente enunciáveis e elementos básicos de reconstrução do conflito, evita-se que a discussão judicial se converta em guerra infinita de tecnicidades opacas, na qual cada lado fala idioma diverso e a decisão acaba entregue à deferência intuitiva à parte mais organizada.

The fourth is that the parameter invents a right or stifles innovation. As to invention, besides the rooting in the duty to inform, there is the support of Karl Larenz, who describes the legal system as open and, to a certain degree, mobile, so that the two operators do not replace the system and allow it to see through controllable criteria what abbreviated formulas tend to erase. As to innovation, the answer is that auditability is a criterion of legitimacy of platform architectures, and not hostility toward them. Platforms may innovate, update, fix, rebalance, migrate and protect their technical assets. What they cannot do is do so under a regime of institutional irreconstructibility.

Where it comes from

Larenz describes the system as an “‘open’ system and, to a certain degree, a ‘mobile’ one”, always incomplete and continually put into question, whose function is to make clear the “intrinsic rationality”, the guiding values and the principles of Law Larenz(Larenz, 1997, p. 241).p. 481

PTLarenz descreve o sistema como “sistema ‘aberto’ e, até um certo grau, ‘móvel’”, sempre incompleto e continuamente posto em questão, cuja função é tornar clara a “racionalidade intrínseca”, os valores diretivos e os princípios do Direito (Larenz, 1997, p. 241) .

Open auditability and functional value equivalence occupy this place: they do not replace the system; they allow it to see, through controllable criteria, what abbreviated formulas tend to erase.p. 481

PTAuditabilidade aberta e equivalência de valor funcional ocupam esse lugar: não substituem o sistema; permitem que ele enxergue, por critérios controláveis, aquilo que fórmulas abreviadas tendem a apagar.

For this reason, open auditability must be understood as a criterion of legitimacy of platform architectures, and not as abstract hostility toward them. Platforms may innovate, update, fix, rebalance, migrate and protect their technical assets. What they cannot do is do so under a regime of institutional irreconstructibility, as if the very complexity of the system authorized them to decide alone what matters, what was changed and what may or may not be known by those who enjoy the service and by those who control it legally.p. 647

PTPor isso, a auditabilidade aberta deve ser compreendida como critério de legitimidade das arquiteturas de plataforma, e não como sua hostilidade abstrata. Plataformas podem inovar, atualizar, corrigir, reequilibrar, migrar e proteger seus ativos técnicos. O que não podem é fazê-lo em regime de irreconstrutibilidade institucional, como se a própria complexidade do sistema as autorizasse a decidir sozinhas o que importa, o que foi alterado e o que pode ou não ser conhecido por quem frui o serviço e por quem o controla juridicamente.

What does open auditability not reach?

The name calls for a careful reading. Open auditability does not require total transparency, unrestricted opening of the infrastructure, exposure of source code, the full architecture of the system or sensitive trade secrets, nor complete opening of proprietary models and metrics. Nor does it require absolute explainability or technical omniscience of the judge, and the platform remains the holder of its intangible assets and of its internal architecture. Whoever frames the claim as a full search of the system asks for what the parameter does not give.

Where it comes from

The name needs to be read carefully so as not to produce misunderstanding. It is not a matter of requiring the opening of code, a total search of the infrastructure, full publicity of internal systems or theatrical laboratory transparency.p. 636

PTO nome precisa ser lido com cuidado para não produzir mal-entendido. Não se trata de exigir abertura de código, devassa total da infraestrutura, publicidade integral dos sistemas internos ou transparência teatral de laboratório.

We are not faced with a requirement of total transparency, nor with unrestricted opening of the technical infrastructure, nor with an obligation to expose source code, the full architecture of the system or sensitive trade secrets.p. 640

PTNão se está diante de exigência de transparência total, nem de abertura irrestrita da infraestrutura técnica, nem de obrigação de expor código-fonte, arquitetura integral do sistema ou segredos industriais sensíveis.

Its focus is not to produce full transparency of the technical-organizational environment nor to impose complete opening of models, source code, proprietary metrics or trade secrets.p. 472

PTO seu foco não é produzir transparência integral do ambiente técnico-organizacional nem impor abertura completa de modelos, códigos-fonte, métricas proprietárias ou segredos industriais.

The parameter is calibrated precisely to make control possible without requiring technical omniscience of the judge or disorganizing the economic functioning of the ecosystem.p. 728

PTO parâmetro é calibrado exatamente para tornar o controle possível sem exigir onisciência técnica do julgador nem desorganizar o funcionamento econômico do ecossistema.

The platform remains the holder of its intangible assets and of its internal architecture, but can no longer invoke them to prevent, completely, the institutional reconstruction of events that affect the consumer's enjoyment in a legally relevant way.p. 729

PTA plataforma continua titular de seus ativos intangíveis e de sua arquitetura interna, mas já não pode invocá-los para impedir, por completo, a reconstrução institucional de eventos que afetem de modo juridicamente relevante a fruição do consumidor.

As to the changes reached, the parameter leaves out the microscopic adjustment, the cosmetic change and the irrelevant fluctuation, and does not turn each update into litigation. Not every change is unlawful, and not every improvement becomes a problem, because updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility. As to the effects, the lack of auditability makes the change legally suspect, and the inadequacy of transparency only reaches structural abusiveness depending on the intensity of the case. What the parameter delivers is a basis for reconstruction, without dispensing with the material examination of equivalence.

Where it comes from

This problem does not turn every improvement into an unlawful act. Updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility in a competitive ecosystem.p. 470

PTEsse problema não transforma toda melhoria em ilícito. Atualizações podem ser necessárias para compatibilidade, segurança, estabilidade e legibilidade em um ecossistema competitivo.

The section, thus, does not turn every change into an unlawful act. It merely refuses that the change become an automatic alibi for irresponsibility.p. 639

PTA seção, assim, não transforma toda alteração em ilícito. Ela apenas recusa que a alteração se torne álibi automático para irresponsabilidade.

What remains, after these cuts, is a minimum that the supplier cannot refuse without consequence. Business secrecy, economic exclusivity and the complexity of the service cease to operate as absolute vetoes and become elements to be reconciled with duties of minimum verifiability. Opacity ceases to be a zone naturally immune to protection and becomes a legal problem treatable by preservation orders, delimited production of documents, restricted expert examination and verifiable reasoning. The mutability of the service remains possible, without standing above legal control, and private governance ceases to approach a practical sovereignty.

Technical opacity, by itself, does not work as a free zone of liability.p. 481

PTA opacidade técnica, por si, não funciona como zona franca de responsabilidade.

Where it comes from

Business secrecy, economic exclusivity and the complexity of the service cease to operate as absolute vetoes on control and become elements to be reconciled with duties of minimum verifiability.p. 728-729

PTSegredo empresarial, exclusividade econômica e complexidade do serviço deixam de operar como vetos absolutos ao controle e passam a ser elementos a compatibilizar com deveres de verificabilidade mínima.

Structural opacity thus ceases to be a zone naturally immune to protection and becomes a legal problem treatable by preservation orders, delimited production of documents, restricted expert examination, verifiable reasoning and other instruments compatible with the procedural and administrative rationality of the protective system.p. 729

PTA opacidade estrutural deixa, assim, de ser zona naturalmente imune à tutela e passa a ser problema jurídico tratável por ordens de preservação, exibição delimitada, perícia restrita, motivação verificável e outros instrumentos compatíveis com a racionalidade processual e administrativa do sistema protetivo.

Without this standard, private governance comes too close to practical sovereignty. With it, the mutability of the service remains possible, but no longer hovers above legal control.p. 648

PTSem esse standard, a governança privada se aproxima demais de soberania prática. Com ele, a mutabilidade do serviço permanece possível, mas já não paira acima do controle jurídico.
