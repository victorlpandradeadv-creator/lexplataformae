Situations

For handling cases · 7.1.4 · p. 752 to 755

Limits on the platform's powerOn digital citizenship and limits on the private power of platforms

Supply may extend to practical conditions of participation and permanence. The regime limits this power without equating the platform with the State.

Read in the full text

How far does the platform's private power go?

Supply may bear upon practical conditions of participation, visibility, reputation, permanence and contestation. With long-lasting objects, unilateral sanctions, erasures and degradations may reach biographical continuity, relational insertion and symbolic identity, beyond what is patrimonial. This does not turn every digital discomfort into an identity drama.

Where it comes from

It increasingly bears upon practical conditions of participation, visibility, reputation, permanence and contestation in environments that structure relevant portions of contemporary social life.p. 752

PTEle incide, cada vez mais, sobre condições práticas de participação, visibilidade, reputação, permanência e contestação em ambientes que estruturam parcelas relevantes da vida social contemporânea.

The point is not to inflate every digital discomfort into an identity drama.p. 753

PTNão se trata de inflacionar qualquer desconforto digital à condição de drama identitário.

Is the platform equated with the State?

No. Functional public relevance does not make the platform equivalent to the State, and the CDC does not become a parallel constitution of digital life. But the platform is not an institutionally neutral supplier either. The legal order already has frameworks for limiting it, such as the Constitution and the CDC, reconciled with the social function of property and with intellectual property.

Where it comes from

When private powers administer access, permanence, visibility, reputation and contestation in environments structurally relevant to social participation, the legal order already has legal frameworks of limitation.p. 753

PTQuando poderes privados administram acesso, permanência, visibilidade, reputação e contestação em ambientes estruturalmente relevantes para participação social, o ordenamento já dispõe de matrizes jurídicas de limitação.

Functional public relevance does not make the platform equivalent to the State, but neither does it authorize treating the platform as an institutionally neutral supplier.p. 753

PTRelevância pública funcional não produz equiparação estatal, mas tampouco autoriza tratar a plataforma como fornecedor institucionalmente neutro.

The point is not to equate the platform with the State, nor to turn the CDC into a parallel constitution of digital life.p. 753

PTNão se trata de equiparar plataforma a Estado, nem de converter o CDC em constituição paralela da vida digital.

What should be made of the platform's self-descriptions?

The platform usually presents itself as a mere intermediary, a neutral technology or a global provider governed by its own terms. These framings move the discussion away from imputation, and the regime does not accept them as a sufficient criterion. What matters is how it organizes access, permanence, visibility, contestation and enjoyment.

The intermediary will say that it merely connects. The neutral technology will say that it merely executes. The global provider will say that it merely operates according to its own universal terms.p. 754

PTO intermediário dirá que apenas conecta. A tecnologia neutra dirá que apenas executa. O prestador global dirá que apenas opera segundo seus próprios termos universais.

Where it comes from

What matters is not how the platform names itself, but how it organizes access, permanence, visibility, contestation and enjoyment.p. 754

PTO que importa não é como a plataforma se nomeia, mas como organiza acesso, permanência, visibilidade, contestação e fruição.

What changes when this is applied to the families of platforms?

Application becomes recognizable without depending on the cultural sensitivity of the reader or the judge. The argument then operates through criteria of supply, governance and limitation of private power, and not through emotional attachment to the social importance of the service. Even amid claims of neutrality, innovation, global reach or intermediation, the case returns to what matters, which is who organizes enjoyment, who administers intelligibility, who controls permanence and who defines contestation.

Where it comes from

The argument no longer needs emotional attachment to the social importance of a given service and instead operates through criteria of supply, governance and limitation of private power.p. 754

PTO argumento deixa de precisar de adesão emotiva à importância social de determinado serviço e passa a operar por critérios de fornecimento, governança e limitação do poder privado.

who organizes enjoyment, who administers its intelligibility, who controls its permanence and who defines the conditions of its contestation.p. 754

PTquem organiza a fruição, quem administra sua inteligibilidade, quem controla sua permanência e quem define as condições de sua contestação.

What is the role of digital citizenship?

It is a functional problem of participation under private governance, and not a celebratory category. It does not replace the regime. It helps explain why certain conflicts cannot be treated as low-density contractual annoyances when the platform administers points of entry, criteria of permanence, visibility and means of contestation.

Because when private power begins to govern participation as if it were weather, and not an institutionally controllable choice, the Law is already far too late.p. 755

PTPorque quando o poder privado começa a governar a participação como se fosse clima, e não escolha institucionalmente controlável, o Direito já está atrasado demais.

Where it comes from

It helps illuminate why certain conflicts of governed enjoyment cannot be treated as mere low-density contractual annoyances.p. 754

PTEla ajuda a iluminar por que certos conflitos de fruição governada não podem ser tratados como meros aborrecimentos contratuais de baixa densidade.

It appears as a functional problem of participation under private governance.p. 754

PTEla comparece como problema funcional de participação sob governança privada.

Digital citizenship, in this context, does not replace the regime of the IOC.p. 754

PTA cidadania digital, nesse contexto, não substitui o regime do OIC.
