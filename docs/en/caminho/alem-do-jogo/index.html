Does this only happen in games?

Maybe you do not even play. The same applies to the artificial intelligence assistant you use to study or work and to the online store where you shop. It applies to the app that hands out rides and deliveries and to the friendly customer service that makes your complaint seem like an overreaction.

The game was the starting point because everything shows up more clearly there. The account, the currency, the penalties and the changes are in plain view for any player. Outside the game, the same effects usually come wrapped as something inevitable, and so they are harder to see.

Where it comes from

The movement of consolidation and of passage to the procedure of dogmatic reconstruction shows, with special clarity, how the interactive entertainment industry, by reason of the continuous provision that characterizes it, operates as an ostensive surface of exposure of the Lex Plataformaep. 497

PTO movimento de consolidação e de passagem ao procedimento de reconstrução dogmática evidencia, com especial clareza, como a indústria do entretenimento interativo, em razão da prestação contínua que a caracteriza, opera como superfície ostensiva de exposição da Lex Plataformae

The difference is that, outside the game, the same effects tend to be presented as inevitable, and not as architectural choicesp. 23

PTA diferença é que, fora do jogo, os mesmos efeitos tendem a ser apresentados como inevitáveis, e não como escolhas de arquitetura

These traits manifest themselves, with particular clarity, in three digital business models: games operated as a service, marketplaces and artificial intelligence services provided by platform.p. 486

PTEsses traços se manifestam, com particular nitidez, em três modelos de negócios digitais: jogos operados como serviço, marketplaces e serviços de inteligência artificial fornecidos por plataforma.

The focus on electronic game platforms serves a methodological reason, and not a thematic preference. These ecosystems tend to concentrate, in high resolution, the defining elements of the IOC regimep. 484

PTO recorte por plataformas de jogos eletrônicos atende a razão metodológica, e não a preferência temática. Esses ecossistemas tendem a concentrar, em alta resolução, elementos definidores do regime do OIC

What unites these cases is not the sector of the economy or the type of technology. It is the way the platform governs use, manages what you expect, controls what can be understood and makes contesting easier or harder.

That is why it is not enough to look at the name of the service, whether it presents itself as an app, a store, a network or an assistant. What matters is the main mechanism by which your experience is organized, adjusted, remade and made contestable or not. It is this mechanism, and not the sector label, that makes it possible to compare cases that look so different at first sight.

Where it comes from

The common point is not on the surface of the environment, but in the way in which enjoyment is governed, expectation is managed, intelligibility is modulated and contestation is made more or less practicable by private design.p. 787

PTO ponto comum não está na superfície do ambiente, mas na forma pela qual a fruição é governada, a expectativa é administrada, a inteligibilidade é modulada e a contestação é tornada mais ou menos praticável por desenho privado.

The unity of the examination lies not in the economic branch or in the marketing label of the service, but in the predominant mechanism by which the user's experience is organized, modulated, reconfigured or made contestable.p. 755

PTA unidade do exame não está no ramo econômico nem na etiqueta mercadológica do serviço, mas no mecanismo predominante pelo qual a experiência do usuário é organizada, modulada, reconfigurada ou tornada contestável.

And when the assistant you use changes its behavior?

An artificial intelligence assistant does not deliver only loose answers. It organizes how you understand a problem, what you trust and how you decide. You bring what it says into your work, review it, correct it and ask again.

That is why the problem is not limited to a wrong answer here and there. What matters is the trust that sustains the use and the possibility of reconstructing what happened. If the quality of the service changes, the question is the same as with the skin. What was promised, what changed and with what effect on those who depended on it?

Where it comes from

What is offered is an environment of cognitive orientation.p. 757

PTO que se oferece é ambiente de orientação cognitiva.

It is a functional infrastructure that organizes how the user understands the problem submitted, trusts the answer received, incorporates it into their decision-making, reviews it, corrects it or reassesses it in the face of error, fluctuation or change in performance.p. 757

PTTrata-se de infraestrutura funcional que organiza como o usuário compreende o problema submetido, confia na resposta recebida, a incorpora à sua tomada de decisão, a revê, a corrige ou a reavalia diante de erro, oscilação ou mudança de desempenho.

The first is to treat the conflict as a mere discussion about an isolated factual errorp. 757

PTA primeira é tratar o conflito como mera discussão sobre erro factual isolado

the institutional possibility of reconstructing the link between promise, delivery, alteration and impactp. 757

PTa possibilidade institucional de reconstruir o nexo entre promessa, entrega, alteração e impacto

Who decides what you see in an online store?

An online store usually says it only brings buyers and sellers together, like a neutral square. In practice, it chooses what appears first, what stays hidden, who seems trustworthy, what is comparable and what takes a while to show up.

In other words, you do not see the market. You see the market through the platform's filters. Rankings, reputation, featuring and recommendation are not details of the shop window, because they are part of what it delivers and weigh on your expectation, on the risk you perceive and on your choice.

Where it comes from

Platforms of this nature do not merely connect. They select, rank, highlight, hide, bring closer, delay, prioritize and reorganize the way in which the reality of the environment becomes perceptible to the user.p. 761

PTPlataformas dessa natureza não apenas conectam. Elas selecionam, hierarquizam, destacam, ocultam, aproximam, retardam, priorizam e reorganizam a forma pela qual a realidade do ambiente se torna perceptível ao usuário.

In their formal language, they often appear as neutral meeting spaces between seller and buyerp. 761

PTEm sua linguagem formal, elas frequentemente aparecem como espaços neutros de encontro entre vendedor e comprador

Rankings, reputation, featuring, recommendation, prioritization and modulation of reach are not peripheral elements. They are part of the functional utility of the ecosystem and directly influence the formation of expectation, the assessment of risk and the possibility of rational choice.p. 761

PTRankings, reputação, destaque, recomendação, priorização e modulação de alcance não são elementos periféricos. Integram a utilidade funcional do ecossistema e influenciam diretamente a formação da expectativa, a avaliação do risco e a possibilidade de escolha racional.

Who is in charge of work done through an app?

Delivery and ride apps call drivers and couriers independent partners. But the one that hands out the calls, gives the rating, decides who has priority, sets the rules for accepting rides and applies the penalties is the app.

For those who work this way, the app provides more than a payment per task. It provides the very environment in which the work becomes possible, and that environment is governed by it. That is why it is not enough to look at what the contract says about autonomy. You have to look at who actually organizes access to demand and staying in the flow.

Where it comes from

The platform organizes access to demand, distribution of calls, minimum predictability of pay, permanence in the productive flow, reputational criteria, prioritization patterns, acceptance rules and sanction mechanisms.p. 765

PTA plataforma organiza acesso à demanda, distribuição de chamadas, previsibilidade mínima de remuneração, permanência no fluxo produtivo, critérios reputacionais, padrões de priorização, regras de aceitação e mecanismos de sanção.

What is provided, in functional terms, is a private infrastructure of economic possibility.p. 765

PTO que se fornece, em termos funcionais, é uma infraestrutura privada de possibilidade econômica.

The instruments deployed by iFood, 99Food, Uber and 99 insist, with variations in language, on characterizing the partners as independentp. 765

PTOs instrumentos mobilizados por iFood, 99Food, Uber e 99 insistem, com variações de linguagem, em caracterizar os parceiros como independentes

This point is important because it prevents the analysis from being captured by excessively formal narratives about contractual autonomy.p. 765

PTEsse ponto é importante porque impede que a análise seja capturada por narrativas excessivamente formais sobre autonomia contratual.

Why does complaining seem like an overreaction?

And there is the tone. A friendly screen, kind replies, a caring manner and close, almost friend-like language can make the loss seem acceptable and the complaint seem like an overreaction, even before any dispute begins.

In many cases the conflict is managed before it exists. The platform does not take care only of your initial sign-up. It also takes care of how much harm you tolerate and how much energy you have to react. Frustration is gradually absorbed as part of the normal, and the complaint never gets made.

Where it comes from

In many cases, it is governed before that.p. 780

PTEm muitos casos, ele é governado antes disso.

The cordial interface, the assistive tone, the diffuse promise of care, merely performative transparency, the appearance of communicational horizontality and the calculated personalization of language materially make up the environment of enjoyment.p. 781

PTA interface cordial, o tom assistivo, a promessa difusa de cuidado, a transparência apenas performática, a aparência de horizontalidade comunicacional e a personalização calculada da linguagem compõem materialmente o ambiente de fruição.

They function as a technique for governing perception, organizing the threshold from which the loss will be perceived as acceptable, the abuse will be read as a minor failure and contestation will be perceived as unnecessary, premature or disproportionate.p. 781

PTFuncionam como técnica de governo da percepção, organizando o limiar a partir do qual a perda será percebida como aceitável, o abuso será lido como falha menor e a contestação será percebida como desnecessária, prematura ou desproporcional.

The platform, in this context, does not manage only initial adhesion. It manages the user's tolerance of harm and energy to react.p. 781

PTA plataforma, nesse contexto, não administra apenas adesão inicial. Administra a tolerância ao dano e a energia de reação do usuário.

Does every platform have the same problem?

Showing the mechanism is not condemning the service. Not every platform reproduces the problem, and those that do so do not do it with the same force. Nor does every relationship mediated by technology fall under consumer protection.

The question is always the same, anywhere. Can you know what was promised and what changed? And can you react?

Where it comes from

The point here is not to gather digital examples under a single heading out of mere technological affinity, nor to suggest that every contemporary platform reproduces, with identical intensity, the same form of vulnerability, opacity or capture of enjoymentp. 755

PTO ponto, aqui, não é reunir exemplos digitais sob uma mesma rubrica por mera afinidade tecnológica, nem sugerir que toda plataforma contemporânea reproduz, com idêntica intensidade, a mesma forma de vulnerabilidade, opacidade ou captura da fruição

Not every relationship economically mediated by technology will justify application of the CDC.p. 780

PTNem toda relação economicamente mediada por tecnologia justificará incidência do CDC.

This is not to say that every digital mediation automatically turns any good into an IOC.p. 487

PTNão se afirma, com isso, que toda mediação digital converta automaticamente qualquer bem em OIC.
