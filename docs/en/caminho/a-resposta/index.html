And when it goes wrong?

Let's say your skin lost what it had. Or that your account was closed for a reason you cannot understand. The first problem that shows up is proof.

The system records, the old versions of the screens, the criteria used to punish, the history of changes and the internal support conversations are all on the other side. In conflicts of this kind, this is the normal situation, and not an exception. When the proof arises and stays under the platform's control, the proceedings cannot pretend that both parties stand on an equal footing.

Where it comes from

In disputes over IOCs, this is precisely the normal configuration: logs, interface trails, change histories, sanction criteria, internal support communications and relevant technical records are concentrated with the supplier.p. 659

PTEm litígios sobre OICs, essa é precisamente a configuração normal: logs, trilhas de interface, históricos de alteração, critérios de sanção, comunicações internas de suporte e registros técnicos relevantes ficam concentrados no fornecedor.

When the proof arises and remains under the platform's control, the procedural system cannot pretend neutrality.p. 659

PTQuando a prova nasce e permanece sob controle da plataforma, o sistema processual não pode fingir neutralidade.

What can you yourself keep as proof?

What is within your reach is more than it seems. Dated screenshots, screen recordings, the purchase and confirmation screens and the messages about rarity, time remaining, discount, event or change show how the environment was put together. It also helps to keep what was promised, like ads, posts and notices, to compare with what you received later.

The difference is big. You stop saying only that you did not understand and start showing that the environment was designed in a certain way. What counts is not your memory, but the shape the platform gave to the path you traveled.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, time remaining, discount, event or changep. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração

What is relevant there is not the user's subjective memory, but the architecture actually presented. The claim ceases to be “I did not understand” and becomes “the environment was designed this way”.p. 607

PTO relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário, mas a arquitetura efetivamente apresentada. A alegação deixa de ser “eu não entendi” e passa a ser “o ambiente foi desenhado desta forma”.

The advertising and institutional promise is compared with the enjoyment actually delivered and with its later changes.p. 607

PTCompara-se a promessa publicitária e institucional com a fruição efetivamente entregue e com suas alterações posteriores.

And when all the proof is with the company?

And the law already has tools for the rest. One of them is the reversal of the burden of proof. Normally, whoever alleges a fact needs to prove it. In a consumer relationship, the judge can shift this weight in favor of the consumer. And, when the service is very technical and the supplier controls everything, this reversal is often the only way for protection to work.

There are others. The judge can order the platform to keep the evidence and produce documents. And the judge can divide the work of proving in another way when one party has great difficulty and the other has great ease in producing the proof.

None of this is automatic. Some argue that the reversal should always apply, for every digital relationship, but today it depends on the judge's assessment in the case. That is why you still need to tell a coherent story that seems true, and make clear what you are disputing. What changes is that you cannot be required to give detailed proof of something that was never in your hands. And, to begin with, you do not need the game's entire code. A minimum is enough, like comparing the before and after and showing what was affected.

Where it comes from

The technological specialization of the service and the dominant position of the supplier make the reversal of the burden of proof, in many cases, the only instrument capable of effectively enabling consumer protectionp. 583

PTA especialização tecnológica do serviço e a posição dominante do fornecedor fazem da inversão do ônus da prova, em muitos casos, o único instrumento capaz de viabilizar efetivamente a tutela do consumidor

The third mechanism is properly procedural: preservation of evidence, production of documents and dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof.p. 659

PTO terceiro mecanismo é propriamente processual: preservação de evidências, exibição de documentos e redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova.

Art. 373, § 1, allows a different distribution of the burden of proof when there is excessive difficulty for one party to discharge the burden or greater ease for the other to produce proof of the contrary factp. 659

PTO art. 373, § 1º, permite a distribuição diversa do ônus probatório quando houver excessiva dificuldade para uma parte cumprir o encargo ou maior facilidade da outra em produzir a prova do fato contrário

[...] which is why digital vulnerability and the corresponding hyposufficiency should be affirmed in a universal key, with reversal of the burden of proof ope legis, and not only ope judicisp. 593

PT[...] razão pela qual a vulnerabilidade digital e a hipossuficiência correspondente deveriam ser afirmadas em chave universal, com inversão do ônus da prova ope legis, e não apenas ope judicis

This does not eliminate the need for plausibility, for argumentative sequencing or for delimitation of the disputed object.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido.

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to require from the consumer a detailed demonstration of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The minimum signal of proof requires a functional comparison between before and after, examination of relevant versions, identification of the affected core of utility and assessment of the object's role in the experiencep. 672

PTO sinal mínimo de prova exige comparação funcional entre o antes e o depois, exame de versões relevantes, identificação do núcleo de utilidade afetado e avaliação do papel do objeto na experiência

Who pays when fraud goes through an official game function?

There is a case that shows how the risk can land in your lap. In an online game, sending gifts to friends was an official function, which the company itself offered inside the game, with rules and limits. At the same time, outside stores sold the game's currency as something normal, safe and advantageous, and nobody made clear what was allowed, tolerated or forbidden.

When card fraud appeared, the company switched off the gift function in Brazil for a long period, removed items received and blocked accounts or rolled them back to an earlier state. The measure also hit those who had received gifts in good faith, unaware of any fraud. And those who lost skins were left with the task of proving, item by item, where each one came from, with old statements, emails and screenshots they might have kept.

The case helps to understand three things. The company already knew the risk of fraud, and so this risk is part of its activity and cannot be treated as the fault of whoever received the gift. A revocable license in the contract is not enough to throw the system's failure onto you. And from this arise concrete requests, like returning what was removed when no individual proof rules out your good faith, keeping and showing the records and correcting the information about where to buy safely.

Where it comes from

On the contrary, Riot itself presented it as a regular functionality, integrated into the game client, subject to objective eligibility requirements, quantitative limits, regional restrictions and standardized sending flowsp. 610

PTAo contrário, a própria Riot a apresentava como funcionalidade regular, integrada ao client do jogo, submetida a requisitos objetivos de elegibilidade, limites quantitativos, restrições regionais e fluxos padronizados de envio

promotional materials and digital storefronts presented the external purchase of virtual currency as an ordinary, safe, advantageous practice compatible with the game's ecosystemp. 612

PTmateriais promocionais e vitrines digitais apresentavam a compra externa de moeda virtual como prática ordinária, segura, vantajosa e compatível com o ecossistema do jogo

The vulnerability does not stem from private naivety, but from the absence of robust communicational demarcation between what was admitted, tolerated, official or forbidden.p. 612

PTA vulnerabilidade não decorre de ingenuidade privada, mas da ausência de demarcação comunicacional robusta entre o que era admitido, tolerado, oficial ou proibido.

In this environment, it is not legally serious to presume that the average consumer would always distinguish, with sufficient clarity, a tolerated partner, legitimate resale, gray market and fraud linked to a cloned cardp. 612

PTNessa ambiência, não é juridicamente sério presumir que o consumidor médio sempre distinguiria, com clareza suficiente, parceiro tolerado, revenda legítima, mercado cinzento e fraude vinculada a cartão clonado

The company deactivated the gift functionality on the Brazilian server for a long period, suppressed assets received and carried out regressive restorations or blocks on affected accountsp. 611

PTA empresa desativou por longo período a funcionalidade de presentes no servidor brasileiro, suprimiu ativos recebidos e operou restaurações regressivas ou bloqueios em contas atingidas

[...] it suppressed assets, interrupted an official content circulation functionality and applied sanctions based on broad presumptions of co-responsibility, including on users who did not knowingly take part in the fraudp. 610

PT[...] suprimiu ativos, interrompeu funcionalidade oficial de circulação de conteúdo e operou sanções fundadas em presunções amplas de corresponsabilidade, inclusive sobre usuários que não participaram conscientemente da fraude

Once the assets are removed or the account state is reverted, the user comes to depend on old statements, scattered emails, past screenshots and reconstructed memory to prove that a given content legitimately formed part of their position of enjoymentp. 616

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição

The episode shows how the platform, by carrying out a broad reversion of the account state, transfers to the player the almost archaeological burden of reconstructing item by item what was already part of their sphere of enjoymentp. 616

PTO episódio mostra como a plataforma, ao operar reversão ampla do estado da conta, transfere ao jogador o encargo quase arqueológico de reconstituir item por item aquilo que já fazia parte de sua esfera de fruição

It was an institutionally recognized operational risk, with internal flows for investigation, refund and suspensionp. 610

PTTratava-se de risco operacional reconhecido institucionalmente, com fluxos internos de apuração, reembolso e suspensão

The risk comes to form part of the professional circle of the economic operation itself.p. 611

PTO risco passa a integrar o círculo profissional da própria operação econômica.

The central dogmatic point, however, is that this contractual vocabulary is not enough to convert the consumer into the legitimate recipient of systemic risk.p. 615

PTO ponto dogmático central, porém, é que esse vocabulário contratual não basta para converter o consumidor em destinatário legítimo do risco sistêmico.

The consumer regime admits, here, requests for functional restoration of the position of enjoyment, restoration of assets when the user's good faith has not been refuted by robust individualized proof, preservation and display of minimum records of the supply, informational correction about legitimate channels of acquisitionp. 617

PTO regime consumerista comporta, aqui, pedidos de recomposição funcional da posição de fruição, restauração de ativos quando a boa-fé do usuário não foi infirmada por prova individualizada robusta, preservação e exibição de registros mínimos do fornecimento, correção informacional sobre canais legítimos de aquisição

Functional restorationFunctional restoration

And what is asked for, in the end? Before money, that what was taken be returned. This may mean restoring your access, reviewing or undoing the punishment and giving back to the skin the function it had. It may also mean preserving your history, offering a substitute that plays the same role and correcting the procedure that caused the problem.

The reason is simple. When what you lost was the possibility of continuing to use something, compensation alone is usually a poor remedy. It puts a number in place of the loss, but it does not give back the account, the history or the place you had in the game.

Where it comes from

In cases of architectural injury, the legal response must favor functional restoration and procedural correction, instead of automatically resorting to monetization.p. 751

PTEm hipóteses de lesão arquitetural, a resposta jurídica deve privilegiar recomposição funcional e correção procedimental, em vez de recorrer automaticamente à monetização.

[...] the integrity of the experience, the permanence of access, the intelligibility of the bond or the consumer's relational position in the ecosystem, a simple monetary award tends to operate as a poor and structurally ineffective remedyp. 751

PT[...] a integridade da experiência, a permanência do acesso, a inteligibilidade do vínculo ou a posição relacional do consumidor no ecossistema, a simples condenação pecuniária tende a operar como remédio pobre e estruturalmente ineficaz

What is required, whenever possible, is restoration of access, reversal or review of sanctions, restoration of functionality, preservation of history, equivalent replacement and adjustment of governance procedures.p. 751

PTO que se exige, sempre que possível, é restauração de acesso, reversão ou revisão de sanções, recomposição de funcionalidade, preservação de histórico, substituição equivalente e ajuste de procedimentos de governança.

Does money leave the scene?

Money does not leave the scene. Compensation remains legitimate and often indispensable, whether to cover material losses or to recognize harms that are not only material. It just stops being the automatic answer, as if every loss of this kind could become a number on a spreadsheet, without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Monetary compensation remains, of course, a legitimate and in many cases indispensable remedy. The parameter formulated here does not eliminate it.p. 741

PTA indenização pecuniária permanece, evidentemente, como remédio legítimo e em muitos casos indispensável. O parâmetro aqui formulado não a elimina.

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when presentp. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes

What is rejected is its transformation into the automatic and preferred response in any casep. 742

PTO que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese

Regime correctionRegime correction

Sometimes your case is only a symptom. If the same design produces the same loss in series, fixing your case does not solve it, because tomorrow the loss happens to someone else, or to you again.

In these cases, the response needs to reach the source. This opens room for orders that prevent repetition, for changes in the structure of the service, for collective actions and for mandatory compliance mechanisms, linked to the duty to make changes verifiable. Without this, the law only puts a price on the harm and leaves intact the machine that produces it.

Where it comes from

Regime correction, on the contrary, makes it possible to treat the harm as architectural and not merely as an episode. With this, it becomes possible to justify injunctive relief, structural measures, collective measures and even mandatory compliance mechanisms compatible with open auditability and institutional control of enjoyment.p. 741

PTA correção de regime, ao contrário, permite tratar o dano como arquitetural e não apenas como episódio. Com isso, torna-se possível justificar tutela inibitória, medidas estruturais, providências coletivas e até mecanismos de compliance compulsório compatíveis com auditabilidade aberta e controle institucional da fruição.

The primacy of functional restoration and regime correction prevents consumer protection from limiting itself to pricing harms while keeping intact the machine that produces them.p. 742

PTA primazia da recomposição funcional e da correção de regime impede que a tutela consumerista se limite a precificar danos enquanto preserva intacta a máquina que os produz.

Architecture harmArchitecture harm

Sometimes many people lose in the same way because there is only one source. These are not several harms that happen to look alike. It is one harm that arises from the very design of the service and that, for this reason, has a unity that justifies treating it collectively, and not person by person.

Where it comes from

In platform environments, the harmful source may lie in the very design of the supply regime, so that the unity of the problem does not stem primarily from the empirical repetition of episodesp. 774

PTEm ambientes de plataforma, a fonte lesiva pode residir no próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, de modo que a unidade do problema não decorre primariamente da repetição empírica de episódios

It refers to an injury produced by the very design of the supply regime, and therefore endowed with sufficient functional unity to justify collective institutional treatment.p. 774

PTRefere-se a lesão produzida pelo próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, e por isso dotada de unidade funcional suficiente para justificar tratamento institucional coletivo.

LawConsumer by equivalence

And what about someone who bought nothing but depends on the same environment? That is the small seller who can only sell through a large platform's store. This seller is not a consumer in the ordinary sense, but is exposed to the commercial practices, the supply methods and the governance of the platform, in a fragile position.

The law allows this person to be protected as if they were a consumer. But not every participant falls under this protection. Only those who are truly exposed, in a situation of vulnerability that matters to the law.

Where it comes from

Once the applicability of art. 29 of the CDC1 is admitted to qualify legal entities in a factual position of vulnerability, the regime proves fully pertinent when the immaterial object in question is the enjoyment bundle in a sustained position exploited as a product in the B2B market by the holder and offered, through the marketplace platform, to the seller (vendedor)p. 434

PTAdmitida a aplicabilidade do art. 29 do CDC para qualificar pessoas jurídicas em posição factual de vulnerabilidade, o regime se mostra plenamente pertinente quando o objeto imaterial em questão é o feixe de fruição em posição sustentada explorado como produto no mercado B2B pela detentora e oferecido, por via da plataforma marketplace, ao seller (vendedor)

[...] although it does not fit comfortably into the traditional figure of the final acquirer as imagined in linear and simple relationships, it is exposed to the commercial practices, the supply methods and the private governance mechanisms of the environment in a condition of legally relevant vulnerabilityp. 779

PT[...] embora não se encaixe com conforto na figura tradicional do adquirente final tal como imaginada em relações lineares e simples, encontra-se exposto às práticas comerciais, aos métodos de fornecimento e aos mecanismos de governança privada do ambiente em condição de vulnerabilidade juridicamente relevante

Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTNem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

LawCompetition

The same design that holds you in can keep competitors away. When the way your experience is built also becomes an advantage for the company against others in the market, the problem stops being only the consumer's. It also becomes a competition problem, which affects whoever could offer an alternative.

Where it comes from

In this key, the IOC regime does not replace competition law. It provides an entry grammar for understanding when the architecture of the consumer's experience has already become, simultaneously, the architecture of competitive advantage.p. 771

PTNessa chave, o regime do OIC não substitui o direito concorrencial. Ele fornece gramática de entrada para compreender quando a arquitetura da experiência do consumidor já se tornou, simultaneamente, arquitetura da vantagem competitiva.

None of this becomes an automatic formula. The way only organizes questions. What form of platform governance is at stake? What duty was breached? What minimum proof can be required? How does the case qualify in law? And what response makes sense for it?

The rest is judgment, case by case, by whoever will decide. What these names do is make sure the right questions get asked.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

It organizes, in consultable language, the passage between governance structure, breached duty, minimum evidentiary requirement, legal qualification and possible institutional consequence.p. 748

PTEle organiza, em linguagem consultável, a passagem entre estrutura de governança, dever violado, exigência probatória mínima, qualificação jurídica e consequência institucional possível.

What can you demand?

The proof the platform holds has to come outThe proof the platform holds has to come out

When does the platform hold all the proof?

When almost everything that shows what happened is kept by the platform. The system records, your account history, the old versions of the screens and the reasons for each decision are on its side, and not on yours.

What must the platform do with the records?

Keep and show the minimum records needed to reconstruct what happened. Being open to checking is part of the legitimate way of supplying.

What can you show and what does the platform hold?

On your side, give a plausible account of what you went through, show how it fits into the game and gather what was within your reach. On the platform's side, present the records, histories, versions and technical justifications that matter for the case.

The more what happened depends on what only the platform records, the less reasonable it is to demand from you detailed proof of something that was never in your hands.

How should proof be shared according to access to the records?

The burden of proof should be shared according to who has access to it. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions in your favor, which make certain facts count as true until proven otherwise, and a heightened duty for the platform to cooperate with the proof.

Are you excused from telling what happened?

This does not excuse you from telling a coherent story that seems true and from clearly defining what you are asking for. What falls away is the fiction that you and the platform are on equal footing to prove things. Lack of access to the records cannot become a wall against you.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform concentrates the essential elements for narrating, demonstrating and reconstructing the conflict, the evidentiary discipline must recognize that verifiability is part of the very legitimate regime of supply.p. 747

PTSempre que a plataforma concentrar os elementos essenciais para narrar, demonstrar e reconstruir o conflito, a disciplina probatória deverá reconhecer que a verificabilidade integra o próprio regime legítimo do fornecimento.

The corresponding duty is to preserve and exhibit the minimum records necessary for reconstructing the disputed event.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de preservar e exibir registros mínimos necessários à reconstrução do evento controvertido.

The minimum required proof, from the consumer's point of view, consists of a plausible presentation of the harmful experience, of its insertion in the ecosystem and of the accessible contextual elementsp. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível, do ponto de vista do consumidor, consiste em apresentação plausível da experiência lesiva, de sua inserção no ecossistema e dos elementos de contexto acessíveis

from the supplier's point of view, the exhibition of trails, logs, histories, versions and technically delimited justifications is requiredp. 747

PTdo ponto de vista do fornecedor, exige-se exibição de trilhas, logs, históricos, versões e justificativas tecnicamente delimitadas

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to require from the consumer a detailed demonstration of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The possible legal qualification involves dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof, presumptions favorable to the consumer and reinforcement of the duty of cooperationp. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível envolve redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova, presunções favoráveis ao consumidor e reforço do dever de cooperação

This does not eliminate the need for plausibility, argumentative linkage or delimitation of the disputed object. What it eliminates is the fiction of symmetry in environments whose very structure operates through asymmetry of meaning and of proof.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido. O que elimina é a ficção de simetria em ambientes cuja própria estrutura opera pela assimetria de sentido e de prova.

The institutional consequence consists of preventing technical opacity from turning into procedural shieldingp. 747

PTA consequência institucional consiste em impedir que a opacidade técnica se converta em blindagem processual

The response must restore what you lostThe response must restore what you lost

When does the loss call for more than money?

When the problem affects your access, the continuity of what you use or the equivalence of what you bought. Or when it affects your history, your standing before other players or the weight that it had for you.

What response must the platform give?

Give a response that matches the kind of loss. If what you lost was a function, the response should aim at that function, and not only at a sum of money left over at the end.

How do you link the loss to what the platform did?

The link between the platform's decision or mechanism and the loss you suffered. And that it is possible, technically or institutionally, to restore, undo, replace with something equivalent or correct the procedure.

What solution can restore what was lost?

The priority is to recover what was lost, when that is feasible. The solution must match the loss shown and the possibilities of restoration. This may mean restoring access, undoing a penalty, preserving your history, replacing it with something that serves the same function, adjusting the rules and stopping the problem from continuing. When the harm reveals a pattern that repeats, it is also possible to ask for correction of the structure that produces it.

And compensation in money?

Money does not leave the conversation. Compensation remains possible, including for harms that are not only material, when restoring is not feasible or not enough. What is refused is treating it as an automatic answer to everything, as if every loss of this kind became a number without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving pecuniary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

The corresponding duty is to structure protection in accordance with the nature of the loss, aiming at the affected enjoyment and not only at its residual monetary translation.p. 748

PTO dever correspondente é o de estruturar a tutela em conformidade com a natureza da perda, mirando a fruição afetada e não apenas sua tradução monetária residual.

The minimum required proof involves demonstrating the link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The possible legal qualification varies according to the main incidence of the case, but the institutional consequence must favor restoration of access, reversal of sanctions, preservation of history, equivalent functional substitution, governance adjustments, injunctive protection and structural correction when the harm reveals a repeatable patternp. 748

PTA qualificação jurídica possível varia conforme a incidência principal do caso, mas a consequência institucional deve privilegiar restauração de acesso, reversão de sanções, preservação de histórico, substituição funcional equivalente, ajustes de governança, tutela inibitória e correção estrutural quando o dano revelar padrão repetível

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when present. What is refused is its transformation into an automatic and preferred response in any case, as if the injury to the IOC could always be adequately translated into a figure without consideration for the structure of the compromised enjoyment.p. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes. O que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese, como se a lesão ao OIC pudesse ser sempre adequadamente traduzida em cifra sem consideração pela estrutura da fruição comprometida.
