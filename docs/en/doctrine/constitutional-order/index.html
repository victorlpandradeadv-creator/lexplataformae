The jurist's path

Part one of two

The constitutional order and the sectoral framework

Platform governance is an exercise of private economic power over positions of continued enjoyment, and the jurist needs to know on what basis the legal order limits it without treating the company as the State or reducing the conflict to the contract. That basis comes from the economic Constitution, the horizontal effect of fundamental rights and the CDC, while the Legal Framework for Electronic Games and the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents help bring the sector into view without resolving the conflict of enjoyment on their own.

What power does the legal order need to see when the platform governs enjoyment?

In ecosystems of continued enjoyment, the conflict no longer fits the image of a contractual disagreement or of shifting preferences, because the value of the object depends on an administered environment in which visibility, reputation, access, permanence and sanction are distributed by internal criteria, mainly through architectural and informational means. This ordering has a name. The law of the platform weaves terms of use, community standards, interfaces, visibility hierarchies, enforcement systems, meaning the platform's own application of its rules, update routines, evidence regimes and customer service practices into a unitary practical normativity. It is not a metaphor to ornament the digital but an analytical category, whose core lies in the coordination of these mechanisms and in the effects they produce on who gains access, how they remain, what can be lost and on what terms the loss can be contested.

Where it comes from

The reorganization of immaterial consumption around governed ecosystems makes it insufficient to treat the conflict as a simple contractual disagreement or as a fluctuation of individual preferences. The enjoyment that sustains the value of the IOC depends on an administered environment in which visibility, reputation, access, permanence and sanction are distributed according to internal criteria, by predominantly architectural and informational means.p. 347

PTA reorganização do consumo imaterial em torno de ecossistemas governados torna insuficiente tratar o conflito como simples divergência contratual ou como oscilação de preferências individuais. A fruição que sustenta o valor do OIC depende de um ambiente administrado no qual visibilidade, reputação, acesso, permanência e sanção são distribuídos segundo critérios internos, por meios predominantemente arquiteturais e informacionais.

It is on this plane that it becomes necessary to name the Lex Plataformae. The expression designates the private ordering through which the platform structures and governs the enjoyment of the Immaterial Object of Consumption, articulating terms of use, community standards, interfaces, visibility hierarchies, enforcement systems, update routines, evidence regimes and customer service practices into a unitary practical normativity.p. 503

It is not a metaphor to ornament the digital, but an analytical category aimed at making legally visible what would otherwise appear fragmented among contract, technique and everyday operation. Its core lies not in an isolated instrument nor in a specific algorithm, but in the institutional coordination of heterogeneous mechanisms that produce consistent normative effects on who gains access, how they remain, what can be perceived, what can be lost and on what terms the loss may be understood or contested.p. 503

Seu núcleo não está em um instrumento isolado nem em um algoritmo específico, mas na coordenação institucional de mecanismos heterogêneos que produzem efeitos normativos consistentes sobre quem acessa, como permanece, o que pode ser percebido, o que pode ser perdido e em que termos a perda poderá ser compreendida ou contestada.

When the platform concentrates the modulation of access, the reorganization of visibility, the alteration of the conditions of use, the filtering of evidence and the definition of the channels of contestation, the problem becomes one of the institutionalized exercise of private economic power over positions of continued enjoyment, and the asymmetry is not only informational or economic but also semiotic, the subject of the chapter on semiotic vulnerability. The response does not call for a rupture with the existing order but for a more demanding reading of its premises, because private power no longer operates only through price or clause but through the coordination of architecture, curation, sanction and ordinary management of the environment. Consumer protection as a fundamental right and a principle of the economic order is enough to frame these private forms of government, and the Constitution, Brazil's Consumer Protection Code (CDC) and intellectual property come in as sources summoned by the same problem. The aim is not to moralize business choices but to produce decidable categories.

The constitutional reading of the market, therefore, does not serve here as a decorative frame, but as a criterion of intelligibility and limitation.p. 502

A leitura constitucional do mercado, por isso, não serve aqui como moldura decorativa, mas como critério de inteligibilidade e limitação.

In ecosystems of continued enjoyment, value is not exhausted in the initial delivery, because it depends on the persistence of technical, symbolic and organizational conditions that remain under the supplier's control. The utility consumed is realized over time, under permanent updating and curation, so that the consumer's vulnerability does not arise only from a lack of information about the object's characteristics, but also from structural dependence on the signs, metrics, interfaces and framings through which that object becomes intelligible, comparable and verifiable. Consumer protection as a fundamental right and a principle of the economic order already offers sufficient basis to frame, without artificiality, private forms of government that produce broad and repeated effects on the experience of enjoyment, even though they are not to be confused with state power.p. 502

Why can this power be limited without being treated as public power?

The first support comes from The Right to Privacy, an article by Samuel D. Warren and Louis D. Brandeis published in 1890 in the Harvard Law Review. Faced with recent inventions and business practices that intensified intrusion, the authors defended a right to be let alone, distinct from defamation and literary property, and described the invasion of private life as an industry in which supply itself manufactures demand. The article is used methodologically, because technical change alters the conditions of exposure, harm and vulnerability and requires adjusting the categories, and the argument does not depend on an institutional transplant of the common law, the Anglo-American legal tradition, because the point is to readjust categories when the available frame becomes anachronistic. Transposed to the platform, the core of the problem lies not in isolated breaches but in a private governance infrastructure capable of organizing at scale the conditions of participation and enjoyment.

The recourse to Warren and Brandeis does not operate by thematic identity, but by homology of situation.p. 348

PTO recurso a Warren e Brandeis não opera por identidade temática, mas por homologia de situação.

Where it comes from

The prudence of the design proposed by Warren and Brandeis appears clearly in the section on limitations, in which the right to privacy is framed as calibrated protection, incapable of functioning as a general instrument of informational blocking.p. 349

Their work also sets limits by institutional context by recognizing that certain informational flows are part of the very functioning of institutions and, therefore, cannot be paralyzed by the new protection, as is the case with publications made in judicial and legislative bodies, as well as in equivalent communications Brandeis(Warren; Brandeis, [201-?], [p. 19]). Where does the Constitution place consumer protection?

The Constitution inscribes consumer protection on two planes. Article 5, item XXXII, of the Constitution1 orders the State to promote it, as provided by law, and Article 170, items III, IV and V, of the Constitution2 places it among the principles of an economic order founded on the valuing of human labor and on free enterprise and intended to ensure a dignified existence, alongside the social function of property and free competition. That is why consumer protection is an internal condition of the market's legitimacy and not a moralizing appendix. Free enterprise enters the same matrix, since Article 1, items III and IV, of the Constitution3 links it to the dignity of the human person and to social values. Eros Roberto Grau reads consumer protection as a mandatory principle shaping economic activity and argues that free enterprise, free competition, the social function of property, consumer protection and the repression of abuse of economic power, which Article 173, paragraph 4, of the Constitution4 provides for, shape one another.

Where it comes from

Consumer protection does not enter the Brazilian constitutional order as a moralizing appendix of the market, but as one of its internal conditions of legitimacy. The Constitution inscribes it, on the one hand, on the plane of fundamental rights and, on the other, in the principled core of the economic order, which prevents it from being treated as lateral protection, available at the supplier's convenience or subordinated to the private grammar of contract.p. 504

PTA defesa do consumidor não ingressa na ordem constitucional brasileira como apêndice moralizante do mercado, mas como uma de suas condições internas de legitimidade. A Constituição a inscreve, de um lado, no plano dos direitos fundamentais e, de outro, no núcleo principiológico da ordem econômica, o que impede seu tratamento como tutela lateral, disponível à conveniência do fornecedor ou subordinada à gramática privada do contrato.

There is, therefore, no constitutional neutrality in favor of private arrangements that deepen structural dependencies under the pretext of efficiency, innovation or sectoral dynamism. In platform-governed markets, in which access, permanence, visibility and contestation of enjoyment come to be administered by technical-organizational design, the containment of private power ceases to be a peripheral theme and moves to the center of the problem. The Brazilian Constitution does not idolize private power merely because it is not state power; on the contrary, it provides express language to control it when it takes a structurally asymmetric form, especially because consumer protection shapes the legitimate functioning of economic activity and does not depend on the supplier's private tolerance Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 37).p. 506

Em mercados governados por plataforma, nos quais acesso, permanência, visibilidade e contestação da fruição passam a ser administrados por desenho técnico-organizacional, a contenção do poder privado deixa de ser tema periférico e passa ao centro do problema. A Constituição brasileira não idolatra o poder privado pelo simples fato de ele não ser estatal; ao contrário, ela fornece linguagem expressa para controlá-lo quando assume forma estruturalmente assimétrica, especialmente porque a defesa do consumidor conforma o funcionamento legítimo da atividade econômica e não depende de tolerância privada do fornecedor (Miragem, 2020, p. 37) .

PTA função social, nessa chave, incide sobre o próprio conteúdo do direito, sobre seu reconhecimento e sobre sua garantia, introduzindo na estrutura interna da posição jurídica um interesse que não se confunde necessariamente com o interesse isolado do titular (Silva, 2005, p. 283) .

The constitutional containment of private economic power and the subordination of the exercise of property to socially justifiable purposes are not an isolated innovation of recent Brazilian constitutionalism, but an expression of a broader historical process of affirming the centrality of the human person against patrimonially absolutized forms of power. In this key, the evolution of human rights is not exhausted in negative liberties opposable to the State, since the very development of economic, social and cultural rights led to the recognition that, besides the Public Powers, individuals and legal entities of private law also have the duty to respect human rights of any kind Comparato(Comparato, 2015, p. 70).p. 507

Tudo isso explica por que a eficácia horizontal dos direitos fundamentais, embora indispensável, ainda não basta sozinha como gramática operacional do problema. É preciso reconduzir essa contenção constitucional a uma linguagem dogmática de controle que consiga tocar, de modo concreto, arquitetura, práticas, informação, sanção e regimes de prova. É aí que o direito do consumidor entra não como apêndice setorial, mas como infraestrutura normativa de contenção das assimetrias produzidas pela Lex Plataformae.

Because of the consumer's vulnerability before the supplier, who holds a dominant position in the contract, the CDC establishes a series of norms limiting the supplier's private power, through clear state legislative intervention in contractual autonomy aimed at protecting the interests of the vulnerable party Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 274). This reading gains density once one recognizes that, in contemporary society, the consumer relationship appears less as an expression of contractual freedom and more as the satisfaction of a need to consume. In this context, the notion of abuse expresses the prevalence of the supplier's dominant position over the consumer's vulnerability, with good faith as the central legal brake on the exercise of this bargaining superiority Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 324).p. 519

PTEm razão da vulnerabilidade do consumidor diante do fornecedor, que exerce posição dominante no contrato, o CDC estabelece uma série de normas de limitação do poder privado do fornecedor, por meio de clara intervenção legislativa estatal na autonomia contratual, voltada à proteção dos interesses do vulnerável (Miragem, 2020, p. 274) . Essa leitura ganha densidade quando se reconhece que, na sociedade contemporânea, a relação de consumo se apresenta menos como expressão de liberdade contratual e mais como realização de uma necessidade de consumir. Nesse contexto, a noção de abuso traduz a prevalência da posição dominante do fornecedor sobre a vulnerabilidade do consumidor, sendo a boa-fé o freio jurídico central ao exercício dessa superioridade negocial (Miragem, 2020, p. 324) .

Convergently, Marques defines abusiveness as an "imbalance or mismatch of rights and obligations between the parties", associated with the authorization of conduct contrary to good faith and with the abuse of the preponderant contractual position Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 162).p. 514

PTEm sentido convergente, Marques define a abusividade como "desequilíbrio ou descompasso de direitos e obrigações entre as partes", associado à autorização de atuação contrária à boa-fé e ao abuso da posição contratual preponderante (Marques, 2016, p. 162) .

In contexts of informational asymmetry, practical dependence and unilateral pre-drafting of the rules of the relationship, consumer protection requires that the exercise of contractual powers be subject to standards of loyalty, reasonableness, economic and social purpose and respect for the trust legitimately placed in the relationship. This is exactly the point Marques develops when she states that "the notion of objective good faith, as a new principle guiding the conduct of the contracting parties in captive contracts, means a new and important limitation on the exercise of subjective rights", so that the exercise of a right will be abusive when diverted from its economic and social function or exercised in disloyal circumstances Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 111). She adds that good faith has a limiting function, "reducing the freedom of action of the contractual partners" and controlling abusive conduct and clauses Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 221).p. 519

PTEm contextos de assimetria informacional, dependência prática e predisposição unilateral das regras do vínculo, a proteção do consumidor exige que o exercício dos poderes contratuais se submeta a parâmetros de lealdade, razoabilidade, finalidade econômico-social e respeito à confiança legitimamente depositada na relação. É exatamente esse o ponto desenvolvido por Marques ao afirmar que "a noção de boa-fé objetiva, enquanto novo princípio a guiar a conduta dos contraentes nos contratos cativos significa uma nova e importante limitação ao exercício de direitos subjetivos", de modo que o exercício do direito será abusivo quando desviado de sua função econômico-social ou exercido em circunstâncias desleais (Marques, 2016, p. 111) . A autora acrescenta que a boa-fé possui função limitadora, "reduzindo a liberdade de atuação dos parceiros contratuais" e controlando condutas e cláusulas abusivas (Marques, 2016, p. 221) .

Articles 39 and 51 are protective mechanisms designed precisely to equalize a factually unequal relationship, typifying abusive practices and sanctioning abusive clauses with nullity by operation of law, in a clear mitigation of pacta sunt servanda Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 741).p. 519

PTOs arts. 39 e 51 compõem mecanismos de proteção destinados justamente a equalizar relação faticamente desigual, tipificando práticas abusivas e sancionando cláusulas abusivas com nulidade de pleno direito, em nítida mitigação do pacta sunt servanda (Miragem, 2020, p. 741) .

In practical terms, the containment of the platform need not be built only through lofty constitutional mediation: good faith, abuse of a dominant position, excessive disadvantage and the nullity of abusive clauses already offer enough grammar to bring it down to earth within the CDC.p. 519-520

PTEm termos práticos, a contenção da plataforma não precisa ser construída só por mediação constitucional elevada: boa-fé, abuso de posição dominante, desvantagem exagerada e nulidade de cláusulas abusivas já oferecem gramática suficiente para trazê-la ao chão do CDC.

These doors are found in the provisions. Article 14 of the CDC16 holds the service supplier liable for defects in the service and for insufficient information about enjoyment and risks, Article 20 of the CDC17 reaches quality defects that make the service unfit or reduce its value, and Article 39, items IV, V and XII, of the CDC18 prohibits taking advantage of the consumer's weakness or ignorance, demanding a manifestly excessive advantage and failing to set a time limit for the obligation or leaving its starting date to the supplier's sole discretion. Through these doors, the problem can already be grasped as defect, inadequacy, abuse, exploitation of vulnerability and unilateral manipulation of the conditions of enjoyment, and the focus shifts from isolated clauses to structural patterns of governance, verifiability and reasoning. The platform's public relevance does not turn it into a State, but it prevents its governance from being read as an internal business detail, and control needs to reach the architecture of the relationship.

Where it comes from

Strict liability for defects in the service and for insufficient or inadequate information, the rules on quality defects that make the service unfit or reduce its value, and the prohibition of abusive practices that take advantage of the consumer's weakness, impose an excessive advantage or leave performance of the obligation to the supplier's sole discretion show that the problem of the Lex Plataformae need not be framed only as a lofty conflict between Constitution and market. It can already be grasped, operationally, as defect, inadequacy, abuse, exploitation of vulnerability and unilateral manipulation of the conditions of enjoyment. This shifts the focus from isolated clauses to structural patterns of governance, verifiability and reasoning.p. 520

PTA responsabilidade objetiva por defeitos do serviço e por informações insuficientes ou inadequadas, a disciplina dos vícios de qualidade que tornem o serviço impróprio ou lhe diminuam o valor e a vedação a práticas abusivas que se aproveitem da fraqueza do consumidor, imponham vantagem excessiva ou deixem o cumprimento da obrigação ao exclusivo critério do fornecedor mostram que o problema da Lex Plataformae não precisa ser recortado apenas como conflito elevado entre Constituição e mercado. Ele já pode ser apreendido, de modo operacional, como defeito, inadequação, abuso, exploração da vulnerabilidade e manipulação unilateral das condições de fruição. Isso desloca o foco de cláusulas isoladas para padrões estruturais de governança, verificabilidade e motivação.

“Art. 39. The supplier of products or services is prohibited, among other abusive practices, from: IV - taking advantage of the consumer's weakness or ignorance […]; V - demanding from the consumer a manifestly excessive advantage; XII - failing to stipulate a time limit for performing its obligation or leaving the setting of its starting date to its exclusive discretion.” Brasil(Brasil, [2022]).p. 520

PT“Art. 39. É vedado ao fornecedor de produtos ou serviços, dentre outras práticas abusivas: IV - prevalecer-se da fraqueza ou ignorância do consumidor […]; V - exigir do consumidor vantagem manifestamente excessiva; XII - deixar de estipular prazo para o cumprimento de sua obrigação ou deixar a fixação de seu termo inicial a seu exclusivo critério.” (Brasil, [2022]) .

The public relevance of the powers of the Lex Plataformae does not license confusing the platform with the State, but it prevents its governance from being read as an internal, legally neutral business detail. When a private supplier organizes, through design and enforcement, the conditions of access, permanence, equivalence, visibility, sanction and contestation of a continuous consumption experience, legal control cannot remain a prisoner of the contractual surface. It must reach the architecture of the relationship and reintroduce, within it, criteria of controllability, reasoning and verifiability. Without this, opacity, unilateral mutation and systematic difficulties of contestation no longer appear as pathologies and come to be naturalized as the ordinary condition of digital consumption.p. 520

PTA relevância pública dos poderes da Lex Plataformae não autoriza confundir plataforma com Estado, mas impede que sua governança seja lida como detalhe empresarial interno, juridicamente neutro. Quando um fornecedor privado organiza, por design e enforcement, as condições de acesso, permanência, equivalência, visibilidade, sanção e contestação de uma experiência de consumo contínuo, o controle jurídico não pode permanecer prisioneiro da superfície contratual. Ele precisa alcançar a arquitetura da relação e reintroduzir, no interior dela, critérios de controlabilidade, motivação e verificabilidade. Sem isso, opacidade, mutação unilateral e dificuldades sistemáticas de contestação deixam de aparecer como patologias e passam a ser naturalizadas como condição ordinária do consumo digital.
