Outside the nine · statement of the Parameters · 7.1.1.10

Functional restoration and regime correction

Version 0.12

What is the priority form of protection?

Whenever technically and legally feasible, it is the functional restoration of enjoyment, together with the correction of the regime that made it inadequate. Correcting the regime makes it possible to treat the harm as architectural, and not as an episode. This is the parameter that closes the regime.

The priority protection, whenever technically and legally feasible, must seek functional restoration of enjoyment and correction of the regime that made it inadequate.p. 739

PTA tutela prioritária, sempre que técnica e juridicamente viável, deve buscar recomposição funcional da fruição e correção do regime que a tornou inadequada.

Where it comes from

This parameter thus fulfills the function of closing the regime.p. 740

PTEsse parâmetro cumpre, assim, função de fechamento do regime.

Regime correction, by contrast, makes it possible to treat the harm as architectural and not merely as an episode.p. 741

PTA correção de regime, ao contrário, permite tratar o dano como arquitetural e não apenas como episódio.

Why is compensation not enough?

Treating every injury of this kind as compensable only in money means, in many cases, accepting that the supplier may degrade, suppress or reconfigure enjoyment and then absorb the cost as an ordinary cost of doing business. Compensation remains legitimate and often indispensable. The parameter only prevents it from being the automatic response. Nor does it require literally reproducing the previous state at any cost. What it requires is a functionally adequate restitution of the injured experience.

Where it comes from

Treating every injury of this kind as subject to merely pecuniary compensation means, in many cases, accepting that the supplier may unilaterally degrade, suppress or reconfigure enjoyment and then merely internalize the monetary cost of this conduct as an ordinary price of business. That is not adequate protection. It is the automatic monetization of abuse.p. 797

PTTratar toda lesão desse tipo como passível de compensação meramente pecuniária significa, em muitos casos, aceitar que o fornecedor possa degradar, suprimir ou reconfigurar unilateralmente a fruição e depois apenas internalizar o custo monetário dessa conduta como preço ordinário do negócio. Isso não é tutela adequada. É monetização automática do abuso.

Monetary compensation remains, of course, a legitimate and in many cases indispensable remedy. The parameter formulated here does not eliminate it.p. 741

PTA indenização pecuniária permanece, evidentemente, como remédio legítimo e em muitos casos indispensável. O parâmetro aqui formulado não a elimina.

The criterion is not the literal reproduction of the past state at any cost, but the functionally adequate restitution of the injured experience [...]p. 740

PTO critério não é a reprodução literal do estado pretérito a qualquer custo, mas a restituição funcionalmente adequada da experiência lesada [...]
