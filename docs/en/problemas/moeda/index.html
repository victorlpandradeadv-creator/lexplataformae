Your rights · By problem

The service's currency now buys less. Is that allowed?

You bought in-app currency with real money, like a game's coins or an app's credits, and now the same item costs more coins. The company that sells the currency is the same one that sets the prices inside, and that is why the change needs to be looked at carefully.

Where it comes from

Riot does not describe the game's currency as a simple neutral medium of exchange. It treats it as part of a closed circuit, whose equivalence can be recalibrated unilaterally. The terms admit that, “sometimes”, the company may change the purchasing power of the In-Game Currency, including by increasing the number of coins needed to acquire virtual products such as skins, and place this movement under the generic promise of notice and gradualness Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e).p. 176

PTA Riot não descreve a moeda do jogo como simples meio de troca neutro. Ela a trata como parte de um circuito fechado, cuja equivalência pode ser recalibrada de modo unilateral. O termo admite que, “às vezes”, a empresa pode alterar o poder de compra da Moeda do Jogo, inclusive aumentando o número de moedas necessárias para adquirir produtos virtuais como skins, e coloca esse movimento sob a promessa genérica de aviso e de progressividade (Riot Games, 2024e) .

What is observed, however, in platform-governed immaterial consumption, is an inversion of logic. The supplier does not limit itself to competing within a market. It designs the very world of reference in which consumption takes place, controls catalog, visibility, rules of circulation, the practical unit of account and conditions of permanence, and then begins to exercise, simultaneously, functions analogous to those of a market and of a normative authority, defining on its own the level of prices and the pace of internal inflation.p. 179

PTO que se observa, porém, no consumo imaterial governado por plataforma, é uma inversão de lógica. O fornecedor não se limita a competir dentro de um mercado. Ele desenha o próprio mundo de referência em que o consumo ocorre, controla catálogo, visibilidade, regras de circulação, unidade prática de conta e condições de permanência, e então passa a exercer, simultaneamente, funções análogas às de mercado e às de autoridade normativa, definindo por conta própria o patamar de preços e o ritmo de inflação interna.

How do you know whether the change in the currency affected what you had?

The loss can come in several ways. The item starts costing more coins, prices change by region and bonuses on currency purchases come and go. And certain event currencies simply expire after a date.

When prices are given in in-app currency, in bundles and in conversions, it becomes hard to know how much you are really spending and to compare it with anything else. Each currency works like a language of its own, and you are the one who has to translate everything.

A change in what the currency buys goes through the same test as other changes. The question is not whether the company keeps selling something under the same name. It is whether what you acquired remains equivalent to what justified your trust and your spending.

Where it comes from

Likewise, the instability of the real cost is not limited to the internal design of the store. It also appears when the platform adjusts economic parameters by region and runs bonuses that reconfigure, by time window, the perceived advantage of buying paid currency.p. 398

PTNo mesmo sentido, a instabilidade do custo real não se limita ao desenho interno da loja. Ela também aparece quando a plataforma ajusta parâmetros econômicos por região e opera bônus que reconfiguram, por janela, a percepção de vantagem na compra de moeda paga.

Thus, by adopting as a procedure the creation of multiple currencies that do not merge and have multiple durations within the ecosystem, including expiring entirely when their scheduled period ends, the platform stands out for further aggravating the user's difficulty in understanding the real value of what the user is able to acquire, giving rise to and mobilizing, above all, the fear of missing out in its users.p. 398

PTAssim, ao adotar por procedimento a criação de múltiplas moedas que não se confundem e possuem múltiplos prazos de duração dentro do ecossistema, inclusive expirando integralmente quando findado seu período previsto, a plataforma se destaca por asseverar ainda mais a dificuldade do usuário em compreender o real valor daquilo que é capaz de adquirir, ensejando e mobilizando, sobretudo, o fear of missing out em seus usuários.

The consumer no longer deals with a price that can be compared and audited in a straight line, and begins to deal with multiple internal grammars that require situational reading, because each currency “speaks” a different language.p. 397

PTO consumidor deixa de lidar com um preço que pode ser comparado e auditado em linha reta, e passa a lidar com múltiplas gramáticas internas que exigem leitura situacional, porque cada moeda “fala” um idioma diferente.

The strength of the criterion appears clearly in scenarios such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that hollow out acquired utility, interface changes that disrupt ordinary use, redefinitions of rules that lower the equivalence of what was promised, changes in currency or internal purchasing power, mutations in progression systems, reclassifications of rarity, deletion or compression of inventories, and changes in visibility patterns that affect the consumer's position in the ecosystem. In all these cases, the decisive question should not be whether the platform kept offering “something” under the same label. The correct question is whether the thing legally acquired, as a position of enjoyment in a governed environment, retains sufficient functional equivalence with what was the object of the consumer's expectation, trust and investment.p. 637-638

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida, mudanças de interface que desorganizam o uso ordinário, redefinições de regras que rebaixam a equivalência do que foi prometido, alterações de moeda ou de poder de compra interno, mutações em sistemas de progressão, reclassificações de raridade, apagamento ou compressão de inventários, e mudanças em padrões de visibilidade que afetam a posição do consumidor no ecossistema. Em todos esses casos, a pergunta decisiva não deve ser se a plataforma continuou oferecendo “alguma coisa” com o mesmo rótulo. A pergunta correta é se a coisa juridicamente adquirida, enquanto posição de fruição em ambiente governado, conserva equivalência funcional suficiente com aquilo que foi objeto de expectativa, confiança e investimento do consumidor.

Which records show how much your currency was worth?

It is worth keeping how much you paid in reais for each bundle, the balance you had and the coin price of the items that matter, before and after the change. The company's notice is worth keeping too, because the contract of a major game promises to give notice when a change affects the currency in a negative and significant way.

For currencies that expire, it helps to write down the expiration dates that appeared on the screen, because the deadline is part of the system's own rule.

Where it comes from

We normally do this only in progressive steps, but you will be notified (through posts on our website, app or game) if we intend to make changes that significantly affect your In-Game Currency in a negative way.p. 614

PTNormalmente, fazemos isso apenas em etapas progressivas, mas você será avisado (por meio de postagens em nosso site, aplicativo ou jogo) se pretendermos fazer alterações que afetem significativamente sua Moeda do Jogo de maneira negativa.

The minimum required proof calls for identification of what was shown, at what moment, with what salience, through which channels and to what extent this organization allowed the consumer to understand what was legally relevant in order to adhere, remain or react.p. 744

PTA prova mínima exigível requer identificação do que foi mostrado, em que momento, com qual saliência, por quais canais e em que medida essa organização permitia ao consumidor compreender o que era juridicamente relevante para aderir, permanecer ou reagir.

This shows that urgency does not depend on an external emotional appeal, because expiration is designed as a rule of the token system itself.p. 398

PTAqui, demonstra-se que a urgência não depende de um apelo emocional externo, porque a expiração é desenhada como regra do próprio sistema de tokens.

What can you ask for when the currency is worth less?

The request may include clear information about how much each item costs and about what changed. The ad, the value and the costs that did not show need to match what you receive. Depending on the case, the lack of clarity may also lead to restoring what you lost because of it.

If the change means that what you already had is no longer worth the same, it is fair to ask that the loss be restored, corrected or reviewed. And the same contract says that price adjustments apply to the future, except for what you have already acquired. That may help you demand that what you already bought be left untouched.

Where it comes from

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure correspondence between promise, announced value, hidden costs and the experience actually made available under managed mutability.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar correspondência entre promessa, valor anunciado, custos ocultados e experiência efetivamente disponibilizada sob mutabilidade administrada.

The possible institutional consequence includes a duty to correct the communicational arrangement, reinforcement of useful transparency, orders to display and restoration of the enjoyment affected by decisional opacity.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional possível inclui dever de correção do arranjo comunicacional, reforço de transparência útil, ordens de exibição e recomposição da fruição afetada por opacidade decisória.

Functional value equivalence allows, precisely, a much more robust intermediate position: it recognizes that the platform may make changes, but requires that the change preserve, in substantial terms, the functional value of the acquired experience or, failing that, make room for restoration, correction, review or another adequate remedyp. 637

PTA equivalência de valor funcional permite, justamente, uma posição intermediária muito mais robusta: reconhece-se que a plataforma pode alterar, mas exige-se que a alteração preserve, em termos substanciais, o valor funcional da experiência adquirida ou, não o fazendo, abra espaço a recomposição, correção, revisão ou outro remédio adequado

We may adjust the prices of any part of the Riot Services at any time (with future effect, that is, with the exception of what you have already acquired). This may affect the purchasing power of your In-Game Currency, although we normally do so only in gradual steps.p. 614

PTPodemos ajustar os preços de qualquer parte dos Serviços da Riot a qualquer momento (com efeito futuro, isto é, com a exceção daquilo que você já adquiriu). Isso pode afetar o poder de compra da sua Moeda do Jogo, embora normalmente o façamos apenas em etapas graduais.

Who can you turn to when a change to the currency affects everyone?

The first step is usually the company itself. The contract that says the currency is non-refundable makes an exception for when your consumer rights guarantee a refund. That is why the answer does not stop at what is written there.

A change in the currency affects everyone who has a balance at the same time. That is why it is the kind of case that can go to Procon, the Public Defender's Office or the Public Prosecutor's Office, which the channels page describes. In these cases the response can target the whole rule, and not just your balance.

Where it comes from

Subject to applicable law, any applicable amounts and other charges for value-based services (including Game Currency) are paid in advance and are not refundable, in whole or in part, except as expressly provided in these Terms or in our refund policy or unless your rights as a consumer entitle you to a refund.p. 614

PTSujeito à lei aplicável, quaisquer valores aplicáveis e outras cobranças por serviços baseados em valores (inclusive a Moeda do Jogo) são pagos antecipadamente e não são reembolsáveis, no todo ou em parte, exceto conforme expressamente previsto nestes Termos ou em nossa política de reembolsos ou a menos que seus direitos como consumidor lhe concedam direito a um reembolso.

When the dispute reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or unverifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case. It may take on a structural form: a duty to preserve minimum trails, to establish protocols for qualified communication of relevant changes, to organize intelligible contestation procedures, to maintain minimum functional impact reports or to adjust the supply regime to prevent the problem from recurring. This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not as a single piece.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual. Ela pode assumir feição estrutural: dever de preservar trilhas mínimas, protocolizar comunicação qualificada para alterações relevantes, organizar procedimentos de contestação inteligíveis, manter relatórios mínimos de impacto funcional ou ajustar o regime de fornecimento para impedir repetição do problema. Isso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is changing the price of the currency always forbidden?

No. A company can, often legitimately, set its own prices, especially when there is real competition and you can switch services without losing everything. The problem appears when leaving is costly, comparison becomes hard and the value depends only on what the company decides.

Nor is the service's economy required to stand still. Rebalancing and adjustments can happen, and what is not allowed is using the change to destroy the value of what you already had.

Where it comes from

It must be recognized that certain agents may, often legitimately, act as price setters when their position stems from a verifiable combination of innovation recognized and protected within the limits of the system, consolidated reputation and competitive contestability, that is, the real possibility of substitution by competitors and of consumer migration without prohibitive costs.p. 178

PTFaz-se necessário reconhecer que é possibilidade, frequentemente legítima, de determinados agentes atuarem enquanto formadores de preço quando sua posição decorre de combinação verificável entre inovação reconhecida e protegida nos limites do sistema, reputação consolidada e contestabilidade concorrencial, isto é, a possibilidade real de substituição por concorrentes e de migração do consumidor sem custos proibitivos.

It is an appropriation of the position of price setter without the counterweights that legitimize it, since exit is costly, external comparison is artificially hindered (potentially made impossible) and the value of the object depends on parameters controlled unilaterally by the supplier itself.p. 179

PTTrata-se de uma apropriação da posição de formador de preços sem os contrapesos que a legitimam, pois a saída é onerosa, a comparação externa é artificialmente dificultada (potencialmente impossibilitada) e o valor do objeto depende de parâmetros controlados unilateralmente pelo próprio fornecedor.

It is important to note that functional value equivalence does not freeze the ecosystem. It does not require absolute identity between before and after, nor does it prohibit rebalancing, adjustments, corrections or developments of the environment. What it prevents is the use of mutability as a screen for the opportunistic destruction of value.p. 638

PTÉ importante notar que a equivalência de valor funcional não congela o ecossistema. Ela não exige identidade absoluta entre antes e depois, nem proíbe reequilíbrios, ajustes, correções ou evoluções do ambiente. O que ela impede é o uso da mutabilidade como biombo para destruição oportunista de valor.

What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
