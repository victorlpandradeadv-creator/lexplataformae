You complained, were treated well and nothing happened. Why?

Something went wrong with your account, your purchase, your community or your work on the app. You complained, were treated kindly, waited and, in the end, let it go.

1 What went wrong?

It can be anything. The blocked account, the item that changed, the group taken down, the order that did not arrive, the assistant that got it wrong, the ride that paid less, the store that vanished from search.

Each of these problems has its own page. This one deals with what happens to the complaint after you make it.

Where it comes from

What matters is not whether the platform presents itself as a cognitive tool, market infrastructure, productive environment or reputation system, but how it organizes access, modulates expectations, distributes risks and conditions the possibility of institutional reconstruction of the conflict.p. 756

PTO que importa não é se a plataforma se apresenta como ferramenta cognitiva, infraestrutura de mercado, ambiente produtivo ou sistema de reputação, mas como ela organiza acesso, modula expectativas, distribui riscos e condiciona a possibilidade de reconstrução institucional do conflito.

2 Why is customer service so kind?

Because kindness is also designed. The friendly tone, the ready-made apologies and the request to wait make you feel heard. None of that is wrong in itself.

The problem starts when that care serves to calm the complaint, not to resolve it.

Where it comes from

It administers the tolerance for harm and the user's energy to react.p. 781

PTAdministra a tolerância ao dano e a energia de reação do usuário.

3 How does the loss change its name?

The loss becomes a technical adjustment, temporary instability, a security measure or a necessary update. Under another name, it stops looking like a loss and starts looking like part of the service.

The language of customer service does not just describe the frustration. It manages it.

Where it comes from

It manages it.p. 782

PTEla a administra.

4 Why does customer service always go back to the start?

You try again and get another protocol number and another canned answer. Each contact starts from zero, and none reaches anyone who can resolve it.

The loop does not need to deny your complaint. It is enough that it never reaches the end.

Where it comes from

What matters is not whether the user “felt” more or less welcomed, but whether the platform structured signs, routines and protocols capable of shifting the reading of the loss, reducing the critical force of the experience and weakening the probability of intelligible contestation.p. 782

PTO que importa não é saber se o usuário “sentiu-se” mais ou menos acolhido, mas se a plataforma estruturou signos, rotinas e protocolos capazes de deslocar a leitura da perda, reduzir a força crítica da experiência e enfraquecer a probabilidade de contestação inteligível.

5 Why do you end up giving up?

Because fatigue counts too. In a service you use every day, small frustrations are absorbed one by one, until the loss seems normal.

When you give up, the conflict does not disappear. It still exists, but it stays trapped inside the company.

Where it comes from

The conflict does not disappear.p. 782

PTO conflito não desaparece.

6 What does the loop prevent?

It prevents the loss from becoming a problem someone outside examines. A complaint that dissolves in customer service does not become a Procon statistic, does not become a lawsuit and does not show that many people went through the same thing.

That is how the conflict is governed before it becomes a case.

Where it comes from

In platform-governed ecosystems, the conflict does not begin when the user decides to litigate, complain or formalize their dissatisfaction.p. 780

PTEm ecossistemas governados por plataforma, o conflito não começa quando o usuário decide litigar, reclamar ou formalizar inconformidade.

Contestation may be postponed, demobilized or dissolved before it takes institutional form.p. 782

PTA contestação pode ser adiada, desmobilizada ou dissolvida antes de ganhar forma institucional.

7 How to get out of the loop?

Record everything, with protocol numbers, conversations, screenshots and dates. Ask for a human agent, which the customer service rules guarantee. And if the company does not resolve it, take the complaint outside it, to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court.

When the same loss hits many people, each complaint helps show the pattern, and the response can correct customer service for everyone.

Where it comes from

It may call for correction of customer service flows, reinforcement of intelligibility, prohibition of functionally misleading discursive routines, procedural duties to give reasons and the design of avenues of contestation that do not serve merely to absorb the grievance, but to make it effectively processable and verifiable.p. 783

PTPode reclamar correção dos fluxos de atendimento, reforço de inteligibilidade, proibição de rotinas discursivas funcionalmente enganosas, deveres procedimentais de motivação e desenho de vias de contestação que não sirvam apenas para absorver a inconformidade, mas para torná-la efetivamente processável e verificável.

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Is there already a rule for this?

There is. Since 2 November 2026 a new customer service (SAC) decree has been in force. It allows bots and artificial intelligence in customer service, but guarantees the option of speaking to a person at any stage, including the first. This applies from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., every day, by phone and on electronic channels such as chats and messaging apps.

Consumidor.gov.br resolves most of the complaints filed, but does not sanction a company that does not resolve them. That is why it is a quick path, and Procon and the Small Claims Court remain available when it is not enough.
