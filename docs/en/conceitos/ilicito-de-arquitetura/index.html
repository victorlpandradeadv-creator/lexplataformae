Can an infringement lie in the design of an app?Unlawful act of architecture

We are used to looking for abuse in a clause of the contract, in a sentence you can underline and show the judge. But there are cases in which no clause, on its own, is the problem.

The problem is the pattern that keeps repeating. Options that come already checked, friction whenever you try to do what you want, manufactured urgency and a cancellation path that seems built to make you give up. And also equivalences that shift whenever it suits the company, punishments that are hard to understand and evidence that is impossible to gather. Each piece seems small. Together, they work as a method.

Where it comes from

The unlawful act does not arise, therefore, from a singular episode that can be treated as an accident. It emerges when defaults, frictions, manufactured urgencies, hostile cancellation routes, elastic equivalences, sanction regimes or proof arrangements work together as a method of exploitation.p. 666

PTO ilícito não nasce, portanto, de um episódio singular que pode ser tratado como acidente. Ele surge quando defaults, fricções, urgências fabricadas, rotas de cancelamento hostis, equivalências elásticas, regimes de sanção ou arranjos de prova funcionam em conjunto como método de exploração.

When this governance repeatedly organizes informational noise, decisional friction, evidentiary opacity, non-auditable mutation and insufficient restoration, the unlawful act is no longer only in the statement. It is in the architecture.p. 664

PTQuando essa governança organiza, de forma reiterada, ruído informacional, fricção decisória, opacidade probatória, mutação não auditável e recomposição insuficiente, o ilícito já não está apenas no enunciado. Está na arquitetura.

Unlawful act of architecture

Unlawful means what the law does not accept, and architecture, here, means the design of the service, its screens, paths, built-in rules and punishment systems. That design is also the company's conduct, not a neutral backdrop where things simply happen. So when the design organizes losses in a stable way, the infringement lies in the design. The question is no longer only whether an isolated promise was kept but how the service itself was built to work.

Where it comes from

The core of the parameter lies in treating architecture as conduct.p. 732

PTO núcleo do parâmetro está em tratar a arquitetura como conduta.

When inducement, extraction and normalization of losses become stably incorporated into the design of the ecosystem, the problem ceases to be merely one of fragmentary nonperformance and comes to constitute a teleological unlawful act of architecture.p. 732

PTQuando indução, extração e normalização de perdas se incorporam de maneira estável ao desenho do ecossistema, o problema deixa de ser apenas de inadimplemento fragmentário e passa a configurar ilícito teleológico de arquitetura.

Abusiveness, then, ceases to appear merely as an isolated accident or a severable clause and comes to reside in the very operating program of the service.p. 664

PTA abusividade, então, deixa de aparecer apenas como acidente pontual ou cláusula destacável e passa a residir no próprio programa de funcionamento do serviço.

How can a building forbid without writing anything?

Design speaks. It is a language made of screens, buttons, paths and automatic rules, and this language defines what can be seen, proven, contested, repaired and even remembered.

In a building, the emergency exit can sit behind three locked doors. No one wrote a rule saying it is forbidden to leave. The design settles that before any conversation. Something similar happens with apps, because whoever designs decides a lot before you notice there was anything to decide.

Where it comes from

Technique comes to operate as manifest language, that is, fully operative. A language of architecture and engineering that defines what can be seen, proven, contested, repaired and even remembered.p. 32

PTA técnica passa a operar como linguagem manifesta, isto é, plenamente operante. Uma linguagem de arquitetura e engenharia que define o que pode ser visto, provado, contestado, reparado e até lembrado.

The second is the layer of architecture and implementation, in which such statements cease to be simple declarations and are converted into a configuration of possibilities by means of interface, flows, access patterns, visibility parameters and technical restrictions that delimit the range of the possible before any interpretive disputep. 365

PTA segunda é a camada de arquitetura e implementação, na qual tais enunciados deixam de ser simples declarações e se convertem em configuração de possibilidades por meio de interface, fluxos, padrões de acesso, parâmetros de visibilidade e restrições técnicas que delimitam o intervalo do possível antes de qualquer disputa interpretativa

Which five pieces form the unlawful act?

There are five pieces that are organized together and repeat. Noise in information, which confuses instead of guiding. Friction at the moment of deciding, which pushes you to one side. Evidence out of the user's reach. Changes that cannot be checked. And repair that does not cover the loss.

None of them, alone, makes the unlawful act. What makes it is the stable set. And this avoids two common mistakes. One is accepting that everything is innovation, as if changing all the time left the platform free to downgrade anything. The other is hunting for loose clauses, as if the service could only be controlled in pieces of text and never in the way it works.

Where it comes from

When this governance repeatedly organizes informational noise, decisional friction, evidentiary opacity, non-auditable mutation and insufficient restoration, the unlawful act is no longer only in the statement. It is in the architecture.p. 664

PTQuando essa governança organiza, de forma reiterada, ruído informacional, fricção decisória, opacidade probatória, mutação não auditável e recomposição insuficiente, o ilícito já não está apenas no enunciado. Está na arquitetura.

The first would be to dissolve the problem in the vague language of “innovation” or of the platform's “dynamic nature”, as if the continuous reorganization of the environment were enough to immunize any relevant downgrading of enjoyment. The second would be to reduce the legal response to the atomized hunt for isolated clauses, as if supply could only be controlled in its textual fragments and never in its operational logic.p. 664

PTO primeiro seria dissolver o problema na linguagem vaga da “inovação” ou da “natureza dinâmica” da plataforma, como se a contínua reorganização do ambiente bastasse para imunizar qualquer rebaixamento relevante da fruição. O segundo seria reduzir a resposta jurídica à caça atomizada de cláusulas isoladas, como se o fornecimento só pudesse ser controlado em seus fragmentos textuais e nunca em sua lógica operacional.

Which screen traps already have a name?

Some screen traps already have a well-known name, dark patterns. They are the box that comes already checked, the hidden cancellation path and the urgency that pressures you to decide fast.

What weighs in these cases is not an isolated sentence, but the way choices are presented and induced. And Brazilian consumer protection law already allows many cases of deceptive design to be punished. Thus design stops being treated as neutral aesthetics and goes back to being what it is, a commercial practice with an economic goal, which the law can qualify.

Where it comes from

[...] the asymmetry of intelligibility and agency can be produced and exploited by the interaction architecture itself, making the design of options and avenues of reaction legally relevant [...] This point becomes clearer when interface patterns are described as a sanctionable commercial practice, since abusiveness stops depending on an isolated textual statement and comes to include the way choices are presented and inducedp. 405

PT[...] a assimetria de inteligibilidade e de agência pode ser produzida e explorada pela própria arquitetura de interação, tornando juridicamente relevante o desenho das opções e das vias de reação [...] Esse ponto se torna mais nítido quando padrões de interface são descritos como prática comercial sancionável, já que a abusividade deixa de depender de uma afirmação textual isolada e passa a incluir a forma como escolhas são apresentadas e induzidas

several of these provisions are related to dark patterns, which allows us to conclude that under the Brazilian legal framework it is possible to sanction several cases of deceptive designp. 405

PTvárias dessas disposições estão relacionadas a padrões obscuros, o que nos permite concluir que sob o quadro legal brasileiro é possível sancionar vários casos de desenho enganoso

It emerges when defaults, frictions, manufactured urgencies, hostile cancellation routesp. 666

PTEle surge quando defaults, fricções, urgências fabricadas, rotas de cancelamento hostis

When it is recognized that it is possible to sanction cases of deceptive design and the provisions of art. 39 of the CDC1 are linked to dark patterns, design is put back in its correct place, as an economically oriented and legally qualifiable commercial practice, and not as aesthetic neutralityp. 432

PTQuando se reconhece que é possível sancionar casos de desenho enganoso e se vinculam as disposições do art. 39 do CDC a padrões obscuros, o design é recolocado no seu lugar correto, como prática comercial economicamente orientada e juridicamente qualificável, e não como neutralidade estética

Is the Consumer Protection Code already enough?

This does not call for a new law or a separate law for platforms. The Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor, CDC) already has the grammar to see the problem, as long as you look at the program of the service, and not only at the text of the contract.

And the response can reach the source. When the loss comes from the design, fixing one case at a time is not enough. You can ask for the design itself to be corrected, for more transparency, for changes in the service's paths and for measures that reach everyone facing the same problem.

Where it comes from

It is this shift that makes it possible to qualify the unlawful act of architecture without leaving the grammar of the CDC and without yielding to the temptation of inventing a parallel law for platforms.p. 665

PTÉ esse deslocamento que permite qualificar o ilícito de arquitetura sem sair da gramática do CDC e sem cair na tentação de inventar um direito paralelo para plataformas.

The possible legal qualification extends to structural abusive practice, defect in the supply regime, possible cumulation with a quality defect and grounds for injunctive and corrective protection of architecture.p. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível alcança prática abusiva estrutural, vício do regime de fornecimento, eventual cumulação com vício de qualidade e fundamento para tutela inibitória e corretiva de arquitetura.

The institutional consequence goes beyond redress of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced duties of transparency, design correction and structural or collective measures.p. 747

PTA consequência institucional vai além da reparação de episódios isolados e pode incluir reconfiguração de fluxos, deveres reforçados de transparência, correção de desenho e medidas estruturais ou coletivas.

Is the screen's beauty what gets judged?

Neither the style nor the beauty of the screen is judged. Design can and should organize the experience, because that is what it exists for. What it cannot do is turn this into a stable program of exploitation.

Nor is it about turning every power difference into suspicion, or moralizing economic activity. The category only makes sense when there is a stable pattern, one that repeats and that relevantly affects your practical freedom and what you bought.

Where it comes from

What is called into question is not design as style, visual language or interface aesthetics [...]p. 664

PTO que se põe em questão não é o design enquanto estilo, linguagem visual ou estética de interface [...]

[...] the architecture of the service may organize enjoyment, but it cannot do so as a stable program of exploitation.p. 665

PT[...] a arquitetura do serviço pode organizar a fruição, mas não pode fazê-lo como programa estável de exploração.

It is not a matter of moralizing the exercise of economic activity nor of turning every asymmetry into generic suspicion.p. 664

PTNão se trata de moralizar o exercício da atividade econômica nem transformar toda assimetria em suspeita genérica.

The category is only justified when the exploitative effect stems from the very design of the flow and of governance, when the mechanism presents itself as a stable and replicable pattern of operation and when this pattern relevantly affects the consumer's practical autonomy and the integrity of enjoymentp. 666

PTA categoria só se justifica quando o efeito exploratório decorre do próprio desenho do fluxo e da governança, quando o mecanismo se apresenta como padrão estável e replicável de funcionamento e quando esse padrão atinge de modo relevante a autonomia prática do consumidor e a integridade da fruição

When does loss become a routine of the game?

When several small things work together to take advantage of your fragility or to worsen your experience as a routine of the game. They are small frictions that wear you down, losses everyone already finds normal and manufactured urgencies. They are also artificial scarcity, a lack of clarity that works in the company's favor and equivalences that change according to convenience.

You do not need to prove that someone wanted to harm you. What matters is the purpose that shows in the very functioning of the environment. It can be seen in the pattern that repeats, the coherence among the pieces and the effect produced again and again.

What must the game's design not aim at?

Not to build the experience with an exploitative purpose that goes against good faith, transparency and balance, or that disrespects what you legitimately acquired.

How do you show that the problem forms a pattern?

The pattern, the fact that it repeats and the way the pieces of the environment combine. And the relevant impact this has on your freedom of choice in practice, on your understanding or on the utility of what you use.

Can you ask for more than fixing one case?

More than fixing an episode. You can ask for the game's paths to be redone, for transparency to increase and for the design to be corrected. When this design produces a pattern of injury that repeats, the response can also reach the structure that reproduces it.

Is every way of selling and persuading abusive?

Not every way of selling, persuading or running the game is abusive. What is controlled is a program that can be recognized in the functioning of the environment, and not someone's hidden will.

Where it comes from

Whenever microfrictions, normalized losses, manufactured urgencies, artificial scarcities, strategic opacity, elastic equivalences and other mechanisms operate in a coordinated way to extract value from vulnerability or degrade enjoyment as a routine of the environment, there will be a case of abusive teleology.p. 746

PTSempre que microfricções, perdas normalizadas, urgências fabricadas, escassezes artificiais, opacidade estratégica, equivalências elásticas e outros mecanismos operarem de modo coordenado para extrair valor da vulnerabilidade ou degradar a fruição como rotina do ambiente, haverá hipótese de teleologia abusiva.

This is not about investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor about moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governance. The focus is on the functional purpose objectively inscribed in the arrangement, as revealed by its replicability, its internal coherence and its repeated impact on the consumer's experience.p. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada. O foco está na finalidade funcional objetivamente inscrita no arranjo, tal como se revela por sua replicabilidade, por sua coerência interna e por seu impacto reiterado sobre a experiência do consumidor.

The corresponding supply duty is not to structure the experience on the basis of an exploitative purpose incompatible with good faith, transparency, balance and protection of the enjoyment legitimately incorporated into the bond.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de não estruturar a experiência a partir de finalidade exploratória incompatível com boa-fé, transparência, equilíbrio e tutela da fruição legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo.

The minimum required proof consists of demonstrating the pattern, its replicability, the coordination between mechanisms of the ecosystem and the relevant impact produced on practical autonomy, intelligibility or utility of the experience.p. 746-747

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste na demonstração do padrão, de sua replicabilidade, da coordenação entre mecanismos do ecossistema e do impacto relevante produzido sobre autonomia prática, inteligibilidade ou utilidade da experiência.

The institutional consequence goes beyond redress of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced duties of transparency, design correction and structural or collective measures.p. 747

PTA consequência institucional vai além da reparação de episódios isolados e pode incluir reconfiguração de fluxos, deveres reforçados de transparência, correção de desenho e medidas estruturais ou coletivas.

What is controlled is not an inner will, but a recognizable programp. 732

PTO que se controla não é uma vontade íntima, mas um programa reconhecível

When does architecture become an unlawful act?

When governance repeatedly organizes informational noise, decisional friction, evidentiary opacity, non-auditable mutation and insufficient restoration. In this case, unlawfulness no longer lies only in the wording of the contract. The inducement, extraction and normalization of losses become a stable part of the design of the environment, and the case is no longer merely fragmentary nonperformance.

When inducement, extraction and normalization of losses become stably incorporated into the design of the ecosystem, the problem ceases to be merely one of fragmentary nonperformance and comes to constitute a teleological unlawful act of architecture.p. 732

PTQuando essa governança organiza, de forma reiterada, ruído informacional, fricção decisória, opacidade probatória, mutação não auditável e recomposição insuficiente, o ilícito já não está apenas no enunciado. Está na arquitetura.

Why is it imputable conduct?

In continuous services that administer immaterial objects of consumption, performance is not exhausted in the contractual text and cannot be understood without the governance that executes it. Architecture is read as conduct, and therefore it can be imputed to the supplier. The service may organize enjoyment through its architecture, but it cannot do so as a stable program of exploitation.

Where it comes from

The IOC incorporates this layer, but adds another: the reading of architecture as conduct and of abusive teleology as a design defect of the regime of governed enjoyment.p. 573

PTO OIC incorpora essa camada, mas adiciona outra: a leitura da arquitetura como conduta e da teleologia abusiva como vício de concepção do regime de fruição governada.

In continuous digital services that administer Immaterial Objects of Consumption, performance is not exhausted in the contractual text nor can it be understood without the governance that executes it.p. 664

PTEm serviços digitais contínuos que administram Objetos Imateriais de Consumo, a prestação não se exaure no texto contratual nem se deixa compreender sem a governança que a executa.

It is in this sense that the teleological unlawful act of architecture can be qualified as legally imputable conduct.p. 733

PTÉ nesse sentido que o ilícito teleológico de arquitetura pode ser qualificado como conduta juridicamente imputável.

[...] the architecture of the service may organize enjoyment, but it cannot do so as a stable program of exploitation.p. 665

PT[...] a arquitetura do serviço pode organizar a fruição, mas não pode fazê-lo como programa estável de exploração.

What is not at issue?

The examination does not concern style, visual language or interface aesthetics. Nor is there any need to invent a parallel law for platforms, because the qualification is made within the grammar of the CDC.

Function in the regime

It names the form of the unlawful act. It is architecture read as conduct. "The core of the parameter lies in treating architecture as conduct."p. 732 The thesis does not define it autonomously. It always appears alongside abusive teleology, which the conclusion calls a "category of unlawful act of architecture"p. 794. Section 7.1.2 treats it as a "broader hypothesis of unlawful act of architecture"p. 746. Proposed reading, to be confirmed. The unlawful act of architecture is the genus. Abusive teleology is the criterion that identifies it by its purpose.
