You built a community on a social network. Whose is it?

You spent years building a group, a page or a profile, and thousands of people came because of you. One day the network took it all down, and nobody properly explained why.

1 What did you build there?

You created the group, wrote the rules, accepted members, put out fights and kept the conversation alive. People did not come for the network. They came because of the place you built and the way you looked after it.

That has value, and not only for you. An active community brings attention, new people and conversation every day, and that is exactly what the network lives on.

Where it comes from

In other words, community does not appear merely as a “social effect” of the product, but as part of its economic value and of its monetization architecture.p. 135

PTEm outras palavras, a comunidade não aparece apenas como “efeito social” do produto, mas como parte de seu valor econômico e de sua arquitetura de monetização.

2 Does the network just host your group?

No. The network decides who sees what you post, in what order, how prominently and for how long. It also decides what can stay online and what comes down.

That is why the community was never only yours. It exists inside an environment another company maintains, adjusts and can change at any time, with rules you accepted but did not write.

Where it comes from

It manages how this content enters the user's field of perception and how its relevance is socially stabilized.p. 762

PTEla administra a forma como esses conteúdos entram no campo de percepção do usuário e como sua relevância é socialmente estabilizada.

3 Is every community what it seems?

Not every one. Today community building is sold as a marketing strategy, and the network encourages those who create and run groups, because engagement is its product.

There are also communities set up to sell, with fake profiles, bought likes and ordered enthusiasm. The network defines what looks popular and what looks trustworthy, and profits from both, the real one and the facade.

Where it comes from

The platform defines what stands out, what is hidden, what seems more trustworthy, what acquires the appearance of consensus, what emerges as comparable and what becomes practically invisible.p. 763

PTA plataforma define o que se destaca, o que se esconde, o que parece mais confiável, o que adquire aparência de consenso, o que surge como comparável e o que se torna praticamente invisível.

Visibility, narrative, belonging and engagement are not ornaments.p. 22

PTVisibilidade, narrativa, pertencimento e engajamento não são ornamentos.

Community does not arise out of nothing, nor is it sustained by chance.p. 22

PTA comunidade não nasce do nada e nem se mantém por acaso.

4 Why did your posts start reaching fewer people?

One day reach drops. The same posts, made with the same care, start reaching a fraction of the people. You do not know whether it was a new rule, a silent punishment or a ranking criterion that changed.

That is the problem. The drop never even becomes something you can check or challenge, and often the punishment is not expulsion but demotion.

Where it comes from

The change of rule, the lowering of reach or the suspension do not even become a verifiable and disputable object within the system itself.p. 381

PTA alteração de regra, o rebaixamento de alcance ou a suspensão sequer se transformam em objeto verificável e disputável no próprio sistema.

The relevant punishment is often not to expel, but to demote, because remaining inside, but less visible, less eligible and less recognizable, amounts to degrading the position of enjoyment and eroding the value that depended on commensurability and validation in circuit.p. 373

PTA punição relevante muitas vezes não é expulsar, mas rebaixar, porque continuar dentro, porém menos visível, menos elegível e menos reconhecível, equivale a degradar a posição de fruição e a corroer o valor que dependia de comensurabilidade e validação em circuito.

5 And when the whole group disappears?

A generic message arrives about a breach of the community standards. The appeal comes back with another generic message, or does not come back. Sometimes the takedown comes from mass false reports, made by people who wanted the group offline.

The network can punish. What it cannot do is punish without a verifiable reason, without consistency from one decision to the next and without a real path of defence.

Where it comes from

They require verifiable reasons, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTExigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

6 What went away with the group?

The people went, the years of conversation, the files, the reputation you built and the place you held there. For those who earned money from the community, the income went too.

Leaving was never free, and being thrown out costs even more. Whoever loses the group loses the very ability to take part in a community that only existed inside that environment.

Where it comes from

Leaving means abandoning history, reputation, library, items, progression, emotional investments, networks of sociability and, in certain cases, the very ability to take part in communities that organise themselves within that ecosystem.p. 217

PTSair significa abandonar histórico, reputação, biblioteca, itens, progressão, investimentos afetivos, redes de sociabilidade e, em certos casos, a própria capacidade de participar de comunidades que se organizam dentro daquele ecossistema.

7 What can you demand?

You can demand that the network state which rule it applied and for what concrete reason, offer a path of challenge that works and preserve the group's records. When the removal has no verifiable reason, review and restoration can be requested.

If you used the community to earn money, the case can proceed under consumer law or, when it does not reach, under the Civil Code, with more care over proof. What weighs is your real dependence on the network, and not the name the contract gives the relationship.

Where it comes from

This may occur both in e-commerce and in the social circulation of content, whenever the platform substantially alters the functional role of the infrastructure it offered as an environment of exchange, reach or recognition.p. 764

PTIsso pode ocorrer tanto no comércio eletrônico quanto na circulação social de conteúdo, sempre que a plataforma altere substancialmente o papel funcional da infraestrutura que oferecia como ambiente de troca, alcance ou reconhecimento.

What decides is the real structure of subjection, not the contractual appearance of horizontality.p. 780

PTO que decide é a estrutura real da sujeição, não a aparência contratual de horizontalidade.

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Has this happened before?

In 2025, administrators reported losing all the groups they ran on Facebook at once, with accusations that did not match the content. Meta confirmed a technical error affecting the groups and said it was working to fix it, without explaining the cause of the suspensions.

Before that, in 2019, a meme group with almost 500 thousand members was taken down after mass false reports and only came back after the public outcry. And in 2018 the network itself announced the removal of groups and pages in Brazil that bought and sold reactions and followers, facade engagement.
