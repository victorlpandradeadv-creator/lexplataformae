What did the platform take from you?Why doesn't a digital purchase end at delivery?When did this start to seem normal?

It was not your carelessness. The same company that sells the access writes the rules, applies the punishments, keeps the evidence and can change everything after the purchase. Start with what happened to you.

Where it comes from

The vulnerability does not stem from private naivety, but from the absence of robust communicational demarcation between what was admitted, tolerated, official or forbidden.p. 612

PTA vulnerabilidade não decorre de ingenuidade privada, mas da ausência de demarcação comunicacional robusta entre o que era admitido, tolerado, oficial ou proibido.

[...] the Lex Plataformae designates a type of private ordering centred on a platform as an institutional core that defines rules, visibilities, sanctions and regimes of proof through continuous architecture and updatingp. 27

PT[...] a Lex Plataformae designa um tipo de ordenação privada centrada em uma plataforma enquanto núcleo institucional que define regras, visibilidades, sanções e regimes de prova por meio de arquitetura e atualização contínuas

When what is sold is a position of use sustained by a platform, the consumer relationship continues after payment. The supplier keeps governing the object, and it is this continuous governance that calls for its own criteria of duty, proof and consequence.

Where it comes from

What is claimed is that, whenever the utility consumed presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there will be an intangible product in the legally relevant sense for the application of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1.p. 718

PTO que se afirma é que, sempre que a utilidade consumida se apresente como posição de fruição sustentada por governança privada contínua, haverá produto imaterial em sentido juridicamente relevante para a incidência do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC .

The criterion, therefore, is not the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

How the loss happens

  1. 1

    You paid

    You paid with money, with time or with attention, in a store, an app, a social network, an assistant or a game. What you received stayed inside an environment the company maintains.

    Where it comes from

    Passage already quoted abovep. 719

  2. 2

    The company kept control

    The sale ended, but the company's power continued. It keeps writing the rules, updating the service and deciding what you see, what you can use and who stays.

    Where it comes from

    Passage already quoted abovep. 27

  3. 3

    It changed, punished or switched off

    One day the function gets worse, reach drops, the account is suspended or the service ends. Often the decision arrives without a reason you can check or challenge.

    Where it comes from

    The change of rule, the lowering of reach or the suspension do not even become a verifiable and disputable object within the system itself.p. 381

    PTA alteração de regra, o rebaixamento de alcance ou a suspensão sequer se transformam em objeto verificável e disputável no próprio sistema.

  4. 4

    The loss stayed with you

    The loss stays with you, and the records stay with whoever decided. But a digital service cannot change in just any way, and when it crosses the line someone answers for it.

    Where it comes from

The regime in one paragraph

The regime begins with the qualification of the object. When what was offered is a governed enjoyment bundle sustained by continuous governance, and it can lose value without total rupture, the other parameters come into play. The preferred response is to restore the lost function, and monetary compensation remains a residual or complementary response.

Where it comes from

This parameter thus functions as a trigger for the others.p. 719

PTEsse parâmetro exerce, assim, função de gatilho para os demais.

Application is decided neither by technological intuition nor by automatic deference to the contract, but by the cumulative presence of verifiable elements of the regimep. 429

PTA incidência não se decide por intuição tecnológica nem por deferência automática ao contrato, mas pela presença cumulativa de elementos verificáveis do regime

Monetization remains possible, but as a residual or complementary response, not as an automatic reflex of the injury.p. 748

PTA monetização permanece possível, mas como resposta residual ou complementar, não como reflexo automático da lesão.

The original case. You bought a skin. Is it yours?

You paid real money. The skin is in your account, you use it in matches and other players see it.

It looks like yours, and for the most part it is.

Tomorrow it may look different, lose the effect you liked or vanish along with your account, without anyone properly explaining why.

Where it comes from

The promise of distinction remains nominally in the account, but no longer finds, in the world of the game, the fit that gave value to the investment of time, money and attention.p. 104

PTA promessa de distinção permanece nominalmente na conta, mas deixa de encontrar, no mundo do jogo, o encaixe que conferia valor ao investimento de tempo, dinheiro e atenção.

Once the account is terminated, the user will no longer have access to the account, nor to the associated data, nor to the Virtual Content, including in-game currency, and the consequence is definitive loss without refund whenever the landlord deems there was causep. 155

PTEncerrada a conta, o usuário não terá mais acesso à conta, nem aos dados associados, nem ao Conteúdo Virtual, incluindo moeda do jogo, e a consequência é a perda definitiva sem reembolso quando o senhorio entende que havia motivo

Who would you complain to, and on what grounds under Brazilian law?

It is not just games. An AI assistant, an online store or a work app can work the same way whenever what you use depends on an environment the company maintains, updates, reorganizes and controls.

Where it comes from

The criterion, therefore, is not the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

The point here is not to gather digital examples under a single heading out of mere technological affinity, nor to suggest that every contemporary platform reproduces, with identical intensity, the same form of vulnerability, opacity or capture of enjoymentp. 755

PTO ponto, aqui, não é reunir exemplos digitais sob uma mesma rubrica por mera afinidade tecnológica, nem sugerir que toda plataforma contemporânea reproduz, com idêntica intensidade, a mesma forma de vulnerabilidade, opacidade ou captura da fruição

Nothing stands still in these places. Many changes are normal and part of the life of the service. Others are not, and the time, attention, reputation and money you put in never show up on any receipt. When did this start to seem normal?

Where it comes from

It allows us to recognize the sophisticated obvious: continuous digital services change, but they cannot change in just any wayp. 655

PTEla permite reconhecer o óbvio sofisticado: serviços digitais contínuos mudam, mas não podem mudar de qualquer jeito

Ordinary updates, regular curation, anti-abuse interventions and changes that do not reach the functional core of the enjoyment bundle remain within the normal field of performance and do not constitute relevant degradation by definition.p. 430

PTAtualizações ordinárias, curadoria regular, intervenções antiabuso e mudanças que não atingem o núcleo funcional do feixe de fruição permanecem no campo normal de execução e não constituem degradação relevante por definição.

The law cited on this page

  1. Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 3. A supplier is any natural or legal person, public or private, national or foreign, as well as entities without legal personality, that carry out activities of production, assembly, creation, construction, transformation, import, export, distribution or commercialization of products or provision of services. § 1. A product is any good, movable or immovable, material or immaterial. § 2. A service is any activity supplied in the consumer market for remuneration, including those of a banking, financial, credit and insurance nature, except those arising from employment relationships.”

    PT“Art. 3° Fornecedor é toda pessoa física ou jurídica, pública ou privada, nacional ou estrangeira, bem como os entes despersonalizados, que desenvolvem atividade de produção, montagem, criação, construção, transformação, importação, exportação, distribuição ou comercialização de produtos ou prestação de serviços. § 1° Produto é qualquer bem, móvel ou imóvel, material ou imaterial. § 2° Serviço é qualquer atividade fornecida no mercado de consumo, mediante remuneração, inclusive as de natureza bancária, financeira, de crédito e securitária, salvo as decorrentes das relações de caráter trabalhista.”
