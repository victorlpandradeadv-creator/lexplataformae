Your rights

The evidence held by the platform has to come outThe evidence held by the platform has to come out

When does the platform hold all the proof?

When almost everything that shows what happened stays with the platform. The system logs, your account history, the old versions of the screens and the reasons behind each decision are on its side, not yours.

What must the platform do with the records?

The platform must keep and show the minimum records needed to reconstruct what happened, because being open to checking is part of the legitimate way of supplying.

What can you show and what does the platform hold?

On your side, it helps to give a plausible account of what you went through, show how it fits into the game and gather what was within your reach. On the platform's side, it falls to it to present the records, histories, versions and technical justifications that matter for the case.

The more what happened depends on what only the platform records, the less reasonable it is to demand from you detailed proof of something that was never in your hands.

What do you ask for when the records are with the platform?

You can ask for the burden of proof to be distributed according to who has access to the evidence. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions in your favor, which make certain facts count as true until proven otherwise, and a reinforced duty for the platform to cooperate with the evidence.

Does this mean you no longer have to explain what happened?

You still have to tell a coherent, believable story and be clear about what you are asking for. What falls away is the fiction that you and the platform are on equal footing when it comes to proof. Your lack of access to the records cannot become a wall against you.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform holds the elements essential to narrating, demonstrating and reconstructing the conflict, the rules of evidence must recognize that verifiability is part of the very legitimate regime of supply.p. 747

PTSempre que a plataforma concentrar os elementos essenciais para narrar, demonstrar e reconstruir o conflito, a disciplina probatória deverá reconhecer que a verificabilidade integra o próprio regime legítimo do fornecimento.

The corresponding duty is to preserve and disclose the minimum records needed to reconstruct the disputed event.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de preservar e exibir registros mínimos necessários à reconstrução do evento controvertido.

The minimum required proof, from the consumer's side, consists of a plausible account of the harmful experience, of how it fits into the ecosystem and of the accessible contextual elementsp. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível, do ponto de vista do consumidor, consiste em apresentação plausível da experiência lesiva, de sua inserção no ecossistema e dos elementos de contexto acessíveis

from the supplier's side, it requires disclosure of trails, logs, histories, versions and technically delimited justificationsp. 747

PTdo ponto de vista do fornecedor, exige-se exibição de trilhas, logs, históricos, versões e justificativas tecnicamente delimitadas

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to demand from the consumer a detailed showing of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The possible legal qualification involves dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof, presumptions in the consumer's favor and a reinforced duty of cooperationp. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível envolve redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova, presunções favoráveis ao consumidor e reforço do dever de cooperação

This does not remove the need for plausibility, for a coherent line of argument or for a clear delimitation of what is in dispute. What it removes is the fiction of symmetry in environments whose very structure runs on asymmetry of meaning and of proof.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido. O que elimina é a ficção de simetria em ambientes cuja própria estrutura opera pela assimetria de sentido e de prova.

The institutional consequence is to prevent technical opacity from becoming a procedural shieldp. 747

PTA consequência institucional consiste em impedir que a opacidade técnica se converta em blindagem processual
