What if your skin changes tomorrow?

One day the update arrives. Your skin changes its look, gets a different effect or loses an animation you liked. Or the character it is made for gets weakened and stops being worth playing in matches, and almost nobody picks it anymore.

The skin is still in the account and the name is still the same. But what made it valuable, the fit with the rest of the game, with the story and with the recognition of other players, is no longer there. You have the same thing and, at the same time, you do not.

Where it comes from

When an update alters appearance, effects, perceived rarity or economic insertion, the compatibility between experience, narrative and recognition may come undone. The promise of distinction remains nominally in the account, but no longer finds, in the world of the game, the fit that gave value to the investment of time, money and attention.p. 104

PTQuando uma atualização altera aparência, efeitos, raridade percebida ou inserção econômica, pode se desfazer a compatibilidade entre experiência, narrativa e reconhecimento. A promessa de distinção permanece nominalmente na conta, mas deixa de encontrar, no mundo do jogo, o encaixe que conferia valor ao investimento de tempo, dinheiro e atenção.

Does the contract let the company weaken what you bought?

This is provided for in the contract. The terms of use say that the company may alter, update, suspend and even weaken features of the service, including the virtual products you bought. They also say it may apply automatic patches, in the background, with or without notice.

And, in the game, strengthening and weakening are no accident. They are the routine through which each update cycle decides what is worth using and what gets left behind for a while. Patches, character reworks, changes to visual effects and adjustments to the game's economy are not just technical maintenance, because they rewrite who stays, what is worth what and what is recognized inside.

Where it comes from

The Terms of Service state that the supplier may alter, update, suspend and even “nerf” features and parts of the services, including virtual products, in addition to providing for the application of background patches and automatic updates, with or without prior notice Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e).p. 123

PTOs Termos de Serviço afirmam que o fornecedor pode alterar, atualizar, suspender e até “nerfar” recursos e partes dos serviços, inclusive produtos virtuais, além de prever a aplicação de patches em segundo plano e atualizações automáticas, com ou sem aviso prévio (Riot Games, 2024e) .

In League of Legends, buffs and nerfs are not an accessory added after the game, but the very means by which the ecosystem defines, in regular cycles, which characters and strategies remain competitively viable and which are, for a period, downgraded to the condition of suboptimal choicesp. 121

PTNo League of Legends, buffs e nerfs não são um adereço posterior ao jogo, mas o próprio meio pelo qual o ecossistema define, em ciclos regulares, quais personagens e estratégias permanecem competitivamente viáveis e quais ficam, por um período, rebaixadas à condição de escolhas subótimas

Patches, reworks, changes to visual effects and adjustments to the internal economy do not function merely as technical maintenance; they rewrite conditions of permanence, equivalence and recognition within the ecosystem.p. 120

PTPatches, reworks, alterações de efeitos visuais e ajustes de economia interna não funcionam apenas como manutenção técnica; reescrevem condições de permanência, equivalência e reconhecimento dentro do ecossistema.

Is game balance just a technical matter?

A real sequence took place in an online game. A character arrived in the game tied to the universe of a famous animated series, on a set date announced as a big moment.

Over the following year, her strength and the kind of defense she had became a constant topic in players' discussions and received successive technical interventions. At the end of the year, she got a prestige window, tied to a rare cosmetic, with a publicly announced opening date. And at the start of the next year an update replaced the core mechanism that protected her with a shield and expressly took away her damage immunity.

There is no need to guess the intention of whoever decided any of this. The sequence is enough to show that game balance is not just a technical matter. It also regulates who stays at the center, what is acceptable and how much each thing is worth, in step with the cycles of prestige and scarcity that the platform itself administers.

Where it comes from

The official introduction of the character was presented as a narrative and temporal milestone associated with the Arcane universe, with an institutionally fixed release datep. 131-132

PTA introdução oficial da personagem foi apresentada como marco narrativo e temporal associado ao universo de Arcane, com data de disponibilização institucionalmente fixada

The episode involving the champion Mel makes it possible to observe, more sharply, how the technical rationality of balancing can operate coupled to the symbolic economy of the ecosystem, without the need to attribute subjective intention to the decision-maker.p. 131

PTO episódio envolvendo a campeã Mel permite observar, com maior nitidez, como a racionalidade técnica do balanceamento pode operar acoplada à economia simbólica do ecossistema, sem que seja necessário atribuir intenção subjetiva ao decisor.

First, her strength and the kind of defensive interaction her kit allowed became a recurring object of public evaluation and technical interventionp. 132

PTEm primeiro lugar, sua força e o tipo de interação defensiva que seu kit permitia passaram a ser objeto recorrente de avaliação pública e de intervenção técnica

Second, already in the end-of-year cycle, this centrality was connected to a prestige window linked to rare cosmetic content, with a publicly recorded opening datep. 132

PTEm segundo lugar, já no ciclo de fim de ano, essa centralidade se conectou a uma janela de prestígio vinculada a conteúdo cosmético raro, com data de abertura publicamente registrada

Then, at the start of 2026, patch 26.3 brought about a relevant respecification of the champion's mode of enjoyment by altering the core protection mechanism that sustained her pattern of interaction, replacing the previous logic with a shield and expressly removing damage immunityp. 132

PTEm seguida, no início de 2026, o patch 26.3 promoveu uma reespecificação relevante do modo de fruição da campeã ao alterar o mecanismo central de proteção que sustentava seu padrão de interação, substituindo a lógica anterior por um escudo e retirando, de forma expressa, a imunidade a dano

The sequence is sufficient to support the view that design does not act merely as technical optimization, but as social mediation that regulates centrality, acceptability and functional value, including when this regulation is synchronized with cycles of prestige and scarcity administered by the platform itselfp. 132

PTO encadeamento é suficiente para sustentar que o design não atua apenas como otimização técnica, mas como mediação social que regula centralidade, aceitabilidade e valor funcional, inclusive quando essa regulação se encontra sincronizada com ciclos de prestígio e escassez administrados pela própria plataforma

Object and laboratoryNerf

Nerf is the word players use for the deliberate weakening, done by update, of an attribute, an ability or an item. The character hits less, the item protects less, the ability takes longer to come back.

It is the clearest example of what this part shows. The name stays the same and access stays the same, while what you actually receive changes inside. And the permanence of the name comes to work as proof in favor of whoever made the change, because, at first sight, nothing has disappeared.

Where it comes from

A privileged way of visualizing this loss of verifiability is the nerf, understood as the technically deliberate weakening of attributes, abilities or items, by means of a patch, to make something less powerful in the practical functioning of the gamep. 328

PTUm modo privilegiado de visualizar essa perda de verificabilidade é o nerf, entendido como enfraquecimento tecnicamente deliberado de atributos, habilidades ou itens, por meio de patch, para tornar algo menos poderoso no funcionamento prático do jogo

The nerf is the limit case because it shows, with precision, how the continuity of the name and of access can work as self-justifying proof, while the relevant delivery shifts in what the environment counts as power and utility.p. 329

PTO nerf é o caso-limite porque mostra, com precisão, como a continuidade do nome e do acesso pode funcionar como prova autojustificante, enquanto a entrega relevante se desloca naquilo que o ambiente conta como potência e utilidade.

Now, an important caution. Not every change is a loss, and changing is not illegal. An online game has to stay alive. A new graphics engine, memory limits, loading times, compatibility with different computers and technical balance may require adjustments to the way the skin looks.

That is why the question is not whether the company can change something. It can. The question is under what conditions a change remains compatible with what was promised and with the value you bought.

And there is one more caution for those who bought a skin. A purely cosmetic change is not, as a rule, enough. It starts to matter when the symbolic value of the skin was at the core of what was sold, or when the change combines with other signs of a pattern that points to exploiting the user.

Where it comes from

Mutability, in itself, is not unlawful.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita.

Therefore, gradual changes to the graphics engine, the game's memory budget, loading routines and technical balance itself may, out of the need for performance and compatibility, modify the way the item presents itselfp. 439

PTPor isso, alterações graduais do motor gráfico, do orçamento de memória do jogo, das rotinas de carregamento e do próprio equilíbrio técnico podem, por necessidade de performance e compatibilidade, modificar o modo como o item se apresenta

The question ceases to be whether changes are possible and becomes under what conditions they can be treated as compatible with the promise and with the preservation of relevant value.p. 330

PTA questão deixa de ser se mudanças são possíveis e passa a ser em que condições elas podem ser tratadas como compatíveis com a promessa e com a preservação do valor relevante.

The third limit concerns situations in which symbolic or semiotic value is functionally accessory and is not part of the core of the promised enjoyment bundle.p. 434

PTO terceiro limite recai sobre situações em que o valor simbólico ou semiótico é funcionalmente acessório e não integra o núcleo do feixe de fruição prometido.

Therefore, as a rule, predominantly aesthetic changes fall outside the reach of the concept, except when, in the concrete case, they form a convergent set of signs capable of supporting objective recognition of an unlawful act of architecture through structural indications of abusive teleology.p. 434

PTPor isso, em regra, ficam fora do alcance do conceito alterações predominantemente estéticas, salvo quando, no caso concreto, componham um conjunto convergente de sinais apto a sustentar reconhecimento objetivo de ilícito arquitetural por indícios estruturais de teleologia abusiva.

Functional value equivalence

There is a test that answers this. After the change, does the skin still play the same role in your experience? Does it still do what it used to do, hold the same place among other players and, when that matters, still say what it said about you?

The test is demanding. It is not enough for the skin to keep working, or to keep the same numbers in some isolated attribute. What counts is the role it had, and that includes its usefulness, its relationship with others and, in some cases, its meaning. An item can stay in your account and open normally and still have lost what justified the purchase.

At the same time, nobody demands that nothing change, or that the new version be identical to the old one. What is demanded is that the change not hollow out what was bought. Digital services change, but they cannot change in just any way.

Where it comes from

What matters is the substantial preservation of the role played by the object in the acquired experience, considering its concrete utility in the ecosystem, its relational insertion in the environment of use and, when legally relevant, its identity or symbolic density.p. 729

PTO que importa é a preservação substancial do papel exercido pelo objeto na experiência adquirida, considerando sua utilidade concreta no ecossistema, sua inserção relacional no ambiente de uso e, quando juridicamente relevante, sua densidade identitária ou simbólica.

Functional value equivalence cannot be reduced to maintaining technical performance in the strict sense, nor to the quantitative preservation of isolated attributes. Its content is more demanding.p. 729

PTA equivalência de valor funcional não se reduz à manutenção de desempenho técnico em sentido estrito, nem à preservação quantitativa de atributos isolados. Seu conteúdo é mais exigente.

The point is to affirm that the lawfulness of the change cannot be assessed merely by the nominal subsistence of the object, by the formal maintenance of access or by the abstract persistence of some residual utility.p. 795

PTO ponto é afirmar que a licitude da alteração não pode ser aferida apenas pela subsistência nominal do objeto, pela manutenção formal de acesso ou pela persistência abstrata de alguma utilidade residual.

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between the earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.

It allows us to recognize the sophisticated obvious: continuous digital services change, but they cannot change in just any way; IOCs evolve, but they cannot be emptied under the label of evolutionp. 655

PTEla permite reconhecer o óbvio sofisticado: serviços digitais contínuos mudam, mas não podem mudar de qualquer jeito; OICs evoluem, mas não podem ser esvaziados sob o rótulo de evolução

Was the change normal, or did it hollow out what you bought?

A few questions help separate a normal change from one that hollows out what you bought.

Did the update take away an important function of the item? Did it weaken the item so much that it lost the usefulness you paid for? Did it change the screen in a way that disrupts everyday use? Did it rewrite the rules in a way that downgrades what had been promised as equivalent?

If any answer is yes, the change may have broken the equivalence. The platform was allowed to change things, but in that case it should have preserved the value of what you had or made room for restoration, correction or review. And you do not need to prove you fully own the item to complain about this. It is enough to show that the usefulness you acquired was downgraded arbitrarily.

Where it comes from

The strength of the criterion appears sharply in cases such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that empty acquired utility, interface changes that disrupt ordinary use, redefinitions of rules that downgrade the equivalence of what was promisedp. 637

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida, mudanças de interface que desorganizam o uso ordinário, redefinições de regras que rebaixam a equivalência do que foi prometido

Functional value equivalence allows, precisely, a much more robust intermediate position: it recognizes that the platform may make changes, but requires that the change preserve, in substantial terms, the functional value of the acquired experience or, failing that, make room for restoration, correction, review or another adequate remedyp. 637

PTA equivalência de valor funcional permite, justamente, uma posição intermediária muito mais robusta: reconhece-se que a plataforma pode alterar, mas exige-se que a alteração preserve, em termos substanciais, o valor funcional da experiência adquirida ou, não o fazendo, abra espaço a recomposição, correção, revisão ou outro remédio adequado

It is not necessary to demonstrate full dominion over the object to maintain that the acquired enjoyment cannot be arbitrarily downgraded.p. 637

PTNão é preciso demonstrar domínio pleno sobre o objeto para sustentar que a fruição adquirida não pode ser rebaixada arbitrariamente.

What pattern emerges when the skin gets worse but stays in your account?

The pattern is this. The worsened skin does not disappear. It stays there, weaker, less central and less useful, or less equivalent to what justified the purchase.

You did not lose a minor detail. You lost part of what you bought and, often, you are nudged to look for something else in the same store to get back what you had. This is extraction by attrition, where the company profits from wearing down what it has already sold. The thing still exists, but existing is not the same as still being what you bought.

Where it comes from

Nerfs, reconfigurations, balancing adjustments, gradual suppressions of function, shifts of utility to new layers of acquisition and ordinary redefinitions of equivalence operate as techniques of extraction by erosion. The object does not leave the scene entirely. It remains there, but worsened, weaker, less central, less useful or less equivalent to what justified the acquisition.p. 672

PTNerfs, reconfigurações, ajustes de balanceamento, supressões graduais de função, deslocamentos de utilidade para novas camadas de aquisição e redefinições ordinárias de equivalência operam como técnicas de extração por erosão. O objeto não sai de cena por completo. Continua ali, mas piorado, mais fraco, menos central, menos útil ou menos equivalente ao que justificou a aquisição.

The consumer does not lose merely “a feature”; they lose the functional substance of what they bought and are often pushed to seek substitutes within the same ecosystem.p. 672

PTO consumidor não perde apenas “uma feature”; perde substância funcional do que comprou e é frequentemente empurrado a buscar substitutos dentro do mesmo ecossistema.

An object may continue to exist and, even so, no longer be, in a legally relevant sense, what was acquired.p. 795

PTUm objeto pode continuar existindo e, ainda assim, já não ser, em sentido jurídico relevante, aquilo que foi adquirido.

LawCaptive contracts

Why not just accept it and move on? Because in a relationship that lasts for years, you are not as free to say no as it seems. If you have already put in time and money, and you depend on the game to keep using what you have, you accept the change because leaving would cost more. In these long contracts, the yes that comes later is often just forced acceptance of the new state of things.

That is why freedom of contract has limits when a contract runs over time. The power to change what is delivered cannot be used to defeat the purpose that justified the purchase. And Brazil's Consumer Protection Code already says clearly that a clause allowing the supplier to change, on its own, the content or quality of what it delivers after the contract is made is void.

Where it comes from

[...] but because it helps to visualize something that continuous digital consumption lays wide open: performance is not exhausted in the initial adherence, and the bond comes to be structured by practical dependence, permanent asymmetry, sedimented trust and the supplier's renewed power of imposition over timep. 559

PT[...] mas porque ela ajuda a visualizar algo que o consumo digital contínuo escancara: a prestação não se esgota na adesão inicial, e o vínculo passa a ser estruturado por dependência prática, assimetria permanente, confiança sedimentada e poder renovado de imposição do fornecedor ao longo do tempo

In captive contracts, the so-called supervening “consent” is often nothing more than forced adherence to the new state of affairs, especially when the consumer is already invested in the relationship and depends on the ecosystem to keep enjoying what they acquired.p. 654-655

PTEm contratos cativos, o chamado “consenso” superveniente frequentemente não passa de adesão forçada ao novo estado de coisas, especialmente quando o consumidor já está investido na relação e depende do ecossistema para continuar fruindo aquilo que adquiriu.

Contractual freedom finds limits in the social function of the contract, and, in continued consumer relationships, this limitation takes a very concrete form: the power to alter, modulate or reconfigure the performance cannot be exercised so as to frustrate the function that justified the consumer's original acquisitionp. 561

PTA liberdade contratual encontra limites na função social do contrato, e, em relações de consumo continuadas, essa limitação ganha forma muito concreta: o poder de alterar, modular ou reconfigurar a prestação não pode ser exercido de modo a frustrar a função que justificou a aquisição original pelo consumidor

On the most classic plane of the CDC, this prohibition already appears sufficiently clearly. A clause authorizing unilateral modification of the content or quality of the performance after contracting is voidp. 561

PTNo plano mais clássico do CDC, essa vedação já aparece de forma suficientemente clara. É nula a cláusula que autorize modificação unilateral do conteúdo ou da qualidade da prestação após a contratação

Now try to prove what changed. You would need to show when the change happened, on what criterion and with what effect on what you had. But it is the platform that keeps the system records, the old versions, the histories and the reasons behind each decision.

Whoever controls these traces largely controls how what happened can be reconstructed. And as long as you cannot show the difference, the fact that the name and the signs stayed the same works as proof for the other side.

Where it comes from

Whoever controls logs, histories, decision criteria, relevant versions, interface records, moderation flows, alteration parameters and traces of sanction controls, to a large extent, the conditions for the legal reconstruction of what happened.p. 734

PTQuem controla logs, históricos, critérios decisórios, versões relevantes, registros de interface, fluxos de moderação, parâmetros de alteração e vestígios de sanção controla, em larga medida, as condições de reconstrução jurídica do que ocorreu.

Without this minimum, the dispute tends to be resolved by surface and impression, because the continuity of signs works as self-justifying proof while the consumer cannot demonstrate what changed, by which criteria and with what effect on the acquired positionp. 326

PTSem esse mínimo, a disputa tende a ser resolvida por superfície e impressão, porque a continuidade dos sinais funciona como prova autojustificante enquanto o consumidor não consegue demonstrar o que mudou, por quais critérios e com qual efeito sobre a posição adquirida

Open auditability

The platform cannot change what you use and, at the same time, keep in absolute secrecy what changed, when, why, what effect it had and which records make it possible to reconstruct the case. This duty is not a new law with a name of its own. It comes from the duty to inform, from good faith and from making it easier for consumers to defend their rights, which the law already provides for, and its purpose is to make the change verifiable.

Nor is this about opening up the game's code or handing over the company's secrets. What is asked for is a minimum that can be demonstrated, enough to see whether there was a relevant change, connect it to what was promised and check whether it was justified. The transparency required is sufficient, not total.

There is also a limit. This duty does not arise with every small adjustment or purely cosmetic change. It begins when the change genuinely affects what you use, your ability to stay or your chance to prove and contest what happened.

Where it comes from

In other words, the platform cannot claim to alter the regime of enjoyment and, at the same time, keep in absolute secrecy what it actually altered, when it altered it, why it altered it, what relevant effects the change produces and which records make it possible to reconstruct the episode in case of conflictp. 636

PTEm outras palavras, a plataforma não pode pretender alterar o regime de fruição e, ao mesmo tempo, manter em sigilo absoluto o que de fato alterou, quando alterou, por que alterou, que efeitos relevantes a mudança produz e quais registros permitem reconstruir o episódio em caso de conflito

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and trade secrets, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of performance; an unfolding of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of facilitating the consumer's defensep. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor

The standard does not require exposure of industrial secrets as a purifying fetish. It requires, rather, a demonstrable minimum capable of preventing information, good faith and transparency from turning into empty performances.p. 636

PTO standard não exige exposição do segredo industrial como fetiche purificador. Exige, isso sim, um mínimo demonstrável capaz de impedir que informação, boa-fé e transparência se convertam em performances vazias.

What is needed is sufficient intelligibility to identify the occurrence of a relevant change, relate it to the promised enjoyment, verify whether there was a justification compatible with the legal regimep. 728

PTO que precisa é de inteligibilidade suficiente para identificar a ocorrência de alteração relevante, relacioná-la com a fruição prometida, verificar se houve justificativa compatível com o regime jurídico

Not total transparency, but sufficient transparency.p. 728

PTNão transparência total, mas transparência suficiente.

Open auditability is not demandable in the face of any microscopic adjustment, any cosmetic change or any irrelevant fluctuation of the service.p. 642

PTAuditabilidade aberta não é exigível diante de qualquer ajuste microscópico, qualquer mudança cosmética ou qualquer oscilação irrelevante do serviço.

And what about the frustration you feel? If the platform promised value, rarity, performance or an edge, and then downgraded all of it on criteria nobody can see, that frustration is not just an annoyance. It may be a sign that the delivery itself failed, and so it may carry legal weight.

Where it comes from

If the platform promises a curated experience of value, rarity, performance or advantage, and then rebalances or downgrades it under opaque criteria, the frustration is not mere displeasure. It may be a relevant inadequacy of the governed enjoyment itself.p. 634

PTSe a plataforma promete uma experiência curada de valor, raridade, desempenho ou vantagem, e depois a reequilibra ou a rebaixa sob critérios opacos, a frustração não é mero dissabor. Pode ser inadequação relevante da própria fruição governada.

What can you demand?

A relevant change has to leave a trailA relevant change has to leave a trail

When does a change need to leave a trail?

When the platform changes something important about your access, the item's performance, its usefulness or the rules of the game, or when it hands out a punishment, and does so without keeping the minimum record needed to reconstruct what happened. Think of an item that loses strength in an update with no explanation you can check, or an account suspended without anyone knowing on what basis.

What trail must the platform keep?

A minimum of verifiability, which means making it possible to know what was changed, when, on what justification and with what effect on what you use.

This duty is not a new law with a name of its own. It comes from the duty to inform, from good faith and from making it easier for consumers to defend their rights, which the law already provides for. It does not require opening up all of the game's code or making the company's secrets public. The examination should be limited to what matters for the case, while keeping the necessary confidentiality.

Which records make it possible to understand the change or the punishment?

The essential system records, the history of the versions that matter, the records of the punishment and minimal justifications. Together with the context data, this should be enough for whoever decides the case to understand the problem. Almost all of it is usually in the platform's hands, not yours.

What can you ask for to reconstruct the change?

You can ask that these records be preserved and shown, and that an expert examination, meaning a review by a specialist, be carried out on specific points. You can also ask for a check, based on the minimum clarity the platform owes, of whether the change or the punishment was legitimate. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions against the platform, which make certain facts count in your favor when the records only it had are missing.

Does every small adjustment require a record?

Not every small adjustment, purely cosmetic change or minor fluctuation calls for this trail. The duty begins when the change significantly affects what you use, your ability to stay or your chance to prove and contest what happened.

Where it comes from

Whenever relevant changes of access, performance, utility, operating rule or sanction affect enjoyment without the preservation of minimum trails for institutional reconstruction, there will be a violation of the open auditability parameter.p. 745

PTSempre que alterações relevantes de acesso, desempenho, utilidade, regra de funcionamento ou sanção incidirem sobre a fruição sem preservação de trilhas mínimas de reconstrução institucional, haverá violação do parâmetro de auditabilidade aberta.

The corresponding duty of supply is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was altered, when it was altered, on what grounds it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de manter verificabilidade mínima sobre o que foi alterado, quando o foi, por que motivo foi justificado e quais efeitos funcionalmente relevantes produziu.

It is for this reason that open auditability must be understood as an internal unfolding of the duty to inform and not as an institution foreign to the microsystem.p. 641

PTÉ por essa razão que auditabilidade aberta deve ser entendida como desdobramento interno do dever de informação e não como instituto estranho ao microssistema.

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and trade secrets, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of performance; an unfolding of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of facilitating the consumer's defensep. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor

The requirement of open auditability is not to be confused with full opening of the source code nor with suppression of the technical confidentiality of the software.p. 643

PTA exigência de auditabilidade aberta não se confunde com abertura integral do código-fonte nem com supressão do sigilo técnico do software.

The minimum required proof consists of essential logs, history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements sufficient to make the controversy cognizable.p. 745

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste em logs essenciais, histórico de versões relevantes, registros de sanção, justificativas operacionais mínimas, dados de contexto e elementos técnicos delimitados suficientes para tornar a controvérsia cognoscível.

The institutional consequence includes orders for preservation and display of records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and review of the lawfulness of the alteration or sanction based on minimum institutional intelligibility.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional inclui ordens de preservação e exibição de registros, perícia delimitada, presunções desfavoráveis ao fornecedor e controle da licitude da alteração ou sanção com base em inteligibilidade institucional mínima.

Open auditability is not demandable in the face of any microscopic adjustment, any cosmetic change or any irrelevant fluctuation of the service.p. 642

PTAuditabilidade aberta não é exigível diante de qualquer ajuste microscópico, qualquer mudança cosmética ou qualquer oscilação irrelevante do serviço.

It may affect access and permanence, the functional integrity of the object, promised equivalences, the symbolic position of the IOC in the ecosystem, or even the consumer's conditions of proof and contestation.p. 642

PTPode atingir acesso e permanência, integridade funcional do objeto, equivalências prometidas, posição simbólica do OIC no ecossistema, ou mesmo as condições de prova e contestação do consumidor.

If it changes, it must keep the value it hadIf it changes, it must keep the value it had

When does an update take away the function of what you bought?

When the platform changes the experience in a way that genuinely worsens, hollows out or diverts the function that led you to buy. Think of an item that, after an adjustment, is still in your inventory but has become so weak it no longer has a reason to exist, or a feature that let you do something and was taken away.

You can still be in the game, with your account, seeing the item and logging in whenever you like, and still have lost what mattered most. Keeping something working is not the same as keeping its value.

What must the platform preserve in the item?

In substance, the role the item had in your experience. That includes what it does, the relationship it creates with other players and, when this matters legally, what it means.

How can you show what changed in the item's usefulness?

Show what usefulness the item had before, what changed, what concrete impact the change had and why the compensation offered, if any, is not enough.

What solution can restore the lost usefulness?

First, getting the function back. That may mean undoing the change when it is technically possible, replacing the item with something equivalent or correcting the way the update was carried out. Money should not be the automatic answer. Depending on the case, the loss may be treated as a quality defect in the product or service, which is the law's name for a problem that makes something less useful than it should be.

Does this mean the game can never change?

The platform can update the game, and nobody demands that the item stay identical forever or that the game stand still. Many changes really are the natural evolution of the environment. The problem is a change that seriously worsens what you bought and leaves you to bear the cost, with no restoration to match.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform alters the experience so as to substantially degrade, empty or divert the function that justified the acquisition, there will be a rupture of functional value equivalence.p. 745

PTSempre que a plataforma alterar a experiência de modo a degradar, esvaziar ou desviar substancialmente a função que justificou a aquisição, haverá ruptura da equivalência de valor funcional.

The strength of the criterion appears sharply in cases such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that empty acquired utilityp. 637

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida

The consumer remains formally “inside” the ecosystem, still has the account, still sees the object, can still access the platform. Even so, the enjoyed utility may be substantially downgradedp. 635

PTO consumidor continua formalmente “dentro” do ecossistema, continua com a conta, continua vendo o objeto, continua podendo acessar a plataforma. Ainda assim, a utilidade fruída pode ser substancialmente rebaixada

The corresponding duty of supply is to preserve, in substantial terms, the functional, relational and, when legally relevant, symbolic role of the object in the consumer's experience.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de preservar, em termos substanciais, o papel funcional, relacional e, quando juridicamente relevante, simbólico do objeto na experiência do consumidor.

The minimum required proof calls for demonstration of the utility previously incorporated into the bond, of the change that occurred, of the concrete functional impact of the alteration and of the insufficiency of any restoration offered.p. 745-746

PTA prova mínima exigível requer demonstração da utilidade anteriormente incorporada ao vínculo, da mudança ocorrida, do impacto funcional concreto da alteração e da insuficiência de eventual recomposição oferecida.

The possible legal qualification tends to fall on a defect in the quality of the product or servicep. 746

PTA qualificação jurídica possível tende a recair em vício de qualidade do produto ou serviço

The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent replacement and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional possível prioriza recomposição funcional, reversão técnica quando viável, substituição equivalente e correção da forma de atualização, em vez de mera monetização automática da perda.

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between the earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.

What the parameter rejects is not change itself, but its instrumentalization to transfer to the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration.p. 730

PTO que o parâmetro recusa não é a mudança em si, mas sua instrumentalização para transferir ao consumidor o custo de reconfigurações que destroem ou rebaixam significativamente a utilidade adquirida sem recomposição compatível.

Nerfs, removals, reworks, migrations, rule changes, reordering of access, reconfiguration of interfaces and suppression of functionalities are usually narrated as the natural evolution of the environment. In many cases, they may in fact be just that.p. 730

PTNerfs, remoções, reworks, migrações, mudanças de regra, reordenação de acessos, reconfiguração de interfaces e supressão de funcionalidades costumam ser narrados como evolução natural do ambiente. Em muitos casos, de fato, podem sê-lo.
