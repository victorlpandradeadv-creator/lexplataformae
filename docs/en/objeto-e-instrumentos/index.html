Object and instruments

The method movement of the thesis, on one page. The object is the platform's regime seen in the laboratory, and each field of knowledge enters as a lens to read it, until it reaches legal qualification.

In the original text

This passage separates what the research studies, the regime of governed enjoyment, from what it reads it with, the conceptual keys of other fields.

To keep the inferential path controllable, the literature mobilized throughout the work is treated through a methodological distinction between object and instruments16 Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 69). The object consists of the regime of enjoyment governed by platform and of its mechanisms of promise, expectation, modulation and sanction. The instruments are the conceptual keys used to qualify this regime in language apt for normative incidence, which makes it possible to convert phenomena of architecture, communication and technical execution into operational categories compatible with duties, limits and legal consequences. In practical terms, frameworks from political economy, sociology, philosophy, anthropology of consumption and communication theory enter to guide the identification of relevant mechanisms and discipline inferences, without imposing an exegetical reconstruction of the works mobilized, so that the argument p. 47 remains traceable to trackable evidence and to recognizable normative parameters Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 69-70). Such a distinction aims to prevent both exegetical drift and the merely ornamental use of concepts. p. 46-47

PTPara manter controlável o percurso inferencial, a literatura mobilizada ao longo do trabalho é tratada por distinção metodológica entre objeto e instrumentos (Eco, 2007, p. 69) . O objeto consiste no regime de fruição governada por plataforma e nos seus mecanismos de promessa, expectativa, modulação e sanção. Os instrumentos são as chaves conceituais utilizadas para qualificar esse regime em linguagem apta à incidência normativa, o que permite converter fenômenos de arquitetura, comunicação e execução técnica em categorias operacionais compatíveis com deveres, limites e consequências jurídicas. Em termos práticos, referenciais de economia política, sociologia, filosofia, antropologia do consumo e teoria da comunicação ingressam para orientar a identificação de mecanismos relevantes e disciplinar inferências, sem impor reconstrução exegética das obras mobilizadas, de modo que o argumento permaneça reconduzível a evidências rastreáveis e a parâmetros normativos reconhecíveis (Eco, 2007, p. 69-70) . Tal distinção visa impedir tanto a deriva exegética quanto o uso meramente ornamental de conceitos.
