Your store sells through the app. Who runs your business?

You run a restaurant on iFood, or sell on Mercado Livre, Shopee or Amazon. Customers arrive through the platform's screen, and it decides who appears, how much it costs to appear and who stays there.

1 Whose customer is it?

The customer buys from you, but arrives through the platform. It shows your store, keeps the order history and decides whether the customer sees you again tomorrow.

Without its screen, your store is invisible. That dependence is the starting point of everything.

Where it comes from

Here, the formal freedom to join or leave the environment coexists with intense dependence on the private conditions governing access to demand, earnings calculation, reputation building, performance assessment and continued eligibility in the productive flow.p. 766

PTA liberdade formal de aderir ou sair do ambiente convive, aqui, com dependência intensa em relação às condições privadas de acesso à demanda, de cálculo de ganhos, de formação de reputação, de leitura de desempenho e de manutenção da elegibilidade no fluxo produtivo.

2 How much does it cost to show up?

More and more. Promotions, coupons you subsidise and featured plans become a condition for showing up well. The platform decides which stores will be seen, in what order and with which badges.

Whoever controls where you appear controls, in practice, how much you sell.

Where it comes from

It defines, or at least strongly conditions, which competitors will be visible, on what terms they will be compared, under what reputation signals they will appear, what kind of friction they will suffer, what strategic data will be extracted from their activity and how the user will perceive the very space of choice.p. 771

PTEla define, ou pelo menos condiciona fortemente, quais concorrentes serão visíveis, em que termos serão comparados, sob que sinais de reputação aparecerão, que tipo de atrito sofrerão, que dados estratégicos serão extraídos de sua atuação e como o usuário perceberá o próprio espaço de escolha.

3 And when the rule changes?

The commission rises, the cancellation policy changes and the ranking criterion is redone. It all arrives by notice, without negotiation. You stay inside, but in a worse position.

A change like that does not need to exclude you to take away the value of staying there.

Where it comes from

Functional value equivalence, in turn, makes it possible to control rule changes that, without formally excluding certain agents, significantly lower their practical position in the ecosystem, emptying the usefulness of competitive permanence under the pretext of updating, optimisation or a change of internal policy.p. 772

PTA equivalência de valor funcional, por sua vez, permite controlar alterações de regra que, sem excluir formalmente determinados agentes, rebaixem de modo relevante sua posição prática no ecossistema, esvaziando a utilidade da permanência concorrencial sob o pretexto de atualização, otimização ou mudança de política interna.

4 Is the rating fair?

Not always. Your rating drops because of a delivery delay you do not control, a cancellation that was not yours or a complaint you cannot challenge. And the rating defines who appears first.

You answer to a reputation system whose criteria you cannot check.

5 Who owns the buyers' data?

The platform. It knows who buys from you, how much, when and what else they look for, and you see only part of it. This data is worth a lot, including to the platform itself.

Where it comes from

Open auditability applies as a requirement of minimum intelligibility over relevant criteria of ranking, highlighting, recommendation, self-preferencing, economic moderation of the environment and any use of strategic data obtained from the intermediation itself.p. 772

PTA auditabilidade aberta incide como exigência de inteligibilidade mínima sobre critérios relevantes de ranking, destaque, recomendação, preferenciamento, moderação econômica do ambiente e eventual uso de dados estratégicos obtidos da própria intermediação.

6 Does the platform also compete with you?

Sometimes, yes. It organises the market in which you sell and, at the same time, can sell on its own account, favour partners or sign exclusivity deals. When that happens, the referee also plays.

The variety of stores on the screen does not guarantee a real choice, neither for the customer nor for you.

Where it comes from

The platform that governs the user's experience governs, at the same time, the perceptible field in which competition becomes possible, legible and economically effective.p. 769

PTA plataforma que governa a experiência do usuário governa, ao mesmo tempo, o campo perceptível em que a competição se torna possível, legível e economicamente eficaz.

The illusion of plurality can thus coexist with a severe compression of the real conditions of choice.p. 770

PTA ilusão de pluralidade pode, assim, conviver com severa compressão das condições reais de escolha.

7 What can you demand when the store disappears?

You can demand the concrete reason for the suspension, a real chance of defence, the preservation of the store's records and minimum clarity about the ranking and reputation criteria. When the suspension has no verifiable reason, reinstatement can be requested.

Selling does not strip you of all protection. Consumer law can reach the merchant who depends on the platform to that degree. When it does not reach, the case proceeds under the Civil Code, and practices that distort competition can be taken to Cade, the competition authority.

Where it comes from

A seller who depends on ranking, reputation, visibility and opaque prioritization rules does not become immune to consumer protection merely because they sell.p. 778

PTO seller que depende de ranking, reputação, visibilidade e regras opacas de priorização não se torna imune à tutela consumerista apenas porque vende.

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Has this happened before?

In Goiás, a merchant had her iFood profile blocked without notice. The Public Defender's Office argued that the block, without notification and without a chance of defence, violated the right to be heard and interrupted an activity of more than two years. The Court of Justice recognised that the block was wrongful, with reinstatement and compensation.

At Cade, the investigation into iFood's exclusivity with restaurants ended in a 2023 settlement that banned exclusivity with chains of 30 or more outlets and set other limits. iFood was not fined, and the settlement itself treats exclusivity as lawful within those limits. The case shows the weight of whoever organises the market in which everyone sells.
