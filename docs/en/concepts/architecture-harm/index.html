Architecture harm

In plain words, on the path of the skin. What if many people lose out the same way?

What it is

It is the injury produced by the very design of the supply regime. The unity of the problem does not come mainly from the repetition of episodes, but from the common harmful source. This functional unity is what justifies collective institutional treatment.

Where it comes from

In platform environments, the harmful source may lie in the very design of the supply regime, so that the unity of the problem does not stem primarily from the empirical repetition of episodesp. 774

PTEm ambientes de plataforma, a fonte lesiva pode residir no próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, de modo que a unidade do problema não decorre primariamente da repetição empírica de episódios

It refers to an injury produced by the very design of the supply regime, and therefore endowed with sufficient functional unity to justify collective institutional treatment.p. 774

PTRefere-se a lesão produzida pelo próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, e por isso dotada de unidade funcional suficiente para justificar tratamento institucional coletivo.

Where can you study it in depth?

In the chapter Abusive teleology and the unlawful act of architecture, on the jurist's path.
