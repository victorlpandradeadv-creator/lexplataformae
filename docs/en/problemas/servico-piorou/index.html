Your rights · By problem

The service got worse, and the price is still the same. Can I complain?

You pay the same as before but get less, because the service lost features, tightened limits, got slower or started demanding more to deliver the same thing. The name and the price stayed the same while the delivery shrank, and consumer law treats as void any change the company makes on its own to the content or quality of what it sells.

Where it comes from

The reduction rarely appears as an explicit cut of “classes”, but it may manifest as loss of reach, persistent downgrading of performance, cumulative restriction of functionalities, compression of limits, reordering of visibility or structural changes that raise the cost of enjoying the same utility. The logic is the same. The label and the price are kept, and the delivery of value is downgraded.p. 480

PTA redução raramente aparece como corte explícito de “aulas”, mas pode se manifestar como perda de alcance, rebaixamento persistente de performance, restrição cumulativa de funcionalidades, compressão de limites, reordenação de visibilidade ou alterações estruturais que elevam o custo de fruir a mesma utilidade. A lógica é a mesma. Mantêm-se o rótulo e o preço, e rebaixa-se a entrega de valor.

If continuity is the normal regime, the enjoyed utility stabilizes as an expectation that the level of delivery will be maintained, and not as an expectation of formal access to a service that can be reconfigured at the supplier's convenience. That is why the CDC treats unilateral modification of content or quality as nullity.p. 479

PTSe a continuidade é o regime normal, a utilidade fruída se estabiliza como expectativa de manutenção do patamar de entrega, e não como expectativa de acesso formal a um serviço que pode ser reconfigurado por conveniência do fornecedor. Por isso, o CDC trata a modificação unilateral de conteúdo ou qualidade como nulidade.

How can you tell the decline is not just your impression?

A course that cuts half its classes in the middle of the semester and keeps charging the same price does what anyone would find wrong. On platforms the cut almost never goes by that name, and instead shows up as reduced reach, performance degraded little by little, features that disappear and tighter limits.

This applies to any service that lasts over time, such as subscriptions, apps and software. It applies even to devices that receive firmware updates, firmware being the internal program that makes the device work. What brings these cases together is not the sector, but the way each service organizes access and what you expect from it.

What counts is a persistent and significant decline that takes away the essentials of what you signed up for. The shell of the service can stay intact while its content has been hollowed out.

Where it comes from

Commenting on art. 51, XIII, Nunes asks, in a deliberately blunt way, how it would be possible to change a contract unilaterally and still give the change legal validity Nunes(Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]). The example he offers, of the supplier that cuts classes while keeping the same price Nunes(Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]), works as an objective yardstick for platforms.p. 479-480

PTAo comentar o art. 51, XIII, Nunes pergunta, de modo deliberadamente frontal, como seria possível alterar unilateralmente um contrato e, ainda assim, conferir validade jurídica à alteração (Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]) . O exemplo que ele oferece, do fornecedor que reduz aulas mantendo o mesmo preço (Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]) , funciona como régua objetiva para plataformas.

The example of updates that degrade accessibility and compatibility in software or firmware helps visualize the structure of the problem. In these cases, no one seriously argues that every update is unlawful. What is at issue is whether, under the pretext of improvement, the update significantly compromises the use, performance or compatibility of goods still within their expected durability, without adequate information, without effective consent and without means of mitigation.p. 655

PTO exemplo de atualizações que degradam acessibilidade e compatibilidade em software ou firmware ajuda a visualizar a estrutura do problema. Nesses casos, ninguém discute seriamente que toda atualização seja ilícita. O que se discute é se, sob o pretexto de melhoria, a atualização compromete de modo relevante o uso, o desempenho ou a compatibilidade de bens ainda dentro da durabilidade esperada, sem informação adequada, sem consentimento efetivo e sem vias mitigatórias.

What matters is not whether the platform presents itself as a cognitive tool, market infrastructure, productive environment or reputation system, but how it organizes access, modulates expectations, distributes risks and conditions the possibility of institutional reconstruction of the conflict.p. 756

PTO que importa não é se a plataforma se apresenta como ferramenta cognitiva, infraestrutura de mercado, ambiente produtivo ou sistema de reputação, mas como ela organiza acesso, modula expectativas, distribui riscos e condiciona a possibilidade de reconstrução institucional do conflito.

In less pompous and more useful language: it is not enough to leave the shell of the relationship intact if the content enjoyed has been hollowed out.p. 651

PTEm linguagem menos pomposa e mais útil: não basta deixar a casca do vínculo intacta se o conteúdo fruído foi esvaziado.

How do you record a decline that creeps in gradually?

It is worth keeping the description of the plan or offer from the day you signed up, with what was included. Dated screenshots showing the before and after also help. The change notices the company sent count too, because it should have told you the essentials in time for you to understand and react.

Noting the day you noticed the decline also helps, since in a continuous service the loss may only show up with prolonged use. And you do not need to have everything filed, because the history of versions and decisions stays with the company.

Where it comes from

The offer and the trust it aroused provide a direct basis for this identification, since the consumer contracts in view of the advantages and qualities advertised, from which arises the legal duty to maintain the conditions offered or, at least, to substantially preserve their utility Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 424).p. 652

PTA oferta e a confiança por ela despertada oferecem base direta para essa identificação, pois o consumidor contrata em vista das vantagens e qualidades anunciadas, surgindo daí o dever jurídico de manutenção das condições ofertadas ou, ao menos, de preservação substancial de sua utilidade (Miragem, 2020, p. 424) .

When the change is capable of affecting the core of enjoyment of the IOC, good faith requires communication focused on what is essential, prioritized and timed to the moment when the consumer can still understand the impact, react, decide and, if necessary, contest.p. 657

PTQuando a mudança é apta a impactar o núcleo de fruição do OIC, a boa-fé exige comunicação orientada ao essencial, hierarquizada e temporalmente adequada ao momento em que o consumidor ainda pode compreender o impacto, reagir, decidir e, se necessário, contestar.

The count must track the real moment at which the inadequacy becomes knowable, above all when it only emerges with prolonged use or with the supervening deterioration of serviceabilityp. 558

PTA contagem deve dialogar com o momento real de cognoscibilidade da inadequação, sobretudo quando esta só emerge no uso prolongado ou na deterioração superveniente da prestabilidade

The platform holds the complete history of decisions, versions and criteria, while the consumer receives only communicational fragmentsp. 471

PTA plataforma detém o histórico completo de decisões, versões e critérios, enquanto o consumidor recebe apenas fragmentos comunicacionais

What can you ask for when the delivery shrank but the charge did not?

The request can start with restoring the service to the level you signed up for. That can mean undoing the change, offering an alternative version or swapping it for something that serves the same function. It can also mean correcting the settings or keeping compatibility with what you already use.

Depending on the case, the decline may be a quality defect that reduces the value of the service. The law then also lets you ask for an abatement, which is a proportional discount on the price, or end the contract without a penalty. You do not need to argue over whether the company was at fault.

You can also ask the company to say what changed, when and why, instead of just answering that the service evolved. When it speaks of an update or an improvement, what needs to be shown is that the essentials were preserved.

Where it comes from

In an IOC environment, the most legally adequate remedy is often to restore equivalence of enjoyment: reversal of the alteration, alternative version, equivalent functional replacement, restoration of attributes, correction of parameters, preservation of trajectory, maintenance of compatibility or other measures capable of reestablishing, as far as possible, the position of enjoyment that justified the purchase.p. 654

PTEm ambiente de OIC, muitas vezes o remédio juridicamente mais adequado é restaurar equivalência de fruição: reversão da alteração, versão alternativa, substituição funcional equivalente, recomposição de atributos, correção de parâmetros, preservação de trajetória, manutenção de compatibilidade ou outras medidas capazes de restabelecer, na medida do possível, a posição de fruição que justificou a aquisição.

Faced with quality defects that reduce the value of the service, the consumer may demand termination of the contract without penalty or a proportional abatement of the price, with no need to discuss the supplier's fault, since this is strict liability Bessa(Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 354]).p. 563

PTDiante de vícios de qualidade que diminuam o valor do serviço, o consumidor pode exigir a rescisão contratual sem multa ou o abatimento proporcional do preço, sem necessidade de discutir culpa do fornecedor, pois se trata de responsabilidade objetiva (Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 354]) .

Whenever the platform invokes “update”, “adjustment” or “evolution of the service” to justify persistent changes, the core of the debate must fall on the demonstration of substantial preservation of the enjoyment bundle, and not on the rhetoric of complexity.p. 480

PTSempre que a plataforma invoca “atualização”, “ajuste” ou “evolução do serviço” para justificar alterações persistentes, o núcleo do debate deve recair sobre a demonstração de preservação substancial do feixe de fruição, e não sobre a retórica da complexidade.

The corresponding duty of supply is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was altered, when it was altered, on what grounds it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de manter verificabilidade mínima sobre o que foi alterado, quando o foi, por que motivo foi justificado e quais efeitos funcionalmente relevantes produziu.

Where can you take a complaint about a service that got worse for everyone?

The complaint starts with the company itself, and continuing to pay does not mean you accepted the decline. When the change is significant, it should have been communicated in a way you could understand, and your silence does not count as agreement.

When the decline hits all customers at once, it is the kind of problem that can go to Procon, the Public Defender's Office or the Public Prosecutor's Office, presented on the channels page. In these cases the response can fix the service for everyone, rather than just repairing your case.

Where it comes from

This duty of qualified communication also prevents the platform from turning the consumer's silence, passivity or mere permanence in the environment into fictitious acceptance of relevant mutations.p. 658

PTEsse dever de comunicação qualificada também impede que a plataforma transforme silêncio, passividade ou mera permanência do consumidor no ambiente em aceitação ficta de mutações relevantes.

When the dispute reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or unverifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case. It may take on a structural form: a duty to preserve minimum trails, to establish protocols for qualified communication of relevant changes, to organize intelligible contestation procedures, to maintain minimum functional impact reports or to adjust the supply regime to prevent the problem from recurring. This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not as a single piece.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual. Ela pode assumir feição estrutural: dever de preservar trilhas mínimas, protocolizar comunicação qualificada para alterações relevantes, organizar procedimentos de contestação inteligíveis, manter relatórios mínimos de impacto funcional ou ajustar o regime de fornecimento para impedir repetição do problema. Isso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When does a change in the service give you no grounds to complain?

Services that last for years need to change, and switching the means or the method can be legitimate and even necessary for them to remain viable. The limit is using the change to shift onto you risks that belong to the company, to lower the quality or to cut relevant features.

A one-day fluctuation or a small adjustment is not enough, and not every reduction is illegal. Nor can one assume that every digital service causes this problem with the same force, because each case depends on how that service works.

Where it comes from

Changes of means and method may be legitimate, and sometimes necessary, to keep the supply viable. The limit is that they cannot be used to shift structural risks onto the consumer, degrade quality, restrict relevant features, erode conditions of access or reconfigure regimes of participation and recognition that were part of economically stabilized enjoyment.p. 480

PTMudanças de meio e método podem ser legítimas, e por vezes necessárias, para manter o fornecimento viável. O limite é que elas não podem ser usadas para deslocar riscos estruturais ao consumidor, degradar qualidade, restringir funcionalidades relevantes, corroer condições de acesso ou reconfigurar regimes de participação e reconhecimento que integravam a fruição economicamente estabilizada.

The trigger is not any minor change, any visual touch-up or any marginal fluctuation typical of the normal life of the service.p. 651

PTO gatilho não é qualquer mudança miúda, qualquer retoque visual ou qualquer oscilação marginal própria da vida normal do serviço.

Was there a marginal loss, within the ordinary evolution of the service, or was there a substantial emptying of the functional core of enjoyment? Not every reduction is unlawful.p. 653

PTHouve perda marginal, inserida na evolução ordinária do serviço, ou houve esvaziamento substancial do núcleo funcional da fruição? Nem toda diminuição é ilícita.

The point, here, is not to gather digital examples under a single heading out of mere technological affinity, nor to suggest that every contemporary platform reproduces, with identical intensity, the same form of vulnerability, opacity or capture of enjoyment.p. 755

PTO ponto, aqui, não é reunir exemplos digitais sob uma mesma rubrica por mera afinidade tecnológica, nem sugerir que toda plataforma contemporânea reproduz, com idêntica intensidade, a mesma forma de vulnerabilidade, opacidade ou captura da fruição.

One word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
