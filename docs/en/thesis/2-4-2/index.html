2.4.2 On the documentary analysis of terms of use and internal policies

See this section in the PDF

This section in the layers of the site

  • Method · The protocol

p. 63

Empirical collection includes a targeted documentary analysis of the supplier's terms of use, licenses and internal policies, understood as institutional instruments that structure enjoyment and govern the permanence of the relationship in continuous digital services. The set of documents actually mobilized in the text is selected for its pertinence to the problem investigated and serves to capture the declared norm of the ecosystem, that is, the repertoire of promises, duties, unilateral powers and enforcement procedures presented as a p. 64 condition of access and use. In platform-governed objects, such instruments do not operate as mere informational accessories, for they take part in configuring the content of the supply over time and delimit, explicitly or implicitly, the range of permitted modulations, the sanctioning regimes and the mechanisms of challenge and restoration.

The documentary corpus analyzed consists, as a rule, of the versions in force and publicly accessible during the period in which the writing was closed, taking the month of March 2026 as the temporal reference. The investigation is not oriented toward an exhaustive historical reconstruction of earlier versions, nor toward the systematic indexing of contractual changes over time. Past versions and relevant changes will be considered only exceptionally, when there is a verifiable public record attributable to the supplier, such as official announcements, patch notes, institutional history pages or equivalent materials, and when such an element is necessary to qualify a legally relevant modulation of the promise, of the sanctioning regime or of restoration.

For methodological purposes, the primary institutional documents included are those officially attributable to the supplier and intended to guide the use of the service, internal governance and the performance of the relationship. Depending on their pertinence to the problem investigated, this set includes terms of use and general conditions, licensing instruments and authorizations of use, conduct and integrity policies, rules on penalties and moderation, refund and chargeback policies, policies on updates and changes to features, security and anti-fraud rules, as well as policies and notices that affect the form of access and permanence. Official support and communication materials may also be incorporated, with complementary status, such as help pages, institutional FAQs, announcements and public statements by the supplier, when they function as relevant statements of promise, of procedure or of the institutional justification offered to the user.

The reading of the documents is guided by categories stabilized in the theoretical path and by analytical axes oriented toward decidability. Instead of a full exegesis of each instrument, normatively relevant elements are extracted in a targeted way to reconstruct, over the course of the relationship, (i) the ways in which the object and the performance are qualified, (ii) the powers of unilateral modification and their triggers, (iii) the forms of institutional justification and of risk allocation, and (iv) the promises of compensation, refund and restoration. In this sense, the aim is to identify how the service is described and promised, how access is structured and conditioned, which limits of use are set, which rules of conduct organize belonging, which mechanisms of sanction and restriction are provided for, which channels of challenge and review are offered, which refund and restoration regimes are p. 65 declared and which informational duties are provided for in situations of change, punishment or restriction. This extraction makes it possible to reconstruct the formal design of the relationship and to locate the points at which the platform converts governance into continuous enforcement, especially when the performance is reconfigured by rule changes, updates and unilateral interventions on access and on the internal conditions of enjoyment.

In the case of computer programs, documentary extraction will also be conducted in a targeted way, seeking (i) references to the period of technical validity of the version marketed, update cycles, discontinuation milestones and conditions for withdrawal from circulation; and (ii) the design and effectiveness of the channels for support, maintenance and provision of complementary technical services. These elements speak directly to specific legal duties of the sector, notably the requirement of a clear indication of the period of technical validity in the license contract and related documents (Law No. 9,609/1998, Art. 7) and the obligation to ensure, during that period, complementary technical services relating to the proper functioning of the program, considering its specifications, even in the event of withdrawal from commercial circulation, subject to compensation for losses possibly caused to third parties (Law No. 9,609/1998, Art. 8).

The documentary survey is treated under an internal hierarchy, so as to avoid undue equivalences between types of institutional instrument. Terms of use, licenses and penalty and conduct policies are taken as the primary normative core of the governance regime, because they structure powers, duties and consequences. Support materials, help pages and official announcements are mobilized with complementary status, capable of evidencing public promises, declared procedures and justifications offered to the user, without replacing the normative core when the analysis depends on definitions of formal powers, of sanctioning regimes or of risk allocation. This distinction is relevant to keep under control the passage from the document to dogmatic attribution, preventing auxiliary communications from being treated as equivalent, in authority and stability, to the central normative instruments of the ecosystem itself.

Since terms and policies in continuous digital services may be modified over time, documentary temporality is handled in a controlled manner compatible with the scope of the corpus. The documents actually mobilized are collected and preserved in the state in which they appeared at the moment of capture, with a record of context and of minimum traceability metadata, so as to allow reconstruction of what was declared in the version used. When earlier changes are pertinent to the argument, they enter only occasionally and with verifiable public support, avoiding p. 66 any suggestion of a broad historical reconstruction and preserving coherence between the material cited and the empirical moment to which the analysis refers.

Documentary analysis operates in coordination with the situated institutional observation described above. While observation makes it possible to reconstruct the norm in operation at the level of enjoyment, the reading of terms and policies captures the declared norm and makes explicit the formal repertoire of powers, duties and procedures that sustains private governance. Triangulation between these planes makes it possible to identify convergences and frictions between formal promise and practical enforcement, especially in sensitive matters such as unilateral modification, sanctions, account restrictions and restoration, providing a verifiable basis for the institutional reading of contractual governance throughout the path and for the later dogmatic conversion into parameters of control of abusiveness, verifiability and protection.

The scope of the procedure is to delimit, with traceability, the declared institutional design of the ecosystem, without claiming that documents, by themselves, prove the concrete way in which the rules are applied day to day. The central utility of the documentary corpus is to provide a normative-practical map of the powers and promises that structure continuous enjoyment, allowing the empirical analysis and the dogmatic reconstruction to operate on verifiable and intersubjectively controllable bases, consistent with the methodological purpose of making consumer conflicts in regimes of platform governance decidable.
