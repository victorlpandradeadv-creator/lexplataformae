Who makes the rules inside a platform?The law of the platformLex Plataformae

Inside a platform, someone decides what you can do, what others will see of you and what happens if you break a rule. When you created the account, you accepted the terms of use, and it seems the rules are all there.

But the rules that weigh on your day are not only in the contract nobody reads. They are in the very operation of the app. They are in the paths the screen offers and in those it hides, in the punishments that arrive without warning and in the internal procedures by which the company itself decides conflicts. Whoever wrote the rule is also the one who enforces it and who keeps the records of what happened.

Where it comes from

[...] because, while the Lex Mercatoria is usually described as an ordering emerging from diffuse practices and multiple actors, the Lex Plataformae designates a type of private ordering centered on a platform as the institutional core that defines rules, visibilities, sanctions and evidentiary regimes through continuous architecture and updating.p. 27

PT[...] porque, enquanto a Lex Mercatoria costuma ser descrita como ordenação emergente de práticas difusas e de múltiplos atores, a Lex Plataformae designa um tipo de ordenação privada centrada em uma plataforma enquanto núcleo institucional que define regras, visibilidades, sanções e regimes de prova por meio de arquitetura e atualização contínuas.

The Lex Plataformae is treated here as the contemporary form of practical normativity exercised through protocols, flows, interfaces, sanctions and private adjudication procedures that organize consumption in an environment.p. 392

PTA Lex Plataformae é tratada, aqui, como a forma contemporânea de normatividade prática exercida por protocolos, fluxos, interfaces, sanções e procedimentos privados de adjudicação que organizam o consumo em ambiente.

This normativity is not limited to the contractual clauses that govern the relationship on the formal plane, since it materializes in interface design, choice patterns, selective frictions and enforcement mechanismsp. 27

PTEssa normatividade não se limita às cláusulas contratuais que regem a relação no plano formal, uma vez que se materializa em desenho de interface, padrões de escolha, fricções seletivas e mecanismos de enforcement

The law of the platformLex Plataformae

The name in Latin is Lex Plataformae, and plataformae means of the platform. It is not a contract sitting in a drawer, but an order that runs as a system, aims at an economic result and seems natural because it is repeated so often. It does not only announce rules. It classifies, records, recommends, blocks, restricts and changes the very environment in which you try to use what you bought. And whenever we talk about the platform that governs the place, we are talking about this law. The name comes from an old expression, the law merchant, used for the rules that traders created among themselves, without depending on the law of any country. The difference is that here the center is not the practice of many traders. It is a single company, which controls the front door, the way you understand what happens and the conditions under which you can use what is yours.

Where it comes from

The expression Lex Plataformae therefore seeks to name not a set of predisposed clauses, but a private ordering that is technically executable, economically oriented and semiotically naturalized.p. 27

PTA expressão Lex Plataformae pretende nomear, portanto, não um conjunto de cláusulas predispostas, mas uma ordenação privada tecnicamente executável, economicamente orientada e semioticamente naturalizada.

The variation “plataformae” corresponds to the genitive singular of the first declension, signaling “of the platform”p. 27

PTA variação “plataformae” corresponde ao genitivo singular de primeira declinação, sinalizando “da plataforma”

It does not merely interpret behavior, but classifies, records, prioritizes, makes invisible, recommends, blocks, expels, restricts, monetizes and reconfigures the very environment in which the user tries to exercise enjoyment of the acquired object.p. 27

PTEla não apenas interpreta comportamentos, mas classifica, registra, prioriza, invisibiliza, recomenda, bloqueia, expulsa, restringe, monetiza e reconfigura o próprio ambiente em que o usuário tenta exercer a fruição do objeto adquirido.

Lex Plataformae is an operative Latinization built by analogy with classical formulas for naming private orderings, especially the Lex Mercatoria, an expression used to designate an a-national body of rules and principles associated with the practice of international tradep. 27

PTLex Plataformae é uma latinização operativa construída por analogia com fórmulas clássicas de nomeação de ordenações privadas, especialmente a Lex Mercatoria, expressão usada para designar um corpo a-nacional de regras e princípios associado à prática do comércio internacional

it designates a private order whose center is not only mercantile circulation between actors, but control of the access infrastructure, of the intelligibility of the experience and of the concrete conditions for exercising the user's positionp. 28

PTdesigna uma ordem privada cujo centro não é apenas a circulação mercantil entre atores, mas o controle da infraestrutura de acesso, da inteligibilidade da experiência e das condições concretas de exercício da posição do usuário

Why does the platform's rule feel like weather?

Nobody sues the rain. The weather happens and we adjust. The law of the platform works like manufactured weather, which produces the facts of consumption in a predictable sequence, with a shallow explanation and an air of normality.

It also resembles a custom, because what is repeated many times starts to seem agreed by everyone. But on a platform, repetition may be merely the effect of a design the company chose on its own. Once it is shown that all this is organized by design and by punishment, what seemed like weather looks like a decision again, and a decision can be controlled.

Where it comes from

Technique comes to operate as manifest language, that is, fully operative. A language of architecture and engineering that defines what can be seen, proven, contested, repaired and even remembered. It manufactures facts of consumption the way one manufactures weather in a predictable sequence, with superficial justification and an appearance of strict normality.p. 32

PTA técnica passa a operar como linguagem manifesta, isto é, plenamente operante. Uma linguagem de arquitetura e engenharia que define o que pode ser visto, provado, contestado, reparado e até lembrado. Ela fabrica fatos do consumo como quem fabrica clima em sequência previsível, com justificativa superficial e aparência de estrita normalidade.

On the platform, the repetition of practices and solutions should not be read naively as a sufficient sign of normative legitimacy.p. 28

PTNa plataforma, a repetição de práticas e soluções não deve ser lida ingenuamente como sinal suficiente de legitimidade normativa.

Often, what presents itself as market habit or custom of the ecosystem is already the product of unilateral designp. 28

PTMuitas vezes, aquilo que se apresenta como hábito do mercado ou costume do ecossistema já é produto de desenho unilateral

what previously looked like weather looks like a decision again. And what looks like a decision again can be controlled.p. 793

PTo que antes parecia clima volta a parecer decisão. E o que volta a parecer decisão pode ser controlado.

What are the three layers of the law of the platform?

The law of the platform works in three layers, one on top of the other.

The first is the text, with the terms of use, the conduct policies, the moderation rules and the criteria for taking part. The second is the design, which turns the text into screens, paths, access limits and visibility criteria, and decides what is possible before any discussion. If the screen has no button, it does not matter that the contract allows it. The third is enforcement, which grades punishments, cuts visibility, suspends functions and, at the extreme, expels.

A rule only really bites when it passes through all three layers, because only then does it change what you can do and what happens to you.

Where it comes from

The first is the internal normative text, formed by terms of service, conduct policies, moderation rules and declared criteria for participationp. 364

PTA primeira é o texto normativo interno, formado por termos de serviço, políticas de conduta, regras de moderação e critérios declarados de participação

The second is the layer of architecture and implementation, in which such statements stop being simple declarations and become a configuration of possibilities through interface, flows, access patterns, visibility parameters and technical restrictions that delimit the range of the possible before any interpretive dispute.p. 365

PTA segunda é a camada de arquitetura e implementação, na qual tais enunciados deixam de ser simples declarações e se convertem em configuração de possibilidades por meio de interface, fluxos, padrões de acesso, parâmetros de visibilidade e restrições técnicas que delimitam o intervalo do possível antes de qualquer disputa interpretativa.

The third is the layer of enforcement and sanctions, made up of enforcement mechanisms that grade consequences, apply restrictions, lower visibility, suspend features and, in extreme cases, exclude the user, reconfiguring their position in the environment.p. 365

PTA terceira é a camada de enforcement e sanções, composta por mecanismos de aplicação que graduam consequências, aplicam restrições, rebaixam visibilidade, suspendem funcionalidades e, em casos extremos, excluem o usuário, reconfigurando sua posição no ambiente.

The internal rule only gains normative density when it is converted into conditions of possibility by architecture and into consequences by enforcement.p. 365

PTA regra interna só ganha densidade normativa quando se converte em condições de possibilidade por arquitetura e em consequências por enforcement.

How does an Honor system become a rule?

In an online game, the company created an Honor system, in which behavior becomes a score. Those who behave in a sportsmanlike way earn benefits. Those who behave badly receive fewer rewards and lose part of the right to speak in chat.

When you are punished, with a temporary suspension or a chat restriction, your score drops, your progress stalls and the account goes without certain rewards until you show, consistently, that you have changed. None of this goes through a court. The punishment is built into the system and affects what you can do, earn and show. And since the company builds the game, measures the matches and sets the punishment criteria without showing how they work, the question in a conflict is not only whether you broke a rule. It is also whether the criteria used to say so are legitimate.

Where it comes from

In official documentation, Riot presents Honor as a system that rewards sporting conduct with additional benefits and penalizes inappropriate behavior with reduced rewards and limited communication privilegesp. 343

PTEm documentação oficial, a Riot apresenta a Honra como sistema que recompensa conduta esportiva com benefícios adicionais e penaliza comportamentos inadequados com recompensas reduzidas e privilégios de comunicação limitados

In parallel, the company states that penalties such as temporary suspension and chat restrictions drop the Honor level, freeze progress in the system and make the account ineligible for rewards linked to the Hextech Crafting regime until there is consistent demonstration of behavioral “reform”p. 343

PTEm paralelo, a empresa afirma que penalidades como suspensão temporária e restrições de chat derrubam o nível de Honra, travam o progresso no sistema e tornam a conta inelegível para recompensas vinculadas ao regime de Hextech Crafting até que haja demonstração consistente de “reforma” comportamental

Seen this way, the central question is no longer only whether the user broke a rule and comes to include the legitimacy of the very criteria by which the deviation is produced, read and punished.p. 343

PTNessa chave, a questão central deixa de ser saber apenas se o usuário infringiu uma regra e passa a incluir a legitimidade dos próprios critérios pelos quais o desvio é produzido, lido e punido.

If the rule that counts is the one that is running, what changes?

When the rule that counts is the one that is running, the dispute is no longer only about the text of the contract. It becomes a dispute about who governs what can be done in there.

This changes what you can ask for. If the platform exercises this kind of power, it needs limits proportional to it. Among them are transparency that actually serves a purpose, a minimum of stability, reasons you can check, a real way to contest and the restoration of what was lost.

Where it comes from

[...] since the implemented rule becomes the real mode of existence of the object and, consequently, the point at which the conflict stops being a dispute about clauses and becomes a dispute about the governance that defines what is possible in the ecosystemp. 372

PT[...] uma vez que a regra implementada se torna o modo real de existência do objeto e, por consequência, o ponto em que o conflito deixa de ser disputa sobre cláusulas e passa a ser disputa sobre a governança que define o possível no ecossistema

For this reason, proportional parameters of qualified transparency, minimum stability, verifiable reasoning, contestability and restoration are required.p. 364

PTPor isso, impõem-se parâmetros proporcionais de transparência qualificada, estabilidade mínima, motivação verificável, contestabilidade e recomposição.

Does the law of the platform replace the laws of the country?

This does not make the platform a State, nor does it place it above the law of the country. The law of the platform does not replace Brazilian law. It is a layer of private rules that sits between you and what you bought, and that the law cannot treat as a mere technical setting. Nor is it about taking any game dispute to the Constitution.

Where it comes from

The Lex Plataformae, thus understood, does not replace State law or eliminate the application of the public legal order. It designates, rather, the private normative layer that interposes itself between the subject and enjoyment, organizing the concrete experience of consumption by means that the Law cannot treat as a mere technological backdrop.p. 28

PTA Lex Plataformae, assim compreendida, não substitui o Direito estatal nem elimina a incidência do ordenamento público. Ela designa, antes, a camada normativa privada que se interpõe entre o sujeito e a fruição, organizando a experiência concreta do consumo por meios que o Direito não pode tratar como simples pano de fundo tecnológico.

Here the point is not to convert the platform into a State [...]p. 366

PTAqui o ponto não é converter a plataforma em Estado [...]

This does not mean equating the platform with the State or constitutionalizing every private disputep. 348

PTIsso não implica equiparar plataforma a Estado nem constitucionalizar toda disputa privada

What can you demand?

A penalty needs a reason and a chance to defend yourselfA penalty needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself

When does a penalty go too far?

When your access, your permanence, your visibility or your chance to complain depend on penalties that nobody explains. Or on hurdles placed in front of only some people, on a cost too high to push back or on a way back that is much harder than the way in. It is the account blocked or banned without explanation, with an appeal route that leads nowhere. When the account is closed, access to everything in it goes too, including what you bought.

What must the platform do before penalizing?

The platform must follow procedures you can understand, give a reason that can be checked, let you contest in a reasonable way and not treat you arbitrarily.

How do you document the penalty and the attempt to contest it?

What helps are the records of the punishment, the appeal path that existed, the criteria used, the difficulties in undoing the measure and what it did to the continuity of your experience.

What review can be requested for the penalty?

If the penalty has no verifiable reason or no reasonable appeal route, it may be possible to have it reviewed or reversed. You can also ask the platform to fulfill its procedural duties and to create or improve a real appeal route. When the problem happens to many people, you can also ask for the penalty system as a whole to be corrected.

So the platform cannot penalize at all?

The platform has the power to penalize, but having that power is not enough. A penalty needs a reason that can be checked, consistency from one decision to the next and a clear way to challenge it.

Where it comes from

Whenever access, permanence, visibility or contestation are conditioned by opaque sanctions, selective frictions, excessive costs of reaction or asymmetric reversal mechanisms, there will be an indication of abusive governance of the bond.p. 746

PTSempre que acesso, permanência, visibilidade ou contestação forem condicionados por sanções opacas, fricções seletivas, custos de reação excessivos ou mecanismos assimétricos de reversão, haverá indício de governança abusiva do vínculo.

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure minimally intelligible procedures, verifiable reasoning, reasonable possibility of contestation and non-arbitrary treatment of the user in the administration of enjoyment.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar procedimentos minimamente inteligíveis, motivação verificável, possibilidade razoável de contestação e tratamento não arbitrário do usuário na administração da fruição.

The minimum required proof falls on sanction records, contestation flow, criteria deployed, structural difficulties of reversal and concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience.p. 746

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre registros de sanção, fluxo de contestação, critérios mobilizados, dificuldades estruturais de reversão e impactos concretos da medida sobre a continuidade da experiência.

The institutional consequence includes reversal or review of sanctions, procedural duties, creation or improvement of contestation mechanisms and, in structurally repetitive cases, correction of the ecosystem's enforcement regime.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional inclui reversão ou revisão de sanções, deveres procedimentais, criação ou aperfeiçoamento de mecanismos de contestação e, em casos estruturalmente repetitivos, correção do regime de enforcement do ecossistema.

For this reason, they must be controlled by denser criteria than the mere formal existence of sanctioning power. They require verifiable reasoning, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTPor isso, devem ser controladas por critérios mais densos do que a simples existência formal de poder sancionatório. Exigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

Added to this is a reward architecture, with progression and grind systems, and a sanctioning architecture, with bans, restrictions, automated penalties and opaque policiesp. 211

PTSoma-se uma arquitetura de recompensa, com sistemas de progressão e grind, e uma arquitetura sancionatória, com banimentos, restrições, penalidades automatizadas e políticas opacas

PT[...] inclusive ao chamado Conteúdo Virtual, ocorre por licença limitada e revogável, condicionada ao cumprimento dos Termos, e que o encerramento da conta acarreta cancelamento automático da licença e perda do acesso aos conteúdos associados

What is the law of the platform?

It is a private ordering, and not a set of pre-drafted clauses. It is technically executable, economically oriented and semiotically naturalized. Its institutional core is the platform, which defines rules, visibilities, sanctions and evidentiary regimes through continuous architecture and updating, based on curation, sanction and unilateral mutation.

The expression Lex Plataformae therefore seeks to name not a set of predisposed clauses, but a private ordering that is technically executable, economically oriented and semiotically naturalized.p. 27

PTA expressão Lex Plataformae pretende nomear, portanto, não um conjunto de cláusulas predispostas, mas uma ordenação privada tecnicamente executável, economicamente orientada e semioticamente naturalizada.

Where it comes from

[...] designates a type of private ordering centered on a platform as the institutional core that defines rules, visibilities, sanctions and evidentiary regimes by means of continuous architecture and updating.p. 27

PT[...] designa um tipo de ordenação privada centrada em uma plataforma enquanto núcleo institucional que define regras, visibilidades, sanções e regimes de prova por meio de arquitetura e atualização contínuas.

[...] a private ordering founded on architecture, curation, sanction and unilateral mutation, here called Lex Plataformae [...]Abstract

PT[...] ordenação privada fundada em arquitetura, curadoria, sanção e mutação unilateral, ora denominada Lex Plataformae [...]

Why does it change the object of the dispute?

Rule, implementation and enforcement organize participation, visibility and sanction, with a direct effect on the position of enjoyment that gives the IOC its economic value. The implemented rule becomes the real mode of existence of the object. That is why the conflict stops being a dispute over clauses and becomes a dispute over the governance that defines what is possible in the environment.

Where it comes from

The Lex Plataformae designates this regime in operation, in which rule, implementation and enforcement organize participation, visibility and sanction, with direct effects on the position of enjoyment that economically constitutes the IOC.p. 364

PTA Lex Plataformae designa esse regime em funcionamento, no qual regra, implementação e enforcement organizam participação, visibilidade e sanção, com efeitos diretos sobre a posição de fruição que constitui economicamente o OIC.

It is for this reason that the Lex Plataformae must be treated as practical normativity in operation, since the implemented rule becomes the real mode of existence of the object and, consequently, the point at which the conflict ceases to be a dispute over clauses and becomes a dispute over the governance that defines the possible in the ecosystem.p. 372

PTÉ por tal fator que a Lex Plataformae deve ser tratada como normatividade prática em operação, uma vez que a regra implementada se torna o modo real de existência do objeto e, por consequência, o ponto em que o conflito deixa de ser disputa sobre cláusulas e passa a ser disputa sobre a governança que define o possível no ecossistema.

What is the limit of the concept?

The law of the platform does not replace State law or set aside the public legal order. Nor does the concept turn the platform into a State. It shows the normative power at work within supply, to which the public legal order still applies.
