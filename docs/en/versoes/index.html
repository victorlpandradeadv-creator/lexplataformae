How the site has changed

The site grows in public, and each version is recorded with its date and what changed. The current version appears at the top of each page and in the suggested reference in the footer.

  1. Version 0.12, 10/11/2026. The English version of the whole site arrives, with the full text of the thesis in machine translation and the Portuguese original at hand for every literal passage.

  2. Version 0.11, 10/11/2026. A unified menu, a narrative cover, an editorial identity, the Path, a suggested citation on every page and this version history.

  3. Version 0.10, 09/11/2026. The layers are adjusted, with Doctrine in fifteen chapters, the 24 problems in Information and the How to complain page.

  4. Version 0.9, 05/11/2026. A review of the page layout and of the site's voice.

  5. Version 0.8, 03/11/2026. Chapters 5 and 6 are fully covered in Information and Doctrine, along with the problem pages and the design audit.

  6. Version 0.7, 27/10/2026. Adds the palette, the Mode switch with Information, Doctrine and Science, a menu for each mode and the full text block.

  7. Version 0.6, 27/10/2026. Adds the path of the skin and the three-step reading switch.

  8. Version 0.5, 27/10/2026. The site is now read in three layers, and the author's name comes last.

  9. Version 0.4, 27/10/2026. Adds the situations of the framing table and the platform families.

  10. Version 0.3, 26/10/2026. The first published version, still under construction, with the full text divided into citable sections and one page per concept.
