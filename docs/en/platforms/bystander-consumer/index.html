Deepened finalism and the consumer by equivalence in platform ecosystemsOn deepened finalism and the consumer by equivalence in platform ecosystems

Consumer protection may reach those who, without being classic final purchasers, are exposed to the platform's governance. The criterion is real subjection, and not the label of the contract.

Read in the full text, with the notes

Who else can invoke Brazil's Consumer Protection Code (CDC)?

Sellers, partners and peripheral professional users may remain exposed to unilateral governance to a degree incompatible with the presumption of parity. Selling or earning income does not, in itself, set aside the vulnerability arising from the governance of the environment. What weighs is the density of this governance over the subject's insertion, that is, whether permanence, intelligibility, reconstruction of the conflict and contestation remain under the platform's private control.

What decides is the real structure of subjection, not the contractual appearance of horizontality.p. 780

PTO que decide é a estrutura real da sujeição, não a aparência contratual de horizontalidade.

Where it comes from

A seller who depends on ranking, reputation, visibility and opaque prioritization rules does not become immune to consumer protection merely because they sell.p. 778

PTO seller que depende de ranking, reputação, visibilidade e regras opacas de priorização não se torna imune à tutela consumerista apenas porque vende.

The existence of an economic interest on the part of the user does not, in itself, eliminate the vulnerability arising from the governance of the ecosystem.p. 779

PTA existência de interesse econômico do usuário não elimina, por si, a vulnerabilidade decorrente da governança do ecossistema.

In all these cases, the legally relevant question lies in the density of the platform's governance over their insertion in the ecosystem.p. 778

PTEm todos esses casos, a questão juridicamente relevante está na densidade da governança da plataforma sobre sua inserção no ecossistema.

What does the equivalence rest on?

Deepened finalism works as a reading criterion, and functional exposure and semiotic vulnerability are dimensions of factual vulnerability. The functional reading of Article 29 of the CDC1 reaches those who, without being classic final purchasers, are exposed to commercial practices, supply methods and private governance in a condition of legally relevant vulnerability.

Where it comes from

It is in this context that deepened finalism appears here as a reading criterion and not as an autonomous object of doctrinal exposition.p. 778

PTÉ nesse contexto que o finalismo aprofundado comparece aqui como critério de leitura e não como objeto autônomo de exposição doutrinária.

is exposed to the commercial practices, the supply methods and the private governance mechanisms of the environment in a condition of legally relevant vulnerability.p. 779

PTencontra-se exposto às práticas comerciais, aos métodos de fornecimento e aos mecanismos de governança privada do ambiente em condição de vulnerabilidade juridicamente relevante.

How far does equivalence go?

The criterion is demanding. Not every participant in the ecosystem is a consumer by equivalence, and not every technology-mediated relationship justifies the CDC. The question is whether the relationship, as structured, remains marked by material asymmetry of power and by submission to controllable private governance.

Where it comes from

Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTNem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

The question then ceases to be whether the subject is “too much of a businessperson” to deserve protection and becomes whether the relationship, as structured, remains marked by material asymmetry of power and by submission to legally controllable private governance.p. 780

PTA questão deixa então de ser se o sujeito é “empresário demais” para merecer tutela e passa a ser se a relação, tal como estruturada, continua marcada por assimetria material de poder e por submissão a governança privada juridicamente controlável.
