Your rights · By problem

The service is going to end. What happens to what I bought?

The company announced that it will shut down the game, the app or the service, or turn off the servers, which are the computers on which everything runs. It can end the business, but this right does not include erasing everything without any care for the people who were inside.

Where it comes from

The use value of the bundle, in this condition, is not stock, it is flow. It requires continuity of servers, institutional connectivity and maintenance of the environment.p. 409

PTO valor de uso do feixe, nessa condição, não é estoque, é fluxo. Ele exige continuidade de servidores, conectividade institucional e manutenção do ambiente.

The right to exploit, terminate or reorganize economic activity is not to be confused with a supposed right to annihilate, without duties of transition, preservation or transfer, the functional enjoyment already stabilized in the consumer's sphere.p. 709

PTO direito de explorar, encerrar ou reorganizar atividade econômica não se confunde com um suposto direito de aniquilar, sem deveres de transição, preservação ou transferência, a fruição funcional já estabilizada na esfera do consumidor.

How do you know whether the end of the service affects what you had?

What you bought only works while someone keeps the servers on and the environment running. When the service stops, the item may stay in the account, but it stops being good for anything.

The end affects more than loose items. Photos, histories, libraries, contacts, progress, reputation and years of investment go with it, and they do not become worthless just because they are stored in another company's structure.

The contract may call everything a precarious license, which is a permission that can end at any time. Even so, this does not on its own authorize everything to be sacrificed at once.

Where it comes from

When the service becomes unavailable, it is not only access that fails, but the virtual content itself loses immediate usefulness, because the environment that makes it enjoyable stops operating.p. 409

PTQuando o serviço apresenta indisponibilidade, não é apenas o acesso que falha, mas o próprio conteúdo virtual perde utilidade imediata, porque o ambiente que o torna fruível deixa de operar.

Photographs, histories, libraries, contact networks, progressions, projected identities, reputational positions and long-term investments cannot be legally treated as residues without density merely because they are technically housed in someone else's infrastructure.p. 796

PTFotografias, históricos, bibliotecas, redes de contato, progressões, identidades projetadas, posições reputacionais e investimentos de longa duração não podem ser juridicamente tratados como resíduos sem densidade apenas porque se encontram tecnicamente alojados em infraestrutura alheia.

As relevant portions of contemporary life migrate to private digital ecosystems, the formal precariousness of use licenses can no longer be read as automatic authorization for the complete sacrifice of immaterial objects of consumption incorporated into the consumer's trajectory.p. 709

PTÀ medida que parcelas relevantes da vida contemporânea migram para ecossistemas digitais privados, a precariedade formal das licenças de uso já não pode ser lida como autorização automática para o sacrifício integral de objetos imateriais de consumo incorporados à trajetória do consumidor.

What is worth saving before the servers are shut down?

It is worth keeping the shutdown announcement, the receipts for what you bought and dated screenshots of your account, library and progress. If it is a program, the invoice, the license and the packaging also matter, because Brazilian law requires them to state how long the version sold will keep working.

The history of your purchases and the company's communications also stay with it, and you can ask for them to be preserved.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

Art. 7 requires that the license contract, the tax document and the media and packaging indicate, in an easily legible way, the technical validity period of the version marketed, and art. 8 converts this period into an obligation to ensure complementary technical services relating to the proper functioning of the program during the technical validity, including when it is withdrawn from commercial circulationp. 51

PTO art. 7º impõe que o contrato de licença, o documento fiscal e os suportes e embalagens indiquem, de forma facilmente legível, o prazo de validade técnica da versão comercializada, e o art. 8º converte esse prazo em obrigação de assegurar serviços técnicos complementares relativos ao adequado funcionamento do programa durante a validade técnica, inclusive quando houver retirada de circulação comercial

What is required here is not a total sweep of the system, but a minimum documentary core: history of relevant versions; essential change records with functional impact; trails of consent and decision flow; history of relevant transactions; communications of change; and, when there is enforcement, basic records of the decision event, such as the category applied, reason and consequence.p. 658

PTO que se exige aqui não é uma devassa total do sistema, mas um núcleo documental mínimo: histórico de versões relevantes; registros essenciais de alteração com impacto funcional; trilhas de consentimento e de fluxo decisório; histórico de transações relevantes; comunicações de mudança; e, quando houver enforcement, registros básicos do evento decisório, como categoria aplicada, motivo e consequência.

What can you ask for when the end is inevitable?

As far as is technically and legally possible, the request may include keeping the service running in another way or letting you take what you have elsewhere. It may also include restoring what was lost or handing the operation over to another company. In short, the exit needs to come with transition, preservation or transfer.

If it is a computer program, whoever sells it must guarantee the technical services that keep it working during the stated period, even after they stop selling it.

And when none of this is possible or enough, monetary compensation remains on the table.

Where it comes from

If shutting down the service becomes unavoidable, the constitutional order requires that it be reconciled, as far as technically and legally possible, with solutions ensuring continuity, portability, functional restoration, transfer of the operation or other measures capable of preventing private architecture from becoming a lawful instrument for the economic and existential devastation of the consumer.p. 709

PTSe o encerramento do serviço se impõe, a ordem constitucional exige que ele seja compatibilizado, tanto quanto técnica e juridicamente possível, com soluções de continuidade, portabilidade, recomposição funcional, cessão da operação ou outras medidas idôneas a impedir que a arquitetura privada se converta em instrumento lícito de devastação patrimonial e existencial do consumidor.

Law No. 9,609/1998 Brasil(Brasil, [1998]) does not describe only exclusivity and licensing as an enclosure of utilities, but structures positive duties of the supplier linked to the technical life cycle of the software.p. 51

PTA Lei nº 9.609/1998 (Brasil, [1998]) não descreve apenas exclusividade e licenciamento como cercamento de utilidades, mas estrutura deveres positivos do fornecedor ligados ao ciclo de vida técnico do software.

Pecuniary compensation remains necessary in many cases, but it cannot automatically occupy the position of sovereign remedy.p. 797

PTA indenização pecuniária permanece necessária em muitos casos, mas não pode ocupar, por automatismo, a posição de remédio soberano.

Who can you turn to when an entire service is going offline?

The first step is usually to ask the company itself, in writing, how the transition will work and what happens to what is in your account. Owning the game or the program is not enough, on its own, to justify destroying everything.

The shutdown hits all users at once. That is why it is the kind of case in which Procon, the state consumer protection agency, the Public Defender's Office and the Public Prosecutor's Office can act, and the channels page presents each one.

PTEm relações de consumo contínuas, não há direito de propriedade intelectual que sustente, por si só, a extinção integral da infraestrutura com consequente sacrifício integral dos OICs.

The sixth mechanism is coordinated institutional action. Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets.p. 662

PTO sexto mecanismo é a atuação institucional coordenada. Sem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

How far does this protection go when the service ends?

The company is not obliged to keep the service running forever, and the right to close or reorganize the business still exists. What can be asked depends on what is technically and legally possible, and you do not become the owner of the game, the work or the code.

When the service ends because the company went bankrupt, the question of what happens to what was inside it still has no ready answer. For now it remains open.

Where it comes from

The possible legal qualification preserves the validity of intellectual property on its own plane, but rules out its invocation as a general veto on the application of the CDC.p. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível preserva a validade da propriedade intelectual em seu plano próprio, mas afasta sua invocação como veto geral à incidência do CDC.

Nor is it necessary to resort to a strong idea of consumer ownership over the digital good in order to demand minimum stability.p. 708

PTTambém não é necessário recorrer a uma ideia forte de propriedade do consumidor sobre o bem digital para exigir estabilidade mínima.

The limit hypothesis, at this point, is not only the opportunistic platform that alters rules, degrades functionalities or intensifies value extraction while it remains operational. It is also the platform that fails.p. 796

PTA hipótese-limite, nesse ponto, não é apenas a plataforma oportunista que altera regras, degrada funcionalidades ou intensifica extração de valor enquanto permanece operante. É também a plataforma que fracassa.

The question that emerges is whether the Law can go on treating business closure as a simple exercise of economic freedom when it results in the burial of collections, identities, reputations, histories and immaterial objects of consumption incorporated into the practical life of consumers.p. 797

PTA pergunta que daí emerge é se o Direito poderá continuar tratando o encerramento empresarial como simples exercício de liberdade econômica quando dele resultar o soterramento de acervos, identidades, reputações, históricos e objetos imateriais de consumo incorporados à vida prática dos consumidores.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
