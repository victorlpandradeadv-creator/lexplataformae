You bought a skin. Is it yours?

Find my problem Explore the ways Search the sources

You paid real money. The skin is in your account, you use it in matches and other players see it.

It looks like yours, and for the most part it is.

Tomorrow it may look different, lose the effect you liked or vanish along with your account, and no one may ever properly explain why.

Where it comes from

The promise of distinction remains nominally in the account, but no longer finds, in the world of the game, the fit that gave value to the investment of time, money and attention.p. 104

PTA promessa de distinção permanece nominalmente na conta, mas deixa de encontrar, no mundo do jogo, o encaixe que conferia valor ao investimento de tempo, dinheiro e atenção.

Once the account is terminated, the user will no longer have access to the account, nor to the associated data, nor to the Virtual Content, including in-game currency, and the consequence is definitive loss without refund whenever the landlord deems there was causep. 155

PTEncerrada a conta, o usuário não terá mais acesso à conta, nem aos dados associados, nem ao Conteúdo Virtual, incluindo moeda do jogo, e a consequência é a perda definitiva sem reembolso quando o senhorio entende que havia motivo

Who would you complain to, and what would you complain about?

It is not just games. An AI assistant, an online store or a work app can work the same way whenever what you use depends on an environment the company maintains, updates, reorganizes and controls.

Where it comes from

The criterion, therefore, is not the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

The point, here, is not to gather digital examples under a single heading out of mere technological affinity, nor to suggest that every contemporary platform reproduces, with identical intensity, the same form of vulnerability, opacity or capture of enjoymentp. 755

PTO ponto, aqui, não é reunir exemplos digitais sob uma mesma rubrica por mera afinidade tecnológica, nem sugerir que toda plataforma contemporânea reproduz, com idêntica intensidade, a mesma forma de vulnerabilidade, opacidade ou captura da fruição

Nothing stands still in these places. Many changes are normal and part of the life of the service. Others are not, and the time, attention, reputation and money you put in never show up on any receipt. When did this start to seem normal?

Where it comes from

It allows us to recognize the sophisticated obvious: continuous digital services change, but they cannot change in just any wayp. 655

PTEla permite reconhecer o óbvio sofisticado: serviços digitais contínuos mudam, mas não podem mudar de qualquer jeito

Ordinary updates, regular curation, anti-abuse interventions and changes that do not reach the functional core of the enjoyment bundle remain within the normal field of performance and do not constitute relevant degradation by definition.p. 430

PTAtualizações ordinárias, curadoria regular, intervenções antiabuso e mudanças que não atingem o núcleo funcional do feixe de fruição permanecem no campo normal de execução e não constituem degradação relevante por definição.

When did it become normal that time, attention, reputation, collection, bond and property can be captured and undone by design, without this being named with the weight it deserves and, above all, without producing the consequences it should produce?p. 21

PTEm que momento se naturalizou que tempo, atenção, reputação, acervo, vínculo e patrimônio podem ser capturados e desfeitos por desenho, sem que isso seja nomeado com o peso que merece e, sobretudo, sem gerar as consequências que deveria gerar?

Musical epigraphs

The nine musical epigraphs of the thesis are part of the method. They do not prove the argument and they are not ornament, because each one anticipates in the language of art what the chapter will say in the language of law.

Where it comes from

The epigraphs do not appear as ornament, nor as an authority substituting for the scientific method, nor as empirical proof of the argument.p. 73

PTAs epígrafes não comparecem como ornamento, nem como autoridade substitutiva do método científico, tampouco como prova empírica do argumento.

Each chapter opening condenses, in artistic language, the specific movement that the dogmatic development will carry out in legal language.p. 77

PTCada abertura de capítulo condensa, em linguagem artística, o movimento específico que o desenvolvimento dogmático realizará em linguagem jurídica.
