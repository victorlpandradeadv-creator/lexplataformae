Your rights

When what you buy only exists inside a platform, the sale does not end at the click. The company keeps maintaining, changing and controlling what you use, and that is why, under Brazilian law, it carries duties that stay with the purchase for as long as it lasts. The relationship is no longer centered on the contract you accepted one day but on the use you make, every day, of what you bought.

Below are the main duties, one for each situation. For each one you will find when it arises, what the platform owes, what helps show the problem, what you can ask for and how far that goes.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Your rights, one by one

  1. What you bought is a product, even with nothing in your hands
  2. The platform cannot design the experience so that you fail to understand it
  3. Information has to be of real use
  4. What was promised has to be delivered
  5. A relevant change has to leave a trail
  6. If it changes, it must keep the value it had
  7. Punishment needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself
  8. When loss is the rule, the problem is the design
  9. The proof held by the platform has to come out
  10. Owning the work is not an excuse for everything
  11. The response should restore what you lost
