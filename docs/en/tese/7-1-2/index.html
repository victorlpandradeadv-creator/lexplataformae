7.1.2 On the legal qualification and supply duties of the IOC

See this section in the PDF

p. 742

The incidence of the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption requires a framing instrument capable of reducing the institutional cost of translation between the complexity of the governed ecosystem and the legal response required in the concrete case. This requirement does not stem from a didactic concern in a weak sense, but from the need to make operable, in a decisional key, a dogmatic grammar built precisely to confront bonds in which enjoyment is administered by private architecture, ordinary mutability, structural opacity and asymmetric mechanisms of sanction, visibility and contestation. When the consumer's experience is produced, modulated and narrated by the supplier itself, the interpreter's difficulty lies not only in understanding the environment, but in converting that understanding into a legally controllable framing.

For this reason, the present systematization works as a reference tool. Its usefulness lies not in artificially simplifying the object, but in organizing, in a manageable way, the passage between governance mechanism, supply duty, minimum evidentiary requirement, legal qualification and possible institutional consequence. The framework operates through cumulative filters. One starts from the identification of the IOC and of the governed enjoyment regime. Next, semiotic vulnerability is qualified and, as the case may be, structural opacity, relevant mutability and governance by sanction are verified. Open auditability and functional value p. 743 equivalence operate as transversal filters, bearing upon different forms of conflict. Only then does one proceed to the legal qualification of the dispute, without prejudice to cumulation when the same arrangement reveals, simultaneously, relevant inadequacy of supply and repeated exploitation of vulnerability.

The underlying logic remains the same. In continuous digital services, the dispute does not enter the system as raw fact. It already arrives traversed by regimes of perception, expectation and intelligibility produced by the supplier. That is why the reconstruction of the decisional experience occupies a central position. Without it, evidence tends to be replaced by a cultural impression about technology, and the legal response degrades into intuitive deference or generic hostility toward the digital. The function of the framework is precisely to avoid this drift, providing a framing grammar that makes it possible to operate the CDC and to reconcile intellectual property without requiring total technical mastery of the ecosystem and without accepting complexity as an alibi for non-accountability.

The following framing blocks may then be established:

a) governed enjoyment regime and incidence of the IOC;

Whenever the utility consumed depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and mutable by private governance, with impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating of the experience, the field of incidence of the IOC will be configured. The corresponding supply duty is to ensure enjoyment that is functionally accessible, intelligible and preserved within the limits of the legitimate mutability of the environment. The minimum required evidence falls on the demonstration of the structural dependence of the utility on the governed ecosystem, of the way the experience is made available and of the consumer's concrete position in the bond. The resulting legal qualification is that of an immaterial product subject to the regime of Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC, without the service dimension of the bond displacing the control of enjoyment as the core of supply. The possible institutional consequence consists in opening the subsequent filters of the regime, preventing the dispute from being neutralized by the claim of mere service provision or of simple formal licensing.

b) semiotic vulnerability and governance of meaning;

p. 744 Whenever expectation, perception of value, risk assessment or capacity for contestation depend on signs, interfaces, flows and informational trails controlled by the supplier, there will be legally relevant semiotic vulnerability. The corresponding duty is not to organize the experience through a structural asymmetry of meaning incompatible with good faith, transparency and legibility of enjoyment. The minimum required evidence involves reconstruction of the user journey, of the informational architecture of the environment, of communicational saliences, of the paths of understanding and of the records available to reconstitute the formation of expectation. The possible legal qualification may support an abusive practice, informational insufficiency, deceptiveness by ambiguity or relevant omission and, when articulated with other elements, grounds for dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof. The institutional consequence consists in bringing the conflict back to the governance of the environment, avoiding moralization of the consumer's conduct and allowing the incidence of reinforced duties of information, advertising and evidence.

c) inadequate information as functional opacity;

Whenever the platform replaces legibility with textual excess, strategic dispersion, fragmentation of informational trajectories or merely performative transparency, there will be inadequacy of the duty to inform. The corresponding supply duty is to provide information that is materially adequate, useful for the decision and verifiable at a later moment of dispute. The minimum required evidence requires identifying what was shown, at what moment, with what salience, through which channels and to what extent this organization allowed the consumer to understand what was legally relevant to adhere, remain or react. The legal qualification tends to operate as an informational quality defect of supply, an abusive practice by functional obscuring and, as the case may be, support for the invalidity of clauses or of limitations incompatible with the legitimately formed expectation. The possible institutional consequence includes a duty to correct the communicational arrangement, reinforcement of useful transparency, disclosure orders and restoration of enjoyment affected by decisional opacity.

d) advertising and offer as controllable management of expectation;

Whenever enjoyment is preceded by curated cycles of anticipation, promise, suggested scarcity, symbolic distinction or insinuated equivalences, advertising will p. 745 materially integrate the offer. The corresponding supply duty is to ensure correspondence between promise, announced value, concealed costs and the experience actually made available under administered mutability. The minimum required evidence involves reconstruction of the promotional environment, of the communication pieces, of symbolic saliences, of the context of presentation of the object and of the relation between prior promise and subsequent enjoyment. The legal qualification may fall on misleading or abusive advertising, binding offer, abusive practice by administered ambiguity and, when articulated with a broader exploitative program, an element of abusive teleology. The possible institutional consequence encompasses correction of the offer, functional restoration, injunctive relief over the communication and, as the case may be, invalidation of promotional strategies incompatible with the real experience delivered.

e) structural opacity and open auditability;

Whenever relevant changes in access, performance, utility, operating rule or sanction bear upon enjoyment without preservation of minimum trails of institutional reconstruction, there will be a violation of the open auditability parameter. The corresponding supply duty is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was changed, when it was changed, on what grounds it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.

The minimum required evidence consists of essential logs, history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements sufficient to make the dispute cognizable. The possible legal qualification may take the form of an abusive practice by immunizing opacity, inadequacy of the supply regime and reinforcement of the duty of evidentiary cooperation, without prejudice to other combined incidences. The institutional consequence includes orders for preservation and disclosure of records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and control of the lawfulness of the change or sanction based on minimum institutional intelligibility.

f) relevant mutability and functional value equivalence;

Whenever the platform alters the experience so as to degrade, empty or substantially divert the function that justified the acquisition, there will be a rupture of functional value equivalence. The corresponding supply duty is to preserve, in substantial terms, the functional, relational and, when legally relevant, symbolic role of the object in the consumer's experience. The minimum required evidence requires demonstration of the utility p. 746 previously incorporated into the bond, of the change that occurred, of the concrete functional impact of the alteration and of the insufficiency of any restoration offered. The possible legal qualification tends to fall on a quality defect of the product or service, relevant inadequacy of supply and, if there is coordinated exploitation of mutability, an additional element of abusive practice or abusive teleology. The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent substitution and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.

g) governance by friction, sanction and asymmetric reversibility;

Whenever access, permanence, visibility or contestation are conditioned by opaque sanctions, selective frictions, excessive costs of reaction or asymmetric mechanisms of reversal, there will be an indication of abusive governance of the bond. The corresponding supply duty is to ensure minimally intelligible procedures, verifiable reasoning, a reasonable possibility of contestation and non-arbitrary treatment of the user in the administration of enjoyment. The minimum required evidence falls on sanction records, contestation flow, criteria mobilized, structural difficulties of reversal and concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience. The possible legal qualification may involve an abusive practice, a safety defect of the service when the legal and procedural security of enjoyment is compromised, in addition to integration into a broader hypothesis of unlawful act of architecture. The institutional consequence includes reversal or review of sanctions, procedural duties, creation or improvement of contestation mechanisms and, in structurally repetitive cases, correction of the ecosystem's enforcement regime.

h) abusive teleology and unlawful act of architecture;

Whenever microfrictions, normalized losses, fabricated urgencies, artificial scarcities, strategic opacity, elastic equivalences and other mechanisms operate in a coordinated way to extract value from vulnerability or to degrade enjoyment as a routine of the environment, there will be a hypothesis of abusive teleology. The corresponding supply duty is not to structure the experience on the basis of an exploitative purpose incompatible with good faith, transparency, balance and protection of the enjoyment legitimately incorporated into the bond. The minimum required evidence consists in the demonstration of the pattern, of its replicability, of the coordination between mechanisms of the ecosystem and of the relevant impact produced on practical autonomy, p. 747 intelligibility or utility of the experience. The possible legal qualification reaches structural abusive practice, a defect of the supply regime, possible cumulation with a quality defect and grounds for injunctive and corrective relief over architecture. The institutional consequence goes beyond the reparation of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced transparency duties, design correction and structural or collective measures.

i) evidence in an environment of structural asymmetry;

Whenever the platform concentrates the essential elements for narrating, demonstrating and reconstructing the conflict, the evidentiary discipline must recognize that verifiability is part of the very legitimate regime of supply. The corresponding duty is to preserve and disclose the minimum records necessary for reconstructing the disputed event. The minimum required evidence, from the consumer's point of view, consists in a plausible presentation of the harmful experience, of its insertion in the ecosystem and of the accessible context elements; from the supplier's point of view, disclosure of trails, logs, histories, versions and technically delimited justifications is required. The possible legal qualification involves dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof, presumptions favorable to the consumer and reinforcement of the duty of cooperation, without prejudice to the material incidence of the other parameters. The institutional consequence consists in preventing technical opacity from becoming procedural shielding and in making effective standards such as open auditability and functional equivalence.

j) reconciliation with intellectual property;

Whenever the platform invokes exclusive ownership, trade secret, licensing or the proprietary structure of the ecosystem to resist control over the mode of supply, reconciliation must be carried out in light of the social function and of the constitutional limits on the exercise of private positions. The corresponding duty is to exercise exclusivity without turning it into a shield for absolute opacity, degrading mutability or arbitrary suppression of enjoyment. The minimum required evidence falls on the relation between the prerogative invoked and the concrete aspect of the conflict, demonstrating whether the restriction is really necessary to protect the asset or whether it operates, in truth, as undue immunization of governance. The possible legal qualification preserves the validity of intellectual property on its own plane, but rules out its invocation as a general veto on the incidence of the CDC. The p. 748 institutional consequence consists in reconciling exclusivity and consumer-law protection by means of delimited auditability, control of functional equivalence and subjection of the exercise of IP to the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration.

k) remedies adequate to functional restoration and regime correction.

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or meaningful density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving monetary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient. The corresponding duty is to structure protection in accordance with the nature of the loss, targeting the affected enjoyment and not merely its residual monetary translation. The minimum required evidence involves demonstration of the causal link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as of the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction. The possible legal qualification varies according to the principal incidence of the case, but the institutional consequence must favor restoration of access, reversal of sanctions, preservation of history, equivalent functional substitution, governance adjustments, injunctive relief and structural correction when the harm reveals a repeatable pattern. Monetization remains possible, but as a residual or complementary response, not as an automatic reflex of the injury.

This framework does not seek to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula. Its function is more honest and more useful. It organizes, in consultable language, the passage between governance structure, violated duty, minimum evidentiary requirement, legal qualification and possible institutional consequence. With this, the IOC ceases to be merely a dense conceptual construction and presents itself as a framing technology capable of making manageable, without undue flattening, the relation between governed ecosystem and legal response. It is at this point that the category acquires effective decisional utility.
