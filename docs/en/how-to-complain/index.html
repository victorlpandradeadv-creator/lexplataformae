Where can you complain when the platform won't fix the problem?

When talking to the platform gets you nowhere, Brazilian law offers other routes, and almost all of them are free. Here are the main ones, with what each one does, what it asks of you and how much time you have.

Information added on November 8, 2026, based on the law and the official channels. It is a later addition to the thesis, which does not deal with complaint channels.

Where do you start?

The first path is usually the company itself, through the customer service channel it offers. It is worth complaining in writing, or through a channel that gives you a ticket number, and keeping that number.

This has a legal effect. A complaint made to the company, as long as you can prove you made it, stops the clock on the deadline until the company answers with a clear no.

What Article 26 of the CDC says1

Art. 26. The right to complain about apparent or easily noticeable defects lapses in: I - thirty days, in the case of supply of services and non-durable products; II - ninety days, in the case of supply of services and durable products. § 1 The forfeiture period begins to run from the effective delivery of the product or the end of the performance of the services. § 2 The following prevent forfeiture: I - a complaint demonstrably made by the consumer to the supplier of products and services until the corresponding negative answer, which must be transmitted unequivocally; II - (Vetoed). III - the opening of a civil inquiry, until its closing. § 3 In the case of a hidden defect, the forfeiture period begins at the moment the defect becomes evident.

Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor, CDC), Law 8,078/1990, on the Planalto website

PTO que diz o art. 26 do CDC Art. 26. O direito de reclamar pelos vícios aparentes ou de fácil constatação caduca em: I - trinta dias, tratando-se de fornecimento de serviço e de produtos não duráveis; II - noventa dias, tratando-se de fornecimento de serviço e de produtos duráveis. § 1° Inicia-se a contagem do prazo decadencial a partir da entrega efetiva do produto ou do término da execução dos serviços. § 2° Obstam a decadência: I - a reclamação comprovadamente formulada pelo consumidor perante o fornecedor de produtos e serviços até a resposta negativa correspondente, que deve ser transmitida de forma inequívoca; II - (Vetado). III - a instauração de inquérito civil, até seu encerramento. § 3° Tratando-se de vício oculto, o prazo decadencial inicia-se no momento em que ficar evidenciado o defeito. How much time do you have to complain?

For a defect that is easy to notice, the Consumer Protection Code gives thirty days when the service or product is not durable, and ninety days when it is durable. The deadline runs from the delivery of the product or the end of the service.

If the problem stays hidden and only shows up later, the deadline starts when it becomes evident. In a service that continues over time, such as a game or an account, the loss may appear little by little, and the count should follow the moment when it can be noticed.

The law does not say in so many words whether a digital item is durable or not. That is why the safest course is to complain within the thirty days and keep proof that you complained.

When the problem causes greater harm, one that affects you or your property beyond the item itself, the deadline to claim compensation is five years. It runs from when you learned of the harm and of who caused it.

How does consumidor.gov.br work?

consumidor.gov.br is a free public service of the federal government for resolving consumer disputes over the internet. You register the complaint, and the company answers right there.

To use it, you need a silver or gold level gov.br account. The company also needs to be registered on the site, and its participation is voluntary. If it is not, the site itself lets you suggest that it join.

After registration, the company has up to ten days to answer. Then you have up to twenty days to say whether the problem was solved and to rate the service.

The site does not replace Procon, the Public Defender's Office, the Public Prosecutor's Office or the Small Claims Court, which remain available to you.

The rules of consumidor.gov.br
  • A free public service for resolving consumer disputes over the internet, monitored by the National Consumer Secretariat (Senacon) of the Ministry of Justice.
  • To register a complaint you need to sign up, accept the terms of use and have a silver or gold level gov.br account.
  • The company needs to be registered on the site to receive the complaint, and its participation is voluntary. If it is not, the consumer can suggest that it join.
  • The company has up to 10 days to answer. Afterward, the consumer has up to 20 days to evaluate the answer.
  • The service does not replace Procon, the Public Defender's Office, the Public Prosecutor's Office or the Small Claims Courts, which remain available.

What can Procon do?

Procon is the consumer protection agency of your state or city, and it is part of the national consumer protection system. It serves consumers, processes complaints and oversees consumer relations.

Each Procon has its own channels, which may be in person or online. A complaint at Procon also leaves a record, which helps show later that you tried to solve the problem.

When is it worth going to the Small Claims Court?

The Small Claims Court (Juizado Especial Cível) hears less complex cases worth up to forty minimum wages. Filing the claim and getting to the judge's first decision costs nothing.

In cases worth up to twenty minimum wages, you can go without a lawyer. Above that, you need a lawyer, and you also need one if anyone appeals the decision.

When is it better to have a lawyer?

A lawyer helps when the amount is high, when the case depends on evidence held by the company or when an appeal is needed. Those who cannot pay are entitled to free legal assistance, which the Public Defender's Office provides.

When the same problem affects many people because it arises from the very design of the service, the answer may be collective. The Public Prosecutor's Office, consumer protection bodies and consumer protection associations can act on behalf of all those affected.

What should you bring, whichever path you take?

It helps to bring dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase, acceptance and confirmation screens. Messages about rarity, time remaining, discount or change also count.

It helps to compare what was promised with what you received and with what changed later. That is why it is worth keeping the ad, the post and the fine print, as well as the ticket number of each contact with the company.

Some of the most important records stay with the company, such as the version history, the transactions and the change notices. In that case, the request may include asking the company to keep and show these records.

Where it comes from

The first source of grounding consists of interface trails and the decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, time remaining, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

The second source of grounding is the history of offer and mutability. The advertising and institutional promise is compared with the enjoyment actually delivered and with its later changes.p. 607

PTA segunda fonte de lastro é o histórico de oferta e de mutabilidade. Compara-se a promessa publicitária e institucional com a fruição efetivamente entregue e com suas alterações posteriores.

The third source of grounding concerns advertising and its identifying documents. Promotional pieces, posts, campaigns, influencers, disclaimers, asterisks, cascading cross-references and fine print serve to map how expectation was produced and where the relevant conditions were hidden, diluted or shifted to zones of low visibility.p. 607-608

PTA terceira fonte de lastro recai sobre a publicidade e seus documentos de identificação. Peças promocionais, postagens, campanhas, influenciadores, disclaimers, asteriscos, remissões em cascata e letras miúdas servem para mapear como a expectativa foi produzida e onde as condições relevantes foram escondidas, diluídas ou deslocadas para zonas de baixa visibilidade.

Whoever controls the service controls, to a large extent, the decisive records of the relationship. Logs, version histories, transactions, interface parameters, change communications and relevant technical documentation tend to remain in the supplier's possession. For this reason, operationalizing the category needs to be accompanied by requests for preservation and disclosure of minimum documentation of the supply.p. 608

PTQuem controla o serviço controla, em larga medida, os registros decisivos da relação. Logs, históricos de versão, transações, parâmetros de interface, comunicações de mudança e documentação técnica relevante tendem a permanecer em poder do fornecedor. Por isso, a operacionalização da categoria precisa vir acompanhada de pedidos de preservação e exibição de documentação mínima do fornecimento.

Does any of these paths guarantee a result?

None does. Each path gives the company a chance to respond, gives someone outside the case a chance to look at it and gives the law a chance to be applied, but the outcome depends on the evidence and on the particular situation.
