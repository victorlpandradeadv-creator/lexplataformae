Personal integrity

Version 0.12

Which legal good is at stake?

Personal integrity is the preservation of minimum conditions for the consumer's agency, continuity and practical orientation in an environment that permanently administers the possibilities of enjoyment. It is not to be confused with an emotional state or with subjective discomfort arising from the ordinary frustrations of the market.

Where it comes from

Personal integrity, in the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption, does not designate an emotional state or subjective discomfort arising from ordinary market frustrations. It designates the preservation of minimum conditions of agency, continuity and practical orientation of the consumer within a technical-organizational environment that permanently administers the possibilities of enjoyment.p. 457

PTIntegridade pessoal, no regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo, não designa estado emocional nem desconforto subjetivo decorrente de frustrações ordinárias do mercado. Designa a preservação de condições mínimas de agência, continuidade e orientação prática do consumidor dentro de um ambiente técnico-organizacional que administra, de modo permanente, as possibilidades de fruição.

How is the injury measured?

By the attribute of personality objectively affected, and not by the suffering subjectively experienced. The digital experience can affect this good because data protection, informational self-determination and the free development of personality form part of the existential core of the person in an informational environment.

Where it comes from

[...] when the injury is understood not from the suffering subjectively experienced, but from the attribute of personality objectively affected.p. 534

PT[...] quando a lesão é compreendida não a partir do sofrimento subjetivamente experimentado, mas do atributo da personalidade objetivamente atingido.

The digital experience can affect legally relevant dimensions of personal integrity precisely because data protection, informational self-determination and the free development of personality form part of the existential core of the person in an informational environment [...]p. 535

PTA experiência digital pode atingir dimensões de integridade pessoal juridicamente relevantes justamente porque proteção de dados, autodeterminação informacional e livre desenvolvimento da personalidade integram o núcleo existencial da pessoa em ambiente informacional [...]
