The jurist's path

Open auditability

Open auditability is the minimum threshold of external verifiability that makes it possible to reconstruct what the supplier changed, when, why and with what effect, whenever the change or the sanction significantly affects the enjoyment of an Immaterial Object of Consumption. The requirement arises within the CDC, has a delimited scope and does not yield to secrecy or to technical complexity.

What problem does open auditability solve?

In a continuous digital service, asking whether the platform may change the environment gets you little, because change is part of how the service is provided. The legal problem is to distinguish legitimate mutation from mutation that breaks the balance of the supply, degrades the promised enjoyment or makes the very possibility of control opaque. This last case has a trait of its own, since many disputes arise not from abrupt removal but from gradual alterations, interface changes and visibility adjustments that seem trivial when taken one by one. The consumer keeps the account, keeps seeing the object and keeps accessing the platform, while its usefulness may drop and, with it, the consumer's ability to prove the drop.

In IOCs, mutability exists, but it does not hover above the Law.p. 635

PTEm OICs, a mutabilidade existe, mas não paira acima do Direito.

Where it comes from

The legal problem, therefore, lies not in finding out whether the platform may alter the environment, but in establishing the criteria by which legitimate mutation is distinguished from the mutation that breaks the balance of the supply, degrades the promised enjoyment or makes opaque the very possibility of control.p. 635

PTO problema jurídico, portanto, não está em descobrir se a plataforma pode alterar o ambiente, mas em estabelecer os critérios pelos quais se distingue a mutação legítima da mutação que rompe o equilíbrio do fornecimento, degrada a fruição prometida ou torna opaca a própria possibilidade de controle.

The consumer remains formally “inside” the ecosystem, keeps the account, keeps seeing the object, can keep accessing the platform. Even so, the utility enjoyed may be substantially lowered, and the capacity to prove this lowering may be simultaneously eroded by the very way the environment is administered.p. 635

PTO consumidor continua formalmente “dentro” do ecossistema, continua com a conta, continua vendo o objeto, continua podendo acessar a plataforma. Ainda assim, a utilidade fruída pode ser substancialmente rebaixada, e a capacidade de provar esse rebaixamento pode ser simultaneamente erodida pela própria forma como o ambiente é administrado.

The difficulty lies in who holds the information. The supplier controls the architecture, the criteria for change, the logs, which are the automatic records of what the system did, the moment of the change and the very means by which it will be perceived. It thus concentrates the power to reconfigure and also the power to be the sole narrator of what happened. The effect appears in two images, that of the supplier who comes to hold the practical monopoly of the real and that of the consumer reduced to a bearer of symptoms, who feels that something important has been lost but cannot show what was lost, how it was lost or why the loss goes beyond the ordinary annoyances of life online.

Where it comes from

If the supplier controls the architecture, the criteria for change, the relevant logs, the timing of the change and the very means by which the modification will be perceived, it concentrates not only the power of reconfiguration, but also the power to give a unilateral account of what occurred.p. 636

PTSe o fornecedor controla a arquitetura, os critérios de alteração, os logs relevantes, a temporalidade da mudança e os próprios meios pelos quais a modificação será percebida, ele concentra não apenas poder de reconfiguração, mas também poder de narrar unilateralmente o que ocorreu.

The supplier comes to hold the practical monopoly of the “real”, and the consumer is reduced to a bearer of symptoms, without a common language that allows comparison and control.p. 473

That is why opacity, in these environments, is not merely a technical feature. It works as an asymmetric distribution of power within the relationship. Whoever controls the criteria, the records, the system's memory and the language in which events can be described also concentrates the capacity to define what will count as demonstrable. Without a verifiable minimum, the conflict tends to become an unequal contest between the consumer's subjective experience and a self-justifying corporate account, and private governance also becomes power over the institutional conditions of proof. The parameter is aimed at this practical immunity.

Why does neither total transparency nor published information solve the problem?

The debate usually gets stuck between two positions. One holds that any transparency requirement compromises trade secrets, innovation or business autonomy, and the other imagines that effective control requires full disclosure of the platform's inner workings. Both fail, and the clash is a binary theater, because the first turns technical complexity into an alibi and the second confuses legal protection with an invasion of the productive environment. Rejecting both has a practical effect, since making control conditional on total transparency produces paralysis, and accepting mutability as a neutral given normalizes substantial degradation as mere operational variation.

Where it comes from

On one side, it is argued that any transparency requirement would compromise trade secrets, innovation or business autonomy. On the other, it is imagined that control would only be effective if there were complete opening of the platform's internal functioning. Both positions are bad. The first turns technical complexity into an alibi for irresponsibility. The second confuses legal protection with total invasion of the productive environment. Open auditability rejects this binary theater.p. 727

De outro, imagina-se que o controle só seria efetivo se houvesse abertura completa do funcionamento interno da plataforma. As duas posições são ruins. A primeira transforma complexidade técnica em álibi para irresponsabilidade. A segunda confunde tutela jurídica com invasão total do ambiente produtivo. A auditabilidade aberta rejeita esse teatro binário.

The attempt to condition the legal control of opaque ecosystems on total transparency misses the target and produces paralysis.p. 481

The frustration of the enjoyment bundle then tends to present itself as noise, and the legal response oscillates between two equally unproductive extremes: requiring total transparency incompatible with the functioning of the technical-organizational environment or accepting mutability as a neutral given, normalizing substantial degradations as mere operational variation.p. 469

PTA frustração do feixe de fruição tende, então, a se apresentar como ruído, e a resposta jurídica oscila entre dois extremos igualmente improdutivos: exigir transparência total incompatível com o funcionamento do ambiente técnico-organizacional ou aceitar a mutabilidade como dado neutro, normalizando degradações substanciais como mera variação operacional.

The supplier's usual answer is that the information is already available, in the terms, in cross-references, in the FAQs and in the update history. That is not enough, because information, under the CDC, does not fulfill its function merely by existing somewhere. What matters is whether it helps reconstruct events and make decisions, that is, whether it lets the consumer know what happened and decide what to do. Without that, what remains is documentary staging and performative transparency, in which the consumer knows that something changed but cannot tell what changed, by what criterion, with what practical effect or how to prove the impact.

The change exists, the notice exists, the document exists, the history exists, but the useful reconstruction of the event does not exist.p. 641

PTA mudança existe, o aviso existe, o documento existe, o histórico existe, mas a reconstrução útil do evento não existe.

Where it comes from

Information, under the CDC, does not fulfill its function merely because it exists somewhere, nor because it was deposited in lengthy documents, cross-references, FAQs or update histories that the consumer will hardly locate, understand and connect to the actual impact suffered. What matters is its reconstructive and decisional usefulness.p. 641

PTA informação, no CDC, não cumpre sua função só porque existe em algum lugar, nem porque foi depositada em documentos extensos, remissões, FAQs ou históricos de atualização que o consumidor dificilmente localizará, compreenderá e conectará ao impacto efetivo sofrido. Combined with the technical concentration of records, this mutability produces a private evidentiary fortress.

Nor do the two usual doctrinal escape routes help. The civil-law ontology of ownership of the digital good is too narrow for governed consumption, and the microscopic analysis of isolated clauses assumes that the platform produces unlawfulness only through badly drafted contract wording. The focus shifts to the architecture of the supply and to the material controllability of its mutations.

In governed ecosystems, the relevant unlawful act often lies not in a heroically villainous clause, but in the way the environment changes, justifies itself poorly, erases traces and preserves only the formal appearance of continuity.p. 639

Is open auditability a new right?

No, and the distinction matters for anyone grounding a claim. Open auditability is neither an autonomous statutory right nor a body foreign to the consumer protection microsystem. It is an internal development of three things the system already has, the duty to inform, objective good faith and the facilitation of the consumer's defense. Whether called an operator, a standard or a parameter, it always designates a requirement that the interpreter applies to the case, one of technically qualified transparency that institutions can actually use. It can be demanded whenever the supplier makes relevant changes, imposes sanctions or carries out reconfigurations capable of reaching the core of enjoyment.

Where it comes from

It is for this reason that open auditability must be understood as an internal unfolding of the duty to inform and not as an institution foreign to the microsystem.p. 641

PTÉ por essa razão que auditabilidade aberta deve ser entendida como desdobramento interno do dever de informação e não como instituto estranho ao microssistema.

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and of business secrecy, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of execution; a development of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of the facilitation of the consumer's defense; a criterion for reducing evidentiary and informational asymmetry; and an institutional condition for relevant mutations of the IOC to cease being accepted as accomplished facts narrated only by the supplier itself.p. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor; um critério de redução de assimetria probatória e informacional; e uma condição institucional para que mutações relevantes do OIC deixem de ser aceitas como fatos consumados narrados apenas pelo próprio fornecedor.

Open auditability designates, in continuous digital services, a standard of technically qualified and institutionally usable transparency, enforceable whenever the supplier makes relevant changes, sanctions or reconfigurations capable of reaching the core of enjoyment of the Immaterial Object of Consumption.p. 639-640

PTA auditabilidade aberta designa, em serviços digitais contínuos, um standard de transparência tecnicamente qualificada e institucionalmente aproveitável, exigível sempre que o fornecedor promove alterações relevantes, sanções ou reconfigurações capazes de atingir o núcleo de fruição do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo.

The first support is the duty to inform. The manual by Benjamin, Marques and Bessa requires correct, clear, precise and conspicuous information, notes the need for the consumer to be able to review the contract in advance and recalls that information is part of the contractual content and, when it fails, may amount to a quality defect. Ricardo Morishita Wada adds that leaving the time for performance to the supplier's sole determination strips the consumer of the capacity for control, and Bruno Miragem treats the duty to inform as a duty of clarification, which the supplier itself bears the burden of proving it fulfilled and whose breach may trigger Article 46 of the CDC1. On this basis rests the step that grounds the category. A supplier who alters central elements of enjoyment without providing an intelligible basis for reconstructing what happened does more than communicate poorly, since it prevents control and worsens the consumer's position in the relationship.

Where it comes from

The Manual synthesizes this standard by requiring “correct”, “clear”, “precise” and “conspicuous” information Bessa(Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 128]).p. 640

PTO Manual sintetiza esse padrão ao exigir informação “correta”, “clara”, “precisa” e “ostensiva” (Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 128]) .

In addition, clear and adequate information governs the pre-contractual moment, the conclusion of the contract, the contract itself and the post-contractual moment, affecting “the essence of the deal”, since it is part of the contractual content and, if it fails, may represent a defect in the quality of the product or service offered Bessa(Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 126]).p. 640

PTEm complemento, a informação clara e adequada rege o momento pré-contratual, a conclusão do contrato, o próprio contrato e o momento pós-contratual, afetando “a essência do negócio”, pois integra o conteúdo contratual e, se falha, pode representar vício na qualidade do produto ou serviço oferecido (Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 126]) .

Failing to state the time for performance, or leaving its definition to a later moment under the supplier's exclusive control, places the consumer in a situation of extreme vulnerability, because without an intelligible reconstruction of the way the obligation is performed the consumer loses capacity for control and the supplier comes to dictate unilaterally the rules of the game Wada(Wada, 2016, p. 208).p. 641

Demonstrating that this duty was effectively fulfilled is an evidentiary burden of the supplier itself Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 412).p. 643

The second support is objective good faith during performance. In a digital service, performance is not exhausted in an initial delivery, because the relationship extends through updates, fixes, rebalancing and sanctions, and treating this period as a neutral space would be far too crude. Claudia Lima Marques rejects the idea of an empty space between the birth of the obligation and the satisfaction of the performance and describes the relationship as a continuous whole, with ancillary duties to inform, to cooperate and to act loyally. For her, breaching them amounts to nonperformance, even if only partial. The long-term contract, in turn, rests more on trust and on the maintenance of quality, so the supplier cannot keep all the intelligibility of the process of change to itself and leave the consumer with nothing but the imposed result.

Where it comes from

Objective good faith, in relationships of this nature, projects continuous duties of information, cooperation, loyalty and protection of trust, so that the supplier cannot reserve for itself all the intelligibility of the process of mutation of the service and leave the consumer only with the final result already imposedp. 640

PTA boa-fé objetiva, em relações dessa natureza, projeta deveres contínuos de informação, cooperação, lealdade e proteção da confiança, de modo que o fornecedor não pode reservar para si toda a inteligibilidade do processo de mutação do serviço e deixar o consumidor apenas com o resultado final já imposto

Breaching the duty of cooperation and loyalty means nonperformance, even if partial, including when the supplier hinders the consumer's access to their rights Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 236).p. 641

PTDescumprir o dever de cooperação e de lealdade significa inadimplir, ainda que parcialmente, inclusive quando o fornecedor dificulta o acesso do consumidor a seus direitos (Marques, 2016, p. 236) .

In relationships of continued performance, the consumer places trust not only in an initial utility, but in the minimally intelligible permanence of the relationship and in the possibility of understanding its relevant changes.p. 645

The third support is the facilitation of defense. Article 6, item VIII, of the CDC2, according to the manual by Benjamin, Marques and Bessa, is not merely procedural in nature, because it aims at effective protection and redress, and the spirit of the Code is not to place on the vulnerable party the burden of proving elements that belong to the supplier's professional risk. Since the platform concentrates records, histories, enforcement criteria and versions, facilitating the defense and redistributing the burden cease to be occasional instruments and become a condition for the regime to have even minimal effectiveness. Open auditability then works as a technique for reducing informational and evidentiary asymmetry, which puts the conflict back on ground where events can be reconstructed.

Where it comes from

The Manual is clear in observing that the right provided for in art. 6, VIII, although it aims to facilitate the consumer's procedural defense, is not merely procedural in nature: it is a substantive right aimed at the effective protection and reparation of the damages suffered Bessa(Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 146]).p. 642

PTO Manual é claro ao observar que o direito previsto no art. 6.º, VIII, embora vise facilitar a defesa processual do consumidor, não possui natureza meramente processual: trata-se de direito material voltado à proteção efetiva e à reparação dos danos sofridos (Benjamin; Marques; Bessa, 2021, [p. 146]) .

Open auditability, at this point, works as a technique for reducing informational and evidentiary asymmetry.p. 642

The systematic consequence is that the lack of auditability does not open an autonomous path of imputation. Quality defect, abusive practice and abusive clause remain the three doors into the CDC, the subject of the chapter on the unlawful act of architecture. The absence of auditability does not create a fourth door but reinforces any of them, because opacity may intensify the imputation for abusive practice, aggravate the reading of the clause that shields arbitrariness and weaken the defense that there was merely a legitimate adaptation. It is a legal technology of controllability, and what it makes possible is a discussion of defect, inadequacy, abusiveness and breach of the duty to inform on a reconstructible basis.

Why can the Law control without seeing everything?

Control does not depend on full transparency, and the support comes from legal theory. Humberto Ávila proposes shifting the focus from security of meaning to security of criteria, with prior parameters for addressing later interpretive problems, and distinguishes security from transparency. Transparency has its core in information, while security seeks argumentative rationality and the control of arbitrariness. The question is then no longer how to open the black box all the way down but how to define controllable criteria that turn modulations, that is, the continuous changes in the conditions of the service, into legally decidable alternatives.

Where it comes from

It is in this sense that Humberto Ávila formulates an explicit turn by arguing that one should shift the focus from security of meaning to security of criteria, in order to provide, in advance, prior and abstract parameters to face later and concrete interpretive problems Ávila(Ávila, 2011, p. 71).p. 472

PTÉ nesse sentido que Humberto Ávila formula uma virada explícita ao sustentar que se deve deslocar o foco da segurança de significado para a segurança de critérios, a fim de fornecer, de antemão, parâmetros prévios e abstratos para enfrentar problemas interpretativos posteriores e concretos (Ávila, 2011, p. 71) .

The question is not to open the black box all the way down, but to define prior and controllable criteria that make it possible to turn technically opaque modulations into legally decidable alternatives, without reducing control to automatic deference to complexity.p. 472

PTA questão não é abrir a caixa-preta até o fundo, mas definir critérios prévios e controláveis que permitam transformar modulações tecnicamente opacas em alternativas juridicamente decidíveis, sem reduzir o controle à deferência automática à complexidade.

Tércio Sampaio Ferraz Jr. completes the argument through the function of legal dogmatics. He presents it as a technology geared to action, which carves out what can be handled in the form of a decision, and recalls that the actual legal decision works with incomplete information and that its immediate purpose is to absorb insecurity, turning undecidable incompatibilities into decidable alternatives. On the platform, opacity tends to make the dispute endless, because there is always some technical backstage that could explain the effect. A minimum threshold works as a mechanism of finitude, since it organizes the debate around elements that can be argued in adversarial proceedings.

The Law does not need to see everything in order to control.p. 473

PTO Direito não precisa ver tudo para controlar.

Julie E. Cohen and Eros Roberto Grau explain why this control is justified. Cohen describes dominant platforms as agents that produce a territory of their own, made of protocols, data flows and algorithms, and argues that any meaningful control of this environment depends on a baseline level of information about what those mechanisms do. Grau, discussing how the legal rule takes shape in Duguit, links the legal norm to socially organized sanction and observes that the market establishes an order of its own making. On platforms, the sanction takes the form of suspension, banning, reduced visibility and blocked features, carried out through architecture and internal procedures. When this operates as a pattern, the demand for auditability is no longer a matter of transparency for appearance's sake and becomes a minimum requirement for subjecting private coercion to rational control.

What needs to be auditable?

The trigger is a relevant effect on enjoyment, and it is strictly delimited so that the parameter does not slide into maximalism. The parameter's proper field begins when the change, the unilateral modulation, the removal, the migration, the nerf, which is the adjustment that reduces an item's power or usefulness, the discontinuation, the reconfiguration of a rule or of the interface, or the sanction applied to the user affects enjoyment in a relevant way. Relevance may show up in access and permanence, in the functional integrity of the object, in the promised equivalences, in the object's symbolic position in the ecosystem or in the consumer's conditions for proving and challenging. The central point is that the change must be capable of lowering enjoyment, frustrating a legitimate expectation or abusively concentrating in the supplier's hands the intelligibility of what happened.

The CDC was not designed to allow the supplier to turn the harm into a mere accounting cost and move on.p. 645

PTO CDC não foi desenhado para permitir ao fornecedor transformar a lesão em mero custo contábil e seguir adiante.

Without access to the relevant versions, the performance records and the elements showing what was actually supplied, the legal discussion risks being held hostage by the documentary fiction of the base contract, which often says little about the real life of the servicep. 646

PTSem acesso às versões relevantes, aos registros de execução e aos elementos que demonstrem o fornecimento realizado, a discussão jurídica corre o risco de ficar refém da ficção documental do contrato-base, que muitas vezes diz pouco sobre a vida real do serviço

Specific performance of the obligation takes precedence precisely to keep the supplier from turning nonperformance into an advantageous economic calculation, treating harms converted into damages as an ordinary cost of doing business. Conversion into compensation should therefore occur only at the consumer's option or when specific relief or an equivalent practical result is impossible Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 898).p. 646

PTHá prevalência do cumprimento específico da obrigação justamente para evitar que o fornecedor transforme o inadimplemento em cálculo econômico vantajoso, convertendo lesões em perdas e danos como custo ordinário do negócio. Por isso, a conversão em indenização só deve ocorrer por opção do consumidor ou quando impossível a tutela específica ou o resultado prático equivalente (Miragem, 2020, p. 898) .

This framework works together with preliminary measures for the early production of evidence, notably actions for production of documents, especially in disputes where the contract was never delivered to the consumer or where the actual content of the relationship must be reconstructed Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 903).p. 646

PTEsse desenho se articula com providências antecedentes voltadas à produção antecipada de provas, notadamente as ações de exibição, especialmente em litígios em que o instrumento contratual não foi entregue ao consumidor ou em que o conteúdo efetivo do vínculo precisa ser reconstruído (Miragem, 2020, p. 903) .

All the mechanisms answer the same requirement: to keep the supplier's exclusive technical control from becoming a monopoly over the proof of the very unlawful act alleged.p. 663

PTTodos os mecanismos respondem à mesma exigência: impedir que o controle técnico exclusivo do fornecedor se converta em monopólio sobre a prova do próprio ilícito alegado.

Auditability alone does not decide whether a change is materially legitimate, because showing what changed does not answer whether the change preserved the substance of the enjoyment acquired. That second question belongs to functional value equivalence, which has a chapter of its own. Each criterion presupposes the other, since without auditability equivalence is hard to prove, and without equivalence auditability risks becoming a mere ability to narrate changes. In short, one governs the how of the mutation and the other the how much and the what, and this yields two tests for the interpreter. The first asks whether there was minimum institutional verifiability, with reasoning, timing, effects and reconstruction trails. The second asks whether the change preserved sufficient functional equivalence or significantly downgraded the practical and symbolic core of the object. The order matters, because the change is reconstructed first and its legitimacy measured afterward. When one test fails, the change becomes legally suspect, and when both fail, the case comes very close to a supply defect, an abusive practice, a breach of trust or an architecture of structural opacity.

Where it comes from

On its own, open auditability does not resolve the whole dispute, because demonstrating what changed and how it changed does not exhaust the question of the material legitimacy of the change.p. 647

PTSozinha, a auditabilidade aberta não resolve toda a controvérsia, porque demonstrar o que mudou e como mudou não esgota a pergunta sobre a legitimidade material da mudança.

One standard controls the how of the mutation; the other, the how much and the what of the mutation.p. 638

PTUm standard controla o como da mutação; o outro, o quanto e o que da mutação.

First, was there minimum institutional verifiability of the change, with reasoning, timing, effects and trails for reconstructing the supply? Second, did the change preserve sufficient functional equivalence with the enjoyment legitimately acquired, or did it significantly downgrade the practical and symbolic core of the object?p. 639

PTPrimeiro, houve verificabilidade institucional mínima da mudança, com motivação, temporalidade, efeitos e trilhas de reconstrução do fornecimento? Segundo, a alteração preservou equivalência funcional suficiente com a fruição legitimamente adquirida, ou rebaixou de modo relevante o núcleo prático e simbólico do objeto?

First, the change is reconstructed. Then, its material legitimacy is measured. This order matters. Without it, the supplier will always have the advantage of the fog.p. 647

PTPrimeiro, reconstrói-se a mudança. Depois, mede-se sua legitimidade material. Essa ordem importa. Sem ela, o fornecedor sempre terá a vantagem da névoa.

When one of these conditions fails, the legitimacy of the change already becomes legally suspect. When both fail at once, the case comes very close to a supply defect, an abusive practice, a breach of trust or even an architecture of structural opacity incompatible with the consumer protection regime.p. 638-639

PTQuando uma dessas condições falha, a legitimidade da mudança já se torna juridicamente suspeita. Quando ambas falham ao mesmo tempo, a hipótese se aproxima com força de vício de fornecimento, prática abusiva, quebra da confiança ou até de arquitetura de opacidade estrutural incompatível com o regime consumerista.

Doesn't auditing mean disclosing trade secrets?

The first is the secrecy objection, which holds that requiring auditability would mean opening the code, handing over trade secrets and stripping the software of protection. The answer is that the legal order already accommodates limited forms of scrutiny that preserve the informational asset, such as sealed proceedings, restricted expert access, narrowly defined functional analysis and institutional oversight. Denis Borges Barbosa notes that sealing a case restricts third-party access to sensitive information, such as source code, and bars the opposing party from using it for purposes unrelated to the dispute. Reconciling protection of the asset with the need for verification is therefore nothing new.

The protection of software does not turn it into a sacred black box.p. 643

PTA proteção do software não o transforma em caixa-preta sagrada.

Where it comes from

This point is important because the business objection usually comes in predictable guise: requiring auditability would amount to requiring the opening of code, the surrender of trade secrets, the exposure of know-how or the stripping of the software's protection.p. 643

PTEsse ponto é importante porque a objeção empresarial costuma vir com roupagem previsível: exigir auditabilidade equivaleria a exigir abertura de código, entrega do segredo industrial, exposição de know-how ou desproteção do software.

Sealed proceedings, restricted expert access, narrowly defined functional analysis, technically necessary use for certain purposes and institutional oversight do not demolish exclusivity.p. 643

PTSegredo de justiça, acesso pericial restrito, análise funcional delimitada, utilização tecnicamente necessária para certos fins e controle institucional não são demolições da exclusividade.

The legal framework itself provides for sealed proceedings, which restrict third-party access to sensitive information, such as source code, and bar the opposing party from using it for purposes unrelated to the dispute Barbosa(Barbosa, 2017, p. 1940).p. 643

PTA própria disciplina jurídica prevê segredo de justiça, o que restringe o acesso de terceiros a informações sensíveis, como o código-fonte, e impede seu uso pela parte contrária para fins estranhos ao litígio (Barbosa, 2017, p. 1940) .

A secret also has to be shown in order to be protected. Invoking secrecy or technical sensitivity in general terms, without specifying what must remain confidential, is not enough, and Barbosa observes that judicial control requires identifying what the secret is, because a broad assertion that it relates to the company's line of business makes such control impossible. The protection remains legitimate, but what matters to Consumer Law is not, as a rule, the internal ingenuity of the program, but how a given operating regime, change or criterion affected enjoyment. The examination concerns how the system functions, for purposes of oversight, and not the parasitic appropriation of the asset. When technical decisions make continued use of the service depend on invasive requirements, the need for the platform not to hide behind technical opacity and trade secrets becomes all the stronger.

The company can still protect its secret. What it cannot do is claim secrecy over the very impact of the change it imposed on the consumer.p. 644

PTA empresa continua podendo proteger seu segredo. O que ela não pode é querer segredo sobre o próprio impacto da mudança que impôs ao consumidor.

Where it comes from

Invoking “secrecy” or “technical sensitivity” in general terms, without specifying what actually needs to remain confidential and without allowing any institutionally relevant form of reconstruction, is not enough.p. 644

PTInvocar genericamente “segredo” ou “sensibilidade técnica” sem delimitar o que de fato precisa permanecer reservado e sem admitir qualquer forma de reconstrução institucionalmente relevante não basta.

For confidentiality to be legally respected, it needs to be identified and carved out, not treated as a magic fog that dissolves every requirement of accountability.p. 644

PTPara que o sigilo seja juridicamente respeitado, ele precisa ser identificado e recortado, não tratado como névoa mágica que dissolve toda exigência de prestação de contas.

For the matter to be subject to judicial control, “it is necessary to unveil and identify what secret would have been disclosed”, and it is not enough to allege broadly that it is a secret related to the company's line of business, because so sweeping an assertion does not allow effective judicial control of the dispute Barbosa(Barbosa, 2018, p. 392).p. 644

PTPara que a hipótese seja submetida ao controle judicial, “é necessário que se desvende e identifique qual o segredo que teria sido divulgado”, não bastando alegar, de modo amplo, que se trata de segredo relacionado à área de atuação da empresa, porque afirmação assim abrangente não permite efetivo controle judicial da controvérsia (Barbosa, 2018, p. 392) .

The legally relevant examination concerns how the system functions, for purposes of oversight, not the parasitic appropriation of the asset.p. 644

PTO exame juridicamente relevante incide sobre o funcionamento para fins de controle, não sobre a apropriação parasitária do ativo.

When technical decisions and internal policies extend the extraterritorial reach of code, making continued use and participation depend on invasive requirements or on control mechanisms that go beyond the perimeter of the contracted object, they reveal a claim to practical sovereignty over the consumer and the consumer's material means, which reinforces the need for open auditability as a minimum requirement so that the platform does not hide behind technical opacity and trade secrets, and so that governance by architecture is treated as conduct that is imputable, contestable and subject to proportionality.p. 499

PTQuando decisões técnicas e políticas internas ampliam a extraterritorialidade do código, condicionando permanência e participação a exigências invasivas ou a mecanismos de controle que excedem o perímetro do objeto contratado, evidencia-se a pretensão de soberania prática sobre o consumidor e sobre seus meios materiais, o que reforça a necessidade de auditabilidade aberta como exigência mínima para que a plataforma não se oculte atrás de opacidade técnica e de segredo industrial, e para que a governança por arquitetura seja tratada como conduta imputável, contestável e sujeita a proporcionalidade.

The second is the objection of technical complexity. Without the parameter, the supplier can always claim that it made the change for technical reasons, that it gave what information it could, that the impact was small, that the change was provided for in some document and that the consumer simply did not understand or did not like it. With the parameter, one can ask what was changed, on what basis, with what effect and with what reconstruction trails. A generic appeal to internal policy, security, service improvement or ecosystem integrity is not a self-sufficient justification, and legitimate complexity is not the same as obscurity used as a technique of power.

In continuous consumer relationships, complexity does not revoke good faith.p. 647

PTEm relações de consumo contínuo, a complexidade não revoga a boa-fé.

Where it comes from

Without this standard, the supplier can always claim that it made the change for technical reasons, that it gave what information it could, that the impact was small, that the change was provided for in some document and that the consumer simply did not understand or did not like it.p. 645

PTSem esse standard, o fornecedor pode sempre alegar que mudou por razões técnicas, que informou como pôde, que o impacto foi pequeno, que a alteração estava prevista em algum documento e que o consumidor apenas não entendeu ou não gostou.

On the other, it keeps a generic appeal to “internal policy”, “security”, “service improvement” or “ecosystem integrity” from serving as a self-sufficient justification immune to control, which would hollow out the duties of information, loyalty and adequacy in continuing performance.p. 473

PTDe outro, impede que a invocação genérica de “política interna”, “segurança”, “melhoria do serviço” ou “integridade do ecossistema” funcione como justificativa autossuficiente e imune a controle, esvaziando deveres de informação, lealdade e adequação na execução continuada.

In governed environments, this is what separates legitimate complexity from obscurity functionalized as a technique of power.p. 728

PTEm ambientes governados, é isso que separa a complexidade legítima da obscuridade funcionalizada como técnica de poder.

The third is that the requirement would multiply technical litigation. The opposite is true, because the parameter serves to contain disputes. With preserved trails, identifiable versions and reasons that can be stated, it avoids an endless war of opaque technicalities, in which each side speaks a different language and the decision ends up resting on intuitive deference to the better organized party. Expert examinations, production of documents and orders to clarify then bear on knowable objects, and the dispute stays within bounds.

Where it comes from

Open auditability also serves to contain litigation.p. 728

PTA auditabilidade aberta também desempenha função de contenção do litígio.

When there are preserved trails, identifiable relevant versions, reasons for change that can at least minimally be stated and basic elements for reconstructing the conflict, the court case is kept from turning into an endless war of opaque technicalities, in which each side speaks a different language and the decision ends up resting on intuitive deference to the better organized party.p. 728

PTQuando há trilhas preservadas, versões relevantes identificáveis, motivos de alteração minimamente enunciáveis e elementos básicos de reconstrução do conflito, evita-se que a discussão judicial se converta em guerra infinita de tecnicidades opacas, na qual cada lado fala idioma diverso e a decisão acaba entregue à deferência intuitiva à parte mais organizada.

The fourth is that the parameter invents a right or stifles innovation. On invention, besides its roots in the duty to inform, there is support in Karl Larenz, who describes the legal system as open and, to a certain degree, mobile, so the two operators do not replace the system but let it see, through controllable criteria, what shorthand formulas tend to erase. On innovation, the answer is that auditability is a criterion for the legitimacy of platform architectures, not hostility toward them. Platforms may innovate, update, fix, rebalance, migrate and protect their technical assets. What they cannot do is any of this under a regime of institutional irreconstructibility.

Where it comes from

Larenz describes the system as an “‘open’ system and, to a certain degree, a ‘mobile’ one”, always incomplete and continually put into question, whose function is to make clear the “intrinsic rationality”, the guiding values and the principles of Law Larenz(Larenz, 1997, p. 241).p. 481

PTLarenz descreve o sistema como “sistema ‘aberto’ e, até um certo grau, ‘móvel’”, sempre incompleto e continuamente posto em questão, cuja função é tornar clara a “racionalidade intrínseca”, os valores diretivos e os princípios do Direito (Larenz, 1997, p. 241) .

Open auditability and functional value equivalence occupy this place: they do not replace the system; they allow it to see, through controllable criteria, what shorthand formulas tend to erase.p. 481

PTAuditabilidade aberta e equivalência de valor funcional ocupam esse lugar: não substituem o sistema; permitem que ele enxergue, por critérios controláveis, aquilo que fórmulas abreviadas tendem a apagar.

Open auditability must therefore be understood as a criterion for the legitimacy of platform architectures, and not as abstract hostility toward them. Platforms may innovate, update, fix, rebalance, migrate and protect their technical assets. What they cannot do is any of this under a regime of institutional irreconstructibility, as if the very complexity of the system entitled them to decide alone what matters, what was changed and what may or may not be known by those who enjoy the service and by those who oversee it legally.p. 647

PTPor isso, a auditabilidade aberta deve ser compreendida como critério de legitimidade das arquiteturas de plataforma, e não como sua hostilidade abstrata. Plataformas podem inovar, atualizar, corrigir, reequilibrar, migrar e proteger seus ativos técnicos. O que não podem é fazê-lo em regime de irreconstrutibilidade institucional, como se a própria complexidade do sistema as autorizasse a decidir sozinhas o que importa, o que foi alterado e o que pode ou não ser conhecido por quem frui o serviço e por quem o controla juridicamente.

What does open auditability not reach?

The name calls for careful reading. Open auditability does not require total transparency, unrestricted opening of the infrastructure, or disclosure of source code, the full architecture of the system or sensitive trade secrets, nor full disclosure of proprietary models and metrics. Nor does it require absolute explainability or technical omniscience on the part of the judge, and the platform remains the owner of its intangible assets and internal architecture. Anyone who frames the claim as a sweeping search of the system is asking for something the parameter does not provide.

Where it comes from

The name must be read carefully to avoid misunderstanding. It is not about requiring the opening of code, a sweeping search of the infrastructure, full disclosure of internal systems or theatrical laboratory transparency.p. 636

PTO nome precisa ser lido com cuidado para não produzir mal-entendido. Não se trata de exigir abertura de código, devassa total da infraestrutura, publicidade integral dos sistemas internos ou transparência teatral de laboratório.

This is not a requirement of total transparency, nor of unrestricted opening of the technical infrastructure, nor an obligation to disclose source code, the full architecture of the system or sensitive trade secrets.p. 640

PTNão se está diante de exigência de transparência total, nem de abertura irrestrita da infraestrutura técnica, nem de obrigação de expor código-fonte, arquitetura integral do sistema ou segredos industriais sensíveis.

Its aim is neither to make the technical and organizational environment fully transparent nor to require full disclosure of models, source code, proprietary metrics or trade secrets.p. 472

PTO seu foco não é produzir transparência integral do ambiente técnico-organizacional nem impor abertura completa de modelos, códigos-fonte, métricas proprietárias ou segredos industriais.

The parameter is calibrated precisely to make control possible without demanding technical omniscience from the judge or disrupting the economic functioning of the ecosystem.p. 728

PTO parâmetro é calibrado exatamente para tornar o controle possível sem exigir onisciência técnica do julgador nem desorganizar o funcionamento econômico do ecossistema.

The platform remains the owner of its intangible assets and internal architecture, but can no longer invoke them to block entirely the institutional reconstruction of events that affect the consumer's enjoyment in a legally relevant way.p. 729

PTA plataforma continua titular de seus ativos intangíveis e de sua arquitetura interna, mas já não pode invocá-los para impedir, por completo, a reconstrução institucional de eventos que afetem de modo juridicamente relevante a fruição do consumidor.

As to which changes it reaches, the parameter leaves out microscopic adjustments, cosmetic changes and irrelevant fluctuations, and it does not turn every update into litigation. Not every change is unlawful, and not every improvement becomes a problem, because updates may be needed for compatibility, security, stability and legibility. As to effects, a lack of auditability makes the change legally suspect, and inadequate transparency amounts to structural abusiveness only depending on the intensity of the case. What the parameter provides is a basis for reconstruction, which does not dispense with the material examination of equivalence.

Where it comes from

This problem does not turn every improvement into an unlawful act. Updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility in a competitive ecosystem.p. 470

PTEsse problema não transforma toda melhoria em ilícito. Atualizações podem ser necessárias para compatibilidade, segurança, estabilidade e legibilidade em um ecossistema competitivo.

The section, then, does not turn every change into an unlawful act. It simply refuses to let change become an automatic alibi for irresponsibility.p. 639

PTA seção, assim, não transforma toda alteração em ilícito. Ela apenas recusa que a alteração se torne álibi automático para irresponsabilidade.

What remains after these exclusions is a minimum the supplier cannot refuse without consequences. Business secrecy, economic exclusivity and the complexity of the service no longer operate as absolute vetoes and become factors to be reconciled with duties of minimum verifiability. Opacity is no longer a zone naturally immune to legal protection and becomes a legal problem that can be handled through preservation orders, narrowly defined production of documents, restricted expert examination and verifiable reasoning. The service can still change, but not beyond legal control, and private governance no longer edges toward practical sovereignty.

Technical opacity, by itself, does not work as a free zone of liability.p. 481

PTA opacidade técnica, por si, não funciona como zona franca de responsabilidade.

Where it comes from

Business secrecy, economic exclusivity and the complexity of the service no longer operate as absolute vetoes on control and become factors to be reconciled with duties of minimum verifiability.p. 728-729

PTSegredo empresarial, exclusividade econômica e complexidade do serviço deixam de operar como vetos absolutos ao controle e passam a ser elementos a compatibilizar com deveres de verificabilidade mínima.

Structural opacity thus ceases to be a zone naturally immune to legal protection and becomes a legal problem that can be handled through preservation orders, narrowly defined production of documents, restricted expert examination, verifiable reasoning and other instruments consistent with the procedural and administrative rationality of the protective system.p. 729

PTA opacidade estrutural deixa, assim, de ser zona naturalmente imune à tutela e passa a ser problema jurídico tratável por ordens de preservação, exibição delimitada, perícia restrita, motivação verificável e outros instrumentos compatíveis com a racionalidade processual e administrativa do sistema protetivo.

Without this standard, private governance comes too close to practical sovereignty. With it, the mutability of the service remains possible, but no longer hovers above legal control.p. 648

PTSem esse standard, a governança privada se aproxima demais de soberania prática. Com ele, a mutabilidade do serviço permanece possível, mas já não paira acima do controle jurídico.
