The jurist's path

The Immaterial Object of Consumption

The Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) is the category that qualifies as an immaterial product, under art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1, the utility that only exists as a position of enjoyment sustained by private governance. Without this qualification the case seems to have no object, and with it the jurist knows what to demonstrate in court.

What problem does the category solve?

The typical conflict in continuous digital consumption does not look like the conflict legal dogmatics learned to resolve. The consumer still has the account, the item is still in the inventory and access is still open, but the utility that justified the purchase has been downgraded by a series of decisions by the supplier. Anyone looking for a spoiled thing or an interrupted performance finds neither, and the case seems to have no object.

Where it comes from

In platform ecosystems, the legally relevant loss does not always appear as an abrupt disappearance of the object, because it is often produced by ordinary degradation, functional downgrading, informational obscuring, reclassification of access, unilateral mutation of conditions of use and progressive compression of the position of enjoyment.p. 549

PTEm ecossistemas de plataforma, a perda juridicamente relevante nem sempre aparece como desaparecimento abrupto do objeto, porque muitas vezes se produz por degradação ordinária, rebaixamento funcional, obscurecimento informacional, reclassificação de acesso, mutação unilateral de condições de uso e compressão progressiva da posição de fruição.

The legal problem of continuous digital consumption requires parameters of qualification, proof and imputation that neither depend on material analogies nor are confined to the contractual text.p. 391

PTO problema jurídico do consumo digital contínuo exige parâmetros de qualificação, prova e imputação que não dependam de analogias materiais nem se limitem ao texto contratual.

The answer is not to call for a new law. The Consumer Protection Code already has the language it needs, provided one abandons the reading that it only works well with a stable thing, one-off delivery and an obvious defect. The category exists not to describe the digital but to bring sustained enjoyment, reconfiguration and private sanction back into a vocabulary that allows imputation and restoration without arbitrariness.

The CDC already offers sufficient language to face this problem, provided one accepts that, in continuous digital consumption, to govern the environment is to govern enjoyment itself.p. 550

PTO CDC já oferece linguagem suficiente para enfrentar esse problema, desde que se aceite que, no consumo digital contínuo, governar o ambiente é governar a própria fruição.

Where it comes from

Reconstructing the Consumer Protection Code as the normative infrastructure of the IOC regime requires, first of all, abandoning the reductionist reading according to which the CDC only works comfortably when the conflict involves a stable thing, one-off delivery and an obvious defect.p. 549

PTA reconstrução do Código de Defesa do Consumidor como infraestrutura normativa do regime do OIC exige, antes de tudo, abandonar a leitura reducionista segundo a qual o CDC só funciona confortavelmente quando o conflito envolve coisa estável, entrega pontual e defeito ostensivo.

The IOC, at this point, does not exist to “describe” the digital, but to bring sustained enjoyment, reconfiguration and enforcement back to a vocabulary that makes imputation and restoration possible without arbitrariness.p. 418

PTO OIC, nesse ponto, não existe para “descrever” o digital, mas para reconduzir a fruição sustentada, a reconfiguração e o enforcement a um vocabulário que permita imputação e recomposição sem arbitrariedade.

In the architecture of the regime, each category has its own task. Semiotic vulnerability describes the consumer's condition, the IOC describes the object, abusive teleology describes the unlawful act, personal integrity describes the protected goods, and open auditability and functional value equivalence describe the criteria for verification and restoration. Qualifying the object triggers the others, because all of them presuppose a bond in which utility depends on the continued administration of the environment.

Where it comes from

It is a dogmatic articulation in which semiotic vulnerability describes the condition, the IOC describes the object, abusive teleology describes the unlawful act, personal integrity describes the protected goods and open auditability together with functional value equivalence describe the operational criteria of verification and restoration.p. 395

PTÉ uma articulação dogmática em que a vulnerabilidade semiótica descreve a condição, o OIC descreve o objeto, a teleologia abusiva descreve o ilícito, a integridade pessoal descreve os bens protegidos e a auditabilidade aberta com a equivalência de valor funcional descrevem os critérios operacionais de verificação e recomposição.

This parameter thus functions as a trigger for the others.p. 719

PTEsse parâmetro exerce, assim, função de gatilho para os demais.

Without the correct qualification of the object as governed immaterial enjoyment, the subsequent parameters tend to lose traction, because auditability, functional equivalence, abusive teleology and restoration remedies presuppose precisely the existence of a bond in which the consumed utility depends on continued administration of the environment.p. 719

PTSem a correta qualificação do objeto como fruição imaterial governada, os parâmetros subsequentes tendem a perder aderência, porque auditabilidade, equivalência funcional, teleologia abusiva e remédios de recomposição pressupõem justamente a existência de um vínculo em que a utilidade consumida depende de administração continuada do ambiente.

Why doesn't the question of product or service decide the case?

The interpreter's first reaction is usually to classify, and that is where the analysis stalls. Calling everything a product, as if delivery were instantaneous, hides the processual character of the supply, and later modulations are dismissed as irrelevant externalities. Calling everything a generic service produces the opposite error, because it dissolves what was acquired and leaves no way to say what was lost, even while the bond is formally still being performed. These two failures are symmetrical blind spots.

Where it comes from

The first blindness is to reduce everything to a “delivered product” and treat subsequent technical modulations as irrelevant externality.p. 440

PTA primeira cegueira é reduzir tudo a “produto entregue” e tratar modulações técnicas subsequentes como exterioridade irrelevante.

The second blindness is to dissolve the acquired object into a “generic service” and, with that, make unsayable what, concretely, was lost, even though the bond formally remains in execution.p. 440

PTA segunda cegueira é dissolver o objeto adquirido em “serviço genérico” e, com isso, tornar indizível o que, concretamente, se perdeu, ainda que o vínculo continue formalmente em execução.

In this context, the product-service dichotomy tends to become a true semantic trap, given that it sometimes paralyzes the analysis as if the classification closed the regime, and sometimes dissolves the object into a generic “service” and makes invisible relevant losses that do not manifest as total interruption.p. 436

PTNeste contexto, a dicotomia produto-serviço tende a se tornar verdadeira armadilha semântica, dado que ora paralisa a análise como se a classificação encerrasse o regime, ora dissolve o objeto em um “serviço” genérico e invisibiliza perdas relevantes que não se manifestam como interrupção total.

The way out does not abolish the distinction. It still matters for the technical regime of defects, for the point at which a defect is ascertained, for the available remedies and for balancing restoration against compensation. What changes is the function of each term. Product makes intelligible what was acquired, as an economically valued enjoyment bundle, while service comes to designate the mode of continued performance, made up of maintenance, organization and modulation. The service dimension stops being an argument against the existence of the object and appears as the way the object is sustained.

The initial delivery does not exhaust performance, and continued execution does not erase the object.p. 444

PTA entrega inicial não esgota o cumprimento, e a execução continuada não apaga o objeto.

Where it comes from

Product fixes the intelligibility of what is acquired in the IOC, preserving the object as an economically valued and legally consumable enjoyment bundle, even though immaterial and dependent on technical mediations.p. 442

PTProduto fixa a inteligibilidade do adquirido no OIC, preservando o objeto como feixe de fruição economicamente valorado e juridicamente consumível, ainda que imaterial e dependente de mediações técnicas.

Service, in turn, ceases to mean “there is no object” and comes to designate the mode of continued performance of the supply, made up of practices of maintenance, organization and modulation of the conditions of enjoyment that extend over time.p. 442

PTServiço, por sua vez, deixa de significar “não há objeto” e passa a designar o modo de execução continuada do fornecimento, composto por práticas de manutenção, organização e modulação das condições de fruição que se projetam no tempo.

It matters insofar as it makes it possible to adjust specific normative consequences, such as the technical regime of defects, the point of ascertainment, the applicable remedies and the calibration between functional restoration and compensation.p. 564

PTEla importa enquanto permite ajustar consequências normativas específicas, como o regime técnico dos vícios, o marco de constatação, os remédios aplicáveis e a calibração entre recomposição funcional e indenização.

Doctrinal support lies in the way the concepts of Brazil's Consumer Protection Code (CDC) were drafted. Claudia Lima Marques reads them as functional concepts, defined by the problem they solve, and Bruno Miragem treats them as relational. The central question therefore stops being whether the object fits a civil law ontology inherited from another environment and becomes whether there is a final recipient, a professional market agent and a utility supplied in a context of structural dependence. The interpretive foundation is the factual vulnerability of the final recipient. Adhesion contracts, pre-drafted general conditions, network organization, practical dependence and difficulty in understanding the internal logic of the service are signs of it, even when the consumer has technical training.

The CDC does not protect the consumer in spite of their fragility; it protects them because this fragility is the constitutive datum of the consumer relationship.p. 553

PTO CDC não protege o consumidor apesar de sua fragilidade; protege-o porque essa fragilidade é o dado constitutivo da relação de consumo.

Where it comes from

In complex digital supplies, this is decisive: the central question is not whether the object fits comfortably into a civil law ontology inherited from another technological environment, but whether there is a final recipient, a professional market agent and a utility supplied in a context of structural dependence.p. 552-553

PTEm fornecimentos digitais complexos, isso é decisivo: a pergunta central não é se o objeto cabe com conforto numa ontologia civilista herdada de outro ambiente tecnológico, mas se há destinatário final, agente profissional de mercado e utilidade fornecida em contexto de dependência estrutural.

Adhesion contracting, pre-drafted general conditions, network organization, the adherent's practical dependence and difficulty in understanding the internal logic of the service are clear signs of factual vulnerability, even when the consumer has technical training or works professionally in another field.p. 555

PTContratação por adesão, condições gerais predispostas, organização em rede, dependência prática do aderente e dificuldade de compreender a lógica interna do serviço são indícios claros de vulnerabilidade fática, mesmo quando o consumidor tenha formação técnica ou atue profissionalmente em outro campo.

But, for the IOC regime, the true interpretive foundation lies in the factual vulnerability of the final recipient in the face of an immaterial, continuous supply governed by a third party.p. 557

PTMas, para o regime do OIC, o verdadeiro alicerce interpretativo está na vulnerabilidade fática do destinatário final diante de fornecimento imaterial, contínuo e governado por terceiro.

What does the consumer acquire, after all?

Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1 says that a product is any good, movable or immovable, material or immaterial, and works as a coverage clause. Rizzatto Nunes already read immaterial goods into it, such as banking products, and Nelson Nery Jr. saw in it a notion broader than the Civil Code's good or thing. The core of the category is economically organized utility, not physical form. The lack of a physical medium therefore does not take the object outside protection, and the continuity of the bond becomes a feature of the supply rather than a pretext for dissolving it into generic activity.

The legal definition of product in Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1 operates as a coverage clause that prevents escape from protection when the object does not take the form of a physical medium.p. 411

PTA definição legal de produto no art. 3º, §1º, do CDC opera como cláusula de abrangência que impede a fuga da tutela protetiva quando o objeto não se apresenta sob a forma de suporte físico.

Where it comes from

Continuity thus functions as a property of the supply bond and not as a pretext for dissolving the object into generic activity.p. 412

PTA continuidade, assim, funciona como propriedade do vínculo de fornecimento e não como pretexto para dissolver o objeto em atividade genérica.

Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1 already offers a sufficient basis for understanding the IOC as an immaterial product.p. 574

PTO art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC já oferece base suficiente para compreender o OIC como produto imaterial.

The formulation makes three claims. The IOC is an immaterial product whose legal mode of existence is not exhausted by an act of delivery, because it takes shape as a position of enjoyment sustained in a technical and organizational environment that maintains, modulates and conditions utility over time. The object is neither isolated content nor an abstract promise of access, because it is a bundle of functionalities and meanings that makes up the utility offered. And immateriality does not mean the absence of a medium. It means the good is grasped through its function and the regime of enjoyment it establishes.

Where it comes from

Under these dogmatic and methodological premises, the core of the IOC can be formulated in a lean and controllable way as an immaterial product whose mode of legal existence is not exhausted in an act of delivery, because it is realized as a position of enjoyment sustained in a technical-organizational environment that maintains, modulates and conditions the acquired utility over time.p. 421

PTSob tais premissas dogmáticas e metodológicas, o núcleo do OIC pode ser formulado de modo enxuto e controlável como produto imaterial cujo modo de existência jurídica não se esgota em ato de entrega, porque se realiza como posição de fruição sustentada em ambiente técnico-organizacional que mantém, modula e condiciona a utilidade adquirida ao longo do tempo.

The object is not confused with isolated “content” nor with an abstract promise of access, but with a bundle of functionalities and meanings that makes up the economically offered utility and that remains enjoyable through the continuity of infrastructural conditions of availability, mediation and presentation.p. 421

PTO objeto não se confunde com “conteúdo” isolado nem com promessa abstrata de acesso, mas com feixe de funcionalidades e significações que compõe a utilidade economicamente ofertada e que permanece fruível mediante continuidade de condições infraestruturais de disponibilidade, mediação e apresentação.

Immateriality, in this sense, does not reduce the object to the absence of a medium, but identifies that the good offered is graspable through its function and the regime of enjoyment it establishes, and remains legally classifiable as a product.p. 421

PTA imaterialidade, nesse sentido, não reduz o objeto a ausência de suporte, e sim identifica que o bem ofertado é apreensível pela função e pelo regime de fruição que instaura, mantendo-se juridicamente qualificável como produto.

The practical consequence is a shift in what gets examined. Infrastructure stops being the technical backdrop of an object already delivered and becomes part of how the promise is performed, because it is through infrastructure that availability, presentation, circulation, permanence and sanction are maintained. Eros Grau helps describe this layer as practical normativity, a functional coercion that goes beyond the clause. Recognizing it does not mean declaring every technical decision unlawful. It serves to prevent such decisions from being automatically shielded. The resulting criterion is clear, and it leaves out both the physical nature of the medium and the labels the supplier chooses.

Where it comes from

It is part of the form of performance of the promise of enjoyment, because it is through it that conditions of availability, presentation, circulation, permanence and sanction that make the acquired utility effectively enjoyable are maintained and modulated over time.p. 426

PTEla integra a forma de cumprimento da promessa de fruição, porque é por meio dela que se mantêm e se modulam, no tempo, condições de disponibilidade, apresentação, circulação, permanência e sanção que tornam a utilidade adquirida efetivamente fruível.

Recognizing practical normativity, however, is not the same as declaring every technical decision unlawful, nor as turning ordinary interface frictions into abuse.p. 426

PTReconhecer a normatividade prática, porém, não equivale a declarar ilícita toda decisão técnica, nem a transformar fricções ordinárias de interface em abuso.

The criterion, therefore, is neither the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

What does a player's account show?

The Riot Games Terms of Service show the structure in a few clauses. Clause 4.1 treats Virtual Content as the object of a limited license, and clause 4.2 accepts that it may be bought, earned or received from another player as a gift. The terms also define this content and tie it to the account, listing in-game currency, skins, emotes and accessories. The license form tries to make the bond precarious, while the contractual definition proves there is an identifiable core. The clause helps prove the object without exhausting its regime.

The clause helps prove the existence of the object.p. 575

PTA cláusula ajuda a provar a existência do objeto.

Where it comes from

For this reason too, the contractual form of the license is not enough to dissolve the legal density of the acquired enjoyment.p. 575

PTTambém por isso, a forma contratual da licença não basta para dissolver a densidade jurídica da fruição adquirida.

The account is the clearest example, because it brings together in a single record the inventory of licensed content, progression markers, social identifiers and reputation signals. The public status page shows that unavailability strips the content of its utility, and ticket-based account recovery shows that the account is a record managed by the company. Under these conditions, use value is a flow, not a stock. When there is a block, termination or significant degradation, this is the unit where the loss tends to appear, which disposes of the objection that the account is a mere credential.

The account is not a peripheral detail of the provision. It is the very infrastructure of consumption.p. 410

PTA conta não é um detalhe periférico da prestação. Ela é a própria infraestrutura do consumo.

Where it comes from

It is the organizational point that holds the enjoyment bundle together, because it concentrates, in a single institutional record, the inventory of licensed virtual content, progression markers, social identifiers and reputation signals by which the user's presence is read, displayed and recognized in the environment.p. 408

PTEla é o ponto organizacional que mantém unido o feixe de fruição, porque concentra, em um mesmo registro institucional, o inventário de conteúdos virtuais licenciados, os marcadores de progressão, os identificadores sociais e os sinais de reputação pelos quais a presença do usuário é lida, exibida e reconhecida no ambiente.

The use value of the bundle, in this condition, is not stock, it is flow.p. 409

PTO valor de uso do feixe, nessa condição, não é estoque, é fluxo.

When there is a block, termination or significant degradation of the regime, it is in this unit that the relevant loss tends to manifest itself.p. 410

PTQuando há bloqueio, encerramento ou degradação significativa do regime, é nessa unidade que a perda relevante tende a se manifestar.

Evolving skins show the same thing on a smaller scale. An Ultimate Skin depends on scripts and on rendering in every match, and the company itself discloses a memory budget per champion, so system requirements can reconfigure it. Frustration may take the form of a loss of consistency in the model, a change in features or a reconfiguration that keeps the item's name but not the core that justified the purchase.

Where it comes from

The consumer does not buy merely an “image” attached to the character.p. 439

PTO consumidor não compra apenas uma “imagem” acoplada ao personagem.

Frustration may manifest as localized degradation of the aesthetic experience, loss of consistency of the evolving model, alteration of features that were part of the practical promise of enjoyment, or a reconfiguration that preserves the item's name, but does not preserve the core of the utility that justified the purchase decision.p. 440

PTA frustração pode se manifestar como degradação localizada da experiência estética, perda de consistência do modelo evolutivo, alteração de recursos que eram parte da promessa prática de fruição, ou reconfiguração que preserva o nome do item, mas não preserva o núcleo da utilidade que justificou a decisão de compra.

How is it shown that there is an IOC?

The category serves a dogmatic function only when it works as both filter and roadmap. Whether it applies is decided by four cumulative elements, each with its own test. The first is economic and functional. It calls for an identifiable core of utility, promised and enjoyed as acquired, that can be reconstructed from the relevant offer and from how enjoyment actually takes place. The second is structural, because the utility must take shape as a position sustained over time, not as a one-time event. The third is infrastructural, because the utility must depend on governed conditions such as access, permanence, visibility, circulation and sanction. The fourth concerns legal relevance. It calls for the typical possibility of a relevant degradation of the bundle without a total loss of access, in a persistent and substantive way.

The IOC only fulfills a dogmatic function when it operates as a filter and as a roadmap.p. 428

PTO OIC só cumpre função dogmática quando opera como filtro e como roteiro.

Application is decided neither by technological intuition nor by automatic deference to the contract, but by the cumulative presence of verifiable elements of the regime, which makes it possible to stabilize a path of qualification.p. 429

PTA incidência não se decide por intuição tecnológica nem por deferência automática ao contrato, mas pela presença cumulativa de elementos verificáveis do regime, o que permite estabilizar um percurso de qualificação.

Where it comes from

The first element is economic-functional. An identifiable core of utility is required, promised and enjoyed as acquired, reconstructible from the relevant offer and from the effective mode of enjoyment. The second element is structural. The utility is realized as a position of enjoyment sustained over time, and not as an exhausted event. The third element is infrastructural. The utility depends on governed conditions, such as access, permanence, visibility, circulation and sanction, administered by continuous execution. The fourth element is one of legal relevance. There is a typical possibility of relevant degradation of the bundle without total rupture of access, in a persistent and substantive way, and not as one-off discomfort.p. 433

PTO primeiro elemento é econômico-funcional. Exige-se um núcleo de utilidade identificável, prometido e fruído como adquirido, reconstruível a partir da oferta relevante e do modo efetivo de fruição. O segundo elemento é estrutural. A utilidade se realiza como posição de fruição sustentada no tempo, e não como evento exaurido. O terceiro elemento é infraestrutural. A utilidade depende de condições governadas, como acesso, permanência, visibilidade, circulação e sanção, administradas por execução contínua. O quarto elemento é de relevância jurídica. Há possibilidade típica de degradação relevante do feixe sem ruptura total do acesso, de modo persistente e substantivo, e não como desconforto pontual.

The lawyer does not need to prove how the algorithm decides. Criteria that required full access to logs and internal rules would make the category unworkable, and Tércio Sampaio Ferraz Jr. reminds us that legal decision operates under uncertainty and does not presuppose complete information. What must be shown is governance relevant to the acquired utility, stable enough to produce patterns of enjoyment and of degradation. The interpreter, in turn, must state which elements of the regime were used, how they connect to the result, and why ordinary variation was distinguished from significant degradation.

Criteria of incidence cannot depend on full access to algorithms, logs and internal rules as a condition of possibility, since that would turn the IOC into an impracticable category, reserved for exceptional cases.p. 429

PTCritérios de incidência não podem depender de acesso integral a algoritmo, logs e regras internas como condição de possibilidade, pois isso converteria o OIC em categoria impraticável, reservada a casos excepcionais.

Where it comes from

What matters for application is not to prove “how” the algorithm decides at a microscopic level, but to show that there is infrastructural governance relevant to the acquired utility and that it operates with enough stability to produce patterns of enjoyment and of relevant degradation without total rupture of access.p. 429-430

PTO que importa para incidência não é provar “como” o algoritmo decide em nível microscópico, mas demonstrar que existe governança infraestrutural relevante para a utilidade adquirida e que ela opera com estabilidade suficiente para produzir padrões de fruição e de degradação relevante sem ruptura total do acesso.

The core of the IOC, therefore, must impose on the interpreter the duty to indicate the elements of the regime used, the connections between these elements and the result, and the reasons why ordinary variations were distinguished from significant degradationsp. 420

PTO núcleo do OIC, portanto, deve impor ao intérprete o dever de indicar os elementos do regime utilizados, as conexões entre esses elementos e o resultado, e as razões pelas quais variações ordinárias foram distinguidas de degradações significativas

The symbolic dimension calls for particular care. Following Umberto Eco, meaning is part of the bundle when it makes up the core of the utility offered, that is, when the platform sells recognition, visibility, circulation, reputation, belonging or status as part of the value, explicitly or in practice. It stays out when it is aesthetics, marketing, generic advertising narrative or diffuse satisfaction, with no identifiable core and no link to the position of enjoyment. The test is not how attached the consumer is but what was sold.

Where it comes from

It forms part of the bundle when it makes up the core of utility economically offered and enjoyed as a position in the ecosystem, that is, when the platform sells, explicitly or functionally, effects of recognition, visibility, circulation, reputation, belonging or status as part of the value of what is acquired.p. 432

PTEla integra o feixe quando compõe o núcleo de utilidade economicamente ofertada e fruída como posição no ecossistema, ou seja, quando a plataforma vende, explícita ou funcionalmente, efeitos de reconhecimento, visibilidade, circulação, reputação, pertencimento ou status como parte do valor do adquirido.

The dimension does not form part of it when it is a matter of aesthetics, marketing, generic advertising narrative or diffuse satisfaction with no identifiable functional core and no structural link to the sustained position of enjoyment.p. 432

PTA dimensão não integra quando se trata de estética, marketing, narrativa publicitária genérica ou satisfação difusa sem núcleo funcional identificável e sem vínculo estrutural com a posição de fruição sustentada.

What falls outside the category?

The negative boundary is a condition for the concept to work, and there are five limits. The first is one-off access without a sustained position of enjoyment. The second is a platform that functions as a channel or storefront without structurally governing the acquired utility. The third covers symbolic value that is functionally accessory, so predominantly aesthetic changes fall, as a rule, outside the concept. The fourth covers ordinary variations, such as updates, interface adjustments, anti-abuse measures, routine moderation and occasional instability. The fifth is a conflict whose core lies elsewhere, such as a classic one-off defect, an undue charge, isolated misleading advertising or a specific failure to honor an offer, which the CDC's traditional tools already resolve.

The IOC comes to function as a language of proof and screening, and not as a vocabulary of atmosphere.p. 435

PTO OIC passa a funcionar como linguagem de prova e de triagem, e não como vocabulário de atmosfera.

Where it comes from

The first limit is occasional access without a sustained position of enjoyment.p. 433

PTO primeiro limite é o acesso pontual sem posição de fruição sustentada.

The second limit is the scenario in which the platform functions as a channel or storefront but does not structurally govern the acquired utility, lacking the infrastructural centrality characteristic of the IOC.p. 434

PTO segundo limite é a hipótese em que a plataforma funciona como canal ou vitrine, mas não governa, em termos estruturais, a utilidade adquirida, faltando-lhe a centralidade infraestrutural característica do OIC.

The third limit concerns situations in which symbolic or semiotic value is functionally accessory and is not part of the core of the promised enjoyment bundle.p. 434

PTO terceiro limite recai sobre situações em que o valor simbólico ou semiótico é funcionalmente acessório e não integra o núcleo do feixe de fruição prometido.

The fourth limit covers ordinary variations of the ecosystem that do not constitute relevant and persistent degradation of the bundle, such as updates, interface adjustments, anti-abuse measures, regular moderation and isolated instabilities.p. 434

PTO quarto limite abrange variações ordinárias do ecossistema que não configuram degradação relevante e persistente do feixe, como atualizações, ajustes de interface, medidas antiabuso, moderação regular e instabilidades pontuais.

The fifth limit is the case in which the core of the conflict is something else, such as a classic one-off defect, an undue charge, isolated misleading advertising or a specific failure to honor an offer.p. 435

PTO quinto limite é o caso em que o núcleo do conflito é outro, como vício pontual clássico, cobrança indevida, publicidade enganosa localizada ou inadimplemento específico de oferta.

Two limits deserve the lawyer's attention. Aesthetic change stays out as a rule, except when, in the concrete case, it forms part of a converging set of signs capable of supporting the objective recognition of an unlawful act of architecture through structural indications of abusive teleology. The rule and the exception are reconciled through the core that was sold. Nor does the marketplace fall wholly outside the regime. One that merely provides placement and customer acquisition is excluded in principle, but in certain arrangements the mediation layer materially conditions how value is appropriated, and the bundle exploited as a product in the B2B market may reach the seller, through Article 29 of the CDC2, if the seller is in a factual position of vulnerability. Not every digital mediation turns a good into an immaterial object of consumption.

This is not to say that every digital mediation automatically turns any good into an IOC.p. 487

PTNão se afirma, com isso, que toda mediação digital converta automaticamente qualquer bem em OIC.

Where it comes from

Therefore, as a rule, predominantly aesthetic changes fall outside the reach of the concept, except when, in the concrete case, they form a convergent set of signs capable of supporting objective recognition of an unlawful act of architecture through structural indications of abusive teleology.p. 434

PTPor isso, em regra, ficam fora do alcance do conceito alterações predominantemente estéticas, salvo quando, no caso concreto, componham um conjunto convergente de sinais apto a sustentar reconhecimento objetivo de ilícito arquitetural por indícios estruturais de teleologia abusiva.

Once the applicability of Article 29 of the CDC2 is admitted to qualify legal entities in a factual position of vulnerability, the regime proves fully pertinent when the immaterial object in question is the enjoyment bundle in a sustained position exploited as a product in the B2B market by its holder and offered, through the marketplace platform, to the seller.p. 434

PTAdmitida a aplicabilidade do art. 29 do CDC para qualificar pessoas jurídicas em posição factual de vulnerabilidade, o regime se mostra plenamente pertinente quando o objeto imaterial em questão é o feixe de fruição em posição sustentada explorado como produto no mercado B2B pela detentora e oferecido, por via da plataforma marketplace, ao seller (vendedor).

In digital marketplaces, the mediation layer governed by the platform materially conditions the appropriation of value by the consumer, whether the immediate object is material or immaterial.p. 487

PTEm marketplaces digitais, a camada de mediação governada pela plataforma condiciona de modo material a apropriação do valor pelo consumidor, ainda que o objeto imediato seja material ou imaterial.

How does the category relate to the doctrine of digital goods?

The doctrine of digital goods, as formulated by Bruno Zampier Lacerda, solves an earlier problem. It holds that techno-digital assets are legal goods, open to appropriation, circulation, enjoyment and protection, and it blocks the semantic fraud of the revocable license as a formula that can make what has become part of the user's sphere vanish. The Civil Code, in Articles 79 to 103, supplies the classificatory background that gives skins, currencies and items a minimum of legal substance. The category builds on this floor without disputing it.

Where it comes from

Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda's doctrine of digital goods fulfills an important function because it resolves, with good density, a problem that cannot be circumvented by contractual rhetoric or by nostalgia for tangibility: techno-digital assets are legal goods and do not disappear from the world of law just because they are incorporeal.p. 565

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais de Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda cumpre função importante porque resolve, com boa densidade, um problema que não pode ser contornado por retórica contratual ou por nostalgia da tangibilidade: ativos tecnodigitais são bens jurídicos e não desaparecem do mundo do Direito só porque são incorpóreos.

This matters a great deal here because it rules out, from the outset, the semantic fraud of the “revocable license” as a formula capable of evaporating everything that has already been economically incorporated into the user's sphere.p. 565-566

PTIsso importa bastante aqui porque afasta, logo de saída, a fraude semântica da “licença revogável” como fórmula capaz de evaporar tudo o que já foi economicamente incorporado à esfera do usuário.

The doctrine of digital goods builds, in other words, the floor from which the debate about regime becomes possible.p. 569

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais constrói, em outras palavras, o piso a partir do qual o debate sobre regime se torna possível.

The next step shifts the axis. Lacerda confronts the silence of the Civil Code, while the IOC regime confronts the opacity of the CDC about the immaterial product under continued and governed enjoyment. The question stops being who holds the asset and how it circulates, and becomes how enjoyment is maintained, modulated, degraded, made intelligible and restored over time. The civil typology of patrimonial, existential and mixed goods therefore remains useful in disputes over ownership, succession and disposal, but a consumer dispute calls for a prior step, which is to check whether the object is an IOC under governed enjoyment. Without that step, a loss that comes not as abrupt removal but as managed deterioration goes unnoticed.

The nominal permanence of the asset, or even of the account, is not enough to rule out a defect.p. 572

PTA permanência nominal do ativo, ou mesmo da conta, não basta para afastar vício.

Where it comes from

The IOC regime starts from the same recognition of legal standing, only it shifts the focus: instead of asking predominantly who “has” the asset, how it circulates or is transmitted, it comes to ask how enjoyment is maintained, modulated, degraded, made intelligible and restorable over time.p. 567

PTO regime do OIC parte do mesmo reconhecimento de juridicidade, só que desloca o foco: em vez de perguntar predominantemente quem “tem” o ativo, como ele circula ou se transmite, passa a perguntar como a fruição é mantida, modulada, degradada, inteligibilizada e recomponível no tempo.

It does not confront the silence of the Civil Code on the status of the digital asset, but the opacity of the Consumer Protection Code on the regime of the immaterial product under continued and governed enjoyment.p. 568

PTEla não enfrenta o silêncio do Código Civil sobre o estatuto do ativo digital, mas a opacidade do Código de Defesa do Consumidor sobre o regime do produto imaterial sob fruição continuada e governada.

The point, however, is that applying this taxonomy to platform consumer disputes requires a prior methodological step, and this step is verifying that the object at issue is not merely a digital asset generically considered, but an Immaterial Object of Consumption under governed enjoyment.p. 571

PTO ponto, todavia, é que a aplicação dessa taxonomia a controvérsias de consumo em plataforma exige passo metodológico prévio, e esse passo é a verificação de que o objeto em causa não é apenas ativo digital genericamente considerado, mas Objeto Imaterial de Consumo sob fruição governada.

This makes it possible to confront not only abrupt withdrawal, but also administered deterioration.p. 573

PTIsso permite enfrentar não só a retirada abrupta, mas também a deterioração administrada.

What changes in how defect, time limits and the offer are read?

As to defect, protection does not depend on proving that the object has disappeared. It is enough to show that enjoyment ceased to correspond, in a legally relevant way, to the functional standard that was part of the relationship, and Article 18 of the CDC3, which does not require total uselessness, opens the door to this. The downgrading may take the form of a functional downgrade, compressed features, lost relational utility or systematic instability of the environment, and the initial access may have gone through without any hitch. Commenting on Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC4, Rizzatto Nunes gives the close example of a supplier who reduces the intensity of performance without lowering the price.

Where it comes from

For the IOC, this means that protection does not depend on proving the “disappearance” of the object, and it is enough to show that enjoyment ceased to correspond, in a legally relevant way, to the functional standard that was part of the relationship.p. 559

PTPara o OIC, isso significa que a tutela não depende de provar “desaparecimento” do objeto, bastando demonstrar que a fruição deixou de corresponder, de modo juridicamente relevante, ao padrão funcional que integrava a relação.

The downgrading need not come disguised as total cancellation; it may appear as a functional downgrade, compression of features, loss of relational utility or systematic instability of the environment.p. 562

PTO rebaixamento não precisa vir travestido de cancelamento total; pode aparecer como downgrade funcional, compressão de recursos, perda de utilidade relacional ou instabilidade sistemática do ambiente.

The initial access may even occur without a hitch, but enjoyment may be downgraded later, through successive mutations, functional suppressions, silent reconfigurations or environmental alterations that preserve the formal shell of the provision while eroding its concrete utility.p. 558

PTO acesso inicial pode até ocorrer sem tropeço, mas a fruição pode ser rebaixada depois, por mutações sucessivas, supressões funcionais, reconfigurações silenciosas ou alterações de ambiente que preservam a casca formal da prestação enquanto corroem sua utilidade concreta.

As to the time limit, Article 26 of the CDC5 remains in force, with thirty days for non-durable services and products and ninety for durable ones, counted from effective delivery or the end of performance, and, for a hidden defect, with the starting point shifted to the moment the defect becomes evident. The provision is evidence of a tension with forms of performance in which inadequacy emerges gradually, which requires rereading the point of knowability instead of fixing it on the date of first access. Following Zelmo Denari, the initial formal delivery cannot become an artificial marker that prematurely empties protection of its content. The rereading neither sets the time limit aside nor settles, by itself, which of the two bands the IOC falls into, so the lawyer must proceed with that caution.

Where it comes from

Hence the importance of Article 26 of the CDC5, since it serves as evidence of the tension between classic time limits for ascertaining a defect and continuous provisions in which inadequacy may emerge progressively, requiring a rereading of the point of knowability instead of rigid fixing on the date of first access.p. 558

PTDaí a importância do art. 26 do CDC , visto que serve de evidência da tensão entre prazos clássicos de constatação do vício e prestações contínuas em que a inadequação pode emergir progressivamente, impondo releitura do marco de cognoscibilidade em vez de fixação rígida na data do primeiro acesso.

In continuing relationships, the time limit cannot be used to turn the initial formal delivery into an artificial marker for the premature emptying of consumer protection.p. 558

PTEm relações continuadas, o prazo não pode ser usado para converter a entrega formal inicial em marco artificial de esvaziamento precoce da tutela consumerista.

As to the offer, the storefront ceases to be mere advertising. Article 30 of the CDC6 makes sufficiently precise information binding, and the promise remains alive after adhesion, because it is part of the substance of what was acquired. The object is measured by the functional correspondence between the promised utility and the utility maintained in the course of the relationship. On mutability, the social function of the contract, in Article 421 of the Civil Code7, good faith, in Article 422 of the Civil Code8, and the nullity of the unilateral modification clause, in Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC4, form the limit. Claudia Lima Marques treats these bonds as long-term relationships marked by captivity, and Judith Martins-Costa describes the obligation as a program of performance. The parallel with captive contracts is methodological, without forcing the fit.

The continued contract is not a space of the supplier's private sovereignty just because performance remains open over time.p. 561

PTO contrato continuado não é espaço de soberania privada do fornecedor só porque a execução permanece aberta no tempo.

Where it comes from

The immaterial object is also measured by the functional correspondence between promised utility and utility effectively maintained in the course of the relationship.p. 577

PTO objeto imaterial é medido também pela correspondência funcional entre utilidade prometida e utilidade efetivamente mantida no curso da relação.

The contract ceases to be a one-off event and reveals itself as a program of performance developed over time, requiring collaboration, loyalty and respect for the concrete way enjoyment was organized and legitimately entrusted by the consumer.p. 560

PTO contrato deixa de ser evento pontual para se revelar como programa de cumprimento desenvolvido no tempo, exigindo colaboração, lealdade e respeito ao modo concreto como a fruição foi organizada e legitimamente confiada pelo consumidor.

As to remedies, revision does not require unforeseeability, and the consumer may ask for maintenance of the bond with correction of the provision, modification of the harmful clause, functional restoration or, as a last resort, termination with the appropriate effects. The chapter on remedies develops this order.

Continuous provision does not reduce protection; it only changes how the harm is measured and how the remedy is chosen.p. 563

PTA prestação contínua não reduz a tutela; só muda a forma de medir o dano e de escolher o remédio.

Where it comes from

The consumer may demand maintenance of the bond with correction of the provision, modification of the harmful clause, functional restoration of the utility or, as a last resort, termination with the appropriate effects.p. 563

PTO consumidor pode exigir manutenção do vínculo com correção da prestação, modificação da cláusula lesiva, recomposição funcional da utilidade ou, em último caso, rescisão com os efeitos cabíveis.

Isn't it just a service, or just a license?

The first objection is that only a service is provided, or only a license granted. The answer is that these formulas are not enough to naturalize the precariousness of the bond, and that bringing it back to the immaterial product does not deny the provision, which appears as the way of sustaining the object. Without the concept, the platform would always win by writing its own dictionary.

Where it comes from

From this angle, the qualification of the IOC as an immaterial product does not require denying the service dimension of the relationship.p. 718

PTSob essa chave, a qualificação do OIC como produto imaterial não exige a negação da dimensão prestacional do vínculo.

This does not mean denying the relevance of the contract, but refusing its explanatory sufficiency in environments in which the economic content of the relationship is carried out continuously by mechanisms that far exceed the contractual text.p. 718

PTIsso não significa negar a relevância do contrato, mas recusar sua suficiência explicativa em ambientes nos quais o conteúdo econômico da relação se realiza de modo continuado por mecanismos que excedem largamente o texto contratual.

Without it, the platform would always win by private dictionary.p. 573

PTSem ele, a plataforma sempre venceria por dicionário privado.

The second is that the category invents a new right. The answer is that it is a development internal to the CDC, achieved by hermeneutic integration consistent with its protective matrix, with no autonomous microsystem for the digital and no grafting. The third invokes technical necessity. The answer comes from Roberto Mangabeira Unger, for whom institutions are revisable artifacts. Technical restrictions exist, but they do not work as a seal of immunity. The fourth is that the concept would be elastic, and the negative boundary and the methodological safeguard answer it. Miguel Reale requires the concept to integrate facts, values and norms, Karl Larenz warns against abstraction that empties concepts, and Humberto Ávila places on the interpreter the burden of arguing with criteria.

In platform consumption, opacity and technical language facilitate both the rhetoric of “it is not possible to know” and the rhetoric of “this is obviously abusive”, and that is why the category needs to block this short circuit by imposing verifiable criteria.p. 421

PTNo consumo por plataformas, a opacidade e a linguagem técnica facilitam tanto a retórica do “não é possível saber” quanto a retórica do “isso é obviamente abusivo”, e é por isso que a categoria precisa bloquear esse curto-circuito ao impor critérios verificáveis.

Where it comes from

It is not a matter of inventing a new code or of forcibly grafting a foreign regime onto the legal text.p. 549

PTNão se trata de inventar um novo código nem de enxertar, à força, um regime estranho sobre o texto legal.

The construction of this key of qualification remains situated within the discipline of consumption, without purporting to inaugurate an autonomous microsystem for the digital.p. 410

PTA construção dessa chave de qualificação permanece situada no interior da disciplina do consumo, sem pretender inaugurar um microssistema autônomo para o digital.

In this key, the existence of technical restrictions is not denied, but they are not allowed to function as a seal of immunity.p. 414

PTNessa chave, não se nega a existência de restrições técnicas, mas se recusa que elas funcionem como selo de imunidade.

How does the category leave the game and reach other environments?

The focus on games is methodological, because there the central place of collections, appearances and signs of belonging shows clearly how rule changes can produce losses without interruption of access. An IOC ecosystem is the arrangement that integrates continued operation, private governance and update cycles. It has four traits, which tend to operate together. They are continued enjoyment, private governance, constant modulation and structural dependence, whose intensity varies with lock-in, the centrality of collections in the account, the weight of reputation metrics and restrictions on transfer. They appear clearly in games operated as a service, in marketplaces and in artificial intelligence services.

The focus on electronic game platforms answers to a methodological reason, not a thematic preference.p. 484

PTO recorte por plataformas de jogos eletrônicos atende a razão metodológica, e não a preferência temática.

An IOC ecosystem can be described as a technical-organizational arrangement that sustains and conditions enjoyment over time, integrating continued operation, private governance and update cycles that define, in practical terms, what counts as admissible use, recognized performance, displayable collection and position preserved or degraded in the system.p. 485

PTUm ecossistema de OIC pode ser descrito como arranjo técnico-organizacional que sustenta e condiciona a fruição ao longo do tempo, integrando operação continuada, governança privada e ciclos de atualização que definem, em termos práticos, o que conta como uso admissível, desempenho reconhecido, acervo exibível e posição preservada ou degradada no sistema.

Where it comes from

These traits appear with particular clarity in three digital business models: games operated as a service, marketplaces and artificial intelligence services provided through platforms.p. 486

PTEsses traços se manifestam, com particular nitidez, em três modelos de negócios digitais: jogos operados como serviço, marketplaces e serviços de inteligência artificial fornecidos por plataforma.

Inside the game, six categories serve to identify which dimension a modulation has affected. They are cosmetic-ornamental objects, progression objects, access and permanence objects, functionality objects, time objects and reputation objects. The typology is internal to that laboratory and is not a general classification of platforms, and a concrete object may combine more than one category. Outside it, the move to general parameters is not made by extrapolation but by the functional equivalence of governance mechanisms, with explicit conceptual lenses and reconstruction through elements attributable to the supplier and publicly retrievable, such as update logs, stated reasons for change, access policies and sanction criteria. It does not follow that every continuous digital service falls within this framework. Software, accounts, libraries and tools under continuous provision tend to be the most evident cases.

Where it comes from

This typology is deliberately internal to the games laboratory and is not offered as a general classification of platforms.p. 489

PTEssa tipologia é deliberadamente interna ao laboratório de jogos e não se oferece como classificação geral de plataformas.

Generalization here does not proceed by impressionistic extrapolation but by controlled movement between levels of abstraction, guided by the functional equivalence of mechanisms of governance by architecture.p. 497

PTA generalização, aqui, não se dá por extrapolação impressionista, mas por trânsito controlado entre níveis de abstração, orientado pela equivalência funcional de mecanismos de governança por arquitetura.

The legally relevant point is not to assert that every continuous digital service is subject to the same framework, nor to expand the concept on the mere ground that economic life has become digital.p. 713

PTO ponto juridicamente relevante não está em afirmar que toda prestação digital contínua se submete ao mesmo enquadramento, nem em expandir o conceito sob o simples argumento da digitalização da vida econômica.

Thus, the removal or relevant degradation of essential functionality in editing software, the unjustified restriction of access to an account that concentrates files and libraries, or the substantial alteration of the conditions of use of contracted tools may affect the very object enjoyed, and not merely an accessory convenience of the service.p. 713

PTAssim, a remoção ou degradação relevante de funcionalidade essencial em software de edição, a restrição injustificada de acesso a conta que concentra arquivos e bibliotecas, ou a alteração substancial das condições de uso de ferramentas contratadas podem afetar o próprio objeto fruído, e não apenas uma conveniência acessória da prestação.

The typology mobilized here is neither sectoral nor merely technological.p. 713

PTA tipologia aqui mobilizada não é setorial nem meramente tecnológica.
