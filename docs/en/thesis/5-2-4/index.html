5.2.4 From the illustrative locus to the formulation of general parameters: the architectural stripping of the Lex Plataformae

See this section in the PDF

p. 497

In the Riot Games ecosystem, enjoyment manifests itself as a position governed by architecture, sustained by signs, modulated by internal access criteria and subject to unilateral recompositions capable of affecting utility, prestige and expectation. For this reason, the field makes it possible to observe, in traceable and repeatedly verifiable materials, how the central categories of the Immaterial Object of Consumption function, making them operative without relying on case-by-case impressions. It is in this sense that the empirical locus serves as a means of intelligibility by illustration, since it offers ground on which the reading keys already stabilized run through both the case and the technical and communicational environment in which it is produced.

This mobilization corresponds to an extended case approach, in which the choice of field is guided by analytical relevance rather than statistical representativeness. The context is not descriptive ornament, since it allows the passage from architectural choices to modulations of enjoyment and relevant losses to be reconstructed rigorously. Generalization here occurs not by impressionistic extrapolation, but by controlled movement between levels of abstraction, guided by the functional equivalence of mechanisms of governance by architecture.

The movement of consolidation and transition to the procedure of dogmatic reconstruction shows with particular clarity how the interactive entertainment industry, because of the continuous provision that characterizes it, operates as an ostensive surface on which the Lex Plataformae is exposed. This is because, in this sector, governance by architecture manifests itself in concentrated form and is often openly assumed in the everyday administration of what is bought, in the definition of the conditions of access to goods and in the delimitation of what actually counts as permanence of the good enjoyed.

In this sense, the logic of updates, rebalancing, reformulations of access, layers of rarity and internal sanction regimes makes it visible that the value of an IOC does not lie in static utility, but in a regime of intelligibility in which the prospects of balance and equivalence depend on the environment itself in order to exist, an environment that the supplier administers unilaterally, exercising practical sovereignty over its operating conditions, in technical as well as symbolic and, consequently, semiotic terms.

From this perspective, semiotic vulnerability ceases to be a merely descriptive hypothesis and is confirmed as a recurring structure for describing the dynamics of asymmetry, since the platform controls, besides the technical code, the lexicon through which differences are read, displayed and recognized, and thus administers the criteria of visibility and prestige p. 498 that stabilize the user's desire, as well as the mechanisms of commensurability capable of converting signs into access and access into reputation.

Likewise, abusive teleology moves away from any moralizing reading and proves capable of identifying the mechanism by which purposive deviations built into design through architectural choices reorient the service to enable regimes of extraction and to normalize consumer losses as an operating principle, even if the same changes are presented, in their justification, as contingent adjustments or as inevitable effects inherent in the digital environment.

The main result of the path taken so far is the abandonment of fatalistic readings of the regulation and governance of relationships in these environments. The most important confirmation is that, once enjoyment is recognized as a position, legal control is not exhausted by contracts, prices or nominal descriptions of goods and services, since the core of the conflict comes to lie in the preservation, degradation or reconfiguration of the conditions under which the value actually acquired is, in practice, enjoyed and used by the consumer.

Functional integrity and symbolic identity, already treated as dimensions of relevant loss in IOCs, find in the ecosystem under study repeated demonstration that recompositions preserving only the name, appearance or formal access can keep the code running without preserving what, in practice, constituted the position of enjoyment that formed part of the consumer's legitimate expectation. In other words, it has become unequivocal that the decisive question is not whether something “still exists” in a formal sense, but whether the practical equivalence of what was acquired remains recognizable and operative, which shifts the debate to criteria of functional value equivalence and to duties of restoration that are not satisfied by empty symbolic substitutes.

The repeated use of the ecosystem ceases to be merely illustrative and comes to operate as a stabilization of parameters of controllability, because what was observed was not a sectoral particularity but a recurring grammar of governance, that is, the structural description of a type of power that, when transposed by functional equivalence to other domains of platform capitalism, makes it possible to address distinct problems on the basis of common parameters of reading and control.

The transposition of these parameters, however, is not to be confused with free extrapolation or justificatory intuition. Being guided by frictions and sectoral evidence does not license subjectivism, because analytical discipline stems from making the conceptual lenses explicit beforehand and from the requirement of verifiable reconstruction through elements attributable to the supplier and publicly retrievable, including update records, declared rationales p. 499 for change, access policies and internal sanction criteria. Within this framework, methodological reflexivity does not replace objectivity. On the contrary, it reorganizes objectivity as a commitment to criteria for selecting evidence and to theoretical regulation of what counts as relevant observation, reducing confirmation bias and preventing individual experiences from being mistaken for proof.

In light of the study developed so far, when architecture replaces direct purchase with paths mediated by internal currency, collateral bundles and probability regimes, what becomes evident is not only a new sales model, but a way of organizing consumption in which design takes on a normative role, since it redistributes relevant information, administers expectations, produces frictions and turns loss into a naturalized component of access. When technical decisions and internal policies expand the extraterritoriality of code, making permanence and participation conditional on invasive requirements or on control mechanisms that exceed the perimeter of the contracted object, the claim to practical sovereignty over the consumer and over the consumer's material means becomes evident, which reinforces the need for open auditability as a minimum requirement, so that the platform does not hide behind technical opacity and trade secrecy, and so that governance by architecture is treated as attributable, contestable conduct subject to proportionality.

At this point, the choice of locus has proven productive precisely because it ensured intelligibility and allowed the conceptual operators needed for the IOC regime to emerge under methodologically controllable reading criteria. A cycle of qualification thus comes to a close, in which the typology ceased to be merely a conceptual design and became a reading instrument capable of identifying semiotic vulnerability, abusive teleology and relevant losses in their operative form, while also stabilizing the criteria by which architecture can be subjected to legal control.

With the parameters of open auditability and functional value equivalence consolidated, the next step shifts the focus to institutional protection, so that the patterns already made visible and described can be converted into liability, duties of restoration and criteria of control under the Consumer Protection Code, in harmony with the Brazilian constitutional and economic order, so that the Lex Plataformae ceases to operate as a silent sovereignty and becomes answerable to the sovereignty of Brazilian law.
