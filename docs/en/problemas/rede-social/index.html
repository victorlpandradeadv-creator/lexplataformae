Your rights · By problem

My reach dropped or I was punished on a social network. Is there anything I can ask for?

On a social network, what you see and how many people see what you post, known as reach, depend on the platform's own rules. When reach drops, a post disappears or the account is punished, the issue may go beyond the post itself and extend to the way the platform governs what appears.

Where it comes from

Meta's Terms of Service describe an environment in which the use of Facebook is subject to private rules of permanence, visibility, sharing, moderation and organization of circulating content, so that the user's experience is mediated by internal criteria of distribution and management of social interaction FACEBOOK(Facebook, [202-?]).p. 762

PTOs Termos de Serviço da Meta descrevem ambiente em que o uso do Facebook se submete a regras privadas de permanência, visibilidade, compartilhamento, moderação e organização do conteúdo circulante, de modo que a experiência do usuário é mediada por critérios internos de distribuição e gestão da interação social (Facebook, [202-?]) .

It is not enough to examine the final product acquired or the individual content removed, prioritized or hidden. It is necessary to reconstruct the architecture of the experience.p. 763

PTNão basta examinar o produto final adquirido ou o conteúdo individual removido, priorizado ou ocultado. É necessário reconstruir a arquitetura da experiência.

How do you know whether the drop in reach or the punishment went too far?

Every network adjusts what appears, and change is not a problem in itself. There may be a problem when the change alters the visibility logic, the reputation criteria or the circulation rules. It matters when it significantly downgrades what the network offered as a place of reach and recognition, with nothing to make up for the loss.

Punishment works in a similar way. Your access, your permanence, your visibility and your chance to complain may depend on punishments no one explains. When that happens, or when the way back is much harder than the way in, it is a sign that the power to punish may be misused.

Where it comes from

Mutability, in itself, is not unlawful.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita.

Functional equivalence, in turn, applies when changes to the intermediation regime, the visibility logic, the reputational criteria or the circulation rules significantly degrade the utility legitimately incorporated into the relationship, without compatible restoration. This can occur both in e-commerce and in the social circulation of content, whenever the platform substantially changes the functional role of the infrastructure it offered as an environment of exchange, reach or recognition.p. 764

PTA equivalência funcional, por sua vez, incide quando alterações do regime de intermediação, da lógica de visibilidade, dos critérios reputacionais ou das regras de circulação degradam de modo relevante a utilidade legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo, sem recomposição compatível. Isso pode ocorrer tanto no comércio eletrônico quanto na circulação social de conteúdo, sempre que a plataforma altere substancialmente o papel funcional da infraestrutura que oferecia como ambiente de troca, alcance ou reconhecimento.

Whenever access, permanence, visibility or contestation are conditioned by opaque sanctions, selective frictions, excessive costs of reaction or asymmetric reversal mechanisms, there will be an indication of abusive governance of the bond.p. 746

PTSempre que acesso, permanência, visibilidade ou contestação forem condicionados por sanções opacas, fricções seletivas, custos de reação excessivos ou mecanismos assimétricos de reversão, haverá indício de governança abusiva do vínculo.

Which records help show the drop or the punishment?

Dated screenshots help, showing the reach numbers before and after, the punishment notice, the rule the network cited and your messages with customer service. It is also worth keeping the affected content, the way to contest that existed and what the measure did to your use of the network.

What weighs most, however, is the way the feed, the recommendations and the reputation system shape what circulates, and that data stays with the network. That is why it is not reasonable to expect you to give detailed proof of something that was never within your reach.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

The minimum required proof falls on sanction records, contestation flow, criteria deployed, structural difficulties of reversal and concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience.p. 746

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre registros de sanção, fluxo de contestação, critérios mobilizados, dificuldades estruturais de reversão e impactos concretos da medida sobre a continuidade da experiência.

In social networks, this reconstruction may also involve examining the way the feed, recommendation, reach and reputational management condition the very perception of what is in circulation.p. 763

PTEm redes sociais, essa reconstrução pode envolver ainda o exame da forma como o feed, a recomendação, o alcance e a gestão reputacional condicionam a própria percepção do que está em circulação.

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to demand from the consumer a detailed showing of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

What can you ask the network for after the punishment or the drop?

If the punishment has no reason that can be checked and no reasonable way to contest it, you may be entitled to have it reviewed or reversed. You can also ask the network to create or improve a real way for you to defend yourself.

You can also ask for minimum clarity about the moderation criteria, meaning the review of what may stay online, and about the criteria for distributing what appears. This does not oblige the network to open its internal systems, but it must be enough for you to understand how the decision affected you and to contest it.

Where it comes from

The institutional consequence includes reversal or review of sanctions, procedural duties, creation or improvement of contestation mechanisms and, in structurally repetitive cases, correction of the ecosystem's enforcement regime.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional inclui reversão ou revisão de sanções, deveres procedimentais, criação ou aperfeiçoamento de mecanismos de contestação e, em casos estruturalmente repetitivos, correção do regime de enforcement do ecossistema.

Open auditability requires minimum institutional verifiability of the relevant criteria of presentation, prioritization, moderation, reputation and circulation, without this implying unrestricted opening of internal systems. What is demanded is sufficient intelligibility to understand how the environment organizes the user's experience and how certain choices of the platform interfere in the formation of expectation, in the comparability between alternatives and in the possibility of contestation.p. 764

PTA auditabilidade aberta exige verificabilidade institucional mínima sobre os critérios relevantes de apresentação, priorização, moderação, reputação e circulação, sem que isso implique abertura irrestrita de sistemas internos. O que se demanda é inteligibilidade suficiente para compreender como o ambiente organiza a experiência do usuário e como determinadas escolhas da plataforma interferem na formação da expectativa, na comparabilidade entre alternativas e na possibilidade de contestação.

Who can you turn to when the network does not explain the punishment?

The first route is usually the network's own channel for contesting decisions, in writing and with the screens saved. A kind reply that mentions a technical adjustment or a security measure is not an explanation. And feeling heard is not the same as having a real way to contest.

When the same punishment policy hits many accounts without explanation, the response can be collective and reach the whole punishment system. Procon, Consumidor.gov.br, the Public Prosecutor's Office and the Public Defender's Office appear on the channels page.

Where it comes from

The loss no longer appears as an exceptional event and comes to be metabolized as part of the normal functioning of the system. The same happens with friction. Instead of promptly triggering a reading of inadequacy or abusiveness, it may be reconfigured by the platform as a technical adjustment, a temporary limitation, an understandable inconvenience, a security measure, a necessary update or a simple stage of use.p. 781

PTA perda deixa de aparecer como evento excepcional e passa a ser metabolizada como parte do normal funcionamento do sistema. O mesmo ocorre com o atrito. Em vez de desencadear prontamente uma leitura de inadequação ou abusividade, ele pode ser reconfigurado pela plataforma como ajuste técnico, limitação temporária, inconveniente compreensível, medida de segurança, atualização necessária ou simples etapa do uso.

The platform may, for example, offer a sense of transparency without delivering real intelligibility, produce an impression of listening without opening effective paths of contestation, mobilize a human tone without assuming proportional duties of reasoning or justification and distribute signs of care that work less as adequate information and more as semiotic cushioning of criticism.p. 782

PTA plataforma pode, por exemplo, oferecer sensação de transparência sem entregar inteligibilidade real, produzir impressão de escuta sem abrir vias efetivas de contestação, mobilizar tom humano sem assumir deveres proporcionais de motivação ou justificação e distribuir signos de cuidado que funcionam menos como informação adequada e mais como amortecimento semiótico da crítica.

An opaque sanctions policy, a non-auditable regime of functional change, an interface logic that dilutes the essential under informational excess, a materially decisive ranking system without usable history or a trust architecture that absorbs and neutralizes contestation before it becomes litigation are all examples of problems whose unity lies in the design, not in the episode.p. 775

PTUma política opaca de sanções, um regime não auditável de alteração funcional, uma lógica de interface que dilui o essencial sob excesso informacional, um sistema de ranqueamento materialmente decisivo sem histórico aproveitável ou uma arquitetura de confiança que absorve e neutraliza contestação antes que ela se torne litígio são todos exemplos de problemas cuja unidade está no desenho, não no episódio.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When can the network punish you or change your reach without that giving you a claim?

The network has the power to punish and to organize what appears, but that power alone is not enough. It needs a reason that can be checked, consistency from one decision to the next and an understandable way to contest it.

A small or passing drop is not enough, because the loss needs to be significant and lasting, not a one-day nuisance. Nor does every relationship conducted through technology fall under consumer protection.

Where it comes from

In platform ecosystems, sanctions do not operate merely as a one-off disciplinary response to conduct attributed to the user. They can affect access, permanence, visibility, reputation, relational insertion and continuity of enjoyment. For this reason, they must be controlled by denser criteria than the mere formal existence of sanctioning power. They require verifiable reasoning, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTEm ecossistemas de plataforma, sanções não operam apenas como resposta disciplinar pontual a uma conduta atribuída ao usuário. Elas podem afetar acesso, permanência, visibilidade, reputação, inserção relacional e continuidade da fruição. Por isso, devem ser controladas por critérios mais densos do que a simples existência formal de poder sancionatório. Exigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

There is a typical possibility of significant degradation of the bundle without a total loss of access, in a persistent and substantive way, rather than as a one-off discomfort.p. 433

PTHá possibilidade típica de degradação relevante do feixe sem ruptura total do acesso, de modo persistente e substantivo, e não como desconforto pontual.

Not every relationship economically mediated by technology will justify the application of the CDC.p. 780

PTNem toda relação economicamente mediada por tecnologia justificará incidência do CDC.

The rights behind this case

  • Punishment needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself
  • A relevant change has to leave a trail
  • If it changes, it must keep the value it had
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
