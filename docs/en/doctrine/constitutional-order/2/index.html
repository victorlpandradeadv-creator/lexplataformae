The constitutional order and the sectoral framework

Platform governance is an exercise of private economic power over positions of continued enjoyment, and the jurist needs to know on what basis the legal order limits it without treating the company as the State or reducing the conflict to the contract. That basis comes from the economic Constitution, the horizontal effect of fundamental rights and the CDC, while the Legal Framework for Electronic Games and the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents help bring the sector into view without resolving the conflict of enjoyment on their own.

What does Vanguard show when governance reaches the consumer's computer?

The case that puts the construction to the test is Vanguard, Riot Games' anti-cheat program. According to the company's official communications, it consists of a client and a kernel-mode driver, that is, a low-level component installed in the deepest and most privileged layer of the operating system, and it starts at boot, when the computer starts up. In League of Legends it must remain active whenever the client is open, and disabling it prevents play. The Terms of Service allow monitoring use on the user's computer and running the software in the background, and an official update at the end of 2025 extended the concern to the initial stages of hardware startup, with VALORANT possibly blocked until the user updates the firmware, the basic software of the motherboard, or enables required security layers. The protection of the object becomes preventive and continuing governance, which reaches the machine before the decision to enjoy.

What is at stake is the redefinition of the material boundaries of the user's sovereignty over their own equipment.p. 521

PTO que está em jogo é a redefinição das fronteiras materiais de soberania do usuário sobre seu próprio equipamento.

Where it comes from

The case of the Vanguard anti-cheat exposes, in a particularly clear way, a qualitative mutation in the private governance of the immaterial object of consumption. It is no longer only a matter of conditioning the enjoyment of the game on internal rules of conduct, progression or permanence in the ecosystem, but of projecting the requirements of competitive integrity onto the consumer's own material infrastructure.p. 520-521

PTO caso do anti-cheat Vanguard expõe, de forma particularmente nítida, uma mutação qualitativa na governança privada do objeto imaterial de consumo. Já não se trata apenas de condicionar a fruição do jogo a regras internas de conduta, progressão ou permanência no ecossistema, mas de projetar as exigências de integridade competitiva sobre a própria infraestrutura material do consumidor.

In the official support communications devoted to League of Legends and VALORANT, Riot itself describes Vanguard as security software composed of a client active while the game is running and a kernel-mode driver, that is, installed in the deepest and most privileged layer of the operating system, with access far greater than that of ordinary applications Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024r; Riot Games, 2024t).p. 521

PTNas comunicações oficiais de suporte dedicadas a League of Legends e a VALORANT, a própria Riot descreve o Vanguard como software de segurança composto por um cliente ativo durante a execução do jogo e por um driver em modo kernel, isto é, instalado na camada mais profunda e privilegiada do sistema operacional, com acesso muito superior ao de aplicações comuns (Riot Games, 2024r; Riot Games, 2024t) .

In the case of League of Legends, Riot makes explicit that Vanguard must remain active not only during the match, but also whenever the client is open, and that its deactivation, uninstallation or unauthorized interaction prevents use of the game; in the case of VALORANT, it further states that deactivating the system requires restarting the machine to restore the possibility of playing Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024r; Riot Games, 2024t).p. 521

PTNo caso de League of Legends, a Riot explicita que o Vanguard deve permanecer ativo não só durante a partida, mas também sempre que o cliente estiver aberto, e que sua desativação, desinstalação ou interação não autorizada impede o uso do jogo; no caso de VALORANT, informa ainda que a desativação do sistema exige reinicialização da máquina para restauração da possibilidade de jogar (Riot Games, 2024r; Riot Games, 2024t) .

In an official update published at the end of 2025, Riot makes even more explicit the logic of preventive governance that sustains Vanguard. The text states that the company identified a critical flaw in several modern motherboards, capable of allowing the invisible injection of code in the very initial stages of the machine's startup, even before the operating system has fully loaded. On the basis of this finding, Riot justifies the hardening of its integrity requirements by arguing that components loaded earlier in the boot process operate with a higher degree of privilege and, precisely for that reason, can compromise the entire environment before the operating system or the anti-cheat itself can react. The document also clarifies that, given this breach, Vanguard's restriction service may prevent VALORANT from running until the user updates the motherboard firmware or enables certain security layers deemed necessary by the platform.p. 522

PTEm atualização oficial publicada no fim de 2025, a Riot explicita com ainda mais aderência a lógica de governança preventiva que sustenta o Vanguard. O texto informa que a empresa identificou uma falha crítica em diversas placas-mãe modernas, capaz de permitir a injeção invisível de código ainda nas etapas iniciais de inicialização da máquina, antes mesmo do carregamento completo do sistema operacional. A partir dessa constatação, a Riot justifica o endurecimento de suas exigências de integridade ao sustentar que componentes carregados mais cedo no processo de boot operam com grau superior de privilégio e, justamente por isso, podem comprometer todo o ambiente antes que o sistema operacional ou o próprio anti-cheat consigam reagir. O documento também esclarece que, diante dessa brecha, o serviço de restrição do Vanguard poderá impedir a execução de VALORANT até que o usuário atualize o firmware da placa-mãe ou habilite determinadas camadas de segurança reputadas necessárias pela plataforma.

Riot contractually admits the possibility of actively monitoring the use of its services not only on its own servers, but also on the consumer's computer or device, and provides that the anti-cheat software may keep running in the background on the device. As a result, the protection of the IOC does not remain confined to the concrete moment of the match. It becomes a vector of preventive and continuing governance over the technical environment in which the game will run, reaching the machine even before the specific decision to enjoy it.p. 521-522

PTA Riot admite contratualmente a possibilidade de monitorar ativamente o uso de seus serviços não apenas em seus próprios servidores, mas também no computador ou dispositivo do consumidor, e prevê que o software antitrapaça pode permanecer em execução em segundo plano no dispositivo. Com isso, a proteção do OIC não permanece confinada ao momento concreto da partida. Ela se converte em vetor de governança preventiva e continuada sobre o ambiente técnico no qual o jogo será executado, alcançando a máquina antes mesmo da decisão específica de fruição.

The technical documents show the practical reach. Riot admits that Vanguard may block third-party applications and require changes to BIOS or UEFI, the basic interfaces that prepare the machine to start the operating system. It may also require TPM 2.0, an integrity verification module, Secure Boot, which prevents untrusted components from starting, IOMMU, which isolates device access to memory, and memory integrity, and Riot recognizes that incorrect changes to these settings may cause serious problems on the computer. Vanguard may also prevent play when it detects low-level drivers, such as those of outdated monitoring tools and lighting control software. The platform makes the enjoyment of the immaterial object conditional on the machine's prior conformity with private standards of integrity and compatibility that it defines unilaterally.

The user's computer is, functionally, administered as an extension of the game's competitive environment.p. 527

PTO computador do usuário passa, funcionalmente, a ser administrado como extensão do ambiente concorrencial do jogo.

Where it comes from

Riot itself, in documents aimed at VALORANT developers and users, admits that Vanguard may block third-party applications, require sensitive changes to BIOS or UEFI, and require TPM 2.0, Secure Boot, IOMMU, memory integrity and other security layers to be enabled, and it recognizes that incorrect changes to these settings may cause serious problems on the computer.p. 526-527

PTA própria Riot, em documentos voltados a desenvolvedores e a usuários de VALORANT, admite que o Vanguard pode bloquear aplicações de terceiros, exigir alterações sensíveis em BIOS ou UEFI, impor habilitação de TPM 2.0, Secure Boot, IOMMU, integridade de memória e outras camadas de segurança, além de reconhecer que mudanças incorretas nessas configurações podem causar problemas sérios no computador.

These are the acronyms for Basic Input/Output System and Unified Extensible Firmware Interface. They designate the basic software interfaces that reside in the hardware and are responsible for preparing the machine to start the operating system.p. 527

PTSão as siglas para Basic Input/Output System e Unified Extensible Firmware Interface. Elas designam as interfaces de software básicas que residem no hardware e são responsáveis por preparar a máquina para a inicialização do sistema operacional.

On its system restrictions page, Riot expressly admits that it may limit access to the games when it identifies machines considered excessively prone to cheating, including because of outdated versions of Windows or specific equipment configurations, requiring the user to make sensitive changes to BIOS or UEFI, that is, to the machine's basic startup systems, such as enabling TPM 2.0 (a security module used for integrity verification and protected key storage), Secure Boot (a mechanism that prevents untrusted components from starting), IOMMU (a technology that isolates device access to memory) and memory integrity mechanisms, as well as updating the BIOS and the operating system itself Riot Games(Riot Games, 2025q).p. 527

PTJá na página de restrições do sistema, a Riot admite expressamente que poderá limitar o acesso aos jogos quando identificar máquinas consideradas excessivamente aptas à fraude, inclusive por causa de versões desatualizadas do Windows ou de configurações específicas do equipamento, exigindo do usuário alterações sensíveis em BIOS ou UEFI, isto é, nos sistemas básicos de inicialização da máquina, com habilitação de recursos como TPM 2.0 (módulo de segurança usado para verificação de integridade e armazenamento protegido de chaves), Secure Boot (mecanismo que impede a inicialização de componentes não confiáveis), IOMMU (tecnologia de isolamento do acesso de dispositivos à memória) e mecanismos de integridade de memória, além de atualizações de BIOS e do próprio sistema operacional (Riot Games, 2025q) .

Even more concretely, the guidance on incompatible software clarifies that Vanguard may prevent VALORANT from running when it identifies “.sys” files, that is, low-level drivers responsible for communication between the operating system and functions or components of the computer, instructing the user to remove the related software or even to manually delete the blocked file; among the examples mentioned by Riot itself are old drivers, outdated monitoring tools and RGB lighting control programsp. 527

PTDe modo ainda mais concreto, a orientação sobre softwares incompatíveis esclarece que o Vanguard pode impedir a execução de VALORANT ao identificar arquivos “.sys”, isto é, drivers de baixo nível responsáveis pela comunicação entre o sistema operacional e funções ou componentes do computador, orientando o usuário a remover o software relacionado ou até a apagar manualmente o arquivo bloqueado; entre os exemplos mencionados pela própria Riot estão drivers antigos, ferramentas de monitoramento desatualizadas e programas de controle de iluminação RGB

The platform makes the enjoyment of the immaterial object conditional on the prior conformity of the machine's technical ecosystem with private standards of integrity and compatibility that it defines unilaterally.p. 527

PTA plataforma condiciona a fruição do objeto imaterial à conformidade prévia do ecossistema técnico da máquina com padrões privados de integridade e compatibilidade definidos unilateralmente por ela.

Clause 10.2 of the Terms completes the design, because it allows Riot to alter, update, nerf, which means adjusting a feature to reduce its power or usefulness, or restrict features and Virtual Products, to require the installation of software at any time and to place on the user, and not on Riot, the cost of buying any additional software or hardware that new specifications require. The Terms also seek to limit the company's liability. Here the case stops being only a discussion about fighting cheating and becomes a classic problem of a supplier using the contract to shield itself from the effects of an invasive technical architecture that it imposes itself.

In parallel, the contractual wording seeks to reduce the supplier's patrimonial exposure by stating that the services are offered as is, without broad warranties, assigning to the user responsibility for use and limiting Riot's liability for losses and damages, including in relation to failures linked to not installing updates or to maintaining minimum system requirements.p. 526

PTParalelamente, a redação contratual busca reduzir a exposição patrimonial da fornecedora ao afirmar que os serviços são oferecidos no estado em que se encontram, sem garantias amplas, atribuindo ao usuário a responsabilidade pelo uso e limitando a responsabilidade da Riot por perdas e danos, inclusive em relação a falhas ligadas à não instalação de atualizações ou à manutenção de requisitos mínimos de sistema.

The general contractual framework thus seeks to provide in advance for subjecting the consumer's equipment to additional layers of surveillance, compulsory adaptation and risk shifting. Esse é o ponto em que o caso deixa de ser apenas discussão sobre combate a fraudes e se transforma em problema clássico de blindagem contratual do fornecedor diante de uma arquitetura técnica invasiva que ele próprio impõe.

Why does the security argument not settle the question?

In the vocabulary of security, the expansion appears as a technical inevitability, but this discourse does not by itself neutralize the legal relevance of the architecture. Canavez, Santos and Mendes show that the security argument can legitimize the expansion of private powers when the demand for data is normalized through routine acceptances, Mariana Monteiro da Costa argues that monitoring technologies embody human choices and restructure power relations, and Danilo Doneda shows that, when organized data function as operational substitutes for the person, surveillance becomes a potential means of affecting that person's autonomy. The problem becomes one of the proportionality, necessity, transparency and justifiability of the technical interference, and the constitutional filter still stands, because, as Sarlet holds, fundamental rights radiate across the entire legal order, and the platform acts on the consumer's equipment, privacy and real ability to enjoy the object.

Where it comes from

Under the vocabulary of security, this expansion usually appears as if it were simple technical inevitability. But the discourse of security does not, by itself, neutralize the legal relevance of the architecture adopted.p. 522

PTSob o vocabulário da segurança, essa expansão costuma aparecer como se fosse simples inevitabilidade técnica. Mas o discurso da segurança não neutraliza, por si só, a relevância jurídica da arquitetura adotada.

In the digital environment, the storage, sharing and persistent monitoring of information pose a threat to privacy serious enough to require specific normative containment, especially in a surveillance society in which data processing becomes a central asset of business models and of behavioral control Mendes(Canavez; Santos; Mendes, 2022, p. 69; p. 73; p. 82). The security argument itself can serve to legitimize the expansion of private powers over fundamental spheres of informational life, especially when the demand for data and structural surveillance are normalized through routine and barely intelligible acceptancesp. 522-523

PTEm ambiente digital, armazenamento, compartilhamento e monitoramento persistente de informações representam ameaça suficientemente séria à privacidade para exigir contenção normativa específica, sobretudo em uma sociedade de vigilância na qual o tratamento de dados se converte em ativo central de modelos de negócio e de controle comportamental (Canavez; Santos; Mendes, 2022, p. 69; p. 73; p. 82) . O próprio argumento securitário pode funcionar como legitimador da expansão de poderes privados sobre esferas fundamentais da vida informacional, especialmente quando a exigência de dados e a vigilância estrutural são normalizadas por aceites rotineiros e pouco inteligíveis

It is not enough, therefore, to invoke fighting cheating as a magic word. The problem becomes one of proportionality, necessity, transparency and justifiability of the degree of technical interference demanded of the consumer.p. 523

PTNão basta, portanto, invocar combate a trapaças como palavra mágica. O problema passa a ser de proporcionalidade, necessidade, transparência e justificabilidade do grau de ingerência técnica exigido do consumidor.

Monitoring technologies and algorithmic governance embody human choices and restructure power relations over the user, allowing veiled control and the favoring of their own interests by those who dominate the digital infrastructurep. 523

PTTecnologias de monitoramento e governança algorítmica incorporam escolhas humanas e reestruturam relações de poder sobre o usuário, permitindo controle velado e favorecimento de interesses próprios por quem domina a infraestrutura digital

When organized data function as operational substitutes for the person, surveillance ceases to be a simple administrative instrument and becomes a potential mechanism for affecting individuality, autonomy and the free development of personalityp. 524-525

PTQuando dados organizados funcionam como substitutos operacionais da pessoa, a vigilância deixa de ser simples instrumento administrativo e se converte em mecanismo potencial de afetação da individualidade, da autonomia e do livre desenvolvimento da personalidade

Even if the agent remains private, the intensification of control over hardware, informational flows and practical conditions of use remains subject to the protective filter that radiates from the Constitution over the entire legal order Sarlet(Sarlet, 2018, p. 205; p. 531). The constitutional order remains in the room precisely because the platform does not act only on an abstract thing, but on the consumer's practical sphere, their equipment, their privacy and their real possibility of enjoying or not enjoying the object without subjecting the rest of the machine to mutable private standards of integrity.p. 525-526

PTAinda que o agente permaneça privado, a intensificação do controle sobre hardware, fluxos informacionais e condições práticas de uso continua sujeita ao filtro protetivo que irradia da Constituição sobre toda a ordem jurídica (Sarlet, 2018, p. 205; p. 531) . A ordem constitucional permanece no recinto justamente porque a plataforma não atua apenas sobre coisa abstrata, mas sobre a esfera prática do consumidor, seu equipamento, sua privacidade e sua possibilidade real de fruir ou não o objeto sem submeter o restante da máquina a padrões privados de integridade mutáveis.

The parameters of containment come from the CDC and the LGPD. Articles 8, 10 and 12 of the CDC1 limit risks to those that are normal and foreseeable, bar products that the supplier knows or should know to have a high degree of harmfulness or dangerousness and, in extreme cases, reopen the door to treating invasive software as a defective or inadequately informed product. Article 6, items I, III and VI, of Law 13,709/20182, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD), requires a specific and informed purpose, necessity and transparency, subject to commercial and industrial secrets, and Articles 7, 8 and 46 of Law 13,709/20183 call for an adequate legal basis, consent that is not automatically satisfied by ritualized acceptances and security measures capable of protecting the data. The platform cannot invoke security as an all-encompassing justification while keeping the scope, the necessity and the agents of the processing opaque.

Alleged security does not replace legally demonstrated security.p. 533

PTSegurança alegada não substitui segurança juridicamente demonstrada.

Where it comes from

Products and services placed on the market cannot entail risks to health or safety beyond those that are normal and foreseeable given their nature and enjoyment, and the supplier cannot place into circulation a product or service that it knows or should know to present a high degree of harmfulness or dangerousness. In extreme cases, the very liability for design defects or for insufficient or inadequate information about use and risks reopens the door to treating invasive software as a defective or inadequately informed product.p. 532-533

PTProdutos e serviços colocados no mercado não podem acarretar riscos à saúde ou segurança além dos normais e previsíveis de sua natureza e fruição, e o fornecedor não pode colocar em circulação produto ou serviço que saiba ou devesse saber apresentar alto grau de nocividade ou periculosidade. Em hipóteses-limite, a própria responsabilização por defeitos de projeto ou por informações insuficientes ou inadequadas sobre utilização e riscos reabre a porta para tratar software invasivo como produto defeituoso ou inadequadamente informado.

The data processing linked to Vanguard cannot be justified en bloc under the generic heading of “security”, because it must observe a legitimate, specific, explicit and informed purpose, necessity, with limitation to the indispensable minimum, and transparency, with clear, precise and accessible information. It also requires an adequate legal basis, is not automatically satisfied by ritualized and generic acceptances, and presupposes technical and administrative measures capable of effectively protecting the data. In other words, the platform cannot invoke security as an all-encompassing justification while maintaining opacity about the scope, necessity and agents of the processing.p. 533

PTO tratamento de dados vinculado ao Vanguard não pode ser justificado em bloco pela rubrica genérica de “segurança”, porque precisa observar finalidade legítima, específica, explícita e informada, necessidade, com limitação ao mínimo indispensável, e transparência, com informações claras, precisas e acessíveis. Também exige base legal adequada, não se satisfaz automaticamente com aceites ritualizados e genéricos, e pressupõe medidas técnicas e administrativas aptas à proteção efetiva dos dados. Em outras palavras, a plataforma não pode invocar segurança como justificativa totalizante enquanto mantém opacidade sobre alcance, necessidade e agentes do tratamento.

“Art. 6 [...] VI - transparency: guarantee, to data subjects, of clear, precise and easily accessible information about the carrying out of the processing and the respective processing agents, subject to commercial and industrial secrets;” Brasil(Brasil, [2026]a).p. 533

PT“Art. 6º [...] VI - transparência: garantia, aos titulares, de informações claras, precisas e facilmente acessíveis sobre a realização do tratamento e os respectivos agentes de tratamento, observados os segredos comercial e industrial;” (Brasil, [2026]a) .

How much weight does the evidence in the case carry, and what weighs on the other side?

Each source carries its own weight. The central facts come from the supplier's own institutional voice, in its terms and technical documents, and the 2020 press coverage by Wilde, Walker and Hill shows that criticism of the always-on design did not arise as fringe paranoia, although it does not by itself prove any expert conclusion. Reports on the Microsoft Q&A technical forum link blue screens and crashes to Vanguard's technical ecosystem, but in one of them the technical answer does not attribute conclusive causality and mentions competing hypotheses, and the Reddit reports, about conflicts with hardware programs, study routines and debugging tools, have only illustrative value. Bricking, which means rendering the equipment functionally useless, appears in a report that does not prove causality and serves as the extreme form of the problem, not necessarily the most frequent one. The argument does not depend on universal harm, and it is enough to show that the required architecture transfers a legally relevant systemic risk to the consumer.

Where it comes from

Where do loot boxes appear, and what does the Digital Statute prohibit?

Loot boxes, mechanisms for obtaining random items upon payment, appear by name in the preliminary reading of the sector and in sectoral regulation. In them, utility and value depend on internal regimes of distribution, availability and rarity defined by the platform, linked to scarcity and time windows that make decisions dependent on anticipation, urgency and expectation. The institutional attention paid to them does not stem from moralizing the sector but from the fact that the supplier concentrates the criteria on availability, rotation, rarity and, where applicable, probability, with low external reconstructibility. Under the IOC regime, these mechanisms condense instituted rarity, anticipation, criterial opacity and unilateral modulation, with effects that may appear as erosion of utility and reconfiguration of value even when access is formally available. The protection of children and adolescents comes in because, in games operated as a service, consumption decisions tend to fragment over time, mediated by cycles of return, urgency, promises of progression and social comparison, and the focus on minors makes visible asymmetries of understanding and contestation.

Where it comes from

Where it comes from

If the platform is not a State, why does the Constitution apply?

The first objection is that the platform is not a State, and the answer, with Sarlet, is that the reinforced application of fundamental rights does not depend on turning the private party into a quasi-State, since a social and economic power capable of producing relevant subjection is enough. The second concerns acceptance, and Sarmento recalls that the absence of overt coercion does not in itself amount to real freedom to decide, resist or negotiate. The third invokes free enterprise and innovation, and the answer lies in the constitutional matrix, in which free enterprise is neither a metaphysical right of capital nor a screen for business sovereignty. The fourth is that the reasoning moralizes the market, and the answer, again with Warren and Brandeis, is that reconstructing categories in the face of vulnerability produced at scale is not moralism but a necessary response to keep new forms of power under control.

Free enterprise, innovation and technological ownership do not operate as safe-conducts for unauditability, shielded irresponsibility or contractual immunization of the supplier.p. 512

PTLivre iniciativa, inovação e titularidade tecnológica não operam como salvo-condutos para inauditabilidade, irresponsabilidade blindada ou imunização contratual do fornecedor.

Where it comes from

Private autonomy, in a constitutional key, is not an abstract safe-conduct for the user's material subjection, especially when inequality of power turns formal freedom into an elegant fiction Sarmento(Sarmento, 2010, p. 183; p. 286). For this reason too, the reinforced application of fundamental rights in the private sphere does not depend on any rite of transforming the private party into a “quasi-State”; it is enough to recognize that the threat to liberty, material equality and dignity may come from holders of social and economic power intense enough to produce relevant subjection Sarlet(Sarlet, 2018, p. 527; p. 529; p. 530).p. 515-516

PTA autonomia privada, em chave constitucional, não é salvo-conduto abstrato para sujeição material do usuário, sobretudo quando a desigualdade de poder converte liberdade formal em ficção elegante (Sarmento, 2010, p. 183; p. 286) . Também por isso, a incidência reforçada dos direitos fundamentais na esfera privada não depende de qualquer rito de transformação do particular em “quase Estado”; basta reconhecer que a ameaça à liberdade, à igualdade material e à dignidade pode provir de detentores de poder social e econômico suficientemente intensos para produzir sujeição relevante (Sarlet, 2018, p. 527; p. 529; p. 530) .

When the relationship between private parties is organized on non-equal terms, the purely formal invocation of contractual freedom is no longer enough to legitimize the bond, because the absence of overt coercion does not equal, by itself, the existence of real freedom to decide, resist or negotiate under minimally equivalent conditions Sarmento(Sarmento, 2010, p. 183).p. 515

PTQuando a relação entre particulares se organiza em termos não paritários, a invocação puramente formal da liberdade contratual deixa de bastar para legitimar o vínculo, porque a ausência de coação ostensiva não equivale, por si só, à existência de liberdade real para decidir, resistir ou negociar em condições minimamente equivalentes (Sarmento, 2010, p. 183) .

Free enterprise, in this picture, is neither a metaphysical right of capital nor a screen for business sovereignty, but a category functionally conditioned by constitutional purposes and by the protection of the person Grau(Grau, 2010, p. 204; p. 208-209).p. 505-506

PTA livre iniciativa, nesse quadro, não é direito metafísico do capital nem biombo para soberania empresarial, mas categoria funcionalmente condicionada por finalidades constitucionais e pela tutela da pessoa (Grau, 2010, p. 204; p. 208-209) .

When the technical environment reorganizes vulnerability at scale, legal reconstruction is not moralism, but a necessary response to keep the effects of new forms of power and intrusion controllable in institutional terms.p. 348

PTQuando o ambiente técnico reorganiza a vulnerabilidade em escala, a reconstrução jurídica não é moralismo, mas resposta necessária para manter controláveis, em termos institucionais, os efeitos de novas formas de poder e de intrusão.

How far does the containment of platform power go?

The platform need not be treated as a State for its powers to be subject to legal constraint, and containment does not require dissolving the private nature of the relationship or constitutionalizing every private dispute, because it is aimed at certain powers exercised through architecture, information and enforcement at scale. Good faith does not stop the freedom to shape the bond from existing, only from becoming private sovereignty. In the Vanguard case, the kernel anti-cheat is not automatically unlawful, the legitimacy of fighting cheating still stands, and what is refused is only the legal immunization of deep, persistent and opaque technical governance over someone else's machine. This is the foundation on which platform governance becomes legally controllable, and the outcome of each case depends on the criteria of the chapters on open auditability, functional value equivalence and the unlawful act of architecture.

Where it comes from

The platform need not be treated as a State for its powers to be subject to legal constraint.p. 513

PTA plataforma não precisa ser tratada como Estado para que seus poderes sejam juridicamente conformáveis.

At the same time, this containment does not require dissolving the private nature of the relationship nor transplanting the legal regime of the State in full to the private party.p. 513

PTAo mesmo tempo, essa contenção não exige dissolver a natureza privada da relação nem transplantar integralmente para o particular o regime jurídico do Estado.

Technical change structurally alters the conditions of exposure, harm and vulnerability, and requires categories to be adjusted so that protection stays true to the real way in which the conflict is produced. This does not imply equating platform with State nor constitutionalizing every private dispute, but recognizing that certain private powers, when exercised through architecture, information and enforcement at scale, generate asymmetries and risks that are not exhausted by the letter of the contract.p. 348

PTA mudança técnica altera de modo estrutural as condições de exposição, dano e vulnerabilidade, e exige ajuste de categorias para que a tutela permaneça aderente ao modo real de produção do conflito. Isso não implica equiparar plataforma a Estado nem constitucionalizar toda disputa privada, mas reconhecer que certos poderes privados, quando exercidos por arquitetura, informação e efetivação em escala, geram assimetrias e riscos que não se esgotam na literalidade contratual.

In a platform environment, this prevents the freedom to shape the bond from being turned into private sovereignty over the consumer's position.p. 519

PTEm ambiente de plataforma, isso impede que a liberdade de modelar o vínculo seja convertida em soberania privada sobre a posição do consumidor.

The point here is not to automatically assert the total unlawfulness of the kernel anti-cheat, but to refuse its legal immunization.p. 533

PTO ponto aqui não é afirmar automaticamente a ilicitude total do anti-cheat em kernel, mas recusar sua imunização jurídica.

It is not a matter of denying the legitimacy of fighting cheating, but of denying that this legitimacy authorizes, by itself, deep, preventive, persistent and opaque technical governance over someone else's machine.p. 534

PTNão se trata de negar a legitimidade do combate a fraudes, mas de negar que essa legitimidade autorize, por si só, governança técnica profunda, preventiva, persistente e opaca sobre a máquina alheia.

The constitutionally legitimate market is one shaped by dignity, social function, the repression of abuse and consumer protection, and not one that tries to turn private architecture into an unchecked jurisdiction. From this matrix follows the dogmatic possibility of reconstructing, later on, platform governance as a legally controllable phenomenon and of reading the IOC not as an awkward exception to the system, but as one of its hardest contemporary tests.p. 512

PTO mercado constitucionalmente legítimo é aquele atravessado por dignidade, função social, repressão ao abuso e defesa do consumidor, e não aquele que tenta converter arquitetura privada em jurisdição sem controle. É dessa matriz que decorre a possibilidade dogmática de reconstruir, adiante, a governança de plataforma como fenômeno juridicamente controlável e de ler o OIC não como exceção incômoda ao sistema, mas como uma de suas provas contemporâneas mais duras.
