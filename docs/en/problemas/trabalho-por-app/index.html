Your rights · By problem

I work through an app, and the rules changed. Can I ask for anything?

You do deliveries or rides through an app and, after a rule change nobody explained, you started getting fewer requests, your earnings fell or a restriction was imposed. What matters is not only whether your account is still active, but whether your place in the app still matches what supported the earnings and the continuity you expected.

Where it comes from

What is provided, in functional terms, is a private infrastructure of economic possibility.p. 765

PTO que se fornece, em termos funcionais, é uma infraestrutura privada de possibilidade econômica.

The legal test cannot be only whether the account remains active. It needs to be whether the partner's functional position in the environment remains substantially equivalent to the one that structured their legitimate expectation of earnings and continuity.p. 767-768

PTO teste jurídico não pode ser apenas se a conta continua ativa. Precisa ser se a posição funcional do parceiro no ambiente permanece substancialmente equivalente àquela que estruturava sua expectativa legítima de ganho e permanência.

How can you tell whether the drop is linked to the app's rules?

The app is what hands out job requests, calculates earnings, assigns ratings, sets priority and imposes restrictions. That is why your income depends on how it organizes this environment, not only on your effort.

Changes to how job requests are distributed, to pricing, to the rules for staying eligible, to incentives or to the weight of ratings may leave your account and the partnership label in place. Even so, they can substantially drain the earnings that made staying worthwhile.

Saying that you are free to switch the app on and off does not settle the question. The freedom to join and leave goes hand in hand with heavy dependence on the rules for access, earnings calculation and reputation that the app controls.

Where it comes from

The platform organizes access to demand, distribution of calls, minimum predictability of pay, permanence in the productive flow, reputational criteria, prioritization patterns, acceptance rules and sanction mechanisms.p. 765

PTA plataforma organiza acesso à demanda, distribuição de chamadas, previsibilidade mínima de remuneração, permanência no fluxo produtivo, critérios reputacionais, padrões de priorização, regras de aceitação e mecanismos de sanção.

Income does not arise outside the environment and merely pass through it. It depends materially on the environment's architecture.p. 766

PTA renda não nasce fora do ambiente e apenas passa por ele. Ela depende materialmente da arquitetura do ambiente.

Changes to call distribution criteria, pricing parameters, eligibility rules, incentive models or reputational weights may preserve the program's name, the appearance of partnership and even the formal existence of the account, while substantially draining the economic utility that justified joining and sustained the continuity of productive enjoyment.p. 767

PTMudanças em critérios de distribuição de chamadas, em parâmetros de precificação, em regras de elegibilidade, em modelos de incentivo ou em pesos reputacionais podem manter o nome do programa, a aparência da parceria e até a existência formal da conta, mas esvaziar de modo relevante a utilidade econômica que justificava a adesão e sustentava a continuidade da fruição produtiva.

The legally relevant question is not only whether the platform describes itself as an intermediary or whether the partner is formally free to switch the app on and off.p. 766

PTA questão juridicamente relevante não é saber apenas se a plataforma se autodescreve como intermediária ou se o parceiro é formalmente livre para ligar e desligar o aplicativo.

Here, the formal freedom to join or leave the environment coexists with intense dependence on the private conditions governing access to demand, earnings calculation, reputation building, performance assessment and continued eligibility in the productive flow.p. 766

PTA liberdade formal de aderir ou sair do ambiente convive, aqui, com dependência intensa em relação às condições privadas de acesso à demanda, de cálculo de ganhos, de formação de reputação, de leitura de desempenho e de manutenção da elegibilidade no fluxo produtivo.

Which records of your daily work in the app are worth keeping?

Showing only that your earnings fell or that the account was blocked once is not enough. It helps to keep your request history, changes in pay, messages and performance alerts, the criteria disclosed or withheld, records of restrictions and your ratings.

A drop in earnings is not always a normal risk of the job, because it may stem from an unexplained rule change. And the final penalty usually has a backstory, with alerts, incentives and threats that are worth recording from the start.

Where it comes from

It is not enough to show a reduction in earnings or an isolated block. The link between governance mechanism and functional impact has to be reconstructed. This may involve call history, variations in pay, the app's internal communications, performance alerts, criteria disclosed or withheld, records of restrictions, rating patterns and comparative data on how the environment changed.p. 768

PTNão basta demonstrar que houve redução de ganhos ou bloqueio pontual. É necessário reconstruir a relação entre mecanismo de governança e impacto funcional. Isso pode envolver histórico de chamadas, variações de remuneração, comunicações internas do aplicativo, alertas de desempenho, critérios informados ou omitidos, registros de restrição, padrões de avaliação e dados comparativos sobre a alteração do ambiente.

There are recurring blind spots here that must be avoided. The first is treating fluctuating earnings as a purely natural risk of the activity, when they may result from an opaque unilateral reconfiguration of the distribution infrastructure. The second is treating the final sanction as an isolated event, without reconstructing the regime of conduct, incentive and threat that preceded it.p. 768

PTHá, aqui, pontos de cegueira recorrentes que precisam ser evitados. O primeiro é tratar a oscilação de ganho como puro risco natural da atividade, quando ela pode decorrer de reconfiguração unilateral opaca da infraestrutura de distribuição. O segundo é tratar a sanção final como evento isolado, sem reconstruir o regime de conduta, incentivo e ameaça que a antecedeu.

What can you ask for when the app changes the rules?

You may ask the app to preserve and disclose the minimum records, to explain restrictions in a way that can be checked and to review the criteria it uses to restrict. You may also ask for clarity on what determines how job requests are distributed and for the restoration of whatever the change made worse.

Metrics are the numbers the app uses to measure your performance. A significant change to rules, calculations, priority or metrics cannot be justified merely by invoking efficiency, security or optimization. It must be at least minimally verifiable, and that does not require opening up the whole system or putting a judge in charge of running the app.

Where it comes from

The most appropriate institutional response will tend to favor the preservation and display of minimum records, duties of verifiable reasoning, review of restriction criteria, intelligibility of the relevant parameters for distributing opportunities and functional restoration of the regime whenever the platform has, by design, degraded the productive utility it offered as infrastructure.p. 768

PTA resposta institucional mais adequada tenderá a privilegiar preservação e exibição de registros mínimos, deveres de motivação verificável, revisão de critérios de restrição, inteligibilidade sobre parâmetros relevantes de distribuição de oportunidades e recomposição funcional do regime sempre que a plataforma houver degradado, por desenho, a utilidade produtiva que oferecia como infraestrutura.

Significant rule changes, changes in calculation, reconfigurations of priority, redefinitions of metrics or sanctions with material impact cannot be shielded by generic talk of efficiency, security or algorithmic optimization. They must be made minimally verifiable. This does not mean fully opening up the system, nor turning the Judiciary into the platform's technical administrator.p. 767

PTAlterações relevantes de regra, mudanças de cálculo, reconfigurações de prioridade, redefinições de métricas ou sanções com impacto material não podem ser escudadas por discurso genérico de eficiência, segurança ou otimização algorítmica. Devem ser tornadas minimamente verificáveis. Isso não significa abrir integralmente o sistema, nem transformar o Judiciário em administrador técnico da plataforma.

Where can you take a problem with a work app?

The first step is usually the app's own support channel, in writing, asking for the reason for the change and the criteria behind it. Because the decisive records stay with the company, a lawyer can help you request that they be preserved and disclosed.

If the app says you have no protection because you are a partner who earns money from it, that answer does not settle the matter. In certain cases Brazilian consumer law may extend to people who use the platform to earn an income. This happens when the platform concentrates control over access, continued participation, visibility and the ability to challenge decisions.

When the same change affects everyone who works through the app, the response may be collective, seeking minimum distribution and priority criteria for all. The Public Prosecutor's Office, the Public Defender's Office and the other options are listed on the channels page.

Where it comes from

Whoever controls the service controls, to a large extent, the decisive records of the relationship. Logs, version histories, transactions, interface parameters, change notices and relevant technical documentation tend to remain in the supplier's possession. For this reason, operationalizing the category must go hand in hand with requests for the preservation and disclosure of minimum documentation of the supply.p. 608

PTQuem controla o serviço controla, em larga medida, os registros decisivos da relação. Logs, históricos de versão, transações, parâmetros de interface, comunicações de mudança e documentação técnica relevante tendem a permanecer em poder do fornecedor. Por isso, a operacionalização da categoria precisa vir acompanhada de pedidos de preservação e exibição de documentação mínima do fornecimento.

A partner who uses the infrastructure to generate income does not cease to be vulnerable merely because they earn revenue.p. 778

PTO parceiro que utiliza a infraestrutura para produzir renda não deixa de ser vulnerável apenas porque aufere receita.

The CDC may apply not only where there is a classic final acquisition, but also where the platform creates significant functional subjection to a private governance environment that controls, in a concentrated way, access, permanence, visibility, verifiability and challenge.p. 779-780

PTO CDC pode incidir não apenas onde houver aquisição final clássica, mas também onde a plataforma produzir sujeição funcional relevante a ambiente privado de governança que administre, de forma concentrada, acesso, permanência, visibilidade, verificabilidade e contestação.

Collective redress thus proves particularly suited to imposing injunctive, procedural and analytical measures capable of reintroducing institutional verifiability into the environment. This may involve duties to preserve minimum records, a requirement of verifiable reasons for materially relevant sanctions, maintenance of an accessible history of relevant changes, explanation of minimum operational criteria of distribution, priority or ranking, and other measures that are aimed not only at repairing past harms, but at reorganizing the future conditions of controllability of enjoyment.p. 775

PTA tutela coletiva mostra-se, então, particularmente vocacionada a impor medidas inibitórias, procedimentais e analíticas aptas a reintroduzir verificabilidade institucional no ambiente. Isso pode envolver deveres de preservação de registros mínimos, exigência de motivação verificável para sanções materialmente relevantes, manutenção de histórico acessível de alterações relevantes, explicitação de critérios operacionais mínimos de distribuição, prioridade ou ranqueamento, e outras providências que não se dirigem apenas a reparar danos pretéritos, mas a reorganizar as condições futuras de controlabilidade da fruição.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

How far does this protection reach for people who work through apps?

This reading does not decide whether an employment relationship exists, nor does it automatically turn the relationship into an employment contract. It looks at the environment the app provides and at how that environment is governed.

A consumer by equivalence is someone the law treats as a consumer because they are exposed to the company's practices, even if they are not the final buyer. Not every partner will be treated this way. The test is demanding, and the Superior Court of Justice (STJ), the court with the final word on consumer law, treats this extension as an exception.

Where it comes from

The central point is not merely to assert algorithmic subordination in the abstract, nor to automatically reclassify the whole relationship under the traditional categories of the employment contract. For the purposes of this typology, the focus is on the supply regime.p. 766

PTO ponto central não está apenas em afirmar subordinação algorítmica em abstrato, nem em reclassificar automaticamente toda a relação sob categorias tradicionais do contrato de trabalho. O foco, para os fins desta tipologia, está no regime de fornecimento.

The criterion remains demanding. Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTO critério continua exigente. Nem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

“Art. 29. For the purposes of this Chapter and the following one, all persons, whether determinable or not, who are exposed to the practices provided for therein are deemed equivalent to consumers” Brasil(Brasil, [2022]). In STJ case law, the provision serves as one of the bases of the so-called deepened finalist theory, because it allows, in exceptional cases, the strict reading of final recipient to be relaxed and the CDC to be applied to natural or legal persons who, though not final consumers in the classic sense, are in a situation of vulnerability or exposed to abusive commercial and contractual practicesp. 779

PT“Art. 29. Para os fins deste Capítulo e do seguinte, equiparam-se aos consumidores todas as pessoas determináveis ou não, expostas às práticas nele previstas” (Brasil, [2022]) . Na jurisprudência do STJ, o dispositivo funciona como uma das bases da chamada teoria finalista aprofundada, porque permite, em hipóteses excepcionais, mitigar a leitura estrita do destinatário final e reconhecer a incidência do CDC a pessoas físicas ou jurídicas que, embora não sejam consumidoras finais em sentido clássico, se encontrem em situação de vulnerabilidade ou expostas a práticas comerciais e contratuais abusivas

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
