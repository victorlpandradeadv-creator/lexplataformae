What do you receive when you pay for something digital?Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC)

Anyone who buys a refrigerator takes it home. From then on, it works far from the seller, and the store only comes back into the story if a defect shows up.

Much of what is digital does not work that way. Anyone who buys an item in a game, a subscription in an app or access to a platform also buys something. But using it still depends, every day, on an environment that the company maintains, adjusts and can change. What you have works only on the inside, under the rules of whoever runs the place.

The old rules were designed for stable things, delivered all at once, with defects you can spot by looking. On their own, they cannot cope with a product that keeps being tinkered with after the sale. What decides whether you are in this kind of situation is not whether the item is digital, nor what the company calls it. It is how much what you use depends on the environment the platform governs.

Where it comes from

In the industrial economy, the consumer pays to acquire a good whose existence, once purchased, tends to stabilize out of the supplier's reach, at least within reasonable expectations of durability and of the discipline of defects.p. 210

PTNa economia industrial, o consumidor paga para adquirir um bem cuja existência, uma vez comprada, tende a se estabilizar fora do alcance do fornecedor, ao menos dentro de expectativas razoáveis de durabilidade e de disciplina dos vícios.

The problem can no longer be satisfactorily grasped by the classical grammar of the product or the service, conceived under assumptions of stability, one-off delivery and a defect knowable by relatively direct inspection.p. 501

PTO problema já não se deixa apreender satisfatoriamente pela gramática clássica do produto ou do serviço, concebidos sob pressupostos de estabilidade, entrega pontual e defeito cognoscível por inspeção relativamente direta.

The criterion, therefore, is neither the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC)

It is the legal category for this kind of product. What counts in it is not only the image on the screen, but the position of use you come to occupy, which the platform sustains day after day. The category does not say you bought less. It says you bought a product, in the sense of consumer law, whose supply continues after the purchase. The service the platform provides does not erase the product. It is precisely how what was sold is maintained, updated and shaped.

Where it comes from

What is asserted is that, whenever the consumed utility presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there will be an immaterial product in a legally relevant sense for the application of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1.p. 718

PTO que se afirma é que, sempre que a utilidade consumida se apresente como posição de fruição sustentada por governança privada contínua, haverá produto imaterial em sentido juridicamente relevante para a incidência do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC .

From this angle, the qualification of the IOC as an immaterial product does not require denying the service dimension of the relationship.p. 718

PTSob essa chave, a qualificação do OIC como produto imaterial não exige a negação da dimensão prestacional do vínculo.

The performance does not disappear, but appears as a mode of sustaining, updating and shaping the object of consumption itself.p. 718

PTA prestação não desaparece, mas comparece como modo de sustentação, atualização e conformação do próprio objeto de consumo.

What did the 1990 law already provide for, and what did it lack?

The law marked out a lot in 1990, when the Consumer Protection Code said that a product can be immaterial. The space was reserved, but criteria still had to be built on it for products that depend on a platform that keeps controlling them.

The IOC is the building on that plot. And an empty plot has a cost. While a loss lacks a category and a criterion, it is hard to show and hard to claim, and every gap that lasts tends to become someone's business model.

Where it comes from

The Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) presents itself, in this sense, as the definitive subdivision of a plot of land that the legislator of 1990 had already marked out, although contemporary doctrine has kept it in the condition of a vacant lot.p. 24-25

PTO Objeto Imaterial de Consumo (OIC) apresenta-se, nesse sentido, como o loteamento definitivo de um terreno que o legislador de 1990 já havia demarcado, embora a doutrina contemporânea o tenha mantido na condição de um terreno baldio.

By giving legal density to what was merely a residual label, the IOC prevents immateriality from functioning as an alibi for the technical irresponsibility of platforms.p. 25

PTAo conferir densidade jurídica ao que era apenas um rótulo residual, o OIC impede que a imaterialidade funcione como álibi para a irresponsabilidade técnica das plataformas.

It produces predictable institutional effects, because every stable dogmatic gap tends to be converted into a business modelp. 25

PTEla produz efeitos institucionais previsíveis, porque toda lacuna dogmática estável tende a ser convertida em modelo de negócio

How can you tell whether your case is covered?

Four questions help you recognize an immaterial object of consumption, and the answer to all four must be yes.

The first is what was promised. It is not enough to say that some content was available. You need to say which utility was sold, what it was supposed to let you do and what it was supposed to mean, and you will not find that in slogans or decorative lists.

The second is whether the purchase opened a position that lasts. Buying, here, does not mean receiving a package and being done. It means gaining a place in an environment that has to keep existing for the thing to be of any use.

The third is whether the platform really governs that place. Having technology involved is not enough. There must be internal rules, choices about what appears, participation criteria and punishments that shape your use and have consequences for you.

The fourth is whether the thing can get worse without disappearing. This is the case where access continues, but what you had is downgraded, emptied or reorganized against what was promised, in a serious and lasting way.

Where it comes from

The first field covered by the criteria is the promised object, understood as an enjoyment bundle.p. 428

PTO primeiro campo coberto pelos critérios é o objeto prometido, entendido como feixe de fruição.

The criterion is not satisfied by ornamental lists of functionalities or by generic advertising slogans.p. 428

PTO critério não se satisfaz com listas ornamentais de funcionalidades nem com slogans publicitários genéricos.

The second field is the sustained position of enjoyment.p. 428

PTO segundo campo é a posição de fruição sustentada.

In continuous consumption, the acquisition inaugurates a position in the ecosystem and not a delivery exhausted in an instant.p. 428

PTEm consumo contínuo, a aquisição inaugura uma posição no ecossistema e não uma entrega exaurida em um instante.

The third field is infrastructural governance as the mode of executing supply.p. 428

PTO terceiro campo é a governança infraestrutural como modo de execução do fornecimento.

Infrastructure is incorporated as a constitutive dimension of delivery, because it is through it that internal rules, curation, modulations of visibility, participation parameters and sanction mechanisms are implementedp. 428

PTA infraestrutura é incorporada como dimensão constitutiva da entrega, porque é por meio dela que se implementam regras internas, curadoria, modulações de visibilidade, parâmetros de participação e mecanismos de sanção

The fourth field is the typical possibility of relevant degradation without total rupture.p. 429

PTO quarto campo é a possibilidade típica de degradação relevante sem ruptura total.

Application is decided neither by technological intuition nor by automatic deference to the contract, but by the cumulative presence of verifiable elements of the regimep. 429

PTA incidência não se decide por intuição tecnológica nem por deferência automática ao contrato, mas pela presença cumulativa de elementos verificáveis do regime

Why is the account the portrait of the whole problem?

In an online game, the account gathers characters, skins, emotes and the game's currency, and none of it can leave the account. It is more than a login and password. It is where your inventory, progress, player name and the signals by which others recognize you are kept together.

The account organizes your access, but does not give you possession of the content, which still depends on the environment and on the company's rules. When the game goes down, what is in the account is useless until it comes back. That is why the account can be seen as the central immaterial object of consumption, a miniature portrait of the whole category.

Where it comes from

Champions, skins, emotes and in-game currency are part of the catalog of goods that only become enjoyable as an access license associated with the account, and not as things transportable outside itp. 408

PTCampeões, skins, emotes e moeda do jogo integram o catálogo de bens que só se tornam fruíveis como licença de acesso associada à conta, e não como coisas transportáveis para fora dela

It is not reducible to a login credential. It is the organizational point that holds the enjoyment bundle togetherp. 408

PTEla não se resume a uma credencial de login. Ela é o ponto organizacional que mantém unido o feixe de fruição

When the service becomes unavailable, it is not only access that fails, but the virtual content itself loses immediate usefulness, because the environment that makes it enjoyable stops operating.p. 409

PTQuando o serviço apresenta indisponibilidade, não é apenas o acesso que falha, mas o próprio conteúdo virtual perde utilidade imediata, porque o ambiente que o torna fruível deixa de operar.

For this reason, it can be described as the central IOC and, at the same time, as a “concept-infrastructure” in miniature.p. 409

What does the law see once the problem is qualified?

Giving a name changes what the law can see, and this has an effect in two places.

In the first, the company can no longer pull the case out of consumer law just by saying that it provides a service or granted a license. Those words are not enough to make what you bought precarious.

In the second, the discussion shifts its center. Instead of looking only at the contract you accepted one day, the focus is on the use you make, every day, of what you bought. The contract still matters, but it does not on its own explain a relationship that plays out all the time through means that go far beyond the text. So being immaterial no longer works as an excuse for the platform not to answer for what it delivers.

Where it comes from

The consequence of this qualification is relevant on two planes. On the first, it prevents legal control from being neutralized by the supplier's descriptive strategies, especially by the claim that there would be only the provision of a service or a mere content license, as if these formulas sufficed to naturalize the precariousness of the bond and set aside the protective regime applicable to enjoyment.p. 718

PTA consequência dessa qualificação é relevante em dois planos. No primeiro, ela impede que o controle jurídico seja neutralizado por estratégias descritivas do fornecedor, especialmente pela alegação de que haveria apenas prestação de serviço ou mera licença de conteúdo, como se essas fórmulas bastassem para naturalizar a precariedade do vínculo e afastar o regime protetivo incidente sobre a fruição.

On the second, it redefines the reading axis of the CDC. Instead of operating primarily from the contract as a formal statement, the discipline comes to be structured from enjoyment as a governed relationship.p. 718

PTNo segundo, ela redefine o eixo de leitura do CDC. Em vez de operar prioritariamente a partir do contrato como enunciado formal, a disciplina passa a ser estruturada a partir da fruição como relação governada.

This does not mean denying the relevance of the contract, but refusing its explanatory sufficiency in environments in which the economic content of the relationship is carried out continuously by mechanisms that far exceed the contractual text.p. 718

PTIsso não significa negar a relevância do contrato, mas recusar sua suficiência explicativa em ambientes nos quais o conteúdo econômico da relação se realiza de modo continuado por mecanismos que excedem largamente o texto contratual.

Which cases fall outside the category?

Not everything becomes a case, and it helps to know where the category ends before you complain.

A service that happens once and ends there, with no lasting position, does not need the category, because the ordinary rules for services settle it. A platform that only works as a shop window or channel, without really governing what you use, also tends to fall outside.

Purely cosmetic changes, when symbolic value was not at the core of what was sold, as a rule also fall outside. And the mere existence of moderation, rankings, community rules or screen adjustments is not enough. Ordinary updates, regular curation and anti-fraud measures are part of the normal life of the service. What counts is a serious and lasting decline in what was promised.

There are also cases in which the core of the problem lies elsewhere, such as an improper charge, a one-off defect, an isolated misleading ad or an offer that was not honored. In those cases ordinary consumer law tends to settle the matter without this category, and you are still protected that way.

PTO terceiro limite recai sobre situações em que o valor simbólico ou semiótico é funcionalmente acessório e não integra o núcleo do feixe de fruição prometido.

Ordinary updates, regular curation, anti-abuse interventions and changes that do not affect the functional core of the enjoyment bundle remain within the normal field of performance and do not by definition constitute relevant degradation.p. 430

PTAtualizações ordinárias, curadoria regular, intervenções antiabuso e mudanças que não atingem o núcleo funcional do feixe de fruição permanecem no campo normal de execução e não constituem degradação relevante por definição.

The fifth limit is the case in which the core of the conflict is something else, such as a classic occasional defect, undue charge, localized misleading advertising or specific nonperformance of an offer. In these cases, common consumer law dogmatics tends to resolve the dispute with no real gain in mobilizing the IOC.p. 435

PTO quinto limite é o caso em que o núcleo do conflito é outro, como vício pontual clássico, cobrança indevida, publicidade enganosa localizada ou inadimplemento específico de oferta. Nessas hipóteses, a dogmática consumerista comum tende a resolver o litígio sem ganho real em mobilizar o OIC.

When does what you bought depend on the platform?

When what you use depends, structurally, on an environment the platform runs, adjusts and can change. That is the case with the skin that only works inside the game, or the account that holds your inventory and your progress. What matters here is whether this dependence affects your access, whether you get to stay, how much you can understand of what is happening or the updates to the experience.

What must the platform preserve?

It must keep what you bought accessible, understandable and preserved in whatever makes it useful. The platform can change the environment, because change is part of this kind of supply, but only within the limits of what is legitimate.

How do you show that what you use depends on the platform?

Show that what you use depends on the platform's environment, and how the platform delivers this experience. It also helps to show your concrete position in the relationship, such as what is in your account, what you paid and what that lets you do.

What if the company says it is just a service or a license?

You can ask for the case to be handled under consumer law, with what you bought treated as an immaterial product. The company cannot get out of this just by saying it provides a service or granted a simple license, because the service is precisely how the product is kept working.

Does recognizing the product already settle the case?

This is the first step, not the end of the case. Recognizing the product opens the door to the other duties on this page, but each of them still depends on what happened to you. You do not need to fit every problem into this category to be protected.

Where it comes from

Whenever the consumed utility depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and made mutable by private governance, with impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating of the experience, the field of application of the IOC will be established.p. 743

PTSempre que a utilidade consumida depender estruturalmente de ambiente administrado, modulado e mutável por governança privada, com impacto sobre acesso, permanência, inteligibilidade ou atualização da experiência, estará configurado o campo de incidência do OIC.

The corresponding duty of supply is to ensure functionally accessible, intelligible and preserved enjoyment within the limits of the legitimate mutability of the environment.p. 743

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar fruição funcionalmente acessível, inteligível e preservada dentro dos limites da legítima mutabilidade do ambiente.

The minimum required proof concerns the demonstration of the structural dependence of the utility on the governed ecosystem, of the way the experience is made available and of the consumer's concrete position in the bond.p. 743

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre a demonstração da dependência estrutural da utilidade em relação ao ecossistema governado, da forma de disponibilização da experiência e da posição concreta do consumidor no vínculo.

The resulting legal qualification is that of an immaterial product subject to the regime of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1, without the service dimension of the bond setting aside the control of enjoyment as the core of supply.p. 743

PTA qualificação jurídica daí resultante é a de produto imaterial submetido ao regime do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC , sem que a dimensão prestacional do vínculo afaste o controle da fruição como núcleo do fornecimento.

The possible institutional consequence consists in opening the subsequent filters of the regime, preventing the controversy from being neutralized by the claim of mere provision of a service or simple formal licensing.p. 743

PTA consequência institucional possível consiste na abertura dos filtros subsequentes do regime, impedindo que a controvérsia seja neutralizada pela alegação de mera prestação de serviço ou de simples licenciamento formal.

When is there an IOC?

There is an IOC whenever the utility consumed presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance. In that case there is an immaterial product in a legally relevant sense, and art. 3, § 1, of the CDC applies1. The category gives concrete form to the notion of immaterial product that the law already provided for and reads it as a governed enjoyment bundle.

What decides is not the materiality of the medium, nor the name the supplier gives to what it sells. What decides is the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.

The criterion, therefore, is neither the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

What does the qualification of the object unlock?

It is the trigger for the other parameters. Auditability, functional equivalence, abusive teleology and restoration remedies presuppose a relationship in which the consumed utility depends on the continued administration of the environment. The internal articulation among the categories also matters. Without it, each category could be read in isolation, as a label for a specific problem, and would lose its capacity to operate as a unified language for abuses by design, relevant degradations and proportional responses.
