Your rights · By problem

I complain, and the answer never solves anything. What can I do?

Customer service is kind, answers quickly and asks you to wait, but the problem continues, and you end up letting it go. Often the conflict is managed before it becomes a complaint, and the friendly tone does not change what you lost.

Where it comes from

In platform-governed ecosystems, the conflict does not begin when the user decides to litigate, complain or formalize dissatisfaction. In many cases, it is governed before that.p. 780

PTEm ecossistemas governados por plataforma, o conflito não começa quando o usuário decide litigar, reclamar ou formalizar inconformidade. Em muitos casos, ele é governado antes disso.

The user is not merely facing an opaque environment or criteria he does not control. He is facing an ecosystem that produces enough signals of intelligibility and care to induce accommodation, even when the materiality of enjoyment has already been compromised.p. 782

PTO usuário não está apenas diante de ambiente opaco ou de critérios que não controla. Está diante de ecossistema que produz sinais de inteligibilidade e cuidado suficientes para induzir acomodação, mesmo quando a materialidade da fruição já foi comprometida.

How do you know whether customer service is solving the problem or just calming you down?

Some signs come up again and again. The reply seems to explain but does not say what happened, and the tone is friendly, yet no way to contest the decision ever opens.

The loss is also often presented as a technical adjustment, a temporary limitation, a security measure or a normal stage of use. In a service you use every day, the small frustrations get absorbed one by one, until the loss seems normal.

The test is not whether you felt well treated. It is whether customer service works in a way that steers attention away from the loss and drains the force from your complaint.

Where it comes from

The platform may, for example, offer a sense of transparency without delivering real intelligibility, produce an impression of listening without opening effective paths of contestation, mobilize a human tone without assuming proportional duties of reasoning or justification and distribute signs of care that work less as adequate information and more as semiotic cushioning of criticism.p. 782

PTA plataforma pode, por exemplo, oferecer sensação de transparência sem entregar inteligibilidade real, produzir impressão de escuta sem abrir vias efetivas de contestação, mobilizar tom humano sem assumir deveres proporcionais de motivação ou justificação e distribuir signos de cuidado que funcionam menos como informação adequada e mais como amortecimento semiótico da crítica.

The loss no longer appears as an exceptional event and comes to be metabolized as part of the normal functioning of the system. The same happens with friction. Instead of promptly triggering a reading of inadequacy or abusiveness, it may be reconfigured by the platform as a technical adjustment, a temporary limitation, an understandable inconvenience, a security measure, a necessary update or a simple stage of use.p. 781

PTA perda deixa de aparecer como evento excepcional e passa a ser metabolizada como parte do normal funcionamento do sistema. O mesmo ocorre com o atrito. Em vez de desencadear prontamente uma leitura de inadequação ou abusividade, ele pode ser reconfigurado pela plataforma como ajuste técnico, limitação temporária, inconveniente compreensível, medida de segurança, atualização necessária ou simples etapa do uso.

What matters is not whether the user “felt” more or less welcomed, but whether the platform structured signs, routines and protocols capable of shifting the reading of the loss, reducing the critical force of the experience and weakening the probability of intelligible contestation.p. 782

PTO que importa não é saber se o usuário “sentiu-se” mais ou menos acolhido, mas se a plataforma estruturou signos, rotinas e protocolos capazes de deslocar a leitura da perda, reduzir a força crítica da experiência e enfraquecer a probabilidade de contestação inteligível.

Which conversations with customer service are worth keeping?

It is worth keeping the conversations with customer service, the canned replies and the justifications the company gave. It also helps to note the steps you were led through, such as waiting, trying again or accepting a vague explanation, because they show how the loss was handled.

Where it comes from

The evidence compatible with this subtype requires reconstruction of the discursive and procedural environment in which frustration becomes legible or, on the contrary, is diluted. This includes the analysis of the language of the interface, of the customer service flows, of the response templates, of the routines of explanation, of the way the platform frames the loss and of the steps through which the user is led to accept, wait, relativize or close the dissatisfaction.p. 782-783

PTA evidência compatível com esse subtipo exige reconstrução do ambiente discursivo e procedimental em que a frustração se torna legível ou, ao contrário, é diluída. Isso inclui a análise da linguagem da interface, dos fluxos de atendimento, dos modelos de resposta, das rotinas de explicação, da forma como a plataforma enquadra a perda e das etapas pelas quais o usuário é conduzido a aceitar, esperar, relativizar ou encerrar a inconformidade.

What can you ask for to get the complaint moving?

You may ask the company to explain clearly what happened and why, giving the concrete reason for the decision. You may also ask it to offer a way to contest that genuinely deals with your complaint instead of just soothing you.

The original complaint still stands. If the problem was the account, the item or the charge, your request about it still holds, because customer service that merely soothes you does not erase the conflict.

Where it comes from

The legal response compatible with this picture tends to require more than simple retrospective repair. It may call for correction of customer service flows, reinforced intelligibility, prohibition of functionally misleading discursive routines, procedural duties of reasoning and the design of paths of contestation that do not serve only to absorb dissatisfaction, but to make it effectively processable and verifiable.p. 783

PTA resposta jurídica compatível com esse quadro tende a exigir mais do que simples reparação retrospectiva. Pode reclamar correção dos fluxos de atendimento, reforço de inteligibilidade, proibição de rotinas discursivas funcionalmente enganosas, deveres procedimentais de motivação e desenho de vias de contestação que não sirvam apenas para absorver a inconformidade, mas para torná-la efetivamente processável e verificável.

The conflict does not disappear. It is governed in its pre-legal phase, through mechanisms that turn loss into tolerable noise, opacity into normality and asymmetry into an absorbable experience.p. 782

PTO conflito não desaparece. Ele é governado em sua fase pré-jurídica, por meio de mecanismos que transformam perda em ruído tolerável, opacidade em normalidade e assimetria em experiência absorvível.

Where should you take the complaint when customer service just goes in circles?

When the conversation with the company just goes in circles, it may be better to take the complaint elsewhere, to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, routes explained on the channels page. Waiting for customer service itself to acknowledge the loss may leave the problem unanswered, since the company also influences when the loss becomes visible.

There, the saved conversations show how customer service handled the case. And when the same way of answering affects many people, public consumer protection agencies can demand complaint procedures that everyone can understand.

Where it comes from

Contestation may be postponed, demobilized or dissolved before it takes institutional form.p. 782

PTA contestação pode ser adiada, desmobilizada ou dissolvida antes de ganhar forma institucional.

In many environments, the platform does not need to formally prevent conflict in order to reduce it. It is enough to administer how it is perceived.p. 783

PTEm muitos ambientes, a plataforma não precisa impedir formalmente o conflito para reduzi-lo. Basta administrar o modo como ele é percebido.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets. These standards offer a concrete criterion for consent decrees, recommendations, civil investigations and minimum governance requirements: functional impact reports on relevant changes, verifiable transparency protocols, preservation of essential records, understandable contestation procedures and minimum criteria of restoration in case of relevant degradation of enjoyment.p. 662-663

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma. Esses standards oferecem critério concreto para termos de ajustamento, recomendações, investigações civis e exigências mínimas de governança: relatórios de impacto funcional em alterações relevantes, protocolos de transparência verificável, preservação de registros essenciais, procedimentos de contestação compreensíveis e critérios mínimos de recomposição em caso de degradação relevante da fruição.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is kind customer service a problem?

No. Simple language, polite service and the company's effort to keep a good relationship with you are not illegal. The problem begins when this style of service becomes a structural part of a way of draining conflicts of their force.

Nor is there any guarantee that the complaint will succeed. What changes is that the loss is no longer treated as noise and becomes, once again, a problem that can be examined.

Where it comes from

The problem does not lie in the use of accessible language, in polite service or in the legitimate attempt to maintain a functionally stable relationship with the user. The problem begins when these elements come to form, structurally, part of a regime of practical neutralization of conflict.p. 782

PTO problema não está no uso de linguagem acessível, no atendimento polido ou na tentativa legítima de manter relação funcionalmente estável com o usuário. O problema começa quando esses elementos passam a integrar, de forma estrutural, um regime de neutralização prática do conflito.

The conflict does not disappear. It is governed in its pre-legal phase, through mechanisms that turn loss into tolerable noise, opacity into normality and asymmetry into an absorbable experience.p. 782

PTO conflito não desaparece. Ele é governado em sua fase pré-jurídica, por meio de mecanismos que transformam perda em ruído tolerável, opacidade em normalidade e assimetria em experiência absorvível.

One word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
