The platform decides about me · Your rights

When it is not a consumer relationship, does the law still protect you?

Not everyone who depends on a platform is a consumer. Whoever sells in an online store, works through an app or builds a client network inside another company's system can also lose what they built.

When consumer law does not reach the case, the law still protects. The rules become those of the Civil Code, and proof calls for more care.

Page added on 15 November 2026. It is the first major addition after the defence. The thesis does not name the Governed Enjoyment Intangible Object, which it contains without developing.

What do you have when you are not a consumer?

What you built on a platform is an asset, even without a body. Reputation, history, a client base and a collection exist for the law, even when they are kept in someone else's system.

The difference is that another company controls the environment in which this asset works. You have the asset, but you do not control the conditions for using what you have, and that is where the problem lies.

Where it comes from

Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda's doctrine of digital assets serves an important function because it solves, with good density, a problem that cannot be sidestepped by contractual rhetoric or by nostalgia for tangibility: techno-digital assets are legal assets and do not disappear from the world of Law just because they are incorporeal.p. 565

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais de Bruno Torquato Zampier Lacerda cumpre função importante porque resolve, com boa densidade, um problema que não pode ser contornado por retórica contratual ou por nostalgia da tangibilidade: ativos tecnodigitais são bens jurídicos e não desaparecem do mundo do Direito só porque são incorpóreos.

The criterion, therefore, is not the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

Can consumer law apply anyway?

It can. Consumer law also protects those exposed to a company's practices, even when they are not the usual end buyer. A seller or an app worker can come in this way.

But not every participant in a platform is treated as a consumer. What counts is real dependence on the company, and not the name the contract gives the relationship, such as partnership.

Where it comes from

Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTNem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

What decides is the real structure of subjection, not the contractual appearance of horizontality.p. 780

PTO que decide é a estrutura real da sujeição, não a aparência contratual de horizontalidade.

Which rules apply when consumer law is left out?

Every contract must respect its social function, and both parties must act in good faith from beginning to end. That includes the time when the company changes the rules of the service.

When the contract comes ready-made, like the terms of use, an unclear clause is read in favour of the one who merely adhered. And a clause that makes you give up in advance what the deal itself should provide is void.

Whoever uses a right far beyond its purpose also commits an unlawful act. And whoever carries out an activity that by its nature puts others' rights at risk is liable for the harm even without fault.

What becomes harder?

Under consumer law, your weakness before the company is already presumed. Under the Civil Code, a contract between businesses is treated as balanced until someone shows otherwise.

That is why this path calls for showing the inequality with facts. It helps to keep the rules the company changed, the punishments without reason, the records only it holds and everything that shows you depended on its system.

Where it comes from

The change of rule, the lowering of reach or the suspension do not even become a verifiable and disputable object within the system itself.p. 381

PTA alteração de regra, o rebaixamento de alcance ou a suspensão sequer se transformam em objeto verificável e disputável no próprio sistema.

What is the Governed Enjoyment Intangible Object?

The Governed Enjoyment Intangible Object (GEIO) is the civil genus of which the IOC is the consumer species. It designates the position of enjoyment sustained over time over an intangible asset whose utility depends on an environment that a third party maintains, updates and governs. Every IOC is a GEIO, but not every GEIO is an IOC.

The core is the same as the IOC's. The structural dependence of enjoyment on the governed environment, governance as the way the relationship is performed and the possibility of relevant degradation without total rupture. What changes is the normative basis of protection, which moves from the CDC to the Civil Code.

The formula that sums up the shift is simple. One can have without controlling the having. The holder keeps the asset, and another keeps the power over the conditions of enjoyment.

Where it comes from

The criterion, therefore, is not the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

The intensity of this dependence varies according to the degree of lock-in, the centrality of collections tied to the account, the relevance of usage history, the weight of reputation metrics and restrictions on transfer, interoperability or rebuilding what was acquired outside the platform.p. 486

PTA intensidade dessa dependência varia conforme grau de lock-in, centralidade de acervos vinculados à conta, relevância do histórico de uso, peso de métricas de reputação e restrições à transferência, interoperabilidade ou reconstrução do adquirido fora da plataforma.

Where does the category start from?

It starts from Bruno Zampier Lacerda's doctrine of digital assets. That doctrine holds that techno-digital assets are legal assets, divides the phenomenon into patrimonial, existential and mixed assets and blocks the requalification of the asset as a revocable licence by mere contractual nomenclature.

That is the floor, and the GEIO does not dispute it. The next step is the same one the IOC took within the CDC. The question stops being who holds the asset and how it circulates, and becomes how enjoyment is maintained, modulated and degraded by whoever governs the environment. The difference is that here the step happens within the Civil Code itself.

Where it comes from

The doctrine of digital assets builds, in other words, the floor from which the debate about regime becomes possible.p. 569

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais constrói, em outras palavras, o piso a partir do qual o debate sobre regime se torna possível.

The doctrine of digital assets is right to block the business attempt to requalify, by mere contractual nominalism, objects already integrated into the user's sphere as permissions dissolvable by unilateral will.p. 573

PTA doutrina dos bens digitais tem razão ao bloquear a tentativa empresarial de requalificar, por simples nominalismo contratual, objetos já integrados à esfera do usuário como permissões dissolvíveis por vontade unilateral.

When does the case leave the CDC?

The first route is still the CDC. The functional reading of art. 29 reaches the person who is not a typical end buyer but is exposed to the platform's practices and governance in relevant vulnerability. Through it come the marketplace seller and the app worker, when the structure of subjection justifies it.

The criterion, however, is demanding, and not every ecosystem participant will be a consumer by equivalence. Applying the regime to the marketplace is already conditioned on the admission of art. 29. When equivalence is not accepted, the subjection does not disappear. Only the code that controls it changes, and that is where the GEIO operates.

Where it comes from

Once the applicability of art. 29 of the CDC1 is admitted to qualify legal entities in a factual position of vulnerability, the regime proves fully pertinentp. 434

PTAdmitida a aplicabilidade do art. 29 do CDC para qualificar pessoas jurídicas em posição factual de vulnerabilidade, o regime se mostra plenamente pertinente

This does not authorise an indiscriminate inflation of the category. The criterion remains demanding. Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTIsso não autoriza inflacionamento indiscriminado da categoria. O critério continua exigente. Nem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

What regime does the Civil Code offer?

The Civil Code takes in almost all the systemic solutions of the IOC regime. The social function of the contract and objective good faith, in arts. 421 and 422 of the Civil Code2, reach performance, and it is in performance that governance changes rules, lowers visibility and degrades enjoyment. Unilateral change falls under the same purpose control that the CDC exercises through art. 51, XIII.

Terms of use are an adhesion contract. Under arts. 423 and 424 of the Civil Code3, an ambiguous or contradictory clause is interpreted in favour of the adhering party, and a clause imposing advance waiver of a right arising from the nature of the deal is void. Whoever adheres to a governed environment does not waive in advance the functional continuity of what they built there.

Abuse of right, in art. 187 of the Civil Code4, plays the role of abusive teleology. The power to govern the environment is legitimate, but it becomes unlawful when exercised beyond the economic or social purpose that justifies it, or against good faith. The unlawful act can lie in the design, and not only in the isolated act.

Liability for the risk of the activity, in the sole paragraph of art. 927 of the Civil Code5, reaches whoever continuously maintains the environment on which another's enjoyment depends. The preferred response is still to restore the lost function, and compensation remains a residual or complementary response.

Where it comes from

Monetisation remains possible, but as a residual or complementary response, not as an automatic reflex of the injury.p. 748

PTA monetização permanece possível, mas como resposta residual ou complementar, não como reflexo automático da lesão.

What is lost in the passage, and how is it proved?

The greater rigour is above all evidentiary. Under the CDC vulnerability is presumed. Art. 421-A of the Civil Code6 presumes civil and business contracts to be equal and symmetrical, but only until concrete elements justify setting the presumption aside. The work of the GEIO lies in producing those elements.

Semiotic vulnerability serves here as a script for proof. It describes an objective condition of exposure to inequality of intelligibility and agency, and not a defect of the person. That is why it can be shown through the facts of the structure, such as the concentration of records, the opacity of visibility and sanction criteria and the cost of exit.

What is lost in the passage is the list of nullities by operation of law in art. 51 of the CDC7 and the joint liability of the supply chain, which the Civil Code does not presume. The regime still stands, with a greater demand for demonstration.

Where it comes from

Semiotic vulnerability can be defined, in short, as the objective condition of the consumer's exposure to a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in ecosystems of enjoyment governed by a platform, in which the production of meaning and the attribution of value depend on codes, metrics and visibility regimes administered by the environment itself.p. 396

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica pode ser definida, em síntese, como a condição objetiva de exposição do consumidor a uma desigualdade estrutural de inteligibilidade e de agência em ecossistemas de fruição governados por plataforma, nos quais a produção de sentido e a atribuição de valor dependem de códigos, métricas e regimes de visibilidade administrados pelo próprio ambiente.

Power is not stabilised only by price, but by the cost of exit.p. 217

PTO poder não se estabiliza só por preço, mas por custo de saída.

Does the genus depend on a digital platform?

No. What defines the GEIO is governed enjoyment, not technology. A network of direct-sales resellers shows the point. The reseller's client base accumulates value, generates future economic flow and depends on the company's normative infrastructure, with neither a consumer nor an app at the centre of the relationship.

This example proves that the genus exists beyond the species. A game case would only prove that the IOC works.

Author's note · 15 November 2026

Why is the GEIO not in the thesis?

The Governed Enjoyment Intangible Object does not appear in the thesis, and the absence is deliberate. The thesis chose Consumer Law as its scope, because the CDC was designed for mass relationships, and platforms are mass relationships by definition. That is why the structure of governed enjoyment was developed there in its consumer species, the IOC. The genus remained contained in the architecture itself, without a name and without its own development.

The thesis itself foresees the limit of this scope, when it conditions part of the regime on the acceptance of art. 29 of the CDC1 (p. 434) and recognises that not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence (p. 780). This page names what remained contained and develops the way out that the thesis pointed to without taking it. Nothing here contradicts the defended text.

How does this page relate to the doctrine of digital assets in the thesis?

The thesis states that it does not confront the Civil Code's silence about the digital asset, but the CDC's opacity about the intangible product under governed enjoyment (p. 568). It also states that civil-law grammar validates the legal existence of the asset but does not exhaust consumer-law syntax (p. 571). Both passages speak of the Civil Code read as a book of assets, tied to the thing and to ownership.

The GEIO does not return to that reading. It returns to the Civil Code after the turn the thesis made, with the governed enjoyment bundle as its object. The order is this. The doctrine of digital assets provides legal existence, ownership and circulation. The GEIO provides the general civil category of continued enjoyment under another's governance. The IOC is the consumer specialisation, made concrete in the intangible product of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC8.

Where it comes from

It does not confront the Civil Code's silence about the status of the digital asset, but the Consumer Protection Code's opacity about the regime of the intangible product under continued and governed enjoyment.p. 568

PTEla não enfrenta o silêncio do Código Civil sobre o estatuto do ativo digital, mas a opacidade do Código de Defesa do Consumidor sobre o regime do produto imaterial sob fruição continuada e governada.

    PTArt. 421-A. Os contratos civis e empresariais presumem-se paritários e simétricos até a presença de elementos concretos que justifiquem o afastamento dessa presunção, ressalvados os regimes jurídicos previstos em leis especiais, garantido também que: I - as partes negociantes poderão estabelecer parâmetros objetivos para a interpretação das cláusulas negociais e de seus pressupostos de revisão ou de resolução; II - a alocação de riscos definida pelas partes deve ser respeitada e observada; e III - a revisão contratual somente ocorrerá de maneira excepcional e limitada.

    Text in force copied from the Planalto website on 15/11/2026. Later addition, outside the thesis.
