2.3 On the reference ecosystem and exemplification strategy

See this section in the PDF

p. 56

The choice of a specific ecosystem as the axis of observation does not have a merely illustrative purpose, nor is it intended to function as the foundation for building an autonomous empirical section. It is a strategy of intelligibility, in which a paradigmatic case is mobilized to make visible, more clearly than in other contexts, the mechanisms that structure the legal life of continuous digital consumption. When technical architecture, formal terms and execution routines are densely coupled, the case ceases to be an “example” and comes to operate as a field of visibility, because it allows one to follow the passage between declared norm, norm in operation and disputed norm, without depending on abstract inferences or hasty generalizations.

In this key, the intended generalization is not representative, as if the case had to mirror a universe statistically. It is analytical and oriented by mechanisms, insofar as the focus falls on patterns of private governance, control devices and forms of distribution of risks that repeat themselves, with variations, across different platforms and business models. The relevance of the case, therefore, does not stem from a supposed exceptionality, but from the fact that it concentrates, in a single arrangement, elements that help understand how promises of service are stabilized, how expectations of value are modulated and how unilateral alterations capable of impacting the consumer's experience are carried out.

The selection of this ecosystem does not rest on thematic preference, but on criteria of analytical functionality. First, it is a continuous digital service, whose experience is mediated by rules and updates, so that the “product” does not present itself as a one-off delivery, but as a prolonged, calibrated and revisable relationship. Second, the economic dynamic that sustains it is organized around digital objects and intangible performances that, even if perceived as consumer goods, depend entirely on a private technical and normative apparatus to exist, circulate and produce functional value. Third, there is a high density of governance, since the platform not only offers and monetizes, but also disciplines conduct, defines internal metrics, redistributes incentives, applies sanctions and periodically reorganizes the very environment in which the promise of experience is realized and, thus, consolidates the consumer's legitimate expectation.

p. 57 In addition, the ecosystem makes the structural mutability of digital consumption particularly visible, since substantial alterations can occur without a change of ownership, without a new explicit contract and, often, without adequate understanding of the practical scope of the change by the average user. This mutability is not accessory, but constitutive, and for this reason the case is useful for observing how legitimate expectations are reconfigured, which mechanisms sustain perceived continuity despite material alteration and how risk shifts to the consumer when functional value equivalence comes to depend on unilateral decisions of little transparency. Finally, it is an environment in which there are public traces and documentable materials in sufficient quantity to allow referenced anchoring, with minimal triangulation between normative texts, institutional communication and execution practices, reducing the risk that the analysis depends on subjective impression or personal experience.

The mobilization of the ecosystem, however, does not imply that it is treated as an exhaustively described empirical object, nor as a universe to be inventoried in all its social, cultural or economic complexity. Recourse to the case is delimited and instrumental, because it serves to anchor inferences and to make concrete certain mechanisms whose formulation, at the abstract level, could sound excessively generic. For this reason, references to the ecosystem will be employed as vignettes and points of support, triggered only when necessary to stabilize the meaning of a category, illustrate a typical arrangement of governance or show the way a formal rule is converted into execution practice. It is not intended, thereby, to reconstruct the history of the sector, compare competing models or offer a representative overview of the industry, but to use a field of visibility capable of giving density to the theoretical and dogmatic path.

This delimitation also prevents the analysis from being confused with ethnography in the classical sense, with experiential narrative or with “evidence” based on familiarity with the environment. The presence of the case does not replace the conceptual chain, nor does it authorize conclusions by mere plausibility. On the contrary, it functions as a technique of argumentative control, by orienting the text toward observable facts, documented rules and identifiable practices whenever the thesis requires demonstration of the passage between normative statement and its concrete operation. The case, therefore, neither commands the thesis nor defines its reach, since it acts as methodological support so that the proposed architecture can be understood, put under tension and, when necessary, verified in a setting in which the mechanisms appear with sufficient clarity to justify analytical generalization.

For this use to be intellectually controllable, it is necessary to distinguish layers of normativity that coexist in platform ecosystems and that, in practice, produce p. 58 distinct effects on the consumer. There is, first, a declared normativity, which presents itself in the form of terms, policies, rules and institutional announcements, through which the platform describes the service, defines conditions of access, establishes rights and duties and announces, when it wishes, the limits of the promised experience. There is, second, a normativity in operation, which is not reduced to the formal text and manifests itself in the technical architecture, in interface flows, in execution routines, in mechanisms for modulating the experience and in the concrete forms of oversight and sanction. This layer is decisive, because it is in it that the contractual promise takes shape and it is also in it that a relevant part of the informational asymmetry typical of continuous digital consumption is produced.

There is, further, a disputed normativity, which emerges when the two previous layers come into friction, whether because execution reveals consequences that the text did not clarify, or because the text functions as an alibi for practices perceived as abusive, or because unilateral changes shift the balance of the service without the consumer being able to recognize, with precision, what was altered and with what effects. In this arena appear conflicts, complaints, public debates and administrative interventions, which not only describe the problem, but also expose the platform's strategies of legitimation, the consumer's repertoires of contestation and the limits of legal translation of experiences mediated by design and private governance. Delimiting these arenas is not a classificatory whim, but a way of preventing the analysis from confusing statement with execution, promise with delivery or controversy with mechanism, besides ensuring that each argumentative step rests on the appropriate layer of the phenomenon examined.

In the work as a whole, the selected ecosystem fulfills a supporting function, and not a leading one. It operates as a point of anchoring so that categories and distinctions developed in the following sections can be understood without depending on artificial examples or on abstractions distant from the consumption experience. Thus, when the thesis advances in describing the economic and technological structures that sustain platformization, as well as in analyzing the semiotic and institutional mechanisms that modulate the perception of value and the expectation of continuity, the case enters as a controlled reference, only to the extent that it contributes to stabilizing the meaning of the argument and making verifiable the passage between dogmatic formulation and concrete functioning.

This use also gives unity to the path, because it allows one to return, at strategic moments, to the same field of visibility without turning the thesis into a closed case study. In this way, the ecosystem appears as a seam running through the text, offering consistency to the inferences and allowing the reader to recognize the same architecture p. 59 of mechanisms from different angles, as the questions and the theoretical instruments mobilized change. The thesis, therefore, does not derive from the case, but makes use of it to sustain an argument that intends to reach other platforms and other arrangements of digital consumption, precisely because the objective is not the singularity of one environment, but the intelligibility of structures that repeat themselves, with variations, in different markets and sectors.

In order to prevent familiarity with the ecosystem from being confused with a criterion of truth, the analysis adopts a standard of control that separates orientation of the gaze from validation of the argument. Prior experience can help identify points of friction, recognize changes that tend to go unnoticed and formulate hypotheses about how certain mechanisms produce effects on the consumer. However, these hypotheses do not function as premises, nor is subjective impression treated as evidence. Whenever the case is triggered, the support of the argument rests on trackable elements, such as normative texts, institutional communications, identifiable execution routines, observable sanction practices and public records that allow minimal triangulation, so that the reader can follow the inferential chain without needing to adhere to personal testimony. When the text resorts to descriptions of functioning widely disseminated in the sector, they operate as contextualization and not as evidence of a specific occurrence, with legal attribution remaining conditioned on trackable elements when the argument depends on it.

This disciplining also seeks to reduce selection and confirmation biases, since platform ecosystems produce readings skewed by salient episodes, isolated controversies or highly polarized interpretive communities. To prevent the analysis from being captured by these dynamics, references to the case will be built parsimoniously, with attention to the distinction between declared rule, executed rule and public controversy, and with an explicit commitment not to extrapolate conclusions beyond what the material mobilized authorizes. Thus, familiarity does not operate as a rhetorical shortcut, but as a tool that helps choose better what to observe, while the support of the argument remains conditioned on criteria of verifiability and internal coherence with the theoretical-dogmatic apparatus that structures the thesis.
