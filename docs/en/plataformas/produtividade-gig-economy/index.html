Who is in charge of work done through an app?Productivity IOCs under the gig economy

Delivery and ride apps call drivers and couriers independent partners. But it is the app that hands out the calls, gives the ratings, decides who gets priority, sets the rules for accepting rides and applies the penalties.

For people who work this way, the app supplies more than a payment per task. It supplies the very environment that makes the work possible, and the app governs that environment. That is why it isn't enough to look at what the contract says about autonomy. You have to look at who actually controls access to demand, how much you can earn and whether you get to stay in the stream of work.

Where it comes from

The platform organizes access to demand, distribution of calls, minimum predictability of pay, permanence in the productive flow, reputational criteria, prioritization patterns, acceptance rules and sanction mechanisms.p. 765

PTA plataforma organiza acesso à demanda, distribuição de chamadas, previsibilidade mínima de remuneração, permanência no fluxo produtivo, critérios reputacionais, padrões de priorização, regras de aceitação e mecanismos de sanção.

What is provided, in functional terms, is a private infrastructure of economic possibility.p. 765

PTO que se fornece, em termos funcionais, é uma infraestrutura privada de possibilidade econômica.

The instruments deployed by iFood, 99Food, Uber and 99 insist, with variations in language, on characterizing the partners as independentp. 765

PTOs instrumentos mobilizados por iFood, 99Food, Uber e 99 insistem, com variações de linguagem, em caracterizar os parceiros como independentes

This point is important because it prevents the analysis from being captured by excessively formal narratives about contractual autonomy.p. 765

PTEsse ponto é importante porque impede que a análise seja capturada por narrativas excessivamente formais sobre autonomia contratual.

How do you prove the app's rule changed?

Showing that earnings fell or that the account was blocked once isn't enough. You need to link the loss to the way the app governs the work, with the history of requests, the variations in pay, the alerts, the criteria disclosed or hidden and the records of restriction.

And a drop in earnings isn't always a natural risk of the activity. It may come from a rule change that nobody explained.

Where it comes from

It is not enough to demonstrate that there was a reduction in earnings or an isolated block. It is necessary to reconstruct the relationship between the governance mechanism and the functional impact.p. 768

PTNão basta demonstrar que houve redução de ganhos ou bloqueio pontual. É necessário reconstruir a relação entre mecanismo de governança e impacto funcional.

This may involve the history of requests, variations in pay, the app's internal communications, performance alerts, criteria disclosed or omitted, records of restriction, rating patterns and comparative data on the change in the environment.p. 768

PTIsso pode envolver histórico de chamadas, variações de remuneração, comunicações internas do aplicativo, alertas de desempenho, critérios informados ou omitidos, registros de restrição, padrões de avaliação e dados comparativos sobre a alteração do ambiente.

The first is treating the fluctuation of earnings as a pure natural risk of the activity, when it may result from an opaque unilateral reconfiguration of the distribution infrastructure.p. 768

PTO primeiro é tratar a oscilação de ganho como puro risco natural da atividade, quando ela pode decorrer de reconfiguração unilateral opaca da infraestrutura de distribuição.

What can you ask for when the app changes the rule?

An active account doesn't prove that nothing changed. The question is whether your place in the app is still equivalent to the one that backed your expectation of earning and of staying on.

When the change hollows out that place, you may be able to demand the records, the reasons for the restrictions, a review of the criteria and the restoration of what was degraded. This doesn't turn everything into a labor matter, but it doesn't let a contract that talks about autonomy end the conversation either.

Where it comes from

The legal test cannot be only whether the account remains active. It needs to be whether the partner's functional position in the environment remains substantially equivalent to the one that structured their legitimate expectation of earnings and continuity.p. 767-768

PTO teste jurídico não pode ser apenas se a conta continua ativa. Precisa ser se a posição funcional do parceiro no ambiente permanece substancialmente equivalente àquela que estruturava sua expectativa legítima de ganho e permanência.

The most adequate institutional response will tend to favor preserving and displaying minimum records, duties of verifiable reasoning, review of restriction criteria, intelligibility of the relevant parameters for distributing opportunities and functional restoration of the regime [...]p. 768

PTA resposta institucional mais adequada tenderá a privilegiar preservação e exibição de registros mínimos, deveres de motivação verificável, revisão de critérios de restrição, inteligibilidade sobre parâmetros relevantes de distribuição de oportunidades e recomposição funcional do regime [...]

[...] does not dissolve the subtype into undifferentiated labor law, but neither does it let itself be neutralized by contractual self-descriptions of formal autonomy.p. 768

PT[...] não dissolve o subtipo em trabalhismo indiferenciado, mas também não se deixa neutralizar por autodescrições contratuais de autonomia formal.

What does the platform supply to those who work on it?

A private infrastructure of economic possibility. It organizes access to demand, distribution of requests, predictability of pay, permanence in the productive flow, reputational criteria, prioritization and sanction. The self-description as intermediary and the formal freedom to switch the app on and off do not solve the problem. The examination falls on the supply regime, without automatically reclassifying the relationship under the categories of the employment contract and without reducing it to the dichotomy between autonomy and subordination.

The platform asserts independence at the contractual end and intensifies governance at the operational end.p. 767

PTA plataforma afirma independência na ponta contratual e intensifica governança na ponta operacional.

Where it comes from

What is provided, in functional terms, is a private infrastructure of economic possibility.p. 765

PTO que se fornece, em termos funcionais, é uma infraestrutura privada de possibilidade econômica.

The legally relevant question is not only whether the platform describes itself as an intermediary or whether the partner is formally free to switch the app on and off.p. 766

PTA questão juridicamente relevante não é saber apenas se a plataforma se autodescreve como intermediária ou se o parceiro é formalmente livre para ligar e desligar o aplicativo.

The central point lies not only in asserting algorithmic subordination in the abstract, nor in automatically reclassifying the whole relationship under traditional categories of the employment contract.p. 766

PTO ponto central não está apenas em afirmar subordinação algorítmica em abstrato, nem em reclassificar automaticamente toda a relação sob categorias tradicionais do contrato de trabalho.

That is why the dogmatic problem cannot be reduced to the simplistic dichotomy between autonomy and subordination.p. 767

PTÉ por isso que o problema dogmático não pode ser reduzido à dicotomia simplista entre autonomia e subordinação.

What vulnerability does productive dependence add?

Metrics, incentive messages, quality alerts, promotions, acceptance patterns, pricing dynamics and performance notifications work as a private language for governing conduct. Partners depend on these signs to know their own position, their expected earnings and the risk of restriction or exclusion. The opacity bears on the material living conditions of those who depend on the environment to work.

Changes in request distribution, pricing, eligibility, incentives or reputational weights may keep the account and the program's name while hollowing out the economic utility. The test cannot be only whether the account remains active. It must be whether the partner's functional position remains substantially equivalent.

How is the loss proved?

It is not enough to show a reduction in earnings or an isolated block. The governance mechanism has to be linked to the functional impact through the history of requests, variations in pay, communications from the app, performance alerts, criteria disclosed or omitted, records of restriction, ratings and comparative data. The blind spots are treating the fluctuation of earnings as a natural risk, treating the sanction as an isolated event and accepting the lack of transparency as an inevitable effect of technology.

What consequence is possible?

Abusive practice through structural opacity, qualified informational insufficiency, a defect in the productive supply regime and the unlawfulness of degrading changes without restoration. In borderline cases, especially when mutability, metrics, sanctions and restrictions reveal a stable program of normalizing losses and capturing conduct, there may be room for a reading in terms of abusive teleology. The response tends to favor preserving and displaying records, verifiable reasoning, review of restriction criteria, intelligibility about how opportunities are distributed and functional restoration. Applying this framework does not dissolve the case into undifferentiated labor law.
