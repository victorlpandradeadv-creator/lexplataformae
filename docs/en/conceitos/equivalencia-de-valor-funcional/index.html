What about when an update takes away what you bought?Functional value equivalence

Digital services change all the time, and that is normal. A game needs a new graphics engine, balance adjustments, compatibility with different computers.

But sometimes, after an update, the item is still there and no longer serves what it used to. It lost a function, lost the place it held among the other players or lost the meaning it had for the buyer. You still have the account, still see the item and still log into the game, and even so you lost the main thing.

Where it comes from

For this reason, functional value equivalence is a criterion of stability of the performance program, not a requirement of immobility of the object. It allows recognizing the sophisticated obvious: continuous digital services change, but they cannot change in just any wayp. 655

PTPor isso, a equivalência de valor funcional é critério de estabilidade do programa de prestação, não exigência de imobilidade do objeto. Ela permite reconhecer o óbvio sofisticado: serviços digitais contínuos mudam, mas não podem mudar de qualquer jeito

The consumer remains formally “inside” the ecosystem, still has the account, still sees the object, can still access the platform. Even so, the enjoyed utility may be substantially downgradedp. 635

PTO consumidor continua formalmente “dentro” do ecossistema, continua com a conta, continua vendo o objeto, continua podendo acessar a plataforma. Ainda assim, a utilidade fruída pode ser substancialmente rebaixada

The consumer does not lose merely "a feature"; they lose functional substance of what they boughtp. 672

PTO consumidor não perde apenas "uma feature"; perde substância funcional do que comprou

Functional value equivalence

It is the test that asks whether, after the change, the product still plays the same role in your experience. The test is demanding. It is not enough for the item to keep working, nor to keep the same numbers in some isolated attribute. What counts is the whole role it had. This includes its concrete utility in the game, the place it holds among the other users and, when this matters to the law, what it means to whoever has it. An item may remain in the account and open normally, and still have lost what justified the purchase.

Where it comes from

What matters is the substantial preservation of the role played by the object in the acquired experience, considering its concrete utility in the ecosystem, its relational insertion in the environment of use and, when legally relevant, its identity or symbolic density.p. 729

PTO que importa é a preservação substancial do papel exercido pelo objeto na experiência adquirida, considerando sua utilidade concreta no ecossistema, sua inserção relacional no ambiente de uso e, quando juridicamente relevante, sua densidade identitária ou simbólica.

Functional value equivalence is not reduced to the maintenance of technical performance in the strict sense, nor to the quantitative preservation of isolated attributes. Its content is more demanding.p. 729

PTA equivalência de valor funcional não se reduz à manutenção de desempenho técnico em sentido estrito, nem à preservação quantitativa de atributos isolados. Seu conteúdo é mais exigente.

The point is to affirm that the lawfulness of the change cannot be assessed merely by the nominal subsistence of the object, by the formal maintenance of access or by the abstract persistence of some residual utility.p. 795

PTO ponto é afirmar que a licitude da alteração não pode ser aferida apenas pela subsistência nominal do objeto, pela manutenção formal de acesso ou pela persistência abstrata de alguma utilidade residual.

Paying for the whole semester for half a course?

A course that, in the middle of the semester, cuts half the classes and keeps charging the same price does what anyone would find wrong. Consumer law considers void the clause that lets the supplier change alone the content or the quality of what it sells.

On platforms, the cut rarely comes with that name. It appears as smaller reach, performance downgraded little by little, features that keep disappearing, tighter limits or use that gets more expensive to yield the same thing. The label and the price stay the same, while the delivery shrinks. The logic is the same as the course's.

Where it comes from

If continuity is the normal regime, the enjoyed utility stabilizes as an expectation that the level of delivery will be maintained, and not as an expectation of formal access to a service that can be reconfigured at the supplier's convenience. That is why the CDC treats unilateral modification of content or quality as nullity.p. 479

PTSe a continuidade é o regime normal, a utilidade fruída se estabiliza como expectativa de manutenção do patamar de entrega, e não como expectativa de acesso formal a um serviço que pode ser reconfigurado por conveniência do fornecedor. Por isso, o CDC trata a modificação unilateral de conteúdo ou qualidade como nulidade.

The example he offers, of the supplier that reduces classes while keeping the same price Nunes(Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]), works as an objective yardstick for platforms.p. 480

PTO exemplo que ele oferece, do fornecedor que reduz aulas mantendo o mesmo preço (Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]) , funciona como régua objetiva para plataformas.

The reduction rarely appears as an explicit cut of “classes”, but it may manifest as loss of reach, persistent downgrading of performance, cumulative restriction of functionalities, compression of limits, reordering of visibility or structural changes that raise the cost of enjoying the same utility. The logic is the same. The label and the price are kept, and the delivery of value is downgraded.p. 480

PTA redução raramente aparece como corte explícito de “aulas”, mas pode se manifestar como perda de alcance, rebaixamento persistente de performance, restrição cumulativa de funcionalidades, compressão de limites, reordenação de visibilidade ou alterações estruturais que elevam o custo de fruir a mesma utilidade. A lógica é a mesma. Mantêm-se o rótulo e o preço, e rebaixa-se a entrega de valor.

How do you compare the before and the after?

To run the test, the before and the after are compared. The versions that matter are examined and the center of the utility that was affected is identified. Then the object's role in the experience is assessed, looking at the utility, the place it holds among the others and, when it matters, what it means.

Some questions help. Did the update take away an important function? Did it weaken the item to the point that it lost the utility you paid to have? Did it change the screen in a way that messes up everyday use? Did it redefine rules so as to downgrade what had been promised as equivalent? If the answer is yes, the platform could change, but it should have preserved the value of what you had or opened room for a restoration. And you do not need to prove you are the full owner of the item to complain about it.

Where it comes from

The minimum signal of proof requires a functional comparison between before and after, examination of relevant versions, identification of the affected core of utility and assessment of the object's role in the experiencep. 672

PTO sinal mínimo de prova exige comparação funcional entre o antes e o depois, exame de versões relevantes, identificação do núcleo de utilidade afetado e avaliação do papel do objeto na experiência

The strength of the criterion appears sharply in cases such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that empty acquired utility, interface changes that disrupt ordinary use, redefinitions of rules that downgrade the equivalence of what was promisedp. 637

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida, mudanças de interface que desorganizam o uso ordinário, redefinições de regras que rebaixam a equivalência do que foi prometido

Functional value equivalence allows, precisely, a much more robust intermediate position: it is recognized that the platform may alter, but it is required that the alteration preserve, in substantial terms, the functional value of the acquired experience or, failing that, open room for restoration, correction, review or another adequate remedyp. 637

PTA equivalência de valor funcional permite, justamente, uma posição intermediária muito mais robusta: reconhece-se que a plataforma pode alterar, mas exige-se que a alteração preserve, em termos substanciais, o valor funcional da experiência adquirida ou, não o fazendo, abra espaço a recomposição, correção, revisão ou outro remédio adequado

It is not necessary to demonstrate full dominion over the object to maintain that the acquired enjoyment cannot be arbitrarily downgraded.p. 637

PTNão é preciso demonstrar domínio pleno sobre o objeto para sustentar que a fruição adquirida não pode ser rebaixada arbitrariamente.

When does a phone update stop being an improvement?

A phone update can, in the name of improvement, make the device slow or incompatible with apps, still within the lifespan expected of it. No one serious says that every update is illegal.

The question is another one. It is knowing whether the update significantly compromised use, without adequate information, without real consent and without a way out for those who were harmed. With a digital item, the structure is the same.

Where it comes from

The example of updates that degrade accessibility and compatibility in software or firmware helps to visualize the structure of the problem. In these cases, no one seriously argues that every update is unlawful.p. 655

PTO exemplo de atualizações que degradam acessibilidade e compatibilidade em software ou firmware ajuda a visualizar a estrutura do problema. Nesses casos, ninguém discute seriamente que toda atualização seja ilícita.

What is discussed is whether, under the pretext of improvement, the update significantly compromises the use, performance or compatibility of goods still within their expected durability, without adequate information, without effective consent and without mitigating avenuesp. 655

PTO que se discute é se, sob o pretexto de melhoria, a atualização compromete de modo relevante o uso, o desempenho ou a compatibilidade de bens ainda dentro da durabilidade esperada, sem informação adequada, sem consentimento efetivo e sem vias mitigatórias

The legal structure is the same in IOCsp. 655

PTA estrutura jurídica é a mesma em OICs

The item still exists, but is it the same one you bought?

An object may continue to exist and, even so, no longer be what was bought. It is this loss that the test makes visible.

And it changes the conversation when the company says it was just an update, an adjustment or an evolution of the service. From then on, what matters is showing that the essence of what you bought was preserved, and not the size of the technical complexity. When equivalence is lost, the first answer is to give the function back, undoing the change when possible, replacing it with something equivalent or correcting the way of updating, and not just paying.

Where it comes from

An object may continue to exist and, even so, no longer be, in a legally relevant sense, what was acquired.p. 795

PTUm objeto pode continuar existindo e, ainda assim, já não ser, em sentido jurídico relevante, aquilo que foi adquirido.

Whenever the platform invokes “update”, “adjustment” or “evolution of the service” to justify persistent changes, the core of the debate must fall on the demonstration of substantial preservation of the enjoyment bundle, and not on the rhetoric of complexity.p. 480

PTSempre que a plataforma invoca “atualização”, “ajuste” ou “evolução do serviço” para justificar alterações persistentes, o núcleo do debate deve recair sobre a demonstração de preservação substancial do feixe de fruição, e não sobre a retórica da complexidade.

The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent replacement and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional possível prioriza recomposição funcional, reversão técnica quando viável, substituição equivalente e correção da forma de atualização, em vez de mera monetização automática da perda.

So is changing illegal?

Changing is not illegal. The test does not freeze the service nor require the new version to be identical to the old one. Digital environments need to evolve, adapt and, often, rebalance.

What it refuses is change used to throw onto you the cost of seriously worsening what you bought, without a restoration to match. And, if the change is only in appearance and the symbolic value was not part of the center of what was sold, as a rule it stays out.

Where it comes from

Mutability, in itself, is not unlawful.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita.

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service. Continuous digital environments presuppose evolution, adaptation and, in many cases, internal rebalancing.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço. Ambientes digitais contínuos pressupõem evolução, adaptação e, em muitos casos, reequilíbrio interno.

What the parameter rejects is not change itself, but its instrumentalization to transfer to the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration.p. 730

PTO que o parâmetro recusa não é a mudança em si, mas sua instrumentalização para transferir ao consumidor o custo de reconfigurações que destroem ou rebaixam significativamente a utilidade adquirida sem recomposição compatível.

Therefore, as a rule, predominantly aesthetic changes fall outside the reach of the conceptp. 434

PTPor isso, em regra, ficam fora do alcance do conceito alterações predominantemente estéticas

When does the update take away the function of what you bought?

When the platform changes the experience in a way that truly worsens, empties or diverts the function that led you to buy. It is the item that, after an adjustment, remains in the inventory but became so weak that it lost its reason to exist, or the feature that let you do something and was removed.

You may remain inside the game, with the account, seeing the item and logging in whenever you want, and still have lost the main thing. Keeping it working is not the same as keeping its value.

What must the platform preserve in the item?

Preserving, in what is substantial, the role the item had in your experience. This includes what it does, the relationship it creates with other players and, when this matters for the law, what it means.

How to show what changed in the item's utility?

What utility the item had before, what changed, what the concrete impact of the change was and why the compensation offered, if any, is not enough.

What solution can restore the lost utility?

First, that the function come back. This may mean undoing the change when technically possible, replacing the item with something equivalent or correcting the way the update was made. Money should not be the automatic answer. Depending on the case, the loss may be treated as a defect in the quality of the product or service, which is the name the law gives to the problem that makes something less useful than it should be.

Is the game forbidden to change?

The platform can update the game, and nobody demands that the item stay identical forever nor that the game stand still in time. Many changes are, in fact, the natural evolution of the environment. The problem is the change that throws onto you the cost of seriously worsening what you bought, without a fitting restoration.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform alters the experience so as to substantially degrade, empty or divert the function that justified the acquisition, there will be a rupture of functional value equivalence.p. 745

PTSempre que a plataforma alterar a experiência de modo a degradar, esvaziar ou desviar substancialmente a função que justificou a aquisição, haverá ruptura da equivalência de valor funcional.

The strength of the criterion appears sharply in cases such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that empty acquired utilityp. 637

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida

The consumer remains formally “inside” the ecosystem, still has the account, still sees the object, can still access the platform. Even so, the enjoyed utility may be substantially downgradedp. 635

PTO consumidor continua formalmente “dentro” do ecossistema, continua com a conta, continua vendo o objeto, continua podendo acessar a plataforma. Ainda assim, a utilidade fruída pode ser substancialmente rebaixada

The corresponding duty of supply is to preserve, in substantial terms, the functional, relational and, when legally relevant, symbolic role of the object in the consumer's experience.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de preservar, em termos substanciais, o papel funcional, relacional e, quando juridicamente relevante, simbólico do objeto na experiência do consumidor.

The minimum required proof calls for demonstration of the utility previously incorporated into the bond, of the change that occurred, of the concrete functional impact of the alteration and of the insufficiency of any restoration offered.p. 745-746

PTA prova mínima exigível requer demonstração da utilidade anteriormente incorporada ao vínculo, da mudança ocorrida, do impacto funcional concreto da alteração e da insuficiência de eventual recomposição oferecida.

The possible legal qualification tends to fall on a defect in the quality of the product or servicep. 746

PTA qualificação jurídica possível tende a recair em vício de qualidade do produto ou serviço

The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent replacement and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional possível prioriza recomposição funcional, reversão técnica quando viável, substituição equivalente e correção da forma de atualização, em vez de mera monetização automática da perda.

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between the earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.

What the parameter rejects is not change itself, but its instrumentalization to transfer to the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration.p. 730

PTO que o parâmetro recusa não é a mudança em si, mas sua instrumentalização para transferir ao consumidor o custo de reconfigurações que destroem ou rebaixam significativamente a utilidade adquirida sem recomposição compatível.

Nerfs, removals, reworks, migrations, rule changes, reordering of access, reconfiguration of interfaces and suppression of functionalities are usually narrated as the natural evolution of the environment. In many cases, they may in fact be so.p. 730

PTNerfs, remoções, reworks, migrações, mudanças de regra, reordenação de acessos, reconfiguração de interfaces e supressão de funcionalidades costumam ser narrados como evolução natural do ambiente. Em muitos casos, de fato, podem sê-lo.

What does functional value equivalence control?

It controls the lawfulness of unilateral alterations in continuous digital services. It is the material core of the control of mutability and also guides the remedy. What is preserved is the role the object played in the acquired experience, considering its concrete utility in the environment, its relational insertion in use and, when legally relevant, its identity or symbolic density.

An object may continue to exist and, even so, no longer be, in a legally relevant sense, what was acquired.p. 795

PTUm objeto pode continuar existindo e, ainda assim, já não ser, em sentido jurídico relevante, aquilo que foi adquirido.

Where it comes from

It also serves to guide remedies.p. 731

PTServe também para orientar remédios.

Functional value equivalence operates as a criterion of lawfulness of the unilateral alterations carried out in continuous digital services that administer Immaterial Objects of Consumption.p. 648

PTA equivalência de valor funcional opera como critério de licitude das alterações unilaterais promovidas em serviços digitais contínuos que administram Objetos Imateriais de Consumo.

Functional value equivalence, therefore, constitutes the material core of the control of mutability in immaterial objects of consumption.p. 731

PTA equivalência de valor funcional, portanto, constitui o núcleo material de controle da mutabilidade em objetos imateriais de consumo.

What matters is the substantial preservation of the role played by the object in the acquired experience, considering its concrete utility in the ecosystem, its relational insertion in the environment of use and, when legally relevant, its identity or symbolic density.p. 729

PTO que importa é a preservação substancial do papel exercido pelo objeto na experiência adquirida, considerando sua utilidade concreta no ecossistema, sua inserção relacional no ambiente de uso e, quando juridicamente relevante, sua densidade identitária ou simbólica.

What does it not prohibit?

Change, by itself, is not unlawful. The criterion does not require perfect identity between the earlier and the later version, nor the freezing of the service. Mutability that preserves, in substantial terms, the function of the object in the consumer's experience is legitimate.

Where it comes from

Mutability, in itself, is not unlawful.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita.

From this perspective, legally legitimate mutability is that which preserves, in substantial terms, the function of the object in the consumer's experience. This does not mean requiring perfect identity between earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTSob essa perspectiva, a mutabilidade juridicamente legítima é aquela que preserva, em termos substanciais, a função do objeto na experiência do consumidor. Isso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.
