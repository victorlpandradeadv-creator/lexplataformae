The jurist's path

Part one of two

Why technology changes the legal question

The defense that the loss was simply how the software works treats as nature what, in a governed digital environment, is a design choice someone made. The philosophy of technique, of media and of design brings these choices back into the field of attribution, and each of its authors changes the question the jurist puts to the case.

Why is saying it was just an update not enough to close the case?

The conflict this chapter prepares begins with a familiar answer. The consumer buys an item, a subscription or access, what it offers later changes, and the supplier explains that there was a system change, a normal update or an unavoidable service event. These expressions only sound like an answer as long as consumption is read through a strictly material ontology, and that is why the background against which the relationship unfolds is described first, before formulating the categories capable of making these losses attributable and controllable. Against that background, the experience takes the form of permanence in a governed environment, where the interface organizes access, induces choices, limits alternatives and distributes rewards or sanctions.

Contemporary digital consumption can no longer be adequately understood as the simple acquisition of a thing or the contracting of a one-off service.p. 83

PTO consumo digital contemporâneo já não se deixa compreender adequadamente como simples aquisição de coisa ou contratação de serviço pontual.

Where it comes from

When technique operates as environment and the platform operates as market architecture, the consumer relationship comes to be traversed by regimes of access, data, interface and technical and legal exclusivities that condition delivery, enjoyment and the effective space of choice. It is this background that sustains the formulation of categories able to render attributable and controllable those losses and asymmetries that, under a strictly material ontology of consumption, would tend to appear as “system change”, “normal update” or “unavoidable service event”.p. 85

PTQuando a técnica opera como ambiente e a plataforma opera como arquitetura de mercado, a relação de consumo passa a ser atravessada por regimes de acesso, dados, interface e exclusividades técnicas e jurídicas que condicionam a entrega, a fruição e o espaço efetivo de escolha. É esse pano de fundo que sustenta a formulação de categorias aptas a tornar imputáveis e controláveis perdas e assimetrias que, sob uma ontologia estritamente material do consumo, tenderiam a aparecer como “mudança de sistema”, “atualização normal” ou “evento inevitável de serviço”.

In many ecosystems, the experience takes the form of permanence in a governed environment, in which the interface organizes access, induces choices, limits alternatives and distributes rewards or sanctions.p. 83

PTEm muitos ecossistemas, a experiência assume a forma de permanência em ambiente governado, no qual a interface organiza acesso, induz escolhas, limita alternativas e distribui recompensas ou sanções.

The platform, in this picture, does not merely put buyers and sellers in contact. It organizes markets, administers access, structures dependencies and converts circulation into continuous value extraction, and infrastructure, data, interface and operating rules come to compose a single arrangement, in which performance is prolonged and the bond is renewed through frictions and incentives. Economic rationality, therefore, materializes in design, curation and execution protocols, and the practical normativity of the environment does not derive only from texts, contracts or explicit commands. The decisive turn occurs when mediation ceases to be perceived as a neutral channel and comes to operate as a form of organizing experience, so that technique and media frame the possible, define the relevant and stabilize certain conducts as normal through repetition, convenience and design.

This creates a precise difficulty for the jurist. In continuous digital services, the supplier usually treats the modulation of what can be enjoyed as an ordinary operation of the system, while the consumer forms expectations of enjoyment and belonging from what was promised, acquired and experienced. This distance produces qualificatory opacity, which makes it hard to name the injury, identify the affected object and bring the loss back to the traditional categories of adequacy, quality, defect and information. Why does Consumer Law draw on authors from outside law?

This background is organized in three conceptual sets, each with its own function, precisely so that it is not reduced to a sociological impression. The first, which this chapter covers, reconstructs the passage of technique and media from tools to environments of life, with Jacques Ellul, Neil Postman and Willem H. Vanderburg in the diagnosis and Andrew Feenberg in a critical vein that rules out determinism and preserves the controllability of technical choices. The second reconstructs the political economy of platforms as an arrangement of extraction, toll and dependence, with Yanis Varoufakis and Ladislau Dowbor, in dialogue with categories of Karl Marx. The third bridges political economy and the semiotics of consumption through the anthropology of Mary Douglas, Baron Isherwood, Arjun Appadurai, Igor Kopytoff and Grant McCracken, and the last two are left for other chapters.

What changes when technique stops being a tool and becomes an environment?

Ellul describes modern technique as a system of rationalization that ceases to be an instrument subordinate to external ends and comes to condition practices, expectations and institutions. For the jurist, what counts is the effect of this rationality on the form of the debate. In whatever domain it is applied, technique presents itself as a rational process and turns the social problem into a problem of method, so that alternatives come to be filtered by what can be rationalized, measured and operationalized. When the automatism of technical choice sets in, the question of what should be preserved gives way to the question of what best keeps the system running.

Where it comes from

This logic appears, on a smaller scale, in platform-operated games. How do missions, passes and deadlines organize the player's time?

Technique incorporated into routines appears clearly in missions and passes, which organize the player's time as a sequence of tasks with their own inputs, outputs and accounting. The game remains, in appearance, a world of fantasy and competition, but it comes to be traversed by a second regime of orientation, quieter and more constant, in which what counts is meeting progress criteria, maintaining recurrence, not missing reward windows and converting time into currency in the most efficient way. PTA Riot é precisa ao definir que suas missões reconhecem ao jogador especificamente 4 pontos por minuto jogado, ou 6 pontos por minuto caso vença a partida (Riot Games, 2021b) .

Where it comes from

In this kind of organization, technique is not merely a means, because it becomes agenda, calendar and criterion of everyday rationality, including in the form of prospective loss, when institutional communication warns that certain items “will be lost forever” if not spent by the deadline Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024b).p. 115

Nesse tipo de organização, a técnica não é só meio, pois se torna pauta, calendário e critério de racionalidade cotidiana, inclusive sob a forma de perda prospectiva, quando a comunicação institucional avisa que certos itens “serão perdidos para sempre” caso não sejam gastos no prazo (Riot Games, 2024b) .

In the Terms of Service, Riot expressly states that the “limited license” to access virtual currency and content may be obtained not only by purchase, but also by earning, including by completing “missions or game tasks” Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). The same contractual block also cuts off at the root any proprietary reading of “unlocking”, by stating that the consumer does not become the owner of the content, because “what you ‘unlock’ is not the virtual product itself, but a limited non-transferable license to access it” Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e).p. 116

PTNos Termos de Serviço, a Riot descreve expressamente que a “licença limitada” para acessar moeda e conteúdo virtual pode ser obtida não apenas por compra, mas também por ganho, incluindo o cumprimento de “missões ou tarefas de jogo” (Riot Games, 2024e) . O mesmo bloco contratual ainda corta, na raiz, qualquer leitura proprietária do “desbloqueio”, ao formular que o consumidor não se torna proprietário do conteúdo, pois “o que você ‘desbloqueia’ não é o produto virtual em si, mas uma licença limitada intransferível para acessá-lo” (Riot Games, 2024e) .

By turning progression into calculation, and reward into a regime of goals, the ecosystem produces an enjoyment that presents itself as fun but operates as a technically administered routine, in which access to content and currencies is organized as a temporal toll.p. 116

PTAo transformar progressão em cálculo, e ao transformar recompensa em regime de metas, o ecossistema produz uma fruição que se apresenta como diversão, mas opera como rotina tecnicamente administrada, na qual o acesso a conteúdo e a moedas se organiza como pedágio temporal.

Skins and passes cease to be mere “extra content” and come to form part, as form, of the very regime of licensed access, in which time invested is converted into a route of acquisition foreseen in the institutional design of the service.p. 116

PTSkins e passes deixam de ser mero “conteúdo extra” e passam a integrar, como forma, o próprio regime de acesso licenciado, no qual o tempo investido é convertido em via de aquisição prevista no desenho institucional do serviço.

With the points metric too, any reading in terms of the platform's psychological intent is ruled out, and what operates is practical rationality, which keeps the analysis on the environment, and not on the disposition of whoever plays or whoever designed the game.

Where it comes from

PTEssa leitura não depende de sugerir que o consumidor seja incapaz de distinguir jogo e trabalho, nem de reduzir a experiência a “vício” ou “fraqueza”. O ponto é estrutural e está na forma.

PTO ponto, aqui, não é moralizar o jogo, mas reconhecer que esse tipo de ambiente educa atenção e relevância por repetição, e tende a aproximar experiência de desempenho, reforçando o empobrecimento simbólico descrito por Vanderburg.

PTNão se trata de intenção psicológica da plataforma, mas de uma racionalidade prática, na qual a rotina do lazer é reconfigurada como cálculo de eficiência entre tempo e recompensa, instrumentalizada como incentivo estrutural à permanência.

The same diagnosis reaches advertising. For Vanderburg, advertising comes to operate less through information about the object and more through the manufacture of a symbolic shortcut that links the good to a lifestyle, and ads become poorer in informative content while growing more sophisticated in associative engineering. The mechanism itself points beyond the message, because, when desymbolization weakens internal orientation, steering ceases to depend on explicit persuasion and comes to operate by design, through interface choices, operating arrangements, interaction patterns and forms of governance that structure the experience before any message. Where it comes from

Advertising tends to operate less through information about the object and more through the manufacture of a symbolic shortcut that connects the advertised good to a lifestyle and transfers to the purchase the promise of belonging, recognition and fulfillment. In this register, ads become poorer in informative content and more sophisticated in associative engineering, consolidating in the consumer's mind an equivalence between commodity and desirable symbol Vanderburg(Vanderburg, 2011, p. 204).p. 118

PTA publicidade tende a operar menos por informação sobre o objeto e mais pela fabricação de um atalho simbólico que conecta o bem anunciado a um estilo de vida e transfere para a compra a promessa de pertencimento, reconhecimento e realização. Nesse registro, anúncios se empobrecem em conteúdo informativo e se sofisticam em engenharia associativa, consolidando na mente do consumidor uma equivalência entre mercadoria e símbolo desejável (Vanderburg, 2011, p. 204) .

Taken together, the diagnosis shifts the focus from content to form. The decisive point is not only what advertising says, but how a technical-organizational system positions the subject within routines of stimulus, reward, visibility and comparison, making certain paths more probable and others almost impracticable.p. 119-120

PTTomado em conjunto, o diagnóstico desloca o foco do conteúdo para a forma. O ponto decisivo não é apenas o que a publicidade diz, mas como um sistema técnico-organizacional posiciona o sujeito dentro de rotinas de estímulo, recompensa, visibilidade e comparação, tornando certos caminhos mais prováveis e outros quase impraticáveis.

Why is a patch a decision, and not an accident of the object?

Feenberg comes in to prevent the environment described by Ellul, Postman and Vanderburg from being read as destiny, and with him technique comes to be examined as the institutional result of choices incorporated into artifacts, systems and governance routines. In games sold as a continuous service, known as games-as-a-service, patches (updates the platform publishes to alter the state of the service), reworks (broad redesigns of a character or a mechanic), changes to visual effects and adjustments to the internal economy do not function only as maintenance. They rewrite the conditions of permanence, equivalence and recognition in the ecosystem. Feenberg's reading allows us to ask about choices, purposes and responsibilities where the supplier tends to present only the natural evolution of the platform or the game.

In digital environments, especially in games-as-a-service, the relevant changes are not natural accidents of the object, but architectural decisions that redefine what it is, how much it is worth and how it can be enjoyed.p. 120

PTEm ambientes digitais, especialmente nos games-as-a-service, as mudanças relevantes não são acidentes naturais do objeto, mas decisões de arquitetura que redefinem o que ele é, quanto vale e como pode ser fruído.

Where it comes from

Ellul, Postman and Vanderburg allow us to understand the techno-media order as an ambience that reorganizes everyday life, and Feenberg prevents this ambience from being read as destiny. Technique comes to be examined as the institutional result of choices incorporated into artifacts, systems and governance routines.p. 120

PTEllul, Postman e Vanderburg permitem compreender a ordem técnico-midiática como ambiência de reorganização do cotidiano e Feenberg impede que essa ambiência seja lida como destino. A técnica passa a ser examinada como resultado institucional de escolhas incorporadas a artefatos, sistemas e rotinas de governança.

The term “patch” is used in the technical sense of an update published by the platform that alters the operational state of the service (fixes, balance adjustments, changes to rules, content and parameters).p. 121

PTO termo “patch” é utilizado no sentido técnico de atualização publicada pela plataforma que altera o estado operativo do serviço (correções, ajustes de balanceamento, mudanças de regras, conteúdo e parâmetros).

“Rework” describes a broad intervention of functional and structural redesign in a game element (character, system or mechanic), which goes beyond incremental adjustments (balance) and one-off updates.p. 105

PT“Rework” descreve uma intervenção ampla de redesenho funcional e estrutural em um elemento do jogo (personagem, sistema ou mecânica), que excede ajustes incrementais (balance) e atualizações pontuais.

Patches, reworks, changes to visual effects and adjustments to the internal economy do not function only as technical maintenance; they rewrite conditions of permanence, equivalence and recognition within the ecosystem. Feenberg's entry, at this point, allows this modulation to be treated as social rationality incorporated into the infrastructure, making it possible to ask about choices, purposes and responsibilities where the supplier tends to present only the “natural evolution” of the platform or the game.p. 120

PTPatches, reworks, alterações de efeitos visuais e ajustes de economia interna não funcionam apenas como manutenção técnica; reescrevem condições de permanência, equivalência e reconhecimento dentro do ecossistema. A entrada de Feenberg, nesse ponto, permite tratar essa modulação como racionalidade social incorporada à infraestrutura, tornando possível perguntar por escolhas, finalidades e responsabilidades onde o fornecedor tende a apresentar apenas “evolução natural” da plataforma ou do jogo.

What is presented to the consumer as a patch, balancing or conduct policy operates, in practice, as incorporated normativity, because it defines what is worth something, what appears, what gives status, what loses value and, through disciplinary mechanisms that may extend to banning, who remains authorized to participate. Feenberg allows us to treat this set as political rationality materialized in a system, which makes knowable the teleology inscribed in the interface, the rules and the architecture of sanction, instead of reducing the conflict to inevitable technical variation.

Platforms become legally relevant not only for what they “say” in terms of use, but for what they “do” in their architectures.p. 124

PTPlataformas se tornam juridicamente relevantes não só pelo que “dizem” em termos de uso, mas pelo que “fazem” em suas arquiteturas.

Where it comes from

Feenberg's most productive concept for the jurist is the technosystem, the integrated field of technically rational disciplines and operations associated with markets, administrations and technologies, which cease to be autonomous spheres that occasionally influence one another. Applied to digital consumption, it prevents the platform from being read as a mere technical performance. The platform operates at the same time as market, in its models of extraction and monetization, as administration, in private governance and in enforcement, which is the application and execution of rules by the platform itself, including through automated mechanisms, and as technology, in the architecture of mediation. The dogmatic consequence is that supply comes to be seen as an integrated sociotechnical arrangement, and the product of Article 3, paragraph 1, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC)1 cannot be isolated from the regime of visibility, accessibility and modulation that constitutes it. Immaterial consumption appears as governed enjoyment, and the consumer experiences at the same time content and the technical and organizational conditions of access to it, which operate by functioning criteria and are not exhausted by the traditional duty of information.

Where it comes from

Instead of treating “technology”, “market” and “administration” as autonomous spheres that occasionally influence one another, the technosystem describes an integrated field of technically rational disciplines and operations associated with these three domains Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 16).p. 124

PTEm vez de tratar “tecnologia”, “mercado” e “administração” como esferas autônomas que ocasionalmente se influenciam, o tecnossistema descreve um campo integrado de disciplinas e operações tecnicamente racionais associado a esses três domínios (Feenberg, 2017, p. 16) .

PT[...] digital, isso impede que a plataforma seja lida como mera prestação técnica, porque ela opera simultaneamente como mercado (modelos de extração e monetização), como administração (governança privada e enforcement) e como tecnologia (arquitetura de mediação).

Usual corporate term for procedures of application and execution of rules by the platform, including through automated mechanisms.p. 125

PTTermo corporativo usual para se referir a procedimentos de aplicação e execução de regras pela plataforma, inclusive por mecanismos automatizados.

The dogmatic relevance of this framing is clear. Supply ceases to be understood as a simple performance and comes to be seen as an integrated sociotechnical arrangement, in which what the consumer receives is inseparable from the way access, permanence, modulation and loss are administered by technically rational operations Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 16).p. 125

PTA relevância dogmática desse enquadramento é clara. O fornecimento deixa de ser compreendido como simples prestação e passa a ser visto como arranjo sociotécnico integrado, no qual aquilo que o consumidor recebe é inseparável do modo como acesso, permanência, modulação e perda são administrados por operações tecnicamente racionais (Feenberg, 2017, p. 16) .

If the consumed experience depends on continuous mediations and if these mediations are entangled in markets and administrations, then the “product” (as defined under Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC2) cannot be isolated from the regime of visibility, accessibility and modulation that constitutes it Feenberg(Feenberg, 2017, p. 16). Immaterial consumption appears, here, as governed enjoyment. PTSe a experiência consumida depende de mediações contínuas e se essas mediações estão enredadas em mercados e administrações, então o “produto” (conforme definido sob o art. 3º, §1º do CDC ) não pode ser isolado do regime de visibilidade, acessibilidade e modulação que o constitui (Feenberg, 2017, p. 16) . O consumo imaterial aparece, aqui, como fruição governada. O consumidor experimenta simultaneamente um conteúdo e as condições técnicas e organizacionais de acesso a esse conteúdo, condições que operam por critérios de funcionamento e não se esgotam no dever tradicional de informação.
