Your rights · By problem

The records are with the company. How do I prove what happened?

In most disputes with platforms, it is the company, not you, that keeps the records of what happened. That is why the work of proving can be divided according to what each side can access, though you still need to give a coherent account of what you went through.

Where it comes from

In disputes over IOCs, this is precisely the normal configuration: logs, interface trails, change histories, sanction criteria, internal support communications and relevant technical records are concentrated with the supplier.p. 659

PTEm litígios sobre OICs, essa é precisamente a configuração normal: logs, trilhas de interface, históricos de alteração, critérios de sanção, comunicações internas de suporte e registros técnicos relevantes ficam concentrados no fornecedor.

When the platform manages the experience and simultaneously concentrates the traces needed to prove it, the preservation of these elements ceases to be a peripheral question of cooperation and comes to constitute a condition of institutional controllability. The consequence is the admissibility of a dynamic distribution of the burden of proof whenever the relevant evidence is structurally under the supplier's technical control.p. 735

PTQuando a plataforma administra a experiência e simultaneamente concentra os vestígios necessários à sua prova, a preservação desses elementos deixa de ser questão periférica de cooperação e passa a constituir condição de controlabilidade institucional. A consequência disso é a admissibilidade de distribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório sempre que a prova relevante estiver estruturalmente sob controle técnico do fornecedor.

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to require from the consumer a detailed demonstration of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach. This does not eliminate the need for plausibility, argumentative sequencing or delimitation of the disputed object.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance. Isso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido.

How do you know the lack of proof is not your fault?

It happens when the punishment was automatic, the change left no public trace, the purchase screen has already changed or the history disappeared from the account. In these cases the lack of proof may be an effect of the way the service is organized, and not a weakness of your claim.

The company knows what it showed you, when it showed it, which version was in force, what transaction happened and what rule it used in a punishment. When all of this stays with it alone, whoever controls the service also comes to control the proof.

Where it comes from

In environments of governed enjoyment, verifiability is part of the very duty to shape the relationship legitimately.p. 735

PTEm ambientes de fruição governada, a verificabilidade integra o próprio dever de conformação legítima do vínculo.

The formula “you have not proved it” becomes institutionally empty when one does not examine who controlled the conditions for producing, preserving and exhibiting what was supposed to be proved.p. 735-736

PTA fórmula “você não provou” torna-se institucionalmente vazia quando não se examina quem controlava as condições de produção, preservação e exibição daquilo que deveria ser provado.

In IOCs, the supplier controls not only the object and the environment; it also controls the institutional memory of supply. It knows what was shown, when it was shown, how it was altered, which version was in force, which interface trail led to the contract, which parameter was applied in a sanction, which transaction occurred and in what technical context. When all these elements remain under the platform's monopoly, governance of the service also becomes governance of proof.p. 658

PTEm OICs, o fornecedor não controla apenas o objeto e o ambiente; controla também a memória institucional do fornecimento. Sabe o que foi mostrado, quando foi mostrado, como foi alterado, qual versão estava vigente, que trilha de interface conduziu à contratação, qual parâmetro foi aplicado numa sanção, que transação ocorreu e em que contexto técnico. Quando todos esses elementos permanecem sob monopólio da plataforma, a governança do serviço torna-se também governança da prova.

What is within your reach and worth keeping right now?

Even without the company's records, a lot is within your reach. Dated screenshots, screen recordings, the purchase and confirmation screens and the messages about rarity, remaining time, discount or change show how the environment was set up.

It also helps to keep what was promised, such as ads, posts and notices, to compare with what you received later. What counts is not your memory, but the form the screen had when you went through it.

This material does not need to prove everything on its own. It serves to show that your account is plausible and how it fits into the service, and that is what allows you to ask the company for the rest.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

The advertising and institutional promise is compared with the enjoyment actually delivered and with its later changes.p. 607

PTCompara-se a promessa publicitária e institucional com a fruição efetivamente entregue e com suas alterações posteriores.

The first source of support is interface and decision flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, time remaining, discount, event or alteration. What matters there is not the user's subjective memory, but the architecture actually presented.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração. O relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário, mas a arquitetura efetivamente apresentada.

The minimum required proof, from the consumer's side, consists of a plausible account of the harmful experience, of how it fits into the ecosystem and of the accessible contextual elementsp. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível, do ponto de vista do consumidor, consiste em apresentação plausível da experiência lesiva, de sua inserção no ecossistema e dos elementos de contexto acessíveis

How do you ask for the records without making the request too vague?

The request works better when it is precise. Instead of asking for all the system's data, it can target the version history of an item or the screens shown at purchase. It can also target the records of a transaction, the notice of change or the reason for a punishment.

In a lawsuit, the judge can order the company to show documents and records it holds. The judge can also divide the work of proving in another way, when it is very hard for you and much easier for the company.

If the company refuses to show them without justification, the facts the document would prove may be accepted as true. Nor does a reply that the data is internal or no longer exists end the conversation.

Where it comes from

Instead of generically requesting “all the data in the system”, the consumer may request, for example, the history of relevant versions of a given item, the trail of screens shown along the purchase path, the minimum records of the specific transaction, the content of the change communication or the category and reason for the sanction applied.p. 660

PTEm vez de requerer genericamente “todos os dados do sistema”, o consumidor pode postular, por exemplo, o histórico de versões relevantes de determinado item, a trilha de telas exibidas no percurso de compra, os registros mínimos da transação específica, o conteúdo da comunicação de alteração ou a categoria e o motivo da sanção aplicada.

Article 373, paragraph 1, allows the burden of proof to be allocated differently when one party would find it excessively difficult to discharge the burden or the other could more easily prove the contrary factp. 659

PTO art. 373, § 1º, permite a distribuição diversa do ônus probatório quando houver excessiva dificuldade para uma parte cumprir o encargo ou maior facilidade da outra em produzir a prova do fato contrário

The judge may order the party to produce a document or thing in its possessionp. 659

PTO juiz pode ordenar que a parte exiba documento ou coisa em seu poder

Art. 400. In deciding the request, the judge shall admit as true the facts that, by means of the document or thing, the party intended to provep. 660

PTArt. 400. Ao decidir o pedido, o juiz admitirá como verdadeiros os fatos que, por meio do documento ou da coisa, a parte pretendia provar

In platform-related disputes, the procedural guarantees meet the need to react to generic answers such as “we no longer have such information”, “this is internal-use data”, “this information is not accessible to the public” or “we are unable to reconstruct the procedure”.p. 660

PTEm litígios relacionados a plataforma, as garantias processuais satisfazem a necessidade de reação às respostas genéricas como “não mais possuímos tal informação”, “são dados de uso interno”, “são informações não acessíveis ao público” ou “não temos capacidade de reconstruir o procedimento”.

Who can help take the proof out of the company's hands?

The company must keep and show the minimum records of what happened, so your first request can go to it, stating exactly what you want to see. When questioned, it is up to the company to show that it informed you and that it acted within what it promised.

The strongest tools, such as an order to produce documents or a different division of the work of proving, only work inside a lawsuit. So when all the proof is with the company, it helps to have a lawyer or the Public Defender's Office on your side. The Small Claims Court and the other routes are on the channels page.

Public consumer protection agencies can also use these criteria to require the company, once and for all users, to keep records and offer an understandable way to contest decisions.

Where it comes from

The corresponding duty is to preserve and disclose the minimum records needed to reconstruct the disputed event.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de preservar e exibir registros mínimos necessários à reconstrução do evento controvertido.

The supplier does not fulfill its role merely by throwing information into the environment; it must also demonstrate, when questioned, that it informed adequately, that it regularly exercised its power of change and that the impact produced was compatible with the promised performance.p. 660

PTO fornecedor não cumpre seu papel apenas jogando informação no ambiente; também precisa demonstrar, quando questionado, que informou adequadamente, que exerceu regularmente seu poder de alteração e que o impacto produzido foi compatível com a prestação prometida.

In the same sense, the regime for the production of documents or things provided for in arts. 396 to 400 of the CPC1 offers the most direct route to operationalize open auditability.p. 659

PTNo mesmo sentido, o regime de exibição de documento ou coisa previsto nos arts. 396 a 400 do CPC oferece a via mais direta para operacionalizar a auditabilidade aberta.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets. These standards offer a concrete criterion for consent decrees, recommendations, civil investigations and minimum governance requirements: functional impact reports on relevant changes, verifiable transparency protocols, preservation of essential records, understandable contestation procedures and minimum criteria of restoration in case of relevant degradation of enjoyment.p. 662-663

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma. Esses standards oferecem critério concreto para termos de ajustamento, recomendações, investigações civis e exigências mínimas de governança: relatórios de impacto funcional em alterações relevantes, protocolos de transparência verificável, preservação de registros essenciais, procedimentos de contestação compreensíveis e critérios mínimos de recomposição em caso de degradação relevante da fruição.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

What remains your task?

None of this spares you from telling a coherent, plausible story and making clear what you are disputing. What goes away is the idea that you and the company are on an equal footing when it comes to proof.

A different division of proof is not automatic. It responds to the concrete case and becomes stronger the more the records are concentrated with the company.

Nor does anyone ask for the system's entire code or the company's secrets. The technical examination, called an expert examination, looks only at what changed and the effect it had on what you use.

Where it comes from

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to require from the consumer a detailed demonstration of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within the consumer's reach. This does not eliminate the need for plausibility, argumentative chaining or delimitation of the disputed object. What it eliminates is the fiction of symmetry in environments whose very structure operates through the asymmetry of meaning and of evidence.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance. Isso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido. O que elimina é a ficção de simetria em ambientes cuja própria estrutura opera pela assimetria de sentido e de prova.

The more intense the private concentration of records, the greater the requirement of preservation, delimited production and verifiable justification.p. 736

PTQuanto mais intensa a concentração privada de registros, maior a exigência de preservação, de exibição delimitada e de justificação verificável.

The fourth mechanism is technical expert examination oriented to functional impact, and not to the total engineering of the ecosystem.p. 661

PTO quarto mecanismo é a perícia técnica orientada a impacto funcional, e não a engenharia total do ecossistema.

Trade secrets may justify containing exposure; they cannot function as an absolute veto on reconstructing what the platform did when that conduct became the subject of legal dispute.p. 661

PTO segredo industrial pode justificar contenção da exposição; não pode funcionar como veto absoluto à reconstrução do que a plataforma fez quando esse fazer se tornou juridicamente litigioso.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
