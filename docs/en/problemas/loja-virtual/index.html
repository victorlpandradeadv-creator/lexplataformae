Your rights · By problem

The online store hides who paid for placement and who is trustworthy. Does that count?

In an online store with many sellers, the so-called marketplace, you choose among what the platform put up front, with the badges, ratings and highlights it organizes. That counts, because the order of the storefront, the label on paid ads and the reputation displayed are part of what the platform supplies.

Where it comes from

Consumers do not choose in a vacuum, before a transparent and symmetrical universe of offers. They choose within a field the platform has already pre-interpreted.p. 762

PTO consumidor não escolhe no vazio, diante de universo transparente e simétrico de ofertas. Ele escolhe dentro de um campo já pré-interpretado pela plataforma.

Rankings, reputation, featuring, recommendation, prioritization and modulation of reach are not peripheral elements. They are part of the functional utility of the ecosystem and directly influence the formation of expectation, the assessment of risk and the possibility of rational choice.p. 761

PTRankings, reputação, destaque, recomendação, priorização e modulação de alcance não são elementos periféricos. Integram a utilidade funcional do ecossistema e influenciam diretamente a formação da expectativa, a avaliação do risco e a possibilidade de escolha racional.

How do you know whether the storefront weighed on your choice?

The store does not just bring buyers and sellers together. It chooses what shows up first, what stays hidden, who looks trustworthy and what can be compared. Without that storefront, the product would not even have reached you the way it did.

What matters is how prominently a product is featured, how trustworthy a seller appears, how reviews are shown and whether an ad was paid for or appeared without payment. The payment method, the delivery, how categories are organized and how alternatives are displayed matter too.

When the same company organizes the storefront and also profits from what happens in it, the situation becomes more serious. The storefront can bring certain offers closer, make others disappear and make a paid ad look like an ordinary result. That is why many options on the screen do not, by themselves, guarantee a real choice.

Where it comes from

What is offered, in legally relevant terms, is an infrastructure of visibility, reputation, curation, intermediation and logistics without which the final object does not become economically legible, socially rankable or practically accessible in the terms in which the platform itself presents it.p. 761

PTO que se oferece, em termos juridicamente relevantes, é uma infraestrutura de visibilidade, reputação, curadoria, intermediação e logística sem a qual o objeto final não se torna economicamente legível, socialmente hierarquizável ou praticamente acessível nos termos em que a própria plataforma o apresenta.

Platforms of this nature do not merely connect. They select, rank, highlight, hide, bring closer, delay, prioritize and reorganize the way in which the reality of the environment becomes perceptible to the user.p. 761

PTPlataformas dessa natureza não apenas conectam. Elas selecionam, hierarquizam, destacam, ocultam, aproximam, retardam, priorizam e reorganizam a forma pela qual a realidade do ambiente se torna perceptível ao usuário.

The prominent position of an item, the appearance of reliability of a seller, the way reviews are displayed, the distinction between sponsored and organically prioritized products, integration with payment and logistics, the organization of categories and the way alternatives are displayed are all elements that materially interfere with the consumption experience.p. 762

PTA posição de destaque de um item, a aparência de confiabilidade de um seller, a forma como avaliações são exibidas, a distinção entre produto patrocinado e organicamente priorizado, a integração com meios de pagamento e logística, a organização das categorias e a forma de exposição das alternativas são todos elementos que interferem materialmente na experiência de consumo.

In more serious cases, especially when the platform acts at once as arbiter of the environment and as an economic agent with a stake in its results, the opacity of the ranking may also intersect with broader abusive practices and carry competition repercussions of its own.p. 764

PTEm hipóteses mais graves, especialmente quando a plataforma atuar simultaneamente como árbitra do ambiente e agente econômico interessado em seus resultados, a opacidade da hierarquização pode também dialogar com práticas abusivas mais amplas e com repercussões concorrenciais próprias.

Homogenization does not occur only at the level of the product or the contract. It can be produced by the governance of the interface itself, when the environment artificially brings certain offers closer, renders others invisible, shifts what is sponsored into the zone of the apparently organic, manipulates comparability and manages the user's perception of what is worth more, appears more or seems more trustworthy. The illusion of plurality may thus coexist with severe compression of the real conditions of choice.p. 770

PTA homogeneização não se dá apenas no plano do produto ou do contrato. Pode ser produzida pela própria governança da interface, quando o ambiente aproxima artificialmente certas ofertas, invisibiliza outras, desloca o que é patrocinado para a zona do aparentemente orgânico, manipula a comparabilidade e administra a percepção do usuário sobre o que vale mais, aparece mais ou parece mais confiável. A ilusão de pluralidade pode, assim, conviver com severa compressão das condições reais de escolha.

Which storefront screens are worth saving?

The product that arrived shows only the end of the story. The search page is worth saving too, with the order of the offers, the sponsored label or its absence, the reviews, the seller's badge and the delivery promise.

Dated screenshots and screen recordings help show how the storefront looked at the time of purchase. The ranking, the highlight, the seller's rating and the sponsorship show a market that reached you already selected.

Where it comes from

It is not enough to examine the final product acquired or the individual content removed, prioritized or hidden. It is necessary to reconstruct the architecture of the experience. This includes, depending on the case, the way offers are presented, the distinction between organic and paid results, the role of reviews and reputations, the integration between visibility and logistics, the signals of reliability provided by the interface, the context in which content appears, the relevant moderation criteria and the mechanisms by which the platform induces a perception of market, consensus or priority.p. 763

PTNão basta examinar o produto final adquirido ou o conteúdo individual removido, priorizado ou ocultado. É necessário reconstruir a arquitetura da experiência. Isso inclui, conforme o caso, a forma de apresentação das ofertas, a distinção entre resultados orgânicos e pagos, o papel de avaliações e reputações, a integração entre visibilidade e logística, os sinais de confiabilidade fornecidos pela interface, o contexto de aparição do conteúdo, os critérios de moderação relevantes e os mecanismos pelos quais a plataforma induz percepção de mercado, de consenso ou de prioridade.

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

In marketplaces, it may involve the way ranking, prominence, seller rating and sponsorship logic produce a perceptible market that has already been preselected.p. 763

PTEm marketplaces, pode envolver a forma como ranking, destaque, classificação de seller e lógica de patrocínio produzem um mercado perceptível já previamente selecionado.

What can you demand from whoever builds the storefront?

The conversation can go beyond the product and reach the way the storefront is built. It is fair to ask whether it was clear who was selling and what role the platform played, whether paid ads could be told apart from the rest and whether the reputation on display could be checked. It is also fair to ask whether unclear criteria made comparison harder or whether the screen misled you.

The request may include minimum clarity about the criteria that determine order, prominence and reputation, without the company having to open up its internal systems. When it changes the rules and that significantly downgrades what the store offered, restoring what was lost may be in order.

Where it comes from

It becomes necessary to examine, through the lens of the IOC, whether there was adequate information about the nature of the intermediation, whether the distinction between organic priority and sponsored priority was materially intelligible, whether the reputation displayed was functionally verifiable, whether the opacity of the criteria compromised relevant comparability and whether the interface itself produced deceptiveness, obscuring or structural asymmetry incompatible with the consumer regime.p. 764

PTTorna-se necessário examinar, em chave de OIC, se houve informação adequada sobre a natureza da intermediação, se a distinção entre prioridade orgânica e prioridade patrocinada foi materialmente inteligível, se a reputação exibida era funcionalmente verificável, se a opacidade dos critérios comprometeu comparabilidade relevante e se a própria interface produziu enganosidade, obscurecimento ou assimetria estrutural incompatível com o regime consumerista.

Open auditability requires minimum institutional verifiability of the relevant criteria of presentation, prioritization, moderation, reputation and circulation, without this implying unrestricted opening of internal systems.p. 764

PTA auditabilidade aberta exige verificabilidade institucional mínima sobre os critérios relevantes de apresentação, priorização, moderação, reputação e circulação, sem que isso implique abertura irrestrita de sistemas internos.

Functional equivalence, in turn, applies when changes to the intermediation regime, the visibility logic, the reputational criteria or the circulation rules significantly degrade the utility legitimately incorporated into the relationship, without compatible restoration.p. 764

PTA equivalência funcional, por sua vez, incide quando alterações do regime de intermediação, da lógica de visibilidade, dos critérios reputacionais ou das regras de circulação degradam de modo relevante a utilidade legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo, sem recomposição compatível.

Where can you take a complaint about how the store displays its offers?

The first step is usually the store itself, in writing and with the screens saved. The complaint does not have to stop at the purchase that went wrong, because the way the storefront highlighted and hid the offers also counts. That holds even when none of it was written in the contract or the ad.

When the same unclear criterion for ordering or reputation affects everyone who buys there, the response can be collective. Procon, Consumidor.gov.br and the Public Prosecutor's Office are options described on the channels page.

Where it comes from

This subtype has recurring blind spots that must be avoided. The first is the tendency to treat the interface as mere visual support, and not as a constitutive part of the supply. The second is the tendency to imagine that the platform would be legally liable only for what it stated expressly in a clause or in advertising, as if the effective governance of visibility were legally irrelevant. The third is the tendency to reduce the dispute to the terminal act, such as the failed purchase, the removal of a piece of content or the supposed preference for a given offer, without investigating the private structure of salience that conditioned the user's entire experience.p. 763

PTHá, nesse subtipo, pontos de cegueira recorrentes que precisam ser evitados. O primeiro é a tendência de tratar a interface como mero suporte visual, e não como parte constitutiva do fornecimento. O segundo é a tendência de imaginar que a plataforma só responderia juridicamente pelo que dissesse expressamente em cláusula ou publicidade, como se a governança efetiva da visibilidade fosse juridicamente irrelevante. O terceiro é a tendência de reduzir o litígio ao ato terminal, como a compra frustrada, a remoção de um conteúdo ou a suposta preferência por determinada oferta, sem investigar a estrutura privada de saliência que condicionou a experiência inteira do usuário.

An opaque sanctions policy, a non-auditable regime of functional change, an interface logic that dilutes the essential under informational excess, a materially decisive ranking system without usable history or a trust architecture that absorbs and neutralizes contestation before it becomes litigation are all examples of problems whose unity lies in the design, not in the episode.p. 775

PTUma política opaca de sanções, um regime não auditável de alteração funcional, uma lógica de interface que dilui o essencial sob excesso informacional, um sistema de ranqueamento materialmente decisivo sem histórico aproveitável ou uma arquitetura de confiança que absorve e neutraliza contestação antes que ela se torne litígio são todos exemplos de problemas cuja unidade está no desenho, não no episódio.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When is the storefront's ordering no reason to complain?

Not every way of organizing the storefront is illegal, and not every intermediation problem is solved by Brazilian consumer law. The question is whether the lack of clarity hindered your choice in a relevant way.

Also left out, in principle, is the store that only sells advertising space and customer acquisition, without really controlling the sales that come afterward. Buying online does not, by itself, turn any product into a digital object governed by the platform.

When the problem is only a defect in the product, an improper charge or a delivery that never happened, ordinary consumer law tends to solve it. In that case the storefront does not need to enter the conversation.

Where it comes from

The possible institutional consequence is not to assert that every intermediation problem is automatically resolved by the CDC, nor that every interface governance constitutes, in itself, a competition offense.p. 773

PTA consequência institucional possível não é afirmar que todo problema de intermediação se resolve automaticamente pelo CDC, nem que toda governança de interface configura, por si, ilícito concorrencial.

The second limit is the scenario in which the platform functions as a channel or storefront, but does not govern, in structural terms, the acquired utility, lacking the infrastructural centrality characteristic of the IOC. This is the case that rules out, in principle, the marketplace that provides positioning and customer acquisition services through advertising, but does not exercise relevant material interference over the effective governance of subsequent commercial operations.p. 434

PTO segundo limite é a hipótese em que a plataforma funciona como canal ou vitrine, mas não governa, em termos estruturais, a utilidade adquirida, faltando-lhe a centralidade infraestrutural característica do OIC. É o caso que afasta, em princípio, o marketplace que presta serviços de posicionamento e captação de clientela por via publicitária, mas não exerce ingerência material relevante sobre a governança efetiva das operações comerciais subsequentes.

Curation, ranking, reputation, dispute policies, display criteria, delivery parameters and verification mechanisms structure the possibility of finding, comparing, contracting and validating what is acquired. This is not to say that every digital mediation automatically converts any good into an IOC.p. 487

PTCuradoria, ranqueamento, reputação, políticas de disputa, critérios de exibição, parâmetros de entrega e mecanismos de verificação estruturam a possibilidade de encontrar, comparar, contratar e validar o adquirido. Não se afirma, com isso, que toda mediação digital converta automaticamente qualquer bem em OIC.

The fifth limit is the case in which the core of the conflict is something else, such as a classic occasional defect, undue charge, localized misleading advertising or specific nonperformance of an offer. In these cases, common consumer law dogmatics tends to resolve the dispute with no real gain in mobilizing the IOC.p. 435

PTO quinto limite é o caso em que o núcleo do conflito é outro, como vício pontual clássico, cobrança indevida, publicidade enganosa localizada ou inadimplemento específico de oferta. Nessas hipóteses, a dogmática consumerista comum tende a resolver o litígio sem ganho real em mobilizar o OIC.

The rights behind this case

  • The platform cannot design the experience so that you fail to understand it
  • Information has to be of real use
  • What was promised has to be delivered
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
