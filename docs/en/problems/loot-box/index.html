I paid for a draw or a loot box. What do I need to know?

You paid to try your luck, in a loot box or an item draw, and what came out was almost never what you wanted. This is one of the most sensitive issues in game purchases, because the company itself decides the odds, the currency, the rarity and the deadlines.

Where it comes from

The institutional discussion thus tends to focus on minimum transparency in the conditions of acquisition, the protection of vulnerable audiences and the control of practices that induce consumption, not out of a wish to moralize the sector, but because the supplier holds the internal criteria on availability, rotation, rarity and, where applicable, probability, under conditions of low external reconstructibility.p. 494-495

PTA discussão institucional tende, então, a concentrar-se em transparência mínima das condições de aquisição, proteção de públicos vulneráveis e controle de práticas de indução de consumo, não por moralização do setor, mas porque o fornecedor concentra critérios internos sobre disponibilidade, rotação, raridade e, quando aplicável, probabilidade, em condições de baixa reconstruibilidade externa.

Unlike a stage of the game, whose rules exist so that the player can, in theory, beat them, the game embedded in the consumer relationship is designed and refereed by the supplier itself, which defines the probabilities, sets the unit of account, recalibrates the internal exchange rate and establishes the deadlines, systematically reserving for itself the position of the house.p. 449

PTDiferentemente de uma fase do jogo, cujas regras existem para que o jogador possa, em tese, vencê-las, o jogo embutido na relação de consumo é desenhado e arbitrado pelo próprio fornecedor, que define as probabilidades, fixa a unidade de conta, recalibra o câmbio interno e estabelece os prazos, reservando-se sistematicamente a posição da banca.

How can you tell whether the draw has become the only path to the item?

A loot box is the purchase of a randomly drawn result, not of a chosen item. The value of what you get comes to depend on internal rules of distribution, availability and rarity that only the company sets, often combined with time windows and signals of scarcity.

One example shows how this works. In a well-known game, the most exclusive skins became available only through draws, with tokens costing 400 units of the game's currency, a very low chance of success, a guarantee only on the eightieth draw and a limited-time sale.

In this setup, most attempts return currency and items you were not looking for, which soften the spending and encourage you to try again. When direct purchase disappears and the draw becomes mandatory, you no longer choose how to reach the item and start paying just to try. This design may come close to tying (venda casada), which means making the purchase you want conditional on others you did not want.

Where it comes from

The point of greatest sensitivity arises when monetization relies on random mechanisms for obtaining items, often referred to in public debate as loot boxes. In these cases, the utility and value of what was acquired come to depend on internal distribution regimes, availability criteria and limits set by the platform, and are also tied to signals of scarcity, time windows and rarity regimes that make decisions more dependent on anticipation, urgency and expectation.p. 494

PTO ponto de maior sensibilidade aparece quando a monetização se apoia em mecanismos aleatórios de obtenção de itens, frequentemente tratados no debate público como caixas de recompensa. Nesses casos, utilidade e valor do adquirido passam a depender de regimes internos de distribuição, de critérios de disponibilidade e de limites definidos pela plataforma, além de se articularem a sinais de escassez, janelas temporais e regimes de raridade que tornam decisões mais dependentes de antecipação, urgência e expectativa.

In this context, Law No. 15,211/2025 Brasil(Brasil, [2025]c) provides sufficient normative anchoring to locate the focal point, since it regulates, from the standpoint of protecting children and adolescents, loot boxes as a mechanism of random acquisition upon payment.p. 495

PTNesse contexto, a Lei nº 15.211/2025 (Brasil, [2025]c) oferece ancoragem normativa suficiente para situar o ponto focal ao disciplinar, sob o enfoque de proteção de crianças e adolescentes, a funcionalidade de caixas de recompensa como mecanismo de obtenção aleatória mediante pagamento.

Abusive teleology emerges forcefully once one observes that the system not only bars direct purchase of the main good but turns the acquisition into a functional tying sale with a set of items and internal currencies that, along most consumption trajectories, do not match the player's interest. This economic binding does not occur through any declared obligation of joint purchase, but through the design of the path, in which most results do not deliver the desired good and come back as intermediate rewards, especially currencies and collateral cosmetics, which come to function as symbolic cushions for the spending and as an incentive to keep trying Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024a).p. 450

PTA teleologia abusiva aparece com força quando se observa que o sistema não apenas interdita a compra direta do bem principal, mas converte a aquisição em venda casada funcional com um conjunto de itens e moedas internas que, na maior parte das trajetórias de consumo, não correspondem ao interesse do jogador. Essa amarração econômica não se dá por obrigação declarada de compra conjunta, mas pelo desenho do percurso, no qual a maioria dos resultados não entrega o bem desejado e retorna como recompensas intermediárias, em especial moedas e cosméticos colaterais, que passam a funcionar como amortecedores simbólicos do gasto e como incentivo à continuidade da tentativa (Riot Games, 2024a) .

This design makes explicit a relevant loss of autonomy for the consumer, who no longer chooses the means of access to the good and comes to “pay to try”, with accumulated progression and a foreseen spending cap, in a logic that is only completed by continued consumption within the system itself Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024a).p. 450

PTEsse desenho explicita uma perda relevante de autonomia do consumidor, que deixa de escolher a via de acesso ao bem e passa a “pagar para tentar”, com progressão acumulada e teto de gasto previsto, em uma lógica que só se completa com continuidade de consumo no próprio sistema (Riot Games, 2024a) .

It would not be a problem if the option to buy the skin directly at a proportionate and reasonable price were kept. However, by making the path mandatory, the company comes closer to an abusive tying practice than to creating an entertainment experience that genuinely cares about the expectations and wishes of its consumer users.p. 450-451

PTNão se trataria de um problema caso fosse mantida a opção de compra direta da skin por preço proporcional e razoável. No entanto, ao tornar o percurso obrigatório, a empresa mais se aproxima de uma prática abusiva por venda casada do que da criação de uma experiência de entretenimento que realmente se preocupe com as expectativas e desejos de seus usuários consumidores.

Which records show how much it cost to try?

Keep the screen that described the draw, with the odds, the guarantee after a set number of attempts, the spending cap and the deadline. The official description itself is important evidence, because it shows that the path to the item was designed as a series of paid attempts, not as a direct purchase.

Because the game's currency sits between your money and the draw, the real cost in reais tends to stay hidden. That is why the statements and emails for each currency purchase help, along with a list of what came out of each attempt.

Where it comes from

Proof of the exploitative character does not depend on a subjective reading, because the institutional material itself describes the structure in terms of probability, progression and guarantees per attempt, and even presents an investment cap. The relevant evidence is the admission that access is designed as a trajectory of probabilistic extraction, not as a direct purchase of the good Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024a).p. 450

PTA prova do caráter exploratório não depende de leitura subjetiva, porque o próprio material institucional descreve a estrutura em termos de probabilidade, progressão e garantias por tentativas, além de apresentar um teto de investimento. O elemento probatório relevante é a admissão do desenho do acesso como trajetória de extração probabilística, e não como compra direta do bem (Riot Games, 2024a) .

A monetization model based on random draws of virtual items, inspired by capsule vending machines (gashapon). Sob a perspectiva dos OIC, representa arquitetura de extração que condiciona a fruição de bens desejados a mecanismos probabilísticos e sorteios sucessivos, muitas vezes ocultando o custo real da aquisição por meio de moedas intermediárias e opacidade estatística

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição.

What can you demand from the company selling the draw?

The first request is for clear purchase conditions, including the odds, where they exist. That is precisely where the debate on loot boxes usually focuses, along with protecting those who are most vulnerable.

When the draw replaces direct purchase and each attempt turns into serial spending, your complaint can target the whole design, not just one purchase. Depending on the case, this design may be treated as an abusive practice, and the request may include fixing the purchase path, without you having to prove that anyone meant to harm you.

If the draw was presented as a near-certain chance while the result depended on luck, the rules on promises also apply, because the ad is part of the purchase.

The category does not depend on imputing subjective psychological intent nor on locating a single act as sufficient cause, because its object is the architectural program as a set of repeated choices that organizes possibilities, introduces frictions, manages visibilities, distributes sanctions and stabilizes dependencies in the performance of the relationship.p. 448

PTA categoria não depende de imputar intenção psicológica subjetiva nem de localizar um único ato como causa suficiente, porque seu objeto é o programa arquitetural enquanto conjunto de escolhas reiteradas que organiza possibilidades, introduz fricções, administra visibilidades, distribui sanções e estabiliza dependências na execução do vínculo.

In these contexts, the platform produces something more sophisticated than simple ordinary commercial persuasion. It builds cycles of promise, intensification and partial delivery, in which the object is presented as a rare, prestigious, limited or virtually guaranteed opportunity, even though actual enjoyment depends on later mechanisms of randomness, mutability, internal rebalancing or unilaterally defined equivalences.p. 631

PTNesses contextos, a plataforma produz algo mais sofisticado do que simples persuasão comercial ordinária. Ela constrói ciclos de promessa, intensificação e entrega parcial, nos quais o objeto é apresentado como oportunidade rara, prestigiada, limitada ou virtualmente garantida, ainda que a fruição efetiva dependa de mecanismos posteriores de aleatoriedade, de mutabilidade, de reequilíbrio interno ou de equivalências definidas unilateralmente.

Where can you turn when the draw is the same for everyone?

Start with the company itself, asking for the conditions and odds that applied when you bought and for the records of your transactions. The request carries more weight when it is specific rather than a generic demand for all your data.

If the company does not resolve it, the case can go to Procon, the state consumer protection agency, to consumidor.gov.br, the federal government's online complaint platform, or to the Small Claims Court (Juizado Especial Cível), options that the channels page explains. Because the draw is the same for everyone, a flaw in its design tends to affect many people, and a response may come through a collective action or from public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

Instead of generically requesting “all the data in the system”, the consumer may request, for example, the history of relevant versions of a given item, the trail of screens shown along the purchase path, the minimum records of the specific transaction, the content of the change communication or the category and reason for the sanction applied.p. 660

PTEm vez de requerer genericamente “todos os dados do sistema”, o consumidor pode postular, por exemplo, o histórico de versões relevantes de determinado item, a trilha de telas exibidas no percurso de compra, os registros mínimos da transação específica, o conteúdo da comunicação de alteração ou a categoria e o motivo da sanção aplicada.

This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not in a single piece.p. 662

PTIsso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets.p. 662

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is every paid draw in a game prohibited?

No. The draw itself is not the problem. The issue arises when it replaces direct purchase at a reasonable price. It also arises when the whole design is geared, above all, to exploiting your dependence rather than to legitimate ends such as security and game balance.

Brazilian law prohibits loot boxes in games aimed at children and adolescents or likely to be accessed by them. Within that scope or outside it, consumer law also applies, and the sector's own laws do not replace it.

Where it comes from

It would not be a problem if the option of directly purchasing the skin at a proportional and reasonable price were kept.p. 450

PTNão se trataria de um problema caso fosse mantida a opção de compra direta da skin por preço proporcional e razoável.

It must depend on the rational demonstration that the architecture is oriented, predominantly, toward the exploitation of vulnerability and the disproportionate capture of value, with persistent opacity and asymmetry, and not toward legitimate purposes of the service, such as security, stability and the balance of the ecosystem.p. 456

PTEla deve depender da demonstração racional de que a arquitetura é orientada, de modo dominante, à exploração da vulnerabilidade e à captura desproporcional de valor, com opacidade e assimetria persistentes, e não a finalidades legítimas do serviço, como segurança, estabilidade e equilíbrio do ecossistema.

By prohibiting loot boxes in games directed at children and adolescents, as well as in those likely to be accessed by this audience, the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents did not merely recognize in the abstract the economic and behavioral sensitivity of this market, but began to interfere in the very way of organizing products that had previously been presented as fully compatible with the presence of minors in the digital ecosystem Brasil(Brasil, [2025]c).p. 545

PTAo vedar loot boxes em jogos direcionados a crianças e adolescentes, bem como naqueles cujo acesso por esse público seja provável, o Estatuto Digital da Criança e do Adolescente não apenas reconheceu abstratamente a sensibilidade econômica e comportamental desse mercado, mas passou a interferir na própria forma de organização de produtos antes apresentados como plenamente compatíveis com a presença de menores no ecossistema digital (Brasil, [2025]c) .

The existence of sectoral regulation does not replace the consumer-law infrastructure, nor does it authorize conflicts of enjoyment to be entirely absorbed by the grammar of the sector or by the language of innovation.p. 548

PTA existência de regulação setorial não substitui a infraestrutura consumerista, nem autoriza que conflitos de fruição sejam absorvidos integralmente pela gramática do setor ou pela linguagem da inovação.
