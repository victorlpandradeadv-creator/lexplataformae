What did you buy, after all?

Here is the most common scene. You opened the game store, chose a skin and pressed the button that says buy. The money left your card and, a few seconds later, the skin appeared in your inventory next to the ones you already had.

The word on the button is no accident. In everyday Portuguese, and in the Civil Code too, to buy is to exchange money for a thing that becomes yours. A buyer expects the thing to stay, not to vanish for no reason and to be good for something. That holds even when you know the item is digital, because the word raises the expectation on its own, like a shortcut the mind takes without thinking.

Where it comes from

Both common sense and even the Brazilian Civil Code itself use the verb “to buy” to mean acquiring something that becomes “yours”, in that minimum stability, permanence and some form of control over what was acquired are expectedp. 400-401

PTTanto o senso comum quanto até mesmo o próprio Código Civil brasileiro utilizam o verbo “comprar” sob o significado de se estar adquirindo algo que passa a ser “seu”, no sentido de que se espera estabilidade mínima, permanência e alguma forma de controle sobre o que foi adquirido

Its classic structure is the exchange of a thing for a pricep. 400

PTSua estrutura clássica é a troca de coisa por preço

Even when one knows that it is a digital item, the language of purchase and sale automatically triggers this typical expectation and works as an interpretive shortcut.p. 401

PTMesmo quando se sabe que se trata de um item digital, o idioma da compra e venda aciona automaticamente essa expectativa típica e funciona como atalho de interpretação.

The game reinforces this reading with another word, collection. Your inventory does not appear as a list of files but as a collection, arranged so you can complete series, spot what is rare, compare and show off. A collector keeps the story of each piece and builds, from what they have gathered, a bond that lasts.

That is why, when an account is blocked or an item loses the function it had, the loss is not limited to a single file. It hits the whole collection, which only makes sense as long as the game keeps recognizing and showing those pieces the way you came to know them.

Where it comes from

Collecting does not mean merely gathering items. It means ordering a collection, recognizing rarity, comparing value, preserving history, completing series, displaying belonging and building a relationship of continuity between subject and object.p. 401

PTColecionar não significa apenas reunir itens. Significa ordenar um acervo, reconhecer raridade, comparar valor, preservar histórico, completar séries, exibir pertencimento e construir uma relação de continuidade entre sujeito e objeto.

Therefore, any account block, item removal, substantial alteration of functionality or architectural devaluation of the collection does not affect only a detachable digital unit. It affects the intelligibility of the collection itself as a bundle of continued enjoyment, whose value depends on internal recognition, minimum stability and the preservation of the relationship between the consumer, their items and the ecosystem that gives them meaning.p. 401

PTPor isso, eventual bloqueio de conta, remoção de item, alteração substancial de funcionalidade ou desvalorização arquitetural do acervo não atinge apenas uma unidade digital destacável. Atinge a inteligibilidade da própria coleção como feixe de fruição continuada, cujo valor depende de reconhecimento interno, estabilidade mínima e preservação da relação entre o consumidor, seus itens e o ecossistema que lhes atribui sentido.

Now comes the part almost nobody does, which is opening the terms of use, the contract you accepted when you created the account. There the same skin does not appear as a purchase. It appears as virtual content that the company offers under a limited, revocable license tied to your account.

A license, here, means permission to use. Revocable means the permission can be withdrawn, and the contract itself warns that if the account is terminated the license is canceled immediately. So the screen speaks the language of purchase, while the contract speaks the language of conditional access managed by the company.

Where it comes from

The Terms of Service describe the same items as “Virtual Content” offered under an access license tied to the account Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). The consequence is that what the interface presents as a “purchase”, the contractual instrument requalifies as conditional and institutionally administered access.p. 401

PTOs Termos de Serviço descrevem os mesmos itens como “Conteúdo Virtual” oferecido sob licença de acesso vinculada à conta (Riot Games, 2024e) . A consequência é que aquilo que a interface apresenta como “compra”, o instrumento contratual requalifica como acesso condicionado e institucionalmente administrado.

The service's own contractual framework gives shape to this arrangement by establishing that access to the Services, including the so-called Virtual Content, occurs through a limited and revocable license, conditioned on compliance with the Terms, and that the termination of the account entails automatic cancellation of the license and loss of access to the associated contentp. 408

PTA própria disciplina contratual do serviço dá forma a esse arranjo ao estabelecer que o acesso aos Serviços, inclusive ao chamado Conteúdo Virtual, ocorre por licença limitada e revogável, condicionada ao cumprimento dos Termos, e que o encerramento da conta acarreta cancelamento automático da licença e perda do acesso aos conteúdos associados

In plain language, this means that access to the ecosystem is structured as a conditional permission to use, and not as ownership of an autonomous good.p. 408

PTEm linguagem simples, isso significa que o acesso ao ecossistema é estruturado como permissão de uso condicionada, e não como propriedade de um bem autônomo.

And one detail works in your favor. The same terms that speak of a license recognize that virtual content is tied to your account, made up of in-game currency, skins, emotes and other items. They also admit that you may have bought it, earned it or received it as a gift.

That helps prove that what you have exists and can be identified. The contract shows the object is there, but it does not decide on its own which rules apply to it. And Brazilian consumer law also treats as a product something with no physical body, so the lack of a physical medium does not strip the item of protection.

Where it comes from

Riot Games' Terms of Service expressly recognize a core of “Virtual Content” associated with the account, made up of in-game currency, skins, emotes, accessories and other items, and admit that this formation may occur through purchase, earning, gift or other internal dynamics of the ecosystem.p. 575

PTOs Termos de Serviço da Riot Games reconhecem expressamente um núcleo de “Conteúdo Virtual” associado à conta, composto por moeda do jogo, skins, emotes, acessórios e outros itens, e admitem que essa formação pode ocorrer por compra, ganho, presente ou outras dinâmicas internas do ecossistema.

The clause helps prove the existence of the object. It does not exhaust its regime.p. 575

PTA cláusula ajuda a provar a existência do objeto. Não esgota o seu regime.

The legal definition of product in art. 3, §1, of the CDC1 operates as a clause of scope that prevents escape from protective coverage when the object does not present itself in the form of a physical medium.p. 411

PTA definição legal de produto no art. 3º, §1º, do CDC opera como cláusula de abrangência que impede a fuga da tutela protetiva quando o objeto não se apresenta sob a forma de suporte físico.

The law does not condition the product on corporeality, nor tie it to the image of the tangible thing delivered once and for all.p. 574

PTA lei não condiciona o produto à corporeidade, nem o prende à imagem da coisa tangível entregue de uma vez por todas.

Maybe you think it is your fault for not reading it. That is not the point. The game itself steers your decision with a word that promises you are acquiring something, while the contract holds a regime that does not match the ordinary meaning of buying.

Nor does it help to say the information was there. Information can sit in the contract and still guide no one when it is the screen that drives the experience at the moment of choice. You decide by the grammar of purchase, but you use what you bought by the grammar of a revocable license.

Where it comes from

The point is not to assert that the consumer “did not read” or “did not understand”, but to recognize that the ecosystem itself organizes the decision through a sign that frames the relationship as a typical contract, while reserving, on the legal plane, a regime that does not coincide with the ordinary meaning of the verb “to buy”.p. 401

PTO ponto não é afirmar que o consumidor “não leu” ou “não entendeu”, mas reconhecer que o próprio ecossistema organiza a decisão por um signo que enquadra a relação como contrato típico, enquanto reserva, no plano jurídico, um regime que não coincide com o sentido ordinário do verbo “comprar”.

The relevant asymmetry is not merely a lack of information, since information may exist and, even so, not operate as practical guidance when the language of the interface governs the experience and stabilizes expectations in real time.p. 401

PTA assimetria relevante não é apenas falta de informação, pois a informação pode existir e, ainda assim, não operar como orientação prática quando a linguagem da interface governa a experiência e estabiliza expectativas em tempo real.

The result is an objective inequality of decoding and agency, because the consumer decides under the grammar of “buying”, but enjoyment takes place under the grammar of the “revocable license” associated with the accountp. 401-402

PTO resultado é uma desigualdade objetiva de decodificação e de agência, porque o consumidor decide sob a gramática do “comprar”, mas a fruição se realiza sob a gramática da “licença revogável” associada à conta

So is the word buy forbidden?

This does not mean the word buy is illegal. The game can use the usual words, and nobody has to change the button. The problem begins when, behind the word, what you bought has no minimum stability left. Then the comparison with a purchase becomes false, the expectation you rightly had is broken, and only then does an unlawful act appear, meaning something the law does not accept.

That is why the way out is not to forbid the word. It is to require the company to answer for the changes it makes, keeping a record of them, and to preserve an equivalent function when the change is legitimate.

Where it comes from

[...] the consumer decides under the grammar of “buying”, but enjoyment takes place under the grammar of the “revocable license” associated with the account. This does not serve as grounds for asserting that the use of the word “buy” is, by itself and in isolation, unlawful.p. 401-402

PT[...] o consumidor decide sob a gramática do “comprar”, mas a fruição se realiza sob a gramática da “licença revogável” associada à conta. Isso não serve de fundamento para afirmar que o uso da palavra “comprar” se mostra, por si só e isoladamente, ilícito.

The issue is that, without a core that respects the minimum stability of the good, even the superficial parallel becomes false and, therefore, leads to the breach of the legitimate expectation of enjoyment, only thus constituting unlawfulness.p. 402

PTA questão é que, sem um núcleo que respeite a estabilidade mínima do bem, até mesmo o paralelo superficial se torna falso e, portanto, conduz à quebra da legítima expectativa de fruição, somente assim configurando a ilicitude.

It is not a matter of preventing the use of typical expressions, but of ensuring minimum fulfillment of the expectation by establishing responsibility for the auditability of changes and requiring the preservation of equivalent functionality in case of legitimate change.p. 402

PTNão se trata de impedir o uso de expressões típicas, mas sim de garantir o mínimo atendimento à expectativa por via da fixação de responsabilidade pela auditabilidade de alterações e exigência de preservação de funcionalidade equivalente em caso de legítima alteração.

What does your account hold besides the login?

To understand what you have, the best starting point is your account. It is not just a login and password. It is where your inventory, your progress, your player name and the signs by which others recognize you in the game are kept together. Characters, skins, emotes and in-game currency only work tied to it and cannot be taken outside.

That is why the account holds everything together. If someone breaks into your account, you need to open a ticket for the company to recover it, which shows that it is a record the company keeps and administers. And if the company terminates the account because it believes there was cause, you lose access to it and to the data tied to it. You also lose the virtual content, including the currency you paid for, and you lose it without a refund.

The contract also makes no distinction between someone who spent years gathering items and someone who just arrived. The account does not become more yours because it received ten thousand hours of dedication or as much money as a budget car costs.

Where it comes from

The player's account in the Riot ecosystem offers a methodologically privileged example of an immaterial object of consumption in its most “infrastructural” form. It is not reduced to a login credential. It is the organizational point that holds the enjoyment bundle together, because it concentrates, in a single institutional record, the inventory of licensed virtual content, progression markers, social identifiers and the reputation signals by which the user's presence is read, displayed and recognized in the environment.p. 408

PTA conta do jogador no ecossistema Riot oferece um exemplo metodologicamente privilegiado de objeto imaterial de consumo em sua forma mais “infraestrutural”. Ela não se resume a uma credencial de login. Ela é o ponto organizacional que mantém unido o feixe de fruição, porque concentra, em um mesmo registro institucional, o inventário de conteúdos virtuais licenciados, os marcadores de progressão, os identificadores sociais e os sinais de reputação pelos quais a presença do usuário é lida, exibida e reconhecida no ambiente.

Champions, skins, emotes and in-game currency are part of the catalog of goods that only become enjoyable as an access license associated with the account, and not as things transportable outside itp. 408

PTCampeões, skins, emotes e moeda do jogo integram o catálogo de bens que só se tornam fruíveis como licença de acesso associada à conta, e não como coisas transportáveis para fora dela

In official guidance, Riot recognizes the possibility of a compromised account and states that, in that case, the user must open a ticket to recover itp. 409

PTEm orientação oficial, a Riot reconhece a hipótese de conta comprometida e afirma que, nesse caso, o usuário deve abrir um ticket para recuperá-la

Once the account is terminated, the user will no longer have access to the account, nor to the associated data, nor to the Virtual Content, including in-game currency, and the consequence is definitive loss without refund whenever the landlord deems there was causep. 155

PTEncerrada a conta, o usuário não terá mais acesso à conta, nem aos dados associados, nem ao Conteúdo Virtual, incluindo moeda do jogo, e a consequência é a perda definitiva sem reembolso quando o senhorio entende que havia motivo

The contract does not distinguish someone who spent years accumulating signs of belonging from someone who has just joinedp. 153

PTO contrato não distingue quem passou anos acumulando sinais de pertencimento e quem acabou de entrar

because it received ten thousand hours of dedication over fifteen years, nor because it received investment comparable to the cost of a budget carp. 154

PTporque recebeu dez mil horas de dedicação ao longo de quinze anos, nem porque recebeu investimento compatível ao custo de um carro popular

What does a subway turnstile have to do with your skin?

What you bought exists and has value, but it can only be used inside a turnstile, like the ones at a subway station, and whoever controls the turnstile is the platform. It decides who gets through, who stays and on what conditions, both through the contract and through technology.

The contract tries to convince you that the turnstile is everything, as if your payment only bought you leave to stay inside, which can be taken away at any moment. But the turnstile changes the conditions of use and does not erase what you bought.

Where it comes from

The IOC, as the concretization of the immaterial product in the sense of art. 3, §1, of the CDC1, appears here not as neutral content, but as a commodity form whose enjoyment is subordinated to contractual and technological turnstilesp. 186

PTO OIC, como concretização do produto imaterial no sentido do art. 3º, §1º, do CDC , aparece aqui não como conteúdo neutro, mas como forma de mercadoria cuja fruição é subordinada a catracas contratuais e tecnológicas

And the account, far from being a good of the consumer, is described as a private space whose “right” of use comes down to a personal and revocable permission, which is not to be confused with ownership.p. 153

PTE a conta, longe de ser um bem do consumidor, é descrita como um espaço privado cujo “direito” de uso se resume a uma permissão pessoal e revogável, que não se confunde com propriedade.

What one buys, in practice, is a tolerance of access that can be withdrawnp. 153

PTCompra-se, na prática, uma tolerância de acesso que pode ser retirada

And what you bought does not stop existing just because you cannot hold it in your hand. The company itself defines in the contract what virtual content is, names the items and ties them to your account. It also admits that you can obtain this content by buying it, earning it in missions or receiving it as a gift. In doing so, it recognizes that there is a set of things there that can be identified, and this recognition dismantles the idea that nothing was delivered.

Where it comes from

The company expressly defines “Virtual Content” as a set that includes in-game currency, champions, skins, emotes, accessories and other items associated with the accountp. 566

PTA empresa define expressamente “Conteúdo Virtual” como conjunto que inclui moeda do jogo, campeões, skins, emotes, acessórios e outros itens associados à conta

and admits that this content may be obtained not only through classic purchase, but also through earning, reward, gift or other internal dynamics of the ecosystemp. 566

PTe admite que esse conteúdo pode ser obtido não só por compra clássica, mas também por ganho, recompensa, presente ou outras dinâmicas internas do ecossistema

First, because it shows that the contractual design itself recognizes an identifiable core of enjoyment associated with the account, which dismantles the puerile thesis of the legal nonexistence of the object.p. 566

PTPrimeiro, porque evidencia que o próprio desenho contratual reconhece um núcleo identificável de fruição associado à conta, o que desmonta a tese pueril de inexistência jurídica do objeto.

LawDigital goods

Things without a body can also be goods. Information organized on a computer can have its own usefulness and be part of your interests, even when it is not immediately worth money. Once recognized as a good, it can be used, enjoyed, passed on and protected by law.

This starting point is indispensable. Without it, the crude idea would prevail that everything you cannot hold in your hand is light, disposable and can vanish at the mere will of the seller. But it does not settle everything. Knowing that the skin is a good still does not say what happens when the platform changes the game or tampers with the item's function. Nor does it say what applies when the platform decides on its own, over time, how the item can be used.

Where it comes from

Digitally organized information may take on its own usefulness, become part of the subject's sphere of interests and, for that reason, be treated as a legally relevant incorporeal good, with or without immediate economic contentp. 565

PTA informação digitalmente organizada pode assumir utilidade própria, integrar a esfera de interesses do sujeito e, por isso, ser tratada como bem incorpóreo juridicamente relevante, com ou sem conteúdo econômico imediato

It is also a real gain of this framework to recognize that, once admitted as goods, these assets become susceptible to appropriation, circulation, enjoyment and protectionp. 565

PTTambém é um ganho real dessa moldura reconhecer que, uma vez admitidos como bens, esses ativos se tornam suscetíveis de apropriação, circulação, fruição e tutela

This starting point is correct and indispensable. Without it, the debate on continuous digital consumption would remain hostage to the crude fiction according to which everything that cannot be held in the hand would be legally light, dissolvable and disposable at the mere will of the supplier.p. 565

PTEsse ponto de partida é correto e indispensável. Sem ele, o debate sobre consumo digital contínuo ficaria refém da ficção tosca segundo a qual tudo o que não se segura com a mão seria juridicamente leve, dissolúvel e descartável por simples vontade do fornecedor.

The insufficiency would not lie in the inability of this framework to recognize the asset, but in the fact that, even while recognizing appropriation and circulation, it does not exhaust the conflicts of continuous enjoyment, unilateral mutability and functional governance in a platform environmentp. 566

PTA insuficiência não estaria na incapacidade dessa moldura para reconhecer o ativo, mas no fato de que, mesmo reconhecendo apropriação e circulação, ela não esgota os conflitos de fruição contínua, mutabilidade unilateral e governança funcional em ambiente de plataforma

Governed enjoyment bundle

So what, exactly, do you have? It is not just an image stuck onto the character. It is a set of things the skin lets you do and be inside the game. With it you play the match, appear to others, complete the collection and say something about who you are in that place.

Each of these things depends on the game continuing to work in a certain way. The skin needs the game to remain able to render the model, the effects and the animations, which change with every update. And what it means depends on other players seeing and recognizing it. That is why, more than a static thing, what you have is a position of use that the platform maintains and keeps adjusting over time.

Where it comes from

The consumer does not buy merely an “image” attached to the character. They acquire a position of enjoyment that is only realized if the game environment remains able to render models, effects, animations and interfaces that are reconfigured with each cycle of patches and system adjustmentsp. 439

PTO consumidor não compra apenas uma “imagem” acoplada ao personagem. Ele adquire uma posição de fruição que só se realiza se o ambiente do jogo continuar capaz de renderizar modelos, efeitos, animações e interfaces que são reconfigurados a cada ciclo de patches e ajustes do sistema

From this operator, the object can be described as a bundle of functionalities and meanings administered over the course of the relationshipp. 426

PTA partir desse operador, o objeto pode ser descrito como feixe de funcionalidades e significações administradas ao longo da relação

The IOC does not behave as a “good” merely by virtue of an isolated functional utility, but as a functional and semiotic bundle, in which enjoyment depends on operations of public legibility and collective recognition.p. 252

PTO OIC não se comporta como “bem” apenas por uma utilidade funcional isolada, mas como feixe funcional e semiótico, em que a fruição depende de operações de legibilidade pública e reconhecimento coletivo.

Enjoyment ceases to be a simple initial enablement and becomes a position sustained within the ecosystemp. 426

PTA fruição deixa de ser simples habilitação inicial e passa a ser posição sustentada no ecossistema

Is the skin money in a drawer or water in a pipe?

Money kept in a drawer is a stock. It stays there, still, and tomorrow it is still money. Water running through a pipe is a flow, because it only reaches your faucet while someone keeps the network working. The skin is more like the water.

Its value is not stored in a corner that is yours alone. It depends on servers running, on connection and on an environment the company maintains every day. So much so that the company itself publishes a page showing whether the service is working. When the game goes down, the skin stays in the account, but at that moment it is useless, because the place where it works has stopped.

Where it comes from

The use value of the bundle, in this condition, is not stock, it is flow. It requires continuity of servers, institutional connectivity and maintenance of the environment.p. 409

PTO valor de uso do feixe, nessa condição, não é estoque, é fluxo. Ele exige continuidade de servidores, conectividade institucional e manutenção do ambiente.

Riot maintains an official service status page, with current messages and a history of incidentsp. 409

PTA Riot mantém uma página oficial de status do serviço, com mensagens atuais e histórico de ocorrências

When the service becomes unavailable, it is not only access that fails, but the virtual content itself loses immediate usefulness, because the environment that makes it enjoyable stops operating.p. 409

PTQuando o serviço apresenta indisponibilidade, não é apenas o acesso que falha, mas o próprio conteúdo virtual perde utilidade imediata, porque o ambiente que o torna fruível deixa de operar.

With all of this together, it becomes clear why the expression revocable license deserves suspicion. It gives the impression that nothing was delivered and that everything can evaporate without consequence. But if there is an item that can be identified, tied to your account and used in the game, there is something the law needs to take seriously. And this holds even if the contract describes it in words that make it sound precarious.

Where it comes from

it sets aside, from the outset, the semantic fraud of the “revocable license” as a formula capable of evaporating everything that has already been economically incorporated into the user's spherep. 566

PTafasta, logo de saída, a fraude semântica da “licença revogável” como fórmula capaz de evaporar tudo o que já foi economicamente incorporado à esfera do usuário

If there is an identifiable virtual object, associated with the account and enjoyed within the ecosystem, there is legal relevance to be taken seriously, even if the platform tries to cover it with a vocabulary of precariousness.p. 566

PTSe há objeto virtual identificável, associado à conta e fruído no ecossistema, há juridicidade a ser levada a sério, ainda que a plataforma tente recobri-la com vocabulário de precariedade.

Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC)

Brazilian law had already left room for this. In 1990, the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor, CDC) said that a product is any good, material or immaterial. The door was open for goods that circulate without a body and depend on technical conditions maintained all the time. But the way of thinking about the product remained tied to the physical object, or to a delivery that begins and ends in an instant.

It was like a plot of land the law had marked out and that was left empty. The Immaterial Object of Consumption is the building raised on that land. It is the legal category for the case in which what you consume depends, structurally, on an environment the platform administers, adjusts and can change.

When this is the case, there is an immaterial product in the sense of consumer law. The service does not vanish from the picture, because it appears as the way the company maintains, updates and shapes what you bought. That is why the old dispute over whether the case is one of product or service loses force. The question that matters becomes how the company organizes the continuous delivery of what it sold and whether this way of supplying preserves or spoils what you acquired.

Where it comes from

A product is any good, movable or immovable, material or immaterialp. 24

PTProduto é qualquer bem, móvel ou imóvel, material ou imaterial

Article 3, §1, of the Consumer Protection Code already contains the normative opening necessary for the recognition of goods that circulate without a stable corporeal medium and whose enjoyment depends on continuous technical and organizational conditions.p. 24

PTO Artigo 3º, §1º, do Código de Defesa do Consumidor já contém a abertura normativa necessária ao reconhecimento de bens que circulam sem suporte corpóreo estável e cuja fruição depende de condições técnicas e organizacionais contínuas.

It is necessary to recognize, however, that this expansibility of Brazilian Consumer Law has been held back by a strictly material ontology, which insists on reducing the product to the delivery of a physical medium or of a discrete and finalizable performancep. 24

PTÉ necessário reconhecer, entretanto, que essa expansibilidade do Direito do Consumidor brasileiro tem sido freada por uma ontologia estritamente material, a qual insiste em reduzir o produto à entrega de um suporte físico ou de uma prestação discreta e finalizável

The Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) presents itself, in this sense, as the definitive subdivision of a plot of land that the legislator of 1990 had already marked out, although contemporary doctrine has kept it in the condition of a vacant lot.p. 24-25

PTO Objeto Imaterial de Consumo (OIC) apresenta-se, nesse sentido, como o loteamento definitivo de um terreno que o legislador de 1990 já havia demarcado, embora a doutrina contemporânea o tenha mantido na condição de um terreno baldio.

What matters is recognizing situations in which the economically enjoyed utility depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and made mutable by private governancep. 717

PTImporta reconhecer situações em que a utilidade economicamente fruída depende estruturalmente de ambiente administrado, modulado e mutável por governança privada

What is asserted is that, whenever the consumed utility presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there will be an immaterial product in a legally relevant sense for the application of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1.p. 718

PTO que se afirma é que, sempre que a utilidade consumida se apresente como posição de fruição sustentada por governança privada contínua, haverá produto imaterial em sentido juridicamente relevante para a incidência do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC .

The performance does not disappear, but appears as a mode of sustaining, updating and shaping the object of consumption itself. Therefore, the old question of whether the case involves a product or a service, taken in an exclusionary key, loses much of its analytical usefulness. The problem becomes the structure of supply and the way it organizes, preserves or degrades the acquired experience.p. 718

PTA prestação não desaparece, mas comparece como modo de sustentação, atualização e conformação do próprio objeto de consumo. Por isso, a antiga pergunta sobre se o caso envolve produto ou serviço, tomada em chave excludente, perde grande parte de sua utilidade analítica. O problema passa a ser a estrutura do fornecimento e o modo como ela organiza, preserva ou degrada a experiência adquirida.

In how many ways does an item have value inside the game?

Inside a game, what you buy can take quite different forms, and it is worth knowing which one yours is. There is what is valued for its appearance, like the skin, which shows something to others and marks your place in the game. And there is what is valued as a path, like an event pass, in which you do not receive a ready-made prize but the right to work through a track of goals and rewards.

There is also what secures your entry and your stay, like the account, which in practice works as a position that can be withdrawn. There are functions, like permissions and conveniences of use that are part of what you pay for. There is time, with seasons, deadlines and items that only come back to the store now and then, or never again. And there is reputation, the recognition the game itself measures, for example by your conduct, and that can lock rewards when it drops.

In real life these forms mix, and a limited edition skin is valued both for its look and for its timing. Telling them apart helps you see exactly what was affected when something changes, even if the item is still there.

Where it comes from

The first category is that of cosmetic-ornamental objects. They are IOCs whose utility is not exhausted in functional performance attributes, since it incorporates symbolic value realized as distinction, belonging and recognition in the ecosystem.p. 489

PTA primeira categoria é a dos objetos cosmético-ornamentais. São OICs cuja utilidade não se esgota em atributos funcionais de desempenho, pois incorpora valor simbólico realizado como distinção, pertencimento e reconhecimento no ecossistema.

The second category is that of progression objects.p. 489

PTA segunda categoria é a dos objetos de progressão.

A first example, typical of a progression object, is the event pass, in which the acquisition is not directed at a ready result, but at access to a metered trajectory of milestones and rewardsp. 491

PTUm primeiro exemplo, típico de objeto de progressão, é o passe de evento, no qual a aquisição não se dirige a um resultado pronto, mas ao acesso a uma trajetória metrificada de marcos e recompensas

The third category is that of access and permanence objects.p. 489

PTA terceira categoria é a dos objetos de acesso e permanência.

These are IOCs marked by intense dependence on account, credentials and eligibility, so that enjoyment takes, in practice, the form of a revocable positionp. 489

PTTrata-se de OICs marcados por dependência intensa de conta, credenciais e elegibilidade, de modo que a fruição assume, na prática, a forma de posição revogável

The fourth category is that of functionality objects. They are instrumental capabilities made available to the consumer under technical and normative parameters administered by the supplier, such as permissions of use, convenience utilities, configuration possibilities and conversion capabilitiesp. 490

PTA quarta categoria é a dos objetos de funcionalidade. São capacidades instrumentais disponibilizadas ao consumidor sob parâmetros técnicos e normativos administrados pelo fornecedor, como permissões de uso, utilidades de conveniência, possibilidades de configuração e capacidades de conversão

The fifth category is that of time objects. It covers temporal structures that condition when, for how long and within which windows enjoyment may occur, such as validity regimes, seasons, availability cycles and frequency limits.p. 490

PTA quinta categoria é a dos objetos de tempo. Ela abrange estruturas temporais que condicionam quando, por quanto tempo e sob quais janelas a fruição pode ocorrer, como regimes de validade, temporadas, ciclos de disponibilidade e limites de frequência.

In operational language accessible to the public, certain categories of skins are treated as “legacy” or “limited”, with variations as to whether or not they return to the store and under which conditions, so that the symbolic utility of what was acquired also comes to depend on an institutionally administered temporalityp. 491

PTEm linguagem operacional acessível ao público, certas categorias de skins são tratadas como “legado” ou “limitadas”, com variações sobre se retornam ou não à loja e sob quais condições, de modo que a utilidade simbólica do adquirido passa a depender, também, de uma temporalidade institucionalmente administrada

The sixth category is that of reputation objects. They correspond to positions of recognition instituted by the ecosystem through metrics, validation criteria and visibility devices.p. 490

PTA sexta categoria é a dos objetos de reputação. Correspondem a posições de reconhecimento instituídas pelo ecossistema por métricas, critérios de validação e dispositivos de visibilidade.

The honor system operates as a mechanism of comparable and socially intelligible recognition, but, at the same time, as everyday discipline, since behavior penalties can lock reputational progression and affect, in practical terms, access to rewards associated with loot craftingp. 492

PTO sistema de honra opera como mecanismo de reconhecimento comparável e socialmente inteligível, mas, ao mesmo tempo, como disciplina cotidiana, pois penalidades de comportamento podem travar a progressão reputacional e afetar, de forma prática, o acesso a recompensas associadas à criação de espólios

The proposed categories neither eliminate the complexity of the ecosystem nor intend to reduce concrete objects to a single type. They provide comparable language to break down hybrid arrangements into controllable dimensions and to locate, with precision, which aspects were affected by a relevant modulation.p. 492

PTAs categorias propostas não eliminam a complexidade do ecossistema nem pretendem reduzir objetos concretos a um único tipo. Elas fornecem linguagem comparável para decompor arranjos híbridos em dimensões controláveis e localizar, com precisão, quais aspectos foram afetados por uma modulação relevante.

This typology is deliberately internal to the games laboratory and is not offered as a general classification of platforms.p. 489

PTEssa tipologia é deliberadamente interna ao laboratório de jogos e não se oferece como classificação geral de plataformas.

Does your case answer yes to the four questions?

Not everything digital falls into this category, and it is good to know this before you complain. What decides it is not whether the item is digital or what the company calls it. It is how much what you use depends on an environment the platform governs. Four questions help you see this, and your case needs to answer yes to all of them.

The first is what was promised. It is not enough to say that some content was available. You need to say which utility was sold, that is, what the skin was supposed to let you do and mean, and this cannot be drawn from slogans or ornamental lists.

The second is whether the purchase opened a position that lasts. In continuous consumption, buying is not just receiving a package and being done. It is gaining a place in the game that needs to keep existing for the thing to be of use.

The third is whether the platform really governs that place. It is not enough for there to be technology along the way. There need to be internal rules, choices about what appears and punishments that shape your use and bring consequences for you.

The fourth is whether the thing can get worse without disappearing. This is the case in which access continues, but what you had is downgraded, emptied or reorganized against what was promised. A one-day glitch or a neutral change on the screen does not count.

Where it comes from

The criterion, therefore, is neither the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

As a filter, it defines what enters and what remains outside the field of application, preventing any digital relationship with an appearance of continuity from being absorbed by the category and also preventing the supplier from expelling the conflict from the CDC through strategic labelingp. 428

PTComo filtro, define o que entra e o que permanece fora do campo de incidência, impedindo que qualquer relação digital com aparência de continuidade seja absorvida pela categoria e impedindo, também, que o fornecedor expulse o conflito do CDC por rotulagem estratégica

The relevant question is not whether there was “content” available at some moment, but which utility was economically offered in terms of functionalities and meanings that, under the IOC regime, constitute what was acquired.p. 428

PTA pergunta relevante não é se houve um “conteúdo” disponível em algum momento, mas qual utilidade foi economicamente ofertada em termos de funcionalidades e significações que, no regime do OIC, constituem o adquirido.

The criterion is not satisfied by ornamental lists of functionalities or by generic advertising slogans.p. 428

PTO critério não se satisfaz com listas ornamentais de funcionalidades nem com slogans publicitários genéricos.

In continuous consumption, the acquisition inaugurates a position in the ecosystem and not a delivery exhausted in an instant.p. 428

PTEm consumo contínuo, a aquisição inaugura uma posição no ecossistema e não uma entrega exaurida em um instante.

The filter here is decisive, since the presence of technical mediation in a trivial sense is not enough. What matters is the existence of internal criteria and executive mechanisms that structurally shape the experience of enjoyment and distribute consequences to the consumer.p. 428

PTO filtro aqui é decisivo, pois não basta a presença de mediação técnica em sentido trivial. Importa a existência de critérios internos e de mecanismos executivos que conformam estruturalmente a experiência de fruição e distribuem consequências ao consumidor.

The task is to delimit the zone in which enjoyment continues to exist formally, but is downgraded, emptied or reconfiguredp. 429

PTTrata-se de delimitar a zona em que a fruição continua existindo formalmente, mas é rebaixada, esvaziada ou reconfigurada

The qualification roadmap must require the demonstration of a pattern of degradation that is not to be confused with ordinary variations, occasional instabilities or neutral interface adjustments.p. 429

PTO roteiro de qualificação deve exigir a demonstração de um padrão de degradação que não se confunde com variações ordinárias, instabilidades pontuais ou ajustes neutros de interface.

Application is decided neither by technological intuition nor by automatic deference to the contract, but by the cumulative presence of verifiable elements of the regimep. 429

PTA incidência não se decide por intuição tecnológica nem por deferência automática ao contrato, mas pela presença cumulativa de elementos verificáveis do regime

Does every annoyance with the game give you a right?

And not every annoyance gives you a right. This category is not meant to turn every discomfort of digital use into a loss the law repairs, or to condemn every technical choice the company makes. An update, a change on the screen or a new order in the game's lists is not unlawful in itself. What counts is a serious and lasting decline that can be recognized by clear criteria and that hits the core of what was sold, even when you still have access.

Some cases are left out from the start. A service that happens once and ends there, without a position that lasts, follows the ordinary rules for services. A platform that only works as a shop window, without really governing what you use, also tends to be left out. And a change in appearance alone, when symbolic value was not part of the core of what was sold, is as a rule not enough.

There are also cases in which the core of the problem lies elsewhere, such as an improper charge, a one-off defect, an isolated misleading ad or an offer that was not honored. In those cases ordinary consumer law tends to settle the matter without this category, and you are still protected that way.

Where it comes from

Recognizing it means treating as legally relevant the elements that define the consumer's permanence in the regime of enjoyment, without converting mere ordinary discomforts into legal lossesp. 428

PTReconhecê-la significa tratar como juridicamente relevantes os elementos que definem a permanência do consumidor no regime de fruição, sem converter meros desconfortos ordinários em perdas jurídicas

The IOC filter cannot ignore the structural features of platform-governed consumption nor, at the opposite extreme, aim to capture each and every discomfort of digital usep. 429

PTO filtro do OIC não pode ignorar os traços estruturais do consumo governado por plataforma nem, no extremo oposto, pretender capturar todo e qualquer desconforto do uso digital

The category neither expands the unlawful act indefinitely nor moralizes technical choices.p. 426

PTA categoria não amplia indefinidamente o ilícito nem moraliza escolhas técnicas.

The reconfigurability of the environment does not turn any update, interface adjustment, ranking change or ordinary curation choice into unlawfulness. What becomes legally relevant is significant, persistent degradation recognizable by criteria, the kind that alters the core of the economically offered utility or structurally downgrades the conditions of enjoyment that gave meaning to the acquisition.p. 426

PTA reconfigurabilidade do ambiente não transforma qualquer atualização, ajuste de interface, mudança de ranqueamento ou escolha ordinária de curadoria em ilicitude. O que se torna juridicamente relevante é a degradação significativa, persistente e reconhecível por critérios, aquela que altera o núcleo de utilidade economicamente ofertada ou rebaixa, de modo estrutural, as condições de fruição que deram sentido à aquisição.

The first limit is occasional access without a sustained position of enjoyment.p. 433

PTO primeiro limite é o acesso pontual sem posição de fruição sustentada.

The second limit is the case in which the platform works as a channel or shop window, but does not govern, in structural terms, the acquired utilityp. 434

PTO segundo limite é a hipótese em que a plataforma funciona como canal ou vitrine, mas não governa, em termos estruturais, a utilidade adquirida

The third limit concerns situations in which symbolic or semiotic value is functionally accessory and is not part of the core of the promised enjoyment bundle.p. 434

PTO terceiro limite recai sobre situações em que o valor simbólico ou semiótico é funcionalmente acessório e não integra o núcleo do feixe de fruição prometido.

Therefore, as a rule, predominantly aesthetic changes fall outside the reach of the conceptp. 434

PTPor isso, em regra, ficam fora do alcance do conceito alterações predominantemente estéticas

The fifth limit is the case in which the core of the conflict is something else, such as a classic occasional defect, undue charge, localized misleading advertising or specific nonperformance of an offer. In these cases, common consumer law dogmatics tends to resolve the dispute with no real gain in mobilizing the IOC.p. 435

PTO quinto limite é o caso em que o núcleo do conflito é outro, como vício pontual clássico, cobrança indevida, publicidade enganosa localizada ou inadimplemento específico de oferta. Nessas hipóteses, a dogmática consumerista comum tende a resolver o litígio sem ganho real em mobilizar o OIC.

Why does this category make so much difference? Because, when a legal criterion for a loss is missing, it becomes hard to show it, prove it and hold someone accountable for it. And this void does not stay still. It becomes room for the company to profit.

A rule whose fine is almost never applied shows how this works. If enforcement is rare and punishment unlikely, the risk stops acting as a brake and gets a price tag, as if it were just another operating cost. That is how every lasting gap tends to become a business model. Giving a legal name to what you lose is the first step toward making that loss visible and something you can claim.

Where it comes from

When categories capable of making certain losses legally visible, of naming, demonstrating and imputing them, are lacking, the space of irresponsibility does not remain neutral and comes to function as a margin for optimization.p. 25

PTQuando faltam categorias capazes de tornar certas perdas juridicamente visíveis, de nomeá-las, demonstrá-las e imputá-las, o espaço da irresponsabilidade não permanece neutro e passa a funcionar como margem de otimização.

When enforcement is rare and sanction is unlikely, risk ceases to act as a brake and comes to be priced, as if it were merely an operating cost.p. 25

PTQuando a fiscalização é rara e a sanção é improvável, o risco deixa de atuar como freio e passa a ser precificado, como se fosse apenas custo operacional.

It produces predictable institutional effects, because every stable dogmatic gap tends to be converted into a business model.p. 25

PTEla produz efeitos institucionais previsíveis, porque toda lacuna dogmática estável tende a ser convertida em modelo de negócio.

What can you demand?

Information has to be of real useInformation has to be of real use

What if the information exists but gives you no guidance?

When the platform trades clarity for walls of text, scatters information across different places, breaks the trail for anyone trying to understand, or offers transparency that is only for show. Think of important rules buried in a huge contract, in FAQs nobody can find or in patch notes nobody can link to what happened to your item.

What information must the platform give?

To give information that is genuinely useful, that helps you decide and that can be checked later if there is a dispute. Information that exists somewhere but guides no one does not meet this duty.

How do you show what you were told and what was left out?

What was shown to you, at what moment, with what prominence and through which channel. And whether, the way everything was organized, it was possible to understand what mattered in order to buy, to stay or to react.

What can you ask for when the information fell short?

That the platform correct the way it informs and make visible what matters. That it present what it actually displayed. And that it restore what you lost because of the lack of clarity. Depending on the case, the failure may be treated as a defect in the quality of the information or as an abusive practice. It may even lead to the invalidity of clauses that contradict what you had reason to expect.

Is the real question whether you read the contract?

The discussion is not about whether you read the contract or not. It is about whether, the way the information was organized, it served to guide your decision.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform replaces legibility with textual excess, strategic dispersion, fragmentation of informational paths or merely performative transparency, there will be inadequacy in the duty to inform.p. 744

PTSempre que a plataforma substituir legibilidade por excesso textual, dispersão estratégica, fragmentação de trajetórias informacionais ou transparência apenas performática, haverá inadequação do dever de informar.

Information, under the CDC, does not fulfill its function merely because it exists somewhere, nor because it was deposited in lengthy documents, cross-references, FAQs or update histories that the consumer is unlikely to locate, understand and connect to the impactp. 641

PTA informação, no CDC, não cumpre sua função só porque existe em algum lugar, nem porque foi depositada em documentos extensos, remissões, FAQs ou históricos de atualização que o consumidor dificilmente localizará, compreenderá e conectará ao impacto

The corresponding duty of supply is to provide materially adequate information, useful for the decision and verifiable at a later moment of controversy.p. 744

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de prestar informação materialmente adequada, útil para a decisão e verificável em momento posterior de controvérsia.

The minimum required proof calls for identification of what was shown, at what moment, with what salience, through which channels and to what extent this organization allowed the consumer to understand what was legally relevant in order to adhere, remain or react.p. 744

PTA prova mínima exigível requer identificação do que foi mostrado, em que momento, com qual saliência, por quais canais e em que medida essa organização permitia ao consumidor compreender o que era juridicamente relevante para aderir, permanecer ou reagir.

The legal qualification tends to apply as a defect in the informational quality of the supply, an abusive practice by functional obscuring and, depending on the case, grounds for the invalidity of clauses or limitations incompatible with the legitimately formed expectation.p. 744

PTA qualificação jurídica tende a incidir como vício de qualidade informacional do fornecimento, prática abusiva por obscurecimento funcional e, conforme o caso, suporte para invalidade de cláusulas ou de limitações incompatíveis com a expectativa legitimamente formada.

The possible institutional consequence includes a duty to correct the communicational arrangement, reinforcement of useful transparency, orders to display and restoration of the enjoyment affected by decisional opacity.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional possível inclui dever de correção do arranjo comunicacional, reforço de transparência útil, ordens de exibição e recomposição da fruição afetada por opacidade decisória.

The point is not to assert that the consumer “did not read” or “did not understand”p. 401

PTO ponto não é afirmar que o consumidor “não leu” ou “não entendeu”
