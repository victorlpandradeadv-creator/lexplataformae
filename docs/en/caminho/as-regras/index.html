Who is in charge after the purchase?

When you created the account, you accepted the terms of use. It looks as if the rules of the game are all there, in that long text. But the rules that weigh on your daily life are almost never only on paper.

They are in the screen that shows one path and hides another, in the chat that gets cut off in the middle of a match and in the penalty that arrives without warning. The text promises and the system executes. And the penalty is applied by the same company that wrote the rule, designed the screen and keeps the records of what happened. That is why a conflict, when there is one, is rarely only about what a clause says. It is about who governs what you can do in there.

What are the three layers of the platform's rule?

The platform's rule works in three layers, one on top of the other.

The first is the text. These are the terms of use, the conduct policies, the moderation rules and the criteria for taking part. There you find the permissions, the prohibitions and what is expected of you.

The second is the design. This is when the text becomes a screen, a button, a path, an access limit and a visibility criterion. In this layer, the rule stops being a sentence and starts deciding what is possible before any discussion. If the screen has no button, it makes no difference that the contract allows it.

The third is enforcement. These are the mechanisms that grade penalties, restrict features, lower your visibility, suspend the account and, at the extreme, kick you out. A rule only carries real weight when it passes through all three layers, because only then does it change what you can do and what happens to you.

Where it comes from

The law of the platformLex Plataformae

In Latin, which is where the name was born, it is called Lex Plataformae, and plataformae means of the platform. But the law of the platform is not a contract sitting in a drawer. It is an order that runs like a program, aims at an economic result and seems natural because it is repeated so often.

It defines the rules, decides what is seen and what stays hidden, applies the penalties and even establishes how anything is proven in there. And it does not just announce the rules. It classifies, records, recommends, blocks, restricts and changes the very environment in which you try to use what you bought.

The name comes from another old expression, the law merchant, which referred to the rules that traders created among themselves, without depending on the law of any country. The difference is that here the center is not the practice of many traders. It is a single company, which controls the way in, the way you understand what happens and the conditions under which you can use what is yours.

Where it comes from

The variation “plataformae” corresponds to the genitive singular of the first declension, signaling “of the platform”p. 27

PTA variação “plataformae” corresponde ao genitivo singular de primeira declinação, sinalizando “da plataforma”

The expression Lex Plataformae therefore seeks to name not a set of predisposed clauses, but a private ordering that is technically executable, economically oriented and semiotically naturalized.p. 27

PTA expressão Lex Plataformae pretende nomear, portanto, não um conjunto de cláusulas predispostas, mas uma ordenação privada tecnicamente executável, economicamente orientada e semioticamente naturalizada.

[...] because, while the Lex Mercatoria is usually described as an ordering emerging from diffuse practices and multiple actors, the Lex Plataformae designates a type of private ordering centered on a platform as the institutional core that defines rules, visibilities, sanctions and evidentiary regimes through continuous architecture and updating.p. 27

PT[...] porque, enquanto a Lex Mercatoria costuma ser descrita como ordenação emergente de práticas difusas e de múltiplos atores, a Lex Plataformae designa um tipo de ordenação privada centrada em uma plataforma enquanto núcleo institucional que define regras, visibilidades, sanções e regimes de prova por meio de arquitetura e atualização contínuas.

It does not merely interpret behavior, but classifies, records, prioritizes, makes invisible, recommends, blocks, expels, restricts, monetizes and reconfigures the very environment in which the user tries to exercise enjoyment of the acquired object.p. 27

Lex Plataformae is an operative Latinization built by analogy with classical formulas for naming private orderings, especially the Lex Mercatoria, an expression used to designate an a-national body of rules and principles associated with the practice of international tradep. 27

This law does one thing well, which is to look like custom. A practice that repeats many times starts to seem normal, as if everyone had agreed to it. But on a platform, repetition may be just the effect of a design that one company chose on its own and that you had no way to refuse.

That is why the fact that something has always been this way in the game does not prove that it is legitimate. Often, what looks like a market habit is already the product of a unilateral choice, which took hold because nobody had an alternative.

Where it comes from

Often, what presents itself as market habit or custom of the ecosystem is already the product of unilateral design, of structural informational asymmetry and of the naturalization of choices previously filtered by the architecture itself.p. 28

PTNa plataforma, a repetição de práticas e soluções não deve ser lida ingenuamente como sinal suficiente de legitimidade normativa. Muitas vezes, aquilo que se apresenta como hábito do mercado ou costume do ecossistema já é produto de desenho unilateral, de assimetria informacional estrutural e de naturalização de escolhas previamente filtradas pela própria arquitetura.

The critique is relevant because it prevents romanticizing, under the language of custom, what may in truth correspond to the technical stabilization of private impositions.p. 28

PTA crítica é relevante porque impede romantizar, sob a linguagem do costume, o que em verdade pode corresponder à estabilização técnica de imposições privadas.

How does your behavior become a score?

In a reputation system, your behavior becomes a score. One online game created an Honor system. Players who show good sportsmanship earn benefits, and those who behave badly get fewer rewards and lose part of their right to talk in chat.

When you are penalized, with a temporary suspension or a chat cutoff, your score drops, your progress freezes and the account goes without certain rewards until you show, consistently, that you have changed. And the company says the score reflects behavior in and out of the game. None of this goes through any court. The penalty is built into the system itself and affects what you can do, earn and show.

Where it comes from

In official documentation, Riot presents Honor as a system that rewards sporting conduct with additional benefits and penalizes inappropriate behavior with reduced rewards and limited communication privilegesp. 343

PTEm documentação oficial, a Riot apresenta a Honra como sistema que recompensa conduta esportiva com benefícios adicionais e penaliza comportamentos inadequados com recompensas reduzidas e privilégios de comunicação limitados

In parallel, the company states that penalties such as temporary suspension and chat restrictions drop the Honor level, freeze progress in the system and make the account ineligible for rewards linked to the Hextech Crafting regime until there is consistent demonstration of behavioral “reform”p. 343

PTEm paralelo, a empresa afirma que penalidades como suspensão temporária e restrições de chat derrubam o nível de Honra, travam o progresso no sistema e tornam a conta inelegível para recompensas vinculadas ao regime de Hextech Crafting até que haja demonstração consistente de “reforma” comportamental

Official support describes Honor as a general reflection of behavior “in and out of game”p. 355

PTO suporte oficial descreve a Honra como reflexo geral do comportamento “dentro e fora de jogo”

This does not mean the company sabotages players on purpose, and nobody needs to prove that. The point is simpler. The company builds the game, measures the matches, controls the chat channels and sets the criteria for penalties. But it does not show you how those criteria work to the same extent that it applies their effects to you.

That is why, in a conflict, the question is not only whether you broke a rule. It is also whether the criteria used to say you broke it, and to penalize you, are legitimate.

Where it comes from

The point, therefore, is not to sustain, without internal proof, a maximalist thesis of deliberate sabotage, but to recognize that the company governs a competitive environment that is structurally opaque, probabilistic and administered by internal criteria whose deeper intelligibility is not shared with the consumer to the same degree that their effects are imposed on them.p. 343

PTO ponto, portanto, não está em sustentar, sem prova interna, uma tese maximalista de sabotagem deliberada, mas em reconhecer que a empresa governa um ambiente competitivo estruturalmente opaco, probabilístico e administrado por critérios internos cuja inteligibilidade aprofundada não é compartilhada com o consumidor na mesma medida em que seus efeitos lhe são impostos.

Seen this way, the central question is no longer only whether the user broke a rule and comes to include the legitimacy of the very criteria by which the deviation is produced, read and punished.p. 343

PTNessa chave, a questão central deixa de ser saber apenas se o usuário infringiu uma regra e passa a incluir a legitimidade dos próprios critérios pelos quais o desvio é produzido, lido e punido.

Power and subjectivityChoice architecture

Nobody forbids you anything. The path the company wants you to take just becomes easier, more rewarded and more visible, while the other one becomes more costly, more frustrating and harder to find.

It is like a supermarket that puts the candy at eye level and the vegetables at the back of the store. You are still free to choose, but you choose along a route that someone designed. In the game, this goes beyond the options that appear on the screen, because it includes the consequences that make one path rewarded and the other painful.

Where it comes from

Transposed to platform governance, this means that choice architecture is not limited to the presentation of options, because it also involves managing the consequences that make certain trajectories more fluid, rewarded and legible, while others become more costly, frustrating or marginalp. 337

PTTransposto para a governança de plataforma, isso significa que arquitetura de escolha não se resume à apresentação de opções, porque envolve também a administração das consequências que tornam certas trajetórias mais fluidas, recompensadas e legíveis, enquanto outras se tornam mais custosas, frustrantes ou marginais

Informational domination, thus, bears not only on what the consumer knows, but on the very practical economy of their possibilities of acting.p. 337

PTA dominação informacional, assim, não incide apenas sobre o que o consumidor sabe, mas sobre a própria economia prática de suas possibilidades de agir.

LawInformational power

This power does not need police. It creates dependence, shapes your behavior and makes certain ways of taking part seem normal. And it uses technical means that go far beyond the contract.

One detail makes all this harder to see. The simpler the screen, the better hidden the complexity that makes the decisions behind it. You see a clean button and a friendly color, and you do not see the calculations and judgments running underneath.

Where it comes from

Cohen is drawn on to describe the platform as a form of organizing power that produces dependencies, structures conduct and naturalizes patterns of participation through technical and institutional means that are not exhausted in the contract.p. 262

PTCohen é mobilizada para descrever a plataforma como forma de poder organizador que produz dependências, estrutura condutas e naturaliza padrões de participação por meios técnicos e institucionais que não se esgotam no contrato.

This becomes more acute when systems display simplicity on the surface and complexity in their internal functioning, which makes it difficult to translate underlying judgments into forms suited to public understandingp. 340

PTIsso se torna mais agudo quando os sistemas exibem simplicidade na superfície e complexidade no funcionamento interno, o que dificulta traduzir julgamentos subjacentes para formas adequadas à compreensão pública

LawPractical normativity

A rule does not need to come from Congress to work as a rule. What the company calls a condition of use or an internal rule can work as a real order, imposed by whoever has power over the place. It organizes access, permanence and penalties and produces effects on many people at the same time.

That is why it is not enough to say that everything comes down to freedom of contract. And the market is not a force of nature that runs on its own, because it is an order that it sets up itself.

Where it comes from

Eros Roberto Grau contributes to recognizing that normativity can operate as an effective order imposed by an organization endowed with power, making it possible to qualify as practical governance what presents itself as a “condition of use” or an “internal rule”p. 263

PTEros Roberto Grau contribui para reconhecer que a normatividade pode operar como ordem efetiva imposta por organização dotada de poder, permitindo qualificar como governança prática aquilo que se apresenta como “condição de uso” ou “regra interna”

PTEsse vocabulário permite tratar regras internas, arquitetura de implementação e mecanismos de enforcement como direito efetivo do ambiente em sentido operacional, pois organizam acessos, permanência e sanções com capacidade concreta de impor padrões e produzir efeitos coletivos, sem neutralizar o fenômeno como simples exercício formal de autonomia contratual

How far does the game's rule reach into your computer?

The law of the platform can go beyond the screen and reach your computer. To protect competition against cheating, one game began to require an anti-cheat program that runs at the deepest layer of the operating system, the same layer that controls how the machine works. This program starts up with the computer, even before you open the game, and can stop you from playing if it is turned off or removed.

The terms of use also admit that the company may monitor the use of its services on your own device, and not only on its servers. The problem here goes beyond the annoyance of an invasive program. The rule no longer applies only during the match and starts to redraw how much control you have over your own equipment.

Where it comes from

When protection of the IOC's competitive environment comes to depend on software loaded at kernel level, that is, on the deepest and most privileged layer of the operating systemp. 521

PTQuando a proteção do ambiente concorrencial do OIC passa a depender de software carregado em nível de kernel, isto é, na camada mais profunda e privilegiada do sistema operacional

Riot's own official communications show that Vanguard is presented as a system composed of a client and a kernel-mode driver, started at computer boot and maintained as a structural condition of enjoyment in more than one game ecosystem, with the possibility of preventing use of the service if it is disabled or removedp. 521

PTAs comunicações oficiais da própria Riot mostram que o Vanguard é apresentado como sistema composto por client e driver em modo kernel, iniciado no boot do computador e mantido como condição estrutural da fruição em mais de um ecossistema de jogo, com possibilidade de impedir o uso do serviço caso seja desativado ou removido

Riot contractually admits the possibility of actively monitoring the use of its services not only on its own servers, but also on the consumer's computer or devicep. 521

PTA Riot admite contratualmente a possibilidade de monitorar ativamente o uso de seus serviços não apenas em seus próprios servidores, mas também no computador ou dispositivo do consumidor

This fact shifts the legal problem beyond an intuitive irritation with “intrusive” software. What is at stake is the redefinition of the material boundaries of the user's sovereignty over their own equipment.p. 521

PTEsse dado desloca o problema jurídico para além da irritação intuitiva com software “intrusivo”. O que está em jogo é a redefinição das fronteiras materiais de soberania do usuário sobre seu próprio equipamento.

Consumer-citizenConsumer-citizen

So you are not just a customer. You are a consumer, because you bought something and have a legitimate expectation of using it. But your presence inside depends on rules of belonging and continuity that someone else sets, and on penalties that change your standing.

That is why you can also be called a citizen, in a very precise sense. Not a citizen of a country, with a nationality and a Constitution, but a participant in an environment governed by rules, design and penalties. When the company changes these conditions on its own, it changes, in practice, the very thing you are using.

You are also at stake. In a game, your place depends on being seen, being recognized and belonging to a group, so losing reputation, visibility or recognition is not a mere annoyance. LawCategories that keep pace with technology

This has happened before. At the end of the nineteenth century, instant photography and a growing press began to intrude on people's lives in ways the old law could not reach. That was when people argued for a right to privacy, the right to be let alone.

Adjusting the categories of law, in these cases, is not moralism or a grudge against technology. It means keeping the effects of new forms of power and intrusion under control when technology changes the scale at which people become vulnerable.

Where it comes from

In their article, Warren and Brandeis ([201-?]) argue that recent inventions and expanding business practices intensified forms of intrusion that the legal order, if it remains tied to earlier schemes, cannot adequately capture.p. 347

PTEm seu artigo, Warren e Brandeis ([201-?]) sustentam que invenções recentes e práticas empresariais em expansão intensificaram formas de intrusão que o ordenamento, se permanecer preso a esquemas anteriores, não consegue capturar adequadamente.

From there, the authors argue for the recognition of a right to privacy as a legitimate right to be let alonep. 347-348

PTA partir daí, os autores defendem o reconhecimento de um direito à privacidade enquanto legítimo direito de ser deixado em paz

When the technical environment reorganizes vulnerability at scale, legal reconstruction is not moralism, but a necessary response to keep the effects of new forms of power and intrusion controllable in institutional terms.p. 348

PTQuando o ambiente técnico reorganiza a vulnerabilidade em escala, a reconstrução jurídica não é moralismo, mas resposta necessária para manter controláveis, em termos institucionais, os efeitos de novas formas de poder e de intrusão.

So does the platform become a State?

The platform does not become a State because of this, nor does it stand above the law of the country. The law of the platform does not replace Brazilian law. It is a layer of private rules that sits between you and what you bought, and that the law cannot treat as a mere technical backdrop. Nor is it about taking any game dispute to the Constitution.

What can be demanded is proportional to the power the company exercises. Transparency that actually serves a purpose, a minimum of stability, reasons that can be checked, a real way to contest and the restoration of what was lost.

Where it comes from

The Lex Plataformae, thus understood, does not replace State law or eliminate the application of the public legal order. It designates, rather, the private normative layer that interposes itself between the subject and enjoyment, organizing the concrete experience of consumption by means that the Law cannot treat as a mere technological backdrop.p. 28

PTA Lex Plataformae, assim compreendida, não substitui o Direito estatal nem elimina a incidência do ordenamento público. Ela designa, antes, a camada normativa privada que se interpõe entre o sujeito e a fruição, organizando a experiência concreta do consumo por meios que o Direito não pode tratar como simples pano de fundo tecnológico.

This does not mean equating the platform with the State or constitutionalizing every private disputep. 348

PTIsso não implica equiparar plataforma a Estado nem constitucionalizar toda disputa privada

This characterization blocks the generic constitutionalization of the litigation and avoids the statist leap.p. 380

PTEssa caracterização bloqueia a constitucionalização genérica do litígio e evita o salto estatizante.

For this reason, proportional parameters of qualified transparency, minimum stability, verifiable reasoning, contestability and restoration are required.p. 364

PTPor isso, impõem-se parâmetros proporcionais de transparência qualificada, estabilidade mínima, motivação verificável, contestabilidade e recomposição.

When does a penalty go too far?

When your access, your permanence, your visibility or your chance to complain depend on penalties that nobody explains. Or on hurdles placed in front of only some people, on a cost too high to push back or on a way back that is much harder than the way in. It is the account blocked or banned without explanation, with an appeal route that leads nowhere. When the account is closed, access to everything in it goes too, including what you bought.

What must the platform do before penalizing?

Follow procedures that can be understood, give a reason that can be checked, let you contest in a reasonable way and not treat you arbitrarily.

How do you document the penalty and the attempt to contest it?

The records of the penalty, the appeal route that existed, the criteria used, the hurdles to undoing the measure and what it did to the continuity of your experience.

What review can be requested for the penalty?

If the penalty has no verifiable reason or no reasonable appeal route, it may be possible to have it reviewed or reversed. You can also ask the platform to fulfill its procedural duties and to create or improve a real appeal route. When the problem happens to many people, you can also ask for the penalty system as a whole to be corrected.

So the platform cannot penalize at all?

The platform has the power to penalize, but having that power is not enough. A penalty needs a reason that can be checked, consistency from one decision to the next and a clear way to challenge it.
