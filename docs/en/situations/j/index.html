Situation j) · 7.1.2 of the thesis

When intellectual property becomes a shield

j) reconciliation with intellectual property

Which facts show exclusivity used as a shield?

The supplier invokes exclusive ownership, trade secrets, licensing or the proprietary structure of the ecosystem to resist control over the mode of supply. Typical answers say that the data is for internal use or not accessible to the public.

How do you separate protection of the asset from immunization of governance?

The relation between the prerogative invoked and the concrete aspect of the conflict, showing whether the restriction is necessary to protect the asset or operates as immunization of governance.

Which rules tie exclusivity to the social interest?

Article 5, item XXIX, of the Federal Constitution1 ties the protection of industrial creations, trademarks and other signs to the social interest and to development, and does not protect exclusivity as an end in itself. Property is subject to its social function, and consumer protection is part of the economic order. Articles 7 and 8 of Law 9,609/1998, the Software Law2, impose on the software supplier duties of information and of support during technical validity. Protection of computer programs in Brazil follows a special regime that is predominantly copyright-based, and not a patent logic over function, method or result, so technical control of the infrastructure does not turn into total power over the experience consumed.

Is intellectual property a general veto over the CDC?

Intellectual property remains valid on its own level and is not a general veto over the CDC. Reconciliation is achieved through delimited auditability, with instruments that preserve confidentiality, such as judicial secrecy, through control of functional equivalence and by subjecting the exercise of exclusivity to the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration. When the service is shut down or servers are permanently switched off, the formal precariousness of the license does not authorize the total sacrifice of what was incorporated into the consumer's trajectory. The exit must be reconciled, as far as technically and legally possible, with continuity, portability, functional restoration or transfer of the operation. The intellectual property infrastructure is also not homogeneous. Trademarks, copyright, software and distinctive signs do not confer the same type or the same extent of exclusivity, and so technical control of the environment does not derive from a unitary sovereignty over the experience consumed. Exclusivity is not to be confused with immunity from auditability. Intellectual property matters here only insofar as it organizes power over enjoyment. It should be neither reduced nor demonized, but its exclusivity does not authorize opacity, opportunistic mutability or armor against control.

The law cited on this page

  1. Article 5, item XXIX, of the Federal Constitution ↩

    “Art. 5. [...] XXIX - the law shall ensure to the authors of industrial inventions a temporary privilege for their use, as well as protection for industrial creations, the ownership of trademarks, company names and other distinctive signs, in view of the social interest and the technological and economic development of the Country [...].”

    PT“Art. 5º. [...] XXIX - a lei assegurará aos autores de inventos industriais privilégio temporário para sua utilização, bem como proteção às criações industriais, à propriedade das marcas, aos nomes de empresas e a outros signos distintivos, tendo em vista o interesse social e o desenvolvimento tecnológico e econômico do País [...].”

    Wording transcribed in note 790, p. 694.

  2. Articles 7 and 8 of Law 9,609/1998 ↩

    “Art. 8 Whoever markets a computer program [...] is obliged, in the national territory, during the period of technical validity of the respective version, to ensure to the respective users the provision of complementary technical services relating to the proper functioning of the program, considering its specifications.”

    PT“Art. 8º Aquele que comercializar programa de computador [...] fica obrigado, no território nacional, durante o prazo de validade técnica da respectiva versão, a assegurar aos respectivos usuários a prestação de serviços técnicos complementares relativos ao adequado funcionamento do programa, consideradas as suas especificações.”

    Wording transcribed in note 776, p. 683.

    “Art. 8 Whoever markets a computer program, whether holder of the rights to the program or holder of the marketing rights, is obliged, in the national territory, during the period of technical validity of the respective version, to ensure to the respective users the provision of complementary technical services relating to the proper functioning of the program, considering its specifications. Sole paragraph. The obligation shall persist in the event of withdrawal of the computer program from commercial circulation during the period of validity, except for fair compensation for any losses caused to third parties.”

    PT“Art. 8º Aquele que comercializar programa de computador, quer seja titular dos direitos do programa, quer seja titular dos direitos de comercialização, fica obrigado, no território nacional, durante o prazo de validade técnica da respectiva versão, a assegurar aos respectivos usuários a prestação de serviços técnicos complementares relativos ao adequado funcionamento do programa, consideradas as suas especificações. Parágrafo único. A obrigação persistirá no caso de retirada de circulação comercial do programa de computador durante o prazo de validade, salvo justa indenização de eventuais prejuízos causados a terceiros.”

    Wording transcribed in note 22, p. 51.
