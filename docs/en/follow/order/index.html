You bought on a marketplace. Who chose what you bought?

You searched for a product in an online store with many sellers, chose among the offers on the screen and paid. What arrived was not what you expected, or did not arrive, and the store replied that it only intermediates.

1 Were you seeing the market?

It seemed so. The screen showed dozens of offers, with prices, photos and stars, as if the whole market were there, side by side.

But what reached you had already gone through a selection. The store chose what appears first, what stays hidden and what seems comparable.

Where it comes from

The consumer does not choose in a vacuum, before a transparent and symmetrical universe of offers.p. 762

PTO consumidor não escolhe no vazio, diante de universo transparente e simétrico de ofertas.

The consumer chooses within a field already pre-interpreted by the platform.p. 762

PTEle escolhe dentro de um campo já pré-interpretado pela plataforma.

2 Who decided the order on the screen?

The store. The position of each offer depends on criteria it defines, and part of the shop window is sold to whoever pays for prominence. When the paid ad looks like an ordinary result, you cannot tell one from the other.

That makes the store much more than a corridor through which seller and buyer pass. It manufactures the market you see.

Where it comes from

The platform therefore produces the perceptible market itself.p. 762

PTA plataforma, portanto, produz o próprio mercado perceptível.

3 Were the stars telling the truth?

Stars, trusted-seller badges and best-seller tags weighed on your choice. But you cannot check how those ratings are calculated, who gives them or what is left out of them.

The trust the screen conveys is also organised by the store, along with the order of the offers.

Where it comes from

It administers criteria of presentation, trust, prioritisation and legibility of the market accessible to the consumer.p. 762

PTEla administra critérios de apresentação, confiança, priorização e legibilidade do mercado acessível ao consumidor.

4 Whom did you pay?

The store. The money went through its payment system, often the delivery too, and it keeps the record of everything that happened between the click and your front door.

That is why the store is not just a shop window where others sell. It organises the whole sale.

Where it comes from

In both cases, the platform does not merely host listings.p. 762

PTEm ambos os casos, a plataforma não se limita a hospedar anúncios.

5 And when the order goes wrong?

The product arrives different from the photo, counterfeit or defective, or simply does not arrive. The usual answer is that the store only put you in touch with the seller and that the responsibility is his.

That answer ignores what happened before. The purchase went wrong inside an environment the store built, ordered and sold as trustworthy.

Where it comes from

Platforms of this nature do not merely connect.p. 761

PTPlataformas dessa natureza não apenas conectam.

6 Who decides the dispute?

The store itself. It receives your complaint, hears the seller, looks at the records only it holds and decides who is right.

To understand what happened, looking at the product that arrived is not enough. You have to look at how the purchase was set up, with the order of offers, the ratings, the sponsorship and the delivery promise.

Where it comes from

It is not enough to examine the final product purchased or the individual piece of content removed, prioritized or hidden.p. 763

PTNão basta examinar o produto final adquirido ou o conteúdo individual removido, priorizado ou ocultado.

It is necessary to reconstruct the architecture of the experience.p. 763

PTÉ necessário reconstruir a arquitetura da experiência.

7 What can you demand?

You can demand a replacement, a repair or your money back, under consumer law, also from the store that organised the sale. You can demand clarity about who was selling, what was a paid ad and how reputation was calculated, without the store having to open its internal systems.

The path starts with the store itself, with screenshots of the search, the offer and the purchase. If it does not resolve things, next come Procon, Consumidor.gov.br and the Small Claims Court.

Where it comes from

It becomes necessary to examine, in an IOC key, whether there was adequate information about the nature of the intermediation, whether the distinction between organic and sponsored priority was materially intelligible, whether the reputation displayed was functionally verifiablep. 764

PTTorna-se necessário examinar, em chave de OIC, se houve informação adequada sobre a natureza da intermediação, se a distinção entre prioridade orgânica e prioridade patrocinada foi materialmente inteligível, se a reputação exibida era funcionalmente verificável

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Has this happened before?

In December 2024, the National Consumer Secretariat ordered Amazon and Mercado Livre to remove ads for mobile phones without Anatel certification and without an invoice. On the same subject, Anatel's legal office concluded that Mercado Livre's liability comes from its effective participation in the supply chain.

In an earlier proceeding, about pirated books, Mercado Livre said its terms of use transfer to the seller exclusive responsibility for what is advertised. The Secretariat disagreed and stated that the sale of counterfeits is facilitated by the very existence of the platforms.
