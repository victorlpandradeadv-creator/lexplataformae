Your rights · By problem

The artificial intelligence assistant changed or got it wrong. What can I demand?

You used an artificial intelligence assistant, the kind of program that chats, summarizes and gives guidance, and the answer turned out to be wrong or the service started working differently. The problem goes beyond the wrong sentence, because the value of this service lies in the trust it asks of you and in your chance to reconstruct what happened.

Where it comes from

The service is valuable not only because it answers, but because it induces the user to treat the answer as a relevant point of orientation.p. 758

PTO serviço vale não apenas porque responde, mas porque induz o usuário a tratar a resposta como ponto relevante de orientação.

What is at stake is not only the content issued, but the regime of functional trust in which this content is received, used and converted into conduct.p. 758

PTO que está em jogo não é apenas o conteúdo emitido, mas o regime de confiança funcional em que esse conteúdo é recebido, utilizado e convertido em conduta.

When does the assistant's error stop being just a bad answer?

What matters is the way the answer reached you. There may be a problem when it seems more certain and complete than the service really is. There may also be one when you cannot understand why you got that answer or whether the assistant changed.

Version changes count too, meaning the moments when the company swaps the program running behind the assistant. They can alter results and cut features without canceling your access, so the fact that the assistant still opens does not prove that nothing changed.

The service does not need to have lied for the problem to count. It may be enough that it trades clarity for an appearance of trust and leaves you with the cost of the error, the review or the loss.

Where it comes from

There may be informational noise when the architecture of the answer projects an appearance of safety, completeness or sufficiency incompatible with the real limits of the service. There may be functional opacity when the user cannot reconstruct, in minimally verifiable terms, why a given output was presented to them, to what extent there was a relevant change in the system's behavior or which elements of the environment conditioned the answer that guided their conduct.p. 759

PTPode haver ruído informacional quando a arquitetura da resposta projeta aparência de segurança, completude ou suficiência incompatível com os limites reais da prestação. Pode haver opacidade funcional quando o usuário não consegue reconstruir, em termos minimamente verificáveis, por que determinada saída lhe foi apresentada, em que medida houve alteração relevante do comportamento do sistema ou quais elementos do ambiente condicionaram a resposta que orientou sua conduta.

Modulation is constitutive, given that version changes, parameter adjustments, policy revisions, performance variations and reconfigurations of safeguards can alter results, restrict functionalities or reorganize costs of enjoyment without formal cancellation of access.p. 487

PTA modulação é constitutiva, dado que as mudanças de versão, os ajustes de parâmetros, a revisão de políticas, as variações de desempenho e as reconfigurações de salvaguardas podem alterar resultados, restringir funcionalidades ou reorganizar custos de fruição sem cancelamento formal do acesso.

There may also be acute evidentiary asymmetry when the functional impact of the service depends on history, interactional context, internal parameters or records that remain concentrated with the supplier. In these scenarios, the injury does not need to appear as a direct lie to be legally relevant. It is enough that the environment replaces legibility with a performance of trust, shifting to the user the cost of bearing error, review, oscillation or functional loss without adequate institutional trails of reconstruction.p. 759

PTPode haver, ainda, assimetria probatória aguda quando o impacto funcional da prestação depender de histórico, contexto interacional, parâmetros internos ou registros que permanecem concentrados no fornecedor. Nesses cenários, a lesão não precisa aparecer como mentira direta para ser juridicamente relevante. Basta que o ambiente substitua legibilidade por performance de confiança, deslocando para o usuário o custo de suportar erro, revisão, oscilação ou perda funcional sem trilhas institucionais adequadas de reconstrução.

Which parts of the conversation with the assistant are worth saving?

Save the whole conversation, and not only the wrong answer, including what you asked, the warnings that appeared or were missing and the way the answer was shown. It helps to record what you did based on it, because the weight it carried in your decision is part of the case. A generic warning that the system may make mistakes does not settle the matter on its own, and the answer should not be judged apart from what made it seem reliable.

Part of the history and records stays only with the company. That is why your protection should not depend only on what you managed to save, since the rest was never in your hands.

Where it comes from

The relevant evidence tends to require reconstruction of the informational journey, the context of use, the available interactional history, the warnings present or absent, the way the answer was presented and the practical function it served in the user's decision. It also tends to require caution regarding recurring blind spots. The first is the tendency to reduce the conflict to the sentence issued, ignoring the semiotic architecture that made it reliable or operational. The second is the tendency to presume that generic warnings about the system's limitations would suffice, by themselves, to neutralize any problem of informational adequacy.p. 759-760

PTA evidência relevante tende a exigir reconstrução da jornada informacional, do contexto de uso, do histórico interacional disponível, das advertências presentes ou ausentes, da forma de apresentação da resposta e da função prática que ela exerceu na decisão do usuário. Também tende a exigir cautela quanto a pontos de cegueira recorrentes. O primeiro é a tendência de reduzir o conflito à frase emitida, ignorando a arquitetura semiótica que a tornou confiável ou operacional. O segundo é a tendência de presumir que avisos genéricos sobre limitações do sistema bastariam, por si, para neutralizar qualquer problema de adequação informacional.

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to demand from the consumer a detailed showing of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

What can you ask for besides a correct answer?

Depending on the case, the request may include access to the conversation history and its minimum records. It may also include a sufficient explanation of the limits that matter and clarity about changes in how the service can be used. The goal is to make it possible to link what was promised to the effect you suffered.

The failure may be treated as a problem with the quality of the information the service delivers or as a breach of the duty to inform. It may also be read as an abusive practice, which is conduct that Brazilian consumer law prohibits, when it stems from a lack of transparency about how the service works. The most fitting response tends to be correcting how it works and communicates and restoring trust, rather than simply paying compensation.

Where it comes from

It is a matter of recognizing that, in services whose usefulness lies in cognitively guiding the user, there must be minimum institutional verifiability over relevant aspects of the service. This includes, depending on the case, preservation of accessible history, minimum interaction traces, sufficient disclosure of materially relevant limits, intelligibility about changes in the regime of use and the possibility of reconstructing the link between the service's functional promise and the impact produced on the user's experience.p. 759

PTTrata-se de reconhecer que, em serviços cuja utilidade consiste em orientar cognitivamente o usuário, deve haver verificabilidade institucional mínima sobre aspectos relevantes da prestação. Isso inclui, conforme o caso, preservação de histórico acessível, rastros mínimos de interação, explicitação suficiente de limites materialmente relevantes, inteligibilidade sobre alterações de regime de uso e possibilidade de reconstruir o nexo entre promessa funcional do serviço e impacto produzido na experiência do usuário.

The possible dogmatic consequence, in this kind of environment, usually centers on an informational quality defect in the supply, abusive practice through functional opacity, insufficiency of the duty to inform and reinforced duties of verifiability compatible with the promised cognitive enjoyment.p. 760

PTA consequência dogmática possível, nesse tipo de ambiente, costuma gravitar em torno de vício de qualidade informacional do fornecimento, prática abusiva por opacidade funcional, insuficiência do dever de informar e reforço de deveres de verificabilidade compatíveis com a fruição cognitiva prometida.

The most appropriate institutional response will tend to favor procedural correction, stronger verifiable trails, a review of the communicational arrangement and functional restoration of the injured trust, rather than the mere abstract monetization of the conflict.p. 760

PTA resposta institucional mais adequada tenderá a privilegiar correção procedimental, reforço de trilhas verificáveis, revisão do arranjo comunicacional e recomposição funcional da confiança lesada, em vez de mera monetização abstrata do conflito.

Where can you take a problem with the assistant?

The first path is usually the company itself, with a request for the history and an explanation of what changed. Since the decisive records tend to stay with it, a lawyer helps when the case depends on asking for those records to be kept and shown.

If the same lack of clarity affects everyone who uses the service, because it is part of the normal way it works, the response can be collective. Procon (the state consumer protection agency), Consumidor.gov.br (the federal government's online complaint platform), the Public Prosecutor's Office and the Public Defender's Office are paths that the channels page details.

Where it comes from

Whoever controls the service controls, to a large extent, the decisive records of the relationship. Logs, version histories, transactions, interface parameters, change notices and relevant technical documentation tend to remain in the supplier's possession. For this reason, operationalizing the category must go hand in hand with requests for the preservation and disclosure of minimum documentation of the supply.p. 608

PTQuem controla o serviço controla, em larga medida, os registros decisivos da relação. Logs, históricos de versão, transações, parâmetros de interface, comunicações de mudança e documentação técnica relevante tendem a permanecer em poder do fornecedor. Por isso, a operacionalização da categoria precisa vir acompanhada de pedidos de preservação e exibição de documentação mínima do fornecimento.

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

How far can your demands on an assistant go?

The protection does not go as far as requiring the company to open up the model, the code or the inner workings of the assistant. What you can ask for is the minimum needed to check what matters in your case.

Not every digital service that changes over time falls under the same protection, and an artificial intelligence label alone does not bring it in. The strongest reading is that the very design of the service exploits those who use it. That reading may fit the most serious cases, when the lack of transparency is a stable part of the way the service is exploited.

Where it comes from

It is not a matter of requiring unrestricted opening of the model, code or internal architecture.p. 759

PTNão se trata de exigir abertura irrestrita de modelo, código ou arquitetura interna.

The legally relevant point is not to assert that every continuous digital service is subject to the same framework, nor to expand the concept on the mere ground that economic life has become digital.p. 713

PTO ponto juridicamente relevante não está em afirmar que toda prestação digital contínua se submete ao mesmo enquadramento, nem em expandir o conceito sob o simples argumento da digitalização da vida econômica.

In more serious cases, above all when the architecture of the service repeatedly manages practical trust without proportionate instruments for reconstruction and contestation, there may be room for a reading in terms of abusive teleology, especially if opacity is not a mere technical residue, but a stable part of the way the environment is exploited.p. 760

PTEm hipóteses mais graves, sobretudo quando a arquitetura do serviço administrar de forma reiterada confiança prática sem instrumentos proporcionais de reconstrução e contestação, pode haver espaço para leitura em chave de teleologia abusiva, especialmente se a opacidade não for mero resíduo técnico, mas parte estável do modo de exploração do ambiente.

The rights behind this case

  • Information has to be of real use
  • A relevant change has to leave a trail
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out
  • The response should restore what you lost

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
