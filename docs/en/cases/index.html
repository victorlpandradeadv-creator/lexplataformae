Case commentaries

The news tells what happened. Here we tell what it means for those who buy, use or depend on a platform in Brazil.

Each case separates the confirmed fact from the version going around, shows what Brazilian law has to do with it and says what you can ask for when it happens to you.

  • I bought the printer. Who decides which cartridge it takes?HP updates printers to reject cartridges that used to work. What happened, what the social media version mixes up and what Brazilian law says.

    Later addition, of 25/11/2026Case commented on 25 November 2026.

How is each case written?

Only what the sources confirm goes in as fact, with the address of each one alongside. When the version going around on social media mixes up or exaggerates a fact, the text says where the mix-up is and where the correction comes from.

The commentary characterizes what happened and accuses no one beyond what the decisions have already said. When a case changes, the news goes in with its date, and the earlier text does not disappear without notice.

What do the cases offer those who practice law?

In Doctrine, each case brings the legal characterization, the provisions that apply and the response the regime points to. It is an exercise in application on public facts, and neither a precedent nor an opinion on a concrete case.
