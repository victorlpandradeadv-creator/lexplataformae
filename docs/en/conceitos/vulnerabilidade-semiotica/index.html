Why doesn't reading the terms of use solve it?Semiotic vulnerability

Inside a game or an app, things are worth what the environment says they are worth. Rarity, prestige, level, reputation and even what counts as a mistake are defined in there. And it is the platform that defines these signs and decides how they change.

That is why you can read every line of the terms of use and still be unable to check whether you received what was promised. Not for lack of attention, but because the meaning of things in there is fixed by rules and records you do not control. The problem is not just knowing little. It is being unable to verify, understand and contest in an environment that someone else organizes.

Where it comes from

[...] as the objective condition of the consumer's exposure to a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in platform-governed ecosystems of enjoyment, in which the production of meaning and the attribution of value depend on codes, metrics and visibility regimes administered by the environment itselfp. 396

PT[...] como a condição objetiva de exposição do consumidor a uma desigualdade estrutural de inteligibilidade e de agência em ecossistemas de fruição governados por plataforma, nos quais a produção de sentido e a atribuição de valor dependem de códigos, métricas e regimes de visibilidade administrados pelo próprio ambiente

the promise is not auditable through individual reading alone, because the meaning that sustains value and enjoyment is stabilized by rules and records of the ecosystem, while the platform controls which correlations will be activated and which reading paths will be made availablep. 281

PTa promessa não é auditável apenas por leitura individual, porque o sentido que sustenta valor e fruição é estabilizado por regras e registros do ecossistema, enquanto a plataforma controla quais correlações serão ativadas e quais percursos de leitura serão tornados disponíveis

Semiotic vulnerability describes, on this plane, a structural loss of the capacity for verification, and not a defect of the consumer's attention.p. 281

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica descreve, nesse plano, uma perda estrutural de capacidade de verificação, e não um defeito de atenção do consumidor.

Semiotic vulnerability

Semiotics is the study of signs and of how they acquire meaning. Vulnerability, here, is the fragile position of someone who lives in an environment where the meaning of things is organized by someone else. It is neither just a lack of information nor just a lack of control over the signs. It is an inequality of understanding and of the capacity to act. When the environment controls what appears, what is recognized and how consequences are distributed, and keeps the criteria hidden, it becomes harder to plan, to foresee what will happen and to contest a decision. This inequality is created by the conditions under which the platform governs the place.

Where it comes from

To make this regime knowable, semiotics is treated here as a material dimension of the legal problem, not as an interpretive ornamentp. 261

PTPara tornar esse regime cognoscível, a semiótica é tratada aqui como dimensão material do problema jurídico, não como ornamento interpretativo

Semiotic vulnerability can be defined, in summary, as the objective condition of the consumer's exposure to a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in platform-governed ecosystems of enjoymentp. 396

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica pode ser definida, em síntese, como a condição objetiva de exposição do consumidor a uma desigualdade estrutural de inteligibilidade e de agência em ecossistemas de fruição governados por plataforma

When the environment controls the parameters of appearance, recognition and commensuration that structure value and belonging, and keeps the criteria for distributing consequences opaque, the possibility of planning conduct, anticipating effects and contesting decisions is structurally weakened.p. 396

PTQuando o ambiente controla os parâmetros de aparecimento, reconhecimento e comensuração que estruturam o valor e o pertencimento, e mantém opacos os critérios de distribuição de consequências, a possibilidade de planejar condutas, antecipar efeitos e contestar decisões enfraquece-se estruturalmente.

Why can the same name hide a different item?

An item is weakened in an update, but keeps its name. The account shows the same item, the shop shows the same name and the showcase shows the same image, as if nothing had changed.

To someone looking from outside, everything is the same. To you, who used the item, something was lost. And the unchanged name becomes evidence in favor of whoever made the change, because without access to the records you cannot demonstrate the difference. Semiotic vulnerability brings exactly this kind of hard-to-prove loss into view.

Where it comes from

The nerf is the limit case because it shows, with precision, how the continuity of the name and of access can work as self-justifying proof, while the relevant delivery shifts in what the environment counts as power and utility.p. 329

PTO nerf é o caso-limite porque mostra, com precisão, como a continuidade do nome e do acesso pode funcionar como prova autojustificante, enquanto a entrega relevante se desloca naquilo que o ambiente conta como potência e utilidade.

Semiotic vulnerability thus names this kind of structural loss of demonstrabilityp. 330

PTVulnerabilidade semiótica nomeia, assim, esse tipo de perda estrutural de demonstrabilidade

Who writes the code of the game's signs?

To read a sign, you need to know the code. A red light only means stop because everyone learned the traffic rule. In the game, rarity, level, badge and border color work the same way, and only make sense to those who know the rule.

The difference is who writes the rule. In there, it is the platform that defines the codes, the categories and what counts as a meaningful difference. And whoever controls how things are shown usually also controls the records that would make it possible to reconstruct what happened, such as old versions of the screens, highlighting criteria and change histories. The inequality of meaning goes hand in hand with an inequality of evidence.

Where it comes from

There is an asymmetry of meaning, linked to control over reading codes, categories and classifications that define what counts as a relevant difference.p. 322

PTHá assimetria de sentido, ligada ao controle de códigos de leitura, categorias e classificações que definem o que conta como diferença relevante.

The particularity of this mechanism lies in the fact that the asymmetry of meaning tends to come with an asymmetry of proof. Whoever controls the form by which the object is made intelligible frequently also controls the records by which this intelligibility can be reconstructed in case of conflict.p. 720

PTA particularidade desse mecanismo está em que a assimetria de sentido tende a vir acompanhada de assimetria de prova. Quem controla a forma pela qual o objeto é tornado inteligível frequentemente controla também os registros pelos quais essa inteligibilidade pode ser reconstruída em caso de conflito.

Logs, interface histories, screen versions, curation criteria, change records, visibility parameters and decision tracesp. 720

Why does the shop window say buy and the contract say license?

In the game's shop, the button says buy. In the terms of use, the same skin becomes virtual content, delivered through an access license tied to the account. You decide based on the word buy and use the item under the rules of the license.

No one has to misread anything for this to happen. It is the environment itself that uses one word in the shop window and another in the contract. And it does no good to say the information was there, because information can exist and still guide no one when it is the screen that drives the experience at the moment of choice. This disadvantage shows up in exactly that friction, which is simple and easy to verify.

Where it comes from

The Terms of Service describe the same items as “Virtual Content” offered under an access license tied to the account Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). The consequence is that what the interface presents as a “purchase”, the contractual instrument requalifies as conditional and institutionally administered access.p. 401

PTOs Termos de Serviço descrevem os mesmos itens como “Conteúdo Virtual” oferecido sob licença de acesso vinculada à conta (Riot Games, 2024e) . A consequência é que aquilo que a interface apresenta como “compra”, o instrumento contratual requalifica como acesso condicionado e institucionalmente administrado.

The relevant asymmetry is not merely a lack of information, since information may exist and, even so, not operate as practical guidance when the language of the interface governs the experience and stabilizes expectations in real time.p. 401

PTA assimetria relevante não é apenas falta de informação, pois a informação pode existir e, ainda assim, não operar como orientação prática quando a linguagem da interface governa a experiência e estabiliza expectativas em tempo real.

It is in this friction, simple and verifiable, that semiotic vulnerability becomes intelligible.p. 401

PTÉ nesse atrito, simples e verificável, que a vulnerabilidade semiótica se torna inteligível.

What if the problem is not that you weren't paying attention?

That is why more information does not always help. The problem is not knowing more, but being able to find your bearings and react in there.

And this changes how the conflict is handled. Instead of arguing over whether you were attentive enough, the discussion turns back to how the environment was built. From there, the platform takes on stronger duties to inform, to advertise clearly and to present evidence, and who has to prove what may even change.

Where it comes from

The problem shifts, then, from "knowing more" to "being able to orient oneself and react".p. 396

PTO problema desloca-se, então, do "saber mais" para o "poder orientar-se e reagir".

The institutional consequence consists in bringing the conflict back to the governance of the environment, avoiding moralization of the consumer's conduct and allowing reinforced duties of information, advertising and proof to apply.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional consiste em reconduzir o conflito à governança do ambiente, evitando moralização da conduta do consumidor e permitindo incidência de deveres reforçados de informação, publicidade e prova.

The possible legal qualification may support abusive practice, informational insufficiency, deceptiveness through ambiguity or relevant omission and, when articulated with other elements, grounds for dynamic redistribution of the burden of proofp. 744

PTA qualificação jurídica possível pode sustentar prática abusiva, insuficiência informacional, enganosidade por ambiguidade ou omissão relevante e, quando articulada a outros elementos, fundamento para redistribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório

Does being at a disadvantage mean being naive?

This does not mean calling you naive or incapable in the face of technology. Nor is it enough for a screen, an algorithm or the internet to be involved for the problem to exist.

The disadvantage that counts is the one that appears when control over meaning weighs structurally on what you can understand, compare, foresee and contest. This happens in an environment where whoever organizes the signs also has the power to change, punish and adjust the relationship.

Where it comes from

The term does not designate naivety, credulity or cultural deficiency of the consumer.p. 719-720

PTO termo não designa ingenuidade, credulidade ou deficiência cultural do consumidor.

Nor is it a matter of presupposing absolute incapacity in the face of the digital.p. 720

PTTampouco se trata de pressupor incapacidade absoluta diante do digital.

It is not enough that the case involves an interface, an algorithm or an online environment. The governance of meaning must act structurally on the understanding, comparability, predictability or contestation of enjoyment.p. 721

PTNão basta que o caso envolva interface, algoritmo ou ambiente online. É necessário que a governança do sentido atue de forma estrutural sobre a compreensão, a comparabilidade, a previsibilidade ou a contestação da fruição.

What is controlled, therefore, is not the mere existence of technological mediation, but the configuration of an arrangement in which signs, paths and records are asymmetrically organized by whoever also holds the power to alter, sanction or modulate the bondp. 721

PTO que se controla, portanto, não é a mera existência de mediação tecnológica, mas a configuração de um arranjo em que signos, percursos e registros são assimetricamente organizados por quem também detém poder de alteração, sanção ou modulação do vínculo

When do the signs on the screen weigh against you?

Almost everything you know about your skin passes through screens, notices, icons and paths that the platform designs. It is through them that you find out how much it is worth, what risk you run when buying and how to complain if something goes wrong. When your expectation and your chance to contest depend on these signs controlled by the company, you are in a fragile position. And the law takes this fragility into account.

This fragility has nothing to do with naivety, lack of attention or little familiarity with technology. It arises from the way the environment is set up. Whoever designs the screen decides what is highlighted, what is hidden and in what order things appear. And they usually also keep the records that would allow the story to be told later.

How should the platform set up the experience?

By not setting up the experience in a way that only the platform understands. The design of the environment needs to respect good faith, which is the duty of loyalty between the parties, as well as transparency and a real chance for you to understand what you are using.

How do you show the path that led to your decision?

Through the path you took inside the game up to the purchase or the problem, what was highlighted on the screen and where the information appeared. The records that make it possible to reconstruct how your expectation was formed also matter. Dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase and confirmation screens help, because they show how the environment was set up, and not only what you remember.

What can you ask for when the environment is to blame?

That the problem be treated as a matter of the environment, and not as carelessness on your part. If this dependence on the platform's signs is demonstrated, stronger duties to inform, advertise clearly and present proof may apply. Depending on the case, the practice may be seen as abusive, as insufficient information or as deception by ambiguity or by omission. Added to other elements, this may even change who needs to prove what.

Does every complicated game violate rights?

This does not mean that every complicated game violates rights, nor that you are incapable in the face of the digital. The fragility that counts is the one that appears when the signs controlled by the platform steer your expectation or hinder your reaction.

What does semiotic vulnerability describe?

It is an objective condition of the consumer's exposure, and not a defect of attention, naivety or cultural deficiency. The inequality is one of intelligibility and agency, because the production of meaning and the attribution of value depend on codes, metrics and visibility regimes administered by the environment itself. The result is a structural loss of the capacity to verify.

Semiotic vulnerability describes, on this plane, a structural loss of the capacity for verification, and not a defect of the consumer's attention.p. 281

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica descreve, nesse plano, uma perda estrutural de capacidade de verificação, e não um defeito de atenção do consumidor.

Where it comes from

The term does not designate naivety, credulity or cultural deficiency of the consumer. It designates a mechanism of power inscribed in the architecture of supply [...]p. 719-720

PTO termo não designa ingenuidade, credulidade ou deficiência cultural do consumidor. Designa um mecanismo de poder inscrito na arquitetura do fornecimento [...]

Semiotic vulnerability can be defined, in summary, as the objective condition of the consumer's exposure to a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in platform-governed ecosystems of enjoyment, in which the production of meaning and the attribution of value depend on codes, metrics and visibility regimes administered by the environment itself.p. 396

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica pode ser definida, em síntese, como a condição objetiva de exposição do consumidor a uma desigualdade estrutural de inteligibilidade e de agência em ecossistemas de fruição governados por plataforma, nos quais a produção de sentido e a atribuição de valor dependem de códigos, métricas e regimes de visibilidade administrados pelo próprio ambiente.

What can it be used to prove?

The platform alone controls the conditions that make the promise count as delivery, when the good is a position of enjoyment. Meaning, scene, equivalence and time form part of what is put into circulation and remain under unilateral administration. Hence the loss of demonstrability, which is the relevant legal fact. The IOC stops being a mere technical description of the digital good and comes to organize imputation, proof and restoration under the Consumer Protection Code.

Where it comes from

If the value of the IOC depends on controlled semiosis, administered visibility, operative symbolic equivalences and time governed as a program, then the central problem ceases to be merely the content of the item and becomes the demonstrability of the link between promise and enjoyment in a reconfigurable environment.p. 320

PTSe o valor do OIC depende de semiose controlada, de visibilidade administrada, de equivalências simbólicas operatórias e de tempo governado como programa, então o problema central deixa de ser apenas o conteúdo do item e passa a ser a demonstrabilidade do nexo entre promessa e fruição em um ambiente reconfigurável.

Semiotic vulnerability designates the consumer's structural exposure to the platform's unilateral control over the conditions that make the promise count as delivery when the good is a position of enjoyment and not an autonomous thing.p. 321

PTVulnerabilidade semiótica designa a exposição estrutural do consumidor ao controle unilateral, pela plataforma, das condições que fazem a promessa valer como entrega quando o bem é uma posição de fruição e não uma coisa autônoma.

Semiotic vulnerability thus names this kind of structural loss of demonstrability that occurs when meaning, scene, equivalence and time materially form part of what is put into circulation and remain under the unilateral administration of the ecosystem.p. 330

PTVulnerabilidade semiótica nomeia, assim, esse tipo de perda estrutural de demonstrabilidade que ocorre quando sentido, cena, equivalência e tempo integram materialmente o que é colocado em circulação e permanecem sob administração unilateral do ecossistema.

Without understanding that the consumer inhabits an environment whose intelligibility is partially reserved to the supplier, the definition of the Immaterial Object of Consumption as an enjoyment bundle sustained over time would appear as a mere technical description of the digital good, and not as a category that organizes imputation, proof and restoration under the Consumer Protection Code.p. 394

PTSem entender que o consumidor habita um ambiente cuja inteligibilidade é parcialmente reservada ao fornecedor, a definição do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo como feixe de fruição sustentado no tempo apareceria como mera descrição técnica do bem digital, e não como categoria que organiza imputação, prova e recomposição sob o Código de Defesa do Consumidor.

It is in this asymmetry in the production of meaning that the category of semiotic vulnerability is prepared, understood as a structural disadvantage in the grammar of meanings that guides the subject's relationship with the immaterial object of consumption.p. 104

PTÉ nessa assimetria de produção de sentido que se prepara a categoria da vulnerabilidade semiótica, entendida como desvantagem estrutural na gramática de significados que orienta a relação do sujeito com o objeto imaterial de consumo.

How does it differ from informational asymmetry?

The problem is not knowing less, but being unable to find one's bearings and react inside the environment. That is why the concept is not satisfied by the presence of an interface, an algorithm or an online environment. The mechanism of power must be present in the architecture of supply.
