The jurist's path

Functional value equivalence

Functional value equivalence is the criterion for judging whether a change to an IOC preserved, in substantial terms, what justified the purchase. The test rests on Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC1, it reaches the appearance of an item when that appearance is what was bought, and it leads to restoration.

If the platform may change, what does the law control when it does?

Every continuous digital service changes, and that fact is not in dispute. A change, taken in itself, is not unlawful, because the environment presupposes maintenance, improvement and adaptation, and updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility. The difficulty appears elsewhere, when the change stops working that way and begins to degrade, empty or divert, in a relevant way, the function that justified the purchase and structured the legitimate expectation of enjoyment.

Mutability, in itself, is not unlawful.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita.

Where it comes from

The problem begins when it ceases to operate as a legitimate form of maintenance, improvement or adaptation of the supply and begins to degrade, empty or divert, in a relevant way, the function that justified the purchase and structured the legitimate expectation of enjoyment.p. 729

PTO problema começa quando ela deixa de operar como forma legítima de manutenção, aperfeiçoamento ou adaptação do fornecimento e passa a degradar, esvaziar ou desviar, de modo relevante, a função que justificou a aquisição e estruturou a expectativa legítima de fruição.

Updates may be necessary for compatibility, security, stability and legibility in a competitive ecosystem.p. 470

PTAtualizações podem ser necessárias para compatibilidade, segurança, estabilidade e legibilidade em um ecossistema competitivo.

The debate therefore tends to swing between two extremes. One treats any change as a breach, and the other legitimizes every alteration by the operational continuity of the system or by the nominal survival of the object. Neither position holds, and the question changes. Instead of asking whether the change fell within the supplier's general power to update, the interpreter asks whether, after it, the acquired experience remains functionally equivalent, in substantial terms, to the one that justified bringing the object into the user's sphere of consumption.

Where it comes from

On one side, it rejects the claim to freeze the environment in a fixed state, as if any mutation automatically constituted breach.p. 731

PTDe um lado, afasta a pretensão de congelar o ambiente em estado fixo, como se qualquer mutação configurasse automaticamente inadimplemento.

On the other, it rejects the claim that every alteration would be legitimized by the mere operational continuity of the system or by the mere nominal survival of the object.p. 731

PTDe outro, recusa a tese de que toda alteração seria legitimada pela simples continuidade operacional do sistema ou pela mera sobrevivência nominal do objeto.

The legally relevant question then ceases to be whether the change occurred within the supplier's general power to update.p. 730

PTA pergunta juridicamente relevante deixa então de ser se a mudança ocorreu dentro do poder geral de atualização do fornecedor.

It becomes whether, after the alteration, the acquired experience remains functionally equivalent in substantial terms to the one that legitimized the object's entry into the user's sphere of consumption.p. 730

PTPassa a ser se, após a alteração, a experiência adquirida permanece funcionalmente equivalente em termos substanciais àquela que legitimou o ingresso do objeto na esfera de consumo do usuário.

The category that answers this question is functional value equivalence, which serves at once as a criterion of comparison and of restoration. It sets side by side the utility stabilized in the relationship and the utility delivered after relevant modulations, the name given to the alterations the supplier makes while performing the service. Its counterpart is open auditability, treated in its own chapter. The first answers for how much changed and what, and the second for how. Each depends on the other, because without a verifiable minimum about what changed there is no rational basis for comparing promise and delivery, and without a criterion of comparison transparency shrinks to the ability to narrate changes.

Functional value equivalence designates the criterion of comparison and restoration by which one judges whether the utility delivered, after relevant modulations, substantially preserves the core of enjoyed utility, including when part of the value is structured by socially verifiable symbolic dimensions.p. 469

PTEquivalência de valor funcional designa o critério de comparação e recomposição pelo qual se julga se a utilidade entregue, após modulações relevantes, preserva substancialmente o núcleo de utilidade fruída, inclusive quando parte do valor se estrutura por dimensões simbólicas socialmente verificáveis.

Where it comes from

Once such a limit is set, functional value equivalence can be formulated as a criterion of comparison between the utility stabilized in the relationship and the utility delivered after relevant modulations.p. 478

PTUma vez fixado tal limite, a equivalência de valor funcional pode ser formulada como critério de comparação entre a utilidade estabilizada no vínculo e a utilidade entregue após modulações relevantes.

One standard controls the how of the mutation; the other, the how much and the what of the mutation.p. 638

PTUm standard controla o como da mutação; o outro, o quanto e o que da mutação.

Without functional equivalence, auditability runs the risk of becoming transparency without material density, that is, a mere capacity to narrate changes without a criterion to assess their substantive legitimacy.p. 638

PTSem equivalência funcional, a auditabilidade corre o risco de virar transparência sem densidade material, isto é, simples capacidade de narrar mudanças sem critério para avaliar sua legitimidade substantiva.

Why doesn't the reply that the item is still there settle the case?

The supplier's first defense is usually that nothing was taken away, since the item is still in the inventory, the account is still active and the contract is still in force. A reading centered on abstract ownership or on the formal permanence of the asset would accept this answer, but it misses what is in dispute. Relevant alterations may keep the name, the general appearance and the nominal existence of the object and still substantially compromise the enjoyment built into the relationship. What the system protects is the integrity of enjoyment, and not the appearance that something is still there.

Where it comes from

It is precisely for this reason that functional value equivalence presents itself as a more adequate parameter than a reading centered only on abstract ownership or on the formal permanence of the asset: what the system must protect is the integrity of enjoyment, and not the mere appearance that “something is still there”.p. 648

PTÉ precisamente por isso que a equivalência de valor funcional se apresenta como parâmetro mais adequado do que uma leitura centrada apenas em titularidade abstrata ou em permanência formal do ativo: o que o sistema deve proteger é a integridade da fruição, e não a mera aparência de que “algo ainda está lá”.

The permanence of the item, the functionality, the account, the access or the feature does not, by itself, neutralize the emptying of the experience if the concrete role of the object is degraded, diverted or made substantially different from the one that justified the purchase.p. 730

PTA permanência do item, da funcionalidade, da conta, do acesso ou da feature não neutraliza, por si, o esvaziamento da experiência se o papel concreto do objeto for degradado, desviado ou tornado substancialmente diverso daquele que justificou a aquisição.

Consumer law doctrine already points the way. Bruno Miragem treats liability for defects as the breach of a duty of adequacy, that is, of the quality the product or service must have to serve the ends legitimately expected of it, and he distinguishes a defect from absolute nonperformance, because it amounts to partial or imperfect performance. Benjamin, Marques and Bessa describe the functional defect as a nonconformity that prevents or limits the function the good is meant to serve. On this basis, once the legitimately expected utility has been emptied, the inadequacy is already established, even if the account remains active and the item is still listed.

Where it comes from

Functional value equivalence can be upheld as a consumer-law criterion for the lawfulness of alterations because liability for defects arises from the breach of a duty of adequacy, that is, of the quality of the product or service to serve the ends legitimately expected of it.p. 649

PTA equivalência de valor funcional pode ser sustentada como critério consumerista de licitude das alterações porque a responsabilidade por vício decorre da violação de um dever de adequação, isto é, da qualidade do produto ou serviço de servir aos fins que legitimamente dele se esperam.

The defect is distinct from absolute nonperformance, and corresponds to partial or imperfect performance, aimed at satisfying the parties' interest in the very performance of the contractp. 649

PTO vício não se confunde com o inadimplemento absoluto, mas corresponde a cumprimento parcial ou imperfeito, voltado à satisfação do interesse das partes no próprio cumprimento do contrato

The CDC works with the functional defect precisely to reach nonconformities that frustrate the consumer's legitimate expectation without requiring the total disappearance of the objectp. 652

PTO CDC trabalha com vício funcional justamente para alcançar desconformidades que frustram a expectativa legítima do consumidor sem exigir desaparecimento total do objeto

If the legitimately expected utility has been emptied, the inadequacy is already established, even if the account remains active and the item remains listed in the inventoryp. 653-654

PTSe a utilidade legitimamente esperada foi esvaziada, a inadequação já está dada, ainda que a conta permaneça ativa e o item continue listado no inventário

Rizzatto Nunes supplies the image that serves as an objective yardstick, that of a supplier who cuts the number of classes in a course while keeping the same price. On platforms, the cut rarely appears as an explicit reduction. It may show up as lost reach, persistently downgraded performance, cumulative restriction of features, tighter limits, reordered visibility or structural alterations that raise the cost of enjoying the same utility. The form changes, but the logic stays the same.

The label and the price are kept, and the delivery of value is lowered.p. 480

PTMantêm-se o rótulo e o preço, e rebaixa-se a entrega de valor.

Where it comes from

The example he offers, of the supplier that reduces classes while keeping the same price Nunes(Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]), works as an objective yardstick for platforms.p. 480

PTO exemplo que ele oferece, do fornecedor que reduz aulas mantendo o mesmo preço (Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]) , funciona como régua objetiva para plataformas.

The reduction rarely appears as an explicit cut of “classes”, but may manifest itself as loss of reach, persistent downgrading of performance, cumulative restriction of features, compression of limits, reordering of visibility or structural alterations that raise the cost of enjoying the same utility.p. 480

PTA redução raramente aparece como corte explícito de “aulas”, mas pode se manifestar como perda de alcance, rebaixamento persistente de performance, restrição cumulativa de funcionalidades, compressão de limites, reordenação de visibilidade ou alterações estruturais que elevam o custo de fruir a mesma utilidade.

Where does the criterion fit within the consumer protection system?

The first support is reading the contract as a process. Claudia Lima Marques sees the contract as a dynamic relationship, which is born, lives and dies, and she rejects the idea of an empty space between the birth of the obligation and the satisfaction of the main performance, because from the start the parties are bound by ancillary duties of conduct imposed by good faith. If the contract is a process, equivalence calls not for identity of means but for substantial continuity of the expected core of utility, which often requires active conduct from the supplier over time, and not the mere formal preservation of access. Marques also observes that long-term contracts carry an implicit expectation of change, and this observation must be read with its counterpart, so that changes are admissible when aimed at maintaining potential and quality, and not at silently replacing the object with a less valuable version.

Where it comes from

If the contract is a process, equivalence does not require identity of means.p. 478

PTSe o contrato é processo, a equivalência não exige identidade de meios.

It requires substantial continuity of the expected core of utility, especially when the utility depends on a technical and organizational environment in continuous operation.p. 478

PTEla exige continuidade substancial do núcleo de utilidade esperado, sobretudo quando a utilidade depende de um ambiente técnico-organizacional em operação contínua.

Marques rejects the idea of an empty space between the birth of the obligation and the satisfaction of the main performance, stating that the obligational relationship is a continuous whole.p. 478

PTMarques recusa a ideia de um espaço vazio entre o nascimento da obrigação e a satisfação da prestação principal, ao afirmar que a relação obrigacional é um todo contínuo.

In platform-governed enjoyment, preserving functional equivalence often requires active conduct over time, and not the mere formal preservation of access.p. 478-479

PTEm fruição governada por plataforma, preservar equivalência funcional frequentemente exige conduta ativa no tempo, e não mera conservação formal de acesso.

This formulation provides the fine fit for functional value equivalence: changes may be admissible and even necessary, provided they are aimed at maintaining the potential and the quality that structure the enjoyed utility, and not at the silent replacement of the object by a less valuable version.p. 479

PTEssa formulação dá o encaixe fino para a equivalência de valor funcional: mudanças podem ser admissíveis e até necessárias, desde que orientadas à manutenção do potencial e da qualidade que estruturam a utilidade fruída, e não à substituição silenciosa do objeto por versão menos valiosa.

The second support comes from continuing and dependent relationships. The parallel with long-term captive contracts, in which practical dependence binds the consumer to the relationship, serves as disciplined interpretive infrastructure, and not as an automatic transplant. These relationships protect the minimum stability of the performance program and the trust built over time. Marques calls legitimate expectations the minimum objective basis of the consumer contract, and the expression calls for careful reading, because it does not authorize delivering the minimum. It designates that without which the relationship becomes unbalanced, so much so that excluding fundamental expectations makes the imbalance structural.

Where it comes from

The parallel with long-term captive contracts is used here not as an automatic transplant or as academic ornament, but as disciplined interpretive infrastructure.p. 650

PTO paralelo com os contratos cativos de longa duração não entra aqui como transplante automático nem como enfeite acadêmico, mas como infraestrutura interpretativa disciplinada.

In relationships of this kind, what is protected is not only the initial delivery, but the minimum stability of the performance program and the trust legitimately built over timep. 650

PTEm vínculos dessa natureza, não se protege apenas a entrega inicial, mas a estabilidade mínima do programa de prestação e a confiança legitimamente construída no tempo

Legitimate expectations form the “minimum (objective) basis of the relationship, of the consumer contract”, so that, once these expectations are excluded, “the imbalance of the relationship is flagrant”p. 648

PTAs expectativas legítimas formam a “base mínima (objetiva) da relação, do contrato de consumo”, de sorte que, excluídas essas expectativas, “o desequilíbrio da relação é flagrante”

If the fundamental legitimate expectations that underpinned the contract are excluded, the imbalance is no longer a detail: it becomes structuralp. 653

PTSe excluídas as expectativas legítimas fundamentais que sustentaram a contratação, o desequilíbrio já não é detalhe: torna-se estrutural

The third support is objective good faith in lasting relationships. Judith Martins-Costa treats it as a measure of lawfulness and as a brake, in specific cases, on the ius variandi, a party's power to change the performance on its own, and she points to unfavorable surprise and the rupture of previously tolerated patterns as a breach of trust, even if the contract remains nominally in force. She is also the source of the dynamic synallagma, the idea that contractual balance is not exhausted at the moment of agreement but accompanies the relationship over time. The point is decisive for the IOC, whose consumption happens not only at purchase but over the course of enjoyment, so that the lawfulness of an alteration is measured by the functional unity of the relationship. Marques, Benjamin and Miragem add the creative function of good faith, a source of new ancillary duties and of professional risks that cannot be waived by contract, among them the risks of technical obsolescence, compatibility mutations and structural reconfigurations.

Where it comes from

Objective good faith functions expressly as a measure for assessing lawfulness in the exercise of rights derived from legal transactions, including to set the limits of the exercise of termination in lasting contracts of continued performance and to impose brakes on the ius variandi in specific casesp. 650

PTA boa-fé objetiva funciona expressamente como metro de aferição da licitude no exercício de direitos derivados de negócios jurídicos, inclusive para balizar os limites do exercício da denúncia em contratos duradouros e de execução continuada e para impor freios ao ius variandi em hipóteses específicas

Unfavorable and harmful surprise, the abrupt rupture of previously tolerated patterns and the abrupt reconfiguration of the expected utility may constitute a breach of trust and a violation of the contractual program as actually lived, even if the contract remains nominally in forcep. 650-651

PTA surpresa desfavorável e prejudicial, a ruptura brusca de padrões anteriormente tolerados e a reconfiguração abrupta da utilidade esperada podem caracterizar quebra da confiança e violação do programa contratual concretamente vivido, ainda que o contrato continue nominalmente em vigor

There is also a dynamic synallagma, that is, a functional balance that accompanies the relationship over timep. 651

PTExiste também um sinalagma dinâmico, isto é, um equilíbrio funcional que acompanha a relação ao longo do tempo

Therefore, the lawfulness of the alteration must be assessed in light of the functional unity of the relationship, and not only of the abstract existence of a generic authorization to change.p. 651

PTLogo, a licitude da alteração deve ser aferida à luz da unidade funcional da relação, e não apenas da existência abstrata de uma autorização genérica para mudar.

The consequence is plain, since, if continued performance exposes the consumer to risks of technical obsolescence, compatibility mutations and structural reconfigurations, good faith must require care, cooperation and maintenance as duties to preserve functional equivalence, and thus impose liability when trust is broken by reconfigurations that degrade, in a relevant and persistent way, the core of utility.p. 479

PTA consequência é ostensiva, uma vez que, se a execução continuada expõe o consumidor a riscos de obsolescência técnica, mutações de compatibilidade e reconfigurações estruturais, a boa-fé deverá exigir cuidado, cooperação e manutenção como deveres de preservação de equivalência funcional, e assim, impor responsabilidade quando a confiança é quebrada por reconfigurações que degradam, de modo relevante e persistente, o núcleo de utilidade.

The fourth support is the offer. For Miragem, the consumer decides to contract in view of the advantages and qualities offered, and from this arises the legal duty to maintain the offered conditions or, at least, to substantially preserve their utility. Taken together, these supports do not make the category a new right placed beside the CDC. It organizes, for the case of alterations, duties the Code itself already contains, and so the charge of invention finds its answer in Karl Larenz's description of the legal system as open and, to a certain degree, mobile, calling for functional concretization when the factual basis shifts.

Functional value equivalence is an outgrowth of good faith, of the binding force of the offer and of the protection of trust within the CDC.p. 652

PTA equivalência de valor funcional é desdobramento da boa-fé, da vinculação da oferta e da proteção da confiança no interior do CDC.

Where it comes from

The offer and the trust it awakens provide a direct basis for this identification, since the consumer contracts in view of the advantages and qualities advertised, from which arises the legal duty to maintain the offered conditions or, at least, to substantially preserve their utilityp. 652

PTA oferta e a confiança por ela despertada oferecem base direta para essa identificação, pois o consumidor contrata em vista das vantagens e qualidades anunciadas, surgindo daí o dever jurídico de manutenção das condições ofertadas ou, ao menos, de preservação substancial de sua utilidade

This move also protects the construction against the objection of “inventing” law, because the legal system is open and requires functional concretization when the factual basis shifts.p. 481

PTEsse movimento também protege a construção contra a objeção de “invenção” de Direito, porque o sistema jurídico é aberto e exige concretização funcional quando a base fática se desloca.

What does Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC cover?

Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC1 declares null and void any clause that authorizes the supplier to unilaterally modify the content or quality of the contract after it is concluded. Commenting on it, Nunes asks how one party could alter a contract alone and still have the alteration count as valid. Benjamin, Marques and Bessa say that a clause giving the supplier the prerogative to innovate unilaterally clashes with contractual balance, and Miragem explains why. Such a clause concentrates in the supplier the power to direct the relationship and reduces the consumer to a spectator.

Where it comes from

For this reason, the CDC treats the unilateral modification of content or quality as a ground of nullity.p. 479

PTPor isso, o CDC trata a modificação unilateral de conteúdo ou qualidade como nulidade.

Any modification of the consumer contract must observe bilaterality and the absence of abusive imposition, so that a clause arming the supplier with the prerogative to innovate unilaterally in the relationship clashes with contractual balance, its validity being undermined by Article 51, item XIIIp. 654

PTQualquer modificação do contrato de consumo deve observar bilateralidade e ausência de imposição abusiva, de modo que destoa do equilíbrio contratual a cláusula que municie o fornecedor da prerrogativa de inovar unilateralmente na relação, sendo sua validade infirmada pelo art. 51, XIII

Clauses that authorize the supplier to unilaterally modify the content or quality of the contract after its conclusion are abusive, precisely because they concentrate in the supplier the power to direct the relationship and reduce the consumer to a mere spectator of the course of the transactionp. 651-652

PTSão abusivas as cláusulas que autorizam o fornecedor a modificar unilateralmente o conteúdo ou a qualidade do contrato após sua celebração, justamente porque concentram no polo fornecedor o poder de direção da relação e reduzem o consumidor a mero espectador do curso negocial

The provision speaks of a clause, and carrying it over to conduct calls for care. For Miragem, unilateral alteration is enough, by itself, to characterize abusiveness, but this statement concerns the clauses that authorize the change and cannot be repeated without that qualification, as if every alteration were unlawful. Beyond the clause, unilateral alteration is by itself legally sensitive, though the problem does not lie in every change considered in the abstract. What is rejected is the mutation imposed without the consumer's effective participation, without preserving balance and without respect for the functional core of enjoyment.

Where it comes from

If unilateral alteration is already enough, by itself, to characterize abusiveness, this characteristic is aggravated when the content of the change results in the reduction or impairment of the consumer's interest in the performancep. 652

PTSe a alteração unilateral já basta, por si, para caracterizar abusividade, essa característica se agrava quando o conteúdo da mudança resulta na diminuição ou no comprometimento do interesse do consumidor com a prestação

Unilateral alteration is already, by itself, legally sensitive in the CDC; and this sensitivity is aggravated when the change is not neutral, but results in a material reduction or impairment of the consumer's interest in the performancep. 651-652

PTA alteração unilateral já é, por si, juridicamente sensível no CDC; e essa sensibilidade se agrava quando a mudança não é neutra, mas resulta em diminuição ou comprometimento material do interesse do consumidor com a prestação

The problem does not lie in every change considered in the abstract, but in the mutation imposed without the consumer's effective participation, without preserving the balance of the relationship and without respect for the functional core of enjoyment.p. 654

PTO problema não está em toda mudança abstratamente considerada, mas na mutação imposta sem participação efetiva do consumidor, sem preservação do equilíbrio da relação e sem respeito ao núcleo funcional da fruição.

The lawyer therefore faces two different questions. The first concerns the clause, and the answer is that the lawfulness of a change is not measured by its abstract provision in the contract, so a generic authorization to alter does not give the supplier the defense it seeks. The second concerns the concrete alteration, which is judged by the equivalence test. At this stage, a relevant unilateral modification, when untethered from restoration and from the substantial preservation of use value, ceases to be mere management of the service and begins to approach structural abusiveness. The verb is approach, and it is worth keeping.

Where it comes from

The lawfulness of the mutation is not measured by its mere abstract provision in a contractual clause, nor by the formal permanence of the object after the alteration.p. 650

PTA licitude da mutação não se mede por sua mera previsão abstrata em cláusula contratual, nem pela permanência formal do objeto após a alteração.

A relevant unilateral modification, when untethered from restoration and from the substantial preservation of use value, ceases to be mere management of the service and begins to approach structural abusivenessp. 654

PTModificação unilateral relevante, quando desancorada de recomposição e de preservação substancial do valor de uso, deixa de ser simples gestão do serviço e passa a se aproximar de abusividade estrutural

How is the test applied?

The test applies only to an alteration capable of affecting in a relevant way the practical function, the position in the ecosystem, the symbolic density or the continuity of enjoyment of the object. Minor changes, visual touch-ups and marginal fluctuations typical of the normal life of the service stay outside. The trigger comes when the platform carries out a downgrading of use, limitation of access, loss of compatibility, alteration of essential parameters, removal of features, structural rebalancing, discontinuation of a role in the ecosystem, degradation of relational position or emptying of attributes with objective weight in the rationale of the purchase.

Where it comes from

Its field of application opens whenever there is an alteration with the potential to affect in a relevant way the practical function, the ecosystemic position, the symbolic density or the continuity of enjoyment of the IOC.p. 651

PTSeu campo de incidência se abre sempre que houver alteração com potencial de afetar de modo relevante a função prática, a posição ecossistêmica, a densidade simbólica ou a continuidade de fruição do OIC.

The trigger is not any minor change, any visual touch-up or any marginal fluctuation typical of the normal life of the service.p. 651

PTO gatilho não é qualquer mudança miúda, qualquer retoque visual ou qualquer oscilação marginal própria da vida normal do serviço.

The standard switches on when the platform carries out a downgrading of use, limitation of access, loss of compatibility, alteration of essential parameters, removal of features, structural rebalancing, discontinuation of an ecosystemic role, degradation of relational position or emptying of attributes with objective weight in the rationale of the purchasep. 651

PTO standard se acende quando a plataforma promove rebaixamento de uso, limitação de acesso, perda de compatibilidade, alteração de parâmetros essenciais, remoção de funcionalidades, reequilíbrio estrutural, descontinuação de papel ecossistêmico, degradação de posição relacional ou esvaziamento de atributos com peso objetivo na racionalidade da aquisição

The examination is structured in four steps. The first identifies the functional core of the object and asks what practical utility justified the purchase, what role the object played in the ecosystem and, where pertinent, what symbolic, reputational or relational density was objectively built into the enjoyment. The analysis is neither psychologizing nor folkloric. What is sought is a verifiable core that formed part of the legitimately formed expectation, and the offer, with the trust it awakened, is the direct basis for reconstructing it.

It does not matter whether the consumer “loved” that item.p. 652

PTNão interessa saber se o consumidor “amava” aquele item.

Where it comes from

The first step is to identify the functional core of the object.p. 652

PTO primeiro passo consiste em identificar o núcleo funcional do objeto.

Where pertinent, what symbolic, reputational or relational density was objectively built into the enjoyment?p. 652

PTQuando pertinente, que densidade simbólica, reputacional ou relacional estava objetivamente incorporada à fruição?

What matters is whether there was a verifiable core of utility, role and meaning that formed part of the legitimately formed expectationp. 652

PTInteressa saber se havia um núcleo verificável de utilidade, papel e significação que integrava a expectativa legitimamente formada

The second step reconstructs the alteration and its impact, comparing before and after in use, performance, access, compatibility, function in the ecosystem, recognition, rarity, trajectory or continuity of experience. This is where the dependence on auditability shows in practice. When the platform prevents the impact from being reconstructed, opacity ceases to be neutral and becomes a dogmatic aggravating factor, that is, a factor that worsens the supplier's position in the examination, because it makes it impossible to control the adequacy and the very legitimacy of the change. This control cannot depend on whether the consumer happened to archive evidence or has the technical knowledge to produce homemade comparisons.

Functional equivalence without auditability becomes an elegant guess.p. 653

PTA equivalência funcional sem auditabilidade vira chute elegante.

Where it comes from

The second step is to reconstruct the alteration and the impact.p. 652

PTO segundo passo é reconstruir a alteração e o impacto.

How do before and after compare in use, performance, access, compatibility, ecosystemic function, recognition, rarity, trajectory or continuity of experience?p. 652

PTQual o antes e o depois em termos de uso, desempenho, acesso, compatibilidade, função ecossistêmica, reconhecimento, raridade, trajetória ou continuidade de experiência?

If the platform prevents reconstruction of the impact, opacity is not neutral.p. 653

PTSe a plataforma impede reconstrução do impacto, a opacidade não é neutra.

It turns into a dogmatic aggravating factor, because it makes it impossible to control adequacy, trust and the very legitimacy of the mutation.p. 653

PTEla se converte em agravante dogmático, porque inviabiliza o controle da adequação, da confiança e da própria legitimidade da mutação.

In a regime where the supplier can update continuously and even automatically, auditability cannot depend on whether the consumer happened to keep evidence or has the technical knowledge to put together homemade comparisons.p. 471

What is argued is whether, under the pretext of improvement, it compromises in a relevant way the use, performance or compatibility of goods still within their expected durability, without adequate information, without effective consent and without means of mitigation. The legal structure is the same in immaterial objects of consumption, and the example helps the interpreter because it shows that the problem does not belong to gaming culture.

Where it comes from

The example of updates that degrade accessibility and compatibility in software or firmware helps visualize the structure of the problem.p. 655

PTO exemplo de atualizações que degradam acessibilidade e compatibilidade em software ou firmware ajuda a visualizar a estrutura do problema.

What is argued is whether, under the pretext of improvement, the update compromises in a relevant way the use, performance or compatibility of goods still within their expected durability, without adequate information, without effective consent and without mitigating means.p. 655

PTO que se discute é se, sob o pretexto de melhoria, a atualização compromete de modo relevante o uso, o desempenho ou a compatibilidade de bens ainda dentro da durabilidade esperada, sem informação adequada, sem consentimento efetivo e sem vias mitigatórias.

The legal structure is the same in IOCs: update, rebalancing or rule change are not illegitimate merely because they exist, but may become unlawful when they substantially degrade the functional core of the purchase without verifiable justification and without adequate restoration.p. 655

PTA estrutura jurídica é a mesma em OICs: atualização, reequilíbrio ou mudança de regra não são ilegítimos por existirem, mas podem tornar-se ilícitos quando degradam substancialmente o núcleo funcional da aquisição sem justificativa verificável e sem recomposição adequada.

What is the legal consequence when equivalence breaks?

Without substantial preservation of functional value, the alteration tends to constitute a relevant inadequacy of the supply. Depending on the structure of the case, it may be treated as a defect in the product or service and, in more extreme situations of harm or risk, may approach the logic of the safety defect. The phrase product or service does not undo the qualification of the IOC as an immaterial product, studied in the chapter on the object, because the product fixes what was acquired and the service designates the mode of continued performance. When mutability is exploited in a coordinated way, the dispute may gain an additional element of abusive practice or abusive teleology.

Where it comes from

When there is no substantial preservation of functional value, the alteration tends to constitute a relevant inadequacy of the supply.p. 653

PTQuando não há preservação substancial do valor funcional, a alteração tende a configurar inadequação relevante do fornecimento.

Depending on the structure of the case, this may be treated as a defect in the product or service, or, in more extreme situations of harm or risk, approach the logic of the safety defect.p. 653

PTConforme a estrutura do caso, isso pode ser tratado como vício do produto ou do serviço, ou, em situações mais extremas de dano ou risco, aproximar-se da lógica do defeito.

Service, in turn, ceases to mean “there is no object” and comes to designate the mode of continued performance of the supply, made up of practices of maintenance, organization and modulation of the conditions of enjoyment that extend over time.p. 442

PTServiço, por sua vez, deixa de significar “não há objeto” e passa a designar o modo de execução continuada do fornecimento, composto por práticas de manutenção, organização e modulação das condições de fruição que se projetam no tempo.

The possible legal qualification tends to fall on a quality defect in the product or service, relevant inadequacy of the supply and, if there is coordinated exploitation of mutability, an additional element of abusive practice or abusive teleology.p. 746

PTA qualificação jurídica possível tende a recair em vício de qualidade do produto ou serviço, inadequação relevante do fornecimento e, se houver exploração coordenada da mutabilidade, elemento adicional de prática abusiva ou teleologia abusiva.

The qualification framework translates this into duty and proof. The duty is to preserve, in substantial terms, the functional, relational and, when legally relevant, symbolic role of the object. The minimum proof covers the utility previously built into the relationship, the change that occurred, the concrete functional impact and the insufficiency of the restoration offered. When verifiability or equivalence is missing, the legitimacy of the change becomes legally suspect, and when both are missing at once the hypothesis draws close to a defect of supply, abusive practice or breach of trust. A diffuse impression that the experience got worse is not enough, and the path is to reconstruct before and after through version records, documentation of the offer, interface evidence and the history of alterations.

Where it comes from

The corresponding duty of supply is to preserve, in substantial terms, the functional, relational and, when legally relevant, symbolic role of the object in the consumer's experience.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de preservar, em termos substanciais, o papel funcional, relacional e, quando juridicamente relevante, simbólico do objeto na experiência do consumidor.

The minimum required proof calls for demonstration of the utility previously incorporated into the bond, of the change that occurred, of the concrete functional impact of the alteration and of the insufficiency of any restoration offered.p. 745-746

PTA prova mínima exigível requer demonstração da utilidade anteriormente incorporada ao vínculo, da mudança ocorrida, do impacto funcional concreto da alteração e da insuficiência de eventual recomposição oferecida.

When one of these conditions fails, the legitimacy of the change already becomes legally suspect.p. 638

PTQuando uma dessas condições falha, a legitimidade da mudança já se torna juridicamente suspeita.

A diffuse impression that the experience “got worse” is not enough.p. 675

PTNão basta a impressão difusa de que a experiência “piorou”.

Before and after must be reconstructed through version records, documentation of the offer, interface evidence, the history of relevant alterations and a demonstration of the impact on the core of enjoyment.p. 675

PTÉ preciso reconstruir o antes e o depois por registros de versão, documentação da oferta, evidências de interface, histórico de alterações relevantes e demonstração de impacto no núcleo da fruição.

How does equivalence connect to restoration?

Restoration appears at two points in the reasoning, and the two should be kept apart. Within the test, it is an element of lawfulness, because adequate restoration, combined with verifiable justification, may make a relevant loss tolerable. After the test, it is a remedy, because the same yardstick that identifies the break guides the response. The parameter does not serve only to identify unlawfulness, and so restoration is the natural conclusion of the examination.

Where it comes from

When equivalence is broken without adequate restoration, the alteration ceases to be read as ordinary evolution and comes to be treated as a relevant inadequacy of the supply regime.p. 731

PTQuando a equivalência é rompida sem recomposição adequada, a alteração deixa de ser lida como evolução ordinária e passa a ser tratada como inadequação relevante do regime de fornecimento.

It does not serve only to identify unlawfulness.p. 731

PTEle não serve apenas para identificar ilicitude.

It also serves to guide remedies.p. 731

PTServe também para orientar remédios.

Once equivalence is broken, the priority form of protection must be functional restoration, whenever technically and legally feasible. It may take the form of reversal of the alteration, an alternative version, equivalent functional replacement, restoration of attributes, correction of parameters, preservation of trajectory or maintenance of compatibility, as far as possible. The criterion is not the literal reproduction of the past at any cost, but functionally adequate restitution. Compensation remains legitimate and, in many cases, indispensable, but takes on a residual or complementary nature when restoration is unfeasible, insufficient or incapable of covering all the losses. The chapter on remedies develops this order.

Where it comes from

In these cases, the priority form of protection must be functional restoration.p. 654

PTNesses casos, a tutela prioritária deve ser a recomposição funcional.

In an IOC environment, the most legally adequate remedy is often to restore equivalence of enjoyment: reversal of the alteration, alternative version, equivalent functional replacement, restoration of attributes, correction of parameters, preservation of trajectory, maintenance of compatibility or other measures capable of reestablishing, as far as possible, the position of enjoyment that justified the purchase.p. 654

PTEm ambiente de OIC, muitas vezes o remédio juridicamente mais adequado é restaurar equivalência de fruição: reversão da alteração, versão alternativa, substituição funcional equivalente, recomposição de atributos, correção de parâmetros, preservação de trajetória, manutenção de compatibilidade ou outras medidas capazes de restabelecer, na medida do possível, a posição de fruição que justificou a aquisição.

The criterion is not the literal reproduction of the past state at any cost, but the functionally adequate restitution of the injured experience, at a level compatible with what the consumer had legitimately built into their sphere of enjoyment.p. 740

PTO critério não é a reprodução literal do estado pretérito a qualquer custo, mas a restituição funcionalmente adequada da experiência lesada, em patamar compatível com aquilo que o consumidor tinha legitimamente incorporado à sua esfera de fruição.

It takes on a residual or complementary nature whenever functional restoration is unfeasible, insufficient or incapable of fully covering the losses suffered.p. 741-742

PTEla assume natureza residual ou complementar sempre que a recomposição funcional for inviável, insuficiente ou incapaz de abarcar integralmente os prejuízos sofridos.

Two points help avoid mistakes in the pleading. First, the refund and credit mentioned in the cosmetics case do not contradict this order, because there they appear as alternatives within a choice mechanism meant to prevent captivity by surprise, and what is rejected is the automatic conversion of every frustration into money. Second, equivalence also judges the restoration the platform offers, which does not become equivalent just because the platform says so. If the system keeps the appearance and the label but downgrades functions, restrictions, access, performance or the social operativity that defined the position, the restoration is merely nominal and the delivery has not been preserved.

Where it comes from

The consumer protection system was not designed to automatically convert every relevant frustration into residual monetary compensation.p. 654

PTO sistema consumerista não foi desenhado para converter automaticamente toda frustração relevante em indenização monetária residual.

The effect of the parameter is to separate legitimate adjustment from opportunistic substitution: if the system keeps appearance and label, but substantially downgrades functions, restrictions, access, performance or the social operativity that defined the position, there was nominal restoration and not preservation of delivery.p. 327

PTO efeito do parâmetro é separar ajuste legítimo de substituição oportunista: se o sistema conserva aparência e etiqueta, mas rebaixa substancialmente funções, restrições, acesso, performance ou a operatividade social que definia a posição, houve recomposição nominal e não preservação da entrega.

With this, restoration ceases to be arbitrated by the platform as “equivalent by declaration” and becomes controllable by a substantive yardstick, compatible with a reconfigurable market, but hostile to the sign's zone of impunity.p. 327

PTCom isso, a recomposição deixa de ser arbitrada pela plataforma como “equivalente por declaração” e passa a ser controlável por uma régua substantiva, compatível com um mercado reconfigurável, mas hostil à zona de impunidade do signo.

Does controlling change stall innovation?

The central objection invokes innovation, on the view that controlling change would prevent the service from evolving. The answer is that the criterion does not prohibit evolution, adaptation of the ecosystem, balancing of mechanics or ordinary innovation, and that changes of means, method and architecture may be legitimate and even necessary. What it rejects is the use of change to shift onto the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration. That is why invoking an update, an adjustment or the evolution of the service does not end the debate, which turns on demonstrating that the bundle, the set of functionalities and meanings that make up the utility, has been substantially preserved.

The standard does not prevent the life of the service. It prevents the life of the service from being used as a screen for the material breach of what was functionally promised.p. 655

PTO standard não impede a vida do serviço. Impede que a vida do serviço seja usada como biombo para o descumprimento material do que foi funcionalmente prometido.

Where it comes from

The standard does not prohibit the evolution of the service, the adaptation of the ecosystem, the balancing of mechanics or the ordinary innovation inherent to continuous platforms and games.p. 649

PTO standard não proíbe a evolução do serviço, a adaptação do ecossistema, o balanceamento de mecânicas nem a inovação ordinária inerente a plataformas e jogos contínuos.

What the parameter rejects is not change itself, but its instrumentalization to transfer to the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration.p. 730

PTO que o parâmetro recusa não é a mudança em si, mas sua instrumentalização para transferir ao consumidor o custo de reconfigurações que destroem ou rebaixam significativamente a utilidade adquirida sem recomposição compatível.

Whenever the platform invokes “update”, “adjustment” or “evolution of the service” to justify persistent changes, the core of the debate must fall on the demonstration of substantial preservation of the enjoyment bundle, and not on the rhetoric of complexity.p. 480

PTSempre que a plataforma invoca “atualização”, “ajuste” ou “evolução do serviço” para justificar alterações persistentes, o núcleo do debate deve recair sobre a demonstração de preservação substancial do feixe de fruição, e não sobre a retórica da complexidade.

The second objection is that the consumer accepted the change by staying in the service. Marques observes that, in captive contracts, so-called supervening consent is often no more than forced adhesion, especially when the consumer is already invested in the relationship and depends on the ecosystem to keep enjoying what was acquired. Alterations that empty the utility may amount, even under the appearance of continued acceptance, to a factually unilateral variation of the contract, incompatible with good faith. The third objection invokes the license, but qualifying the content as a limited license does not settle the analysis in the platform's favor, because it tries to absorb substantive losses as internal variations.

Where it comes from

In captive contracts, the so-called supervening “consent” is often nothing more than forced adherence to the new state of affairs, especially when the consumer is already invested in the relationship and depends on the ecosystem to keep enjoying what they acquired.p. 654-655

PTEm contratos cativos, o chamado “consenso” superveniente frequentemente não passa de adesão forçada ao novo estado de coisas, especialmente quando o consumidor já está investido na relação e depende do ecossistema para continuar fruindo aquilo que adquiriu.

Alterations that empty the utility, the quality or the material position of the consumer in the relationship may constitute, even under the appearance of continued acceptance, a form of factually unilateral variation of the contract incompatible with good faithp. 655

PTAlterações que esvaziam a utilidade, a qualidade ou a posição material do consumidor no vínculo podem configurar, mesmo sob aparência de aceitação continuada, uma forma de variação faticamente unilateral do contrato incompatível com a boa-fé

Mentioning the term does not serve as a means to legitimize closing the analysis in favor of the platform, but to make visible the kind of language that, in case of conflict, tries to absorb substantive losses as if they were internal variations without legal relevance, which aggravates the loss of verifiability of the promise-enjoyment link.p. 328-329

PTA menção ao termo não serve de condão para legitimar o encerramento da análise em favor da plataforma, mas para tornar visível o tipo de linguagem que, em caso de conflito, tenta absorver perdas substantivas como se fossem variações internas sem relevância jurídica, o que agrava a perda de verificabilidade do nexo promessa-fruição.

The fourth objection invokes technical complexity, on the view that no outsider could assess the change. The answer lies in Humberto Ávila's distinction between security and transparency, since control is exercised through public, contestable and auditable criteria, without requiring everything to be opened up. The fifth objection holds that the object is merely a service, with no content to preserve. This is where Larenz's warning about abbreviated formulas comes in. They serve external systematics but not argument about content and limits, because they erase functional distinctions. Service in the generic sense may work as a label, and it fails where dogmatics needs to define what is being compared.

Technical opacity, by itself, does not work as a free zone of liability.p. 481

PTA opacidade técnica, por si, não funciona como zona franca de responsabilidade.

Where it comes from

Within this framework, Ávila's distinction between security and transparency provides additional protection against deviations.p. 481

PTNesse encaixe, a distinção de Ávila entre segurança e transparência fornece uma proteção adicional contra desvios.

It shifts the axis of control to public, contestable and auditable criteria applicable to decisions that modulate enjoyment.p. 481

PTEla desloca o eixo do controle para critérios públicos, contestáveis e auditáveis aplicáveis a decisões que modulam a fruição.

In platform-governed enjoyment, reducing the IOC to “service” in the generic sense may work as an external label, but it fails where dogmatics needs to work, which is in defining content, limits and criteria of comparison.p. 480

PTEm fruição governada por plataforma, reduzir o OIC a “serviço” em sentido genérico pode funcionar como etiqueta externa, mas falha onde a dogmática precisa trabalhar, que é na definição de conteúdo, limites e critérios de comparação.

Is the consumer entitled to an eternal version?

Equivalence is neither a promise to freeze the service nor a right to an eternal version of it. It does not require literal repetition of attributes or absolute maintenance of layout, stats, parameters or isolated micro-features, and it allows an alteration that makes sense for the ecosystem to go ahead. Mere preference, discontent with ordinary variations and the measurement of the consumer's internal states, feelings or psychological accounts fall outside it. Nor does the requirement turn every adjustment into litigation, and whether the break qualifies as a quality defect, a safety defect or an abusive practice depends on the structure of the concrete case.

Where it comes from

Functional value equivalence does not mean freezing the object, literal repetition of attributes, absolute maintenance of layout, stats, parameters or isolated micro-features.p. 648-649

PTEquivalência de valor funcional não significa congelamento do objeto, repetição literal de atributos, manutenção absoluta de layout, stats, parâmetros ou microcaracterísticas isoladas.

Equivalence is not the measurement of the consumer's internal states, feelings or psychological accounts.p. 477

PTNão é equivalência a medida de estados internos, sentimentos ou relatos psicológicos do consumidor.

Functional value equivalence is neither a promise to freeze the supply nor a right to an eternal version of the service.p. 476

PTA equivalência de valor funcional não é promessa de congelamento do fornecimento nem direito a uma versão eterna do serviço.

The test also presupposes what comes before it. Without the correct qualification of the object as governed enjoyment, the parameters lose their grip, and so equivalence does not apply to every digital purchase but to the relationship in which utility depends on continued administration of the environment. Within this field, the criterion gives the interpreter an intermediate yardstick, which recognizes mutability as compatible with continuous supply as long as it does not destroy the core of enjoyment that gave the purchase its economic and legal meaning. The result is to treat change as conduct subject to limits, proof and normative consequence, without anticipating the outcome of any case.

Where it comes from

Without the correct qualification of the object as governed immaterial enjoyment, the subsequent parameters tend to lose traction, because auditability, functional equivalence, abusive teleology and restoration remedies presuppose precisely the existence of a bond in which the consumed utility depends on continued administration of the environment.p. 719

PTSem a correta qualificação do objeto como fruição imaterial governada, os parâmetros subsequentes tendem a perder aderência, porque auditabilidade, equivalência funcional, teleologia abusiva e remédios de recomposição pressupõem justamente a existência de um vínculo em que a utilidade consumida depende de administração continuada do ambiente.

It makes it possible to recognize that mutability is compatible with continuous supply, but only as long as it does not destroy the core of enjoyment that gave economic and legal meaning to the purchase.p. 731

PTEla permite reconhecer que a mutabilidade é compatível com o fornecimento contínuo, mas apenas enquanto não destruir o núcleo de fruição que deu sentido econômico e jurídico à aquisição.

It is at this point that the IOC regime proves able to treat mutation not as a brute fact of technology, but as conduct subject to limits, proof and normative consequence.p. 731

PTÉ nesse ponto que o regime do OIC se mostra apto a tratar a mutação não como fato bruto da tecnologia, mas como conduta sujeita a limites, prova e consequência normativa.
