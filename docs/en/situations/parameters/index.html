Parameters

The ten parameters organize how each case is read, one per situation, in the order in which the analysis proceeds. Each one states what it controls, what is not enough and where it leads.

What do the parameters resolve?

The conflict is not resolved by how the sector looks or by how familiar society is with the technology. The case must be brought back to parameters dense enough to stabilize expectations, guide proof and allow consistent consequences. They reduce reliance on the interpreter's subjective repertoire and curb the tendency to decide on vague impressions about innovation, technical complexity or freedom of enterprise. What decides is how the environment organizes private power, redistributes the capacity to understand and to contest, and produces relevant frictions in the consumption experience.

The digital condition, by itself, decides nothing.p. 715

PTA condição digital, por si, não decide nada.

Where it comes from

The case must be brought back to parameters dense enough to stabilize institutional expectations, guide the formation of proof and allow consistent normative consequences.p. 714

PTImpõe-se a recondução do caso a parâmetros suficientemente densos para estabilizar expectativas institucionais, orientar a formação da prova e permitir consequências normativas consistentes.

What decides, legally, is the way in which the environment organizes private power, redistributes capacities for understanding and contestation and produces relevant frictions in the consumption experience.p. 715

PTO que decide, juridicamente, é a forma pela qual o ambiente organiza poder privado, redistribui capacidades de compreensão e contestação e produz fricções relevantes sobre a experiência de consumo.

In what order are they applied?

Application starts from the predominant governance mechanism and the type of friction it produces. It identifies the central vulnerability triggered in the relationship. It sets the standard of proof suited to the structure of the conflict. It points to the possible dogmatic consequence. The sequence works as a safeguard, because it prevents both breaking the conflict down into isolated clauses and replacing legal analysis with a diffuse impression about technology.

In the order of the parameters, the qualification of the object and semiotic vulnerability come first. Information and advertising follow. Then come open auditability, functional value equivalence, abusive teleology and burdens of proof. Intellectual property and functional restoration complete the sequence.

What do the legitimacy filters not require?

Open auditability, functional value equivalence and abusive teleology distinguish legitimate modulation from alteration that degrades enjoyment. They do not freeze innovation, and they require neither absolute transparency about the supplier's internal processes nor exhaustive technical expertise from the judge.

When what was bought depends on the environment the platform administers

7.1.1.1 On the parameters for qualifying the Immaterial Object of Consumption as an immaterial product under governed enjoyment

Qualification is the gateway to the regime. The focus moves away from the civil-law ontology of the good and toward the continuous regime of maintaining, making available and modulating enjoyment. The consumer does not enjoy a presumably stable good but an experience conditioned by technical infrastructure, curation of access, updates, visibility, rules of interaction and private enforcement mechanisms. Formal ownership, a use license and nominal availability do not exhaust the problem.

Qualification has effects on two levels. It prevents control from being neutralized by the claim that there is merely a service or merely a content license. And it redefines the reading of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC), which now takes as its starting point enjoyment as a governed relationship, without denying the relevance of the contract. The service dimension does not disappear, because it appears as a way of sustaining, updating and shaping the object itself. The old question of product or service, framed as mutually exclusive, loses much of its analytical usefulness.

The criterion, therefore, is neither the materiality of the medium nor the nomenclature used by the supplier, but the structural dependence of enjoyment on a governed environment.p. 719

PTO critério, portanto, não é a materialidade do suporte nem a nomenclatura empregada pelo fornecedor, mas a dependência estrutural da fruição em relação a um ambiente governado.

Where it comes from

It shifts the focus from the civil-law ontology of the good to the continuous regime of maintenance, availability and modulation of enjoyment.p. 717

PTEla desloca o foco da ontologia civilista do bem para o regime continuado de manutenção, disponibilização e modulação da fruição.

In such contexts, formal ownership, a use license or the mere nominal availability of the object do not exhaust the legal problem.p. 718

PTEm tais contextos, titularidade formal, licença de uso ou mera disponibilidade nominal do objeto não esgotam o problema jurídico.

On the first, it prevents legal control from being neutralized by the supplier's descriptive strategies, especially by the claim that there would be only the provision of a service or a mere content licensep. 718

PTNo primeiro, ela impede que o controle jurídico seja neutralizado por estratégias descritivas do fornecedor, especialmente pela alegação de que haveria apenas prestação de serviço ou mera licença de conteúdo

Instead of operating primarily from the contract as a formal statement, the discipline comes to be structured from enjoyment as a governed relationship.p. 718

PTEm vez de operar prioritariamente a partir do contrato como enunciado formal, a disciplina passa a ser estruturada a partir da fruição como relação governada.

The performance does not disappear, but appears as a mode of sustaining, updating and shaping the object of consumption itself.p. 718

PTA prestação não desaparece, mas comparece como modo de sustentação, atualização e conformação do próprio objeto de consumo.

7.1.1.2 On the parameters of semiotic vulnerability as a mechanism

The relevant vulnerability is not a generic attribute of the digital, nor economic hyposufficiency (hipossuficiência), nor lesser individual aptitude. The question is objective. Whenever the experience is mediated by signs and interfaces whose design, hierarchy, salience and mutability the supplier controls, a field of potentially relevant asymmetry exists. Whether the user was attentive, wary or informed is not the question.

Asymmetry of meaning usually comes with asymmetry of proof, because whoever controls how the object is made intelligible often also controls logs, interface histories, screen versions, curation criteria and change records. The category serves attribution and control. It explains why the duty to inform is not met by sheer volume of text and why advertising is read as the management of expectations.

The parameter also delimits. It is not enough that the case involves an interface, an algorithm or an online environment. The governance of meaning must act structurally on the understanding, comparability, predictability or contestation of enjoyment.

Semiotic vulnerability is relational, structural and functional.p. 721

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica é relacional, estrutural e funcional.

Where it comes from

The vulnerability relevant to the application of the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption cannot be understood as a generic attribute of the digital, nor be reduced to economic hyposufficiency or to the consumer's lesser individual aptitude to understand technically complex environments.p. 719

PTA vulnerabilidade relevante para a incidência do regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo não pode ser compreendida como atributo genérico do digital, nem ser reduzida à hipossuficiência econômica ou à menor aptidão individual do consumidor para compreender ambientes tecnicamente complexos.

Whenever the consumption experience is mediated by signs and interfaces whose design, hierarchy, salience and mutability are controlled by the supplier, there will be a field of semiotic asymmetry potentially relevant to Law.p. 720

PTSempre que a experiência de consumo for mediada por signos e interfaces cujo desenho, hierarquia, saliência e mutabilidade sejam controlados pelo fornecedor, haverá um campo de assimetria semiótica potencialmente relevante para o Direito.

What is particular about this mechanism is that asymmetry of meaning tends to come with asymmetry of proof.p. 720

PTA particularidade desse mecanismo está em que a assimetria de sentido tende a vir acompanhada de assimetria de prova.

Semiotic vulnerability does not operate merely as a sociological description of the environment, but as a category of attribution and control.p. 720

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica não opera apenas como descrição sociológica do ambiente, mas como categoria de imputação e de controle.

The governance of meaning must act structurally on the understanding, comparability, predictability or contestation of enjoyment.p. 721

PTÉ necessário que a governança do sentido atue de forma estrutural sobre a compreensão, a comparabilidade, a previsibilidade ou a contestação da fruição.

When the information exists but gets buried in excess

7.1.1.3 On the parameters of the duty to inform as verifiable adequacy

The duty to inform is not measured by quantity. Piling up texts, links, notices, policies and terms does not meet the requirement and may even work against it. To inform is to make the essential elements of enjoyment intelligible when the decision is being formed and to preserve the conditions for later reconstructing what was promised, shown and altered.

Inadequacy may take three forms. Excess, when overload makes it impossible to tell the essential from the accessory. Dispersion, when what matters is scattered across unconnected documents, screens and paths. Flow architecture, when the attractive is highlighted and the burdensome is pushed into low-visibility zones. The case is one of functional opacity produced by design.

The question is no longer whether the information was somewhere. It becomes whether it was accessible, understandable and relevant at the moment it could influence the expectation or the reaction. That is why the defense that everything was in the contract is not enough, since it confuses formal existence with material performance of the duty.

The duty to inform does not protect only against direct factual lies. It also protects against the strategic shifting of what is legally relevant into regions of low intelligibility.p. 723

PTO dever de informar não protege apenas contra mentira factual direta. Protege também contra o deslocamento estratégico do juridicamente relevante para regiões de baixa inteligibilidade.

Where it comes from

The mere accumulation of texts, links, notices, policies, terms and explanatory windows does not, by itself, satisfy the requirement of adequate information.p. 722

PTA simples acumulação de textos, links, avisos, políticas, termos e janelas explicativas não satisfaz, por si, a exigência de informação adequada.

To inform, in a legally relevant sense, is to make the essential elements of enjoyment intelligible at the moment the consumer's decision is formed and, at the same time, to preserve minimum conditions for later reconstruction of what was promised, what was shown and what was altered.p. 722

PTInformar, em sentido juridicamente relevante, é tornar inteligíveis os elementos essenciais da fruição no momento em que a decisão do consumidor se forma e, ao mesmo tempo, preservar condições mínimas para reconstrução posterior do que foi prometido, do que foi mostrado e do que foi alterado.

Informational inadequacy may thus take the form of excess, dispersion and flow architecture.p. 722

PTA inadequação informacional, assim, pode assumir a forma de excesso, dispersão e arquitetura de fluxo.

This is functional opacity produced by designp. 723

PTTrata-se de opacidade funcional produzida por desenho

It becomes whether this information was made accessible, understandable and relevant at the moment it could influence the legitimate formation of the consumer's expectation or reaction.p. 723

PTPassa a ser se esse dado foi tornado acessível, compreensível e relevante no momento em que poderia influenciar a formação legítima da expectativa ou a reação do consumidor.

This formula confuses formal existence with material performance of the duty.p. 723

PTEssa fórmula confunde existência formal com cumprimento material do dever.

When the storefront promised more than was delivered

7.1.1.4 On the parameters of advertising and offer as controllable management of meaning

In ecosystems where enjoyment is preceded, prepared and guided by continuous cycles of anticipation, advertising and the offer are not an external layer of consumption. In such cases, the offer is built through an economy of signals that precedes enjoyment and conditions how consumers interpret what they acquire. Teasers, storefronts, promotional events, previews, countdowns, suggestions of rarity and promises of prestige or performance make up the offer program when they help form the legitimate expectation.

Deceptiveness does not depend on outright falsehood. It may result from constructed ambiguity, from a relevant omission strategically placed or from a mismatch between the overall meaning of the promise and the real conditions of enjoyment. Advertising may suggest stability where there is precariousness, equivalence where there is asymmetry, rarity where there is artificiality and continuity where there is intense mutability.

Control looks at what was promised as value and as function, at what was concealed as cost or limitation and at whether this frame matches real enjoyment. A single sentence from one piece is not read in isolation, because the promise may emerge from several elements working together, including salience, timing and repetition. The persuasive function of economic activity is not denied.

What matters is not only what was formally said, but what was legally promised through the management of signs, expectations and salience.p. 726

PTNão interessa apenas o que foi formalmente dito, mas o que foi juridicamente prometido por meio da gestão de signos, expectativas e saliências.

Where it comes from

The offer, in such cases, is not reduced to the final purchase statement.p. 724

PTA oferta, nessas hipóteses, não se resume ao enunciado final de compra.

They make up the offer program insofar as they take part in forming the legitimate expectation and in determining the value attributed to the object.p. 724

PTEles compõem o programa de oferta na medida em que participam da formação da expectativa legítima e da determinação do valor atribuído ao objeto.

Advertising can suggest stability where there is structural precariousness, equivalence where there is relevant asymmetry, rarity where there is programmed artificiality, freedom of use where there is asymmetric reversibility, continuity where there is intense mutability.p. 725

PTA publicidade pode sugerir estabilidade onde há precariedade estrutural, equivalência onde há assimetria relevante, raridade onde há artificialidade programada, liberdade de uso onde há reversibilidade assimétrica, continuidade onde há mutabilidade intensa.

Control of the offer must fall on what was promised as value and as function, on what was concealed as cost, limitation or condition of precariousness, and on the correspondence between this semiotic frame and the real enjoyment made available to the consumer.p. 725

PTO controle da oferta deve recair sobre o que foi prometido como valor e como função, sobre o que foi ocultado como custo, limitação ou condição de precariedade, e sobre a correspondência entre essa moldura semiótica e a fruição real disponibilizada ao consumidor.

The point is not to deny the persuasive function proper to economic activityp. 725

PTNão se trata de negar a função persuasiva própria da atividade econômica

The legally relevant promise is not always concentrated in a single statement.p. 725

PTA promessa juridicamente relevante nem sempre se encontra concentrada em um único enunciado.

When the platform changed the rules and left no trace

7.1.1.5 On the parameters of open auditability for control without indiscriminate disclosure of code

Relevant alterations must be reconstructable if a dispute arises, so that opacity does not work as immunity from control. The requirement is selective and functional. What must be auditable is what matters to make the dispute decidable, such as changes to access rules, reconfigurations with functional impact, version changes, sanctions and criteria with material effects. The platform must preserve and disclose trails, records, minimum logs, operational justifications and contextual elements.

The parameter requires neither disclosure of source code nor a general waiver of secrecy, and it does not accept technical complexity as an alibi. Trade secrets, economic exclusivity and complexity cease to be absolute vetoes and must be reconciled with minimum verifiability. The instruments are preservation orders, limited disclosure, restricted expert examination and verifiable reasoning. Minimum verifiability rationalizes the litigation, because it gives expert examinations and orders for clarification something knowable to work on.

What is required is not to strip the whole system bare, but to make controllable, to a sufficient threshold, what legally interferes with enjoyment.p. 727

PTO que se exige não é desnudar o sistema inteiro, mas tornar controlável, em patamar suficiente, aquilo que interfere juridicamente na fruição.

Where it comes from

The requirement, therefore, is selective and functional. What must be auditable is not everything, but what matters to make the controversy decidable.p. 727

PTA exigência, por isso, é seletiva e funcional. O que deve ser auditável não é tudo, mas aquilo que importa para tornar decidível a controvérsia.

In these cases, the platform must be able to preserve and disclose trails, records, minimum logs, operational justifications and contextual elements sufficient to allow limited institutional verification.p. 727

PTNesses casos, a plataforma deve ser capaz de preservar e exibir trilhas, registros, logs mínimos, justificativas operacionais e elementos de contextualização suficientes para permitir verificação institucional delimitada.

The first turns technical complexity into an alibi for irresponsibility.p. 727

PTA primeira transforma complexidade técnica em álibi para irresponsabilidade.

It rationalizes it.p. 728

PTEle a racionaliza.

Structural opacity thus ceases to be a zone naturally immune to legal protection and becomes a legal problem that can be handled through preservation orders, narrowly defined production of documents, restricted expert examination, verifiable reasoning and other instruments consistent with the procedural and administrative rationality of the protective system.p. 729

PTA opacidade estrutural deixa, assim, de ser zona naturalmente imune à tutela e passa a ser problema jurídico tratável por ordens de preservação, exibição delimitada, perícia restrita, motivação verificável e outros instrumentos compatíveis com a racionalidade processual e administrativa do sistema protetivo.

When the update took away the value of what was bought

7.1.1.6 On the parameters of functional value equivalence as the core of enjoyment in a changeable environment

Change, in itself, is not unlawful. The problem begins when it significantly degrades, empties or diverts the function that justified the acquisition. The criterion is not reduced to technical performance or to isolated attributes. It looks at the role of the object in the acquired experience, its utility in the ecosystem, its place in relationships and, when it objectively and recognizably forms the core of the acquisition, its symbolic dimension.

The question is no longer whether the change fell within the general power to update. It becomes whether the experience remains substantially equivalent in function. The parameter rules out both extremes, freezing the environment and legitimizing every alteration because the object nominally survives. It also guides the remedy, giving priority to restoration, equivalent substitution and technical reversal when feasible.

The permanence of the item, the functionality, the account, the access or the feature does not, by itself, neutralize the emptying of the experience if the concrete role of the object is degraded, diverted or made substantially different from the one that justified the purchase.p. 730

PTA permanência do item, da funcionalidade, da conta, do acesso ou da feature não neutraliza, por si, o esvaziamento da experiência se o papel concreto do objeto for degradado, desviado ou tornado substancialmente diverso daquele que justificou a aquisição.

Where it comes from

The problem begins when it ceases to operate as a legitimate form of maintenance, improvement or adaptation of the supply and begins to degrade, empty or divert, in a relevant way, the function that justified the purchase and structured the legitimate expectation of enjoyment.p. 729

PTO problema começa quando ela deixa de operar como forma legítima de manutenção, aperfeiçoamento ou adaptação do fornecimento e passa a degradar, esvaziar ou desviar, de modo relevante, a função que justificou a aquisição e estruturou a expectativa legítima de fruição.

Functional value equivalence is not reduced to maintaining technical performance in the strict sense, nor to the quantitative preservation of isolated attributes.p. 729

PTA equivalência de valor funcional não se reduz à manutenção de desempenho técnico em sentido estrito, nem à preservação quantitativa de atributos isolados.

The legally relevant question then ceases to be whether the change occurred within the supplier's general power to update.p. 730

PTA pergunta juridicamente relevante deixa então de ser se a mudança ocorreu dentro do poder geral de atualização do fornecedor.

The parameter thus prevents two opposite distortions.p. 731

PTO parâmetro, assim, impede duas distorções opostas.

By shifting the focus to the functional core of the acquired experience, it makes it possible to prioritize restoration, equivalent substitution, technical reversal when feasible or other forms of restoration that preserve the practical meaning of the relationship, instead of automatically reducing the injury to later monetization.p. 731

PTAo deslocar o foco para o núcleo funcional da experiência adquirida, torna possível priorizar restauração, substituição equivalente, reversão técnica quando viável ou outras formas de recomposição que preservem o sentido prático do vínculo, em vez de reduzir automaticamente a lesão a monetização posterior.

When loss is a routine part of the design, not an accident

7.1.1.7 On the parameters of abusive teleology and the unlawful act of architecture as conduct

Not all abusiveness appears as an isolated clause, a single event or a one-off failure. In some cases exploitation derives from the operating program, and it is the coordination of several elements that reveals the exploitative purpose. Architecture is treated as conduct, because it organizes access, visibility, friction, irreversibility, contestation and salience. Appeals to efficiency, system balance or the need to update are not enough, by themselves, to neutralize control.

Attribution does not arise from discomfort with the business model. It requires an objectively recognizable functional purpose, revealed by how governance mechanisms, vulnerability, a pattern of opacity, frictions and relevant effects on practical autonomy and enjoyment fit together. Once abusive teleology is recognized, the response may reach the regime itself, with injunctive relief, reconfiguration of flows, duties to give reasons and more symmetrical reversibility mechanisms.

What is controlled is not an inner will, but a recognizable program of operation.p. 732

PTO que se controla não é uma vontade íntima, mas um programa reconhecível de funcionamento.

Where it comes from

In these cases, the injury cannot be adequately grasped by breaking the conflict down into autonomous incidents, because it is precisely the coordination between multiple elements of the environment that makes its exploitative purpose knowable.p. 732

PTNesses casos, a lesão não pode ser adequadamente apreendida pela decomposição do conflito em incidentes autônomos, porque é precisamente a coordenação entre múltiplos elementos do ambiente que torna cognoscível sua finalidade exploratória.

The core of the parameter lies in treating architecture as conduct.p. 732

PTO núcleo do parâmetro está em tratar a arquitetura como conduta.

For this reason, claims of efficiency, optimization, system balance, operational convenience or need for updating are not enough, by themselves, to neutralize control.p. 733

PTPor isso, alegações de eficiência, otimização, equilíbrio do sistema, conveniência operacional ou necessidade de atualização não bastam, por si, para neutralizar o controle.

The attribution of abusive teleology cannot arise from vague discomfort with the business model nor from intuitive criticism of the existence of monetization, engagement or curation.p. 734

PTA imputação de teleologia abusiva não pode nascer de desconforto vago com o modelo de negócios nem de crítica intuitiva à existência de monetização, engajamento ou curadoria.

It requires demonstration of an objectively recognizable functional purpose, revealed by the articulation between governance mechanisms, the vulnerability at stake, the pattern of opacity, the frictions produced and relevant effects on the consumer's practical autonomy and enjoyment.p. 734

PTEla exige demonstração de uma finalidade funcional objetivamente reconhecível, revelada pela articulação entre mecanismos de governança, vulnerabilidade incidente, padrão de opacidade, fricções produzidas e efeitos relevantes sobre a autonomia prática e a fruição do consumidor.

It becomes appropriate to consider injunctive relief, reconfiguration of flows, review of selective frictions, duties to give reasons, more symmetrical mechanisms of reversibilityp. 733

PTTorna-se cabível pensar em tutela inibitória, em reconfiguração de fluxos, em revisão de fricções seletivas, em deveres de motivação, em mecanismos de reversibilidade mais simétricos

How to prove a case when the evidence is in the platform's hands

7.1.1.8 On the parameters of proof and dynamic distribution of the burden in an environment of structural opacity

Proof is part of how the relationship is governed. Whoever controls logs, histories, decision criteria, versions, interface records and traces of sanctions largely controls the conditions for reconstructing what happened. Requiring exhaustive proof from the vulnerable party, as if conditions were symmetrical, rewards private control of obscurity. Preserving and disclosing minimum records is part of the duty to shape the relationship legitimately.

A dynamic distribution of the burden is admissible whenever the relevant evidence is under the supplier's technical control. The more records are concentrated in private hands, the stronger the requirement of preservation, limited disclosure and verifiable justification. This does not dispense with plausibility, a coherent chain of argument or a defined object. An unjustified lack of relevant records may authorize presumptions in the consumer's favor and strengthen the attribution that the supply was inadequate.

The law cannot accept that the platform simultaneously administers enjoyment, the narrative of the event and the conditions of its verifiability, converting this triple concentration into a self-legitimating procedural advantage.p. 736

PTO Direito não pode aceitar que a plataforma administre simultaneamente a fruição, a narrativa do evento e as condições de sua verificabilidade, convertendo essa tripla concentração em vantagem processual autolegitimadora.

Where it comes from

Whoever controls logs, histories, decision criteria, relevant versions, interface records, moderation flows, alteration parameters and traces of sanction controls, to a large extent, the conditions for the legal reconstruction of what happened.p. 734

PTQuem controla logs, históricos, critérios decisórios, versões relevantes, registros de interface, fluxos de moderação, parâmetros de alteração e vestígios de sanção controla, em larga medida, as condições de reconstrução jurídica do que ocorreu.

In these scenarios, requiring exhaustive proof from the vulnerable party as if the conditions under which the litigation is produced were symmetrical means, in practice, rewarding private control of obscurity.p. 735

PTNesses cenários, exigir prova exaustiva da parte vulnerável como se as condições de produção do litígio fossem simétricas significa, na prática, premiar o controle privado da obscuridade.

The consequence is that a dynamic distribution of the burden of proof is admissible whenever the relevant evidence is structurally under the supplier's technical control.p. 735

PTA consequência disso é a admissibilidade de distribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório sempre que a prova relevante estiver estruturalmente sob controle técnico do fornecedor.

The more intense the private concentration of records, the greater the requirement of preservation, delimited production and verifiable justification.p. 736

When intellectual property becomes a shield

7.1.1.9 On the parameters for reconciling the CDC and intellectual property rights in light of the primacy of the social function and Brazilian constitutional limits

Protected exclusivity does not give unrestricted legitimacy to the way it is exercised in the consumer relationship. Protection remains intact on its own level, but it does not grant immunity from control of adequacy, transparency, loyalty, functional equivalence and protection of enjoyment. The holder keeps economic and technical prerogatives, including to organize the environment, protect assets and preserve internal elements. What the holder does not keep is the power to rule out control over supply in advance.

Reconciliation goes through the filters of the regime. Open auditability does not require full disclosure of secrets, and functional equivalence does not deny the prerogative to update. Where there is a conflict, the solution comes from reconciling the institutions functionally, within the constitutional limits and the social function.

Intellectual property protects the ecosystem, but it does not absolve its governance.p. 739

PTA propriedade intelectual protege o ecossistema, mas não absolve sua governança.

PTO titular da exclusividade conserva, em consequência, suas prerrogativas econômicas e técnicas, inclusive para organizar o ecossistema, proteger seus ativos e preservar elementos internos de funcionamento.

What to ask for, with restoration before compensation

7.1.1.10 On the parameters for remedies suited to the primacy of functional restoration and regime correction

The response does not start by automatically converting the injury into money. Whenever technically and legally feasible, the priority remedy is functional restoration and regime correction. Payment after the fact may leave the harmful mechanism untouched and normalize the loss as an ordinary cost of the model. Restoration may involve restoring access, reversing or reviewing sanctions, preserving history, reactivating features, equivalent substitution, reversibility of versions, verifiable reasoning and contestation mechanisms.

Technical complexity does not amount to impossibility. Neither freezing the environment nor subjecting technical decisions to the consumer's individual will is required. When the injury is architectural and repeatable, regime correction becomes available, and injunctive relief, structural measures, collective measures and even compulsory compliance compatible with open auditability and institutional control of enjoyment may be justified. Compensation remains legitimate, in a residual or complementary role, to make good what specific relief cannot reach and to recognize non-pecuniary damages where they exist.

Turning structural abuses into mere operating expenses is, in practice, to neutralize the law through accounting.p. 742

PTTransformar abusos estruturais em meras despesas operacionais é, na prática, neutralizar o Direito pela via contábil.
