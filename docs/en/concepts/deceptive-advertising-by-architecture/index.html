Misleading advertising by architecture

In plain words, on the path of the skin. Can an ad mislead without lying?

What it is

Communication can mislead without lying, because it manages the framing asymmetrically. The architecture of the ad becomes legally relevant when it reinforces the positive side of the experience and pushes the onerous, changeable or limited side out of focus. The effect may come from the hierarchy of what is essential, from selective emphasis on advantages and from moving the relevant conditions to notices, low-visibility layers, later links and zones that are not part of the moment of decision.

Advertising can tell the truth in pieces and still lie as a whole.p. 626

PTA publicidade pode dizer a verdade em pedaços e ainda assim mentir no conjunto.

Where it comes from

In simpler terms, communication does not need to lie in order to mislead. It is enough that it manages the interpretive framing in a sufficiently asymmetric way.p. 626

PTEm termos mais simples, a comunicação não precisa mentir para enganar. Basta que ela administre o enquadramento interpretativo de forma suficientemente assimétrica.

This is the core of misleading advertising by semiotic architecture. The architecture of the advertisement becomes legally relevant when the supplier structures the consumer's understanding so as to reinforce the positive side of the experience and push the onerous, mutable or limited side out of focusp. 626

PTEsse é o núcleo da publicidade enganosa por arquitetura semiótica. A arquitetura do anúncio passa a ser juridicamente relevante quando o fornecedor estrutura a compreensão do consumidor de modo a reforçar o lado positivo da experiência e empurrar o lado oneroso, mutável ou limitado para fora do foco

It can be produced by the way the essential is ranked, by selective emphasis on advantages and by the displacement of relevant conditions to disclaimers, low-visibility layers, later links or discursive zones that do not usefully form part of the decisional momentp. 626

PTEle pode ser produzido pela forma como o essencial é hierarquizado, pela ênfase seletiva nas vantagens e pelo deslocamento das condições relevantes para disclaimers, camadas de baixa visibilidade, links posteriores ou zonas discursivas que não integram de modo útil o momento decisório
