Why does all this seem normal?

You have probably heard that this is how it works, that it was just an update or that this is the rule of the game. Phrases like these make a choice look like a thing of nature, which nobody decided and which nobody could have decided differently.

And this does not happen only in games, which merely let you see, almost with the naked eye, the production of dependence and of symbolic value. Outside them, in apps for work, study, reputation and shopping, the same effects are usually presented as inevitable, and not as design choices. As long as the platform can say that it merely works, merely updates and merely applies rules, the conflict stays trapped in the language of the inevitable.

Where it comes from

If the experience of interactive entertainment allows one to see, almost with the naked eye, the production of dependence, the fabrication of symbolic value, the conversion of routine into bond and the naturalization of asymmetriesp. 23

PTSe a experiência do entretenimento interativo permite ver, quase a olho nu, a produção de dependência, a fabricação de valor simbólico, a conversão de rotina em vínculo e a naturalização de assimetrias

The difference is that, outside the game, the same effects tend to be presented as inevitable rather than as architectural choicesp. 23

PTA diferença é que, fora do jogo, os mesmos efeitos tendem a ser apresentados como inevitáveis, e não como escolhas de arquitetura

As long as the platform can say that it merely works, merely updates, merely executes, merely applies rules, the conflict will remain trapped in the idiom of the inevitable.p. 792

PTEnquanto a plataforma puder dizer que apenas funciona, apenas atualiza, apenas executa, apenas aplica regras, o conflito permanecerá preso ao idioma do inevitável.

This is where the law comes in. One of its functions is to name what, without a name, becomes bad luck, the rule of the game or technical necessity. When it works, the law prevents the will of the strongest from becoming normality just because they can impose it.

Where it comes from

Law is the language of the earthly gods. It is the way in which the collectivity that organizes itself as a State transforms forces into forms, conflicts into criteria, desires into limits and powers into responsibilities. When it works, it prevents the will of the strongest from becoming normality through sheer capacity for imposition. It names and organizes what, without a name, becomes “bad luck”, “the rule of the game” or “technical necessity”.p. 23

PTO Direito é a língua dos deuses terrenos. É o modo pelo qual a coletividade que se organiza como Estado transforma forças em formas, conflitos em critérios, desejos em limites e poderes em responsabilidades. Quando funciona, impede que a vontade do mais forte vire normalidade por pura capacidade de imposição. Ele nomeia e organiza aquilo que, sem nome, vira “azar”, “regra do jogo” ou “necessidade técnica”.

Why does nobody sue the rain?

Nobody sues the rain, because weather happens and we make do. That is why, when a loss arrives looking like weather, nobody thinks of complaining, and life goes on.

On a platform, though, this weather is manufactured and comes in a predictable sequence, with a shallow explanation and an appearance of total normality. The harm stops being an event and becomes part of the routine of whoever governs the place. And when a company starts to govern your participation as if it were weather, and not a choice that can be controlled, the law is already too late.

Where it comes from

Technique comes to operate as a manifest language, that is, fully operative. A language of architecture and engineering that defines what can be seen, proven, contested, repaired and even remembered. It manufactures facts of consumption the way one manufactures weather in a predictable sequence, with superficial justification and an appearance of strict normality. The harm ceases to be an event, since it is part of the very routine of government.p. 32

PTA técnica passa a operar como linguagem manifesta, isto é, plenamente operante. Uma linguagem de arquitetura e engenharia que define o que pode ser visto, provado, contestado, reparado e até lembrado. Ela fabrica fatos do consumo como quem fabrica clima em sequência previsível, com justificativa superficial e aparência de estrita normalidade. O dano deixa de ser um acontecimento, pois integra a própria rotina de governo.

Because when private power begins to govern participation as if it were weather, and not an institutionally controllable choice, the Law is already far too late.p. 755

PTPorque quando o poder privado começa a governar a participação como se fosse clima, e não escolha institucionalmente controlável, o Direito já está atrasado demais.

Technique and mediaAutonomy of technique

Technique usually seems to move on its own. It always chooses the most efficient means, and, once what is efficient has been defined, it grows by itself and spreads. Over time, it starts to impose its logic, as if nobody were deciding anything, and the criterion for decisions simply becomes what works best.

That is why so much change arrives with the excuse of efficiency. It seems the machine asked for it, and not that someone wanted it.

Where it comes from

Jacques Ellul (1968) describes technique as a system that ceases to operate as a mere instrument and comes to present itself as a reality of its own, no longer limited to being a means, but capable of imposing its logic as an object in itselfp. 86

PTJacques Ellul (1968) descreve a técnica como um sistema que deixa de operar como simples instrumento e passa a se apresentar como realidade própria, já não limitada a ser meio, mas capaz de impor sua lógica como objeto em si

This dynamic is oriented by efficiency and tends to automatically select the most effective method, so that, once the criterion of efficiency has been measured and established, technique consolidates and expands by its own forcep. 86

PTEssa dinâmica se orienta pela eficiência e tende a selecionar automaticamente o método mais eficaz, de modo que, uma vez medido e estabelecido o critério de eficiência, a técnica se consolida e se expande por sua própria força

In this scenario, values and purposes lose centrality because technical rationality operates as a machine of instrumentalization, converting everything into a means and shifting the decision criterion to the performance of the method and to what works bestp. 86

PTNesse cenário, valores e finalidades perdem centralidade porque a racionalidade técnica opera como máquina de instrumentalização, convertendo tudo em meio e deslocando o critério de decisão para a performance do método e para aquilo que funciona melhor

Technique and mediaMedia as a form of life

The medium changes what counts. When television took the place of the printed word as the main form of public conversation, appearance and performance gained weight over argument. It was not only the way of saying things that changed, but what seemed important and what seemed true.

The same happens on platforms, only more strongly. It is the screen that decides what appears first, what can be understood and what seems true. Personalizing, ranking and measuring are not mere features of an app, because they carry criteria of value and visibility that guide the behavior of millions of people.

Where it comes from

In Amusing Ourselves to Death, the author analyzes how television, by replacing the printed word as the main medium of public communication, reorganizes the form of discourse and, with this, reconfigures cultural content itselfp. 89

PTEm Amusing Ourselves to Death, o autor analisa como a televisão, ao substituir a palavra impressa como principal meio de comunicação pública, reorganiza a forma do discurso e, com isso, reconfigura o próprio conteúdo cultural

In the political field, visual logic shifts the weight of argument to appearance, performance and impression managementp. 89

PTNo campo político, a lógica visual desloca o peso do argumento para aparência, performance e administração de impressões

Neil Postman shifts the problem to media as a form of life, showing that the means of communication alter criteria of relevance, intelligibility and socially practicable truth.p. 85-86

PTNeil Postman desloca o problema para a mídia enquanto forma de vida, mostrando que os meios de comunicação alteram critérios de relevância, inteligibilidade e verdade socialmente praticável.

Personalization, ranking and metrification do not constitute mere functionalities, since they materialize criteria of value and visibility that guide behavior at scale.p. 90

PTPersonalização, ranqueamento e metrificação não constituem meras funcionalidades, pois materializam critérios de valor e visibilidade que orientam comportamento em escala.

Technique and mediaDesymbolization

Advertising takes symbols from culture, such as friendship, courage and prestige, and sticks them onto products, as if buying solved needs for affection and for a place in the group. Over time, these symbols wear out, and the capacity to give meaning to one's own experiences becomes poorer and more fragmented.

In the digital world, this does not stay in the ad, because it becomes design, and the screen decides when, how and for whom each sign appears, repeats and is rewarded. Manipulation, then, is not only in the content of the message, but in the way it is distributed.

Where it comes from

For the author, companies mobilize cultural symbols to attribute to material products the illusory capacity to satisfy emotional and social needsp. 90

PTPara o autor, empresas mobilizam símbolos culturais para atribuir a produtos materiais a capacidade ilusória de satisfazer necessidades emocionais e sociais

42 According to Willem H. Vanderburg (2016), cultural desymbolization represents the process of weakening the human capacity to attribute meaning to experiences, resulting in a fragmented and impoverished understanding of life as a wholep. 90

PT42 Segundo Willem H. Vanderburg (2016) , a dessimbolização cultural representa o processo de enfraquecimento da capacidade humana de se atribuir significado às experiências, resultando em uma compreensão fragmentada e empobrecida da vida como um todo

desymbolization does not act only in advertising discourse, but couples itself to technical architectures that administer visibility, repetition and rewardp. 91

PTa dessimbolização não atua apenas no discurso publicitário, mas se acopla a arquiteturas técnicas que administram visibilidade, repetição e recompensa

Semiotic manipulation, therefore, does not occur only through the content of messages, but through the form of their distribution and through the interface logic that defines when, how and for whom certain signs appear.p. 91

PTA manipulação semiótica, portanto, não se dá apenas pelo conteúdo das mensagens, mas pela forma de sua distribuição e pela lógica de interface que define quando, como e para quem certos signos aparecem.

Technique and mediaTechnical code

But technique does not fall from the sky, because someone decides what it does. Screens, locks, permissions, rewards and punishments carry social and economic purposes. If technology is made by people, concrete design choices matter to the law.

The code is the set of decisions that defines what a system does. If this code is written by people, it has a direction, and a direction can be reconstructed, examined and criticized. There is no fatalism in this, because what was chosen could also have been chosen differently.

Where it comes from

Andrew Feenberg's critique prevents this diagnosis from being received as fatalism.p. 86

PTA crítica de Andrew Feenberg impede que esse diagnóstico seja recebido como fatalismo.

If technology is a social construction, concrete mediations matter legally: interfaces, frictions, permissions, incentives and punishments materialize social and economic purposes in artifacts and platforms.p. 86

PTSe a tecnologia é construção social, as mediações concretas importam juridicamente: interfaces, fricções, permissões, incentivos e punições materializam finalidades sociais e econômicas em artefatos e plataformas.

For this reason, technical architecture is not a mere support of the consumer relationshipp. 86

PTPor isso, a arquitetura técnica não é mero suporte da relação de consumo

If the technical code is determined by actors, it is politically situated and economically orientable and, therefore, its teleologies can be reconstructed and criticized.p. 135

PTSe o código técnico é determinado por atores, ele é politicamente situado e economicamente orientável e, por isso, suas teleologias podem ser reconstruídas e criticadas.

So the skin that changes, the character remade from scratch and the punishment that keeps escalating all the way to a ban are not natural effects of a technology that evolves. They are design decisions, because someone decided it would be this way, and that decision is something you can ask questions about.

Where it comes from

The alteration of a rare cosmetic, the reconfiguration of a character by rework and the gradation of sanctions up to the ban are not natural effects of an evolving technology, but architectural decisionsp. 120-121

PTA alteração de um cosmético raro, a reconfiguração de um personagem por rework e a gradação de sanções até o banimento não são efeitos naturais de uma tecnologia em evolução, mas decisões de arquitetura

Semiotics and languageReading codes

To read a sign, you need to know the code. A red light in traffic only means stop because everyone learned the rule. In the game, rarity, level, badge and border color work the same way, and only make sense to those who know the rule that organizes them.

The difference is who writes the rule, which in traffic is the law. Inside the game, whoever defines the codes, the categories and what counts as an important difference is the platform itself. It is the platform that decides what is rare, what is common and what each mark means.

Where it comes from

[...] semiotics is treated here as a material dimension of the legal problem, not as an interpretive ornament. [...] In this key, Umberto Eco's contribution allows us to understand how codes and conventions make the performance legible and organize the consumer's expectationp. 261

PT[...] a semiótica é tratada aqui como dimensão material do problema jurídico, não como ornamento interpretativo. [...] Nessa chave, a contribuição de Umberto Eco permite compreender como códigos e convenções tornam legível a prestação e organizam a expectativa do consumidor

There is an asymmetry of meaning, linked to control over reading codes, categories and classifications that define what counts as a relevant difference.p. 322

PTHá assimetria de sentido, ligada ao controle de códigos de leitura, categorias e classificações que definem o que conta como diferença relevante.

Semiotic vulnerability

Remember the purchase in the shop window and the license in the contract? This friction, simple and easy to check, is the clearest picture of a disadvantage that goes beyond knowing or not knowing.

You can read every line of the terms of use and still be unable to check whether you received what was promised. This is not because you weren't paying attention. The meaning of things in there is fixed by rules and records the platform controls, including what has value, what is rare and what counts as an error. It is the platform that decides which links between things stay visible and which paths of reading stay open to you.

That is why the problem stops being knowing more and becomes being able to find your bearings and react. When the environment controls what appears, what is recognized and how consequences are distributed, it becomes harder to plan, to foresee what will happen and to contest a decision. What you face is an inequality of understanding and of capacity to act, created by the way the platform governs the place.

Where it comes from

It is in this friction, simple and verifiable, that semiotic vulnerability becomes intelligible.p. 401

PTÉ nesse atrito, simples e verificável, que a vulnerabilidade semiótica se torna inteligível.

the promise is not auditable through individual reading alone, because the meaning that sustains value and enjoyment is stabilized by rules and records of the ecosystem, while the platform controls which correlations will be activated and which reading paths will be made availablep. 281

PTa promessa não é auditável apenas por leitura individual, porque o sentido que sustenta valor e fruição é estabilizado por regras e registros do ecossistema, enquanto a plataforma controla quais correlações serão ativadas e quais percursos de leitura serão tornados disponíveis

Semiotic vulnerability describes, on this plane, a structural loss of the capacity for verification, and not a defect of the consumer's attention.p. 281

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica descreve, nesse plano, uma perda estrutural de capacidade de verificação, e não um defeito de atenção do consumidor.

When the environment controls the parameters of appearance, recognition and commensuration that structure value and belonging, and keeps opaque the criteria for distributing consequences, the possibility of planning conduct, anticipating effects and contesting decisions is structurally weakened. The problem then shifts from "knowing more" to "being able to find one's bearings and react".p. 396

PTQuando o ambiente controla os parâmetros de aparecimento, reconhecimento e comensuração que estruturam o valor e o pertencimento, e mantém opacos os critérios de distribuição de consequências, a possibilidade de planejar condutas, antecipar efeitos e contestar decisões enfraquece-se estruturalmente. O problema desloca-se, então, do "saber mais" para o "poder orientar-se e reagir".

Semiotic vulnerability can be defined, in summary, as the objective condition of the consumer's exposure to a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in platform-governed ecosystems of enjoymentp. 396

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica pode ser definida, em síntese, como a condição objetiva de exposição do consumidor a uma desigualdade estrutural de inteligibilidade e de agência em ecossistemas de fruição governados por plataforma

Who keeps the records of what happened?

There is a detail that makes the disadvantage of those who only see the environment from outside even more serious. Whoever controls the way things are shown usually also controls the records that would allow reconstructing what happened. The old versions of the screens, the criteria for highlighting, the history of changes and the traces of each decision stay with the platform.

In other words, the inequality of meaning usually comes together with an inequality of proof. Whoever designed the screen that confused you is the one who keeps the snapshot of what it looked like.

Where it comes from

The particularity of this mechanism lies in the fact that the asymmetry of meaning tends to come with an asymmetry of proof. Whoever controls the form by which the object is made intelligible frequently also controls the records by which this intelligibility can be reconstructed in case of conflict.p. 720

PTA particularidade desse mecanismo está em que a assimetria de sentido tende a vir acompanhada de assimetria de prova. Quem controla a forma pela qual o objeto é tornado inteligível frequentemente controla também os registros pelos quais essa inteligibilidade pode ser reconstruída em caso de conflito.

Logs, interface histories, screen versions, curation criteria, change records, visibility parameters and decision tracesp. 720

PTLogs, históricos de interface, versões de telas, critérios de curadoria, registros de alteração, parâmetros de visibilidade e rastros de decisão

Performative transparencyPerformative transparency

Too much information can also hide things. There are layered documents, links that lead to other links, cross-references, overly technical language, the important detail in fine print and the irrelevant one highlighted, and cascades of warnings. In the end, you receive text, but you do not receive guidance.

It is transparency as theater, in which the platform puts on a show of opening everything up while keeping up a noise that, in practice, has the same effect as hiding. The problem is not missing information, but the excess, the dispersion and the wrong ordering of what is essential.

Where it comes from

Successive layers of documents, serialized links, cross-references, excessively technical language, inverted visual emphasis, cascading disclaimers and the displacement of relevant content to improbable zones of the contracting flow can produce the same legal effect as omission.p. 619

PTCamadas sucessivas de documentos, links serializados, remissões cruzadas, linguagem excessivamente técnica, destaque visual invertido, disclaimers em cascata e deslocamento do conteúdo relevante para zonas improváveis do fluxo de contratação podem produzir o mesmo efeito jurídico da omissão.

The consumer receives text, but does not receive guidance. The platform stages informational openness while maintaining, under an appearance of compliance, a regime of noise.p. 619-620

PTO consumidor recebe texto, mas não recebe orientação. A plataforma encena abertura informacional enquanto mantém, sob aparência de conformidade, um regime de ruído.

Performative transparency does not consist in the absence of information, but in the excess, dispersion and defective hierarchization of essential content.p. 620

PTA transparência performática não consiste em ausência de informação, mas em excesso, dispersão e hierarquização defeituosa do conteúdo essencial.

Meaning-vitiated consentMeaning-vitiated consent

You clicked accept, but you accepted within a frame of language that already left you outside the center of the relationship. It was not a mistake about a price or a deadline, which can be pointed out and corrected.

It was something deeper, because the very way the product was supplied made it hard to decipher, compare and foresee the consequences that mattered. You received the text, but you did not receive the key to understand the code that gives it meaning. It is a controlled incapacity, produced by the conditions of communication, and not carelessness on your part.

Where it comes from

The consumer does not merely get one piece of data wrong. They adhere within a frame of language that already places them outside the center of intelligibility of the relationship. It is at this point that one can speak of meaning-vitiated consent. It is not a matter of a one-off factual error, such as confusing a price or an isolated deadline.p. 603

PTO consumidor não erra apenas um dado. Ele adere dentro de uma moldura de linguagem que já o coloca fora do centro de inteligibilidade da relação. É nesse ponto que se pode falar em consentimento viciado por sentido. Não se trata de erro factual pontual, como confundir um preço ou um prazo isolado.

The consumer receives the text, but does not receive real access to the code that organizes its meaning.p. 603

PTO consumidor recebe o texto, mas não recebe acesso real ao código que organiza seu significado.

It is a controlled incapacity.p. 603

PTTrata-se de incapacidade controlada.

structurally produced, by the communicational conditions of supply, to decode, compare and anticipate relevant consequencesp. 604

PTestruturalmente produzida, pelas condições comunicacionais do fornecimento, de decodificar, comparar e antecipar consequências relevantes

Why not just call all this digital vulnerability? Because that expression says where the injury happens, but does not show the machinery. Digital is the setting, and, to arrive at duties, proof and redress, you need to pin down the mechanism that produces the disadvantage, and that is what semiotic vulnerability does.

Where it comes from

The expression “digital vulnerability” will not be adopted here because, although descriptively useful, it offers little dogmatic yield. It names the environment in which the injury occurs, but does not isolate the legally relevant mechanism of the asymmetry.p. 584

PTA expressão “vulnerabilidade digital” não será adotada aqui porque, embora possua utilidade descritiva, entrega pouco rendimento dogmático. Ela nomeia o ambiente em que a lesão ocorre, mas não isola o mecanismo juridicamente relevante da assimetria.

In this vocabulary, the “digital” works as a marker of setting, not as an operator of imputation.p. 584

PTO “digital” funciona, nesse vocabulário, como marcador de cenário, e não como operador de imputação.

stabilizing what matters for duty, proof, unlawfulness and remedyp. 584-585

PTestabilizando aquilo que importa para dever, prova, ilicitude e remédio

Does every digital environment put you at a disadvantage?

This does not mean that every digital environment makes you vulnerable in this sense, nor that you are incapable in the face of technology. The disadvantage that counts is not a generic attribute of the digital nor a failing of yours. It is the one that arises when the signs, screens and records that guide your decision are controlled by whoever is on the other side.

Where it comes from

The vulnerability relevant to the application of the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption cannot be understood as a generic attribute of the digital, nor be reduced to economic hyposufficiency or to the consumer's lesser individual aptitude to understand technically complex environments.p. 719

PTA vulnerabilidade relevante para a incidência do regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo não pode ser compreendida como atributo genérico do digital, nem ser reduzida à hipossuficiência econômica ou à menor aptidão individual do consumidor para compreender ambientes tecnicamente complexos.

Nor is it a matter of presupposing absolute incapacity in the face of the digital.p. 720

PTTampouco se trata de pressupor incapacidade absoluta diante do digital.

What can you demand?

The platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understandingThe platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understanding

When do the signs on the screen weigh against you?

Almost everything you know about your skin passes through screens, notices, icons and paths that the platform designs. It is through them that you find out how much it is worth, what risk you run when buying and how to complain if something goes wrong. When your expectation and your chance to contest depend on these signs controlled by the company, you are in a fragile position. And the law takes this fragility into account.

This fragility has nothing to do with naivety, lack of attention or little familiarity with technology. It arises from the way the environment is set up. Whoever designs the screen decides what is highlighted, what is hidden and in what order things appear. And they usually also keep the records that would allow the story to be told later.

How should the platform set up the experience?

By not setting up the experience in a way that only the platform understands. The design of the environment needs to respect good faith, which is the duty of loyalty between the parties, as well as transparency and a real chance for you to understand what you are using.

How do you show the path that led to your decision?

Through the path you took inside the game up to the purchase or the problem, what was highlighted on the screen and where the information appeared. The records that make it possible to reconstruct how your expectation was formed also matter. Dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase and confirmation screens help, because they show how the environment was set up, and not only what you remember.

What can you ask for when the environment is to blame?

That the problem be treated as a matter of the environment, and not as carelessness on your part. If this dependence on the platform's signs is demonstrated, stronger duties to inform, advertise clearly and present proof may apply. Depending on the case, the practice may be seen as abusive, as insufficient information or as deception by ambiguity or by omission. Added to other elements, this may even change who needs to prove what.

Does every complicated game violate rights?

This does not mean that every complicated game violates rights, nor that you are incapable in the face of the digital. The fragility that counts is the one that appears when the signs controlled by the platform steer your expectation or hinder your reaction.

Where it comes from

Whenever expectation, perception of value, risk assessment or capacity to contest depend on signs, interfaces, flows and informational trails controlled by the supplier, there will be legally relevant semiotic vulnerability.p. 744

PTSempre que expectativa, percepção de valor, avaliação de risco ou capacidade de contestação dependerem de signos, interfaces, fluxos e trilhas informacionais controlados pelo fornecedor, haverá vulnerabilidade semiótica juridicamente relevante.

The term does not designate naivety, credulity or cultural deficiency of the consumer.p. 719

PTO termo não designa ingenuidade, credulidade ou deficiência cultural do consumidor.

Whoever controls the form by which the object is made intelligible often also controls the records by which this intelligibility can be reconstructed in the event of a conflict.p. 720

PTQuem controla a forma pela qual o objeto é tornado inteligível frequentemente controla também os registros pelos quais essa inteligibilidade pode ser reconstruída em caso de conflito.

The corresponding duty is not to organize the experience through a structural asymmetry of meaning incompatible with good faith, transparency and legibility of enjoyment.p. 744

PTO dever correspondente é o de não organizar a experiência por meio de assimetria estrutural de sentido incompatível com boa-fé, transparência e legibilidade da fruição.

The minimum required proof involves reconstructing the user's journey, the informational architecture of the environment, the communicational salience, the paths of understanding and the records available to reconstitute the formation of the expectation.p. 744

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve reconstrução da jornada do usuário, da arquitetura informacional do ambiente, das saliências comunicacionais, dos caminhos de compreensão e dos registros disponíveis para reconstituir a formação da expectativa.

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

What is relevant there is not the user's subjective memoryp. 607

PTO relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário

The possible legal qualification may support abusive practice, informational insufficiency, deceptiveness by ambiguity or relevant omission and, when articulated with other elements, grounds for dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof.p. 744

PTA qualificação jurídica possível pode sustentar prática abusiva, insuficiência informacional, enganosidade por ambiguidade ou omissão relevante e, quando articulada a outros elementos, fundamento para redistribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório.

The institutional consequence consists in bringing the conflict back to the governance of the environment, avoiding moralization of the consumer's conduct and allowing reinforced duties of information, advertising and proof to apply.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional consiste em reconduzir o conflito à governança do ambiente, evitando moralização da conduta do consumidor e permitindo incidência de deveres reforçados de informação, publicidade e prova.

Nor is it a matter of presupposing absolute incapacity in the face of the digital.p. 720

PTTampouco se trata de pressupor incapacidade absoluta diante do digital.
