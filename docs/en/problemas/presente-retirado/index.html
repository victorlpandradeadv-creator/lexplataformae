Your rights · By problem

An item I received as a gift was removed from my account. Now what?

You received an item as a gift through the game's official feature, and later it disappeared from your account because there was fraud somewhere along the way. When the fraud passes through a feature the company itself offers, the risk is part of its business and cannot fall entirely on whoever received it in good faith.

Where it comes from

The second layer is that of the operational risk already known to the supplier. Riot maintains, in more than one title of its portfolio, specific guidance for cases of unauthorized charges, financial fraud and later contestation of transactions. These communications show that the problem of chargeback and misuse of means of payment was not an external, extraordinary or unforeseeable fact. It was an institutionally recognized operational risk, with internal flows for investigation, refund and suspension.p. 610

PTA segunda camada é a do risco operacional já conhecido pela fornecedora. A Riot mantém, em mais de um título de seu portfólio, orientação específica para hipóteses de cobrança não autorizada, fraude financeira e contestação posterior de transações. Essas comunicações demonstram que o problema de chargeback e uso indevido de meios de pagamento não era fato externo, extraordinário ou imprevisível. Tratava-se de risco operacional reconhecido institucionalmente, com fluxos internos de apuração, reembolso e suspensão.

When the supplier already knows the fragility of the transactional ecosystem, it cannot treat its effects as if they were the consumer's residual fault or an accident wholly exogenous to the activity. The risk comes to form part of the professional circle of the economic operation itself.p. 611

PTQuando o fornecedor já conhece a fragilidade do ecossistema transacional, não pode tratar seus efeitos como se fossem culpa residual do consumidor ou acidente totalmente exógeno à atividade. O risco passa a integrar o círculo profissional da própria operação econômica.

The existence of fraud in the ecosystem does not authorize the supplier to shift entirely to the good-faith user the effects of the instability of its own monetization environment.p. 615

PTA existência de fraude no ecossistema não autoriza o fornecedor a deslocar integralmente ao usuário de boa-fé os efeitos da instabilidade do próprio ambiente de monetização.

How do you know whether your gift fits this situation?

The situation is that of someone who received a gift through an official channel, or bought game currency on a site that looked legitimate, and later lost items or had the account suspended. Sometimes the whole account goes back to an earlier state, which is called a rollback, and takes along items you had obtained through normal means.

What usually lies behind it is the chargeback, which happens when someone disputes the card purchase and the bank reverses the payment. The company's rule for these cases may punish not only whoever sent the gift, but also whoever received it.

It matters a lot whether the feature was official. In one concrete case, sending gifts was a regular feature, inside the game and with rules and limits, so the trust of those who received gifts was created by the company itself.

At the same time, outside stores sold the game currency as something safe and advantageous, and no one made clear what was official, tolerated or forbidden. That is why one cannot presume that anyone would know how to tell legitimate resale apart from cloned-card fraud.

Where it comes from

The company deactivated the gifting feature on the Brazilian server for a long period, suppressed received assets and carried out regressive restorations or blocks on affected accounts, without any equivalent effort being seen, at the level of private policy, of consistent individualization between intentional fraud, excusable error, acquisition through an apparently reliable channel and the recipient's good faith.p. 611

PTA empresa desativou por longo período a funcionalidade de presentes no servidor brasileiro, suprimiu ativos recebidos e operou restaurações regressivas ou bloqueios em contas atingidas, sem que se veja, no plano da política privada, esforço equivalente de individualização consistente entre fraude dolosa, erro escusável, aquisição por canal aparentemente idôneo e boa-fé do destinatário.

There is a recent report of a rollback with the permanent disappearance of skins and other cosmetics that the consumer claims to have acquired legitimately through different ordinary means, in a context in which reconstructing the origin of each item becomes extremely burdensome and fragmented.p. 616

PTHá relato recente de rollback com desaparecimento permanente de skins e outros cosméticos que o consumidor afirma ter adquirido legitimamente por diferentes vias ordinárias, em contexto no qual a reconstrução da origem de cada item se torna extremamente onerosa e fragmentada.

In a specific official policy, the company states that bank reversal authorizes suspension of the Riot Account until full reimbursement, and may even give rise to permanent ban in case of recurrence, and extends the sanctioning effect also to gift operations, reaching not only who sent but also who received the content.p. 612

PTEm política oficial específica, a empresa declara que a reversão bancária autoriza suspensão da Conta Riot até ressarcimento integral, podendo inclusive ensejar banimento permanente em caso de recorrência, e estende o efeito sancionatório também às operações de presente, alcançando não apenas quem enviou, mas também quem recebeu o conteúdo.

The gifting feature did not operate on the margins of the platform, nor did it constitute a clandestine or intrinsically deviant practice. On the contrary, Riot itself presented it as a regular feature, integrated into the game client, subject to objective eligibility requirements, quantitative limits, regional restrictions and standardized sending flows.p. 610

PTA função de presentes não operava à margem da plataforma, nem constituía prática clandestina ou intrinsecamente desviada. Ao contrário, a própria Riot a apresentava como funcionalidade regular, integrada ao client do jogo, submetida a requisitos objetivos de elegibilidade, limites quantitativos, restrições regionais e fluxos padronizados de envio.

Gifting was part of the ordinary architecture of the service. The consumer's trust in the lawfulness of the circulation of virtual content through this channel was not a subjective whim. It was trust produced by the platform itself and supported by an officially offered featurep. 610

PTO gifting integrava a arquitetura ordinária do serviço. A confiança do consumidor na licitude da circulação de conteúdo virtual por esse canal não era capricho subjetivo. Era confiança produzida pela própria plataforma e apoiada em funcionalidade oficialmente oferecida

The market for Riot Points and external codes was crossed by a semiotic gray zone, in which promotional materials and digital storefronts presented the external purchase of virtual currency as an ordinary, safe, advantageous practice compatible with the game's ecosystem. The language used in these environments mimicked commercial legitimacy, visual professionalism, subsequent functional integration with the official client, plausible promises of savings and a rhetoric of security. In this ambience, it is not legally serious to presume that the average consumer would always distinguish, with sufficient clarity, a tolerated partner, legitimate resale, a gray market and fraud linked to a cloned card. The vulnerability does not stem from private naivety, but from the absence of robust communicational demarcation between what was admitted, tolerated, official or forbidden.p. 611-612

PTO mercado de Riot Points e de códigos externos era atravessado por uma zona cinzenta semiótica, na qual materiais promocionais e vitrines digitais apresentavam a compra externa de moeda virtual como prática ordinária, segura, vantajosa e compatível com o ecossistema do jogo. A linguagem empregada nesses ambientes mimetizava legitimidade comercial, profissionalismo visual, integração funcional posterior com o client oficial, promessas plausíveis de economia e retórica de segurança. Nessa ambiência, não é juridicamente sério presumir que o consumidor médio sempre distinguiria, com clareza suficiente, parceiro tolerado, revenda legítima, mercado cinzento e fraude vinculada a cartão clonado. A vulnerabilidade não decorre de ingenuidade privada, mas da ausência de demarcação comunicacional robusta entre o que era admitido, tolerado, oficial ou proibido.

What helps prove where each item came from?

After the items disappear, you are left with the task of reconstructing, one by one, where each item came from. That is why the purchase emails, the card statements and the old screenshots of the inventory and of the gifts received are worth so much.

If the currency was bought on an outside site, it helps to keep how that site presented itself, with the ad, the promise of security and the professional look, because that shows why the purchase seemed legitimate.

The rest stays with the company, such as the transaction history, the origin of the removed items and the criterion used to return the account to an earlier state.

Where it comes from

The episode shows how the platform, by carrying out a broad reversal of the account's state, transfers to the player the almost archaeological burden of reconstructing item by item what was already part of his sphere of enjoyment.p. 616

PTO episódio mostra como a plataforma, ao operar reversão ampla do estado da conta, transfere ao jogador o encargo quase arqueológico de reconstituir item por item aquilo que já fazia parte de sua esfera de fruição.

Once the assets are removed or the state of the account is reverted, the user comes to depend on old statements, scattered emails, past captures and reconstructed memory to prove that a given content legitimately was part of his position of enjoyment.p. 616

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição.

The language used in these environments mimicked commercial legitimacy, visual professionalism, subsequent functional integration with the official client, plausible promises of savings and a rhetoric of security.p. 612

PTA linguagem empregada nesses ambientes mimetizava legitimidade comercial, profissionalismo visual, integração funcional posterior com o client oficial, promessas plausíveis de economia e retórica de segurança.

The fifth is that of evidentiary asymmetry, with the need to preserve trails, transaction histories, the origin of the removed items, the criteria used for the rollback and individualized justification of the measures adopted.p. 617

PTA quinta é a de assimetria probatória, com necessidade de preservação de trilhas, históricos de transação, origem dos itens removidos, critérios utilizados para rollback e justificativa individualizada das medidas adotadas.

What can you ask to get back?

When no individual and consistent proof rules out your good faith, the request may be that the removed items come back and that your account be restored. It is also fair to ask the company to keep and show the records and explain why the measure hit you in particular.

The request may also include that the company state clearly where it is safe to buy and that it stop applying rules that place the whole risk of fraud on the buyer.

The terms of use, which are the contract accepted when creating the account, call what you have a revocable license, a permission to use that the company says it can withdraw. That label is not enough to put the system's failure on you. The company itself showed there was another way when, in another of its games, it began to prefer payment methods less exposed to disputes.

Where it comes from

The consumer regime allows, here, requests for functional restoration of the position of enjoyment, restoration of assets when the user's good faith has not been refuted by robust individualized proof, preservation and disclosure of minimum records of the supply, informational correction about legitimate acquisition channels and neutralization of clauses or practices that transfer entirely to the consumer the structural risks of the system.p. 617

PTO regime consumerista comporta, aqui, pedidos de recomposição funcional da posição de fruição, restauração de ativos quando a boa-fé do usuário não foi infirmada por prova individualizada robusta, preservação e exibição de registros mínimos do fornecimento, correção informacional sobre canais legítimos de aquisição e neutralização de cláusulas ou práticas que transfiram integralmente ao consumidor os riscos estruturais do sistema.

If the platform itself institutionalizes a given functionality, knows the related risks and allows the circulation of value to unfold in an objectively confusing communicational environment, it cannot treat the consumer as the universal guarantor of the system's failures.p. 615

PTSe a própria plataforma institucionaliza determinada funcionalidade, conhece os riscos correlatos e permite que a circulação de valor se desenvolva em ambiente comunicacional objetivamente confuso, não pode tratar o consumidor como fiador universal das falhas do sistema.

The central dogmatic point, however, is that this contractual vocabulary is not enough to convert the consumer into the legitimate recipient of systemic risk.p. 615

PTO ponto dogmático central, porém, é que esse vocabulário contratual não basta para converter o consumidor em destinatário legítimo do risco sistêmico.

In another title of the same ecosystem, the company began to favor payment instruments less exposed to later disputes, such as prepaid cards and single-use codes, with strong discouragement of purchases on unauthorized third-party sites. The move is telling: the response to the chargeback risk was not only punitive, but also reorganized the transactional environment toward means more controllable from the supplier's point of view. This confirms that there were structural alternatives for mitigating the risk.p. 616-617

PTEm outro título do mesmo ecossistema, a empresa passou a valorizar instrumentos de pagamento menos expostos a contestação posterior, como cartões pré-pagos e códigos de uso único, com forte desestímulo à aquisição em sites de terceiros não autorizados. O movimento é eloquente: a resposta ao risco de chargeback não foi apenas punitiva, mas também reorganizadora do ambiente transacional em direção a meios mais controláveis do ponto de vista do fornecedor. Isso confirma que havia alternativas estruturais de mitigação do risco.

Who should you take the case to when many people lost gifts at the same time?

The first request goes to the company itself, with what you kept about the origin of the items. If the answer is only that the terms of use allow the content to be removed, the case can go on through Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, paths that the channels page details.

In a lawsuit, it is fair to ask the company to show the records that only it has. If it does not cooperate, certain facts may start to count in your favor.

When the measure hits a mass of players at the same time, the case also lends itself to a collective response, which can correct the rule for everyone.

Where it comes from

In the case of the irregular gifts, this means that Riot could not treat the withdrawal of skins, the suspension of functionalities and the imputation of debt to the good-faith recipient as a simple neutral consequence of its terms of use.p. 615-616

PTNo caso dos presentes irregulares, isso significa que a Riot não poderia tratar a retirada de skins, a suspensão de funcionalidades e a imputação de débito ao destinatário de boa-fé como simples decorrência neutra de seus termos de uso.

Given the concentration of records, preservation and disclosure of supply trails are warranted, with rational redistribution of the burden and presumptions favorable to the consumer when the supplier does not cooperate or keeps the proof in a black box.p. 609

PTDiante da concentração de registros, cabe preservação e exibição de trilhas do fornecimento, com redistribuição racional do ônus e presunções favoráveis ao consumidor quando o fornecedor não coopera ou mantém a prova em caixa-preta.

Instead of treating fraud as a structural problem of its own transactional environment, Riot redistributed the risk to an undifferentiated mass of consumers, suppressed assets, interrupted an official content-circulation feature and imposed sanctions based on broad presumptions of co-responsibility, including on users who did not knowingly take part in the fraud.p. 610

PTEm vez de tratar a fraude como problema estrutural de seu próprio ambiente transacional, a Riot redistribuiu o risco para uma massa indistinta de consumidores, suprimiu ativos, interrompeu funcionalidade oficial de circulação de conteúdo e operou sanções fundadas em presunções amplas de corresponsabilidade, inclusive sobre usuários que não participaram conscientemente da fraude.

When the litigation reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or non-verifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is every purchase made outside the game protected?

Buying outside the game does not bring automatic protection. What weighs is whether the channel seemed legitimate and whether you acted in good faith, because the difference between deliberate fraud and a gift received in good faith still holds.

Getting items back depends on your good faith not having been ruled out by individual and consistent proof.

Reports from other players help show the picture, but on their own they do not prove what happened. And asking is not the same as getting, because each case depends on what can be shown.

Where it comes from

The company deactivated the gifting feature on the Brazilian server for a long period, suppressed received assets and carried out regressive restorations or blocks on affected accounts, without any equivalent effort being seen, at the level of private policy, of consistent individualization between intentional fraud, excusable error, acquisition through an apparently reliable channel and the recipient's good faith.p. 611

PTA empresa desativou por longo período a funcionalidade de presentes no servidor brasileiro, suprimiu ativos recebidos e operou restaurações regressivas ou bloqueios em contas atingidas, sem que se veja, no plano da política privada, esforço equivalente de individualização consistente entre fraude dolosa, erro escusável, aquisição por canal aparentemente idôneo e boa-fé do destinatário.

The consumer regime allows, here, requests for functional restoration of the position of enjoyment, restoration of assets when the user's good faith has not been refuted by robust individualized proofp. 617

PTO regime consumerista comporta, aqui, pedidos de recomposição funcional da posição de fruição, restauração de ativos quando a boa-fé do usuário não foi infirmada por prova individualizada robusta

Community reports are not the central proof of an unlawful act, but they are useful to formulate and illustrate a, even if hypothetical, long-lasting consumer experience in which one glimpses the restriction and sedimentation of a broad punitive policy over the Brazilian basep. 611

PTOs relatos comunitários não são a prova central de um ilícito, mas têm utilidade para formular e ilustrar uma, ainda que hipotética, experiência consumerista de longa duração na qual se vislumbra restrição e sedimentação de uma política punitiva ampla sobre a base brasileira

The minimum required proof involves demonstrating the link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The rights behind this case

  • The platform cannot set up the experience so that you do not understand
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out
  • The response should restore what you lost

Read with one caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how whoever decides reads the facts. What changes is the way of telling the conflict, which stops being a loose episode and becomes the way the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way of narrating the conflict changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the program of supply.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
