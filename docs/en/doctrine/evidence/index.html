The jurist's path

Proof

In platform-governed consumption, the disputed fact arises and remains within the supplier's infrastructure, so whoever controls records, versions and traces of sanction also controls the conditions for reconstructing what happened. Semiotic vulnerability then turns into allegation, proof and claim, and the CDC and the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC) provide the tools to request the right record and allocate the burden.

Why is proof part of the relationship itself?

Whether the IOC regime actually takes effect depends on rules of evidence suited to the way supply is organized today. In platform-governed ecosystems, whoever controls the logs (the automatic records of what the system did), histories, decision criteria, versions, interface records, change parameters and traces of sanctions largely controls the conditions for reconstructing, in legal terms, what happened. That is why proof is not external to the relationship but part of how the relationship is governed, and structural opacity cannot be treated as a neutral fact of technology or as an inconvenience the consumer must bear in full.

Why does neither the impressionistic complaint nor formal equality of burdens settle the matter?

A mistake is possible on each side. The first lies in the allegation and consists in asserting, impressionistically, that the consumer was manipulated or confused. That approach does not work, because what matters is showing that the communicational regime of supply was structured to produce a relevant asymmetry of understanding, comparison and contestation, traceable to legally controllable duties, defects and remedies.

Where it comes from

The point is not to assert, impressionistically, that the consumer was “manipulated” or “confused”. The point is to show that the communicational regime of supply was structured so as to produce a relevant asymmetry of understanding, comparison and contestation, and that this asymmetry can be traced back to legally controllable duties, defects and remedies.p. 605-606

PTO ponto não está em afirmar, de modo impressionista, que o consumidor foi “manipulado” ou “confundido”. O ponto está em mostrar que o regime comunicacional do fornecimento foi estruturado de modo a produzir assimetria relevante de compreensão, comparação e contestação, e que essa assimetria pode ser reconduzida a deveres, vícios e remédios juridicamente controláveis.

The second mistake lies in procedure. In many conflicts, the disputed fact arises, changes and remains within the supplier's technical infrastructure, the sanction may be automated and the essential information may sit in internal systems the consumer cannot reach. Demanding exhaustive proof from the vulnerable party, as if the conditions under which the dispute was produced were symmetrical, in practice rewards private control of obscurity, and formal equality of burdens may end up operating as a mechanism of material inequality.

When the evidence arises and remains under the platform's control, the procedural system cannot feign neutrality.p. 659

What is alleged when semiotic vulnerability is alleged?

Semiotic vulnerability only becomes useful to legal doctrine when it stops being a general diagnosis of the environment and starts to operate as a scheme of attribution and control. That shift is organized on three planes, the mechanism alleged, the objective basis that demonstrates it and the typical consequence that the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC) already allows one to draw. On the first plane, what is alleged is not the consumer's failure to read, distraction or psychological incapacity, but a communicational and architectural structure that distributes power. The platform controls language, framings, signs of equivalence, criteria of rarity, acceptance flows, update messages and the ways the object itself is made visible, and in doing so it administers the means by which enjoyment will be perceived and assessed. Semiotic vulnerability can, for that reason, be alleged as a legal fact.

Since the platform permanently reorganizes the decision environment and the environment of enjoyment, the allegation takes on stable forms. PTO que se protege aqui não é curiosidade tecnológica do consumidor, mas a possibilidade institucional de reconstrução de alterações e impactos que atingem sua fruição.

The evidentiary discipline proposed here is also important because it prevents standards such as open auditability and functional value equivalence from turning into mere conceptual rhetoric. Without a compatible evidentiary regime, these parameters would remain correct in theory, but ineffective in practice.p. 736

PTA disciplina probatória aqui proposta também é importante porque impede que standards como auditabilidade aberta e equivalência de valor funcional se convertam em mera retórica conceitual. Sem regime de prova compatível, esses parâmetros permaneceriam corretos em tese, mas ineficazes na prática.

It is not enough to state that relevant changes must be intelligible or that enjoyment must remain functionally equivalent. There must be instruments to demonstrate, contest and reconstruct the occurrence of modulations, losses, reconfigurations and sanctions. Evidence, therefore, is not a later and accessory stage of the reasoning. It is a means of effectiveness of the regime itself.p. 736

PTNão basta afirmar que alterações relevantes devem ser inteligíveis ou que a fruição deve permanecer funcionalmente equivalente. É necessário haver instrumentos para demonstrar, contestar e reconstruir a ocorrência de modulações, perdas, reconfigurações e sanções. A prova, portanto, não é etapa posterior e acessória do raciocínio. É meio de eficácia do próprio regime.

The platform does more than supply, alter and sanction, because it also shapes the ways in which these operations can be seen, narrated and verified. Recognizing this is a condition for the CDC to remain effective in technically complex ecosystems, and without this recognition protection risks being proclaimed on paper while neutralized by architectures that concentrate power over the very possibility of proving the degradation.

In environments of structural opacity, evidence must be read as an internal dimension of the governance of the immaterial object of consumption.p. 737

PTEm ambientes de opacidade estrutural, a prova deve ser lida como dimensão interna da governança do objeto imaterial de consumo.

It is necessary to leave the plane of distant ideals and actually enter the terrain where the platform can be legally obliged to explain, show, preserve, restore and stop hiding behind its own black box.p. 663

PTÉ necessário deixar o plano dos ideais longínquos para adentrar, de fato, no terreno onde a plataforma pode ser juridicamente obrigada a explicar, mostrar, preservar, recompor e parar de se esconder atrás da própria caixa-preta.

Where it comes from

The platform does not only supply, alter and sanction. It also conditions the ways in which these operations can be seen, narrated and verified. Recognizing this is a condition for the CDC to retain effectiveness in ecosystems of high technical complexity. Without this recognition, consumer protection runs the risk of remaining formally proclaimed, but materially neutralized by architectures that concentrate power not only over enjoyment, but over the very possibility of proving that it was degraded, obscured or abusively governed.p. 737

PTA plataforma não apenas fornece, altera e sanciona. Ela também condiciona as formas pelas quais essas operações podem ser vistas, narradas e verificadas. Reconhecer isso é condição para que o CDC conserve eficácia em ecossistemas de alta complexidade técnica. Sem esse reconhecimento, a tutela consumerista corre o risco de continuar formalmente proclamada, mas materialmente neutralizada por arquiteturas que concentram poder não só sobre a fruição, mas sobre a própria possibilidade de provar que ela foi degradada, obscurecida ou abusivamente governada.
