Your rights · By problem

What was advertised is not what I received. Now what?

You bought after seeing previews, countdowns, rarity badges or promises of prominence, and what arrived later does not match what all of that led you to expect. When the ad helped shape what you understood you were buying, it becomes part of the offer, and the difference between what was promised and what was delivered can be claimed.

Where it comes from

Whenever enjoyment is preceded by curated cycles of anticipation, promise, suggested scarcity, symbolic distinction or insinuated equivalences, advertising will materially be part of the offer.p. 744-745

PTSempre que a fruição for precedida por ciclos curados de antecipação, promessa, escassez sugerida, distinção simbólica ou equivalências insinuadas, a publicidade integrará materialmente a oferta.

Whenever promotional communication takes part in determining what the consumer legitimately understands they are acquiring, it is incorporated into the normative field of the offer and becomes subject to control for deceptiveness, ambiguity or relevant omission.p. 725

PTSempre que a comunicação promocional participar da determinação daquilo que o consumidor legitimamente entende estar adquirindo, ela se incorpora ao campo normativo da oferta e se torna suscetível de controle por enganosidade, ambiguidade ou omissão relevante.

How do you know whether the ad became part of the purchase?

The offer is what the company commits to deliver when it advertises. It is not always in a single sentence, because previews, storefronts, events, countdowns, hints of rarity and promises of prestige or performance can together form the idea of what you were buying.

The deception also does not need to be an outright lie. The ad may suggest that the item is stable when it can change at any moment, that it is rare when the rarity was programmed or that it is worth as much as another when it is not.

The deciding point is whether the ad really weighed on how you understood the item, on the value you gave it and on the decision to buy. When the promise was of prestige or recognition, the failure may not even appear as a technical defect, but as the loss of the place that was sold as the core of the purchase.

Where it comes from

Teasers, storefronts, promotional events, announcement cycles, previews, countdowns, suggestions of rarity, insinuated equivalences, promises of prestige, belonging or performance and narrative models of scarcity do not operate, in these environments, as mere accessory persuasion. They make up the program of the offer insofar as they take part in forming legitimate expectation and in determining the value attributed to the object.p. 724

PTTeasers, vitrines, eventos promocionais, ciclos de anúncio, prévias, contagens regressivas, sugestões de raridade, equivalências insinuadas, promessas de prestígio, pertencimento ou desempenho e modelos narrativos de escassez não operam, nesses ambientes, como mera persuasão acessória. Eles compõem o programa de oferta na medida em que participam da formação da expectativa legítima e da determinação do valor atribuído ao objeto.

The legally relevant promise is not always concentrated in a single statement.p. 725

PTA promessa juridicamente relevante nem sempre se encontra concentrada em um único enunciado.

In immaterial objects of consumption, inducement into error may result from constructed ambiguity, from strategically distributed relevant omission or from a mismatch between the overall meaning of the promise and the real conditions of enjoyment under managed mutability. Advertising may suggest stability where there is structural precariousness, equivalence where there is relevant asymmetry, rarity where there is programmed artificiality, freedom of use where there is asymmetric reversibility, continuity where there is intense mutability.p. 724-725

PTEm objetos imateriais de consumo, a indução em erro pode resultar de ambiguidade construída, de omissão relevante estrategicamente distribuída ou de descompasso entre o sentido global da promessa e as condições reais da fruição sob mutabilidade administrada. A publicidade pode sugerir estabilidade onde há precariedade estrutural, equivalência onde há assimetria relevante, raridade onde há artificialidade programada, liberdade de uso onde há reversibilidade assimétrica, continuidade onde há mutabilidade intensa.

Where prior communication significantly shapes the reading of the object, becomes part of the perception of its value and conditions the consumer's adherence, there will be an offer in a materially controllable sense.p. 726

PTOnde a comunicação antecedente modelar de modo relevante a leitura do objeto, integrar a percepção de seu valor e condicionar a adesão do consumidor, haverá oferta em sentido materialmente controlável.

Control must therefore capture the overall regime of signification produced by the supplier. This requirement is particularly important in immaterial objects of consumption whose enjoyment depends on visibility, reputation, collective recognition or insertion into an ecosystem of symbolic value. In them, the mismatch between promise and delivery may not manifest itself as an evident technical defect, but as a relevant frustration of the symbolic or relational position presented as part of the core of the acquisition.p. 726

PTO controle precisa, por isso, captar o regime global de significação produzido pelo fornecedor. Essa exigência é particularmente importante em objetos imateriais de consumo cuja fruição depende de visibilidade, reputação, reconhecimento coletivo ou inserção em ecossistema de valor simbólico. Neles, o descompasso entre promessa e entrega pode não se manifestar como defeito técnico evidente, mas como frustração relevante da posição simbólica ou relacional apresentada como parte do núcleo da aquisição.

What evidence of the ad is worth saving before it is taken down?

It pays to keep the ads, the previews, the store screens showing what was featured, and the purchase and confirmation screens, all with dates. It also helps to record what happened next, so you can compare the earlier promise with what you were able to use.

These records carry more weight than your memory, because they show how the sales environment was set up. The conversation stops being about what you understood and becomes about what the screen presented.

Where it comes from

The minimum required proof involves reconstruction of the promotional environment, of the communication pieces, of the symbolic saliences, of the context of presentation of the object and of the relationship between prior promise and later enjoyment.p. 745

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve reconstrução do ambiente promocional, das peças de comunicação, das saliências simbólicas, do contexto de apresentação do objeto e da relação entre promessa antecedente e fruição posterior.

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

What is relevant there is not the user's subjective memory, but the architecture actually presented. The claim ceases to be “I did not understand” and becomes “the environment was designed this way”.p. 607

PTO relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário, mas a arquitetura efetivamente apresentada. A alegação deixa de ser “eu não entendi” e passa a ser “o ambiente foi desenhado desta forma”.

What can you demand when the delivery fell short of the ad?

You may ask for the offer to be fulfilled or corrected and for the function or position the ad promised, or something equivalent, to be restored to you. When the same message keeps misleading other people, you can also ask for it to stop.

Depending on the case, Brazilian consumer law may treat this as an offer that binds whoever made it, or as misleading advertising, meaning advertising that creates an image the delivered product does not live up to. It may also treat it as abusive advertising, meaning advertising that exploits the buyer's weak spots to pressure the decision.

When the company promises rarity, performance or an advantage and later downgrades it by criteria no one can check, your frustration need not be dismissed as mere annoyance. It may be a significant failure in the supply itself.

Where it comes from

The possible institutional consequence covers correction of the offer, functional restoration, injunctive protection over the communication and, depending on the case, invalidation of promotional strategies incompatible with the real experience delivered.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional possível abrange correção da oferta, recomposição funcional, tutela inibitória sobre a comunicação e, conforme o caso, invalidação de estratégias promocionais incompatíveis com a experiência real entregue.

The legal qualification may fall under misleading or abusive advertising, binding offer, abusive practice through managed ambiguityp. 745

PTA qualificação jurídica poderá recair em publicidade enganosa ou abusiva, oferta vinculante, prática abusiva por ambiguidade administrada

Misleading advertising, in turn, manifests itself when the image of utility or equivalence built by the promotional environment finds no verifiable correspondence in the actual experience of enjoyment, either because the platform later downgrades the object, or because the initial promise already concealed, through ambiguity or through the architecture of visibility, the real way in which the utility would be delivered.p. 631

PTA publicidade enganosa, por sua vez, manifesta-se quando a imagem de utilidade ou equivalência construída pelo ambiente promocional não encontra correspondência verificável na experiência efetiva de fruição, seja porque a plataforma rebaixa posteriormente o objeto, seja porque a promessa inicial já ocultava, por ambiguidade ou por arquitetura de visibilidade, o modo real como a utilidade seria entregue.

Abusive advertising, finally, appears when the design of the environment systematically exploits the consumer's vulnerability, compressing his space for deliberation through artificial urgency, symbolic reward, ritualization of acceptance and fragmentation of the relevant conditions.p. 631

PTA publicidade abusiva, por fim, comparece quando o desenho do ambiente explora de maneira sistemática a vulnerabilidade do consumidor, comprimindo seu espaço de deliberação por urgência artificial, recompensa simbólica, ritualização do aceite e fragmentação das condições relevantes.

If the platform promises a curated experience of value, rarity, performance or advantage, and then rebalances or downgrades it under opaque criteria, the frustration is not mere displeasure. It may be a relevant inadequacy of the governed enjoyment itself.p. 634

PTSe a plataforma promete uma experiência curada de valor, raridade, desempenho ou vantagem, e depois a reequilibra ou a rebaixa sob critérios opacos, a frustração não é mero dissabor. Pode ser inadequação relevante da própria fruição governada.

Who should you take the gap between the ad and the delivery to?

Your first stop is usually the company itself, with the ad and the screens in hand, because the duty to make what was promised match what was delivered belongs to it.

If the company does not fix it, the case can go to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, routes explained on the channels page. When the same ad reaches many people, the response can go beyond your case and change the message itself, through a class action or through the work of public consumer protection agencies.

Where it comes from

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure correspondence between promise, announced value, hidden costs and the experience actually made available under managed mutability.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar correspondência entre promessa, valor anunciado, custos ocultados e experiência efetivamente disponibilizada sob mutabilidade administrada.

When the litigation reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or non-verifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets.p. 662

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Does every exaggerated ad entitle you to something?

No. Selling with enthusiasm, drawing attention and stirring the desire to buy are part of commerce. What consumer law does not accept is for this to work by hiding the essentials and capturing the buyer's expectation.

What is examined is what was promised as value and as function, what was hidden as cost, limit or fragility, and whether that matches what you received. A loose, upbeat phrase with no weight in your decision is not enough.

Where it comes from

The market may promote, persuade, dramatize and organize interest. What the CDC does not allow is for this organization of interest to operate through functional opacity, capture of expectation and displacement of the essential outside the useful field of understanding.p. 631

PTO mercado pode promover, persuadir, dramatizar e organizar interesse. O que o CDC não admite é que essa organização do interesse opere mediante opacidade funcional, captura da expectativa e deslocamento do essencial para fora do campo útil de compreensão.

Control of the offer must fall on what was promised as value and as function, on what was concealed as cost, limitation or condition of precariousness, and on the correspondence between this semiotic frame and the real enjoyment made available to the consumer.p. 725

PTO controle da oferta deve recair sobre o que foi prometido como valor e como função, sobre o que foi ocultado como custo, limitação ou condição de precariedade, e sobre a correspondência entre essa moldura semiótica e a fruição real disponibilizada ao consumidor.

One word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
