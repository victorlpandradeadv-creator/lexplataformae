How this work was born

The work was born from a path that went through the institutional dimension of industrial property and gained sharpness in interactive entertainment, where enjoyment depends on private centers of decision and on opaque infrastructures. It was not born from a prior institutional agenda, but from a discomfort that repeated itself for years, while technical layers stopped being tools and began to function as an environment.

It also came from teaching and from the need to sustain Consumer Law as a language of protection, and not as ritual. Teaching and following discussions on collective redress in consumption took the perspective beyond the contract as paper, to consumption as routine, expectation and frustration.

There, a pattern repeated itself: contemporary injury rarely announces itself as an explicit blow. It usually comes as administered normality, as a promise of experience, as an incentive to stay, as a cost one learns to call inevitable.p. 22

PTAli, repetiu-se um padrão: a lesão contemporânea raramente se anuncia como golpe explícito. Ela costuma vir como normalidade administrada, como promessa de experiência, como incentivo de permanência, como custo que se aprende a chamar de inevitável.
