Your rights · By problem

The offer said last chance. Was I pressured into buying?

The countdown, the limited edition or the notice that the item would disappear made you decide quickly, perhaps before you understood everything you were buying. The store itself may have set up that rush, and what matters is when it comes with hidden information and no real chance to compare.

Where it comes from

The first family of these techniques is that of artificial scarcity and manufactured urgency. Its mechanism is well known. Short windows, accelerated rotation of offers, countdown timers, “limited editions”, ephemeral events and temporary benefits are used to shift the decision from the field of rational comparison to that of reactive response.p. 670

PTA primeira família dessas técnicas é a da escassez artificial e da urgência fabricada. Seu mecanismo é conhecido. Janelas curtas, rotação acelerada de ofertas, contadores regressivos, “edições limitadas”, eventos efêmeros e benefícios temporários são usados para deslocar a decisão do campo da comparação racional para o da resposta reativa.

The problem is not the isolated use of temporality in the market, but its articulation with opacity and with the practical impossibility of comparison.p. 670

PTO problema não é o uso isolado de temporalidade no mercado, mas sua articulação com opacidade e com impossibilidade prática de comparação.

How do you know whether the rush was manufactured?

A digital item could be sold to everyone, at any time, without running out. When it only appears in short windows, disappears, comes back now and then or becomes a last chance, that scarcity is the seller's choice, not the nature of the item.

The rush can also come in series, with passes, time-bound goals, staged events and rewards that depend on always coming back. In this arrangement, giving up no longer just means stopping. It means losing what you accumulated and falling behind the others.

The strongest sign is the combination. The urgency is highlighted, while the limits of use, the chance that the item will change and the possible losses stay in a corner. And you do not need to prove that someone meant to rush you.

Where it comes from

In digital objects, the marginal cost of reproduction tends to fall drastically and, in theory, availability could expand without requiring productive capacity comparable to the factory, logistics and the extraction of inputs. Therefore, when a persistent regime of unavailability, exclusivity and last chance is observed in these markets, one is not facing a natural condition of the object, but a deliberate institutional choice, so that scarcity appears not as a fact, but as a projectp. 209

PTEm objetos digitais, o custo marginal de reprodução tende a cair drasticamente e, em tese, a disponibilidade poderia expandir-se sem exigir capacidade produtiva comparável à fábrica, à logística e à extração de insumos. Por isso, quando se observa nesses mercados um regime persistente de indisponibilidade, exclusividade e última chance, não se está diante de condição natural do objeto, mas de escolha institucional deliberada, de modo que a escassez não aparece como fato, mas como projeto

Teasers, pre-sales, conditioned progressions, passes, serialized rewards and temporary goals build a trajectory in which value no longer resides only in the object and comes to reside in the cost of “falling behind”. The relevant legal effect is the conversion of enjoyment into a directed path, in which giving up no longer means a mere interruption of consumption and comes to represent loss of accumulated value, regression of position and discontinuity of an expectation already installed.p. 670-671

PTTeasers, pré-vendas, progressões condicionadas, passes, recompensas seriadas e metas temporárias constroem uma trajetória em que o valor deixa de residir apenas no objeto e passa a residir no custo de “ficar para trás”. O efeito jurídico relevante é a conversão da fruição em percurso dirigido, no qual a desistência deixa de significar mera interrupção do consumo e passa a representar perda de valor acumulado, regressão de posição e descontinuidade de expectativa já instalada.

Missing a window, failing a milestone or ignoring a cycle is not equivalent to “losing a chance”, but to falling in position in the public regime of comparison that the ecosystem updates in real time.p. 318

PTPerder uma janela, falhar em um marco ou ignorar um ciclo não equivale a “perder uma chance”, mas a cair de posição no regime público de comparação que o ecossistema atualiza em tempo real.

The supplier emphasizes urgency, benefit, rarity or prestige, but relegates to side conditions what really defines the substance of enjoyment, such as relevant limitations of use, the ordinary mutability of the object, equivalence parameters, possible losses, restricted windows of use, additional requirements for fulfilling the promise or specific conditions for maintaining the advertised value.p. 631

PTO fornecedor enfatiza urgência, benefício, raridade ou prestígio, mas relega a condições laterais aquilo que realmente define a substância da fruição, como limitações relevantes de uso, mutabilidade ordinária do objeto, parâmetros de equivalência, perdas possíveis, janelas restritas de aproveitamento, exigências adicionais para concretização da promessa ou condições específicas para manutenção do valor anunciado.

It is not a matter of investigating the subjective psychological intent of business agents, nor of moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governance.p. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada.

Which screens show the rush after the offer is over?

It is worth keeping the screen with the remaining time, the limited edition notice and the offer as it appeared, with a date. It also helps to note when the same campaign or the same last chance came back, because the repetition shows that the urgency was the store's routine.

For passes and events, the event rules, the time-bound goals, the announcements of what was coming and the changes made midway are useful. With them you can compare the promise at the start with what was delivered at the end.

Where it comes from

The minimum sign of proof, here, does not depend on intuition about manipulation. It appears in remaining-time screens, in the history of repeated temporary campaigns, in the programmed rotation of offers and in the very visual architecture that brings the benefit forward and postpones the relevant condition.p. 670

PTO sinal mínimo de prova, aqui, não depende de intuição sobre manipulação. Ele aparece em telas de tempo restante, em histórico de repetição de campanhas temporárias, em rotação programada de ofertas e na própria arquitetura visual que antecipa benefício e posterga condição relevante.

The minimum evidentiary sign arises in progression structures, event rules, time requirements, changes introduced mid-cycle and the comparison between the initial promise and what is actually delivered along the way.p. 671

PTO sinal mínimo de prova surge em estruturas de progressão, regras de evento, exigências temporais, modificações introduzidas no meio do ciclo e comparação entre a promessa inicial e a entrega efetiva ao longo da trajetória.

What can you ask for when you were rushed into the purchase?

The request can start with information. When the store uses intensity and scarcity to rush the decision, it needs to show the essentials right there, at the time of purchase, prominently, in a way that allows comparison and at the right moment.

If what was sold as a one-time chance did not deliver what it promised, you may also ask for the offer to be fulfilled or corrected and for you to recover what you lost.

When the rush combines with staged rarity, lack of clarity and difficulty in backing out, and this repeats as a pattern, Brazilian consumer law may treat the whole as an abusive practice. Then the request can go beyond your purchase and reach the very design of the store.

Where it comes from

It is not enough for text to exist. The essential content must be presented in a comparable, ranked, conspicuous and timely way. If the promotional architecture of the environment creates intensity and scarcity to hasten the decision, the duty to inform needs to work as a counterweight to this acceleration, and not as a bureaucratic ritual completed in background documents.p. 633

PTNão basta existir texto. É preciso que o conteúdo essencial seja apresentado de forma comparável, hierarquizada, ostensiva e tempestiva. Se a arquitetura promocional do ambiente cria intensidade e escassez para apressar a decisão, o dever de informar precisa funcionar como contrapeso dessa aceleração, e não como ritual burocrático consumado em documentos de fundo.

The possible institutional consequence covers correction of the offer, functional restoration, injunctive relief over the communication and, depending on the case, invalidation of promotional strategies incompatible with the real experience delivered.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional possível abrange correção da oferta, recomposição funcional, tutela inibitória sobre a comunicação e, conforme o caso, invalidação de estratégias promocionais incompatíveis com a experiência real entregue.

When artificial scarcity, manufactured urgency, cycles of promise, manufacture of risk, asymmetric frictions and hindered reversibility operate in a predictable and repeated way to exploit semiotic vulnerability and govern the consumer's choice, the problem no longer lies only in the final quality of the performance, but in the very way the supplier organizes the relationship.p. 676

PTQuando escassez artificial, urgência fabricada, ciclos de promessa, fabricação de risco, fricções assimétricas e reversibilidade dificultada operam de modo previsível e repetido para explorar vulnerabilidade semiótica e governar a escolha do consumidor, o problema já não está apenas na qualidade final da prestação, mas na própria forma como o fornecedor organiza a relação.

The institutional consequence goes beyond redress of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced duties of transparency, design correction and structural or collective measures.p. 747

PTA consequência institucional vai além da reparação de episódios isolados e pode incluir reconfiguração de fluxos, deveres reforçados de transparência, correção de desenho e medidas estruturais ou coletivas.

Who can you turn to when the last chance comes back every week?

The company itself is the first place to go, with the screens you saved, to ask for the missing information or for the offer to be corrected.

If the company does not resolve it, the case can go to Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, routes explained on the channels page. Since manufactured urgency is usually the store's routine, and not something that happened only to you, it lends itself to a collective response, through a class action or through public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure correspondence between promise, announced value, hidden costs and the experience actually made available under managed mutability.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar correspondência entre promessa, valor anunciado, custos ocultados e experiência efetivamente disponibilizada sob mutabilidade administrada.

When the dispute reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or unverifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case. It may take on a structural form: a duty to preserve minimum trails, to establish protocols for qualified communication of relevant changes, to organize intelligible contestation procedures, to maintain minimum functional impact reports or to adjust the supply regime to prevent the problem from recurring. This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not as a single piece.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual. Ela pode assumir feição estrutural: dever de preservar trilhas mínimas, protocolizar comunicação qualificada para alterações relevantes, organizar procedimentos de contestação inteligíveis, manter relatórios mínimos de impacto funcional ou ajustar o regime de fornecimento para impedir repetição do problema. Isso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is every limited-time promotion illegal?

No. A time-limited promotion, a limited edition and a reward are not illegal just for existing, nor when they appear on their own.

The problem may lie in the sum of pieces that, one by one, look harmless. The rush starts to matter when it works together with a lack of clarity, in a stable and repeated way, against good faith, which is the duty of loyalty between the parties.

Where it comes from

Scarcity, urgency, serial promise, threat of loss, functional erosion and symbolic dependence are not unlawful merely because they exist in the abstract or appear in isolation.p. 674

PTEscassez, urgência, promessa seriada, ameaça de perda, erosão funcional e dependência simbólica não são ilícitas por existirem em abstrato nem por aparecerem de modo isolado.

In contexts of hypercuratorial anticipation, unlawfulness may lie precisely in the articulation between elements that, seen separately, seem neutral, but that, combined, govern the consumer's decision in a structurally asymmetric way.p. 632

PTEm contextos de antecipação hipercuratorial, a ilicitude pode residir precisamente na articulação entre elementos que, vistos separadamente, parecem neutros, mas que, combinados, governam a decisão do consumidor de forma estruturalmente assimétrica.

Scarcity, urgency, serialized promise, threat of loss, functional erosion and symbolic dependence are not unlawful for existing in the abstract nor for appearing in isolation. They become teleologically abusive when they operate in combination with opacity and permanent asymmetry, in a stable and replicable pattern, directing enjoyment toward ends incompatible with good faith, balance and controllability of the supply.p. 674

PTEscassez, urgência, promessa seriada, ameaça de perda, erosão funcional e dependência simbólica não são ilícitas por existirem em abstrato nem por aparecerem de modo isolado. Tornam-se teleologicamente abusivas quando operam de forma combinada com opacidade e assimetria permanente, em padrão estável e replicável, orientando a fruição para fins incompatíveis com boa-fé, equilíbrio e controlabilidade do fornecimento.
