And when it goes wrong?

Say your skin lost what it had. Or your account was closed for a reason you cannot make sense of. The first problem you run into is proof.

The system records, the old versions of the screens, the criteria used to punish, the history of changes and the internal support conversations are all on the other side. In conflicts like these, that is the normal situation, not the exception. When the evidence is created and kept under the platform's control, the courts cannot pretend that both sides stand on an equal footing.

Where it comes from

In disputes over IOCs, this is precisely the normal configuration: logs, interface trails, change histories, sanction criteria, internal support communications and relevant technical records are concentrated with the supplier.p. 659

PTEm litígios sobre OICs, essa é precisamente a configuração normal: logs, trilhas de interface, históricos de alteração, critérios de sanção, comunicações internas de suporte e registros técnicos relevantes ficam concentrados no fornecedor.

When the proof arises and remains under the platform's control, the procedural system cannot feign neutrality.p. 659

PTQuando a prova nasce e permanece sob controle da plataforma, o sistema processual não pode fingir neutralidade.

What can you keep as proof yourself?

More is within your reach than it seems. Dated screenshots, screen recordings, the purchase and confirmation screens and the messages about rarity, time remaining, discounts, events or changes all show how the environment was set up. It also helps to save what was promised, such as ads, posts and notices, so you can compare it with what you got later.

That makes a big difference. Instead of saying only that you did not understand, you can show that the environment was designed in a certain way. What counts is not your memory but the shape the platform gave to the path you took.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, time remaining, discount, event or changep. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração

What is relevant there is not the user's subjective memory, but the architecture actually presented. The claim ceases to be “I did not understand” and becomes “the environment was designed this way”.p. 607

PTO relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário, mas a arquitetura efetivamente apresentada. A alegação deixa de ser “eu não entendi” e passa a ser “o ambiente foi desenhado desta forma”.

The advertising and institutional promise is compared with the enjoyment actually delivered and with its later changes.p. 607

PTCompara-se a promessa publicitária e institucional com a fruição efetivamente entregue e com suas alterações posteriores.

And what if all the proof is in the company's hands?

For everything else, the law already has tools. One of them is the reversal of the burden of proof. Normally, whoever alleges a fact has to prove it. In a consumer relationship, the judge can shift that burden in the consumer's favor. And when the service is highly technical and the supplier controls everything, this reversal is often the only way the protection can work.

There are others. The judge can order the platform to preserve the evidence and produce documents. The judge can also divide the work of proving things differently when one side would find it very hard to produce the evidence and the other would find it very easy.

None of this is automatic. Some argue that the reversal should always apply in every digital relationship, but today it depends on the judge's assessment of the case. So you still need to tell a coherent, believable story and make clear what you are disputing. What changes is that you cannot be required to prove in detail something that was never in your hands. And to get started, you do not need the game's entire code. A minimum is enough, such as comparing before and after and showing what was affected.

Where it comes from

The technological specialization of the service and the supplier's dominant position make the reversal of the burden of proof, in many cases, the only instrument capable of making consumer protection effective in practicep. 583

PTA especialização tecnológica do serviço e a posição dominante do fornecedor fazem da inversão do ônus da prova, em muitos casos, o único instrumento capaz de viabilizar efetivamente a tutela do consumidor

The third mechanism is procedural in the strict sense: preservation of evidence, production of documents and dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof.p. 659

PTO terceiro mecanismo é propriamente processual: preservação de evidências, exibição de documentos e redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova.

Art. 373, § 1, allows the burden of proof to be allocated differently when one party would find it excessively difficult to discharge the burden or the other could more easily prove the contrary factp. 659

PTO art. 373, § 1º, permite a distribuição diversa do ônus probatório quando houver excessiva dificuldade para uma parte cumprir o encargo ou maior facilidade da outra em produzir a prova do fato contrário

[...] which is why digital vulnerability and the corresponding hyposufficiency should be affirmed in a universal key, with reversal of the burden of proof ope legis, and not only ope judicisp. 593

PT[...] razão pela qual a vulnerabilidade digital e a hipossuficiência correspondente deveriam ser afirmadas em chave universal, com inversão do ônus da prova ope legis, e não apenas ope judicis

This does not remove the need for plausibility, for a coherent line of argument or for a clear delimitation of what is in dispute.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido.

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to demand from the consumer a detailed showing of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The minimum showing of proof requires a functional comparison of before and after, an examination of the relevant versions, identification of the core utility affected and an assessment of the object's role in the experiencep. 672

PTO sinal mínimo de prova exige comparação funcional entre o antes e o depois, exame de versões relevantes, identificação do núcleo de utilidade afetado e avaliação do papel do objeto na experiência

Who pays when fraud runs through an official game feature?

One case shows how the risk can land in your lap. In an online game, sending gifts to friends was an official feature, offered by the company itself inside the game, with rules and limits. At the same time, outside stores sold the game's currency as something normal, safe and advantageous, and nobody made clear what was allowed, tolerated or forbidden.

When card fraud surfaced, the company switched off the gift feature in Brazil for a long time, removed items people had received and blocked accounts or rolled them back to an earlier state. The measure also hit people who had received gifts in good faith, unaware of any fraud. And those who lost skins were left to prove, item by item, where each one came from, with whatever old statements, emails and screenshots they happened to have kept.

The case helps explain three things. The company already knew about the risk of fraud, so that risk is part of its business and cannot be treated as the fault of whoever received the gift. A revocable license in the contract is not enough to shift the system's failure onto you. And concrete requests follow from this, such as returning what was removed when no individual proof rules out your good faith, keeping and showing the records and correcting the information about where it is safe to buy.

Where it comes from

On the contrary, Riot itself presented it as a regular feature, built into the game client and subject to objective eligibility requirements, quantity limits, regional restrictions and standardized sending flowsp. 610

PTAo contrário, a própria Riot a apresentava como funcionalidade regular, integrada ao client do jogo, submetida a requisitos objetivos de elegibilidade, limites quantitativos, restrições regionais e fluxos padronizados de envio

promotional materials and digital storefronts presented the external purchase of virtual currency as an ordinary, safe, advantageous practice compatible with the game's ecosystemp. 612

PTmateriais promocionais e vitrines digitais apresentavam a compra externa de moeda virtual como prática ordinária, segura, vantajosa e compatível com o ecossistema do jogo

The vulnerability does not stem from private naivety but from the lack of a robust communicative line between what was admitted, tolerated, official or forbidden.p. 612

PTA vulnerabilidade não decorre de ingenuidade privada, mas da ausência de demarcação comunicacional robusta entre o que era admitido, tolerado, oficial ou proibido.

In such an environment, it is not legally serious to presume that the average consumer would always tell apart, with sufficient clarity, a tolerated partner, legitimate resale, the gray market and fraud tied to a cloned cardp. 612

PTNessa ambiência, não é juridicamente sério presumir que o consumidor médio sempre distinguiria, com clareza suficiente, parceiro tolerado, revenda legítima, mercado cinzento e fraude vinculada a cartão clonado

The company disabled the gift feature on the Brazilian server for a long period, removed assets that had been received and carried out rollbacks or blocks on the affected accountsp. 611

PTA empresa desativou por longo período a funcionalidade de presentes no servidor brasileiro, suprimiu ativos recebidos e operou restaurações regressivas ou bloqueios em contas atingidas

[...] it removed assets, shut down an official feature for circulating content and imposed sanctions based on broad presumptions of shared responsibility, including on users who had not knowingly taken part in the fraudp. 610

PT[...] suprimiu ativos, interrompeu funcionalidade oficial de circulação de conteúdo e operou sanções fundadas em presunções amplas de corresponsabilidade, inclusive sobre usuários que não participaram conscientemente da fraude

Once the assets are removed or the account is rolled back, the user must rely on old statements, scattered emails, past screenshots and reconstructed memory to prove that a given item legitimately belonged to their position of enjoymentp. 616

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição

The episode shows how the platform, by broadly rolling back the account, shifts onto the player the almost archaeological burden of reconstructing, item by item, what was already part of their sphere of enjoymentp. 616

PTO episódio mostra como a plataforma, ao operar reversão ampla do estado da conta, transfere ao jogador o encargo quase arqueológico de reconstituir item por item aquilo que já fazia parte de sua esfera de fruição

This was an institutionally recognized operational risk, with internal procedures for investigation, refunds and suspensionp. 610

PTTratava-se de risco operacional reconhecido institucionalmente, com fluxos internos de apuração, reembolso e suspensão

The risk becomes part of the professional sphere of the economic operation itself.p. 611

PTO risco passa a integrar o círculo profissional da própria operação econômica.

The central dogmatic point, however, is that this contractual vocabulary is not enough to make the consumer the legitimate bearer of systemic risk.p. 615

PTO ponto dogmático central, porém, é que esse vocabulário contratual não basta para converter o consumidor em destinatário legítimo do risco sistêmico.

Here the consumer law regime allows requests for functional restoration of the position of enjoyment, restoration of assets when the user's good faith has not been rebutted by robust individualized proof, preservation and disclosure of minimum records of the supply, informational correction about legitimate purchase channelsp. 617

PTO regime consumerista comporta, aqui, pedidos de recomposição funcional da posição de fruição, restauração de ativos quando a boa-fé do usuário não foi infirmada por prova individualizada robusta, preservação e exibição de registros mínimos do fornecimento, correção informacional sobre canais legítimos de aquisição

Functional restorationFunctional restoration

So what do you actually ask for? Before money, you ask for what was taken to be given back. That may mean restoring your access, reviewing or undoing the punishment and giving the skin back the function it had. It may also mean preserving your history, offering a substitute that plays the same role and fixing the procedure that caused the problem.

The reason is simple. When what you lost was the chance to keep using something, compensation on its own is usually a poor remedy. It puts a number where the loss was, but it does not give back the account, the history or the place you had in the game.

Where it comes from

In cases of architectural injury, the legal response must favor functional restoration and procedural correction rather than automatically resorting to monetization.p. 751

PTEm hipóteses de lesão arquitetural, a resposta jurídica deve privilegiar recomposição funcional e correção procedimental, em vez de recorrer automaticamente à monetização.

[...] the integrity of the experience, the permanence of access, the intelligibility of the bond or the consumer's relational position in the ecosystem, a simple monetary award tends to operate as a poor and structurally ineffective remedyp. 751

PT[...] a integridade da experiência, a permanência do acesso, a inteligibilidade do vínculo ou a posição relacional do consumidor no ecossistema, a simples condenação pecuniária tende a operar como remédio pobre e estruturalmente ineficaz

What is required, whenever possible, is restoration of access, reversal or review of sanctions, restoration of functionality, preservation of history, equivalent replacement and adjustment of governance procedures.p. 751

PTO que se exige, sempre que possível, é restauração de acesso, reversão ou revisão de sanções, recomposição de funcionalidade, preservação de histórico, substituição equivalente e ajuste de procedimentos de governança.

Is money off the table?

Money is not off the table. Compensation remains legitimate and often indispensable, whether to cover material losses or to recognize harms that are not only material. It simply stops being the automatic answer, as if every loss of this kind could become a number on a spreadsheet without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Monetary compensation remains, of course, a legitimate and in many cases indispensable remedy. The parameter formulated here does not eliminate it.p. 741

PTA indenização pecuniária permanece, evidentemente, como remédio legítimo e em muitos casos indispensável. O parâmetro aqui formulado não a elimina.

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when presentp. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes

What is rejected is turning it into the automatic, preferred response in every casep. 742

PTO que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese

Regime correctionRegime correction

Sometimes your case is just a symptom. If the same design causes the same loss again and again, fixing your case does not solve anything, because tomorrow the loss happens to someone else, or to you again.

In those cases, the response has to reach the source. That opens the way to orders that prevent repetition, changes to how the service is built, collective actions and mandatory compliance mechanisms tied to the duty to make changes verifiable. Without that, the law only puts a price on the harm and leaves the machine that produces it intact.

Where it comes from

Regime correction, by contrast, makes it possible to treat the harm as architectural and not merely as an isolated episode. This makes it possible to justify injunctive relief, structural measures, collective measures and even mandatory compliance mechanisms compatible with open auditability and institutional control of enjoyment.p. 741

PTA correção de regime, ao contrário, permite tratar o dano como arquitetural e não apenas como episódio. Com isso, torna-se possível justificar tutela inibitória, medidas estruturais, providências coletivas e até mecanismos de compliance compulsório compatíveis com auditabilidade aberta e controle institucional da fruição.

The primacy of functional restoration and regime correction keeps consumer protection from merely pricing harms while leaving intact the machine that produces them.p. 742

PTA primazia da recomposição funcional e da correção de regime impede que a tutela consumerista se limite a precificar danos enquanto preserva intacta a máquina que os produz.

Architecture harmArchitecture harm

Sometimes many people lose in the same way because the harm has a single source. These are not separate harms that happen to look alike. It is one harm that springs from the very design of the service, and that is why it has a unity that justifies dealing with it collectively rather than person by person.

Where it comes from

In platform environments, the harmful source may lie in the very design of the supply regime, so that the unity of the problem does not stem primarily from the empirical repetition of episodesp. 774

PTEm ambientes de plataforma, a fonte lesiva pode residir no próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, de modo que a unidade do problema não decorre primariamente da repetição empírica de episódios

It refers to an injury produced by the very design of the supply regime, and therefore endowed with sufficient functional unity to justify collective institutional treatment.p. 774

PTRefere-se a lesão produzida pelo próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, e por isso dotada de unidade funcional suficiente para justificar tratamento institucional coletivo.

LawConsumer by equivalence

And what about someone who bought nothing but depends on the same environment? Think of the small seller who can only sell through a large platform's store. This seller is not a consumer in the ordinary sense, yet is exposed, from a fragile position, to the platform's commercial practices, supply methods and governance.

The law allows this person to be protected as if they were a consumer. But not every participant qualifies for this protection. Only someone who is genuinely exposed, in a situation of vulnerability that matters to the law.

Where it comes from

Once art. 29 of the CDC1 is accepted as applicable to qualify legal entities in a factual position of vulnerability, the regime proves fully pertinent when the immaterial object in question is the enjoyment bundle in a sustained position, exploited by the holder as a product in the B2B market and offered, through the marketplace platform, to the seller (vendedor)p. 434

PTAdmitida a aplicabilidade do art. 29 do CDC para qualificar pessoas jurídicas em posição factual de vulnerabilidade, o regime se mostra plenamente pertinente quando o objeto imaterial em questão é o feixe de fruição em posição sustentada explorado como produto no mercado B2B pela detentora e oferecido, por via da plataforma marketplace, ao seller (vendedor)

[...] although it does not fit comfortably into the traditional figure of the final purchaser as imagined in simple, linear relationships, it is exposed to the commercial practices, supply methods and private governance mechanisms of the environment in a condition of legally relevant vulnerabilityp. 779

PT[...] embora não se encaixe com conforto na figura tradicional do adquirente final tal como imaginada em relações lineares e simples, encontra-se exposto às práticas comerciais, aos métodos de fornecimento e aos mecanismos de governança privada do ambiente em condição de vulnerabilidade juridicamente relevante

Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence.p. 780

PTNem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado.

LawCompetition

The same design that holds you in can keep competitors out. When the way your experience is built also gives the company an edge over others in the market, the problem is no longer only the consumer's. It also becomes a competition problem, affecting whoever could offer an alternative.

Where it comes from

Seen this way, the IOC regime does not replace competition law. It provides an entry grammar for understanding when the architecture of the consumer's experience has already become, at the same time, the architecture of competitive advantage.p. 771

PTNessa chave, o regime do OIC não substitui o direito concorrencial. Ele fornece gramática de entrada para compreender quando a arquitetura da experiência do consumidor já se tornou, simultaneamente, arquitetura da vantagem competitiva.

None of this becomes an automatic formula. The path only puts the questions in order. What form of platform governance is at stake? What duty was breached? What minimum proof can be required? How does the case qualify in law? And what response makes sense for it?

The rest is judgment, case by case, by whoever decides. What these names do is make sure the right questions get asked.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

It sets out, in language that can be consulted, the path from governance structure to breached duty, minimum evidentiary requirement, legal qualification and possible institutional consequence.p. 748

PTEle organiza, em linguagem consultável, a passagem entre estrutura de governança, dever violado, exigência probatória mínima, qualificação jurídica e consequência institucional possível.

What can you demand?

The evidence held by the platform has to come outThe evidence held by the platform has to come out

When does the platform hold all the proof?

When almost everything that shows what happened stays with the platform. The system logs, your account history, the old versions of the screens and the reasons behind each decision are on its side, not yours.

What must the platform do with the records?

Keep and show the minimum records needed to reconstruct what happened. Being open to verification is part of supplying legitimately.

What can you show and what does the platform hold?

On your side, give a plausible account of what you went through, show how it fits into the game and gather what was within your reach. On the platform's side, present the records, histories, versions and technical justifications that matter for the case.

The more what happened depends on what only the platform records, the less reasonable it is to demand from you detailed proof of something that was never in your hands.

How should proof be shared according to access to the records?

The burden of proof should be shared according to who has access to it. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions in your favor, which make certain facts count as true until proven otherwise, and a heightened duty for the platform to cooperate with the proof.

Does this mean you no longer have to explain what happened?

You still have to tell a coherent, believable story and be clear about what you are asking for. What falls away is the fiction that you and the platform are on equal footing when it comes to proof. Your lack of access to the records cannot become a wall against you.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform holds the elements essential to narrating, demonstrating and reconstructing the conflict, the rules of evidence must recognize that verifiability is part of the very legitimate regime of supply.p. 747

PTSempre que a plataforma concentrar os elementos essenciais para narrar, demonstrar e reconstruir o conflito, a disciplina probatória deverá reconhecer que a verificabilidade integra o próprio regime legítimo do fornecimento.

The corresponding duty is to preserve and disclose the minimum records needed to reconstruct the disputed event.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de preservar e exibir registros mínimos necessários à reconstrução do evento controvertido.

The minimum required proof, from the consumer's side, consists of a plausible account of the harmful experience, of how it fits into the ecosystem and of the accessible contextual elementsp. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível, do ponto de vista do consumidor, consiste em apresentação plausível da experiência lesiva, de sua inserção no ecossistema e dos elementos de contexto acessíveis

from the supplier's side, it requires disclosure of trails, logs, histories, versions and technically delimited justificationsp. 747

PTdo ponto de vista do fornecedor, exige-se exibição de trilhas, logs, históricos, versões e justificativas tecnicamente delimitadas

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to demand from the consumer a detailed showing of facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The possible legal qualification involves dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof, presumptions in the consumer's favor and a reinforced duty of cooperationp. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível envolve redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova, presunções favoráveis ao consumidor e reforço do dever de cooperação

This does not remove the need for plausibility, for a coherent line of argument or for a clear delimitation of what is in dispute. What it removes is the fiction of symmetry in environments whose very structure runs on asymmetry of meaning and of proof.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido. O que elimina é a ficção de simetria em ambientes cuja própria estrutura opera pela assimetria de sentido e de prova.

The institutional consequence is to prevent technical opacity from becoming a procedural shieldp. 747

PTA consequência institucional consiste em impedir que a opacidade técnica se converta em blindagem processual

The answer should restore what you lostThe answer should restore what you lost

When does the loss call for more than money?

When the problem affects your access, the continuity of what you use or the equivalence of what you bought. Or when it affects your history, your standing among other players or what the item meant to you.

What response must the platform give?

A response that fits the kind of loss. If what you lost was a function, the response should target that function, not just a sum of money at the end.

How do you link the loss to what the platform did?

The link between the platform's decision or mechanism and the loss you suffered. And the fact that it is technically or institutionally possible to restore, undo, replace with something equivalent or correct the procedure.

What solution can restore what was lost?

The priority is to recover what was lost, when that is feasible. The solution has to match the loss you showed and what can realistically be restored. That may mean restoring access, undoing a penalty, preserving your history, replacing the item with something that serves the same function, adjusting the rules and stopping the problem from continuing. When the harm reveals a recurring pattern, you can also ask for the structure that produces it to be corrected.

What about financial compensation?

Money stays on the table. Compensation remains possible, including for harms that are not only material, when restoring is not feasible or not enough. What is rejected is treating it as the automatic answer to everything, as if every loss of this kind turned into a number without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving pecuniary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

The corresponding duty is to shape protection according to the nature of the loss, aiming at the affected enjoyment and not only at its residual translation into money.p. 748

PTO dever correspondente é o de estruturar a tutela em conformidade com a natureza da perda, mirando a fruição afetada e não apenas sua tradução monetária residual.

The minimum required proof involves demonstrating the link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The possible legal qualification varies with the main ground at play in the case, but the institutional consequence must favor restoration of access, reversal of sanctions, preservation of history, equivalent functional substitution, governance adjustments, injunctive relief and structural correction when the harm reveals a repeatable patternp. 748

PTA qualificação jurídica possível varia conforme a incidência principal do caso, mas a consequência institucional deve privilegiar restauração de acesso, reversão de sanções, preservação de histórico, substituição funcional equivalente, ajustes de governança, tutela inibitória e correção estrutural quando o dano revelar padrão repetível

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when present. What is rejected is turning it into the automatic, preferred response in every case, as if the injury to the IOC could always be adequately translated into a figure without regard for the structure of the enjoyment compromised.p. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes. O que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese, como se a lesão ao OIC pudesse ser sempre adequadamente traduzida em cifra sem consideração pela estrutura da fruição comprometida.
