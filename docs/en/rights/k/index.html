Your rights

The answer should restore what you lostThe answer should restore what you lost

When does the loss call for more than money?

When the problem affects your access, the continuity of what you use or the equivalence of what you bought. Or when it affects your history, your standing among other players or what the item meant to you.

What response must the platform give?

The platform must give an answer that matches the kind of loss. If what you lost was a function, the answer should aim at that function, and not only at an amount of money left over at the end.

How do you link the loss to what the platform did?

It helps to show the link between the platform's decision or mechanism and the loss you suffered. It also helps to show that it is technically or institutionally possible to restore, undo, swap for something equivalent or correct the procedure.

What solution can restore what was lost?

The priority is to recover what was lost, when that is feasible. The solution has to match the loss you showed and what can realistically be restored. That may mean restoring access, undoing a penalty, preserving your history, replacing the item with something that serves the same function, adjusting the rules and stopping the problem from continuing. When the harm reveals a recurring pattern, you can also ask for the structure that produces it to be corrected.

What about financial compensation?

Money stays on the table. Compensation remains possible, including for harms that are not only material, when restoring is not feasible or not enough. What is rejected is treating it as the automatic answer to everything, as if every loss of this kind turned into a number without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving pecuniary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

The corresponding duty is to shape protection according to the nature of the loss, aiming at the affected enjoyment and not only at its residual translation into money.p. 748

PTO dever correspondente é o de estruturar a tutela em conformidade com a natureza da perda, mirando a fruição afetada e não apenas sua tradução monetária residual.

The minimum required proof involves demonstrating the link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The possible legal qualification varies with the main ground at play in the case, but the institutional consequence must favor restoration of access, reversal of sanctions, preservation of history, equivalent functional substitution, governance adjustments, injunctive relief and structural correction when the harm reveals a repeatable patternp. 748

PTA qualificação jurídica possível varia conforme a incidência principal do caso, mas a consequência institucional deve privilegiar restauração de acesso, reversão de sanções, preservação de histórico, substituição funcional equivalente, ajustes de governança, tutela inibitória e correção estrutural quando o dano revelar padrão repetível

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when present. What is rejected is turning it into the automatic, preferred response in every case, as if the injury to the IOC could always be adequately translated into a figure without regard for the structure of the enjoyment compromised.p. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes. O que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese, como se a lesão ao OIC pudesse ser sempre adequadamente traduzida em cifra sem consideração pela estrutura da fruição comprometida.
