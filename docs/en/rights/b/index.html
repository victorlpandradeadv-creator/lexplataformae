Your rights

The platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understandingThe platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understanding

When do the signs on the screen weigh against you?

Almost everything you know about your skin passes through screens, notices, icons and paths that the platform designs. It is through them that you find out how much it is worth, what risk you run when buying and how to complain if something goes wrong. When your expectation and your chance to contest depend on these signs controlled by the company, you are in a fragile position. And the law takes this fragility into account.

This fragility has nothing to do with naivety, lack of attention or little familiarity with technology. It arises from the way the environment is set up. Whoever designs the screen decides what is highlighted, what is hidden and in what order things appear. And they usually also keep the records that would allow the story to be told later.

How should the platform set up the experience?

The platform cannot build the experience in a way that only it understands. The design of the environment has to respect good faith, which is the duty of loyalty between the parties, transparency and your real ability to understand what you are using.

How do you show the path that led to your decision?

It helps to show the path you took inside the game up to the purchase or the problem, what was highlighted on the screen and where the information went. The records that make it possible to reconstruct how your expectation was formed also matter. Dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase and confirmation screens help, because they show how the environment was set up, and not only what you remember.

What can you ask for when the environment is to blame?

You can ask for the problem to be treated as an issue of the environment, and not as carelessness on your part. If this dependence on the platform's signals is shown, reinforced duties to inform, to advertise clearly and to present evidence may apply. Depending on the case, the practice can be seen as abusive, as insufficient information or as deception by ambiguity or omission. Combined with other elements, this can even change who has to prove what.

Does every complicated game violate rights?

This does not mean that every complicated game violates rights, nor that you are incapable in the face of the digital. The fragility that counts is the one that appears when the signs controlled by the platform steer your expectation or hinder your reaction.

Whenever expectation, perception of value, risk assessment or capacity to contest depend on signs, interfaces, flows and informational trails controlled by the supplier, there will be legally relevant semiotic vulnerability.p. 744

The term does not designate naivety, credulity or cultural deficiency of the consumer.p. 719

PTO termo não designa ingenuidade, credulidade ou deficiência cultural do consumidor.

Whoever controls the form by which the object is made intelligible often also controls the records by which this intelligibility can be reconstructed in the event of a conflict.p. 720

PTQuem controla a forma pela qual o objeto é tornado inteligível frequentemente controla também os registros pelos quais essa inteligibilidade pode ser reconstruída em caso de conflito.

The corresponding duty is not to organize the experience through a structural asymmetry of meaning incompatible with good faith, transparency and legibility of enjoyment.p. 744

PTO dever correspondente é o de não organizar a experiência por meio de assimetria estrutural de sentido incompatível com boa-fé, transparência e legibilidade da fruição.

The minimum required proof involves reconstructing the user's journey, the informational architecture of the environment, the communicational salience, the paths of understanding and the records available to reconstitute the formation of the expectation.p. 744

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve reconstrução da jornada do usuário, da arquitetura informacional do ambiente, das saliências comunicacionais, dos caminhos de compreensão e dos registros disponíveis para reconstituir a formação da expectativa.

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

What is relevant there is not the user's subjective memoryp. 607

PTO relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário

The possible legal qualification may support abusive practice, informational insufficiency, deceptiveness by ambiguity or relevant omission and, when articulated with other elements, grounds for dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof.p. 744

PTA qualificação jurídica possível pode sustentar prática abusiva, insuficiência informacional, enganosidade por ambiguidade ou omissão relevante e, quando articulada a outros elementos, fundamento para redistribuição dinâmica do ônus probatório.

The institutional consequence consists in bringing the conflict back to the governance of the environment, avoiding moralization of the consumer's conduct and allowing reinforced duties of information, advertising and proof to apply.p. 744

PTA consequência institucional consiste em reconduzir o conflito à governança do ambiente, evitando moralização da conduta do consumidor e permitindo incidência de deveres reforçados de informação, publicidade e prova.

PTTampouco se trata de pressupor incapacidade absoluta diante do digital.
