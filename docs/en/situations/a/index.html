Situation a) · 7.1.2 of the thesis

When what was bought depends on the environment the platform administers

a) regime of governed enjoyment and application of the IOC

Which facts indicate a product that depends on the environment?

An item, account, subscription or access whose utility can be realized only inside the platform's environment, with loss of access, permanence or intelligibility, or with a change in the experience through an update. The supplier's typical defense is that it provides only a service or that it granted a mere content license. In games, the object may be one of appearance, progression, access and permanence, functionality, time or reputation, and a single item often combines more than one of these types. This typology is internal to the games laboratory, and not a general classification of platforms. The same traits appear in three business models, games operated as a service, marketplaces and artificial intelligence services supplied by platforms. In all of them, enjoyment depends on the continuous operation of a privately governed environment. In the marketplace, however, not every digital mediation turns the good into an immaterial object of consumption.

How do you demonstrate dependence on the environment?

The structural dependence of the utility on the governed environment, the way the experience is made available and the consumer's concrete position in the relationship. Helpful evidence includes the terms of use that tie the content to the account, the status page that shows dependence on servers and the record of what is in the account and what was paid.

What legal provision underpins the immaterial product?

Article 3, paragraph 1, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC)1 already admits immaterial products. Whenever the utility consumed presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there is an immaterial product within the meaning of that provision, and the service dimension does not rule out control over enjoyment. Terms of use that recognize virtual content associated with the account, such as currency, skins and other items, help prove that the object exists, but they do not exhaust its regime. The IOC does not replace the immaterial product of Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1. It gives that product concrete form in continuous and governed consumption. The legal definition of product works as a scope clause, which keeps protection from being evaded when the object has no physical medium.

What stays outside the entry gate?

The four fields of the entry gate are cumulative. Left out are one-off access, the platform that works only as a channel or storefront, the predominantly aesthetic alteration when the symbolic element is accessory and the ordinary variations of the service. There are also conflicts the CDC already resolves with nothing gained by invoking the IOC, such as undue charges, an isolated defect or a specific breach of an offer, when that is the core of the case. Qualification opens the following filters and does not decide the case on its own. Arguing over whether the case concerns a product or a service does not settle the question. The distinction remains useful for calibrating specific consequences, but it does not define the entire regime. The Legal Framework for Electronic Games, Law 14,852/2024, recognizes the sector as an economic and cultural activity, but the sectoral framing helps one see the market and is not enough to control governance, so it does not replace the CDC. The interpretive rule is that initial delivery does not exhaust performance, and continued performance does not erase the object.

When it applies
When the utility consumed depends structurally on an environment that private governance administers, modulates and can change, with an impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating.
Whenever the utility consumed depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and changeable by private governance, with an impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating of the experience, the IOC's field of application will be established.
Duty of supply
To ensure accessible, intelligible and preserved enjoyment, within the limits of the legitimate mutability of the environment.
The corresponding duty of supply is to ensure functionally accessible, intelligible and preserved enjoyment within the limits of the legitimate mutability of the environment.
Minimum evidence
The dependence of the utility on the ecosystem, the way the experience is made available and the consumer's concrete position in the relationship.
The minimum evidence required falls on demonstrating the structural dependence of the utility on the governed ecosystem, the way the experience is made available and the consumer's concrete position in the relationship.
Legal qualification
Immaterial product under Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1. The service dimension of the relationship does not rule out control over enjoyment.
The resulting legal qualification is that of an immaterial product subject to the regime of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1, without the service dimension of the relationship ruling out control over enjoyment as the core of supply.
Institutional consequence
The following filters open, and the claim that there is mere service provision or a simple license no longer neutralizes the controversy.
The possible institutional consequence consists in opening the subsequent filters of the regime, preventing the controversy from being neutralized by the claim that there is a mere provision of services or simply a formal license.
