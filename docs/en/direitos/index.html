Your rights

When what you buy only exists inside a platform, the sale does not end at the click. The company keeps maintaining, changing and controlling what you use, and that is why, under Brazilian law, it carries duties that stay with the purchase for as long as it lasts. The relationship is no longer centered on the contract you accepted one day but on the use you make, every day, of what you bought.

Below are the main duties, one for each situation. For each one you will find when it arises, what the platform owes, what helps show the problem, what you can ask for and how far that goes.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

What you bought is a product, even with nothing in your handsWhat you bought is a product, even with nothing in your hands

When does what you bought depend on the platform?

When what you use depends, structurally, on an environment the platform runs, adjusts and can change. Think of the skin that only works inside the game, or the account that holds your inventory and your progress. What matters here is whether this dependence affects your access, whether you get to stay, how much you can understand of what is happening or the updates to the experience.

What must the platform preserve?

It must keep what you bought accessible, understandable and preserved in whatever makes it useful. The platform can change the environment, because change is part of this kind of supply, but only within the limits of what is legitimate.

How can you show what you bought and how you use it?

Show that what you use depends on the platform's environment, and how the platform delivers this experience. It also helps to show your concrete position in the relationship, such as what is in your account, what you paid and what that lets you do.

What if the company says it is just a service or a license?

You can ask for the case to be handled under consumer law, with what you bought treated as an immaterial product. The company cannot get out of this just by saying it provides a service or granted a simple license, because the service is precisely how the product is kept working.

Does recognizing the product already settle the case?

This is the first step, not the end of the case. Recognizing the product opens the door to the other duties on this page, but each of them still depends on what happened to you. You do not need to fit every problem into this category to be protected.

The platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understandingThe platform cannot design the experience to keep you from understanding

When do the signs on the screen weigh against you?

Almost everything you know about your skin passes through screens, notices, icons and paths that the platform designs. It is through them that you find out how much it is worth, what risk you run when buying and how to complain if something goes wrong. When your expectation and your chance to contest depend on these signs controlled by the company, you are in a fragile position. And the law takes this fragility into account.

This fragility has nothing to do with naivety, lack of attention or little familiarity with technology. It arises from the way the environment is set up. Whoever designs the screen decides what is highlighted, what is hidden and in what order things appear. And they usually also keep the records that would allow the story to be told later.

How should the platform set up the experience?

By not setting up the experience in a way that only the platform understands. The design of the environment needs to respect good faith, which is the duty of loyalty between the parties, as well as transparency and a real chance for you to understand what you are using.

How do you show the path that led to your decision?

Through the path you took inside the game up to the purchase or the problem, what was highlighted on the screen and where the information appeared. The records that make it possible to reconstruct how your expectation was formed also matter. Dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase and confirmation screens help, because they show how the environment was set up, and not only what you remember.

What can you ask for when the environment is to blame?

That the problem be treated as a matter of the environment, and not as carelessness on your part. If this dependence on the platform's signs is demonstrated, stronger duties to inform, advertise clearly and present proof may apply. Depending on the case, the practice may be seen as abusive, as insufficient information or as deception by ambiguity or by omission. Added to other elements, this may even change who needs to prove what.

Does every complicated game violate rights?

This does not mean that every complicated game violates rights, nor that you are incapable in the face of the digital. The fragility that counts is the one that appears when the signs controlled by the platform steer your expectation or hinder your reaction.

Information has to be of real useInformation has to be of real use

What if the information exists but gives you no guidance?

When the platform trades clarity for walls of text, scatters information across different places, breaks the trail for anyone trying to understand, or offers transparency that is only for show. Think of important rules buried in a huge contract, in FAQs nobody can find or in patch notes nobody can link to what happened to your item.

What information must the platform give?

To give information that is genuinely useful, that helps you decide and that can be checked later if there is a dispute. Information that exists somewhere but guides no one does not meet this duty.

How do you show what you were told and what was left out?

What was shown to you, at what moment, with what prominence and through which channel. And whether, the way everything was organized, it was possible to understand what mattered in order to buy, to stay or to react.

What can you ask for when the information fell short?

That the platform correct the way it informs and make visible what matters. That it present what it actually displayed. And that it restore what you lost because of the lack of clarity. Depending on the case, the failure may be treated as a defect in the quality of the information or as an abusive practice. It may even lead to the invalidity of clauses that contradict what you had reason to expect.

Is the real question whether you read the contract?

The discussion is not about whether you read the contract or not. It is about whether, the way the information was organized, it served to guide your decision.

Where it comes from

The minimum required evidence requires identifying what was shown, at what moment, with what salience, through which channels and to what extent this organization allowed the consumer to understand what was legally relevant to join, remain or react.p. 744

What was promised has to be deliveredWhat was promised has to be delivered

When does advertising become part of the offer?

When the purchase comes after careful preparation. It may be a countdown, a promise of exclusivity, a warning that it will run out, a status signal or the suggestion that one item is worth as much as another. In these cases, advertising stops being just advertising and becomes part of the offer itself.

What does the announcement oblige the platform to deliver?

It must make what was announced match what is delivered. The promise, the announced value and the costs that were not shown have to match the experience you receive, including after the platform changes the game.

How do you compare the promise with what was delivered?

What the sales environment was like, which ads were running, what was featured, how the item was presented and what happened afterwards, compared with what was promised.

What do you ask for when the promise is not kept?

That the offer be corrected and fulfilled, that what you lost be restored and that the misleading communication stop. In some cases, you can ask for sales strategies incompatible with what is really delivered to be struck down. Depending on the case, the law may treat this as misleading or abusive advertising, or as an offer that binds whoever made it.

Is pushy advertising a problem in itself?

What counts is not whether the advertising is lively or pushy. It is the relationship between what was presented before and what you were able to use afterwards.

Where it comes from

PTSempre que a fruição for precedida por ciclos curados de antecipação, promessa, escassez sugerida, distinção simbólica ou equivalências insinuadas, a publicidade integrará materialmente a oferta.

The corresponding duty of supply is to ensure correspondence between promise, announced value, concealed costs and the experience actually made available under administered mutability.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar correspondência entre promessa, valor anunciado, custos ocultados e experiência efetivamente disponibilizada sob mutabilidade administrada.

The minimum required evidence involves reconstructing the promotional environment, the communication pieces, the symbolic saliences, the context of presentation of the object and the relation between prior promise and subsequent enjoyment.p. 745

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve reconstrução do ambiente promocional, das peças de comunicação, das saliências simbólicas, do contexto de apresentação do objeto e da relação entre promessa antecedente e fruição posterior.

The legal qualification may consist of misleading or abusive advertising, a binding offer, abusive practice through administered ambiguityp. 745

PTA qualificação jurídica poderá recair em publicidade enganosa ou abusiva, oferta vinculante, prática abusiva por ambiguidade administrada

The possible institutional consequence encompasses correction of the offer, functional restoration, injunctive protection over the communication and, depending on the case, invalidation of promotional strategies incompatible with the real experience delivered.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional possível abrange correção da oferta, recomposição funcional, tutela inibitória sobre a comunicação e, conforme o caso, invalidação de estratégias promocionais incompatíveis com a experiência real entregue.

A relevant change has to leave a trailA relevant change has to leave a trail

When does a change need to leave a trail?

When the platform changes something important about your access, the item's performance, its usefulness or the rules of the game, or when it hands out a punishment, and does so without keeping the minimum record needed to reconstruct what happened. Think of an item that loses strength in an update with no explanation you can check, or an account suspended without anyone knowing on what basis.

What trail must the platform keep?

A minimum of verifiability, which means making it possible to know what was changed, when, on what justification and with what effect on what you use.

This duty is not a new law with a name of its own. It comes from the duty to inform, from good faith and from making it easier for consumers to defend their rights, which the law already provides for. It does not require opening up all of the game's code or making the company's secrets public. The examination should be limited to what matters for the case, while keeping the necessary confidentiality.

Which records make it possible to understand the change or the punishment?

The essential system records, the history of the versions that matter, the records of the punishment and minimal justifications. Together with the context data, this should be enough for whoever decides the case to understand the problem. Almost all of it is usually in the platform's hands, not yours.

What can you ask for to reconstruct the change?

You can ask that these records be preserved and shown, and that an expert examination, meaning a review by a specialist, be carried out on specific points. You can also ask for a check, based on the minimum clarity the platform owes, of whether the change or the punishment was legitimate. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions against the platform, which make certain facts count in your favor when the records only it had are missing.

Does every small adjustment require a record?

Not every small adjustment, purely cosmetic change or minor fluctuation calls for this trail. The duty begins when the change significantly affects what you use, your ability to stay or your chance to prove and contest what happened.

If it changes, it must keep the value it hadIf it changes, it must keep the value it had

When does an update take away the function of what you bought?

When the platform changes the experience in a way that genuinely worsens, hollows out or diverts the function that led you to buy. Think of an item that, after an adjustment, is still in your inventory but has become so weak it no longer has a reason to exist, or a feature that let you do something and was taken away.

You can still be in the game, with your account, seeing the item and logging in whenever you like, and still have lost what mattered most. Keeping something working is not the same as keeping its value.

What must the platform preserve in the item?

In substance, the role the item had in your experience. That includes what it does, the relationship it creates with other players and, when this matters legally, what it means.

How can you show what changed in the item's usefulness?

Show what usefulness the item had before, what changed, what concrete impact the change had and why the compensation offered, if any, is not enough.

What solution can restore the lost usefulness?

First, getting the function back. That may mean undoing the change when it is technically possible, replacing the item with something equivalent or correcting the way the update was carried out. Money should not be the automatic answer. Depending on the case, the loss may be treated as a quality defect in the product or service, which is the law's name for a problem that makes something less useful than it should be.

Does this mean the game can never change?

The platform can update the game, and nobody demands that the item stay identical forever or that the game stand still. Many changes really are the natural evolution of the environment. The problem is a change that seriously worsens what you bought and leaves you to bear the cost, with no restoration to match.

The possible legal qualification tends to be a quality defect of the product or servicep. 746

PTA qualificação jurídica possível tende a recair em vício de qualidade do produto ou serviço

The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent replacement and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional possível prioriza recomposição funcional, reversão técnica quando viável, substituição equivalente e correção da forma de atualização, em vez de mera monetização automática da perda.

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.

What the parameter refuses is not change itself, but its instrumentalization to transfer to the consumer the cost of reconfigurations that destroy or significantly downgrade the acquired utility without compatible restoration.p. 730

PTO que o parâmetro recusa não é a mudança em si, mas sua instrumentalização para transferir ao consumidor o custo de reconfigurações que destroem ou rebaixam significativamente a utilidade adquirida sem recomposição compatível.

Nerfs, removals, reworks, migrations, rule changes, reordering of access, reconfiguration of interfaces and removal of features are usually presented as the natural evolution of the environment. In many cases, in fact, they may be just that.p. 730

PTNerfs, remoções, reworks, migrações, mudanças de regra, reordenação de acessos, reconfiguração de interfaces e supressão de funcionalidades costumam ser narrados como evolução natural do ambiente. Em muitos casos, de fato, podem sê-lo.

A penalty needs a reason and a chance to defend yourselfA penalty needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself

When does a penalty go too far?

When your access, your permanence, your visibility or your chance to complain depend on penalties that nobody explains. Or on hurdles placed in front of only some people, on a cost too high to push back or on a way back that is much harder than the way in. It is the account blocked or banned without explanation, with an appeal route that leads nowhere. When the account is closed, access to everything in it goes too, including what you bought.

What must the platform do before penalizing?

Follow procedures that can be understood, give a reason that can be checked, let you contest in a reasonable way and not treat you arbitrarily.

How do you document the penalty and the attempt to contest it?

The records of the penalty, the appeal route that existed, the criteria used, the hurdles to undoing the measure and what it did to the continuity of your experience.

What review can be requested for the penalty?

If the penalty has no verifiable reason or no reasonable appeal route, it may be possible to have it reviewed or reversed. You can also ask the platform to fulfill its procedural duties and to create or improve a real appeal route. When the problem happens to many people, you can also ask for the penalty system as a whole to be corrected.

So the platform cannot penalize at all?

The platform has the power to penalize, but having that power is not enough. A penalty needs a reason that can be checked, consistency from one decision to the next and a clear way to challenge it.

Where it comes from

Whenever access, permanence, visibility or challenge are conditioned by opaque sanctions, selective frictions, excessive costs of reaction or asymmetric reversal mechanisms, there will be an indication of abusive governance of the bond.p. 746

PTSempre que acesso, permanência, visibilidade ou contestação forem condicionados por sanções opacas, fricções seletivas, custos de reação excessivos ou mecanismos assimétricos de reversão, haverá indício de governança abusiva do vínculo.

The corresponding duty of supply is to ensure minimally intelligible procedures, verifiable reasoning, a reasonable possibility of challenge and non-arbitrary treatment of the user in the administration of enjoyment.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar procedimentos minimamente inteligíveis, motivação verificável, possibilidade razoável de contestação e tratamento não arbitrário do usuário na administração da fruição.

The minimum required evidence concerns sanction records, the challenge flow, the criteria mobilized, structural difficulties of reversal and the concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience.p. 746

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre registros de sanção, fluxo de contestação, critérios mobilizados, dificuldades estruturais de reversão e impactos concretos da medida sobre a continuidade da experiência.

The institutional consequence includes reversal or review of sanctions, procedural duties, creation or improvement of challenge mechanisms and, in structurally repetitive cases, correction of the ecosystem's enforcement regime.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional inclui reversão ou revisão de sanções, deveres procedimentais, criação ou aperfeiçoamento de mecanismos de contestação e, em casos estruturalmente repetitivos, correção do regime de enforcement do ecossistema.

For this reason, they must be controlled by criteria denser than the mere formal existence of sanctioning power. They require verifiable reasoning, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of challenge.p. 749

PTPor isso, devem ser controladas por critérios mais densos do que a simples existência formal de poder sancionatório. Exigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

Added to this is a reward architecture, with progression and grind systems, and a sanctioning architecture, with bans, restrictions, automated penalties and opaque policiesp. 211

PTSoma-se uma arquitetura de recompensa, com sistemas de progressão e grind, e uma arquitetura sancionatória, com banimentos, restrições, penalidades automatizadas e políticas opacas

[...] including the so-called Virtual Content, takes place under a limited and revocable license, conditioned on compliance with the Terms, and that closing the account entails automatic cancellation of the license and loss of access to the associated contentp. 408

PT[...] inclusive ao chamado Conteúdo Virtual, ocorre por licença limitada e revogável, condicionada ao cumprimento dos Termos, e que o encerramento da conta acarreta cancelamento automático da licença e perda do acesso aos conteúdos associados

When loss is the rule, the problem is the designWhen loss is the rule, the problem is the design

When does loss become part of the game's routine?

When several small things work together to take advantage of your weaknesses or to make your experience worse as a matter of routine. They are small frictions that wear you down, losses everyone already treats as normal and manufactured urgency. They are also artificial scarcity, a lack of clarity that works in the company's favor and equivalences that shift whenever it suits the company.

You do not need to prove that someone wanted to harm you. What matters is the purpose that shows in the very functioning of the environment. It can be seen in the pattern that repeats, the coherence among the pieces and the effect produced again and again.

What must the game's design not aim at?

It must not build the experience around an exploitative purpose that goes against good faith, transparency and balance, or that disregards what you legitimately acquired.

How do you show that the problem forms a pattern?

By showing the pattern, the fact that it repeats and the way the pieces of the environment combine. And the relevant impact this has on your freedom of choice in practice, on your understanding or on the utility of what you use.

Can you ask for more than fixing one case?

Yes, more than fixing one episode. You can ask for the game's paths to be redone, for transparency to increase and for the design to be corrected. When this design produces a pattern of injury that repeats, the response can also reach the structure that reproduces it.

Is every way of selling and persuading abusive?

Not every way of selling, persuading or running the game is abusive. What is controlled is a program that can be recognized in the functioning of the environment, and not someone's hidden will.

Where it comes from

Whenever microfrictions, normalized losses, manufactured urgencies, artificial scarcities, strategic opacity, elastic equivalences and other mechanisms operate in a coordinated way to extract value from vulnerability or to degrade enjoyment as a routine of the environment, there will be a case of abusive teleology.p. 746

PTSempre que microfricções, perdas normalizadas, urgências fabricadas, escassezes artificiais, opacidade estratégica, equivalências elásticas e outros mecanismos operarem de modo coordenado para extrair valor da vulnerabilidade ou degradar a fruição como rotina do ambiente, haverá hipótese de teleologia abusiva.

It is not a matter of investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor of moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governance. The focus is on the functional purpose objectively inscribed in the arrangement, as revealed by its replicability, by its internal coherence and by its repeated impact on the consumer's experience.p. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada. O foco está na finalidade funcional objetivamente inscrita no arranjo, tal como se revela por sua replicabilidade, por sua coerência interna e por seu impacto reiterado sobre a experiência do consumidor.

The corresponding duty of supply is the duty not to structure the experience around an exploitative purpose incompatible with good faith, transparency, balance and the protection of the enjoyment legitimately incorporated into the relationship.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de não estruturar a experiência a partir de finalidade exploratória incompatível com boa-fé, transparência, equilíbrio e tutela da fruição legitimamente incorporada ao vínculo.

The minimum required evidence consists in demonstrating the pattern, its replicability, the coordination among mechanisms of the ecosystem and the relevant impact produced on practical autonomy, intelligibility or utility of the experience.p. 746-747

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste na demonstração do padrão, de sua replicabilidade, da coordenação entre mecanismos do ecossistema e do impacto relevante produzido sobre autonomia prática, inteligibilidade ou utilidade da experiência.

The institutional consequence goes beyond the redress of isolated episodes and may include reconfiguration of flows, reinforced transparency duties, design correction and structural or collective measures.p. 747

PTA consequência institucional vai além da reparação de episódios isolados e pode incluir reconfiguração de fluxos, deveres reforçados de transparência, correção de desenho e medidas estruturais ou coletivas.

What is controlled is not an inner will, but a recognizable programp. 732

PTO que se controla não é uma vontade íntima, mas um programa reconhecível

The evidence held by the platform has to come outThe evidence held by the platform has to come out

When does the platform hold all the proof?

When almost everything that shows what happened stays with the platform. The system logs, your account history, the old versions of the screens and the reasons behind each decision are on its side, not yours.

What must the platform do with the records?

Keep and show the minimum records needed to reconstruct what happened. Being open to verification is part of supplying legitimately.

What can you show and what does the platform hold?

On your side, give a plausible account of what you went through, show how it fits into the game and gather what was within your reach. On the platform's side, present the records, histories, versions and technical justifications that matter for the case.

The more what happened depends on what only the platform records, the less reasonable it is to demand from you detailed proof of something that was never in your hands.

How should proof be shared according to access to the records?

The burden of proof should be shared according to who has access to it. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions in your favor, which make certain facts count as true until proven otherwise, and a heightened duty for the platform to cooperate with the proof.

Does this mean you no longer have to explain what happened?

You still have to tell a coherent, believable story and be clear about what you are asking for. What falls away is the fiction that you and the platform are on equal footing when it comes to proof. Your lack of access to the records cannot become a wall against you.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform concentrates the essential elements to narrate, demonstrate and reconstruct the conflict, the evidentiary discipline must recognize that verifiability is part of the very legitimate regime of supply.p. 747

PTSempre que a plataforma concentrar os elementos essenciais para narrar, demonstrar e reconstruir o conflito, a disciplina probatória deverá reconhecer que a verificabilidade integra o próprio regime legítimo do fornecimento.

The corresponding duty is to preserve and disclose the minimum records necessary to reconstruct the disputed event.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de preservar e exibir registros mínimos necessários à reconstrução do evento controvertido.

The minimum required evidence, from the consumer's point of view, consists in a plausible presentation of the harmful experience, of its insertion in the ecosystem and of the accessible context elementsp. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível, do ponto de vista do consumidor, consiste em apresentação plausível da experiência lesiva, de sua inserção no ecossistema e dos elementos de contexto acessíveis

from the supplier's point of view, it requires the disclosure of trails, logs, histories, versions and technically delimited justificationsp. 747

PTdo ponto de vista do fornecedor, exige-se exibição de trilhas, logs, históricos, versões e justificativas tecnicamente delimitadas

The more the reconstruction of the conflict depends on internal governance, the less reasonable it will be to require the consumer to demonstrate in detail facts whose documentary and technical support was never within their reach.p. 736

PTQuanto mais dependente da governança interna for a reconstrução do conflito, menos razoável será exigir do consumidor demonstração minuciosa de fatos cujo suporte documental e técnico jamais esteve sob seu alcance.

The possible legal qualification involves dynamic redistribution of the burden of proof, presumptions favorable to the consumer and reinforcement of the duty of cooperationp. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível envolve redistribuição dinâmica do ônus da prova, presunções favoráveis ao consumidor e reforço do dever de cooperação

This does not eliminate the need for plausibility, argumentative linkage or delimitation of the disputed object. What it eliminates is the fiction of symmetry in environments whose very structure operates through asymmetry of meaning and of proof.p. 736

PTIsso não elimina a necessidade de verossimilhança, de encadeamento argumentativo ou de delimitação do objeto controvertido. O que elimina é a ficção de simetria em ambientes cuja própria estrutura opera pela assimetria de sentido e de prova.

The institutional consequence consists in preventing technical opacity from turning into procedural shieldingp. 747

PTA consequência institucional consiste em impedir que a opacidade técnica se converta em blindagem processual

Owning the work is not an excuse for everythingOwning the work is not an excuse for everything

When do the rights over the game become a shield?

When the company uses the rights it holds over the game to resist any control over the way it supplies what you bought. This includes copyright, trade secrets, licenses and control over the system itself.

It is worth understanding the role of these rights. Intellectual property does not appear here only as an obstacle coming from outside. It is what helps the company define what can be used, for how long and under what conditions. It protects creation, but it also establishes the company's authority over the way you use what you bought. That is why it is part of the very structure of what is sold.

How should the company use exclusivity?

By using exclusivity without turning it into a shield to hide everything, worsen what you bought or take away your access without reason. The balance between the company's rights and yours is struck through the social function, according to which these rights also exist to serve the interest of society. And it is struck through the limits the Constitution imposes on whoever exercises private power.

How do you assess the justification for the restriction?

Whether the restriction the company invokes is really necessary to protect the game and the work, or whether it is serving to shield the way it governs the environment.

What can you ask for when the company invokes the work?

That consumer law keeps applying. In practice, this may mean a delimited examination of the records that preserves the secrets, and control over the equivalence of what you bought. It may also mean that the company respects the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration. And, if the game is shut down, that the exit comes, as far as possible, with transition, preservation or transfer of what you had in there.

Does the company lose its rights over the game?

The intellectual property rights over the game, the art and the code remain protected. Nobody asks the company to give up the work. What is at issue is using this exclusivity as a general veto against consumer protection.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform invokes exclusive ownership, trade secrets, licensing or the proprietary structure of the ecosystem to resist control of the mode of supply, reconciliation must be made in light of the social function and the constitutional limits on the exercise of private positions.p. 747

PTSempre que a plataforma invocar titularidade exclusiva, segredo empresarial, licenciamento ou estrutura proprietária do ecossistema para resistir ao controle do modo de fornecimento, a compatibilização deverá ser feita à luz da função social e dos limites constitucionais do exercício das posições privadas.

On the contrary, it expressly subordinates the legal guarantee of the privilege over industrial inventions and of the protection of industrial creations, trademarks, company names and other distinctive signs to the social interest and to the technological and economic development of the countryp. 702

PTAo contrário, subordina expressamente a garantia legal do privilégio sobre inventos industriais e da proteção às criações industriais, marcas, nomes de empresa e outros signos distintivos ao interesse social e ao desenvolvimento tecnológico e econômico do País

In this scenario, intellectual property appears as an infrastructure of semiotic and technical governance. It not only protects creations, but helps delimit uses, restrict reconfigurations and consolidate the supplier's authority over the conditions of enjoymentp. 130

PTNesse cenário, a propriedade intelectual comparece como infraestrutura de governança semiótica e técnica. Ela não apenas protege criações, mas contribui para delimitar usos, restringir reconfigurações e consolidar a autoridade do fornecedor sobre as condições de fruição

Licenses and technical restrictions are not treated merely as contractual contentp. 54

PTLicenças e restrições técnicas não são tratadas apenas como conteúdo contratual

that delimits what can be used, for how long, under what conditionsp. 54

PTque delimita o que pode ser usado, por quanto tempo, em que condições

The corresponding duty is to exercise exclusivity without turning it into a shield for absolute opacity, degrading mutability or arbitrary suppression of enjoyment.p. 747

PTO dever correspondente é o de exercer a exclusividade sem convertê-la em escudo para opacidade absoluta, mutabilidade degradante ou supressão arbitrária da fruição.

The minimum required evidence concerns the relation between the prerogative invoked and the concrete aspect of the conflict, showing whether the restriction is really necessary to protect the asset or in fact operates as an undue immunization of governance.p. 747

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre a relação entre a prerrogativa invocada e o aspecto concreto do conflito, demonstrando-se se a restrição é realmente necessária à proteção do ativo ou se opera, em verdade, como imunização indevida da governança.

The possible legal qualification preserves the validity of intellectual property on its own plane, but rules out its invocation as a general veto on the application of the CDC.p. 747

PTA qualificação jurídica possível preserva a validade da propriedade intelectual em seu plano próprio, mas afasta sua invocação como veto geral à incidência do CDC.

institutional consequence consists in reconciling exclusivity and consumer protection through delimited auditability, control of functional equivalence and subjection of the exercise of IP to the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration.p. 748

PTconsequência institucional consiste em compatibilizar exclusividade e tutela consumerista por meio de auditabilidade delimitada, controle da equivalência funcional e sujeição do exercício da PI aos deveres de adequação, transparência e recomposição.

The right to exploit, close or reorganize an economic activity is not to be confused with a supposed right to annihilate, without duties of transition, preservation or transfer, the functional enjoyment already stabilized in the consumer's sphere.p. 709

PTO direito de explorar, encerrar ou reorganizar atividade econômica não se confunde com um suposto direito de aniquilar, sem deveres de transição, preservação ou transferência, a fruição funcional já estabilizada na esfera do consumidor.

The answer should restore what you lostThe answer should restore what you lost

When does the loss call for more than money?

When the problem affects your access, the continuity of what you use or the equivalence of what you bought. Or when it affects your history, your standing among other players or what the item meant to you.

What response must the platform give?

A response that fits the kind of loss. If what you lost was a function, the response should target that function, not just a sum of money at the end.

How do you link the loss to what the platform did?

The link between the platform's decision or mechanism and the loss you suffered. And the fact that it is technically or institutionally possible to restore, undo, replace with something equivalent or correct the procedure.

What solution can restore what was lost?

The priority is to recover what was lost, when that is feasible. The solution has to match the loss you showed and what can realistically be restored. That may mean restoring access, undoing a penalty, preserving your history, replacing the item with something that serves the same function, adjusting the rules and stopping the problem from continuing. When the harm reveals a recurring pattern, you can also ask for the structure that produces it to be corrected.

What about financial compensation?

Money stays on the table. Compensation remains possible, including for harms that are not only material, when restoring is not feasible or not enough. What is rejected is treating it as the automatic answer to everything, as if every loss of this kind turned into a number without anyone looking at what was lost.

Where it comes from

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving monetary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

The corresponding duty is to structure protection in accordance with the nature of the loss, aiming at the affected enjoyment and not only at its residual monetary translation.p. 748

PTO dever correspondente é o de estruturar a tutela em conformidade com a natureza da perda, mirando a fruição afetada e não apenas sua tradução monetária residual.

The minimum required evidence involves demonstrating the causal link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent replacement or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The possible legal qualification varies according to the main incidence of the case, but the institutional consequence should favor restoration of access, reversal of sanctions, preservation of history, equivalent functional replacement, governance adjustments, injunctive protection and structural correction when the harm reveals a repeatable patternp. 748

PTA qualificação jurídica possível varia conforme a incidência principal do caso, mas a consequência institucional deve privilegiar restauração de acesso, reversão de sanções, preservação de histórico, substituição funcional equivalente, ajustes de governança, tutela inibitória e correção estrutural quando o dano revelar padrão repetível

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to reimburse material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when present. What is refused is its transformation into an automatic and preferential response in every case, as if injury to the IOC could always be adequately translated into a figure without regard to the structure of the compromised enjoyment.p. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes. O que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese, como se a lesão ao OIC pudesse ser sempre adequadamente traduzida em cifra sem consideração pela estrutura da fruição comprometida.
