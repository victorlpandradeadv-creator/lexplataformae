Situation k) · 7.1.2 of the thesis

What to ask for, with restoration before compensation

k) remedies suited to functional restoration and regime correction

Which losses call for restoration?

Injury to access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or the meaningful density of enjoyment, such as the closed account, the sanction kept without review, the downgraded item or the erased history.

How do you connect governance to the functional loss?

The link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, and the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.

Which rules support specific relief?

Article 35 of the CDC1 allows the consumer to demand specific relief, accept an equivalent performance or terminate with losses and damages. Article 84 of the CDC2 enshrines specific relief for the obligation and a practical result equivalent to performance.

Does compensation drop out of the picture?

The response prioritizes restoring access, reversing sanctions, preserving history, equivalent functional substitution and adjustments to governance. Compensation remains possible and plays an important role, including for non-pecuniary damages, but not as an automatic response. When the harm reveals a repeatable pattern, regime correction is warranted, with injunctive relief, structural measures and collective measures.

Where it comes from

In these cases, monetary compensation continues to play an important role, whether to make good material losses or to recognize non-pecuniary damages when present. What is rejected is its transformation into an automatic and preferred response in every casep. 742

PTNessas hipóteses, a compensação monetária continua a desempenhar papel importante, seja para ressarcir perdas materiais, seja para reconhecer danos extrapatrimoniais quando presentes. O que se recusa é sua transformação em resposta automática e preferencial em qualquer hipótese

Regime correction, by contrast, makes it possible to treat the harm as architectural and not merely as an isolated episode. This makes it possible to justify injunctive relief, structural measures, collective measures and even mandatory compliance mechanisms compatible with open auditability and institutional control of enjoyment.p. 741

PTA correção de regime, ao contrário, permite tratar o dano como arquitetural e não apenas como episódio. Com isso, torna-se possível justificar tutela inibitória, medidas estruturais, providências coletivas e até mecanismos de compliance compulsório compatíveis com auditabilidade aberta e controle institucional da fruição.

The law cited on this page

  1. Article 35 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 35. If the supplier of products or services refuses to honor the offer, presentation or advertising, the consumer may, alternatively and at their free choice: I - demand specific relief of the obligation […]; II - accept another equivalent product or provision of service; III - rescind the contract […], and to losses and damages.”

    PT“Art. 35. Se o fornecedor de produtos ou serviços recusar cumprimento à oferta, apresentação ou publicidade, o consumidor poderá, alternativamente e à sua livre escolha: I - exigir o cumprimento forçado da obrigação […]; II - aceitar outro produto ou prestação de serviço equivalente; III - rescindir o contrato […], e a perdas e danos.”

    Wording transcribed in note 573, p. 540.

  2. Article 84 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 84. In an action whose object is the performance of an obligation to do or not to do, the judge shall grant specific relief for the obligation or order measures that ensure a practical result equivalent to that of performance.”

    PT“Art. 84. Na ação que tenha por objeto o cumprimento da obrigação de fazer ou não fazer, o juiz concederá a tutela específica da obrigação ou determinará providências que assegurem o resultado prático equivalente ao do adimplemento.”

    Wording transcribed in note 574, p. 540.
