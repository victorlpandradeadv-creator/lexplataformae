Follow a case from beginning to end

Each path follows something you have on a platform, from the beginning to the day something goes wrong, and ends with what you can demand.

Choose the one closest to your story. Each one can be read on its own.

  • Follow the skinYou bought a skin. Is it yours?
  • Follow the communityYou built a community on a social network. Whose is it?
  • Follow the orderYou bought on a marketplace. Who chose what you bought?
  • Follow the assistantYou trust an artificial intelligence assistant. Who decides what it does for you?
  • Follow the rideYou work through an app. Who decides how much you earn tomorrow?
  • Follow the storeYour store sells through the app. Who runs your business?
  • Follow the complaintYou complained, were treated well and nothing happened. Why?
