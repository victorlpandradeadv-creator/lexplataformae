2.1 On the theoretical-methodological strategy

See this section in the PDF

p. 47

p. 48 The research adopts a qualitative approach and a theoretical-empirical nature, treating the problem as a legal phenomenon that cannot be reduced, in isolation, to “contract” or to “technology”. In continuous digital services, effective normativity emerges from the coupling of clauses, interface architecture and practices of executing sanctions, which requires a methodological design capable of keeping visible the articulation between socially relevant facts, values in dispute and applicable norms, yielding neither to abstraction that floats above the object nor to description that is satisfied with the operational level. The object of analysis is not “a game”, but a regime of platform-governed enjoyment, in which the value delivered, the permanence of the bond and the internal conditions of use are modulated by private governance over time.

The method is organized as theoretical-dogmatic research combined with institutional analysis of platform governance, triangulated with material from the selected ecosystem employed as situated and referenced exemplification. The dogmatic reconstruction operates on the law in force and seeks to produce a justifiable interpretation in light of recognizable normative parameters, without normative invention and without proposing an autonomous microsystem.

At the legal-normative level, the proposed reconstruction does not operate through a compartmentalized reading of the sources, as if the Constitution, the Consumer Protection Code and the intellectual property regimes applied in watertight and mutually exclusive blocks. The path adopts, as a methodological criterion of normative articulation, a coordinated reading of sources simultaneously applicable to the same governed consumer relationship, especially when the conflict involves, at the same time, constitutional protection of the person and the consumer, the consumer-law discipline of supply and the structures of exclusivity, licensing and technical control proper to intellectual property rights. In this key, the theory of the dialogue of sources appears less as an autonomous topic than as a methodological discipline of interpretation, allowing a systematically controlled reconstruction of the problem without artificial isolation of the pertinent legal regimes. Article 7 of the CDC18 provides, here, a relevant normative point of support, by making explicit that the rights provided for in the Code do not exclude others arising from domestic legislation, treaties, regulations and p. 49 general principles, reinforcing the openness of the consumer-law microsystem to coordination with other applicable normative sources.

The dogmatic ambition consists in interpreting and rearticulating categories of the Consumer Protection Code and of intellectual property rights under the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption, preserving the protective matrix and refusing to let exclusivity, licensing or technical powers operate as a title of immunity against the control of adequacy, transparency and abusiveness. Because of the researcher's insertion in the field, experience functions as a position of observation and interpretation to guide the selection and reading of the material, but not as a criterion of evidence.

The empirical dimension of the work is mobilized for illustrative purposes and disciplined by mechanisms, so as to make intelligible institutional arrangements and legally relevant points of friction. It is not intended to describe the ecosystem exhaustively, nor to demonstrate statistical prevalence, nor to offer a complete overview of its history or its variations. The empirical references are selected for their pertinence to the argument and for their capacity to make verifiable the passage between promise, execution and loss in continuous bonds, serving as support for the theoretical-dogmatic reconstruction and not as a demonstration of volume.

The theoretical reconstruction that precedes the dogmatic stage is organized by functional axes and by problems, and not by an exegetical inventory of authors. The object analyzed is contemporary, dynamic and institutionally hybrid, so that intelligibility depends less on “explaining authors” than on describing mechanisms that operate in the service and then leading them back to legal parameters of protection. In this design, the frameworks mobilized serve to offer a point of support19 for stabilizing questions and delimiting observable mechanisms, without this implying cult, uncritical reproduction or full reconstitution of the author's path Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 41). The support serves to take the p. 50 analysis out of vagueness and also to prevent the description from becoming too intuitive in a domain in which the exercise of power tends to present itself as mere “technical functioning”.

So that this path does not become disorganized, a clear separation between sources and critical literature is also preserved20 Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 70). Sources are the materials that directly make up the observed institutional world or the law in force that applies to it, such as terms of use, internal policies, supplier announcements, interface records, legal documents and accessible judicial decisions. Critical literature is the set of texts that comment on, interpret, systematize or dispute these materials, such as doctrine, academic articles and third-party analyses. The distinction is not formalism, since it prevents commentary from being treated as evidence of what the platform declared or did, and also prevents indirect reading from replacing contact with primary material when the argument depends on dogmatic attribution.

A disciplined hierarchy is thus preserved. The conceptual instruments guide the gaze and help to frame more precise questions. The sources support what can be asserted about the promises, powers, procedures and effects of the regime under observation. The critical literature qualifies debates, puts readings under tension and makes interpretive choices explicit, without replacing the documentary grounding that supports the reconstruction. This architecture avoids the erudite drift that loses sight of the phenomenon, and it also avoids the rhetoric that uses authors as a seal of prestige, keeping the argument traceable to verifiable evidence and justifiable criteria Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 69-70).

This set of precautions does not alter the dogmatic direction of the inquiry, but it fixes the conditions under which the reconstruction remains controllable when dealing with an object administered through technical and communicational decisions. The task from there on is to identify normative points capable of anchoring the institutional reading of how the service functions and of limiting the tendency to treat mutability and automated execution as mere operational neutralities. At this stage, legal provisions that already recognize the temporality of supply and the consumer's dependence on the technical life cycle p. 51 of the good become especially relevant, since they offer external criteria for distinguishing changes compatible with the relationship from legally relevant reconfigurations of the promised content.

This anchoring becomes especially verifiable in the field of computer programs. Law No. 9,609/1998 Brasil(Brasil, [1998]) does not merely describe exclusivity and licensing as an enclosure of utilities. It also structures positive duties of the supplier tied to the technical life cycle of the software. Article 721 requires the license contract, the tax document and the media and packaging to state, in an easily legible way, the period of technical validity of the version marketed, and Article 822 turns this period into an obligation to ensure complementary technical services for the proper functioning of the program during its technical validity, even when it is withdrawn from commercial circulation, subject to compensation for losses. These legal provisions therefore operate as control points for separating “mere operational variation” from a relevant reconfiguration of the content of supply in continuous relationships.

The institutional analysis seeks to identify how internal rules, design and enforcement mechanisms structure conduct, risks and expectations, producing a practical normativity that must be legally graspable. Triangulating material from the selected ecosystem serves to anchor the categories and to test them in a directed way, so that inferences remain traceable to verifiable evidence and legal reasoning turns neither into moralizing nor into mere description. Here the empirical material does not serve as exhaustive demonstration or statistical generalization. It serves as controlled exemplification, meant to make typical mechanisms intelligible and to guide the application of the dogmatic keys.

In services structured around continuous updating, the reference to technical validity does not operate as a requirement to anticipate specific changes or a schedule of modulations, which is readily seen to be impossible in factual and operational terms. It serves as a control point for qualifying duties of information, support and restoration when the exercise of unilateral power significantly reconfigures the p. 52 content of supply and shifts onto the consumer the losses arising from this mutability, without turning the evolving dynamic of the service into unlawfulness in itself.

The Consumer Protection Code remains the central matrix of protection, while the intellectual property regime enters with a functional status, describing contemporary forms of enclosure and circulation of utilities through licenses, technical conditions and powers of modulation. This coordination makes it possible to reconstruct verifiable criteria for qualifying the object, legitimate expectation fabricated as a promise, enjoyment, loss and restoration in platform-governed ecosystems, without turning exclusivity into a title of immunity from the CDC. It also prevents the legal relevance of losses from depending on material subtraction, recognizing that persistent changes in access, in position and in the internal conditions of use may reconfigure the link between promise, legitimate expectation and delivery in a legally relevant way, provided they can be identified by controllable criteria.

The research strategy combines complementary processes aimed at analyzing legislation, legal scholarship, contract models and institutional documentation, without treating these levels as disconnected layers. The illustrative empirical locus is organized by explicit criteria of selection, recording and treatment, so that contracts, policies, institutional announcements, interface artifacts, public statements, available technical records and selected court decisions can be read as evidence of the normativity actually in operation. The empirical material supports the qualification of the object and the testing of interpretive hypotheses, under a discipline of traceability and coherence.

Michael Burawoy's methodological perspective of the extended case study23 is drawn on here for a limited purpose, as a discipline for using the selected ecosystem as an instrumental empirical laboratory. The paradigmatic case is used to make p. 53 observable, at the situated level, structural forces that show up in the ordinary functioning of the ecosystem, with explicit support from pre-existing theory, without turning the work into a case study pursued as an end in itself. Within this frame, the move from micro to macro does not depend on statistical representativeness or broad inductive generalization, since it aims at the controlled reconstruction of the theoretical apparatus being used, through frictions and anomalies identifiable in the case Burawoy(Burawoy, 2009, p. 21; p. 43).

The methodological function of the laboratory, therefore, is to ensure institutional intelligibility and to allow directed testing of the operational categories developed in the thesis, through triangulation and referenced selection of verifiable evidence, while keeping the object legally describable. This directed testing works together with illustrative applications and tests of fit of the proposed keys in typical contemporary situations of continuous digital consumption, without turning the inquiry into an exhaustive empirical demonstration. The thesis nonetheless acknowledges the risk inherent in the reconstructive gesture itself, which Burawoy describes as a coercive process of “double fitting”, in which complex situations can be fitted to the theory and the theory then refitted to the case to “digest” the anomaly Burawoy(Burawoy, 2009, p. 60). To contain this risk, inferences are disciplined by explicit criteria for selecting material and by a hierarchy of sources, giving priority to primary documentation attributable to the supplier and using public narratives only subsidiarily, as verifiable leads, so that familiarity with the field never serves as a criterion of proof.

The theoretical scope works as a grid of observation and control, meant to guide the reading of the empirical material and to discipline inferences. Rather than an inventory of authors, it is organized along functional axes, each tied to research questions and reading criteria. The first axis treats technique and infrastructure as a form of rationality that structures possibilities of action and interpretation. The second treats the cultural and communicational dimension as a condition for the formation of expectations, promises and intelligibility in interface-mediated environments, and it also draws, in a structural key, on the anthropology of consumption to describe how goods, belonging and routines stabilize meanings and coordinate conduct. The third reconstructs political economy and extraction in order to locate the dependencies, tolls and forms of capture associated with the ecosystem's operation. The fourth describes the semiotic dimension as a material component of value and as a condition for the legal cognizability of the conflict, including the notion of regimes of value and of the socially administered mutability of the object over time. The fifth reconstructs platform governance as the exercise of private power through internal rules p. 54 and sanction mechanisms. The authors drawn on in each axis are introduced and discussed in the corresponding theoretical sections.

In this design, the operational categories of the Immaterial Object of Consumption and of semiotic vulnerability guide the identification of promises and legitimate expectations, the description of the mediations that modulate enjoyment, and the qualification of losses that are socially perceived and legally disputable in continuous relationships. The consumer-law tradition and the dogmatic frameworks used here operate as parameters for the legal qualification of the conflict and as interpretive limits, keeping the focus on protection, adequacy, transparency and restoration. The empirical material, in turn, illustrates, constrains and offers tests, requiring formulations to remain traceable to observable features of the ecosystem's institutional functioning, without confusing a description of the field with normative justification. The formalization of responses in terms of protection, control of abusiveness and restoration is reserved for the dogmatic stage of the work, in which the dialogue between theory, exemplification and normative parameters is converted into applicable operational criteria.
