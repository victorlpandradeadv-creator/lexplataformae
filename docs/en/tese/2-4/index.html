2.4 On the empirical sources and collection techniques

See this section in the PDF

This section in the layers of the site

  • Method · The protocol

p. 59

The Riot Games ecosystem, centered on League of Legends, was selected for the density and diversity of the public traces it produces. The continuous operation of the service, the repeated administration of identity and reputation and the institutional presence of the game as a p. 60 platform of participation generate abundant and heterogeneous empirical material, with user statements, records of conflicts and technical-normative documentation circulating in multiple layers. This availability is not treated as a mere convenience, but as a methodological condition for articulating evidence on promises, relevant losses and mechanisms of private governance, in proportion to the argumentative weight of each statement, preventing the case from being reconstructed only on the basis of official terms or only on the basis of narratives of experience.

To preserve proportionality between documentation effort and argumentative weight, a distinction is drawn between three layers of information mobilized throughout the work. The first comprises elements of general knowledge widely disseminated in the sector, used only to situate the reader and make the logic of continuous digital services intelligible, without any claim to prove a specific occurrence. The second comprises auxiliary technical-operational descriptions, used to name internal structures and to understand the vocabulary of the field, with subsidiary support from collaborative repositories when indispensable. In this layer, public narratives may be mobilized as illustrative scenarios to formulate hypotheses and test the applicability of the proposed categories, without any claim to specific institutional attribution. The third comprises normatively relevant statements, such as institutional promises, powers of modulation, sanction criteria, challenge procedures and restoration parameters, which are supported, whenever mobilized as a premise of legal qualification, by traceable and attributable sources, with priority given to the supplier's primary documentation and to verifiable public records.

Empirical collection is organized by a controlled inductive-deductive movement. It starts from categories previously stabilized in the theoretical path to guide the reading of the material and, at the same time, admits that empirical frictions may require refinement of those categories, without converting empirical work into an inventory or into statistical sampling. The material actually mobilized in the text is organized into three families of sources, with distinct and complementary functions: (i) official documents and communications of Riot Games; (ii) public video statements by users and content producers, above all on YouTube; (iii) community discussions referred to by such statements, with priority for threads retrieved on Reddit when there is a direct reference, and analyses by the specialized media and press when they serve as a historical and conceptual record relevant to the analysis. The general rule is to work by triangulation and sufficiency of the core of evidence pertinent to the argument, rather than pursuing exhaustiveness or representativeness.

p. 61 The company's official and public documentation is part of the research survey as a structural source for reconstructing the private governance regime of the ecosystem, including terms, policies, public communications and institutional materials relevant to understanding promises, internal rules of permanence and forms of sanction. The dogmatic and bibliographic analysis, in turn, is not treated as an “empirical source”, but as a lens of interpretation that organizes what counts as evidence, what counts as a promise and what counts as a relevant loss in the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption. To preserve the fluidity of the central text, only the level of description necessary to make the path auditable will be kept in the body of the section. The methodology used and the justification for the choice of theoretical framework will be duly set out and discussed, when pertinent, in their respective development sections.

The empirical references mobilized in the work are kept traceable by precise indication of origin and access date, with priority for stable public records. In the case of public content subject to change, traceability is ensured by a minimum record of the item's identifier, the source address and the consultation date, without any claim to constitute an exhaustive repository of full captures.

The objective is to reduce the volatility effects typical of digital environments, such as later changes, removals, blocks and reorganizations, preserving the content as it was available at the moment of collection. Preservation focuses on the textual and documentary material actually mobilized in support of the argument, while interface records and references to visual elements are included only if strictly indispensable to understanding the mechanism described and to the minimum verifiability of the point being made.

The three families of sources are detailed in the following subsections, organized by methodological strand. The strand of situated institutional observation (2.4.1) describes the norm in operation at the level of enjoyment. The strand of documentary analysis (2.4.2) captures the declared norm and the institutional design of the relationship. The strand of public statements (2.4.3) records the plane of social perception of frictions, losses and frustrated expectations, with a subsidiary function disciplined by the hierarchy described in the preceding paragraphs.
