A vocabulary for contesting

Your skin is a product, but a product of a special kind. You do not take it home, and its use, what it shows to others and the place it gives you only exist while the platform keeps the environment running. The legal category for this product is the Immaterial Object of Consumption, or IOC.

Brazilian law had already made room for it in 1990, when the Consumer Protection Code said that a product can be immaterial. It was also already known that digital goods are goods. What was missing were criteria for cases in which use still depends on a platform that keeps controlling, changing and punishing over time.

Where it comes from

What is asserted is that, whenever the consumed utility presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there will be an immaterial product in a legally relevant sense for the application of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC1.p. 718

PTO que se afirma é que, sempre que a utilidade consumida se apresente como posição de fruição sustentada por governança privada contínua, haverá produto imaterial em sentido juridicamente relevante para a incidência do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC .

Article 3, §1, of the Consumer Protection Code already contains the normative opening needed to recognize goods that circulate without a stable corporeal medium and whose enjoyment depends on continuous technical and organizational conditions.p. 24

PTO Artigo 3º, §1º, do Código de Defesa do Consumidor já contém a abertura normativa necessária ao reconhecimento de bens que circulam sem suporte corpóreo estável e cuja fruição depende de condições técnicas e organizacionais contínuas.

[...] with good density, a problem that cannot be circumvented by contractual rhetoric or by nostalgia for tangibility: techno-digital assets are legal goods and do not disappear from the world of Law just because they are incorporealp. 565

PT[...] com boa densidade, um problema que não pode ser contornado por retórica contratual ou por nostalgia da tangibilidade: ativos tecnodigitais são bens jurídicos e não desaparecem do mundo do Direito só porque são incorpóreos

The insufficiency would not lie in this framework's inability to recognize the asset, but in the fact that, even while recognizing appropriation and circulation, it does not exhaust the conflicts of continuous enjoyment, unilateral mutability and functional governance in a platform environmentp. 566

PTA insuficiência não estaria na incapacidade dessa moldura para reconhecer o ativo, mas no fato de que, mesmo reconhecendo apropriação e circulação, ela não esgota os conflitos de fruição contínua, mutabilidade unilateral e governança funcional em ambiente de plataforma

Without the correct qualification of the object as governed immaterial enjoyment, the subsequent parameters tend to lose traction, because auditability, functional equivalence, abusive teleology and restoration remedies presuppose precisely the existence of a bond in which the consumed utility depends on continued administration of the environment.p. 719

Together, they speak the same language, and the center of that language is the IOC, because without it the others lose their footing.
