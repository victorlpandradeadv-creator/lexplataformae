Why nine songs?

The thesis opens with two general epigraphs and gives each development chapter a song, nine in all, under the method set out in section 2.6. They serve neither as ornament nor as authority and do not prove the argument. Each opening condenses, in artistic language, the movement the chapter carries out in legal language.

Where it comes from

The epigraphs do not appear as ornament, nor as an authority substituting for scientific method, nor as empirical proof of the argument.p. 73

PTAs epígrafes não comparecem como ornamento, nem como autoridade substitutiva do método científico, tampouco como prova empírica do argumento.

The argumentation is not founded on the excerpt, nor does it draw its chain of validation from it.p. 74

PTA argumentação não se funda no excerto, nem dele extrai sua cadeia de validação.

Each chapter opening condenses, in artistic language, the specific movement that the dogmatic development will carry out in legal language.p. 77

PTCada abertura de capítulo condensa, em linguagem artística, o movimento específico que o desenvolvimento dogmático realizará em linguagem jurídica.

What are the songs for?

Platform governance is not exhausted by the written rule. It also runs through the design of screens, the language of the brand and continuous symbolic production. The curation of the epigraphs enters the method to grasp this register, made of anxiety, desire to belong, euphoria, frustration and capture.

The procedure is selective. Each song enters through the excerpt that matters to the chapter, without presuming total coincidence between the work and the problem, and the legal analysis does not rest on it.

Where it comes from

In the platform economy, governance is not exhausted by the written rule.p. 73

PTNa economia de plataforma, a governança não se esgota na regra escrita.

It is also exercised through choice architecture, interface design, brand language, curation of affects and continuous symbolic production.p. 73

PTEla se exerce também por arquitetura de escolhas, desenho de interface, linguagem de marca, curadoria de afetos e produção simbólica contínua.

It is in this context that the curation of epigraphs finds its place in the method, as a way of grasping, in an artistic and culturally situated register, tensions constitutive of contemporary digital consumption, among them anxiety, desire for belonging, disorientation, euphoria, frustration, capture and performative compulsion.p. 73

PTÉ nesse contexto que a curadoria de epígrafes se insere no método, como forma de apreender, em registro artístico e culturalmente situado, tensões constitutivas do consumo digital contemporâneo, entre elas ansiedade, desejo de pertencimento, desorientação, euforia, frustração, captura e compulsão performática.

Nor does the epigraphic curation import into the text the full content of the songs, nor does it presume total coincidence between the artistic work and the problem investigated.p. 74

PTA curadoria epigráfica tampouco importa para o texto o teor integral das canções, nem presume coincidência total entre a obra artística e o problema investigado.

The procedure is selective.p. 74

PTO procedimento é seletivo.

Why nine?

In ancient Greek poetry, the Muses were invoked at the start of the tale to set the tone and situate the voice about to speak. The nine songs serve as that kind of frame, without claiming the standing of the Muses. They also work as a chorus, keeping the atmosphere of each conflict alive while the argument moves forward.

Where it comes from

In archaic Greek literature, the invocation of the Muses, as in Hesiod, organizes the tale, delimits its tone and situates the voice that speaks.p. 74

PTNa literatura grega arcaica, a invocação das Musas, tal como em Hesíodo, organiza o relato, delimita seu tom e situa a voz que fala.

The presence of nine epigraphs emulates the nine Muses and reinforces the framing character of the device.p. 74

PTA presença de nove epígrafes emula as nove Musas e reforça o caráter de moldura do recurso.

In a dramatic key, the function also approaches that of the chorus, not because the text wants to theatricalize its object, but because it recognizes that certain conflicts require, beyond conceptual formulation, a voice that keeps their moral and affective atmosphere in circulation.p. 74

PTEm chave dramática, a função se aproxima também da do coro, não porque o texto queira teatralizar seu objeto, mas porque reconhece que certos conflitos exigem, além da formulação conceitual, uma voz que mantenha em circulação sua atmosfera moral e afetiva.

As a formal detail, the numerical coincidence between the nine Muses and the nine epigraphs of the work helps to make explicit, without any claim to equivalence of status, the function of the epigraphs as a narrative frame and a key of interpretive orientation.p. 74

PTComo detalhe formal, a coincidência numérica entre as nove Musas e as nove epígrafes do trabalho ajuda a explicitar, sem qualquer pretensão de equivalência de estatuto, a função das epígrafes como moldura narrativa e chave de orientação interpretativa.

Why are some in English and others in Portuguese?

The songs in English record the global grammar of digital consumption, with promises of belonging, incentives to self-exploitation and an opacity that comes to seem normal. The Brazilian ones mark the return to the country's own law and the passage from the problem to the answer.

The change of language does work in the argument. The problem arises on a global scale, inside the platforms, while the legal containment is built from the Constitution and the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code.

Where it comes from

The anglophone block records the global grammar of digital consumption in its promises of belonging, in its incentives to self-exploitation, in its normalization of capture and in its diffuse regime of opacity.p. 77

PTO bloco anglófono registra a gramática global do consumo digital em suas promessas de pertencimento, em seus incentivos à autoexploração, em sua normalização da captura e em seu regime difuso de opacidade.

The Brazilian block, in turn, signals the return to the institutional ground of national law and the passage from the experience of the problem to the formulation of the answer.p. 77

PTO bloco brasileiro, por sua vez, assinala o retorno ao solo institucional do Direito nacional e a passagem da experiência do problema à formulação da resposta.

The transition between languages is not a peripheral aesthetic detail.p. 77

PTA transição entre idiomas não é detalhe estético lateral.

The problem emerges on a global scale, inside private platform architectures.p. 77

PTO problema emerge em escala global, no interior de arquiteturas privadas de plataforma.

Legal containment, however, is built with national instruments, under the constitutional order and the Consumer Protection Code.p. 77

PTA contenção jurídica, porém, é construída com instrumentos nacionais, sob a ordem constitucional e o Código de Defesa do Consumidor.

What are the nine songs?

  1. 1Lily Allen, The Fear (2009)

    Opens the thesis, as a general epigraph · The full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    General epigraph of the thesis. It portrays the beginning of digital life, when a performed self becomes a requirement and the networks' promise of freedom soon shows its other side of exposure, falsity and capture.

    Where it comes from

    As regards the historical and cultural delimitation of the repertoire mobilized, it is worth noting that the meaning the songwriter herself attributes to The Fear, the general epigraph of the work, originates as a diagnosis of an inaugural moment of digital life, the one in which a “performed self” begins to be demanded and in which the liberating promise of the networks begins to reveal, almost immediately, its face of continuous exposure, falsity and capture.p. 76

    PTNo que toca à delimitação histórico-cultural do repertório mobilizado, importa notar que o sentido atribuído pela própria compositora a The Fear, epígrafe geral do trabalho, nasce como diagnóstico de um instante inaugural da vida digital, aquele em que passa a se exigir um “eu performado” e em que a promessa libertadora das redes começa a revelar, quase de imediato, sua face de exposição contínua, falsidade e captura.

  2. 2Lorde, The Love Club (2012)

    Opens 1 Introduction · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Second general epigraph, which also opens the introduction. It speaks of the pull of digital communities and the loneliness of closed groups, where joining an environment gradually exacts distance from the world outside.

    Where it comes from

    It is along this itinerary that The Love Club, the second general epigraph and opening of the introductory section, introduces the problem of the attraction exerted by digital communities and of the loneliness produced by closed forms of belonging, in which entry into an ecosystem exacts, little by little, a withdrawal from outer reality.p. 76

    PTÉ nesse itinerário que The Love Club, segunda epígrafe geral e abertura da seção introdutória, introduz o problema da atração exercida por comunidades digitais e da solidão produzida por pertencimentos fechados, nos quais o ingresso em um ecossistema cobra, pouco a pouco, afastamento da realidade exterior.

  3. 3Danni Carlos, Coisas Que Eu Sei (2008)

    Opens 2 On the methodological design and the observation protocol · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the method chapter. It marks the passage from the affective universe of the problem to the terrain of law, where one must understand and control.

    Where it comes from

    The problem emerges on a global scale, inside private platform architectures. Legal containment, however, is built with national instruments, under the constitutional order and the Consumer Protection Code. For this reason, opening the methodological section, Coisas Que Eu Sei marks the passage from the affective and cultural universe of the problem to the legal horizon of intelligibility and control.p. 77

    PTO problema emerge em escala global, no interior de arquiteturas privadas de plataforma. A contenção jurídica, porém, é construída com instrumentos nacionais, sob a ordem constitucional e o Código de Defesa do Consumidor. Por isso, abrindo a seção metodológica, Coisas Que Eu Sei marca a passagem do universo afetivo e cultural do problema ao horizonte jurídico de inteligibilidade e controle.

  4. 4Christina Aguilera, Birds of Prey (2010)

    Opens 3 Technology, platforms and the techno-media economic order of digital consumption · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the chapter on technique, platforms and the economy of digital consumption. It shows the predatory face of this economy, in an environment where surveillance and extraction turn use into a hunting ground.

    Where it comes from

    The following section is greeted by Birds of Prey, which states the predatory face of the contemporary economic system by portraying an environment in which surveillance and extraction turn enjoyment into a hunting ground.p. 76

    PTA seção seguinte é recebida por Birds of Prey, que enuncia a face predatória do sistema econômico contemporâneo ao retratar um ambiente em que vigilância e extração convertem a fruição em território de caça.

  5. 5MARINA, Oh No! (2010)

    Opens 4 Semiotics and governance of enjoyment in continuous immaterial consumption · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the chapter on meaning and the governing of use. It condenses the capture of those who live for performance, trapped between the obsession with measured success, the mechanization of conduct and submission to their own digital possessions.

    Where it comes from

    In the fourth section, Oh No! condenses the semiotic and psychopolitical capture of the achievement subject, imprisoned between the obsession with metricized success, the mechanization of conduct and the paradoxical submission to one's own digital assets and possessions.p. 76

    PTNa quarta seção, Oh No! condensa a captura semiótica e psicopolítica do sujeito do desempenho, aprisionado entre a obsessão por sucesso metrificado, a mecanização das condutas e a paradoxal submissão aos próprios ativos e posses digitais.

  6. 6K/DA, Villain (2020), in dialogue with Scarlet Envy, RuPaul’s Drag Race: All Stars 6 Promo (2021)

    Opens 5 On the legal regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC) under the Lex Plataformae · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the chapter on the legal regime of the IOC. It is a dialogue between Scarlet Envy's speech, in a promotional video for a drag queen competition, and the song Villain. Together, they show governing by design in its most assertive form, with one-sided control and manipulation of affects as the rule.

    Where it comes from

    The fifth section closes this block through a dialogue between Scarlet Envy's performance and the lyrics of Villain.p. 76

    PTA quinta seção encerra esse bloco por meio de um diálogo entre a performance de Scarlet Envy e a lírica de Villain.

    The joint composition no longer merely insinuates, but personifies governance by architecture in its most assertive form, by portraying a regime in which unilateral control and manipulation of affects appear as the constitutive logic of operation.p. 76

    PTA composição conjunta já não apenas insinua, mas personifica a governança por arquitetura em sua forma mais assertiva, ao retratar um regime em que controle unilateral e manipulação de afetos aparecem como lógica constitutiva de funcionamento.

  7. 7Zélia Duncan, Diz Nos Meus Olhos (Inclemência) (2005)

    Opens 6 On the protection of the consumer's semiotic vulnerability in light of Brazil's constitutional and economic order · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the chapter on consumer protection in the constitutional order and in the law. It expresses the violence of losing the time, the life investment and the continuity that accumulated inside a bond administered by the company.

    Where it comes from

    In the sixth section, Diz Nos Meus Olhos (Inclemência) expresses the violence of the expropriation of time, existential investment and subjective continuity accumulated under administered bonds.p. 77

    PTNa sexta seção, Diz Nos Meus Olhos (Inclemência) exprime a violência da expropriação de tempo, investimento existencial e continuidade subjetiva acumulados sob vínculos administrados.

  8. 8Marisa Monte, Infinito Particular (2006)

    Opens 7 On the typology of the models of incidence of the Immaterial Object of Consumption in different functional categories of platform · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the chapter on platform models beyond games. It stages the confrontation with the technical black box and, by refusing to accept illegibility as destiny, mirrors the demand to be able to verify what the platform does.

    Where it comes from

    Next, for the seventh section, Infinito Particular stages the confrontation with the technical black box and, by rejecting illegibility as destiny, mirrors the demand for open auditability.p. 77

    PTEm seguida, para a sétima seção, Infinito Particular encena o enfrentamento da caixa-preta técnica e, ao rejeitar a ilegibilidade como destino, espelha a exigência de auditabilidade aberta.

  9. 9Pitty, Temporal (2003)

    Opens 8 Conclusion · The section in the full text · Read the epigraph in the PDF

    Opens the final chapter. It conveys the disorientation of people who no longer recognize themselves, fragile in the face of the platform's signals and trapped in their own identity inside it.

    Where it comes from

    Temporal, in turn, closes the journey by translating, for the eighth and last section of development, the disorientation of the subject in the face of the loss of self-recognition under conditions of semiotic vulnerability and identity captivity.p. 77

    PTTemporal, por sua vez, encerra o percurso ao traduzir, para a oitava e última seção de desenvolvimento, a desorientação do sujeito diante da perda de reconhecimento de si sob condições de vulnerabilidade semiótica e cativeiro identitário.

Why was one of them made by the game company itself?

The sixth song comes from the very environment that serves as the thesis's illustrative reference, because it was produced by the game company. It describes, in the first person, a power founded on one-sided command, on the use of affects and on the discarding of the other.

It is not a confession, but it shows the environment narrating itself as culture. That is why the law of the platform also governs through plot, style, character and performance, and not only through contract, code and screen.

Where it comes from

Produced by Riot Games itself as part of the artistic universe of the ecosystem here taken as an illustrative reference, the composition describes, in the first person and in the language of entertainment, a structure of power founded on unilateral command, affective instrumentalization, discarding of the other and deliberate absence of empathy.p. 76

PTProduzida pela própria Riot Games como parte do universo artístico do ecossistema aqui tomado como referência ilustrativa, a composição descreve, em primeira pessoa e em linguagem de entretenimento, uma estrutura de poder fundada em comando unilateral, instrumentalização afetiva, descarte do outro e ausência deliberada de empatia.

The ecosystem, in narrating itself culturally, projects an intelligible image of its own regime of power, and it is this self-descriptive gesture, not a legal confession, that matters to the argument.p. 76-77

PTO ecossistema, ao se narrar culturalmente, projeta uma imagem inteligível do próprio regime de poder, e é esse gesto autodescritivo, não uma confissão jurídica, que importa ao argumento.

The Lex Plataformae does not govern only by contract, code and interface. It also governs by plot, style, character and performance.p. 77

PTUma Lex Plataformae não governa apenas por contrato, código e interface. Governa também por enredo, estilo, personagem e performance.

Why from fear to the fermata?

The sequence goes from fear, in the first song, to the fermata, in the last. A fermata is the pause that suspends the time of a note in music. Here it becomes the image of a law able to interrupt technical arbitrariness and to place the time of supply under rules of stability and control.

Along the way, the songs make the same passage as the argument, from identifying the problem to the law's answer, and show that popular culture perceived digital capture before law had words to name it.

Where it comes from

The sequence of the nine epigraphs traverses, in a symbolic key, the same passage the thesis traverses in a dogmatic key: from the finding of the problem to its legal containment, from capture to the institutional response, from fear to the fermata.p. 77-78

PTA sequência das nove epígrafes percorre, em chave simbólica, a mesma passagem que a tese percorre em chave dogmática: da constatação do problema à sua contenção jurídica, da captura à resposta institucional, do medo à fermata.

[...] the conclusive image of the epigraphic method emerges: the demand for a legal regime capable of interrupting technical arbitrariness and of subjecting the temporality of supply to criteria of stability, integrity and controllability compatible with the constitutional economic order and with the CDC.p. 77

PT[...] emerge a imagem conclusiva do método epigráfico: a exigência de um regime jurídico capaz de interromper o arbítrio técnico e de submeter a temporalidade do fornecimento a critérios de estabilidade, integridade e controlabilidade compatíveis com a ordem econômica constitucional e com o CDC.

Contemporary popular culture captured the experience of digital capture before dogmatics had a vocabulary fine enough to name it.p. 78

PTA cultura popular contemporânea captou a experiência da captura digital antes que a dogmática dispusesse de vocabulário suficientemente fino para nomeá-la.
