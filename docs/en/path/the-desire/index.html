Why is this skin worth so much?

A skin changes the character's look, effects and animations, and does so again in every match. Some people put a lot of money, many hours and a lot of attention into it. Seen from outside, it looks excessive to pay a high price for an outfit that does not exist off the screen.

Seen from inside, it makes sense, because inside the game the skin is a sign that everyone knows how to read. It says who you are, how long you have been playing and which group you belong to. Someone who buys a rare skin is not buying just a nice effect, but belonging, prestige and the chance of being recognized by others.

Where it comes from

The form, the visual reading, the logic of transformation and the aesthetic experience happen as a performance repeated in each match.p. 439

PTA forma, a leitura visual, a lógica de transformação e a experiência estética acontecem como desempenho repetido em cada partida.

A rare cosmetic item or a reconfigured character is valuable because it operates as a legible social sign, a marker of identity and a point of belonging within a proprietary grammar.p. 104

PTUm item cosmético raro ou um personagem reconfigurado valem porque operam como signo social legível, marcador de identidade e ponto de pertencimento dentro de uma gramática proprietária.

What is consumed, in this type of environment, is not only technical function, but belonging, prestige and recognition mediated by signs.p. 105

PTO que se consome, nesse tipo de ambiente, não é apenas função técnica, mas pertencimento, prestígio e reconhecimento mediado por signos.

That is why your frustration, when the skin changes, is not a whim. An update can alter its appearance, its effects, the rarity everyone could see or its place in the game's economy. The skin stays in your account, with the same name, but no longer fits into the game world the way it used to. And it was this fit that gave value to all the time, money and attention you put into it.

The law can look at this in two ways. It can treat it as a personal heartache, something for people who get too attached, or it can treat it as the deterioration of something whose core is made of meaning and of relationships with others. Only the second reading makes it possible to take the loss seriously.

Where it comes from

When an update alters appearance, effects, perceived rarity or economic insertion, the compatibility between experience, narrative and recognition may come undone. The promise of distinction remains nominally in the account, but no longer finds, in the world of the game, the fit that gave value to the investment of time, money and attention.p. 104

PTQuando uma atualização altera aparência, efeitos, raridade percebida ou inserção econômica, pode se desfazer a compatibilidade entre experiência, narrativa e reconhecimento. A promessa de distinção permanece nominalmente na conta, mas deixa de encontrar, no mundo do jogo, o encaixe que conferia valor ao investimento de tempo, dinheiro e atenção.

When the platform rewrites these signs, the nominal permanence of the object in the account may coexist with the emptying of what it meant and delivered.p. 105

PTQuando a plataforma reescreve esses signos, a permanência nominal do objeto na conta pode conviver com o esvaziamento daquilo que ele significava e entregava.

This framing matters to the legal problem because it makes it possible to describe the injury as the degradation of an enjoyment bundle whose core is semiotic and relational, and not as mere subjective frustration.p. 105

PTEsse enquadramento interessa ao problema jurídico porque permite descrever a lesão como degradação de um feixe de fruição cujo núcleo é semiótico e relacional, e não como mera frustração subjetiva.

Anthropology of consumptionValue as judgment

The store price says little about the value of the skin, because the value of a thing does not live inside it. It is a judgment that people make and that exchange, real or imagined, confirms. A skin is valuable because many people desire it, compare it with others and consider it rare.

That is why the question that matters is not how much it costs, but what makes something seem desirable, comparable and rare. And here is the point, because it is the platform that decides the catalog, the rarity and the visibility rules. Whoever manages these things manages, in practice, the conditions of value itself.

Where it comes from

The consequence is that the relevant question stops being “how much does it cost” and becomes “which frames make something appear desirable, comparable and rare”, because value is not inherent to the object, but a judgment produced by subjects and endorsed by a real or imagined exchangep. 250

PTA consequência é que a pergunta relevante deixa de ser “quanto custa” e passa a ser “quais molduras fazem algo aparecer como desejável, comparável e raro”, porque o valor não é inerente ao objeto, mas um julgamento produzido por sujeitos e endossado por uma troca real ou imaginada

If value is an effect of situated recognition, managing catalog, rarity and visibility rules (curation, windows, criteria) is managing the practical conditions of value itself.p. 251

PTSe o valor é efeito de reconhecimento situado, administrar catálogo, raridade e regras de visibilidade (curadoria, janelas, critérios) é administrar as condições práticas do próprio valor.

Anthropology of consumptionConsumption as ritual

The things we consume work like signs, some saying welcome and others saying keep out. Through them we align ourselves with some people, set ourselves apart from others and give meaning to what we experience. The skin does this inside the game, because it shows which crowd you belong to and who is left out.

Staying outside this circuit, therefore, is not just uncomfortable, because it means losing your voice and losing options. When you think about leaving a game, the cost is not only the money left behind, but also the risk of isolating yourself and weakening ties you formed inside it.

Where it comes from

Douglas and Isherwood (1996, p. xxiv) describe goods as devices of alignment and of boundary.p. 251

PTDouglas e Isherwood (1996, p. xxiv) descrevem bens como dispositivos de alinhamento e de fronteira.

Social life is organized by signs of adhesion and exclusion, and goods function as public marks that raise fences against the unwanted, consolidating solidaritiesp. 251

PTA vida social se organiza por sinais de adesão e de exclusão, e bens funcionam como marcas públicas que erguem cercas contra indesejados, consolidando solidariedades

Staying out of reach of this system is not a subjective discomfort. It is a risk of practical silencing, reduction of voice and of future optionsp. 251

PTFicar fora do alcance desse sistema não é um desconforto subjetivo. É risco de silenciamento prático, redução de voz e de opções futuras

Douglas and Isherwood (1996, p. 43) formulate this by treating goods as ritual adjuncts and consumption as a ritual process whose function is to produce meaning in the unstable flow of eventsp. 254

PTDouglas e Isherwood (1996, p. 43) formulam isso ao tratar bens como adjuntos rituais e o consumo como processo ritual cuja função é produzir sentido no fluxo instável dos acontecimentos

[...] a vocabulary for describing the immaterial object of consumption as something whose social utility depends on social legibility and belonging within a managed ecosystem, which makes it possible to read the exit cost not only as patrimonial loss, but as a risk of isolation and weakening of bondsp. 249

PT[...] vocabulário para descrever o objeto imaterial de consumo como algo cuja utilidade social depende de legibilidade social e pertencimento no interior de um ecossistema administrado, o que permite ler o custo de saída não apenas como perda patrimonial, mas como risco de isolamento e enfraquecimento de vínculo

What does a piece of music teach about the value of a skin?

In a piece of music, a single note says almost nothing, because the music lies in the relationship between the notes. Something similar happens with the things we consume. An isolated skin says little, because its meaning comes from the place it occupies beside the others, from what is common and what is rare.

That is why, when the platform tinkers with the whole, it tinkers with the meaning of each piece. Launching a new skin can change what yours said, even if nobody touched it. Whoever looks after the shop window also looks after the language that makes your item mean something.

Where it comes from

The isolated item is semantically incomplete, since meaning emerges from the reciprocal position among goods, as happens in a grammar or in a musical harmony.p. 249

PTO item isolado é semanticamente incompleto, pois o sentido emerge da posição recíproca entre bens, como ocorre em uma gramática ou em uma harmonia musical.

Goods operate as the hardware and software of an informational system in which meaning emerges from the relationships between things, and not from an essence stuck to each objectp. 249

PTBens operam como hardware e software de um sistema informacional no qual o significado emerge das relações entre coisas, e não de uma essência grudada em cada objeto

Regulating curation and circulation windows amounts to regulating the grammar by which value and belonging are expressed, because it alters the relationships that make the collection “say” something.p. 249

PTRegular curadoria e janelas de circulação equivale a regular a gramática pela qual valor e pertencimento são expressos, porque altera as relações que fazem o acervo “dizer” algo.

What is delivered is not just an item. The conditions of language that make this item exist as a socially legible difference within this ecosystem are maintained, or reordered.p. 249

PTNão se entrega apenas um item. Mantêm-se, ou reordenam-se, as condições de linguagem que fazem esse item existir como diferença socialmente legível dentro desse ecossistema.

Anthropology of consumptionMeaning transfer

Where does the skin's meaning come from? It isn't born in the skin. It is poured into it. The trailer, the event, the shop window and the story around it link the skin to a mood, a type of person and a moment. Advertising works like a pipe through which meaning flows down into things.

And that meaning goes stale over time. Part of what a skin means expires, like food, and so renewal is not optional. There will always be a new collection, a new trailer and a new event to pour fresh meaning into things and take the shine off the old ones.

Where it comes from

Advertising functions as a conduit through which meaning is continuously poured into goodsp. 256

PTA publicidade funciona como conduto pelo qual o significado se despeja continuamente nos bens

Renewal is not optional because part of the meaning is perishable.p. 256

PTA renovação não é opcional porque parte do significado é perecível.

In them, the form of presentation and the dramaturgy of the offer, expressed in shop windows, trailers, events and narratives of permanent updating, becomes a constitutive component of the experience consumed.p. 99

PTNeles, a forma de apresentação e a dramaturgia da oferta, expressa em vitrines, trailers, eventos e narrativas de atualização permanente, torna-se componente constitutivo da experiência consumida.

Semiotics and languageSpectacle

The spectacle is not a pile of images but people relating to each other through images. In a match, your skin is seen, compared and commented on, and its value takes shape in that scene, which never stops.

This means the skin's promise does not sit still in the store, because it circulates as a social scene in which some people watch and others are watched. That permanent shop window is what keeps desire alive and keeps you wanting to stay.

Where it comes from

Debord formulates this perspective by stating that the spectacle is not a set of images, but a social relationship between people mediated by imagesp. 282

PTDebord formula tal perspectiva ao afirmar que o espetáculo não é conjunto de imagens, mas relação social entre pessoas mediada por imagens

Thus, the spectacle can be drawn on to understand platforms as environments in which the promise of the IOC circulates as a continuous social scene, that is, as a dynamic of visibility and comparison that stabilizes desire and permanence as part of value itselfp. 282

PTAssim, o espetáculo pode ser mobilizado para compreender plataformas como ambientes em que a promessa do OIC circula como cena social contínua, isto é, como dinâmica de visibilidade e de comparação que estabiliza desejo e permanência como parte do próprio valor

Semiotics and languageGamification

Level, rank, achievements, win counter. When everything becomes a scoreboard, the experience is no longer just lived but measured all the time, so what matters becomes ranking well and keeping up.

Then staying in the game stops being just leisure and becomes a chore. Staying means remaining on the leaderboard, up to date and visible on a scale that the game itself manages. And the platform turns this control of time into urgency, with windows and opportunities that push you to decide again and again.

Where it comes from

Practical life stops being merely lived and comes to be accompanied by indices of position, signals of performance and parameters of progress that make comparison permanent and cumulative.p. 289

PTA vida prática deixa de ser apenas vivida e passa a ser acompanhada por índices de posição, sinais de desempenho e parâmetros de progresso que tornam a comparação permanente e cumulativa.

In this regime, permanence takes the form of a task, because remaining comes to mean staying ranked, open to updating and legible within a scale that the ecosystem itself manages.p. 289

PTNesse regime, a permanência ganha forma de tarefa, porque permanecer passa a significar manter-se sob ranqueamento, passível de atualização e legível no interior de uma escala que o próprio ecossistema administra.

The gamification of everyday life, in this key, is not a playful device added to consumption, but the translation of value into scoreboards of relevance, in which what matters is to be positioned and updated in real time.p. 289

PTA gamificação da vida cotidiana, nessa chave, não é um artifício lúdico adicionado ao consumo, mas a tradução do valor em placares de relevância, nos quais o que importa é estar situado e atualizado em tempo real.

On this basis, it becomes possible to explain how the platform turns the governance of time and visibility into practical urgency, by organizing windows and opportunities as a recurring pressure to decidep. 289

PTCom essa base, torna-se possível explicar como a plataforma transforma a governança do tempo e da visibilidade em urgência prática, ao organizar janelas e oportunidades como pressão recorrente de decisão

Why does staying out of the game become costly?

Put all this together and a silent rule appears. If what you can lose is the right moment, status and belonging, being absent becomes costly. Disappear for a few weeks and you lose your position, miss the event and miss the skin that was only on sale in that window.

Absence stops being a pause and becomes a measurable fall. And the risk lies not only in the item, but in the meaning of what is lost and in the calendar, which turns delay into disadvantage, even when the skin is still there, technically accessible.

Where it comes from

When the efficient risk is losing timing, status and belonging, the discipline of presence sets in as a technique for governing time. Absence stops being mere interruption and comes to produce measurable decline, progressive erasure and practical discontinuity, making permanence the rational way to avoid loss.p. 254

PTQuando o risco eficiente é perder timing, status e pertencimento, a disciplina de presença se instala como técnica de governo do tempo. A ausência deixa de ser mera interrupção e passa a produzir queda mensurável, apagamento progressivo e descontinuidade prática, tornando a permanência a forma racional de evitar perda.

It is semiotic because it depends on the social meaning of what is lost, and temporal because it works through windows, calendars, cycles and rhythms that convert delay into objective disadvantage even when the immaterial object remains technically accessible.p. 254

PTÉ semiótico porque depende do sentido social do que se perde, e temporal porque funciona por janelas, calendários, ciclos e ritmos que convertem atraso em desvantagem objetiva mesmo quando o objeto imaterial permanece tecnicamente acessível.

Political economyAttention economy

What is scarce in this market is not skins, since a digital copy never runs out, and so, left to itself, the price would tend to fall. To charge for it, the company has to create scarcity by its own decision.

What actually runs out is your screen time. The longer you stay, the more the platform can observe how you behave, test stimuli and adjust the triggers that make you come back. It is this predictability that sustains the pass, the subscription, the can't-miss event and the offer that rotates every week.

Where it comes from

The decisive economic point is simple and direct, since, in the market for immaterial goods, reproducibility would tend to dissolve the price, so that income depends on reintroducing scarcity by institutional decisionp. 234

PTO ponto econômico decisivo é simples e direto, pois, no mercado de bens imateriais, a reprodutibilidade tenderia a dissolver o preço, de modo que a renda depende de reintroduzir escassez por decisão institucional

The decisive scarce resource is not the technical availability of the good, but the user's exposure time. Retention provides volume for observation, profiling, continuous testing of stimuli and calibration of triggers. It is this predictability that stabilizes extraction by subscription, pass, “can't-miss event”, offer rotation and successive tolls.p. 249

PTO recurso escasso decisivo não é a disponibilidade técnica do bem, mas o tempo de exposição do usuário. A retenção fornece volume para observação, perfilização, teste contínuo de estímulos e calibragem de gatilhos. É essa previsibilidade que estabiliza extração por assinatura, passe, “evento imperdível”, rotatividade de oferta e pedágios sucessivos.

Anthropology of consumptionBiography of things

Things have biographies too, and buying something does not define what it will become later. A skin is born rare, becomes common in a relaunch, leaves the catalog and comes back for an event, and each turn changes what it is worth and the status of whoever owns it. All this happens without it ever disappearing from your account.

The platform writes this biography. It retires items, changes their rarity, restricts how they can be obtained, repositions them around events and reorganizes what gets featured. Your name stays linked to the skin, but its story, which is what sustains the prestige and the promise, has been rewritten by someone else.

Where it comes from

The fact that an object is bought or exchanged says nothing about its later statusp. 253

PTO fato de um objeto ser comprado ou trocado nada diz sobre seu estatuto posterior

For the immaterial object of consumption, this describes precisely why reworks, relaunches, trivializations and repositionings alter value and status without the item “disappearing”.p. 253

PTPara o objeto imaterial de consumo, isso descreve com precisão por que reworks, relançamentos, banalizações e reposicionamentos alteram valor e status sem que o item “desapareça”.

On platforms, this singularization takes the form of catalog retirement, change of rarity, restrictions on how items can be obtained, repositioning by event, recoding by meta and rearrangement of display criteria. The user's nominal ownership is maintained, but the cultural biography of the item is materially reordered, which is what sustains social utility, prestige and equivalence between promise and delivery.p. 254

PTEm plataformas, essa singularização assume forma de aposentadoria de catálogo, mudança de raridade, restrição de obtenção, reposicionamento por evento, recodificação por meta e rearranjo de critérios de exibição. Mantém-se a titularidade nominal do usuário, mas reordena-se materialmente a biografia cultural do item, que é o que sustenta utilidade social, prestígio e equivalência entre promessa e entrega.

How is a rarity manufactured?

Rarity can be manufactured. In one online game, the company created a new shelf above the top of the catalog, for skins it called Exalted, and presented them as hyper-exclusive, almost luxury goods.

To try for one, you do not buy the skin, but tokens that cost 400 of the game's currency and are worth one draw. The chance of getting the skin is very low, the guarantee only comes on the eightieth draw and everything is on sale for a limited time. This item-draw model, which imitates capsule machines, usually hides the real cost of the purchase behind the intermediate currency and odds that are hard to see.

What stands out is where the exclusivity comes from. It is not something the community comes to recognize over time, because it is a rule written into the system. The prestige comes from the rule that controls access, and not from the skin necessarily delivering more than the others.

Where it comes from

A contemporary example of how signs of delivery can be artificially reordered to sustain value, commensurability and prestige is the creation of the Exalted tier and of the acquisition space called Sanctum, presented as a shelf “above” the traditional top of the catalogp. 298

PTUm exemplo contemporâneo de como signos de entrega podem ser artificialmente reordenados para sustentar valor, comensurabilidade e prestígio é a criação do tier Exalted e do espaço de aquisição denominado Sanctum, apresentado como prateleira “acima” do topo tradicional do catálogo

The institutional justification frames Exalted as a set of “hyper-exclusive skins”, that is, goods explicitly described as luxury within the catalogp. 299

PTA justificativa institucional enquadra Exalted como conjunto de “hyper-exclusive skins”, isto é, bens explicitamente descritos como luxo dentro do catálogo

The Sanctum is described as an exclusive store in which the user buys Ancient Sparksp. 299

PTO Sanctum é descrito como uma loja exclusiva em que o usuário compra Ancient Sparks

for 400 RP and uses them to draw rare content, with a specific “banner” for Exalted skins, along with time rotation and no guarantee of return Riot Games(Riot Games, 2025d).p. 299

PTpor 400 RP e as utiliza para sortear conteúdos raros, com “bandeira” específica para skins Exaltadas, além de rotação temporal e ausência de garantia de retorno (Riot Games, 2025d) .

The official description of the Sanctum records that Exalted skins are the only Tier S content of the corresponding banner, with an extremely low chance and a guarantee only on the eightieth draw, as well as availability for a limited timep. 299

PTA descrição oficial do Sanctum registra que skins Exaltadas são o único conteúdo de Tier S da bandeira correspondente, com chance extremamente baixa e garantia apenas no octogésimo sorteio, além de disponibilidade por tempo limitado

A monetization model based on random draws of virtual items, inspired by capsule vending machines (gashapon).p. 299

PTModelo de monetização baseado em sorteios aleatórios de itens virtuais, inspirado em máquinas de venda de cápsulas (gashapon).

From the perspective of IOCs, it represents an extraction architecture that conditions the enjoyment of desired goods on probabilistic mechanisms and successive draws, often hiding the real cost of acquisition through intermediate currencies and statistical opacityp. 299

PTSob a perspectiva dos OIC, representa arquitetura de extração que condiciona a fruição de bens desejados a mecanismos probabilísticos e sorteios sucessivos, muitas vezes ocultando o custo real da aquisição por meio de moedas intermediárias e opacidade estatística

The consequence is that exclusivity stops being a social inference and becomes architecture. Prestige is produced by rule, not by necessary material backing.p. 299

PTA consequência é que a exclusividade deixa de ser inferência social e passa a ser arquitetura. O prestígio é produzido por regra, não por lastro material necessário.

Semiotics and languageSimulacrum

And what if the skin changes inside but still looks top of the line? This can happen, because value can be maintained by signs of delivery, such as a badge, a rarity frame or a featured place in the catalog. As long as the signs are still there, the promise seems kept and the circuit keeps turning.

The risk lies in what these signs hide. The platform can keep up the appearance of continuity while changing effects, restrictions, forms of access and performance. With the facade unchanged, it becomes very hard to prove that anything was lost. So the problem is not that the skin has symbolic value, but that appearance serves to erase the proof that the promise is no longer being kept.

Where it comes from

[...] in the consumer society, the socially valued promise tends to require measurement and proof through objects and signs [...] With this, it becomes possible to sustain, without reducing the argument to a denunciation of illusion, that value can be maintained by signs of delivery and by visible criteria of equivalence, which authorizes treating the simulacrum as a mechanism capable of preserving the economic circuit of the IOC even when the relevant enjoyment changesp. 296

PT[...] na sociedade de consumo, a promessa socialmente valorizada tende a exigir mensuração e prova por objetos e signos [...] Com isso, torna-se possível sustentar, sem reduzir o argumento a denúncia de ilusão, que o valor pode ser mantido por sinais de entrega e por critérios de equivalência visíveis, o que autoriza tratar o simulacro como mecanismo capaz de preservar o circuito econômico do OIC mesmo quando a fruição relevante se altera

When equivalence is stabilized by signs of delivery, such as label, class, rarity frame, displayable position and internal commensurability, public continuity can function as a substitute for the preservation of the relevant enjoymentp. 313

PTQuando a equivalência se estabiliza por sinais de entrega, como etiqueta, classe, moldura de raridade, posição exibível e comensurabilidade interna, a continuidade pública pode funcionar como substituto da preservação da fruição relevante

[...] public continuity can function as a substitute for the preservation of the relevant enjoyment, so that restoring comes to mean preserving recognizable symptoms of persistence, even if restrictions, effects, modes of access and performance conditions that constituted the acquired position are altered from withinp. 313

PT[...] a continuidade pública pode funcionar como substituto da preservação da fruição relevante, de modo que recompor passa a significar conservar sintomas reconhecíveis de persistência, ainda que se alterem, por dentro, restrições, efeitos, modos de acesso e condições de performance que constituíam a posição adquirida

The legal problem is not the symbolic, but the practical neutralization of the proof of the link between promise and enjoyment, which requires criteria of traceability and substantive control of equivalence.p. 313

PTO problema jurídico não é o simbólico, mas a neutralização prática da prova do nexo entre promessa e fruição, o que exige critérios de rastreabilidade e controle substantivo da equivalência.

Hypercuratorial anticipation

And there is what has not even arrived yet. The platform announces the next event, the skin that will come back, the one that will disappear and the next season. It releases short, cryptic trailers, shows the plan for the year and sets dates. The future stops being only your expectation and becomes a calendar that the company organizes and sells.

The present thus becomes a waiting room. You stay on standby for the next cycle, the next update and the next opportunity. And the waiting becomes part of what you consume, as if the announcement were already a piece of the delivery. The platform sells not just the now but also your staying on, waiting for what comes next.

Where it comes from

Hypercuratorial anticipation designates an economic-semiological technique by which the platform produces a consumable future through continuous curation, defining what will come, what will disappear, what will return and what will be raised to the status of “event”.p. 313

PTAntecipação hipercuratorial designa uma técnica econômico-semiológica pela qual a plataforma produz futuro consumível por curadoria contínua, definindo o que virá, o que desaparecerá, o que retornará e o que será elevado ao estatuto de “evento”.

The consumer is kept in continuous readiness for “the next cycle”, “the next update”, “the next opportunity”p. 314

PTO consumidor é mantido em prontidão contínua para “o próximo ciclo”, “a próxima atualização”, “a próxima oportunidade”

The present stops being the place of a stabilized delivery and becomes a governed antechamberp. 314

PTO presente deixa de ser o lugar de uma entrega estabilizada e torna-se uma antecâmara governada

Thus, the platform does not sell only the “now”, since it also sells permanence oriented to the “yet to come”, converting expectation into an asset and transforming waiting into a material component of enjoyment.p. 314

PTAssim, a plataforma não vende apenas o “agora”, posto que ela também vende a permanência orientada ao “por vir”, convertendo expectativa em ativo e transformando a espera em componente material da fruição.

Instead of the future appearing as a simple expectation of the consumer, it is produced as an institutional calendar and as a repertoire of signs, with release cuts, schedule forecasts, teasers and updates that become, in practice, part of what sustains permanence and decision.p. 314

PTEm vez de o futuro aparecer como simples expectativa do consumidor, ele é produzido como calendário institucional e como repertório de sinais, com recortes de lançamento, previsões de cronograma, teasers e atualizações que se tornam, na prática, parte daquilo que sustenta permanência e decisão.

So is desiring a skin a mistake?

None of this means that desiring a skin is a mistake, or that every ad, every draw or every pretty shop window is illegal. Selling, persuading and running a game are not abuses in themselves. What matters here is something else. Because the skin's value is made of signs, and because the platform controls those signs, it can tinker with the value of what you bought without touching the item. That is what needs to be visible and comparable, and something you can demand.

Where it comes from

This is not about investigating the subjective psychological intention of business agents, nor about moralizing every form of monetization, persuasion or private governancep. 732

PTNão se trata de investigar intenção psicológica subjetiva de agentes empresariais, nem de moralizar toda forma de monetização, persuasão ou governança privada

What matters for the argument of the simulacrum is not the moralization of gachap. 299

PTO que importa para o argumento do simulacro não é a moralização do gacha

The legal contribution of the simulacrum, therefore, is not to treat consumption as illusion, but to make nameable the practical displacement of the criterion of performance.p. 313

PTA contribuição jurídica do simulacro, portanto, não é tratar o consumo como ilusão, mas tornar nomeável o deslocamento prático do critério de adimplemento.

What can you demand?

What was promised has to be deliveredWhat was promised has to be delivered

When does advertising become part of the offer?

When the purchase comes after careful preparation. It may be a countdown, a promise of exclusivity, a warning that it will run out, a status signal or the suggestion that one item is worth as much as another. In these cases, advertising stops being just advertising and becomes part of the offer itself.

What does the announcement oblige the platform to deliver?

The platform must make what was advertised match what is delivered. The promise, the advertised value and the costs that did not show up have to match the experience you get, including after the platform changes the game.

How do you compare the promise with what was delivered?

It helps to show what the sales environment was like, what advertising there was, what was highlighted and how the item was presented. It also helps to compare what happened afterwards with what was promised.

What do you ask for when the promise is not kept?

You can ask for the offer to be corrected and honored, for what you lost to be restored and for the misleading communication to stop. In some cases, you can ask for sales strategies that are incompatible with what is really delivered to be struck down. Depending on the case, the law can treat this as misleading or abusive advertising, or as an offer that binds whoever advertised it.

Is pushy advertising a problem in itself?

What counts is not whether the advertising is lively or pushy. It is the relationship between what was presented before and what you were able to use afterwards.
