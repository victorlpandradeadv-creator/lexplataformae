2.7 On the limitations of the research and its potential for generalization

See this section in the PDF

This section in the layers of the site

  • Method · The protocol

p. 78

The methodological design centers on a paradigmatic case, the Riot Games ecosystem with League of Legends, in its desktop and mobile (“Wild Rift”) versions, within a defined time frame and relying on public and documentary sources, so as to build a verifiable p. 79 corpus. This choice limits the mapping of variations by type of platform, by national market and by technological generation, and is not aimed at producing statistical inferences from a representative sample. The extended case study works with a different logic, since it favors the case's contribution to theoretical reconstruction and to the identification of mechanisms, rather than its quantitative representativeness. Consequently, the kind of generalization sought is analytical, not sample-based.

These limits are addressed through a disciplined scope and through triangulation. Reliance on public materials is accepted as a condition for the external verifiability of the path, with explicit criteria for inclusion in and exclusion from the research corpus, with the recording and preservation of the material actually used and with cross-references to organizational appendices. The researcher's position in the field does not operate as a criterion of proof, but as a sensitivity that detects reading hypotheses, which remain subject to testing against institutional documentation and traceable records, so as to prevent frictions and controversies from being treated as anecdote.

The work's potential for generalization derives, first, from what the instrumental empirical laboratory makes observable, not from anything “typical” or “individual” about the case, and it yields analytical generalization through mechanisms and relevant frictions. For this reason, this work does not offer a reflection on League of Legends as an isolated cultural object, but on the regime of the immaterial object of consumption it contains and on the techniques of private governance used by the continuous digital services that sustain it, reconstructed with the support of an ecosystem that lays bare dependence, modulation of access, internal sanctions, an economy of virtual items and the semiotic management of enjoyment.

Second, the transposability of the proposed regime is treated as a complementary methodological stage, through illustrative applications and tests of fit of the keys developed, in typical contemporary situations of continuous digital consumption, designed to make recurring functional equivalences and evidentiary requirements visible without turning the path into an extended case study or an exhaustive empirical demonstration.

In this key, the conclusions are transposable when other environments share equivalent structural mechanisms, such as games under the games-as-a-service model, marketplace and gig economy platforms marked by layers of reputation and conditional access, and account-based content-mediation and artificial intelligence services with unilateral rules and continuous adjustments to the offer. Transposition does not proceed by superficial analogy, but by identifying functional equivalences between governance architectures, forms of p. 80 enforcement and patterns of friction, which makes it possible to carry the IOC regime over to other cases without turning the research into a sectoral thesis on a single product.

In keeping with this discipline of methodological transparency, it should also be noted that, throughout the writing and revision, artificial intelligence systems were used as support tools for organizing the text, comparing formulations and detecting inconsistencies in the argument. The use of these tools does not replace research, does not alter the criterion of proof and does not produce interpretive authority. Their function is to assist in revising the text and controlling its quality. Substantive decisions, including scope, selection of sources, legal qualification, structure of the argument and final wording, remain entirely the author's responsibility.
