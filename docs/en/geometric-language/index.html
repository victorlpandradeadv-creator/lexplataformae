How to read the geometric language

The shapes on this page are conventions of explanation, and not a geometric classification proposed by the thesis. Start with a distinction. The dimension of an object, the scope of an exclusivity and the control exercised by the platform are different things.

The four shapes

Recognizing a shape does not allow the conclusion that legal protection exists. Whether a right applies depends on the case and its requirements.

Lines that say different things

Double outline
Scope of the exclusivity examined in that example.
Dashed boundary
Contractual or technical control by the platform.
Wide band without a tip
Use or functional outcome. A gap indicates interruption.
Thin arrow with a tip
Relation of identification of origin.
Page background
Neutral space. It does not automatically mean the public domain.

An example of reading

The cube keeps its double outline. The platform's dashed boundary crosses the band of use, which appears interrupted. The image distinguishes the protection of the object from the control over its use.
