2.3 On the reference ecosystem and exemplification strategy

See this section in the PDF

p. 56

Choosing a specific ecosystem as the axis of observation is neither merely illustrative nor meant to serve as the foundation for an autonomous empirical section. It is a strategy of intelligibility, in which a paradigmatic case is used to make visible, more sharply than in other contexts, the mechanisms that structure the legal life of continuous digital consumption. When technical architecture, formal terms and enforcement routines are tightly coupled, the case stops being an “example” and begins to operate as a field of visibility, because it makes it possible to follow the passage between declared norm, norm in operation and disputed norm, without relying on abstract inferences or hasty generalizations.

In this key, the intended generalization is not representative, as if the case had to mirror a universe statistically. It is analytical and mechanism-oriented, insofar as the focus is on patterns of private governance, control devices and ways of distributing risk that recur, with variations, across different platforms and business models. The relevance of the case, therefore, does not come from any supposed exceptionality but from the fact that it brings together, in a single arrangement, elements that help explain how service promises become stabilized, how expectations of value are modulated and how unilateral changes capable of affecting the consumer's experience are carried out.

This ecosystem was selected not out of thematic preference but on criteria of analytical functionality. First, it is a continuous digital service whose experience is mediated by rules and updates, so that the “product” appears not as a one-off delivery but as a prolonged, calibrated and revisable relationship. Second, the economic dynamic that sustains it is organized around digital objects and intangible performances which, even when perceived as consumer goods, depend entirely on a private technical and normative apparatus to exist, circulate and produce functional value. Third, governance is highly dense, since the platform not only offers and monetizes but also disciplines conduct, defines internal metrics, redistributes incentives, applies sanctions and periodically reorganizes the very environment in which the promise of experience is fulfilled, thereby consolidating the consumer's legitimate expectation.

p. 57 Moreover, the ecosystem makes the structural mutability of digital consumption particularly visible, since substantial changes can occur without a change of ownership, without a new explicit contract and often without the average user properly understanding the practical scope of the change. This mutability is not incidental but constitutive, which is why the case is useful for observing how legitimate expectations are reconfigured, which mechanisms sustain perceived continuity despite material change and how risk shifts to the consumer when functional value equivalence comes to depend on unilateral decisions that are not very transparent. Finally, it is an environment with enough public traces and documentable material to allow referenced anchoring, with minimal triangulation between normative texts, institutional communication and enforcement practices, reducing the risk that the analysis depends on subjective impression or personal experience.

Drawing on the ecosystem, however, does not mean treating it as an exhaustively described empirical object or as a universe to be inventoried in all its social, cultural or economic complexity. Recourse to the case is limited and instrumental, because it serves to anchor inferences and to make concrete certain mechanisms whose abstract formulation could sound overly generic. For this reason, references to the ecosystem will be used as vignettes and points of support, invoked only when needed to stabilize the meaning of a category, illustrate a typical governance arrangement or show how a formal rule turns into enforcement practice. The aim is not to reconstruct the history of the sector, compare competing models or offer a representative overview of the industry, but to use a field of visibility capable of giving density to the theoretical and dogmatic inquiry.

This delimitation also keeps the analysis from being mistaken for ethnography in the classical sense, for experiential narrative or for “proof” based on familiarity with the environment. The presence of the case does not replace the conceptual chain, nor does it license conclusions on mere plausibility. On the contrary, it works as a technique of argumentative control, steering the text toward observable facts, documented rules and identifiable practices whenever the thesis must show the passage between a normative statement and its concrete operation. The case, therefore, neither drives the thesis nor defines its reach, since it acts as methodological support so that the proposed architecture can be understood, put under tension and, when necessary, verified in a setting where the mechanisms appear clearly enough to justify analytical generalization.

For this use to be intellectually controllable, one must distinguish the layers of normativity that coexist in platform ecosystems and that, in practice, produce p. 58 different effects on the consumer. First, there is a declared normativity, presented in the form of terms, policies, rules and institutional announcements, through which the platform describes the service, sets conditions of access, establishes rights and duties and announces, when it chooses to, the limits of the promised experience. Second, there is a normativity in operation, which cannot be reduced to the formal text and shows itself in the technical architecture, in interface flows, in enforcement routines, in the mechanisms that modulate the experience and in the concrete forms of oversight and sanction. This layer is decisive, because it is here that the contractual promise takes shape, and it is also here that a significant part of the informational asymmetry typical of continuous digital consumption is produced.

There is also a disputed normativity, which emerges when the two previous layers come into friction, whether because enforcement reveals consequences the text did not make clear, because the text serves as an alibi for practices perceived as abusive, or because unilateral changes shift the balance of the service without the consumer being able to recognize precisely what was changed and with what effects. This is the arena of conflicts, complaints, public debates and administrative interventions, which not only describe the problem but also expose the platform's strategies of legitimation, the consumer's repertoires of contestation and the limits of legally translating experiences mediated by design and private governance. Delimiting these arenas is not a classificatory whim. It is a way of keeping the analysis from confusing statement with enforcement, promise with delivery or controversy with mechanism, and of ensuring that each step of the argument rests on the appropriate layer of the phenomenon under examination.

In the work as a whole, the selected ecosystem plays a supporting role, not a leading one. It serves as a point of anchoring so that the categories and distinctions developed in the following sections can be understood without relying on artificial examples or on abstractions remote from the experience of consumption. Thus, when the thesis moves on to describe the economic and technological structures that sustain platformization, and to analyze the semiotic and institutional mechanisms that modulate the perception of value and the expectation of continuity, the case comes in as a controlled reference, only to the extent that it helps stabilize the meaning of the argument and make verifiable the passage between dogmatic formulation and concrete functioning.

This use also gives the inquiry unity, because it allows the same field of visibility to be revisited at strategic moments without turning the thesis into a closed case study. The ecosystem thus works as a thread stitched through the text, lending consistency to the inferences and allowing the reader to recognize the same architecture p. 59 of mechanisms from different angles as the questions and theoretical instruments change. The thesis, therefore, is not derived from the case but uses it to support an argument meant to reach other platforms and other arrangements of digital consumption, precisely because the goal is not the singularity of one environment but the intelligibility of structures that recur, with variations, across different markets and sectors.

To keep familiarity with the ecosystem from being mistaken for a criterion of truth, the analysis adopts a standard of control that separates guiding the gaze from validating the argument. Prior experience can help to identify points of friction, to notice changes that tend to go unnoticed and to formulate hypotheses about how certain mechanisms affect the consumer. However, these hypotheses do not serve as premises, nor is subjective impression treated as evidence. Whenever the case is invoked, the argument rests on traceable elements, such as normative texts, institutional communications, identifiable enforcement routines, observable sanction practices and public records that allow minimal triangulation, so that the reader can follow the chain of inference without having to rely on personal testimony. When the text draws on descriptions of how things work that are widespread in the sector, these serve as context and not as proof of a specific occurrence, and legal attribution remains conditional on traceable elements whenever the argument depends on it.

This discipline also seeks to reduce selection and confirmation biases, since platform ecosystems generate readings skewed by salient episodes, isolated controversies or highly polarized interpretive communities. To keep the analysis from being captured by these dynamics, references to the case will be built sparingly, with attention to the distinction between declared rule, enforced rule and public controversy, and with an explicit commitment not to draw conclusions beyond what the material warrants. Familiarity thus works not as a rhetorical shortcut but as a tool that helps choose better what to observe, while the argument's support remains subject to criteria of verifiability and of internal coherence with the theoretical-dogmatic apparatus that structures the thesis.
