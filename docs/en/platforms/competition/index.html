Platforms

Extension · 7.2.4 · pp. 769 to 773

Competition under the law of the platformOn competition under the Lex Plataformae

The platform that governs enjoyment also governs the perceptible conditions of competition. The regime does not replace competition law, but it provides the entry point for controlling this governance.

Read in the full text, with the notes

Why does competition enter the consumer regime?

The platform that administers visibility, access, comparability and intelligibility also organizes the conditions under which agents compete for attention and preference. In the reading of Daniel Firmato de Almeida Glória, competition protection and consumer protection are instruments of a transparent market, and freedom of choice carries information as a principle. For him, the existence of options does not, in itself, mean real freedom for the consumer. The illusion of plurality can coexist with a severe compression of the real conditions of choice.

The platform does not need to formally expel a competitor in order to restrict its competitiveness.p. 770

PTA plataforma não precisa expulsar formalmente um concorrente para restringir sua competitividade.

Where it comes from

[...] such freedom carries with it information as a guiding principle, because whoever does not know is not truly free to choosep. 770

PT[...] tal liberdade traz consigo a informação como princípio balizador, porque quem não conhece não é verdadeiramente livre para escolher

Glória observes that the existence of options to choose from does not, in itself, mean real freedom for the consumer, especially when products, services and contractual clauses present themselves under a homogeneity capable of obscuring effectively distinct alternativesp. 770

PTGlória observa que a existência de possibilidades de opção não significa, por si, liberdade real do consumidor, especialmente quando produtos, serviços e cláusulas contratuais se apresentam sob homogeneidade capaz de obscurecer alternativas efetivamente distintas

This connection finds solid grounding in the reading of Daniel Firmato de Almeida Glória, for whom competition protection and consumer protection are not autonomous or rival endsp. 769

PTEssa aproximação encontra fundamento sólido na leitura de Daniel Firmato de Almeida Glória, para quem a defesa da concorrência e a defesa do consumidor não constituem fins autônomos ou rivais

The illusion of plurality can thus coexist with a severe compression of the real conditions of choice.p. 770

PTA ilusão de pluralidade pode, assim, conviver com severa compressão das condições reais de escolha.

It is precisely for this reason that the competition reading must reject the so-called myth of competitionp. 770

PTÉ justamente por isso que a leitura concorrencial precisa recusar o chamado mito da concorrência

How does the platform distort competition without excluding anyone?

Internal preferencing, self-preferencing, informational barriers, capture of competitive intelligence and opaque discrimination do not necessarily depend on overt exclusion. They can operate through micro-advantages produced by the continuous administration of visibility. The platform defines or conditions which competitors will be visible, on what terms they will be compared and what data will be extracted from their activity.

Where it comes from

In markets of this nature, internal preferencing, self-preferencing, informational barriers, capture of competitive intelligence and opaque discrimination do not necessarily depend on overt acts of exclusion.p. 770

PTEm mercados dessa natureza, preferenciamento interno, autoprivilegiamento, barreiras informacionais, captura de inteligência competitiva e discriminação opaca não dependem necessariamente de atos ostensivos de exclusão.

It defines, or at least strongly conditions, which competitors will be visible, on what terms they will be comparedp. 771

PTEla define, ou pelo menos condiciona fortemente, quais concorrentes serão visíveis, em que termos serão comparados

What can be demanded?

The regime does not replace competition law, and not all interface governance is a competition offense. It provides the entry point for recognizing when the architecture of the consumer's experience is already the architecture of competitive advantage. Whenever the platform, in organizing enjoyment, also comes to organize the perceptible conditions of competition in an opaque and asymmetric way, there must be, at a minimum, auditability of ranking criteria, intelligibility about highlighting and preferencing, control of alterations that distort competitive permanence and a non-naive reading of the use of data obtained through intermediation.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform, in organizing enjoyment, also comes to organize in an opaque and asymmetric manner the perceptible conditions of competition within the ecosystem, there must be a requirement of controllability compatible with the dual dimension of the problem.p. 773

PTSempre que a plataforma, ao organizar a fruição, passar também a organizar de maneira opaca e assimétrica as condições perceptíveis de competição no interior do ecossistema, deverá haver exigência de controlabilidade compatível com a dupla dimensão do problema.

Seen this way, the IOC regime does not replace competition law.p. 771

PTNessa chave, o regime do OIC não substitui o direito concorrencial.

The possible institutional consequence is not to assert that every intermediation problem is automatically resolved by the CDC, nor that every interface governance constitutes, in itself, a competition offense.p. 773

PTA consequência institucional possível não é afirmar que todo problema de intermediação se resolve automaticamente pelo CDC, nem que toda governança de interface configura, por si, ilícito concorrencial.

This means, at a minimum, minimal auditability of relevant ranking criteria, intelligibility about highlighting and preferencing practices, control of alterations that distort the functional equivalence of competitive permanence and a non-naive reading of the use of data obtained through the intermediation position itself.p. 773

PTIsso significa, no mínimo, auditabilidade mínima sobre critérios relevantes de hierarquização, inteligibilidade sobre práticas de destaque e preferenciamento, controle de alterações que deformem a equivalência funcional da permanência concorrencial e leitura não ingênua do uso de dados obtidos pela própria posição de intermediação.
