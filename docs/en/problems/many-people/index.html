Your rights · By problem

It happened to a lot of people. Can it be solved all at once?

The same problem hit a lot of people on the same service, and each person was left alone with it, thinking it was bad luck. When the loss arises from the very design of the service, the response can be collective and change how it works for everyone, but it is not enough that many people complain about the same thing.

Where it comes from

In platform environments, the harmful source may lie in the very design of the supply regime, so that the unity of the problem does not stem primarily from the empirical repetition of episodes, but from the permanence of a single, repeatable and functionally expansive structure producing loss, opacity and asymmetry. The injury, then, is not only plural. It is architectural.p. 774

PTEm ambientes de plataforma, a fonte lesiva pode residir no próprio desenho do regime de fornecimento, de modo que a unidade do problema não decorre primariamente da repetição empírica de episódios, mas da permanência de uma estrutura única, reiterável e funcionalmente expansiva de produção de perda, opacidade e assimetria. A lesão, então, não é apenas plural. É arquitetural.

How do you know whether the problem lies in the design rather than in each case?

The sign is a single origin that recurs and is part of the normal way the service works. The cases do not need to be identical, because what repeats is the source of the problem.

The ranking is the order in which the platform shows offers or people. Some examples are an unclear punishment policy, changes that leave no trace and a ranking that decides everything without any usable history. Also included are the screen that hides the essential amid too much information and the customer service that absorbs the complaint before it becomes a dispute.

The effects are not the same for everyone, but the origin is common. That is why the difficulty of proving it, when everyone runs into it, may be part of the way the service works and not your own failure.

Where it comes from

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

The typology reveals precisely that certain harms are programmatic. Not because all cases are the same, but because the source of their production remains structurally the same.p. 776

PTA tipologia revela justamente que certos danos são programáticos. Não porque todos os casos sejam iguais, mas porque a fonte de sua produção permanece estruturalmente a mesma.

An opaque sanctions policy, a non-auditable regime of functional change, an interface logic that dilutes the essential under informational excess, a materially decisive ranking system without usable history or a trust architecture that absorbs and neutralizes contestation before it becomes litigation are all examples of problems whose unity lies in the design, not in the episode.p. 775

PTUma política opaca de sanções, um regime não auditável de alteração funcional, uma lógica de interface que dilui o essencial sob excesso informacional, um sistema de ranqueamento materialmente decisivo sem histórico aproveitável ou uma arquitetura de confiança que absorve e neutraliza contestação antes que ela se torne litígio são todos exemplos de problemas cuja unidade está no desenho, não no episódio.

The impact is not homogeneous in all its effects, but it is common in its matrix.p. 776

PTO atingimento não é homogêneo em todos os seus efeitos, mas é comum em sua matriz.

A merely atomized treatment of the conflict tends to produce two bad results. On the one hand, it monetizes episodes without altering the machine that reproduces them. On the other, it turns the systemic difficulty of proof into the private failure of dispersed consumers, each obliged to face alone an informational regime designed precisely to hinder reconstruction.p. 776

PTO tratamento apenas atomizado do conflito tende a produzir dois resultados ruins. De um lado, monetiza episódios sem alterar a máquina que os reproduz. De outro, transforma a dificuldade sistêmica de prova em fracasso privado de consumidores dispersos, cada qual obrigado a enfrentar sozinho um regime informacional desenhado precisamente para dificultar reconstrução.

What can each person keep to help show the pattern?

Your case still matters, and it is worth keeping the dated screens, the company's notices and the messages exchanged with it. It also helps to show that the same thing repeats and how the pieces of the service combine to produce the loss.

Reports from other users are not the main proof of a problem. Even so, they can help show a long experience that keeps recurring and a policy that affects many people.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

The minimum required proof consists of demonstrating the pattern, its replicability, the coordination between mechanisms of the ecosystem and the relevant impact produced on practical autonomy, intelligibility or utility of the experience.p. 746-747

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste na demonstração do padrão, de sua replicabilidade, da coordenação entre mecanismos do ecossistema e do impacto relevante produzido sobre autonomia prática, inteligibilidade ou utilidade da experiência.

Community reports are not the central proof of an unlawful act, but they are useful to formulate and illustrate a long-lasting, even if hypothetical, consumer experience in which one glimpses the restriction and sedimentation of a broad punitive policy over the Brazilian basep. 611

PTOs relatos comunitários não são a prova central de um ilícito, mas têm utilidade para formular e ilustrar uma, ainda que hipotética, experiência consumerista de longa duração na qual se vislumbra restrição e sedimentação de uma política punitiva ampla sobre a base brasileira

What can a collective response ask for that a single case cannot?

The collective response can ask for measures that stop the loss from continuing and that make the service checkable from then on. These include keeping minimum records, giving a checkable reason for relevant punishments, maintaining an accessible history of changes and explaining the minimum criteria for distribution, priority and ranking.

Where it comes from

Collective redress thus proves particularly suited to imposing injunctive, procedural and analytical measures capable of reintroducing institutional verifiability into the environment. This may involve duties to preserve minimum records, a requirement of verifiable reasons for materially relevant sanctions, maintenance of an accessible history of relevant changes, explanation of minimum operational criteria of distribution, priority or ranking, and other measures that are aimed not only at repairing past harms, but at reorganizing the future conditions of controllability of enjoyment.p. 775

PTA tutela coletiva mostra-se, então, particularmente vocacionada a impor medidas inibitórias, procedimentais e analíticas aptas a reintroduzir verificabilidade institucional no ambiente. Isso pode envolver deveres de preservação de registros mínimos, exigência de motivação verificável para sanções materialmente relevantes, manutenção de histórico acessível de alterações relevantes, explicitação de critérios operacionais mínimos de distribuição, prioridade ou ranqueamento, e outras providências que não se dirigem apenas a reparar danos pretéritos, mas a reorganizar as condições futuras de controlabilidade da fruição.

Who can pursue the problem on everyone's behalf?

Those who act on behalf of everyone are the parties with collective standing and the public bodies for consumer protection and oversight. Parties with collective standing are the institutions the law authorizes to defend an entire group. They include the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Public Defender's Office and consumer protection bodies such as Procon. When the problem lies precisely in the lack of records and clarity, that lack can become the very object of the collective claim. The debate then moves from isolated episodes to the way the service is built.

Where it comes from

It is at this point that open auditability turns from a dogmatic standard into a condition for the effectiveness of collective redress. Without it, the action of collective standing parties, administrative protection bodies and regulatory and oversight structures tends to run into the same opacity that already hinders individual reaction. With it, it becomes possible to turn the requirement of verifiability into the main object of protection, shifting the debate from isolated episodes to the shaping of the environment.p. 776

PTÉ nesse ponto que a auditabilidade aberta se converte de standard dogmático em condição de efetividade da tutela coletiva. Sem ela, a atuação de legitimados coletivos, órgãos administrativos de proteção e estruturas de regulação e fiscalização tende a esbarrar na mesma opacidade que já dificulta a reação individual. Com ela, torna-se possível transformar a exigência de verificabilidade em objeto principal da tutela, deslocando o debate de episódios isolados para a conformação do ambiente.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When is a large number of affected people not enough for a collective response?

It is not enough that many people complain about similar facts, because the number alone does not yet show a common origin in the design of the service.

The collective response also does not replace the claim of someone who lost something concrete. It targets the source of the problem, while the individual claim is still needed to repair your loss.

Where it comes from

The basis of collective redress, here, does not lie only in the circumstance that many consumers may be affected by similar facts. That finding, though relevant, would still remain too dependent on a quantitative logic.p. 774

PTO fundamento da tutela coletiva, aqui, não está apenas na circunstância de muitos consumidores poderem ser atingidos por fatos semelhantes. Essa constatação, embora relevante, ainda permaneceria demasiado dependente de lógica quantitativa.

Collective redress, on the contrary, allows the manifestations to be traced back to their unitary source. This does not mean abolishing the role of individual protection. It remains indispensable, above all for the repair of concretely experienced losses and for the situated reconstruction of certain specific impacts.p. 775

PTA tutela coletiva, ao contrário, permite reconduzir as manifestações à sua fonte unitária. Isso não significa abolir o papel da tutela individual. Ela permanece indispensável, sobretudo para reparação de perdas concretamente experimentadas e para reconstrução situada de certos impactos específicos.

The rights behind this case

  • When loss is the rule, the problem is the design
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out
  • Punishment needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself
  • A relevant change has to leave a trail

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.

Where it comes from

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.
