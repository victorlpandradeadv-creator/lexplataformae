I lost access · The service is going to end · Later addition, of 24/11/2026Page added on 24 November 2026.

If the platform leaves, how much time does it owe you?

A company may decide to shut down a service or leave the country. But a company that chose to sell one-year plans, or to keep a service running for fifteen years, cannot walk away as if it had arrived yesterday.

The measure of what it owes comes from the choices it made itself. The yardstick is its own, and the more it tied you to the service, the more care its exit requires.

Who sets the deadline?

The company itself sets the deadline, when it decides how to sell. If it sold a twelve-month plan, it promised twelve months of service and took the money counting on that.

So if it shuts the service down before the plan ends, the notice and the refund are measured by that same period. It remains free to sell monthly plans, but choosing the long plan comes with a commitment to a long time.

The law already does this with computer programs. Whoever sells a program must state how long that version works, and stays bound to keep it working during that time, even after it stops selling it.

Where it comes from

Article 7 requires that the license contract, the tax invoice and the media and packaging state, in an easily legible form, the period of technical validity of the version sold, and Article 8 turns that period into an obligation to ensure complementary technical services related to the proper functioning of the program during the technical validity, including when the program is withdrawn from commercial circulationp. 51

PTO art. 7º impõe que o contrato de licença, o documento fiscal e os suportes e embalagens indiquem, de forma facilmente legível, o prazo de validade técnica da versão comercializada, e o art. 8º converte esse prazo em obrigação de assegurar serviços técnicos complementares relativos ao adequado funcionamento do programa durante a validade técnica, inclusive quando houver retirada de circulação comercial

And what if there was no plan with a set term?

Many services do not sell plans with a set term. You play for free, buy things one at a time and stay inside for years. Even so, over that time, you gather items, history, friends, reputation and money spent.

The longer the service stayed up, the more people built in it, and the more time the exit needs to give them to get organized. A service that existed for fifteen years cannot vanish with the same notice as one that opened last month.

The lifetime of the service is a sign of how much you came to depend on it. It is not proof on its own, but it counts in favor of those who stayed.

Where it comes from

Photographs, histories, libraries, contact networks, progressions, projected identities, reputational positions and long-term investments cannot be legally treated as residues without density merely because they are technically housed in someone else's infrastructure.p. 796

PTFotografias, históricos, bibliotecas, redes de contato, progressões, identidades projetadas, posições reputacionais e investimentos de longa duração não podem ser juridicamente tratados como resíduos sem densidade apenas porque se encontram tecnicamente alojados em infraestrutura alheia.

What must the exit bring, besides notice?

Notice is only the beginning. As far as possible, the exit must bring a way to carry on, to take what is yours somewhere else or to hand the service over to another company. When none of that works, a refund or compensation comes in.

Where it comes from

The right to exploit, close or reorganize an economic activity is not to be confused with a supposed right to annihilate, without duties of transition, preservation or transfer, the functional enjoyment already stabilized in the consumer's sphere.p. 709

PTO direito de explorar, encerrar ou reorganizar atividade econômica não se confunde com um suposto direito de aniquilar, sem deveres de transição, preservação ou transferência, a fruição funcional já estabilizada na esfera do consumidor.

Is the company then bound never to close?

No. It remains free to shut down the service or leave the country, and no one can force it to run forever. What the law controls is the way of leaving, not the decision to leave.

The law also does not yet set a number, such as so many months of notice per year of service. The right period in each case is measured by what the company sold, how long it stayed and what people built inside.

Where it comes from

Exclusivity may organize the market; it cannot legitimize the managed destruction of others' enjoyment as a side effect of its own decision to leave it.p. 709

PTA exclusividade pode organizar o mercado; não pode legitimar a destruição administrada da fruição alheia como efeito colateral da própria decisão de sair dele.

What is autometry?

Autometry is the principle by which the extent of the supplier's duties is measured by the choices it makes itself when organizing its activity. It is the measure the supplier itself provides. The duration of the contracts it sells, the time it keeps the environment running, the control it retains over enjoyment and the power it exercises over third parties provide the yardstick. Economic freedom provides the yardstick of economic responsibility.

The starting point is the 1988 economic order. Free enterprise appears, in Article 170 of the Federal Constitution1, alongside the social function of property, free competition and consumer protection. As Eros Roberto Grau shows, it is not unrestricted patrimonial sovereignty, and consumer protection acts from within economic rationality itself. Autometry gives this coexistence a technique of implementation. The business choice remains free, and its counterpart is no longer arbitrated from outside but read in the material conditions the supplier itself has built.

The same reasoning already supports institutional reciprocity proportional to the dominion exercised over recognition, permanence and sanction. The platform does not become a State, but when it governs access and permanence at scale it owes counterparts in the measure of that governance. Autometry extends this criterion from the power to sanction to the other choices of the business model.

Its application does not depend on an unlawful act. A business model may be fully legitimate and, precisely because it is exercised with the reach it has, attract proportional duties. That is why the autometric principle also works preventively.

Free enterprise, in the constitutional arrangement of 1988, is not to be confused with unrestricted contractual sovereignty nor with a private freedom to design platform regimes immune to control.p. 505

PTA livre iniciativa, no arranjo constitucional de 1988, não se confunde com soberania contratual irrestrita nem com liberdade privada de desenhar regimes de plataforma imunes a controle.

Where it comes from

When practical normativity is exercised through mutable internal rules, access architecture and opaque informational intermediation, the relevant deficit is not the absence of a public-law label, but the absence of institutional reciprocity proportional to the dominion exercised over recognition, permanence and sanction.p. 350

PTQuando a normatividade prática é exercida por regras internas mutáveis, por arquitetura de acesso e por intermediação informacional opaca, o déficit relevante não é a ausência de um rótulo jurídico-público, mas a ausência de reciprocidade institucional proporcional ao domínio exercido sobre reconhecimento, permanência e sanção.

Free enterprise, innovation and technological ownership do not operate as safe-conducts for unauditability, shielded irresponsibility or contractual immunization of the supplier.p. 512

PTLivre iniciativa, inovação e titularidade tecnológica não operam como salvo-condutos para inauditabilidade, irresponsabilidade blindada ou imunização contratual do fornecedor.

What is the yardstick of contractual binding?

When the supplier sells fixed-term contracts, it sets for itself a time horizon for the consumer's planning and collects revenue under that expectation. That horizon measures the duty of advance notice of the shutdown. A platform that sells an annual subscription answers for notice compatible with twelve months, and leaving before the end of the contracted period requires a refund proportional to what will not be enjoyed.

The legal order already knows this structure. Articles 7 and 8 of the Software Law (Law 9,609/1998)2 require whoever sells a computer program to declare the period of technical validity of the version. During that period, the seller must ensure the technical services for the program's proper functioning. The obligation persists even when the program is withdrawn from commercial circulation, save for fair compensation. The period the supplier declares comes to measure the duty it carries after leaving the market.

The yardstick also speaks to the reciprocity required by Article 51, item XI, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor, CDC)3, which declares void the clause that authorizes the supplier to cancel the contract unilaterally without granting the same right to the consumer. Whoever sells time cannot reserve for itself the power to shorten it without consequence.

Where it comes from

Law 9,609/1998 Brasil(Brasil, [1998]) does not merely describe exclusivity and licensing as an enclosure of utilities, but structures positive duties of the supplier tied to the technical life cycle of software.p. 51

PTA Lei nº 9.609/1998 (Brasil, [1998]) não descreve apenas exclusividade e licenciamento como cercamento de utilidades, mas estrutura deveres positivos do fornecedor ligados ao ciclo de vida técnico do software.

What is the yardstick of material binding?

The second yardstick does not depend on the formal duration of contracts. A free platform, with one-off sales and open-ended contracts, can induce cumulative investments for years. The consumer of a game running for fifteen years did not need to sign a fifteen-year contract to build with it a fifteen-year material relationship, with a collection, a history, technical mastery, a social network and a reputational position.

In that case, the time the environment has been active measures the transition period that can be demanded. The sole paragraph of Article 473 of the Civil Code5 provides the model. When one of the parties has made considerable investments to perform the contract, unilateral termination only takes effect after a period compatible with the nature and size of those investments. The right to terminate remains, and its exercise is measured by what the relationship has built.

The reading of continuing relationships points in the same direction. Claudia Lima Marques treats these relationships as long-term captive contracts, and Judith Martins-Costa ties the duties of good faith to the investment of trust legitimized by the predictability of conduct. Duration intensifies dependence and trust, and the stability of the contractual program comes to contain the supplier's power to modify. In continuous supply, the trust built up over time is part of the contractual program itself.

The time of activity is an indicator of potential dependence, and not absolute proof of it. It opens the demonstration, which is completed by the centrality of the collection tied to the account, the weight of the history, the restrictions on transfer and the exit cost.

What Article 473 of the Civil Code says5

Art. 473. Unilateral termination, in the cases in which the law expressly or implicitly permits it, operates by notice served on the other party. Sole paragraph. If, however, given the nature of the contract, one of the parties has made considerable investments for its performance, the unilateral termination shall only take effect after a period compatible with the nature and size of the investments has elapsed.

Civil Code (Law 10,406 of 10 January 2002), on the Planalto website, on 24 November 2026. Later addition, outside the thesis.

Código Civil (Lei nº 10.406, de 10 de janeiro de 2002), no site do Planalto, em 24 de novembro de 2026. Acréscimo posterior, fora da tese.

Where it comes from

The duration of the relationship intensifies dependence, trust and the practical centrality of the performance, requiring the stability of the contractual program to work as a parameter for containing the supplier's power to modify.p. 561

PTA duração da relação intensifica dependência, confiança e centralidade prática da prestação, exigindo que a estabilidade do programa contratual funcione como parâmetro de contenção do poder modificativo do fornecedor.

The duties to act in good faith must be assessed in connection with the investment of trust legitimized by the predictability of conduct, so that the duties of loyalty lie at the point where one cannot disloyally strike at the legitimate expectations of those who trust Martins-Costa(Martins-Costa, 2018, p. 309).p. 560

PTOs deveres de agir segundo a boa-fé devem ser aferidos em conexão com o investimento de confiança legitimado pela previsibilidade de condutas, de modo que os deveres de lealdade se situam no ponto em que não se pode atingir deslealmente as expectativas legítimas de quem confia (Martins-Costa, 2018, p. 309) .

Do the two yardsticks exclude each other?

No. Contractual binding protects expectations expressed in time, and material binding protects expectations and investments consolidated by the continuity of the activity. A platform that sells only monthly subscriptions but keeps the environment running for twenty years would be overprotected if only the first yardstick counted. A recent platform that sells three-year plans answers more under the first than under the second. In each case, the most protective measure the facts support prevails.

Combining them also prevents evasion through form. If only formal duration counted, it would be enough to slice annual plans into successive monthly payments. If only the age of the legal entity counted, it would be enough to reorganize the company to reset the count. The yardstick reaches the economic and functional substance of the activity, not the contractual or corporate clothing it wears.

What does the autometric principle not authorize?

Autometry does not turn a lasting activity into a perpetual duty to operate. The supplier keeps the freedom to leave the market. Proportional duties bear on the conditions of the exit, on the preservation of what was built and on measures of transition, portability, transfer of the operation or restoration. The freedom to shut down is not the freedom to choose any way of shutting down.

The relevant variable is the power exercised and the dependence built, not economic size. A company may grow by selling a better product without concentrating power over the customer. Size may help in assessing the capacity to comply, but it is not the main measure.

Not every dimension is measured by a formula. Time is easy to count, while the degree of architectural dependence or the weight of a symbolic loss call for categories, ranges and verifiable indicators. Proportionality does not presuppose universal quantification, and the law does not yet set a coefficient for the transition period. A shutdown due to insolvency or technical impossibility is also not the same as an exit for strategic convenience, and the regime for that case remains open.

Where it comes from

Nor is it necessary to resort to a strong idea of the consumer's ownership of the digital good in order to demand minimum stability.p. 708

PTTambém não é necessário recorrer a uma ideia forte de propriedade do consumidor sobre o bem digital para exigir estabilidade mínima.

If the shutdown of the service is unavoidable, the constitutional order requires that it be reconciled, as far as technically and legally possible, with solutions of continuity, portability, functional restoration, transfer of the operation or other measures suitable to prevent private architecture from becoming a lawful instrument of patrimonial and existential devastation of the consumer.p. 709

PTSe o encerramento do serviço se impõe, a ordem constitucional exige que ele seja compatibilizado, tanto quanto técnica e juridicamente possível, com soluções de continuidade, portabilidade, recomposição funcional, cessão da operação ou outras medidas idôneas a impedir que a arquitetura privada se converta em instrumento lícito de devastação patrimonial e existencial do consumidor.

Does the autometric principle apply only to shutdowns?

No. The shutdown is the most visible application, but every choice of the business model that expands the supplier's power over third parties attracts a counterpart of the same measure. Whoever decides to investigate, classify conduct and punish owes reasons, defense and gradation that grow more robust the more serious and irreversible the sanctions are. Whoever makes the enjoyment of an already purchased product depend on permanent authentication on its own servers retains control it would not need to retain, and that technical dependence attracts duties of continuity and of alternative means of enjoyment. Whoever reserves the power to change the product answers for proportional control of the changes, which is the field of functional value equivalence.

In none of these cases is dependence unlawful in itself. The problem is creating it without the counterpart it requires. And the autometric principle changes the calculation even before the consumer relationship begins, because the most controlling model is no longer automatically the most convenient once it carries the costs of the dependence it creates. The law does not need to choose between technologies. It is enough to tie to each choice the consequence it produces.

Where it comes from

Exclusivity remains a legitimate element of the organization of the ecosystem.p. 710

PTA exclusividade permanece elemento legítimo de organização do ecossistema.

Author's note · 24 November 2026

Why is autometry not in the thesis?

Autometry is a formulation made after the defense. The defended text contains its pieces, but not their assembly. It demands institutional reciprocity proportional to the dominion exercised (p. 350) and denies the supplier the right to annihilate stabilized enjoyment when it shuts down the service (pp. 709 and 796). It reads in the Software Law duties tied to the period of technical validity, including after withdrawal from circulation (p. 51). And it shows that the duration of relationships intensifies dependence and trust (pp. 560-561).

What this page adds is the measure. The question the thesis left open on shutdowns was how to convert the constitutional duty of preservation into a measurable obligation. The answer is that the measure comes from the supplier's own choices, with a contractual yardstick, drawn from the duration of the contracts it sells, and a material yardstick, drawn from the time it keeps the environment running. Article 473 of the Civil Code5 and Article 51, item XI, of the CDC3 come in as additions, because the thesis does not cite them.

Where does autometry fit among the nine concepts?

Autometry is not a tenth concept. The nine say what is consumed, which regime governs enjoyment, which vulnerability arises there, how the unlawful act is qualified and how the loss is controlled and restored. The autometric principle answers a question that cuts across all of them, namely how much is demanded in each case. That is why it works as a calibration criterion for the regime, and not as a category alongside the others.

Why autometry?

The name comes from the Greek autós, self, and métron, measure. It designates the measure given by oneself, hence the adjective autometric, as in autometric duty. Its formation follows that of hypercuratorial anticipation, also coined from Greek, and avoids words that already have an owner. Proportionality would pull toward the test of suitability, necessity and proportionality in the strict sense, and correspectivity would pull toward the contractual synallagma, when the merit of the principle is precisely to step outside the contract.

There is a deliberate irony in the name. The platform that measures everything about those inside it, every metric, rank and engagement, comes to have its own duties measured by the metric it chose itself.

Its emblem is an old Latin maxim of uncertain attribution, patere legem quam ipse fecisti, bear the law you yourself have made. If the Lex Plataformae is the law the platform makes for others, autometry is the platform bearing the law it made. The principle is not the same as risk-benefit liability, from the maxim ubi emolumentum, ibi onus, where the profit is, there the burden lies. Risk-benefit measures by gain, while autometry measures by the choice and the dependence it created, even without profit.
