Platforms

On what other platforms does this happen?Platforms

Here are the four platform families side by side. The questions are the same for all of them, how the platform governs use, what the people there depend on, what can be proved and what response makes sense when something is lost.

What brings the families together is the mechanism by which the user's experience is organized and remade, not the economic sector or the name of the service.

Where it comes from

The unit of examination lies not in the economic sector or in the marketing label of the service, but in the predominant mechanism by which the user's experience is organized, modulated, reconfigured or made contestable.p. 755

PTA unidade do exame não está no ramo econômico nem na etiqueta mercadológica do serviço, mas no mecanismo predominante pelo qual a experiência do usuário é organizada, modulada, reconfigurada ou tornada contestável.

It is this mechanism, and not the name of the sector, that provides the basis for comparabilityp. 755

PTÉ esse mecanismo, e não o nome do setor, que fornece a base da comparabilidade

Each of them makes it possible to observe how the same grid of controllability behaves in the face of specific problems of promise, trust, opacity, mutability, sanction, reputation, intermediation or administration of expectation.p. 757

PTCada um deles permite observar como a mesma grade de controlabilidade se comporta diante de problemas específicos de promessa, confiança, opacidade, mutabilidade, sanção, reputação, intermediação ou administração da expectativa.

Why compare platforms by their mechanism?

The criterion of comparison is structural. The unit of examination lies not in the economic sector or in the label of the service, but in the predominant mechanism by which the experience is organized, modulated, reconfigured or made contestable. That keeps the typology from turning into a catalog of markets or depending on the interpreter's cultural familiarity with the environment.

Not every platform reproduces, with the same intensity, the same vulnerability, opacity or capture. What is checked is whether the regime retains recognizable application to typical models of continuous supply.

What matters is not whether the platform presents itself as a cognitive tool, market infrastructure, productive environment or reputation system, but how it organizes access, modulates expectations, distributes risks and conditions the possibility of institutional reconstruction of the conflict.p. 756

PTO que importa não é se a plataforma se apresenta como ferramenta cognitiva, infraestrutura de mercado, ambiente produtivo ou sistema de reputação, mas como ela organiza acesso, modula expectativas, distribui riscos e condiciona a possibilidade de reconstrução institucional do conflito.

The aim is to verify whether the regime as constructed retains a recognizable capacity to apply when projected onto typical models of continuous digital supplyp. 755

PTO que se busca é verificar se o regime construído conserva capacidade de incidência reconhecível quando projetado sobre modelos típicos de fornecimento digital contínuo

The criterion of comparison is structural.p. 755

PTO critério de aproximação é estrutural.

The point here is not to gather digital examples under the same heading by mere technological affinity, nor to suggest that every contemporary platform reproduces, with identical intensity, the same form of vulnerability, opacity or capture of enjoyment.p. 755

In what sequence is each family examined?

In four constant steps. First, the governance mechanism that organizes enjoyment and produces the characteristic friction. Then, the central vulnerability it triggers and the way promises, expectations and losses are administered. Next, the compatible evidence and evidentiary requirements, with the blind spots that make it hard to translate the conflict. Only then, the possible dogmatic consequence. This consequence always derives, in a recognizable way, from the operative grid already consolidated.

Where it comes from

To maintain this comparability, the exposition will follow a constant sequence.p. 756

PTPara manter essa comparabilidade, a exposição seguirá sequência constante.

In the following stage, the adequate evidence and the evidentiary requirements compatible with the specific form of the conflict are established, including the blind spots that usually hinder its institutional translation.p. 756

What changes when the regime applies?

It changes how the conflict is told, because attention moves from the isolated event to the supply program. It changes how the conflict is proved, because opacity becomes part of the legal problem. And it changes how the harm is remedied, because money no longer automatically settles the matter when the harm reaches continuity, intelligibility, functional position, practical trust or permanence.

In each family the harmful mechanism, the evidentiary problem and the required restoration are kept apart. This does not mean the regime works for almost everything. The three dimensions are seen as connected, but they are not conflated.

Harmful modulation is not to be confused with its proof. The evidentiary difficulty is not to be confused with the harm itself.p. 788

PTA modulação lesiva não se confunde com sua prova. A dificuldade probatória não se confunde com a própria lesão.

Where it comes from

What emerges from the analysis of the models is therefore not the conclusion that the IOC would work for almost anything.p. 788

PTO que emerge da análise dos modelos não é, portanto, a conclusão de que o OIC serviria para quase tudo.

The way the conflict is narrated changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program.p. 788

PTMuda a forma de narrar o conflito, porque a atenção se desloca do evento isolado para o programa de fornecimento.

And the way of remedying changes, because the response can no longer be automatically satisfied with pecuniary equivalents whenever the harm reaches continuity, intelligibility, functional position, practical trust or conditions of permanence in enjoyment.p. 788

PTE muda a forma de remediar, porque a resposta já não pode se satisfazer automaticamente com equivalentes pecuniários sempre que a lesão atingir continuidade, inteligibilidade, posição funcional, confiança prática ou condições de permanência na fruição.

By keeping distinct, in each platform family, the mechanism of harmful modulation, the corresponding evidentiary problem and the rationale of restoration required by the regime, both empty abstraction and case-by-case dispersion are avoided.p. 788

PTAo manter distinguíveis, em cada família de plataforma, o mecanismo de modulação lesiva, o problema probatório correspondente e a racionalidade de recomposição exigida pelo regime, evita-se tanto a abstração vazia quanto a dispersão casuística.

Do these pages accuse any company?

Showing the mechanism is not condemning the service. Not every platform reproduces the same problem, and those that do don't all do it with the same force. Nor does every technology-mediated relationship fall under consumer protection.

What these pages do is test whether the same questions remain useful when the environment changes. They don't say that a given company is abusive. They say where to look.

The laboratory of origin

The typology was refined in a laboratory of origin, with an illustrative scope of application. The sections below develop it in the full text.

At the level of illustrative application, the work takes as its privileged locus the ecosystem of products and services of the multinational Riot Games, focusing on the game p. 37 League of Legends and its extensions on associated platforms. This scope does not seek to exhaust the diversity of the global interactive entertainment industry, but to offer a field of exemplification in which typical structures of semiotic governance, practices of hypercuratorial anticipation, monetization models based on digital goods and recurring patterns of friction between consumers and supplier become particularly clear.p. 36-37

PTNo plano de aplicação ilustrativa, o trabalho toma como locus privilegiado o ecossistema de produtos e serviços da multinacional Riot Games , com foco no jogo League of Legends e em suas extensões em plataformas associadas. Esse recorte não pretende esgotar a diversidade da indústria global de entretenimento interativo, mas oferecer um campo de exemplificação no qual se tornam particularmente nítidas estruturas típicas de governança semiótica, práticas de antecipação hipercuratorial, modelos de monetização baseados em bens digitais e padrões recorrentes de fricção entre consumidores e fornecedor.
