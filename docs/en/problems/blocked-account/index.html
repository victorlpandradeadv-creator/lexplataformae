Your rights · By problem

My account was blocked or banned. What about what I bought?

When your account is blocked or banned, which means permanent expulsion, you also lose access to everything in it, including what you bought. The company can punish, but the punishment needs a reason that can be checked and a real way for you to defend yourself.

Where it comes from

If the account is terminated, access is lost not only to the “game”, but to the set of data and virtual content that structured the experience, in addition to the assertion that there is no refund in cases of just causep. 375

PTSe a conta é encerrada, perde-se acesso não apenas ao “jogo”, mas ao conjunto de dados e conteúdos virtuais que estruturavam a experiência, além de se afirmar ausência de reembolso em hipóteses de justa causa

In platform ecosystems, sanctions do not operate merely as a one-off disciplinary response to conduct attributed to the user. They can affect access, permanence, visibility, reputation, relational insertion and continuity of enjoyment. For this reason, they must be controlled by denser criteria than the mere formal existence of sanctioning power. They require verifiable reasoning, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTEm ecossistemas de plataforma, sanções não operam apenas como resposta disciplinar pontual a uma conduta atribuída ao usuário. Elas podem afetar acesso, permanência, visibilidade, reputação, inserção relacional e continuidade da fruição. Por isso, devem ser controladas por critérios mais densos do que a simples existência formal de poder sancionatório. Exigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

How do you know whether the punishment went too far?

Punishment is not always expulsion. Often the account stays active but becomes less visible, loses rewards and has its chat cut off, and this also takes value from what you bought, because staying in on worse terms is already a loss.

The warning sign appears when the punishment comes without a reason you can understand or when the path to contest it leads nowhere. It also appears when undoing the measure is much harder than applying it was.

Many punishments come from automatic detection. In League of Legends, for example, the company itself warns that the punishment may arrive up to 15 minutes after the end of the match. These punishments follow criteria the company does not fully reveal, and it is this lack of clarity that makes the punishment so hard to contest.

Where it comes from

The relevant punishment is often not to expel, but to demote, because remaining inside, but less visible, less eligible and less recognizable, amounts to degrading the position of enjoyment and eroding the value that depended on commensurability and validation in circuit.p. 373

PTA punição relevante muitas vezes não é expulsar, mas rebaixar, porque continuar dentro, porém menos visível, menos elegível e menos reconhecível, equivale a degradar a posição de fruição e a corroer o valor que dependia de comensurabilidade e validação em circuito.

Whenever access, permanence, visibility or contestation are conditioned by opaque sanctions, selective frictions, excessive costs of reaction or asymmetric reversal mechanisms, there will be an indication of abusive governance of the bond.p. 746

PTSempre que acesso, permanência, visibilidade ou contestação forem condicionados por sanções opacas, fricções seletivas, custos de reação excessivos ou mecanismos assimétricos de reversão, haverá indício de governança abusiva do vínculo.

The second form is ex post enforcement, in which the ecosystem applies declared or partially declared sanctions after a moderation event or automatic detection.p. 373

PTA segunda forma é o enforcement ex post, no qual o ecossistema aplica sanções declaradas ou parcialmente declaradas depois de um evento de moderação ou de detecção automática.

In the Instant Feedback System, Riot states that users may be punished “up to 15 minutes after the end of the match”, and describes a range of consequences running from restrictions to permanent suspensionsp. 375

PTNo Sistema de Feedback Instantâneo, a Riot afirma que usuários podem ser punidos “em até 15 minutos após o final da partida”, e descreve um leque de consequências que vai de restrições a suspensões permanentes

The legal consequence is that the sanction becomes part of the object itself, since it defines the practical stability of the acquired position and conditions continued access on criteria whose detailed functioning is not fully revealed to the user, under justifications of efficiency, prevention of cheating and management of scalep. 375

PTA consequência jurídica é que a sanção passa a compor o próprio objeto, pois ela define a estabilidade prática da posição adquirida e condiciona a continuidade do acesso a critérios cujo funcionamento detalhado não é plenamente revelado ao usuário, sob justificativas de eficiência, prevenção de burla e gestão de escala

Which records are worth gathering right after the punishment?

Keep the punishment notice with its date, the message explaining the reason, if there is one, and every reply you got when you contested it. Screenshots of the inventory, the history and the purchases while you can still see them also help, as do the emails and receipts for the purchases, because after the block they are what lets you reconstruct what you had.

The rest usually stays with the company, which keeps the type of punishment applied, the reason and the effect it had on the account. That is why your request can ask it to preserve and show these records.

Where it comes from

The minimum required proof falls on sanction records, contestation flow, criteria deployed, structural difficulties of reversal and concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience.p. 746

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre registros de sanção, fluxo de contestação, critérios mobilizados, dificuldades estruturais de reversão e impactos concretos da medida sobre a continuidade da experiência.

Once the assets are removed or the account is reverted to an earlier state, the user must rely on old statements, scattered emails, past screenshots and reconstructed memory to prove that a given item legitimately belonged to their position of enjoyment.p. 616

PTUma vez removidos os ativos ou revertido o estado da conta, o usuário passa a depender de extratos antigos, e-mails dispersos, capturas pretéritas e memória reconstruída para provar que determinado conteúdo integrava legitimamente sua posição de fruição.

What is required here is not a total sweep of the system, but a minimum documentary core: history of relevant versions; essential change records with functional impact; trails of consent and decision flow; history of relevant transactions; communications of change; and, when there is enforcement, basic records of the decision event, such as the category applied, reason and consequence.p. 658

PTO que se exige aqui não é uma devassa total do sistema, mas um núcleo documental mínimo: histórico de versões relevantes; registros essenciais de alteração com impacto funcional; trilhas de consentimento e de fluxo decisório; histórico de transações relevantes; comunicações de mudança; e, quando houver enforcement, registros básicos do evento decisório, como categoria aplicada, motivo e consequência.

Given the concentration of records, preservation and disclosure of supply trails are warranted, with rational redistribution of the burden and presumptions favorable to the consumer when the supplier does not cooperate or keeps the proof in a black box.p. 609

PTDiante da concentração de registros, cabe preservação e exibição de trilhas do fornecimento, com redistribuição racional do ônus e presunções favoráveis ao consumidor quando o fornecedor não coopera ou mantém a prova em caixa-preta.

What can you ask for when the account was closed without explanation?

If the punishment has neither a reason that can be checked nor a reasonable way to contest it, you can ask for it to be reviewed or undone. You can also ask the company to say which punishment it applied and why, and to offer a way to contest it that actually works.

The first thing to seek is getting back what was taken, such as access, history and inventory, when that is possible. In more serious cases, losing the account, the history or years of your journey may even be treated as moral damages, which is harm to the person and not only to the wallet. But a mere annoyance is not enough for that.

Brazil's data protection law (the LGPD) also helps, because the block does not take away your right to ask the company to confirm that it holds data about you and to give you access to it.

Where it comes from

The institutional consequence includes reversal or review of sanctions, procedural duties, creation or improvement of contestation mechanisms and, in structurally repetitive cases, correction of the ecosystem's enforcement regime.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional inclui reversão ou revisão de sanções, deveres procedimentais, criação ou aperfeiçoamento de mecanismos de contestação e, em casos estruturalmente repetitivos, correção do regime de enforcement do ecossistema.

Instead of generically requesting “all the data in the system”, the consumer may request, for example, the history of relevant versions of a given item, the trail of screens shown along the purchase path, the minimum records of the specific transaction, the content of the change communication or the category and reason for the sanction applied.p. 660

PTEm vez de requerer genericamente “todos os dados do sistema”, o consumidor pode postular, por exemplo, o histórico de versões relevantes de determinado item, a trilha de telas exibidas no percurso de compra, os registros mínimos da transação específica, o conteúdo da comunicação de alteração ou a categoria e o motivo da sanção aplicada.

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving pecuniary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

The consequence is not to make the consumer an author, but to recognize that erasures, resets, inventory exclusions, suppressions of history and sanctions that eliminate the biography built in the environment require verifiable reasoning, minimum preservation of records and, where appropriate, restoration through reinstatement of the position of enjoyment.p. 543

PTA consequência não é autoralizar o consumidor, mas reconhecer que apagamentos, resets, exclusões de inventário, supressões de histórico e sanções que eliminem a biografia construída no ambiente exigem motivação verificável, preservação mínima de registros e, quando cabível, recomposição por restauração da posição de fruição.

In qualified cases, the loss of account, history or trajectory may constitute moral damages for injury to personality, especially when it objectively affects biographical continuity, institutionalized belonging in the ecosystem or relevant anchors of reputation and memory, and not mere subjective displeasure.p. 541

PTEm hipóteses qualificadas, a perda de conta, histórico ou trajetória pode configurar dano moral por lesão à personalidade, especialmente quando atinge de modo objetivável continuidade biográfica, pertencimento institucionalizado no ecossistema ou lastros relevantes de reputação e memória, e não mero dissabor subjetivo.

The use of data linked to the consumer's trajectory, history and digital biography must remain compatible with the purposes disclosed and with the context of the relationship, needs to be intelligible to the data subject, and does not eliminate the rights to confirmation of existence, access, correction, deletion or portability of processed data.p. 542

PTO uso de dados vinculados à trajetória, ao histórico e à biografia digital do consumidor deve permanecer compatível com as finalidades informadas e com o contexto da relação, precisa ser inteligível ao titular, e não elimina as faculdades de confirmação da existência, acesso, correção, eliminação ou portabilidade de dados tratados.

Where can you turn when support only repeats that the decision is final?

The first path is the company's own channel for contesting decisions, because it must offer one that really works. There you can ask for the concrete reason and the records of the punishment.

If the company neither explains nor reviews the punishment, the case can go on through Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court (Juizado Especial Cível), paths that the channels page details. In a lawsuit, the judge can order the company to show the records and split the burden of proof according to who has access to them.

When many people are punished the same way, the response can go beyond your case and reach the punishment system itself, through a collective action or through the action of public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

The corresponding supply duty is to ensure minimally intelligible procedures, verifiable reasoning, reasonable possibility of contestation and non-arbitrary treatment of the user in the administration of enjoyment.p. 746

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar procedimentos minimamente inteligíveis, motivação verificável, possibilidade razoável de contestação e tratamento não arbitrário do usuário na administração da fruição.

When the evidence arises and remains under the platform's control, the procedural system cannot feign neutrality.p. 659

PTQuando a prova nasce e permanece sob controle da plataforma, o sistema processual não pode fingir neutralidade.

Article 373, paragraph 1, allows the burden of proof to be allocated differently when one party would find it excessively difficult to discharge the burden or the other could more easily prove the contrary factp. 659

PTO art. 373, § 1º, permite a distribuição diversa do ônus probatório quando houver excessiva dificuldade para uma parte cumprir o encargo ou maior facilidade da outra em produzir a prova do fato contrário

When the litigation reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or non-verifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets. These standards offer a concrete criterion for consent decrees, recommendations, civil investigations and minimum governance requirements: functional impact reports on relevant changes, verifiable transparency protocols, preservation of essential records, understandable contestation procedures and minimum criteria of restoration in case of relevant degradation of enjoyment.p. 662-663

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma. Esses standards oferecem critério concreto para termos de ajustamento, recomendações, investigações civis e exigências mínimas de governança: relatórios de impacto funcional em alterações relevantes, protocolos de transparência verificável, preservação de registros essenciais, procedimentos de contestação compreensíveis e critérios mínimos de recomposição em caso de degradação relevante da fruição.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

How far does the defense of someone who was punished go?

The company has the power to punish, and having that power is not the problem. What is required is that the punishment have a reason that can be checked, consistency from one decision to the next and an understandable way to contest it.

The terms of use, which are the contract you accept when creating the account, usually say there is no refund when the account is terminated for just cause, which is a serious reason attributed to you. This clause carries weight, but it does not turn the erasure of the whole account into a simple internal adjustment, immune to any control.

None of this guarantees that the account comes back, because each case depends on what happened and on what can be shown. And the data protection law does not make you the owner of everything the company records about your account.

Clause 2.3 of Riot's Terms of Service provides that, once the account is terminated, the user loses access to the account, the associated data, the game currency and the virtual content, without refund in cases of just cause, with the further possibility of cancellation of other accounts and of access to other servicesp. 540

PTA cláusula 2.3 dos Termos de Serviço da Riot prevê que, encerrada a conta, o usuário perde acesso à conta, aos dados associados, à moeda do jogo e ao conteúdo virtual, sem reembolso em hipóteses de justa causa, com possibilidade ainda de cancelamento de outras contas e de acesso a outros serviços

Exclusivity is exercised, here, as a power of complete erasure of the consumed experience, which prevents treating sanction, exclusion or rollback as mere internal adjustments without existential legal relevance.p. 541

PTA exclusividade é exercida, aqui, como poder de apagamento integral da experiência consumida, o que impede tratar sanção, exclusão ou rollback como meros ajustes internos sem relevância jurídica existencial.

The minimum required proof involves demonstrating the link between the governance mechanism and the functional loss, as well as the technical or institutional possibility of restoration, reversal, equivalent substitution or procedural correction.p. 748

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve demonstração do nexo entre o mecanismo de governança e a perda funcional, bem como da possibilidade técnica ou institucional de recomposição, reversão, substituição equivalente ou correção procedimental.

The rights behind this case

  • Punishment needs a reason and a chance to defend yourself
  • The proof held by the platform has to come out
  • A relevant change has to leave a trail
  • The response should restore what you lost

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
