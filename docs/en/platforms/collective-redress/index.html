Extension · 7.2.5 · pp. 773 to 777

Collective redress under open auditabilityOn collective redress under open auditability

Some harms draw their unity from the design of the supply, not from a sum of episodes. For these, the response has to reach the regime.

Read in the full text, with the notes

When is the harm collective?

It is not enough for many consumers to be hit by similar facts. Collective redress is justified when the harmful source is unitary, repeatable and built into the ordinary functioning of the platform. Repeated opacity, a persistent absence of history and non-auditable mutability form a defect of regime. This is architecture harm, produced by the design of the supply. The effects are not homogeneous, but they share a common matrix.

The harm is thus not only to the individual result of consumption. It is to the institutional verifiability of the supply.p. 774

PTO dano, assim, não é apenas ao resultado individual do consumo. É à verificabilidade institucional do fornecimento.

Where it comes from

The basis for collective redress here does not lie only in the fact that many consumers may be hit by similar facts.p. 774

PTO fundamento da tutela coletiva, aqui, não está apenas na circunstância de muitos consumidores poderem ser atingidos por fatos semelhantes.

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

Repeated opacity, persistent absence of history and non-auditable mutability together form a defect of regime.p. 774

PTA opacidade reiterada, a ausência persistente de histórico e a mutabilidade não auditável formam, em conjunto, um vício de regime.

The impact is not homogeneous in all its effects, but it is common in its matrix.p. 776

PTO atingimento não é homogêneo em todos os seus efeitos, mas é comum em sua matriz.

What can collective redress impose?

Inhibitory, procedural and analytical measures that reintroduce verifiability, such as preserving minimum records, verifiable reasoning for relevant sanctions, an accessible history of changes and minimum operational criteria for distribution, priority or ranking. Individual protection remains indispensable to repair concrete losses. Purely atomized treatment tends to monetize episodes without changing the structure and to turn the systemic difficulty of proof into the private failure of scattered consumers.

Where it comes from

Collective redress thus proves particularly suited to imposing inhibitory, procedural and analytical measures capable of reintroducing institutional verifiability into the environment.p. 775

PTA tutela coletiva mostra-se, então, particularmente vocacionada a impor medidas inibitórias, procedimentais e analíticas aptas a reintroduzir verificabilidade institucional no ambiente.

This may involve duties to preserve minimum records, a requirement of verifiable reasoning for materially relevant sanctions, maintenance of an accessible history of relevant changes, explicit statement of minimum operational criteria for distribution, priority or rankingp. 775

PTIsso pode envolver deveres de preservação de registros mínimos, exigência de motivação verificável para sanções materialmente relevantes, manutenção de histórico acessível de alterações relevantes, explicitação de critérios operacionais mínimos de distribuição, prioridade ou ranqueamento

This does not mean abolishing the role of individual protection.p. 775

PTIsso não significa abolir o papel da tutela individual.

On the one hand, it monetizes episodes without changing the machine that reproduces them.p. 776

PTDe um lado, monetiza episódios sem alterar a máquina que os reproduz.
