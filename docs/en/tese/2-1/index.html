2.1 On the theoretical-methodological strategy

p. 47

p. 48 The research adopts a qualitative approach and a theoretical-empirical nature, treating the problem as a legal phenomenon that cannot be reduced, in isolation, to “contract” or to “technology”. In continuous digital services, effective normativity emerges from the coupling of clauses, interface architecture and practices of executing sanctions, which requires a methodological design capable of keeping visible the articulation between socially relevant facts, values in dispute and applicable norms, yielding neither to abstraction that floats above the object nor to description that is satisfied with the operational level. The object of analysis is not “a game”, but a regime of platform-governed enjoyment, in which the value delivered, the permanence of the bond and the internal conditions of use are modulated by private governance over time.

The method is organized as theoretical-dogmatic research combined with institutional analysis of platform governance, triangulated with material from the selected ecosystem employed as situated and referenced exemplification. The dogmatic reconstruction operates on the law in force and seeks to produce a justifiable interpretation in light of recognizable normative parameters, without normative invention and without proposing an autonomous microsystem.

At the legal-normative level, the proposed reconstruction does not operate through a compartmentalized reading of the sources, as if the Constitution, the Consumer Protection Code and the intellectual property regimes applied in watertight and mutually exclusive blocks. The path adopts, as a methodological criterion of normative articulation, a coordinated reading of sources simultaneously applicable to the same governed consumer relationship, especially when the conflict involves, at the same time, constitutional protection of the person and the consumer, the consumer-law discipline of supply and the structures of exclusivity, licensing and technical control proper to intellectual property rights. In this key, the theory of the dialogue of sources appears less as an autonomous topic than as a methodological discipline of interpretation, allowing a systematically controlled reconstruction of the problem without artificial isolation of the pertinent legal regimes. Article 7 of the CDC18 provides, here, a relevant normative point of support, by making explicit that the rights provided for in the Code do not exclude others arising from domestic legislation, treaties, regulations and p. 49 general principles, reinforcing the openness of the consumer-law microsystem to coordination with other applicable normative sources.

The dogmatic ambition consists in interpreting and rearticulating categories of the Consumer Protection Code and of intellectual property rights under the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption, preserving the protective matrix and refusing to let exclusivity, licensing or technical powers operate as a title of immunity against the control of adequacy, transparency and abusiveness. Because of the researcher's insertion in the field, experience functions as a position of observation and interpretation to guide the selection and reading of the material, but not as a criterion of evidence.

The empirical dimension of the work is mobilized for illustrative purposes and disciplined by mechanisms, so as to make intelligible institutional arrangements and legally relevant points of friction. It is not intended to describe the ecosystem exhaustively, nor to demonstrate statistical prevalence, nor to offer a complete overview of its history or its variations. The empirical references are selected for their pertinence to the argument and for their capacity to make verifiable the passage between promise, execution and loss in continuous bonds, serving as support for the theoretical-dogmatic reconstruction and not as a demonstration of volume.

The theoretical reconstruction that precedes the dogmatic stage is organized by functional axes and by problems, and not by an exegetical inventory of authors. The object analyzed is contemporary, dynamic and institutionally hybrid, so that intelligibility depends less on “explaining authors” and more on describing mechanisms that operate in the service in order, next, to lead them back to legal parameters of protection. In this design, the frameworks mobilized fulfill the function of offering a point of support19 to stabilize questions and delimit observable mechanisms, without this implying cult, uncritical reproduction or full reconstitution of the author's path Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 41). The support serves to take the p. 50 analysis out of vagueness and also to prevent the description from becoming too intuitive in a domain in which the exercise of power tends to present itself as simple “technical functioning”.

So that this path does not become disorganized, a clear separation between sources and critical literature is also preserved20 Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 70). Sources are the materials that directly make up the observed institutional world or the law in force that applies to it, such as terms of use, internal policies, supplier announcements, interface records, legal documents and accessible judicial decisions. Critical literature is the set of texts that comment on, interpret, systematize or dispute these materials, such as doctrine, academic articles and third-party analyses. The distinction is not formalism, since it prevents commentary from being treated as evidence of what the platform declared or did, and also prevents indirect reading from replacing contact with primary material when the argument depends on dogmatic attribution.

With this, a disciplined hierarchy is preserved. The conceptual instruments guide the gaze and help formulate more precise questions. The sources sustain what can be stated about promises, powers, procedures and effects of the observed regime. The critical literature qualifies debates, puts readings under tension and makes interpretive choices explicit, without replacing the documentary grounding that supports the reconstruction. This architecture avoids the erudite drift that loses sight of the phenomenon and also avoids the rhetoric that uses authors as a seal of prestige, keeping the argument traceable back to trackable evidence and justifiable criteria Eco(Eco, 2007, p. 69-70).

This set of precautions does not alter the dogmatic direction of the path, but fixes the conditions under which the reconstruction remains controllable when facing an object administered by technical and communicational decisions. The task, from there, is to identify normative points capable of anchoring the institutional reading of the functioning of the service and of limiting the tendency to treat mutability and automated execution as simple operational neutralities. At this stage, the use of legal provisions that already recognize the temporality of supply and the consumer's dependence on the technical life cycle p. 51 of the good gains relevance, since they offer external criteria for distinguishing changes compatible with the bond from legally relevant reconfigurations of the promised content.

This anchoring becomes especially verifiable in the domain of computer programs. Law No. 9,609/1998 Brasil(Brasil, [1998]) does not merely describe exclusivity and licensing as an enclosure of utilities, but structures positive duties of the supplier linked to the technical life cycle of the software. Article 721 requires that the license contract, the tax document and the media and packaging indicate, in an easily legible way, the period of technical validity of the version marketed, and Article 822 converts this period into an obligation to ensure complementary technical services relating to the adequate functioning of the program during the technical validity, including when it is withdrawn from commercial circulation, subject to compensation for losses. These legal provisions operate, therefore, as control points for separating “mere operational variation” from relevant reconfiguration of the content of supply in continuous bonds.

The institutional analysis seeks to identify how internal rules, design and execution mechanisms structure conducts, risks and expectations, producing practical normativity that needs to be legally graspable. The triangulation of material from the selected ecosystem functions as anchoring and directed testing of the categories, allowing inferences to remain traceable back to trackable evidence and preventing legal reasoning from turning into moralization or mere description. The empirical material, here, does not take on the role of exhaustive demonstration or statistical generalization, but of controlled exemplification intended to make typical mechanisms intelligible and to guide the application of the dogmatic keys.

In services structured by continuous updating, the reference to technical validity does not operate as a requirement to anticipate specific changes or a schedule of modulations, which is readily deduced to be impossible at the factual level and in operational terms. It functions as a control point for qualifying duties of information, support and restoration when the exercise of unilateral power reconfigures, in a relevant way, the p. 52 content of supply and shifts onto the consumer losses arising from this mutability, without converting the evolutionary dynamic of the service into unlawfulness in itself.

The Consumer Protection Code remains the central matrix of protection, while the intellectual property rights regime enters with a functional status, describing contemporary forms of enclosure and circulation of utilities through licenses, technical conditionings and powers of modulation. This coordination makes it possible to reconstruct verifiable criteria for qualifying object, legitimate expectation fabricated as a promise, enjoyment, loss and restoration in platform-governed ecosystems, without converting exclusivity into a title of immunity against the CDC. It also prevents the legal relevance of losses from being conditioned on material subtraction, admitting that persistent alterations of access, of position and of internal conditions of use may reconfigure the link between promise, legitimate expectation and delivery in a legally relevant way, provided they are identifiable by controllable criteria.

The investigative strategy combines complementary processes directed at the analysis of legislation, doctrine, contractual models and institutional documentation without treating levels as disconnected layers. The illustrative empirical locus is organized by explicit criteria of selection, recording and treatment, so that contracts, policies, institutional announcements, interface artifacts, public statements, available technical records and selected judicial decisions can be read as evidence of the effective normativity in operation. The empirical material operates as support for the qualification of the object and for the verification of reading hypotheses, under a discipline of traceability and coherence.

The methodological perspective of Michael Burawoy's extended case study23 is mobilized here with a delimited function, as a discipline for using the selected ecosystem as an instrumental empirical laboratory. The paradigmatic case is employed to make p. 53 observable, at the situated level, structural forces that manifest themselves in the ordinary functioning of the ecosystem, with explicit support in pre-existing theory, without this converting the work into a case study as an autonomous purpose. In this frame, the passage from micro to macro does not depend on statistical representativeness or broad inductive generalization, since it is oriented toward the controlled reconstruction of the theoretical apparatus mobilized, through frictions and anomalies identifiable in the case Burawoy(Burawoy, 2009, p. 21; p. 43).

The methodological function of the laboratory, therefore, is to ensure institutional intelligibility and to allow directed testing of the operational categories developed in the thesis, through triangulation and referenced selection of trackable evidence, keeping the object legally describable. This directed testing operates in coordination with illustrative applications and tests of fit of the proposed keys in typical contemporary situations of continuous digital consumption, without shifting the path toward an exhaustive empirical demonstration. The risk inherent in the reconstructive gesture itself is, however, recorded, described by Burawoy as a coercive process of “double fitting”, in which complex situations can be fitted to the theory and, next, the theory readjusted to the case to “digest” the anomaly Burawoy(Burawoy, 2009, p. 60). To contain this risk, inferences are disciplined by explicit criteria for selecting the material and by a hierarchy of sources, with priority for primary documentation attributable to the supplier and subsidiary use of public narratives only as verifiable leads, without familiarity with the field operating as a criterion of evidence.

The theoretical scope functions as a grid of observation and control intended to guide the reading of the empirical material and to discipline inferences. Instead of operating as an inventory of authors, it is organized by functional axes, each associated with research questions and reading criteria. A first axis treats technique and infrastructure as a form of rationality that structures possibilities of action and interpretation. A second axis treats the cultural and communicational dimension as a condition for the formation of expectations, promises and intelligibility in interface-mediated environments, also mobilizing, in a structural key, contributions from the anthropology of consumption to describe how goods, belonging and routines stabilize meanings and coordinate conducts. A third axis reconstructs political economy and extraction to situate dependencies, tolls and forms of capture associated with the operation of the ecosystem. A fourth axis describes the semiotic dimension as a material component of value and as a condition of the legal cognizability of the conflict, including the notion of regimes of value and of the socially administered mutability of the object over time. A fifth axis reconstructs platform governance as the exercise of private power through internal rules p. 54 and sanction mechanisms. The authors mobilized in each axis are presented and developed in the respective theoretical sections.

In this design, the operational categories of the Immaterial Object of Consumption and of semiotic vulnerability guide the identification of promises and legitimate expectations, the description of mediations that modulate enjoyment and the qualification of losses socially perceived and legally disputable in continuous bonds. The consumer-law tradition and the dogmatic frameworks mobilized operate as parameters of legal qualification of the conflict and as interpretive limits, preserving a focus on protection, adequacy, transparency and restoration. The empirical material, in turn, illustrates, imposes restrictions and offers tests, requiring formulations to remain traceable back to observable traits of the institutional functioning of the ecosystem, without confusing description of the field with normative justification. The formalization of the responses of protection, control of abusiveness and restoration is reserved for the dogmatic stage of the work, in which the dialogue between theory, exemplification and normative parameters is converted into applicable operational criteria.
