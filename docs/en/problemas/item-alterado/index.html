Your rights · By problem

What I bought changed in an update. What now?

An update, which is the change the company makes to the program after the sale, altered what you bought. The item is still there, but it no longer serves the same way, and what matters is knowing whether the change took away the role that justified the purchase.

Where it comes from

Mutability, by itself, is not unlawful. The problem begins when it ceases to operate as a legitimate form of maintenance, improvement or adaptation of supply and starts to degrade, empty or divert, in a relevant way, the function that justified the acquisition and structured the legitimate expectation of enjoyment.p. 729

PTA mutabilidade, por si, não é ilícita. O problema começa quando ela deixa de operar como forma legítima de manutenção, aperfeiçoamento ou adaptação do fornecimento e passa a degradar, esvaziar ou desviar, de modo relevante, a função que justificou a aquisição e estruturou a expectativa legítima de fruição.

The permanence of the item, the functionality, the account, the access or the feature does not, by itself, neutralize the emptying of the experience if the concrete role of the object is degraded, diverted or made substantially different from the one that justified the purchase.p. 730

PTA permanência do item, da funcionalidade, da conta, do acesso ou da feature não neutraliza, por si, o esvaziamento da experiência se o papel concreto do objeto for degradado, desviado ou tornado substancialmente diverso daquele que justificou a aquisição.

How do you know whether the update went beyond a simple adjustment?

The typical case is the item that stays in the inventory, with the same name, but has become too weak. It may also have lost an important function or the place it had among the other players. The same goes for a screen redesigned in a way that gets in the way of everyday use, and for a new rule that downgrades what had been promised as equivalent.

The question is not whether the item still exists, nor whether the contract let the company make changes. The question is whether it still fulfills, in essence, the role it had in your experience. This role includes usefulness, the relationship with others and, when it matters, what the item means.

Where it comes from

The strength of the criterion appears sharply in cases such as removals of relevant functionalities, nerfs that empty acquired utility, interface changes that disrupt ordinary use, redefinitions of rules that downgrade the equivalence of what was promisedp. 637

PTA força do critério aparece com nitidez em hipóteses como remoções de funcionalidades relevantes, nerfs que esvaziam utilidade adquirida, mudanças de interface que desorganizam o uso ordinário, redefinições de regras que rebaixam a equivalência do que foi prometido

The lawfulness of the mutation is not measured by its mere abstract provision in a contractual clause, nor by the formal permanence of the object after the alteration.p. 650

PTA licitude da mutação não se mede por sua mera previsão abstrata em cláusula contratual, nem pela permanência formal do objeto após a alteração.

What matters is the substantial preservation of the role played by the object in the acquired experience, considering its concrete utility in the ecosystem, its relational insertion in the environment of use and, when legally relevant, its identity or symbolic density.p. 729

PTO que importa é a preservação substancial do papel exercido pelo objeto na experiência adquirida, considerando sua utilidade concreta no ecossistema, sua inserção relacional no ambiente de uso e, quando juridicamente relevante, sua densidade identitária ou simbólica.

Which proof of the before and after is worth keeping?

It is worth keeping the item's advertisement, with the advantages it promised, because the offer is the yardstick for what had to keep existing. Dated screenshots, recordings and the purchase screens also help, because they show what the item was really like and not only what you remember of it.

Patch notes are the texts in which the company tells what changed in each version. They serve as general information, but rarely let you compare an item before and after. That is why your protection cannot depend on you having archived everything, since the complete version history stays with the company.

Where it comes from

The offer and the trust it aroused provide a direct basis for this identification, since the consumer contracts in view of the advantages and qualities advertised, from which arises the legal duty to maintain the conditions offered or, at least, to substantially preserve their utility Miragem(Miragem, 2020, p. 424).p. 652

PTA oferta e a confiança por ela despertada oferecem base direta para essa identificação, pois o consumidor contrata em vista das vantagens e qualidades anunciadas, surgindo daí o dever jurídico de manutenção das condições ofertadas ou, ao menos, de preservação substancial de sua utilidade (Miragem, 2020, p. 424) .

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

The format is useful for general information and for minimum communicational transparency, but it is not designed as a tool of comparative auditability for the consumer who needs to reconstruct, technically, the before and after of a specific cosmetic, with objective parameters of demonstration.p. 471

PTO formato é útil para informação geral e para transparência comunicacional mínima, mas não é desenhado como ferramenta de auditabilidade comparativa voltada ao consumidor que precisa reconstruir tecnicamente um antes e depois de um cosmético específico, com parâmetros objetivos de demonstração.

In a regime where the supplier can update continuously and even automatically, auditability cannot depend on whether the consumer happened to keep evidence or has the technical knowledge to put together homemade comparisons.p. 471

PTEm um regime em que o fornecedor pode atualizar continuamente e até automaticamente, a auditabilidade não pode depender do acaso de o consumidor ter arquivado evidências ou de ter conhecimento técnico para produzir comparações artesanais.

What can you ask for before talking about money?

The first remedy is getting the function back. The request may include undoing the change, when this is technically feasible, or replacing the item with another that serves the same role. It may also include correcting the way the update was carried out.

When the change downgraded what you used in a relevant way, the solution should give you at least a choice. It can be a refund, credit or an exchange for an item of equivalent value. The terms of use, which are the contract you accept when creating the account, help here. Those of a major game already provide for a refund or exchange when a recently bought item is removed entirely.

You can also ask the company to say what changed, when, for what reason and with what effect on the item. And monetary compensation remains possible when restoring is not feasible or not enough.

Where it comes from

The possible institutional consequence prioritizes functional restoration, technical reversal when feasible, equivalent replacement and correction of the form of updating, instead of mere automatic monetization of the loss.p. 746

PTA consequência institucional possível prioriza recomposição funcional, reversão técnica quando viável, substituição equivalente e correção da forma de atualização, em vez de mera monetização automática da perda.

If the alteration downgrades in a relevant way what structured the utility for that consumer, restoration must include, at least, choice mechanisms that prevent captivity by surprise, with alternatives such as refund, credit, exchange for another cosmetic of equivalent value or similar forms of recovering the investment for the consumer's reinsertion in the ecosystem, without requiring from the consumer the impossible proof of a black box.p. 471

PTSe a alteração rebaixa de modo relevante aquilo que estruturava a utilidade para aquele consumidor, a recomposição deve incluir, ao menos, mecanismos de escolha que impeçam o cativeiro por surpresa, com alternativas como reembolso, crédito, troca por outro cosmético de valor equivalente ou formas análogas de recuperação do investimento para reinserção do consumidor no ecossistema, sem exigir dele a prova impossível de uma caixa-preta.

If we decide to completely withdraw certain Virtual Products that you have recently purchased, we will provide you with a refund or a replacement.p. 614

PTSe decidirmos retirar completamente certos Produtos Virtuais que você tenha comprado recentemente, lhe providenciaremos um reembolso ou uma substituição.

The corresponding duty of supply is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was altered, when it was altered, on what grounds it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de manter verificabilidade mínima sobre o que foi alterado, quando o foi, por que motivo foi justificado e quais efeitos funcionalmente relevantes produziu.

Whenever the injury affects access, continuity, equivalence, history, relational position or significant density of enjoyment, the legal response must prioritize functional restoration, reserving pecuniary compensation for cases where restoration is unfeasible or insufficient.p. 748

PTSempre que a lesão atingir acesso, continuidade, equivalência, histórico, posição relacional ou densidade significativa da fruição, a resposta jurídica deverá priorizar recomposição funcional, reservando indenização pecuniária para hipóteses de inviabilidade ou insuficiência da restauração.

Where do you start complaining about an update?

The first step is usually the company itself. Continuing to use the service after the change does not mean, by itself, that you agreed to it. Those who have already invested in the account often carry on because they have no way out.

If the company does not answer, a lawyer or the Small Claims Court can help you ask for specific things, such as the version history of that item. They can also ask for an expert examination, carried out by a specialist and limited to what changed. When the same update affected many people, the case can also go to Procon, the Public Defender's Office or the Public Prosecutor's Office, all described on the channels page.

Where it comes from

This duty of qualified communication also prevents the platform from turning the consumer's silence, passivity or mere permanence in the environment into fictitious acceptance of relevant mutations.p. 658

PTEsse dever de comunicação qualificada também impede que a plataforma transforme silêncio, passividade ou mera permanência do consumidor no ambiente em aceitação ficta de mutações relevantes.

In captive contracts, the so-called supervening “consent” is often nothing more than forced adherence to the new state of affairs, especially when the consumer is already invested in the relationship and depends on the ecosystem to keep enjoying what they acquired.p. 654-655

PTEm contratos cativos, o chamado “consenso” superveniente frequentemente não passa de adesão forçada ao novo estado de coisas, especialmente quando o consumidor já está investido na relação e depende do ecossistema para continuar fruindo aquilo que adquiriu.

Instead of generically requesting “all the data in the system”, the consumer may request, for example, the history of relevant versions of a given item, the trail of screens shown along the purchase path, the minimum records of the specific transaction, the content of the change communication or the category and reason for the sanction applied.p. 660

PTEm vez de requerer genericamente “todos os dados do sistema”, o consumidor pode postular, por exemplo, o histórico de versões relevantes de determinado item, a trilha de telas exibidas no percurso de compra, os registros mínimos da transação específica, o conteúdo da comunicação de alteração ou a categoria e o motivo da sanção aplicada.

The institutional consequence includes orders for preservation and display of records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and review of the lawfulness of the alteration or sanction based on minimum institutional intelligibility.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional inclui ordens de preservação e exibição de registros, perícia delimitada, presunções desfavoráveis ao fornecedor e controle da licitude da alteração ou sanção com base em inteligibilidade institucional mínima.

Collective redress is justified when the harmful source remains unitary, repeatable and incorporated into the ordinary functioning of the platform.p. 774

PTA tutela coletiva se justifica quando a fonte lesiva permanece unitária, reiterável e incorporada ao funcionamento ordinário da plataforma.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Which changes fall outside this protection?

The company can update, and nobody demands that the item stay identical forever or that the service stand still in time. Small touch-ups, minor changes and normal fluctuations in the life of the service are not enough.

A change only in appearance, as a rule, is left out, and liking the old version better is not enough. It starts to count when the item's symbolic value was the core of what was sold. Or when it adds to other signs of a pattern that exploits users.

Even with a relevant loss, the change may still be accepted when the company gives a reason that can be checked. To do so, it must also offer a matching restoration, which means giving back what was lost or something that serves the same function.

Where it comes from

This does not mean requiring perfect identity between the earlier and later versions, nor imposing a freeze on the service.p. 730

PTIsso não significa exigir identidade perfeita entre versão anterior e posterior, nem impor congelamento do serviço.

The trigger is not any minor change, any visual touch-up or any marginal fluctuation typical of the normal life of the service.p. 651

PTO gatilho não é qualquer mudança miúda, qualquer retoque visual ou qualquer oscilação marginal própria da vida normal do serviço.

Equivalence is not mere aesthetic preference for an old experience, nor nonconformity with ordinary variations of the service.p. 477

PTNão é equivalência a mera preferência estética por uma experiência antiga, nem o inconformismo com variações ordinárias do serviço.

Therefore, as a rule, predominantly aesthetic changes fall outside the reach of the concept, except when, in the concrete case, they form a convergent set of signs capable of supporting objective recognition of an unlawful act of architecture through structural indications of abusive teleology.p. 434

PTPor isso, em regra, ficam fora do alcance do conceito alterações predominantemente estéticas, salvo quando, no caso concreto, componham um conjunto convergente de sinais apto a sustentar reconhecimento objetivo de ilícito arquitetural por indícios estruturais de teleologia abusiva.

Even when there is a relevant loss, the mutation may still be legally tolerable if there is verifiable justification and adequate restoration.p. 653

PTMesmo quando há perda relevante, a mutação pode ainda ser juridicamente tolerável se houver justificativa verificável e recomposição adequada.

What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
