Your rights · By problem

I paid a lot for something exclusive, and it came back cheaper. Is that allowed?

You paid more because the item was rare, a special edition or a last chance, and later it went back on sale for less, in another store or in another currency. When exclusivity was used to justify the price, it becomes part of what was sold, and undoing it later can have consequences.

Where it comes from

When the platform sells an item at a high price under the promise of exclusivity, or presents exclusivity as a relevant attribute to justify price and spending, it incorporates this quality into the content of the offer and into the perceived utility of the object.p. 213

PTQuando a plataforma vende um item por valor elevado sob a promessa de exclusividade, ou apresenta a exclusividade como atributo relevante para justificar preço e dispêndio, ela incorpora essa qualidade ao conteúdo da oferta e à utilidade percebida do objeto.

If, after a period, the same item reappears in an alternative store, in a special currency, in an aggressive promotion or at a significantly lower price, and if the temporal limitation of the exclusivity was not adequately and comprehensibly disclosed at the time of acquisition, there is a retroactive reconfiguration of the value delivered, with frustration of legitimate expectation and violation of transparency.p. 214

PTSe, passado um período, o mesmo item reaparece em loja alternativa, em moeda especial, em promoção agressiva ou por preço significativamente inferior, e se a limitação temporal da exclusividade não foi informada de modo adequado e compreensível no momento da aquisição, ocorre reconfiguração retroativa do valor entregue, com frustração de expectativa legítima e violação de transparência.

How do you know whether rarity was part of what you paid for?

A digital item can be copied at almost no cost and sold to everyone, forever. When it becomes rare, disappears from the store or turns into a last chance, that is not down to its nature but to a choice the company made.

This is your case when exclusivity was presented as the reason for the price and the item later came back for much less. It may have come back in an alternative store, in a special currency or in a steep promotion. What defines the problem is that nobody told you clearly, at the time of purchase, that the exclusivity had a time limit.

This is not just vanity. In an item that others see, status, rarity and recognition are part of what you acquired. And the investment also includes the time and the missions you completed to get to it.

Where it comes from

In digital objects, the marginal cost of reproduction tends to fall drastically and, in theory, availability could expand without requiring productive capacity comparable to the factory, logistics and extraction of inputs.p. 209

PTEm objetos digitais, o custo marginal de reprodução tende a cair drasticamente e, em tese, a disponibilidade poderia expandir-se sem exigir capacidade produtiva comparável à fábrica, à logística e à extração de insumos.

For this reason, when a persistent regime of unavailability, exclusivity and last chance is observed in these markets, one is dealing not with a natural condition of the object, but with a deliberate institutional choice, so that scarcity appears not as a fact, but as a project.p. 209

PTPor isso, quando se observa nesses mercados um regime persistente de indisponibilidade, exclusividade e última chance, não se está diante de condição natural do objeto, mas de escolha institucional deliberada, de modo que a escassez não aparece como fato, mas como projeto.

If, after a period, the same item reappears in an alternative store, in a special currency, in an aggressive promotion or at a significantly lower price, and if the temporal limitation of the exclusivity was not adequately and comprehensibly disclosed at the time of acquisition, there is a retroactive reconfiguration of the value delivered, with frustration of legitimate expectation and violation of transparency.p. 214

PTSe, passado um período, o mesmo item reaparece em loja alternativa, em moeda especial, em promoção agressiva ou por preço significativamente inferior, e se a limitação temporal da exclusividade não foi informada de modo adequado e compreensível no momento da aquisição, ocorre reconfiguração retroativa do valor entregue, com frustração de expectativa legítima e violação de transparência.

The conflict appears when the platform retroactively reprograms the regime of circulation of the good and allows items previously treated as rare to return to the market in a new acquisition system. In this scenario, the injury perceived by the original holders does not derive from subjective preference, but from the alteration of the foundation that sustained part of the qualitative expectation of consumption, which was the public signal of exclusivity as a position.p. 452-453

PTO conflito aparece quando a plataforma reprograma retroativamente o regime de circulação do bem e permite que itens antes tratados como raros retornem ao mercado em um novo sistema de aquisição. Nesse cenário, a lesão percebida pelos titulares originais não decorre de preferência subjetiva, mas da alteração do fundamento que sustentava parte da expectativa qualitativa do consumo, que era o sinal público de exclusividade como posição.

In immaterial goods, especially when their enjoyment is publicly visible, utility is inseparable from status, rarity and social recognition, which function as part of the acquired enjoyment bundle.p. 452

PTEm bens imateriais, especialmente quando sua fruição é publicamente visível, a utilidade é indissociável de status, raridade e reconhecimento social, que funcionam como parte do feixe de fruição adquirido.

In the initial cycle, the specialized press described the means of obtaining it as conditioned on a long list of missions and on the circuit of events, which consolidated rarity as an internal marker of distinction relevant to the ecosystem, and not as a simple ordinary aesthetic variation Marshall(Marshall, 2018). The point in question is important because the “investment”, in this sense, is not translated only into pecuniary value, but also includes time, participation in events and conformity with a specific path.p. 452

PTNo ciclo inicial, a imprensa especializada descreveu o modo de obtenção como condicionado a longa lista de missões e ao circuito de eventos, o que consolidava a raridade como marcador de distinção interno relevante para o ecossistema, e não como simples variação estética ordinária (Marshall, 2018) . O ponto em questão é importante porque o “investimento”, neste sentido, não se traduz apenas em valor pecuniário, mas inclui também tempo, adesão a eventos e conformidade com um percurso específico.

What proof of the promise of exclusivity is worth keeping?

It is worth keeping the offer as it appeared, with the words exclusive, rare or last chance, the announced deadline, the price and the currency used. Afterward, it helps to record when and how the item came back, in which store, at what price and in which currency.

Where it comes from

The minimum required proof involves reconstruction of the promotional environment, of the communication pieces, of the symbolic saliences, of the context of presentation of the object and of the relationship between prior promise and later enjoyment.p. 745

PTA prova mínima exigível envolve reconstrução do ambiente promocional, das peças de comunicação, das saliências simbólicas, do contexto de apresentação do objeto e da relação entre promessa antecedente e fruição posterior.

The first source of support is interface trails and decision flow: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screensp. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação

What can you ask for when the rarity has been undone?

Depending on the case, the request may include a refund of what was paid, a price reduction or compensation with something that serves the same function. If the item's return caused you further harm that you can demonstrate, there may also be compensation for losses and damages.

The compensation can target the very point of the loss. In a major game, when old rare skins came back to the store, the company announced that the original owners would receive exclusive versions, which could not be bought with the new currency, in recognition of their investment.

You can also ask for the offer to be corrected and honored and for communication that does not match reality to stop.

Where it comes from

The legal consequence does not end with subjective discomfort, since the subsequent alteration of the attribute that justified the price may demand material restoration through the typical channels of consumer law, by restitution, price reduction or equivalent functional compensation, and may also open room for losses and damages when additional harm arising from the positional deception is demonstrated.p. 214

PTA consequência jurídica não se esgota em desconforto subjetivo, pois a alteração posterior do atributo que justificou o preço pode demandar recomposição material por vias típicas do consumo, por restituição, abatimento ou compensação funcional equivalente, e pode ainda abrir espaço para perdas e danos quando demonstrado prejuízo adicional decorrente do engano posicional.

In a note to the public, the developer states that, when old Prestige skins returned to the store, the original owners would receive exclusive modified versions “in recognition of the investment”, and that these modified versions will not be available for purchase through the new currency then introduced into the ecosystem, Mythic Essence Riot Games(Riot Games, 2022j). This is a symbolic restoration aimed directly at the point of loss, preserving a public differential of distinction for the original holders.p. 453

PTEm nota ao público, a desenvolvedora afirma que, quando skins Prestígio antigas retornassem à loja, os proprietários originais receberiam versões modificadas exclusivas “em reconhecimento do investimento”, e que essas versões modificadas não estarão disponíveis para compra através da nova moeda então introduzida no ecossistema, a Mythic Essence (Riot Games, 2022j) . Trata-se de uma recomposição simbólica dirigida diretamente ao ponto de perda, preservando um diferencial público de distinção para os titulares originais.

The possible institutional consequence covers correction of the offer, functional restoration, injunctive protection over the communication and, depending on the case, invalidation of promotional strategies incompatible with the real experience delivered.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional possível abrange correção da oferta, recomposição funcional, tutela inibitória sobre a comunicação e, conforme o caso, invalidação de estratégias promocionais incompatíveis com a experiência real entregue.

Where can you take a complaint about exclusivity that was undone?

The first step is usually the company itself, with the original offer in hand. The way the item was advertised may be treated as misleading advertising by omission, which is advertising that keeps quiet about what mattered for your decision. The case can also go to Procon and Consumidor.gov.br, both presented on the channels page.

When the same practice is repeated with several items and affects many people, the response can go beyond your case, through the Public Prosecutor's Office or the Public Defender's Office.

Where it comes from

Scarcity, here, may operate as a device for capturing consent under informational inadequacy, with potential classification as misleading advertising by omission and breach of the offer, besides breaking the equivalence between expenditure and utility delivered as legitimately expected.p. 214

PTA escassez, aqui, pode operar como expediente de captação de consentimento sob inadequação informativa, com potencial enquadramento como publicidade enganosa por omissão e descumprimento da oferta, além de romper a equivalência entre dispêndio e utilidade entregue tal como legitimamente esperada.

When the litigation reveals a repeated pattern of opacity, abusive reconfiguration or non-verifiable suppression of functional value, the response need not be limited to the individual case.p. 662

PTQuando o litígio revela padrão reiterado de opacidade, reconfiguração abusiva ou supressão não verificável de valor funcional, a resposta não precisa se limitar ao caso individual.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When is the item's return not a problem?

Creating scarcity, by itself, is not illegal. Selling in a short window can be legitimate when it does not downgrade what was already bought and when it tells you clearly, at the time, about the duration, the way to get the item, the limits of the offer and the chance of it coming back.

Nor do the buyer's feelings settle the matter on their own. Symbolic value counts when it was part, in a way other people can recognize, of the core of what was sold.

Where it comes from

The dividing line does not lie in the fact that artificial scarcity exists, nor in the fact that the offer exploits rarity as a sales technique, but in the way scarcity and temporality are mobilized to produce, predictably, frustration of utility, downgrading of adequacy or precarization of the functional and positional value that grounded the consumer's consent.p. 213

PTA linha de separação não está no fato de existir escassez artificial, nem no fato de a oferta explorar raridade como técnica de venda, mas no modo como escassez e temporalidade são mobilizadas para produzir, de forma previsível, frustração de utilidade, rebaixamento de adequação ou precarização do valor funcional e posicional que fundamentou o consentimento do consumidor.

The sale of a commemorative edition or of a rare cosmetic item for a short window may manufacture urgency and capture value through opportunity, provided it keeps the due delivery intact and offers the consumer clear parameters on duration, form of access, limits of the offer and any future repetition.p. 213

PTA venda de uma edição comemorativa ou de um item cosmético raro por janela curta pode fabricar urgência e captar valor por oportunidade, desde que preserve intacta a entrega devida e ofereça ao consumidor parâmetros claros sobre duração, forma de acesso, limites da oferta e eventual repetição futura.

In these cases, functional equivalence may also require preserving the relational and symbolic density of enjoyment, provided that this dimension makes up, in an objectively recognizable way, the core of the purchase.p. 730-731

PTNesses casos, a equivalência funcional também pode exigir preservação da densidade relacional e simbólica da fruição, desde que essa dimensão componha, de maneira objetivamente reconhecível, o núcleo da aquisição.

Equivalence is not the measurement of the consumer's internal states, feelings or psychological accounts.p. 477

PTNão é equivalência a medida de estados internos, sentimentos ou relatos psicológicos do consumidor.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
