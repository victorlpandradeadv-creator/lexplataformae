Situations · 7.1.2 of the thesis

Situations

Eleven situations in which the regime, grounded in Brazilian law, applies to a dispute with a platform. Each one includes a guide to handling it and the framework, setting out what applies, what is owed, what to prove and what to ask for.

What are the situations for?

The regime needs an operative grid that preserves dogmatic unity across different contexts. The eleven situations play that role as a reference tool. They do not simplify the object. They organize the path from the governance mechanism to the duty of supply, the minimum evidence, the legal qualification and the possible institutional consequence.

The framework operates through cumulative filters. First the object and the regime of enjoyment are identified. Then semiotic vulnerability is qualified and, depending on the case, opacity, mutability and governance by sanction are verified. Open auditability and functional value equivalence operate as transversal filters. Legal qualification comes only afterward, without prejudice to cumulation when the same arrangement reveals more than one problem.

Is this an immaterial object of consumption?

Entry into the regime requires four fields, all of them present. The first is the promised object, identified by the core functions that organize the legitimate expectation of enjoyment. The second is the sustained position of enjoyment, because the acquisition opens a place in the ecosystem, and not a delivery that is exhausted in an instant. The third is infrastructural governance, with internal criteria and executive mechanisms that shape the experience and distribute consequences, and trivial technical mediation is not enough. The fourth is the typical possibility of relevant degradation without total rupture, in which enjoyment still exists on paper but is downgraded, emptied or reconfigured.

Application is decided by these verifiable elements, and not by technological intuition or by automatic deference to the contract. Nor do the criteria depend on full access to algorithms, logs and internal rules. What is demonstrated is the governance relevant to the acquired utility and a stability sufficient to produce patterns of enjoyment and degradation.

How to use this page

Each situation opens with a guide to handling it, covering the facts that usually come up, how to demonstrate them, the normative basis and the precautions. Then comes the framework, in five parts. The parameter is the criterion that organizes the reading of the case. The normative basis is the legal rule on which the claim rests. The consequence is what can be sought, depending on the case.

The CDC functions as the normative infrastructure of these situations. The point is not to invent a new code or to graft a foreign regime onto the statutory text, but to read the categories of the system through the real way supply happens, in which governing the environment is governing enjoyment itself.

The framework does not mechanize the decision. It organizes the path from the governance structure to the duty breached, the minimum evidentiary requirement, the legal qualification and the possible consequence.

The minimum evidence of each situation indicates what makes the conflict demonstrable with possible proof and controllable inferences, so that the discussion does not depend on full access to the platform's algorithms, records and internal rules.

When what was bought depends on the environment the platform administers

a) regime of governed enjoyment and application of the IOC

Which facts indicate a product that depends on the environment?

An item, account, subscription or access whose utility can be realized only inside the platform's environment, with loss of access, permanence or intelligibility, or with a change in the experience through an update. The supplier's typical defense is that it provides only a service or that it granted a mere content license. In games, the object may be one of appearance, progression, access and permanence, functionality, time or reputation, and a single item often combines more than one of these types. This typology is internal to the games laboratory, and not a general classification of platforms. The same traits appear in three business models, games operated as a service, marketplaces and artificial intelligence services supplied by platforms. In all of them, enjoyment depends on the continuous operation of a privately governed environment. In the marketplace, however, not every digital mediation turns the good into an immaterial object of consumption.

How do you demonstrate dependence on the environment?

The structural dependence of the utility on the governed environment, the way the experience is made available and the consumer's concrete position in the relationship. Helpful evidence includes the terms of use that tie the content to the account, the status page that shows dependence on servers and the record of what is in the account and what was paid.

What legal provision underpins the immaterial product?

Article 3, paragraph 1, of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC)1 already admits immaterial products. Whenever the utility consumed presents itself as a position of enjoyment sustained by continuous private governance, there is an immaterial product within the meaning of that provision, and the service dimension does not rule out control over enjoyment. Terms of use that recognize virtual content associated with the account, such as currency, skins and other items, help prove that the object exists, but they do not exhaust its regime. The IOC does not replace the immaterial product of Article 3, paragraph 1, of the CDC1. It gives that product concrete form in continuous and governed consumption. The legal definition of product works as a scope clause, which keeps protection from being evaded when the object has no physical medium.

What stays outside the entry gate?

The four fields of the entry gate are cumulative. Left out are one-off access, the platform that works only as a channel or storefront, the predominantly aesthetic alteration when the symbolic element is accessory and the ordinary variations of the service. There are also conflicts the CDC already resolves with nothing gained by invoking the IOC, such as undue charges, an isolated defect or a specific breach of an offer, when that is the core of the case. Qualification opens the following filters and does not decide the case on its own. Arguing over whether the case concerns a product or a service does not settle the question. The distinction remains useful for calibrating specific consequences, but it does not define the entire regime. The Legal Framework for Electronic Games, Law 14,852/2024, recognizes the sector as an economic and cultural activity, but the sectoral framing helps one see the market and is not enough to control governance, so it does not replace the CDC. The interpretive rule is that initial delivery does not exhaust performance, and continued performance does not erase the object.

When the consumer only understands what the interface lets them understand

b) semiotic vulnerability and governance of meaning

Which facts show meaning governed by the supplier?

Expectation, perception of value, risk assessment or capacity to contest that depend on signs, interfaces and trails controlled by the supplier. The simplest case is a purchase advertised in the storefront as an acquisition and characterized in the contract as a revocable license.

How do you reconstruct the consumer's journey?

The reconstruction of the user's journey, the informational architecture, the communicational salience and the paths of understanding. Useful evidence includes dated screenshots, screen recordings and the purchase, acceptance and confirmation screens, with the messages about rarity, time remaining and discounts. The claim is no longer that the consumer did not understand but that the environment was designed in a certain way.

Which rules deal with vulnerability and information?

Article 4, item I, of the CDC2 recognizes the consumer's vulnerability, which takes its own form in digital environments. Article 6 of the CDC3 guarantees adequate information, protection against misleading advertising and facilitation of the consumer's defense, and Article 39, item IV, of the CDC4 prohibits taking advantage of the consumer's weakness.

Is semiotic vulnerability presumed?

Semiotic vulnerability is not a generic attribute of the digital, and it does not presume the consumer's incapacity. It requires a governance of meaning that acts structurally on understanding, comparability, predictability or contestation. Combined with other elements, it may ground the redistribution of the burden of proof. The expression digital vulnerability names the setting in which the harm occurs, but it does not isolate the mechanism. Semiotic vulnerability targets the unequal administration of meaning and, for that reason, gives proof a sounder framework and better prepares the remedy. It is not a psychological trait of the consumer but a structural mechanism of power.

Which facts show an alteration without a trace?

A relevant change in access, performance, utility, operating rules or sanctions without preserving minimum trails for reconstruction. Which records should be requested to reconstruct the alteration?

Essential logs, the history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements, enough to make the dispute knowable. Where does the duty to leave a trace come from?

Open auditability is a parameter, and not an institution foreign to the system. It unfolds from the duty of information, objective good faith and the facilitation of defense in Article 6 of the CDC3. The request must be surgical, and not a procedural fishing expedition. Articles 8, 10 and 12 of the CDC14 reach the risk that the architecture transfers to the equipment, and Article 6 of Law 13,709/2018, the General Data Protection Law (LGPD)15, requires purpose, necessity and transparency in data processing. When intellectual property becomes a shield

j) reconciliation with intellectual property

Which facts show exclusivity used as a shield?

The supplier invokes exclusive ownership, trade secrets, licensing or the proprietary structure of the ecosystem to resist control over the mode of supply. Typical answers say that the data is for internal use or not accessible to the public.

How do you separate protection of the asset from immunization of governance?

The relation between the prerogative invoked and the concrete aspect of the conflict, showing whether the restriction is necessary to protect the asset or operates as immunization of governance.

Which rules tie exclusivity to the social interest?

Article 5, item XXIX, of the Federal Constitution23 ties the protection of industrial creations, trademarks and other signs to the social interest and to development, and does not protect exclusivity as an end in itself. Property is subject to its social function, and consumer protection is part of the economic order. Articles 7 and 8 of Law 9,609/1998, the Software Law24, impose on the software supplier duties of information and of support during technical validity. Protection of computer programs in Brazil follows a special regime that is predominantly copyright-based, and not a patent logic over function, method or result, so technical control of the infrastructure does not turn into total power over the experience consumed.

Is intellectual property a general veto over the CDC?

Intellectual property remains valid on its own level and is not a general veto over the CDC. Reconciliation is achieved through delimited auditability, with instruments that preserve confidentiality, such as judicial secrecy, through control of functional equivalence and by subjecting the exercise of exclusivity to the duties of adequacy, transparency and restoration. When the service is shut down or servers are permanently switched off, the formal precariousness of the license does not authorize the total sacrifice of what was incorporated into the consumer's trajectory. The exit must be reconciled, as far as technically and legally possible, with continuity, portability, functional restoration or transfer of the operation. The intellectual property infrastructure is also not homogeneous. Trademarks, copyright, software and distinctive signs do not confer the same type or the same extent of exclusivity, and so technical control of the environment does not derive from a unitary sovereignty over the experience consumed. Exclusivity is not to be confused with immunity from auditability. Intellectual property matters here only insofar as it organizes power over enjoyment. It should be neither reduced nor demonized, but its exclusivity does not authorize opacity, opportunistic mutability or armor against control.
