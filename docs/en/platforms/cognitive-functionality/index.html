What happens when the assistant you use changes its behavior?IOCs of cognitive functionality and information regime

You ask, the assistant answers, and you use the answer to decide something, whether that is writing a text, sorting out a work question or understanding a contract.

One day it starts making more mistakes, answering differently or refusing what it used to do, and you have no way of knowing what changed or why.

Where it comes from

It depends on the continuity of the system, the relative stability of the interface, the maintenance of the conditions of access, the model for presenting answers, the warnings, the contextual limits, the memory or history mechanisms where they exist, and the private rules that structure the very field of use.p. 759

PTEla depende da continuidade do sistema, da estabilidade relativa da interface, da manutenção das condições de acesso, do modelo de apresentação das respostas, das advertências, dos limites contextuais, dos mecanismos de memória ou histórico quando existentes, e das regras privadas que estruturam o próprio campo de uso.

What are you actually using?

It does not matter whether the service is called artificial intelligence, an assistant, a chat or a productivity tool. The name decides nothing. What decides is how the service works on the inside and who controls how it works over time.

And the service does not hand out loose answers, the way an encyclopedia would hand out an entry. It chooses what to say, in what order and in what tone, and in doing so it organizes the way you understand the problem that brought you to it.

You trust the answer, put it into your work, review it, correct it and ask again. That is exactly where the value of the service lies, because it does not just answer but makes you treat the answer as a good starting point for acting.

Where it comes from

The application of the regime of the Immaterial Object of Consumption to services of cognitive functionality is not defined by the label of “artificial intelligence”, “assistant”, “chat” or “productivity tool”, but by the governance structure that modulates, over time, the utility delivered and the possibility of reconstructing promise, alteration and functional impact through accessible evidence.p. 757

PTA incidência do regime do Objeto Imaterial de Consumo sobre serviços de funcionalidade cognitiva não se define pelo rótulo de “inteligência artificial”, de “assistente”, de “chat” ou de “ferramenta de produtividade”, mas pela estrutura de governança que modula, no tempo, a utilidade entregue e a possibilidade de reconstruir promessa, alteração e impacto funcional por evidência acessível.

What is offered is an environment of cognitive orientation.p. 757

PTO que se oferece é ambiente de orientação cognitiva.

It is a functional infrastructure that organizes how the user understands the problem submitted, trusts the answer received, incorporates it into their decision-making, reviews it, corrects it or reassesses it in the face of error, fluctuation or change in performance.p. 757

PTTrata-se de infraestrutura funcional que organiza como o usuário compreende o problema submetido, confia na resposta recebida, a incorpora à sua tomada de decisão, a revê, a corrige ou a reavalia diante de erro, oscilação ou mudança de desempenho.

They consume a private arrangement of selection, formulation, prioritization, synthesis, interface language and presentation of answers that organizes the very experience of useful knowledge.p. 758

PTConsome um arranjo privado de seleção, formulação, priorização, síntese, linguagem de interface e apresentação de respostas que organiza a própria experiência do saber útil.

The service is valuable not only because it answers, but because it induces the user to treat the answer as a relevant point of orientation.p. 758

PTO serviço vale não apenas porque responde, mas porque induz o usuário a tratar a resposta como ponto relevante de orientação.

Why is this not just a program you use?

The terms of use of the major assistants themselves show that you do not receive a finished, static tool. You enter an environment governed by the company's rules, policies and guidelines, which it can change, limit and reorganize whenever it wants.

What you use depends on a lot of things that stay on the company's side. It depends on the system staying online, the screen staying similar, the conditions of access, the warnings that appear, the memory of the conversation when there is one and the internal rules of the service.

That is why the law looks at the whole service, and not only at the sentence that appeared on the screen.

Where it comes from

In both cases, the contractual framework does not merely describe access to a static tool.p. 758

PTEm ambos os casos, a moldura contratual não descreve apenas acesso a ferramenta estática.

It describes entry into a regulated ecosystem of use, subject to conditioning, limitation and reorganization by private governance.p. 758

PTDescreve ingresso em ecossistema de uso regulado, passível de condicionamento, limitação e reorganização por governança privada.

In other words, enjoyment does not reside only in the individual answer, but in the functional arrangement that makes it possible to trust, use, review and contest the service rendered.p. 759

PTEm outras palavras, a fruição não reside apenas na resposta individual, mas no arranjo funcional que torna possível confiar, utilizar, revisar e contestar a prestação.

Which two ways of looking at the conflict miss the target?

There are two ways of looking at this conflict that seem reasonable but miss the target.

The first is to think that everything comes down to whether one specific answer was right or wrong. But what you bought was not an answer. It was the continued guidance the service offers.

The second is to treat the case as a simple software license agreement, as if you had installed a program on your computer. That hides the fact that the service changes all the time, and that the one changing it is the company.

Where it comes from

The first is to treat the conflict as a mere dispute over an isolated factual error, as if the usefulness of the service came down to the abstract correctness of each individual answer.p. 757

PTA primeira é tratar o conflito como mera discussão sobre erro factual isolado, como se a utilidade do serviço se resumisse à correção abstrata de cada resposta individual.

The second is reducing the analysis to the generic contractual vocabulary of software licensing or of the technical provision of informational support.p. 757

PTA segunda é reduzir a análise ao vocabulário contratual genérico do licenciamento de software ou da prestação técnica de suporte informacional.

Neither reading reaches the central problem.p. 757

PTNenhuma das duas leituras alcança o problema central.

How does the problem usually show up?

When the answer seems more certain than it is. The text comes well written, organized and confident, and gives an impression of certainty that the service cannot back up.

When you cannot understand what happened. You do not know why you got that answer, whether the service changed its behavior or what influenced the result that guided your decision.

When all the evidence stays with the company. The history, the context of the conversation and the internal records are with whoever provides the service, and not with you.

None of this needs to be an outright lie to count. It is enough that the service trades clarity for an appearance of confidence and leaves you alone with the cost of the error, the change or the loss.

Where it comes from

There may be informational noise when the architecture of the answer projects an appearance of certainty, completeness or sufficiency incompatible with the real limits of performance.p. 759

PTPode haver ruído informacional quando a arquitetura da resposta projeta aparência de segurança, completude ou suficiência incompatível com os limites reais da prestação.

There may be functional opacity when the user cannot reconstruct, in minimally verifiable terms, why a given output was presented to them, to what extent there was a relevant change in the system's behavior or which elements of the environment conditioned the answer that guided their conduct.p. 759

PTPode haver opacidade funcional quando o usuário não consegue reconstruir, em termos minimamente verificáveis, por que determinada saída lhe foi apresentada, em que medida houve alteração relevante do comportamento do sistema ou quais elementos do ambiente condicionaram a resposta que orientou sua conduta.

There may also be acute evidentiary asymmetry when the functional impact of performance depends on history, interactional context, internal parameters or records that remain concentrated in the supplier.p. 759

PTPode haver, ainda, assimetria probatória aguda quando o impacto funcional da prestação depender de histórico, contexto interacional, parâmetros internos ou registros que permanecem concentrados no fornecedor.

In these scenarios, the injury need not appear as an outright lie to be legally relevant.p. 759

PTNesses cenários, a lesão não precisa aparecer como mentira direta para ser juridicamente relevante.

It is enough that the environment replaces legibility with a performance of confidence, shifting to the user the cost of bearing error, revision, fluctuation or functional loss without adequate institutional trails of reconstruction.p. 759

PTBasta que o ambiente substitua legibilidade por performance de confiança, deslocando para o usuário o custo de suportar erro, revisão, oscilação ou perda funcional sem trilhas institucionais adequadas de reconstrução.

This has a name

Semiotic vulnerability. It is the disadvantage of someone who has to trust signals that someone else controls. Here it grows larger, because you depend on the assistant to understand things and have no way of checking the signals that the answer is good, complete and sufficient.

Where it comes from

It is semiotic vulnerability intensified by epistemic dependence, that is, by subjection to an environment that administers the signs of the reliability, usefulness and sufficiency of the answer without granting the recipient, to the same extent, means of verification and audit.p. 758

PTÉ vulnerabilidade semiótica intensificada por dependência epistêmica, isto é, por sujeição a ambiente que administra os signos da confiabilidade, da utilidade e da suficiência da resposta sem franquear, na mesma medida, meios de verificação e auditoria ao destinatário.

How do you prove that the assistant changed?

A wrong answer, on its own, says little. What counts is reconstructing the whole path.

  • The conversation history, with the questions and the answers.
  • The context in which you used the service.
  • The warnings that appeared or were missing.
  • The way the answer was presented.
  • The role the answer played in your decision.

Save what you can while it is still accessible, such as screenshots, exported conversations and dates.

Where it comes from

The relevant evidence tends to require reconstructing the informational journey, the context of use, the available interaction history, the warnings given or omitted, the way the answer was presented and the practical function it served in the user's decision.p. 759-760

PTA evidência relevante tende a exigir reconstrução da jornada informacional, do contexto de uso, do histórico interacional disponível, das advertências presentes ou ausentes, da forma de apresentação da resposta e da função prática que ela exerceu na decisão do usuário.

Which traps make you give up too early?

Thinking the problem is only the sentence. The sentence convinced you because the whole service was built to look reliable, and that counts too.

Thinking the warning that the system can make mistakes settles everything. A generic warning is not enough, on its own, to rule out any problem with the information you received.

Thinking the variation is a technical matter, like changing weather. The assistant's behavior is governed by the company, and that is why the lack of clarity about it must have a limit.

Where it comes from

The first is the tendency to reduce the conflict to the sentence produced, ignoring the semiotic architecture that made it reliable or operational.p. 760

PTO primeiro é a tendência de reduzir o conflito à frase emitida, ignorando a arquitetura semiótica que a tornou confiável ou operacional.

The second is the tendency to presume that generic warnings about the system's limitations would, in themselves, suffice to neutralize any problem of informational adequacy.p. 760

PTO segundo é a tendência de presumir que avisos genéricos sobre limitações do sistema bastariam, por si, para neutralizar qualquer problema de adequação informacional.

The third is the tendency to treat the model's variability as a brute technical fact, rather than as a governed element whose opacity must be legally bearable.p. 760

PTO terceiro é a tendência de tratar a variabilidade do modelo como fato bruto da técnica, e não como elemento governado cuja opacidade precisa ser juridicamente suportável.

What must the company guarantee?

No one can demand that the company open its code or show how the system works on the inside. But in a service made to guide you, it must guarantee a minimum that makes it possible to check what happened.

Depending on the case, this includes keeping the history accessible, keeping minimal records of the conversation and stating clearly the limits that really matter. It also includes giving understandable notice when the rules of use change and making it possible to reconstruct the link between what the service promised and the effect it had.

Without this, the company holds two powers at once. The power to guide you and the power to decide on its own what can be told and proven when the guidance fails.

Where it comes from

It is not a matter of requiring unrestricted opening of the model, code or internal architecture.p. 759

PTNão se trata de exigir abertura irrestrita de modelo, código ou arquitetura interna.

The point is to recognize that, in services whose usefulness consists in guiding the user cognitively, there must be a minimum of institutional verifiability over relevant aspects of the service.p. 759

PTTrata-se de reconhecer que, em serviços cuja utilidade consiste em orientar cognitivamente o usuário, deve haver verificabilidade institucional mínima sobre aspectos relevantes da prestação.

This includes, depending on the case, preservation of accessible history, minimal traces of interaction, sufficient disclosure of materially relevant limits, intelligibility regarding changes in the regime of use and the possibility of reconstructing the link between the functional promise of the service and the impact produced on the user's experience.p. 759

PTIsso inclui, conforme o caso, preservação de histórico acessível, rastros mínimos de interação, explicitação suficiente de limites materialmente relevantes, inteligibilidade sobre alterações de regime de uso e possibilidade de reconstruir o nexo entre promessa funcional do serviço e impacto produzido na experiência do usuário.

Without this, the platform retains not only the power to provide guidance, but also the power to define unilaterally what may be narrated and proven when that guidance proves inadequate, inconsistent or harmful.p. 759

PTSem isso, a plataforma retém não apenas o poder de fornecer orientação, mas também o poder de definir unilateralmente o que poderá ser narrado e provado quando essa orientação se mostrar inadequada, inconsistente ou lesiva.

What can you ask for when trust is broken?

The most fitting response is usually to correct what failed, and not only to pay for the damage. It may be possible to ask for records that can be checked, for the company to review the way the service communicates and for the lost trust to be restored.

In the most serious cases, when the service repeatedly exploits your trust without giving means to check and contest, the very purpose of the design may come into question. This applies above all when the lack of clarity is not a technical leftover but a stable part of the way the service makes money.

Where it comes from

The most appropriate institutional response will tend to favor procedural correction, stronger verifiable trails, a review of the communicational arrangement and functional restoration of the injured trust, rather than the mere abstract monetization of the conflict.p. 760

PTA resposta institucional mais adequada tenderá a privilegiar correção procedimental, reforço de trilhas verificáveis, revisão do arranjo comunicacional e recomposição funcional da confiança lesada, em vez de mera monetização abstrata do conflito.

In more serious cases, above all when the architecture of the service repeatedly manages practical trust without proportionate instruments for reconstruction and contestation, there may be room for a reading in terms of abusive teleology, especially if opacity is not a mere technical residue, but a stable part of the way the environment is exploited.p. 760

PTEm hipóteses mais graves, sobretudo quando a arquitetura do serviço administrar de forma reiterada confiança prática sem instrumentos proporcionais de reconstrução e contestação, pode haver espaço para leitura em chave de teleologia abusiva, especialmente se a opacidade não for mero resíduo técnico, mas parte estável do modo de exploração do ambiente.

How do you know if this is your case?

Answer for yourself.

  • Did you use the service to make decisions or to work, and not just out of curiosity?
  • Did the quality, the behavior or the rules change without a clear explanation?
  • Are you unable to reconstruct what happened without the company's records?
  • Did the change have a concrete effect on what you did or lost?

The more yes answers, the more your case resembles what the law protects here. This does not guarantee the outcome, but it shows where to start.

Enjoyment, here, must be read as governed cognitive enjoyment.p. 758

PTA fruição, aqui, deve ser lida como fruição cognitiva governada.

What is at stake is not only the content issued, but the regime of functional trust in which this content is received, used and converted into conduct.p. 758

PTO que está em jogo não é apenas o conteúdo emitido, mas o regime de confiança funcional em que esse conteúdo é recebido, utilizado e convertido em conduta.

Which vulnerability does the service trigger?

It is semiotic vulnerability intensified by epistemic dependence. The environment administers the signs of reliability, usefulness and sufficiency of the answer without giving the recipient the same means of verification. The inadequacy may appear as informational noise, when the answer projects a confidence incompatible with the real limits. As functional opacity, when it is impossible to reconstruct why the output was presented or whether the system's behavior changed. And as evidentiary asymmetry, when the impact depends on history and parameters concentrated in the supplier.

It tends to require reconstruction of the informational journey, the context of use, the available history, the warnings present or absent, the form in which the answer was presented and the function it played in the decision. Three blind spots recur. Reducing the conflict to the sentence produced. Presuming that generic warnings about limitations suffice. Treating the model's variability as a brute fact of technique, when it is a governed element.

Open auditability plays a central role here, without requiring the opening of the model or code. It includes, depending on the case, accessible history, minimal interaction traces, relevant limits made explicit and intelligibility about alterations to the regime of use.

What consequence is possible?

Informational quality defect, abusive practice through functional opacity, insufficiency of the duty to inform and reinforcement of duties of verifiability. In more serious cases, when opacity is a stable part of the mode of exploitation, there may be room for abusive teleology. The response tends to favor procedural correction, verifiable trails, review of the communicational arrangement and functional restoration.
