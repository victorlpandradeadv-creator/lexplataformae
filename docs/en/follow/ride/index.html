Follow a case

Follow the ride

You work through an app. Who decides how much you earn tomorrow?

You do deliveries or rides through the app and were told you are free to work when, how and where you want. Even so, calls decreased, earnings fell or the account was blocked without explanation.

This path follows your ride, from the moment you turn on the app to the day of the block, and ends with what you can demand.

1 What does the app really supply?

It looks as if it only pays per ride or delivery. But what it supplies is the very place where your work becomes possible, with the demand, the calls, the rating, the priority and the rules for staying there.

Without that place, you do not work. That is why changes to it weigh so much.

Where it comes from

What is provided, in functional terms, is a private infrastructure of economic possibility.p. 765

PTO que se fornece, em termos funcionais, é uma infraestrutura privada de possibilidade econômica.

2 Are you really free?

The contract says so. You switch on and off when you want. But in day-to-day practice, the app measures you all the time and decides what reaches your screen.

Freedom is on paper, and control is in operation.

Where it comes from

The platform asserts independence at the contractual end and intensifies governance at the operational end.p. 767

PTA plataforma afirma independência na ponta contratual e intensifica governança na ponta operacional.

3 Who controls the calls?

The app. It distributes calls, calculates earnings, decides priority and sends alerts, incentives and performance notices. These messages are not just information, because they steer what you do.

You cannot check the criteria. You only see the result at the end of the day.

Where it comes from

Metrics, incentive messages, quality alerts, promotions, acceptance patterns, pricing dynamics and notifications about performance operate as a private language for governing conduct.p. 766

PTMétricas, mensagens de incentivo, alertas de qualidade, promoções, padrões de aceitação, dinâmicas de preço e notificações sobre desempenho operam como linguagem privada de governo da conduta.

4 Why did earnings fall if you worked the same?

Because the rule may have changed. Fare, bonus, area, distribution criterion and the weight of ratings change without clear notice. The account stays active, the name is still partnership, but what you earned shrinks.

That drop is not just a natural risk of the activity when it comes from a change nobody explained.

Where it comes from

Changes to request distribution criteria, pricing parameters, eligibility rules, incentive models or reputational weights may preserve the program's name, the appearance of partnership and even the formal existence of the account, while substantially draining the economic utility that justified joining and sustained the continuity of productive enjoyment.p. 767

PTMudanças em critérios de distribuição de chamadas, em parâmetros de precificação, em regras de elegibilidade, em modelos de incentivo ou em pesos reputacionais podem manter o nome do programa, a aparência da parceria e até a existência formal da conta, mas esvaziar de modo relevante a utilidade econômica que justificava a adesão e sustentava a continuidade da fruição produtiva.

The first is to treat the fluctuation of earnings as a purely natural risk of the activity, when it may result from an opaque unilateral reconfiguration of the distribution infrastructure.p. 768

PTO primeiro é tratar a oscilação de ganho como puro risco natural da atividade, quando ela pode decorrer de reconfiguração unilateral opaca da infraestrutura de distribuição.

5 And when the block arrives?

The account is suspended or deactivated, and the answer is a generic message about a breach of the terms. The path to defend yourself goes round in circles, and meanwhile the income stops.

The app can drop partners. What it cannot do is do so without a verifiable reason and without a real chance of defence.

6 Are you protected?

You are, but the path depends on the case. Consumer law can reach those who use the app to earn an income, when it concentrates control over your permanence, your visibility and your chance to challenge. When it does not reach, the Civil Code applies, with more care over proof.

None of this decides whether there is an employment relationship. What is examined is the environment the app supplies and the way it is governed.

Where it comes from

This incidence does not dissolve the subtype into undifferentiated labor law, but neither does it allow itself to be neutralized by contractual self-descriptions of formal autonomy.p. 768

PTEssa incidência não dissolve o subtipo em trabalhismo indiferenciado, mas também não se deixa neutralizar por autodescrições contratuais de autonomia formal.

7 What can you demand?

You can demand that the app keep and show your account records, explain the concrete reason for the block, review the restriction and give clarity about the criteria that define calls and earnings. This does not require opening the whole system.

Keep the ride history, the statements, the ratings and every message from the app. A lawyer or the Public Defender's Office can help ask the company to show what only it holds.

Where it comes from

This does not mean opening the system entirely, nor turning the Judiciary into the platform's technical administrator.p. 767

PTIsso não significa abrir integralmente o sistema, nem transformar o Judiciário em administrador técnico da plataforma.

Documented case · addition of 17/11/2026

Has this happened before?

Many times. In 2025, the São Paulo Court of Justice upheld iFood's conviction in a blocking case. The courier had his profile blocked, supposedly for recording above-average earnings, and received only generic messages, with no proof of breach. In other cases, couriers banned without notice had their accounts reactivated and were compensated, and a judge found the evidence presented by the company weak.
