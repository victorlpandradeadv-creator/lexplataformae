The person behind this theory

Victor L. P. Andrade

Everything you have read on this site derives from a doctoral thesis in law, submitted to São Paulo State University (Universidade Estadual Paulista) and defended on March 30, 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Luciana Lopes Canavez.

How this work began

The work grew out of a path that ran through the institutional dimension of industrial property and came into focus in interactive entertainment, where enjoyment depends on private decision-making centers and opaque infrastructures. It did not come from a prior institutional agenda but from a discomfort that kept recurring for years, as technical layers stopped being tools and began to work as an environment.

It also came from teaching, and from the need to uphold Consumer Law as a language of protection rather than a ritual. Teaching and following debates on collective consumer redress carried the perspective beyond the contract as a piece of paper, toward consumption as routine, expectation and frustration.

There, a pattern recurred: contemporary harm rarely announces itself as an outright swindle. It tends to arrive as managed normality, as a promise of experience, as an incentive to stay, as a cost one learns to call inevitable.p. 22

PTAli, repetiu-se um padrão: a lesão contemporânea raramente se anuncia como golpe explícito. Ela costuma vir como normalidade administrada, como promessa de experiência, como incentivo de permanência, como custo que se aprende a chamar de inevitável.
