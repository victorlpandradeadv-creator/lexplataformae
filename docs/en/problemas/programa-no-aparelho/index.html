The service requires a program that runs on my device. Can I question it?

Some services only work if you install a program that runs in the background of your computer and starts up with it, even with the game closed. Fighting cheating is legitimate, but the word security does not end the discussion about how far the company can control your machine.

Where it comes from

What is at stake is the redefinition of the material boundaries of the user's sovereignty over their own equipment.p. 521

PTO que está em jogo é a redefinição das fronteiras materiais de soberania do usuário sobre seu próprio equipamento.

The platform's private sovereignty, in other words, no longer governs only the game and begins to contest the minimum conditions of reliability of the user's own machine. Under the vocabulary of security, this expansion usually appears as if it were mere technical inevitability. But the discourse of security does not, by itself, neutralize the legal relevance of the architecture adopted.p. 522

PTA soberania privada da plataforma, em outras palavras, deixa de governar apenas o jogo e começa a disputar as condições mínimas de confiabilidade da própria máquina do usuário. Sob o vocabulário da segurança, essa expansão costuma aparecer como se fosse simples inevitabilidade técnica. Mas o discurso da segurança não neutraliza, por si só, a relevância jurídica da arquitetura adotada.

How do you know whether the program goes beyond what the service needs?

The clearest example is that of an anti-cheat program that runs in the kernel, which is the deepest layer of the operating system, the one that controls how the machine works. It starts when the computer starts, and the company can block the game if it is turned off or removed.

The terms of use, which are the contract you accept when creating the account, also allow the company to monitor, inside your device, the use of the service and to require new programs and updates. And they leave the cost of adapting the equipment to you.

Also included here are requests to change the machine's startup settings and the blocking of other legitimate programs. The company itself acknowledges that changing these settings the wrong way can cause serious problems on the computer.

Fighting cheating is a legitimate interest. The question is whether that interest is enough for the company to gain so much power over your equipment without stronger control.

Where it comes from

When protection of the IOC's competitive environment comes to depend on software loaded at kernel level, that is, on the deepest and most privileged layer of the operating systemp. 521

PTQuando a proteção do ambiente concorrencial do OIC passa a depender de software carregado em nível de kernel, isto é, na camada mais profunda e privilegiada do sistema operacional

Riot's own official communications show that Vanguard is presented as a system composed of a client and a kernel-mode driver, started at computer boot and maintained as a structural condition of enjoyment in more than one game ecosystem, with the possibility of preventing use of the service if it is disabled or removedp. 521

PTAs comunicações oficiais da própria Riot mostram que o Vanguard é apresentado como sistema composto por client e driver em modo kernel, iniciado no boot do computador e mantido como condição estrutural da fruição em mais de um ecossistema de jogo, com possibilidade de impedir o uso do serviço caso seja desativado ou removido

Riot contractually admits the possibility of actively monitoring the use of its services not only on its own servers, but also on the consumer's computer or devicep. 521

PTA Riot admite contratualmente a possibilidade de monitorar ativamente o uso de seus serviços não apenas em seus próprios servidores, mas também no computador ou dispositivo do consumidor

Riot's Terms of Service not only allow active monitoring on the user's device and the running of anti-cheat software in the background, but also reserve to the company the power to change, update, restrict features, require software and compulsory updates, redistribute to the user the costs of technical adaptation and limit its own financial exposure.p. 526

PTOs Termos de Serviço da Riot não só admitem monitoramento ativo no dispositivo do usuário e execução de software antitrapaça em segundo plano, como também reservam à empresa o poder de alterar, atualizar, restringir recursos, exigir software e atualizações compulsórias, redistribuir ao usuário os custos de adequação técnica e limitar sua própria exposição patrimonial.

Riot itself, in documents aimed at VALORANT developers and users, admits that Vanguard may block third-party applications, require sensitive changes to BIOS or UEFI, and require TPM 2.0, Secure Boot, IOMMU, memory integrity and other security layers to be enabled, and it recognizes that incorrect changes to these settings may cause serious problems on the computer.p. 526-527

PTA própria Riot, em documentos voltados a desenvolvedores e a usuários de VALORANT, admite que o Vanguard pode bloquear aplicações de terceiros, exigir alterações sensíveis em BIOS ou UEFI, impor habilitação de TPM 2.0, Secure Boot, IOMMU, integridade de memória e outras camadas de segurança, além de reconhecer que mudanças incorretas nessas configurações podem causar problemas sérios no computador.

The serious legal question was never whether there is a legitimate interest in preserving competitive integrity; of course there is. The question is another: whether that interest is enough to authorize, without reinforced control, the expansion of the supplier's technical power over the material base of the consumer's equipment.p. 532

PTA questão jurídica séria nunca foi se existe interesse legítimo em preservar a integridade competitiva; claro que existe. A questão é outra: se esse interesse basta para autorizar, sem controle reforçado, a expansão do poder técnico do fornecedor sobre a base material do equipamento do consumidor.

What should you record about the program and your computer?

It is worth keeping the screens that asked for the installation or the change of settings, the notices about the program and the date each requirement appeared, because some show up after the purchase, with mandatory updates.

If the computer starts crashing, the failure logs that the system itself generates help. In one reported case, it was by reading these logs that the user linked the failures to the program.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface and decision-flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, remaining time, discount, event or change.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração.

In one of the cases, the user reports recurring BSOD and links the problem to the file vgk.sys, that is, to the low-level driver tied to Vanguard, based on reading the system's own minidumpsp. 529

PTEm um dos casos, o usuário relata BSOD recorrente e associa o problema ao arquivo vgk.sys, isto é, ao driver de baixo nível vinculado ao Vanguard, com base na leitura dos próprios minidumps do sistema

What can you demand from the company about this program?

You can ask for clear information about what the program does, what data it processes, for what purpose and who processes that data. The data protection law requires a specific purpose, limitation to what is necessary and transparency, and clicking accept does not settle that on its own.

You can also question whether what is required is really necessary. Consumer law does not allow risks beyond those that are normal and foreseeable, and an architecture that increases the risk to the device falls under that safety control.

In borderline cases, when the program brings risks and the information about them was insufficient, it may even be treated as a defective or poorly informed product, which opens the door to holding the company liable.

Where it comes from

The processing of data linked to Vanguard cannot be justified en bloc under the generic heading of “security”, because it must observe a legitimate, specific, explicit and informed purpose, necessity, with limitation to the indispensable minimum, and transparency, with clear, precise and accessible information. It also requires an adequate legal basis, is not automatically satisfied by ritualized and generic acceptances, and presupposes technical and administrative measures capable of effectively protecting the data.p. 533

PTO tratamento de dados vinculado ao Vanguard não pode ser justificado em bloco pela rubrica genérica de “segurança”, porque precisa observar finalidade legítima, específica, explícita e informada, necessidade, com limitação ao mínimo indispensável, e transparência, com informações claras, precisas e acessíveis. Também exige base legal adequada, não se satisfaz automaticamente com aceites ritualizados e genéricos, e pressupõe medidas técnicas e administrativas aptas à proteção efetiva dos dados.

The CDC does not allow Vanguard to be rendered invisible as if it were a mere engineering option. Products and services placed on the market may not entail risks to health or safety beyond those normal and foreseeable for their nature and enjoyment, and the supplier may not place into circulation a product or service that it knows or should know presents a high degree of harmfulness or dangerousness.p. 532-533

PTO CDC não permite invisibilizar o Vanguard como se fosse mera opção de engenharia. Produtos e serviços colocados no mercado não podem acarretar riscos à saúde ou segurança além dos normais e previsíveis de sua natureza e fruição, e o fornecedor não pode colocar em circulação produto ou serviço que saiba ou devesse saber apresentar alto grau de nocividade ou periculosidade.

If the architecture intensifies systemic risk, enlarges the attack surface, requires deep technical configurations and may radiate instability to the equipment, then it comes onto the radar of consumer safety control.p. 533

PTSe a arquitetura intensifica risco sistêmico, amplia superfície de ataque, exige conformações técnicas profundas e pode irradiar instabilidade ao equipamento, então ela entra no radar do controle consumerista de segurança.

In borderline hypotheses, liability itself for design defects or for insufficient or inadequate information on use and risks reopens the door to treating invasive software as a defective or inadequately informed product.p. 533

PTEm hipóteses-limite, a própria responsabilização por defeitos de projeto ou por informações insuficientes ou inadequadas sobre utilização e riscos reabre a porta para tratar software invasivo como produto defeituoso ou inadequadamente informado.

Who can you ask what the program does on your computer?

The first request for information goes to the company itself, which must state clearly the reach of the program, the risks and who processes your data, without hiding behind the word security.

If it does not answer or if the program causes damage to your equipment, the case can go on through Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, paths that the channels page details.

Segurança alegada não substitui segurança juridicamente demonstrada.

There are also sources that systematize the case already through the lens of consumer rights and public perception of risk, associating Vanguard with persistent technical presence, absence of a real alternative of refusal, expanded control over the machine and continuous operation outside the strict moment of play. The point is important because it shows that the dispute is neither only technical nor only cultural. It is already perceived as a consumer rights problem, precisely because the enjoyment of the IOC becomes conditioned on a material subordination of the user's equipment to private criteria of security and compatibility.p. 531

PTHá ainda fontes que sistematizam o caso já sob lente de direitos do consumidor e de percepção pública de risco, associando o Vanguard a presença técnica persistente, ausência de alternativa real de recusa, ampliação do controle sobre a máquina e funcionamento contínuo fora do momento estrito de jogo. O ponto é importante porque mostra que a disputa não é apenas técnica nem apenas cultural. Ela já é percebida como problema de direitos do consumidor, precisamente porque a fruição do OIC passa a ser condicionada a uma subordinação material do equipamento do usuário a critérios privados de segurança e compatibilidade.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

Is every deep anti-cheat program illegal?

Not necessarily. A program of this kind is not illegal just because it runs in the deepest part of the machine, and fighting cheating remains legitimate. What is rejected is treating the word security as an explanation that spares the company from showing why so much access is necessary.

Reports from other users about crashes help show the problem, but they do not prove on their own that the program caused the failure. And the extreme case, that of the computer that stops working, is the limit of the problem, and not necessarily the most common one.

Nor is the company being asked to open the program's code. The question is whether the degree of access required is proportional, necessary, transparent and justifiable.

Where it comes from

The point here is not to automatically assert the total unlawfulness of the kernel anti-cheat, but to refuse its legal immunization.p. 533

PTO ponto aqui não é afirmar automaticamente a ilicitude total do anti-cheat em kernel, mas recusar sua imunização jurídica.

It is not a matter of denying the legitimacy of fighting cheating, but of denying that this legitimacy authorizes, by itself, deep, preventive, persistent and opaque technical governance over someone else's machine.p. 534

PTNão se trata de negar a legitimidade do combate a fraudes, mas de negar que essa legitimidade autorize, por si só, governança técnica profunda, preventiva, persistente e opaca sobre a máquina alheia.

Even the most extreme reports of possible bricking or severe loss of availability of the equipment have illustrative value for the argument, even though they do not authorize conclusive expert claims. Their value lies in showing how the risk radiated to the hardware is part of the empirical reception of the case and speaks directly to the thesis that the governance of the IOC may go beyond the game environment and reach the very material functionality of the machine. It is the limit form of the problem. Not the only one, nor necessarily the most frequent, but the one that makes it impossible to pretend that we are only talking about “just another game software”.p. 531-532

PTMesmo os relatos mais extremos de possível bricking ou perda severa de disponibilidade do equipamento têm utilidade ilustrativa para o argumento, ainda que não autorizem afirmações periciais conclusivas. Seu valor está em mostrar como o risco irradiado ao hardware integra a recepção empírica do caso e conversa diretamente com a tese de que a governança do OIC pode ultrapassar o ambiente do jogo e alcançar a própria funcionalidade material da máquina. É a forma-limite do problema. Não a única, nem necessariamente a mais frequente, mas a que torna impossível fingir que estamos só falando de “mais um software do jogo”.

The requirement of open auditability is not to be confused with full opening of the source code nor with suppression of the technical confidentiality of the software.p. 643

PTA exigência de auditabilidade aberta não se confunde com abertura integral do código-fonte nem com supressão do sigilo técnico do software.

The problem becomes one of proportionality, necessity, transparency and justifiability of the degree of technical interference required of the consumer.p. 523

PTO problema passa a ser de proporcionalidade, necessidade, transparência e justificabilidade do grau de ingerência técnica exigido do consumidor.
