5.1.2 On the Immaterial Object of Consumption as an infrastructure-concept

See this section in the PDF

  • Immaterial Object of Consumption (IOC)

p. 407

The description of platform-governed continuous consumption has already established that acquisition is not exhausted by a single act of delivery, since it inaugurates a position of sustained enjoyment in an environment that implements rules, orders priorities and executes sanctions on a permanent basis. It has also been shown, with semiotic vulnerability, that the decisive asymmetry in these regimes cannot be reduced to episodic informational deficits, but arises from a structural inequality of intelligibility and agency in the face of signs, metrics and regimes of visibility administered by the ecosystem itself. These premises call for the construction of a conceptual house capable of converting such a regime into operable legal language, able to represent the object's form of existence, delimit relevant losses and prepare criteria of attribution and restoration compatible with sustained enjoyment.

Properly qualifying the immaterial object of consumption in legal terms requires more than a sociotechnical description of the environment of enjoyment. What is sought is a category capable of organizing, on the basis of the regime actually experienced, the reading of what was offered, of what becomes legally relevant as loss and of what should guide restoration when enjoyment is degraded by unilateral reconfiguration, curation, enforcement or design. By treating the object as a position of sustained enjoyment within a technical-organizational arrangement that administers access, permanence, visibility and circulation, it becomes possible to bring the analysis back to controllable elements of supply, preventing protection from depending exclusively on explicit contractual commands or on episodic assessments of the information provided.

p. 408 The player's account in the Riot ecosystem offers a methodologically privileged example of an immaterial object of consumption in its most “infrastructural” form. It is more than a login credential. It is the organizational point that holds the enjoyment bundle together, because it concentrates, in a single institutional record, the inventory of licensed virtual content, the progression markers, the social identifiers and the reputation signals by which the user's presence is read, displayed and recognized in the environment. In practical terms, most of what is understood as “acquisition” in this type of consumption does not subsist as a detachable object outside this operative account and the maintained service.

This is not described only by the user's experience. The contractual discipline of the service itself gives form to this arrangement by establishing that access to the Services, including so-called Virtual Content, occurs by limited and revocable license, conditioned on compliance with the Terms, and that termination of the account entails automatic cancellation of the license and loss of access to the associated content Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e)420. The account, in this way, functions as the minimal legal infrastructure of the enjoyment bundle, since it organizes the practical entitlement to access, but does not consolidate a regime of possession by the consumer over the content, which remains dependent on the execution environment and on the supplier's rules of private governance.

The contractual definition of Virtual Content reinforces this reading. Champions, skins, emotes and in-game currency make up the catalog of goods that only become enjoyable as an access license associated with the account, and not as things transportable outside it Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e)421. The enjoyment bundle appears, then, as a collection of positions accessible through an p. 409 institutional identifier and an infrastructure that stabilizes what is visible, usable and recognizable in the ecosystem. The account is the unit that aggregates these elements. Therefore, it can be described as the central IOC and, at the same time, as an “infrastructure-concept” in miniature. What one “has” is inseparable from the regime that makes enjoyment operative.

Moving from the declared norm to the norm in operation makes the infrastructural nature of this position even more intelligible. Riot maintains an official service status page, with current messages and a history of incidents, allowing public verification of the integrity of the systems on which enjoyment depends Riot Games(Riot Games, [202-?]f)422. The use value of the bundle, in this condition, is not stock, it is flow. It requires continuity of servers, institutional connectivity and maintenance of the environment. When the service becomes unavailable, it is not only access that fails, but the virtual content itself loses immediate utility, because the environment that makes it enjoyable ceases to operate.

The account is also treated as an object of security and institutional recovery. In official guidance, Riot acknowledges the possibility of a compromised account and states that, in this case, the user must open a ticket to recover it, which reveals that the player's “existence” in the ecosystem is a record susceptible to incidents and dependent on institutional restoration procedures, in addition to good protection practices Riot Games(Riot Games, 2025n)423. It is not, therefore, a good under the consumer's exclusive custody, but a position managed by rules of integrity, validation and intervention, which the supplier admits as a regular part of how the service functions.

The community repository describes the “summoner” as the persistent element, reporting that “summoner accounts” are used to store activities in data and scoreboards, and indicating that, at the end of matches, this profile gains experience, which makes up its progression in the ecosystem (League of Legends Wiki, [202-?]c; League of Legends Wiki, p. 410 [202-?]b)424. This technical vocabulary helps name and make observable what, in the consumer's experience, appears as an accumulation of history, reputation and value. The account aggregates and stabilizes cumulative variables of enjoyment and visibility, which depend on a continuous technical-organizational regime to remain intelligible and operative.

This set makes it possible to understand why the player's account is, here, the paramount example of the IOC. It is the support that makes the bundle enjoyable, commensurable and sanctionable, because it organizes the unit through which the environment grants access, records progression, consolidates inventory and recognizes belonging. When there is blocking, termination or significant degradation of the regime, it is in this unit that the relevant loss tends to manifest itself. The account is not a peripheral detail of performance. It is the very infrastructure of consumption.

The formulation of this category presupposes conceptual sobriety. The focus falls on verifiable structures of governance of the environment and on relevant effects on the consumer's position of enjoyment, so that the category operates as an instrument of qualification, attribution and restoration in continuous consumption. To fulfill this function, the concept must distinguish ordinary variations of the digital regime from significant degradations of the enjoyment bundle that give economic and social meaning to the object.

The construction of this qualifying key remains situated within the discipline of consumption, without purporting to create an autonomous microsystem for the digital. The anchoring point is the concept of product, as provided for in Article 3, paragraph 1, of the Consumer Protection Code, because it is within this framework that it becomes possible to recognize an object of enjoyment that is not reduced to a material medium, but also does not dissolve into an undifferentiated language of “service” capable of erasing what was actually acquired. By starting from the immaterial product as a reference, the intelligibility of supply is preserved and room is opened to describe the object according to its function and to the relationship it establishes, keeping controllable the identification of the promised enjoyment bundle, the characterization of relevant degradations and the orientation of proportional responses when enjoyment is altered by reconfigurations, curations or sanctions executed by design.

The legal usefulness of this category goes beyond naming a contemporary phenomenon of consumption. It must perform a dual function: to qualify the object in a way p. 411 compatible with its form of existence and, at the same time, to provide an interpretive key capable of reorganizing the reading of the offer, of relevant losses and of the possibilities of restoration. From this perspective, the Immaterial Object of Consumption does not function as a descriptive label external to the regime, but as an infrastructure-concept that makes it possible to recognize how promise, execution and governance combine to produce or degrade the enjoyment bundle, even if formal access to the “content” remains available.

Notes

  1. In its Terms of Use, Riot qualifies the use of the services as a “limited [...] revocable license” and links its continuity to the state of the account, by providing that, if the account is terminated, the license is automatically canceled Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). In simple language, this means that access to the ecosystem is structured as a conditioned permission of use, and not as ownership of an autonomous good. The account functions as the institutional mechanism that concentrates this permission and, consequently, the enjoyment bundle. ↩
  2. The Terms define “Virtual Content” as typical digital goods of the game, including “champions, skins, emotes” and “in-game currency”, and state that this is offered as an access license “associated with your account” Riot Games(Riot Games, 2024e). In League of Legends, “Champions” are the playable characters; “skins” are cosmetic variations of these characters; “emotes” are customizable symbols used for visual communication within the game; and “in-game currency” is the balance used to acquire content within the ecosystem. The relevant legal point is that all these items, even when perceived as acquisitions, are described as accesses that remain tied to the account record. This reinforces the account as the infrastructure of the enjoyment bundle, because it functions as the “institutional container” in which the inventory exists and is recognized. This contractual framing produces a relevant semiotic friction. Although the normative text qualifies these items as a revocable license linked to the account, the ordinary experience of acquisition in the client and in the store is organized by the language and rituals of purchase and sale, with commands such as “buy”, “acquire” and “unlock”, which trigger in the consumer expectations typical of stable patrimonial acquisition. With this, the platform mobilizes, at the moment of decision, the social grammar of purchase to induce a perception of possession and permanence, but preserves, in the legal structure of the bond, the possibility of unilateral recomposition and of loss through termination of the account. In this tension, the sign “buy” is not a mere communicational detail. It functions as a category marker that tends to frame virtual content as an acquired good, when, in the regime described by the contract, it is conditioned and institutionally controlled access, which increases the risk of frustrated legitimate expectation and of informational asymmetry in the formation of consent. ↩
  3. The official service status page contains sections such as “Current messages” and records “No recent issues or incidents” when the service is stable Riot Games(Riot Games, [202-?]f). This matters because it makes public a structural fact of continuous consumption, namely that the utility of the enjoyment bundle depends on the technical availability of the environment. When the service is unavailable, it is not only that “the user cannot log in”, but that the virtual content itself ceases to be enjoyable, since it only operates within the service. The page functions as an institutional indicator that the continuity of the object depends on an infrastructure continuously maintained by the supplier. ↩
  4. In official guidance, Riot states that, if the account has been compromised, the user must “submit a ticket [...] to recover it” Riot Games(Riot Games, 2025n), which means that the account is treated as a record of identity and access that can be breached, corrected and restored by the supplier's internal procedures. The relevant difference between the Riot account and a common digital registration is that, in this context, the registration concentrates the enjoyment bundle, and the company itself assumes institutional routines to protect and reestablish this position, which reinforces that the account is not “just a password”, but the infrastructure of the consumer's existence in the ecosystem. ↩
  5. The account is responsible for concentrating the bond of progression and unlocks in the environment, understood on the basis of the completion of tasks and the accumulation of “experience”, a progression marker accumulated at the end of matches, which serves to organize the development of the profile League of Legends Wiki(League of Legends Wiki, [202-?]c). These are concrete variables through which the environment stabilizes accumulated value and identity. This helps make intelligible why the account is the central IOC of the ecosystem, since it is the record that aggregates inventory, history and progression, which is why it is also the point at which enjoyment exists as a position. ↩
