Does Brazilian law already have an answer?

Maybe you are thinking that what we need is a new law just for games and digital platforms. Not quite. What is missing, in most cases, is reading the law we already have in the right light.

The Immaterial Object of Consumption does not call for a separate system with its own rules for the digital world. It is an updated reading that already fits within the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (Código de Defesa do Consumidor, CDC) and the constitutional principles behind it. In other words, the law already had the tools, and what was missing was pointing them in the right direction.

Where it comes from

Instead of proposing an autonomous regulatory microsystem for “electronic games” or “digital platforms”, what is being proposed is a demonstration that the Immaterial Object of Consumption is a form of interpretive updating that, in dogmatic terms, is already internal to the CDC itself and derived from the constitutional principles that inform itp. 36

PTEm vez de propor um microssistema regulatório autônomo para “jogos eletrônicos” ou “plataformas digitais”, o que se está a propor é uma demonstração de que o Objeto Imaterial de Consumo é uma forma de atualização interpretativa que, em termos dogmáticos, já é interna ao próprio CDC e derivada dos princípios constitucionais que o informam

It is not a matter of inventing a new code or of forcibly grafting a foreign regime onto the legal text.p. 549

PTNão se trata de inventar um novo código nem de enxertar, à força, um regime estranho sobre o texto legal.

The CDC already offers sufficient language to face this problem, provided one accepts that, in continuous digital consumption, to govern the environment is to govern enjoyment itself.p. 550

PTO CDC já oferece linguagem suficiente para enfrentar esse problema, desde que se aceite que, no consumo digital contínuo, governar o ambiente é governar a própria fruição.

LawConsumer vulnerability

Consumer law starts from a fact it recognizes on its own terms, that the consumer is the vulnerable party in the relationship.

In digital environments, this vulnerability takes on a shape of its own. It becomes tied to signs, to screens and to private criteria that decide what appears and how each thing can be used. This specific kind of fragility is what gives rise to stronger duties of transparency, loyalty and information for suppliers.

Where it comes from

From this perspective, it is argued that the factual vulnerability of the consumer, expressly recognized in art. 4, I, of the CDC1, takes on in digital environments a form of its own, linked to mediation by signs, interfaces and private criteria of visibility and enjoymentp. 34

PTA partir dessa perspectiva, sustenta-se que a vulnerabilidade fática do consumidor, reconhecida expressamente no art. 4º, I, do CDC , assume nos ambientes digitais uma forma própria, ligada à mediação por signos, interfaces e critérios privados de visibilidade e fruição

[...] in dogmatic terms, the vulnerability intrinsic to immaterial objects of consumption as an interpreted dimension of the factual vulnerability of art. 4, I, of the CDC1, justifying reinforced duties of transparency, loyalty and informationp. 183

PT[...] em termos dogmáticos, a vulnerabilidade intrínseca aos objetos imateriais de consumo como dimensão interpretada da vulnerabilidade fática do art. 4º, I, do CDC , justificando deveres reforçados de transparência, lealdade e informação

What if a course cut half its classes?

A course that cuts half its classes in the middle of the semester and keeps charging the same price is doing something anyone would see as wrong. Consumer law agrees, because it treats as void any clause that lets the supplier change, on its own, the content or quality of what it sells after the contract is signed.

On platforms, this cut rarely goes by that name. Nobody announces they are cutting classes. It shows up as smaller reach, performance worn down little by little, features that keep disappearing, tighter limits or use that costs more to get the same result. The label and the price stay the same while what you get shrinks. The logic, however, is the same as with the course.

Where it comes from

If continuity is the normal regime, the enjoyed utility stabilizes as an expectation that the level of delivery will be maintained, and not as an expectation of formal access to a service that can be reconfigured at the supplier's convenience. That is why the CDC treats unilateral modification of content or quality as nullity.p. 479

PTSe a continuidade é o regime normal, a utilidade fruída se estabiliza como expectativa de manutenção do patamar de entrega, e não como expectativa de acesso formal a um serviço que pode ser reconfigurado por conveniência do fornecedor. Por isso, o CDC trata a modificação unilateral de conteúdo ou qualidade como nulidade.

The example he offers, of the supplier that reduces classes while keeping the same price Nunes(Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]), works as an objective yardstick for platforms.p. 480

PTO exemplo que ele oferece, do fornecedor que reduz aulas mantendo o mesmo preço (Nunes, 2018, [p. 508-509]) , funciona como régua objetiva para plataformas.

The reduction rarely appears as an explicit cut of “classes”, but it may manifest as loss of reach, persistent downgrading of performance, cumulative restriction of functionalities, compression of limits, reordering of visibility or structural changes that raise the cost of enjoying the same utility. The logic is the same. The label and the price are kept, and the delivery of value is downgraded.p. 480

PTA redução raramente aparece como corte explícito de “aulas”, mas pode se manifestar como perda de alcance, rebaixamento persistente de performance, restrição cumulativa de funcionalidades, compressão de limites, reordenação de visibilidade ou alterações estruturais que elevam o custo de fruir a mesma utilidade. A lógica é a mesma. Mantêm-se o rótulo e o preço, e rebaixa-se a entrega de valor.

This does not mean nothing can change. In contracts that last for years, change is part of the deal, because circumstances change. What is expected is that changes serve to keep up the level and quality of what you use, and not to quietly swap what you bought for a version that is worth less.

Good faith, meaning the requirement that the parties deal loyally with each other, goes beyond what is written in the contract. It creates duties of care, cooperation and maintenance. So when the company says the change was just an update, an adjustment or an evolution of the service, that is not the end of the conversation. What matters is showing that the essence of what you bought was preserved, not how complex the technology is.

Where it comes from

[...] and concludes that “the capacity for adaptation, for cooperation between contracting parties, for continuation of the contractual relationship is essential, basic here” [...] This formulation provides the fine fit for functional value equivalence: changes may be admissible and even necessary, provided they are oriented toward maintaining the potential and quality that structure the enjoyed utility, and not toward the silent substitution of the object with a less valuable versionp. 479

PT[...] e conclui que “a capacidade de adaptação, de cooperação entre contratantes, de continuação da relação contratual é aqui essencial, básica” [...] Essa formulação dá o encaixe fino para a equivalência de valor funcional: mudanças podem ser admissíveis e até necessárias, desde que orientadas à manutenção do potencial e da qualidade que estruturam a utilidade fruída, e não à substituição silenciosa do objeto por versão menos valiosa

The consequence is plain, since, if continued performance exposes the consumer to risks of technical obsolescence, compatibility mutations and structural reconfigurations, good faith must require care, cooperation and maintenance as duties of preserving functional equivalencep. 479

PTA consequência é ostensiva, uma vez que, se a execução continuada expõe o consumidor a riscos de obsolescência técnica, mutações de compatibilidade e reconfigurações estruturais, a boa-fé deverá exigir cuidado, cooperação e manutenção como deveres de preservação de equivalência funcional

Whenever the platform invokes “update”, “adjustment” or “evolution of the service” to justify persistent changes, the core of the debate must fall on the demonstration of substantial preservation of the enjoyment bundle, and not on the rhetoric of complexity.p. 480

PTSempre que a plataforma invoca “atualização”, “ajuste” ou “evolução do serviço” para justificar alterações persistentes, o núcleo do debate deve recair sobre a demonstração de preservação substancial do feixe de fruição, e não sobre a retórica da complexidade.

LawSoftware Law

There is even a specific law for computer programs, from 1998. It deals not only with who owns a program and how it is licensed. It also creates duties for the seller.

Under that law, the license contract, the invoice and the packaging must state, in an easy-to-read way, how long the version sold will work technically. And during that period the supplier must guarantee the technical services that keep the program working properly, even if it stops selling that version. In other words, the supplier's responsibility for keeping things working over time has been in the law for decades.

Where it comes from

Law No. 9,609/1998 Brasil(Brasil, [1998]) does not describe only exclusivity and licensing as an enclosure of utilities, but structures positive duties of the supplier linked to the technical life cycle of the software.p. 51

PTA Lei nº 9.609/1998 (Brasil, [1998]) não descreve apenas exclusividade e licenciamento como cercamento de utilidades, mas estrutura deveres positivos do fornecedor ligados ao ciclo de vida técnico do software.

Art. 7 requires that the license contract, the tax document and the media and packaging indicate, in an easily legible way, the technical validity period of the version marketed, and art. 8 converts this period into an obligation to ensure complementary technical services relating to the proper functioning of the program during the technical validity, including when it is withdrawn from commercial circulationp. 51

PTO art. 7º impõe que o contrato de licença, o documento fiscal e os suportes e embalagens indiquem, de forma facilmente legível, o prazo de validade técnica da versão comercializada, e o art. 8º converte esse prazo em obrigação de assegurar serviços técnicos complementares relativos ao adequado funcionamento do programa durante a validade técnica, inclusive quando houver retirada de circulação comercial

LawEconomic order

What about the company's freedom to do business? It exists and is protected, but it is not unlimited sovereignty. Since the 1988 Federal Constitution, free enterprise has gone hand in hand with human dignity and social values.

Consumer protection is, in fact, both a fundamental right and a principle of the economic order itself. That is enough to bring private forms of government, like those of platforms, within the frame of the law when they have broad and repeated effects on your experience, even though they are not the State. Innovating, owning the technology or enjoying market freedom is no license to escape all scrutiny and all responsibility.

Where it comes from

Economic activity, in the 1988 model, is not organized as a space of unrestricted patrimonial sovereignty, because free enterprise appears from the republican foundation bound to the dignity of the human person and to the social values that shape it.p. 504

PTA atividade econômica, no modelo de 1988, não se organiza como espaço de soberania patrimonial irrestrita, porque a livre iniciativa comparece desde a base republicana sob vínculo com a dignidade da pessoa humana e com os valores sociais que a conformam.

Consumer protection as a fundamental right and principle of the economic order already offers sufficient grounds to frame, without artificiality, private forms of government that produce broad and repeated effects on the experience of enjoyment, even though they are not to be confused with state power.p. 502

PTA defesa do consumidor como direito fundamental e princípio da ordem econômica já oferece base suficiente para enquadrar, sem artificialidade, formas privadas de governo que produzem efeitos amplos e reiterados sobre a experiência de fruição, ainda que não se confundam com poder estatal.

Free enterprise, innovation and technological ownership do not operate as safe-conducts for unauditability, shielded irresponsibility or contractual immunization of the supplier.p. 512

PTLivre iniciativa, inovação e titularidade tecnológica não operam como salvo-condutos para inauditabilidade, irresponsabilidade blindada ou imunização contratual do fornecedor.

LawHorizontal effect of fundamental rights

Fundamental rights are those the Constitution guarantees to everyone, such as freedom and equality. For a long time they were seen as protection against the State. Today we recognize that they also apply in relations between private parties, and that is what the expression horizontal effect means.

This matters because the power that weighs on you does not always come from the government. A company can organize, on a large scale, your access, whether you get to stay, your visibility, your reputation and even your chances to prove and to contest. It can also harm your freedom and your equality. And the freedom to contract cannot serve as a screen for such unequal relationships.

Where it comes from

The horizontal effect of fundamental rights no longer seems an abstract theoretical question when one observes that, in contemporary private relations, relevant subjection no longer comes only from the State. It also emerges from private centers of power capable of organizing access, permanence, visibility, reputation, proof and contestation on a scale sufficient to affect, structurally, the practical freedom and material equality of the subject bound by the relationship.p. 512

PTA eficácia horizontal dos direitos fundamentais deixa de parecer questão teórica abstrata quando se observa que, nas relações privadas contemporâneas, a sujeição relevante já não provém apenas do Estado. Ela também emerge de centros privados de poder capazes de organizar acesso, permanência, visibilidade, reputação, prova e contestação em escala suficiente para afetar, de modo estrutural, a liberdade prática e a igualdade material do sujeito submetido à relação.

In such contexts, private autonomy cannot function as a screen to shield materially asymmetrical arrangementsp. 512

PTEm contextos assim, a autonomia privada não pode funcionar como biombo para blindar arranjos materialmente assimétricos

The key lies in recognizing that, in contemporary society, relevant violations of fundamental rights come not only from the State, but also from private subjects endowed with economic, informational and organizational powerp. 24

PTA chave está em reconhecer que, na sociedade contemporânea, violações relevantes a direitos fundamentais não partem apenas do Estado, mas também de sujeitos privados dotados de poder econômico, informacional e organizacional

LawSocial function of intellectual property

The Constitution protects inventions, trademarks and other creations. But it ties this protection to a goal, which is the social interest and the technological and economic development of the country. Exclusivity is not an end in itself.

That is why owning the work is not enough, on its own, to justify changing what you use without limit, deciding in secret or punishing you without a chance to contest. The Constitution gives intellectual property a frame with a purpose, not a blank check. The company still owns the game, but it exercises that right within this frame.

Nor does it stay outside. In practice, intellectual property helps build the environment itself, because it is through it that the platform defines what can be accessed, kept, changed, withdrawn and contested.

Where it comes from

In dealing with industrial property, art. 5, XXIX does not grant a self-justified patrimonial prerogative, nor does it protect exclusivity as an end in itself.p. 702

PTAo tratar da propriedade industrial, o art. 5º, XXIX não outorga prerrogativa patrimonial autojustificada, nem protege a exclusividade como fim em si.

On the contrary, it expressly subordinates the legal guarantee of the privilege over industrial inventions and the protection of industrial creations, trademarks, company names and other distinctive signs to the social interest and the technological and economic development of the Countryp. 702

PTAo contrário, subordina expressamente a garantia legal do privilégio sobre inventos industriais e da proteção às criações industriais, marcas, nomes de empresa e outros signos distintivos ao interesse social e ao desenvolvimento tecnológico e econômico do País

The infraconstitutional framework of patrimonial intellectual property does not, therefore, receive a constitutional blank check; it receives a purposive frame.p. 702

PTA disciplina infraconstitucional da propriedade intelectual patrimonial não recebe, portanto, um cheque em branco constitucional; recebe uma moldura finalística.

Thus, the mere ownership of an IP right is not enough to legitimize, by itself, unilateral mutability of enjoyment, decisional opacity, uncontestable private sanction or destruction of utilities incorporated into the bond of continuous consumption.p. 702-703

PTAssim, a mera titularidade de um direito de PI não basta para legitimar, por si só, mutabilidade unilateral da fruição, opacidade decisória, sanção privada incontrastável ou destruição de utilidades incorporadas ao vínculo de consumo contínuo.

It operates, in practice, as part of the legal infrastructure through which the platform defines what can be accessed, kept, altered, withdrawn, audited and contested.p. 680

PTOpera, na prática, como parte da infraestrutura jurídica pela qual a plataforma define o que pode ser acessado, mantido, alterado, retirado, auditado e contestado.

They do not operate merely as patrimonial shields detached from the consumption experience, but as legal techniques for organizing the very environment in which enjoyment becomes possible, recognizable and economically exploitable.p. 681

PTEles não operam apenas como escudos patrimoniais destacados da experiência de consumo, mas como técnicas jurídicas de organização do próprio ambiente em que a fruição se torna possível, reconhecível e economicamente explorável.

What if the game shuts down? One day the company may end the service or switch off the servers for good. Even so, the fact that everything is stored in its structure does not turn your history, your collection, your progress and your reputation into worthless things that can simply vanish.

The company has the right to end or reorganize the business, but this right does not include erasing everything with no regard for the people who were in it. If the end is inevitable, the exit must be handled with some care, as far as possible. That may mean keeping the service running in another way, letting you take what is yours elsewhere, restoring what was lost or handing the operation over to someone else.

Where it comes from

There is already, within IP itself, internal resistance to the idea that every immaterial bond can be dissolved by a simple dispositive decision of the owner. [...] This limit comes into particularly sharp focus in cases of termination of activities, discontinuation of services or definitive shutdown of servers.p. 709

PTJá há, no interior da própria PI, resistência interna à ideia de que todo vínculo imaterial possa ser dissolvido por simples decisão dispositiva do titular. [...] Esse limite ganha contorno particularmente nítido em hipóteses de encerramento de atividades, descontinuação de serviços ou desligamento definitivo de servidores.

Histories, libraries, progressions, projected identities, reputational positions and long-term investments cannot be treated as residues without density merely because they are technically housed in someone else's infrastructure.p. 709

PTHistóricos, bibliotecas, progressões, identidades projetadas, posições reputacionais e investimentos de longa duração não podem ser tratados como resíduos sem densidade apenas porque se encontram tecnicamente alojados em infraestrutura alheia.

The right to exploit, terminate or reorganize economic activity is not to be confused with a supposed right to annihilate, without duties of transition, preservation or transfer, the functional enjoyment already stabilized in the consumer's sphere.p. 709

PTO direito de explorar, encerrar ou reorganizar atividade econômica não se confunde com um suposto direito de aniquilar, sem deveres de transição, preservação ou transferência, a fruição funcional já estabilizada na esfera do consumidor.

If the termination of the service is unavoidable, the constitutional order requires that it be reconciled, as far as technically and legally possible, with solutions of continuity, portability, functional restoration, assignment of the operation or other suitable measuresp. 709

PTSe o encerramento do serviço se impõe, a ordem constitucional exige que ele seja compatibilizado, tanto quanto técnica e juridicamente possível, com soluções de continuidade, portabilidade, recomposição funcional, cessão da operação ou outras medidas idôneas

LawLegal framework for games

Since 2024, Brazil has had a law that recognizes electronic games as an economic and cultural activity with importance of its own. It covers games that are downloaded or streamed over the internet, and it makes clear that gaming is neither a minor pastime nor a matter beyond the reach of the law.

The law guarantees the freedom to create, sell and exploit games, but under the Constitution and the laws in force. It also says that games must respect fundamental rights, consumer protection, the full protection of children and adolescents and the privacy and personal data of those who play. That is why it is not a free-for-all dressed up as modernity.

Where it comes from

A sufficient milestone to record this fact is Law 14,852/2024 Brasil(Brasil, [2024]b), taken here as a reference of institutional recognition of the electronic games industry as an economic and cultural activity with relevance of its own.p. 493

PTUm marco suficiente para registrar esse dado é a Lei 14.852/2024 (Brasil, [2024]b) , tomada aqui como referência de reconhecimento institucional da indústria de jogos eletrônicos como atividade econômica e cultural com relevância própria.

[...] it regulates its central economic chain — manufacture, import, sale, development and commercial use — and provides a normative definition of what counts as an electronic game, including interactive audiovisual works developed as computer programs, devices and accessories, and software consumed by download or streamingp. 543

PT[...] disciplina sua cadeia econômica central — fabricação, importação, comercialização, desenvolvimento e uso comercial — e fornece definição normativa do que conta como jogo eletrônico, incluindo obras audiovisuais interativas desenvolvidas como programa de computador, dispositivos e acessórios, e softwares consumidos por download ou streaming

The law affirms the freedom of manufacture, import, sale, development and commercial use of electronic games, but does so with express reference to art. 173 of the Constitution2 and to the legislation in force.p. 544

PTA lei afirma a liberdade de fabricação, importação, comercialização, desenvolvimento e uso comercial dos jogos eletrônicos, mas o faz sob remissão expressa ao art. 173 da Constituição e à legislação vigente.

The framework organizes the sector institutionally through principles and guidelines and, at its core most important for this thesis, affirms that electronic games must be conceived in light of respect for fundamental rights and democratic values, consumer protection, the full protection of children and adolescents, and the preservation of privacy, the protection of personal datap. 544

PTO marco organiza o setor institucionalmente mediante princípios e diretrizes e, no seu núcleo mais importante para esta tese, afirma que os jogos eletrônicos devem ser pensados à luz do respeito aos direitos fundamentais e aos valores democráticos, da defesa do consumidor, da proteção integral da criança e do adolescente, e da preservação da privacidade, da proteção de dados pessoais

This is relevant because the framework does not enshrine a sectoral anything-goes dressed up as friendly modernityp. 544

PTIsso é relevante porque o marco não consagra um vale-tudo setorial fantasiado de modernidade simpática

Do platforms need a new law?

The way, then, is not to invent another law, nor to create parallel rules just for platforms. It is to adjust the reading of what is already written. What was missing were names capable of making certain losses visible, so that they can be shown, proved and claimed.

This also means that nothing here decides a case on its own. The tools exist, but each conflict still depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how whoever decides reads the facts.

Where it comes from

It is this shift that makes it possible to qualify the unlawful act of architecture without leaving the grammar of the CDC and without yielding to the temptation of inventing a parallel law for platforms.p. 665

PTÉ esse deslocamento que permite qualificar o ilícito de arquitetura sem sair da gramática do CDC e sem cair na tentação de inventar um direito paralelo para plataformas.

When categories capable of making certain losses legally visible, of naming, demonstrating and imputing them, are lacking, the space of irresponsibility does not remain neutral and comes to function as a margin for optimization.p. 25

PTQuando faltam categorias capazes de tornar certas perdas juridicamente visíveis, de nomeá-las, demonstrá-las e imputá-las, o espaço da irresponsabilidade não permanece neutro e passa a funcionar como margem de otimização.

This framework does not aim to mechanize the decision nor to reduce the complexity of the environment to a rigid formula.p. 748

PTEsse quadro não pretende mecanizar a decisão nem reduzir a complexidade do ambiente a fórmula rígida.

What can you demand?

What you bought is a product, even with nothing in your handsWhat you bought is a product, even with nothing in your hands

When does what you bought depend on the platform?

When what you use depends, structurally, on an environment the platform runs, adjusts and can change. Think of the skin that only works inside the game, or the account that holds your inventory and your progress. What matters here is whether this dependence affects your access, whether you get to stay, how much you can understand of what is happening or the updates to the experience.

What must the platform preserve?

It must keep what you bought accessible, understandable and preserved in whatever makes it useful. The platform can change the environment, because change is part of this kind of supply, but only within the limits of what is legitimate.

How can you show what you bought and how you use it?

Show that what you use depends on the platform's environment, and how the platform delivers this experience. It also helps to show your concrete position in the relationship, such as what is in your account, what you paid and what that lets you do.

What if the company says it is just a service or a license?

You can ask for the case to be handled under consumer law, with what you bought treated as an immaterial product. The company cannot get out of this just by saying it provides a service or granted a simple license, because the service is precisely how the product is kept working.

Does recognizing the product already settle the case?

This is the first step, not the end of the case. Recognizing the product opens the door to the other duties on this page, but each of them still depends on what happened to you. You do not need to fit every problem into this category to be protected.

Where it comes from

Whenever the consumed utility depends structurally on an environment administered, modulated and made mutable by private governance, with impact on access, permanence, intelligibility or updating of the experience, the field of application of the IOC will be established.p. 743

PTSempre que a utilidade consumida depender estruturalmente de ambiente administrado, modulado e mutável por governança privada, com impacto sobre acesso, permanência, inteligibilidade ou atualização da experiência, estará configurado o campo de incidência do OIC.

The corresponding duty of supply is to ensure functionally accessible, intelligible and preserved enjoyment within the limits of the legitimate mutability of the environment.p. 743

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de assegurar fruição funcionalmente acessível, inteligível e preservada dentro dos limites da legítima mutabilidade do ambiente.

The minimum required proof concerns the demonstration of the structural dependence of the utility on the governed ecosystem, of the way the experience is made available and of the consumer's concrete position in the bond.p. 743

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre a demonstração da dependência estrutural da utilidade em relação ao ecossistema governado, da forma de disponibilização da experiência e da posição concreta do consumidor no vínculo.

The resulting legal qualification is that of an immaterial product subject to the regime of art. 3, § 1, of the CDC3, without the service dimension of the bond removing control of enjoyment as the core of the supply.p. 743

PTA qualificação jurídica daí resultante é a de produto imaterial submetido ao regime do art. 3º, § 1º, do CDC , sem que a dimensão prestacional do vínculo afaste o controle da fruição como núcleo do fornecimento.

The possible institutional consequence consists in opening the subsequent filters of the regime, preventing the controversy from being neutralized by the claim of mere provision of a service or simple formal licensing.p. 743

PTA consequência institucional possível consiste na abertura dos filtros subsequentes do regime, impedindo que a controvérsia seja neutralizada pela alegação de mera prestação de serviço ou de simples licenciamento formal.

The performance does not disappear, but appears as a mode of sustaining, updating and shaping the object of consumption itself.p. 718

PTA prestação não desaparece, mas comparece como modo de sustentação, atualização e conformação do próprio objeto de consumo.

The fifth limit is the case in which the core of the conflict is something else, such as a classic occasional defect, undue charge, localized misleading advertising or specific nonperformance of an offer. In these cases, common consumer law dogmatics tends to resolve the dispute with no real gain in mobilizing the IOC.p. 435

PTO quinto limite é o caso em que o núcleo do conflito é outro, como vício pontual clássico, cobrança indevida, publicidade enganosa localizada ou inadimplemento específico de oferta. Nessas hipóteses, a dogmática consumerista comum tende a resolver o litígio sem ganho real em mobilizar o OIC.
