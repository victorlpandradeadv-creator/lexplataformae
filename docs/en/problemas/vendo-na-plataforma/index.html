Your rights · By problem

I sell on a platform. Does consumer law protect me?

You sell in an online store or use the platform to make money, and you have heard that consumer law does not apply to you because you run a business. Earning income through the platform does not, by itself, take that protection away. But it reaches you only under certain conditions, which depend on how much control the platform has over your place there.

Where it comes from

A seller who depends on ranking, reputation, visibility and opaque prioritization rules does not become immune to consumer protection merely because they sell.p. 778

PTO seller que depende de ranking, reputação, visibilidade e regras opacas de priorização não se torna imune à tutela consumerista apenas porque vende.

The existence of an economic interest on the part of the user does not, in itself, eliminate the vulnerability arising from the governance of the ecosystem.p. 779

PTA existência de interesse econômico do usuário não elimina, por si, a vulnerabilidade decorrente da governança do ecossistema.

How can you tell whether your dependence on the platform counts in your favor?

What matters is how far the platform governs your presence on it. The question is whether your continued presence and your ability to understand the environment, reconstruct a dispute and challenge important decisions are in its hands.

There are four strong signs. The platform shapes how the relationship itself is understood, holds the evidence of what it does, allocates opportunities and risks without clarity and decides whether you stay.

Sellers depend on the ranking, the reputation and the prominence that the platform controls. It does not need to expel a seller to take away their real chance to compete, because small advantages in visibility, day after day, can play that role.

Protection is stronger when what the platform sells you, as a product, is a position that is maintained over time within it. One example is your store's place in the shop window.

Where it comes from

The peripheral professionalized user does not automatically become the strong party to the relationship just because their activity has an economic purpose. In all these cases, the legally relevant question lies in the density of the platform's governance over their insertion in the ecosystem. If permanence, the intelligibility of the environment, the reconstruction of the conflict and the challenge of relevant decisions remain concentrated under the platform's private control, the a priori exclusion of the CDC by formal label loses its footing.p. 778

PTO usuário profissionalizado periférico não se converte automaticamente em parte forte da relação só porque sua atividade tem finalidade econômica. Em todos esses casos, a questão juridicamente relevante está na densidade da governança da plataforma sobre sua inserção no ecossistema. Se a permanência, a inteligibilidade do ambiente, a reconstrução do conflito e a contestação de decisões relevantes permanecem concentradas sob controle privado da plataforma, a exclusão apriorística do CDC por rótulo formal perde aderência.

What is asserted is something narrower and more serious. When the platform organizes the very intelligibility of the bond, concentrates the evidence of what it does, opaquely distributes opportunities and risks and conditions the continuity of the subject's insertion in the environment, the automatic exclusion of consumer protection by reference to nominal appearances of professionalism or partnership becomes methodologically poor and normatively dangerous.p. 780

PTO que se afirma é algo mais delimitado e mais sério. Quando a plataforma organiza a própria inteligibilidade do vínculo, concentra a prova do que faz, distribui opacamente oportunidades e riscos e condiciona a continuidade da inserção do sujeito no ambiente, a exclusão automática da tutela consumerista por referência a aparências nominais de profissionalidade ou parceria torna-se metodologicamente pobre e normativamente perigosa.

In marketplaces, it may involve the way ranking, prominence, seller rating and sponsorship logic produce a perceptible market that has already been preselected.p. 763

PTEm marketplaces, pode envolver a forma como ranking, destaque, classificação de seller e lógica de patrocínio produzem um mercado perceptível já previamente selecionado.

The platform does not need to formally expel a competitor in order to restrict its competitiveness.p. 770

PTA plataforma não precisa expulsar formalmente um concorrente para restringir sua competitividade.

In markets of this nature, internal preferencing, self-preferencing, informational barriers, capture of competitive intelligence and opaque discrimination do not necessarily depend on overt acts of exclusion. They may operate through structural micro-advantages, produced by the continuous administration of the visibility, comparability and intelligibility of the ecosystem.p. 770

PTEm mercados dessa natureza, preferenciamento interno, autoprivilegiamento, barreiras informacionais, captura de inteligência competitiva e discriminação opaca não dependem necessariamente de atos ostensivos de exclusão. Podem operar por microvantagens estruturais, produzidas pela administração contínua da visibilidade, da comparabilidade e da inteligibilidade do ecossistema.

Once the applicability of art. 29 of the CDC1 is admitted to qualify legal persons in a factual position of vulnerability, the regime proves fully pertinent when the immaterial object in question is the enjoyment bundle in a sustained position exploited as a product in the B2B market by the holder and offered, through the marketplace platform, to the seller (vendedor).p. 434

PTAdmitida a aplicabilidade do art. 29 do CDC para qualificar pessoas jurídicas em posição factual de vulnerabilidade, o regime se mostra plenamente pertinente quando o objeto imaterial em questão é o feixe de fruição em posição sustentada explorado como produto no mercado B2B pela detentora e oferecido, por via da plataforma marketplace, ao seller (vendedor).

Which records of your store are worth keeping?

The platform can adjust visibility, reputation scores, recommendations and return rules. It can also adjust anti-fraud rules and the way disputes are resolved, and all of this changes your risk and your costs without any explicit cutoff. That is why it is worth keeping a dated record of your reputation, the position of your listings in search and every notice of a rule change.

Sales records, ratings and decisions are held by the platform. It is also worth keeping the penalties you receive, the reason given, the appeal route offered and the replies from customer service, because this is the evidence you can actually get.

Where it comes from

Modulations in visibility algorithms, reputational metrics, recommendation patterns, return policies, anti-fraud rules and dispute parameters can reconfigure risks, transaction costs and the practical viability of choices without taking the form of an explicit interruption.p. 487

PTModulações em algoritmos de visibilidade, métricas reputacionais, padrões de recomendação, políticas de devolução, regras antifraude e parâmetros de disputa podem reconfigurar riscos, custos de transação e viabilidade prática de escolhas sem assumir a forma de interrupção explícita.

Structural dependence manifests itself through the concentration, within the platform, of transaction records, reputational credentials and decision-making instances, intensifying asymmetries of intelligibility and agency.p. 487

PTA dependência estrutural se manifesta pela concentração, na plataforma, de registros de transação, credenciais reputacionais e instâncias decisórias, intensificando assimetrias de inteligibilidade e de agência.

The minimum required proof falls on sanction records, contestation flow, criteria deployed, structural difficulties of reversal and concrete impacts of the measure on the continuity of the experience.p. 746

PTA prova mínima exigível recai sobre registros de sanção, fluxo de contestação, critérios mobilizados, dificuldades estruturais de reversão e impactos concretos da medida sobre a continuidade da experiência.

What can a seller ask of the platform?

The request may include minimum clarity about the criteria that decide the order of offers and about the preference the platform gives to some of them. It may also include oversight of changes that push your position down without excluding you, and a careful look at how it uses the data it collects while intermediating sales.

When consumer law reaches sellers, it applies above all to the platform's commercial practices and to the contract clauses. When you are penalized, it also matters that there is a reason you can check and a real way to challenge it.

Where it comes from

Whenever the platform, in organizing enjoyment, also comes to organize in an opaque and asymmetric manner the perceptible conditions of competition within the ecosystem, there should be a requirement of controllability compatible with the dual dimension of the problem. This means, at the least, minimum auditability over relevant ranking criteria, intelligibility about prominence and preferencing practices, control of changes that deform the functional equivalence of competitive permanence and a non-naive reading of the use of data obtained through the very position of intermediation.p. 773

PTSempre que a plataforma, ao organizar a fruição, passar também a organizar de maneira opaca e assimétrica as condições perceptíveis de competição no interior do ecossistema, deverá haver exigência de controlabilidade compatível com a dupla dimensão do problema. Isso significa, no mínimo, auditabilidade mínima sobre critérios relevantes de hierarquização, inteligibilidade sobre práticas de destaque e preferenciamento, controle de alterações que deformem a equivalência funcional da permanência concorrencial e leitura não ingênua do uso de dados obtidos pela própria posição de intermediação.

[...] the Court describes art. 29 as bearing its own very broad concept of consumer by equivalence, especially aimed at the chapters on commercial practices and contractual protectionp. 779

PT[...] a Corte descreve o art. 29 como portador de um conceito próprio e amplíssimo de consumidor por equiparação, especialmente voltado aos capítulos das práticas comerciais e da proteção contratual

In platform ecosystems, sanctions do not operate merely as a one-off disciplinary response to conduct attributed to the user. They can affect access, permanence, visibility, reputation, relational insertion and continuity of enjoyment. For this reason, they must be controlled by denser criteria than the mere formal existence of sanctioning power. They require verifiable reasoning, decisional coherence and intelligible avenues of contestation.p. 749

PTEm ecossistemas de plataforma, sanções não operam apenas como resposta disciplinar pontual a uma conduta atribuída ao usuário. Elas podem afetar acesso, permanência, visibilidade, reputação, inserção relacional e continuidade da fruição. Por isso, devem ser controladas por critérios mais densos do que a simples existência formal de poder sancionatório. Exigem motivação verificável, coerência decisória e vias inteligíveis de contestação.

Where can a seller take the complaint?

The first step is usually the platform's channel for sellers, in writing and with screenshots saved. Since a seller's protection depends on showing the dependence and the test is demanding, a lawyer can help you see whether your case qualifies.

When the rule change affects all sellers at the same time, because it comes from the platform's design, the response may be collective. The matter may also concern competition law, which looks after fair competition among sellers.

Where it comes from

The question then ceases to be whether the subject is “too much of a businessperson” to deserve protection and becomes whether the relationship, as structured, remains marked by material asymmetry of power and by submission to legally controllable private governance.p. 780

PTA questão deixa então de ser se o sujeito é “empresário demais” para merecer tutela e passa a ser se a relação, tal como estruturada, continua marcada por assimetria material de poder e por submissão a governança privada juridicamente controlável.

An opaque sanctions policy, a non-auditable regime of functional change, an interface logic that dilutes the essential under informational excess, a materially decisive ranking system without usable history or a trust architecture that absorbs and neutralizes contestation before it becomes litigation are all examples of problems whose unity lies in the design, not in the episode.p. 775

PTUma política opaca de sanções, um regime não auditável de alteração funcional, uma lógica de interface que dilui o essencial sob excesso informacional, um sistema de ranqueamento materialmente decisivo sem histórico aproveitável ou uma arquitetura de confiança que absorve e neutraliza contestação antes que ela se torne litígio são todos exemplos de problemas cuja unidade está no desenho, não no episódio.

The platform that governs the user's experience governs, at the same time, the perceptible field in which competition becomes possible, legible and economically effective.p. 769

PTA plataforma que governa a experiência do usuário governa, ao mesmo tempo, o campo perceptível em que a competição se torna possível, legível e economicamente eficaz.

The complaint channels and the deadlines

When does a seller fall outside consumer law?

Not every seller or partner will be treated as a consumer by equivalence, meaning someone the law protects as a consumer because they are exposed to the company's practices. And not every relationship carried out through technology attracts consumer law.

Protection also does not erase the difference between consumer and company. The Superior Court of Justice (STJ), the court that has the final word on consumer law, treats this extension as an exception. It applies above all to a company that contracts something outside its own area of activity.

And when the platform only sells advertising space and help finding customers, without controlling the sales that follow, this reading does not apply, in principle.

Where it comes from

“Art. 29. For the purposes of this Chapter and the following one, all persons, determinable or not, exposed to the practices provided for therein are equated to consumers” Brasil(Brasil, [2022]).p. 779

PT“Art. 29. Para os fins deste Capítulo e do seguinte, equiparam-se aos consumidores todas as pessoas determináveis ou não, expostas às práticas nele previstas” (Brasil, [2022]) .

The criterion remains demanding. Not every participant in a platform ecosystem will be a consumer by equivalence. Not every relationship economically mediated by technology will justify application of the CDC.p. 780

PTO critério continua exigente. Nem todo participante de ecossistema de plataforma será consumidor equiparado. Nem toda relação economicamente mediada por tecnologia justificará incidência do CDC.

The point is not to dissolve every distinction between consumer and economic agent.p. 779

PTO ponto não é dissolver toda distinção entre consumidor e agente econômico.

[...] and from it derives the possibility of equating the vulnerable legal person, especially when it acts outside its core specialty, as a way of preventing formal parity from neutralizing the material protection of the CDC.p. 779

PT[...] e dele extrai a possibilidade de equiparação da pessoa jurídica vulnerável, sobretudo quando atua fora de seu núcleo de especialidade, como forma de impedir que a paridade formal neutralize a tutela material do CDC.

The second limit is the scenario in which the platform functions as a channel or storefront, but does not govern, in structural terms, the acquired utility, lacking the infrastructural centrality characteristic of the IOC. This is the case that rules out, in principle, the marketplace that provides positioning and customer acquisition services through advertising, but does not exercise relevant material interference over the effective governance of subsequent commercial operations.p. 434

PTO segundo limite é a hipótese em que a plataforma funciona como canal ou vitrine, mas não governa, em termos estruturais, a utilidade adquirida, faltando-lhe a centralidade infraestrutural característica do OIC. É o caso que afasta, em princípio, o marketplace que presta serviços de posicionamento e captação de clientela por via publicitária, mas não exerce ingerência material relevante sobre a governança efetiva das operações comerciais subsequentes.

A word of caution. None of this is a ready-made formula or a guarantee of results, because each case depends on what happened, on what can be shown and on how the person deciding reads the facts. What changes is how the conflict is told, which stops being an isolated episode and becomes a matter of how the platform supplies what it sold.
