7.3 On the recurring patterns of friction, evidence and restoration

p. 787

The typological transposition carried out makes it possible to draw a more dogmatically relevant result than the mere illustrative expansion of the field of incidence of the Immaterial Object of Consumption. What the adherence tests make visible is that the keys of the regime do not remain dependent on the original laboratory in which they were formulated. On the contrary, they prove traceable to structural and functional equivalences between distinct ecosystems, even when those ecosystems diverge sharply in sectoral appearance, in technological language or in the immediate object of circulation. The common point lies not on the surface of the environment, but in the way enjoyment is governed, expectation is administered, intelligibility is modulated and contestation is made more or less practicable by private design.

This result matters because it moves the IOC regime from a merely inventive position to an effectively decidable one. The usefulness of the category lies not in producing new nomenclature to describe the digitization of economic life, but in offering stable criteria to recognize when, why and under what conditions certain forms of continuous supply require common legal treatment. Governed enjoyment, semiotic vulnerability, evidentiary asymmetry and private modulation of the environment thus cease to appear as scattered impressions about specific sectors and come to make up an institutionally reusable vocabulary. Without this effort of typification, structurally analogous conflicts would tend to be absorbed by the system as disconnected incidents, episodic discomforts or one-off contractual disputes, losing precisely the functional unity that makes them legally comparable.

p. 788 At the same time, the typological transposition makes it possible to identify something perhaps even more important. In many of these ecosystems, the environment that governs enjoyment is the same one that governs the way frustration will be perceived, the language by which it will be justified, the difficulty of proving it and the channels through which any dissent may be neutralized even before it turns into an institutionalizable conflict. The problem, then, no longer lies only in the content of the loss or in the intensity of the harm individually experienced. It also lies in the private administration of the conditions under which that loss becomes narratable, demonstrable and contestable. This fact explains why certain disputes seem small when observed in isolation, but reveal a much denser structure when reconstructed from the modulation mechanisms that produce them.

By keeping distinguishable, in each family of platform, the mechanism of injurious modulation, the corresponding evidentiary problem and the rationality of restoration required by the regime, both empty abstraction and casuistic dispersion are avoided. Injurious modulation is not to be confused with its proof. The evidentiary difficulty is not to be confused with the injury itself. And adequate restoration cannot be deduced automatically from traditional categories that treat every loss as susceptible of undifferentiated monetization. In ecosystems of continuous enjoyment, these three dimensions need to be seen in connection, but not in collapse. It is this internal distinction that allows the IOC regime to preserve density and usefulness, instead of becoming a formula too broad to guide any concrete response.

What emerges from the analysis of the models is not, therefore, the conclusion that the IOC would serve for almost everything. What emerges is something far more demanding and more useful. It becomes clear what effectively changes when the incidence of the regime is recognized. The way of narrating the conflict changes, because attention shifts from the isolated event to the supply program. The way of proving changes, because the opacity of the environment ceases to be taken as a neutral fact and comes to be treated as part of the legal problem itself. And the way of remedying changes, because the response can no longer automatically be satisfied with pecuniary equivalents whenever the injury reaches continuity, intelligibility, functional position, practical trust or conditions of permanence in enjoyment.

The typological path shows that the controllability of the IOC does not depend on freezing innovation, imposing absolute transparency or turning every platform into a parastatal entity. It depends on something more sober and more defensible. It depends on bringing the private governance of the environment back to verifiable standards of intelligibility, evidence, equivalence and correction. When this does not occur, precariousness ceases to be a mere contingent trait of the p. 789 digital and comes to function as the ordinary mode of organizing the consumer's experience. It is precisely against this naturalization of precariousness that the regime asserts itself.
