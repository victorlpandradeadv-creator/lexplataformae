The jurist's path

Information, advertising and anticipation

On platforms, the decision to buy is formed in an environment in which information, advertising, calendar and signs of prestige operate together, and the value of what is bought depends on criteria the supplier itself administers. For this reason the duty to inform is read as verifiable adequacy, the offer as controllable management of meaning, and the defense that everything was in the accepted terms loses force.

Why is it not enough to examine the clause, the notice and the ad separately?

The traditional examination asks, separately, whether the clause existed, whether the information was technically available and whether the advertisement contained a literal falsehood. On platforms, information, advertising and abusive practices work together to form expectation, urgency, trust and tolerance for frustration, so the object of control becomes the integrated decision-making arrangement through which the platform organizes the consumer's adherence and permanence, distributing visibility, scarcity, comparability and promise of value. Sensitivity is greater for the Immaterial Object of Consumption, because the decision concerns a governed future experience, continuously modulated by architecture, curation and private sanction, and the initial expectation becomes part of the very legal substance of enjoyment.

In contexts under hypercuratorial anticipation, information, advertising and abusive practices belong to the same dogmatic problem.p. 634

PTEm contextos sob antecipação hipercuratorial, informação, publicidade e práticas abusivas pertencem ao mesmo problema dogmático.

Where it comes from

In platform contexts, information, advertising and abusive practices cannot be examined as autonomous phenomena, isolated in watertight compartments, because, in the real process of consumption, they operate jointly in forming expectation, urgency, trust and tolerance for frustration. The legal problem is no longer exhausted in knowing whether a given clause existed, whether certain information was technically available or whether an advertising piece contained literal falsity.p. 629

PTEm contextos de plataforma, informação, publicidade e práticas abusivas não podem ser examinadas como fenômenos autônomos, isolados em compartimentos estanques, porque, no processo real de consumo, operam conjuntamente na formação da expectativa, da urgência, da confiança e da tolerância à frustração. O problema jurídico já não se esgota em saber se determinada cláusula existia, se certa informação estava tecnicamente disponível ou se uma peça publicitária continha falsidade literal.

What needs to be controlled is the integrated decision-making arrangement through which the platform organizes the consumer's adhesion to and permanence in the ecosystem, distributing visibility, scarcity, comparability and promise of value in a way functional to its own interests.p. 629-630

PTO que precisa ser controlado é o arranjo decisório integrado pelo qual a plataforma organiza a adesão e a permanência do consumidor no ecossistema, distribuindo visibilidade, escassez, comparabilidade e promessa de valor de modo funcional aos seus próprios interesses.

The consumer's decision concerns a governed future experience, continuously modulated by architecture, curation and enforcement, so that the initial expectation becomes part of the very legal substance of enjoyment.p. 630

PTA decisão do consumidor recai sobre experiência futura governada, continuamente modulada por arquitetura, curadoria e enforcement, de modo que a expectativa inicial passa a integrar a própria substância jurídica da fruição.

The shift is one of method. Information is no longer read only as declaratory content, advertising is no longer promotional decoration and the abusive practice is no longer a marginal event after the contract. Claudia Lima Marques traces the mandate of transparency back to the search for the consumer's true and free will, as a precondition for consumers to fulfill their legitimate expectations, and it follows that transparency must run through the entire process of approaching the object, from capturing attention to settling expectation. The lawfulness of the relationship is therefore assessed by the whole set of pre-contractual and contextual manifestations that shaped will and the perception of risk, and not only by the final text of the agreement.

Where it comes from

Information cannot be read only as declaratory content; advertising cannot be treated as promotional decoration; and the abusive practice cannot be reduced to a marginal event after the contract.p. 630

PTA informação não pode ser lida apenas como conteúdo declaratório; a publicidade não pode ser tratada como enfeite promocional; e a prática abusiva não pode ser reduzida a evento marginal posterior ao contrato.

The meaning of the mandate of transparency can be traced back to the search for the “true and free will of the consumer”, functioning as a “precondition” for the consumer to freely express their will and realize their legitimate expectations, that is, those that led them, “duly informed about the product or service, aware of their future contractual rights and duties”, to choose a given supplier as contractual partner Marques(Marques, 2016, p. 818-819).p. 630

PTO sentido do mandamento de transparência pode ser reconduzido à procura da “verdadeira e livre vontade do consumidor”, funcionando como “pré-condição” para que ele manifeste livremente sua vontade e realize suas expectativas legítimas, isto é, aquelas que o levaram, “informado devidamente sobre o produto ou serviço, ciente de seus futuros direitos e deveres contratuais”, a escolher determinado fornecedor como parceiro contratual (Marques, 2016, p. 818-819) .

Transparency, in this framework, is not a mere formal requirement of the final moment of contracting, but a presupposition that must run through the entire process of the consumer's approach to the object, from the capture of their attention to the settling of their expectation as to what will be obtained, lost or tolerated as admissible frustration. For this reason, the lawfulness of the relationship cannot be assessed only by the final text of the bond, but by the set of pre-contractual and contextual manifestations that shaped the consumer's will and perception of risk.p. 630

PTA transparência, nessa moldura, não é mero requisito formal do instante final da contratação, mas pressuposto que deve atravessar todo o processo de aproximação do consumidor ao objeto, desde a captação de sua atenção até a sedimentação de sua expectativa quanto ao que será obtido, perdido ou tolerado como frustração admissível. Por isso, a legalidade da relação não pode ser aferida apenas pelo texto final do vínculo, mas pelo conjunto das manifestações pré-contratuais e contextuais que moldaram a vontade e a percepção de risco do consumidor.

When the value of the object depends on administered visibility, symbolic equivalences and time governed as a program, the central problem ceases to be merely the content of the item and becomes the demonstrability of the link between promise and enjoyment. Three descriptions support this diagnosis, the spectacle, the simulacrum and the curated promise, and each identifies for the jurist a form of asymmetry of verifiability.

Where it comes from

If the value of the IOC depends on controlled semiosis, administered visibility, operative symbolic equivalences and time governed as a program, then the central problem ceases to be merely the content of the item and becomes the demonstrability of the link between promise and enjoyment in a reconfigurable environment.p. 320

PTSe o valor do OIC depende de semiose controlada, de visibilidade administrada, de equivalências simbólicas operatórias e de tempo governado como programa, então o problema central deixa de ser apenas o conteúdo do item e passa a ser a demonstrabilidade do nexo entre promessa e fruição em um ambiente reconfigurável.

The contribution of the spectacle to the legal question, at this point, is not to offer a moral critique of media, but to delimit a specific form of asymmetry of verifiability.p. 294

PTA contribuição do espetáculo à questão jurídica, neste ponto, não é oferecer uma crítica moral da mídia, mas delimitar uma forma específica de assimetria de verificabilidade.

The result is an asymmetry of verifiability, which makes it plausible to operate equivalence by appearance as practical shielding against the demonstration of nonperformance.p. 313

PTO resultado é assimetria de verificabilidade, que torna plausível operar a equivalência por aparência como blindagem prática contra a demonstração do inadimplemento.

What does the society of the spectacle change for the jurist?

Guy Debord makes it possible to shift the focus from the sign to the environment, because on platforms the promise circulates not only as meaning but as scene. Spectacle, in this reading, does not mean media excess but the social relation between people mediated by images that Debord describes, a form of mediation that reorganizes experience under a logic of visibility. The platform institutes a regime of legibility in which positions become public through selection, ordering and highlighting, and the offer no longer presents itself as an isolated property of an item but as access to a position whose relevance depends on how the environment produces visibility and comparison. For this reason social proof is part of delivery itself.

Where it comes from

The next step consists in shifting the focus from the sign to the environment, because, on platforms, the promise does not circulate only as meaning, but as scene.p. 281

PTO passo seguinte consiste em deslocar o foco do signo para o ambiente, porque, em plataformas, a promessa não circula só como sentido, mas como cena.

Here, spectacle does not designate media excess nor a mere addition of images to the real, but a social form of mediation in which relations operate through representations and reorganize experience under a logic of visibility. Debord formulates this perspective when he states that the spectacle is not a collection of images, but a social relation between people mediated by images Debord(Debord, 2013, [p. 26]).p. 282

PTAqui, espetáculo não designa excesso midiático nem mero acréscimo de imagens ao real, mas forma social de mediação na qual relações operam por representações e reorganizam a experiência sob uma lógica de visibilidade. Debord formula tal perspectiva ao afirmar que o espetáculo não é conjunto de imagens, mas relação social entre pessoas mediada por imagens (Debord, 2013, [p. 26]) .

Thus, the offer of the IOC does not present itself as an isolated property of an item, but as access to a position whose relevance depends on how the environment produces visibility and provides parameters of comparison.p. 283

PTA plataforma institui um regime de legibilidade no qual posições são tornadas públicas por seleção, ordenação e destaque, fazendo com que diferenças passem a valer economicamente porque se tornam mensuráveis em hierarquias exibidas. Assim, a oferta do OIC não se apresenta como propriedade isolada de um item, mas como acesso a uma posição cuja relevância depende do modo como o ambiente produz visibilidade e fornece parâmetros de comparação.

The acquired position of enjoyment only fully fulfills its economic function when it can be perceived, compared and validated in a public circuit, so that social proof is part of delivery itself.p. 284

PTA posição de fruição adquirida só cumpre plenamente sua função econômica quando pode ser percebida, comparada e validada em circuito público, de modo que a prova social integra a própria entrega.

Gamification operates, in this framework, as a discipline of the present, because remaining comes to mean staying measurable and recognizable on a scale updated in real time. In the ranking system of League of Legends, the official support pages describe tiers and divisions with a risk of regression, a daily penalty of 75 league points for inactivity at the highest levels and the acknowledgment that most of these players play practically every day to keep their position. Nor is the displayed position stable property, because it exists only while the access bond remains operative, and the Terms of Service treat use as a limited and revocable license, automatically canceled when the account is closed. Rank, honor and reputation thus behave as attributes of an administered position.

The stability of prestige, here, is not rest, but vigilance.p. 286

PTA estabilidade do prestígio, aqui, não é repouso, mas vigilância.

How does the simulacrum shift the proof of delivery?

Jean Baudrillard provides the distinction between feigning and simulating. Feigning preserves the reality principle, while simulation throws into crisis the boundary between true and false, and the simulacrum describes a mode of operation in which sign equivalence is enough to keep value circulating and the promise looking fulfilled. The analytical gain is to separate the public continuity of value from the actual continuity of the purchased experience, which makes it possible to describe capture without calling it a lie, as the administration of criteria of equivalence capable of concealing relevant losses without any overt rupture of the object. Sign value can thus be treated as an economic dimension proper to consumption. As Baudrillard recalls, one never consumes the object in itself, because objects are handled as signs that distinguish. The signs of delivery on platforms are novelty, rarity, exclusivity, prestige, belonging, distinction and edition, classificatory categories that organize hierarchy and comparison in real time.

Where it comes from

The difference is structural, because feigning preserves the reality principle, while simulation throws into crisis the boundaries between true and false, real and imaginary Baudrillard(Baudrillard, 1991, [p. 9-10]).p. 295-296

PTA diferença é estrutural, porque o fingimento preserva o princípio da realidade, enquanto a simulação põe em crise as fronteiras entre verdadeiro e falso, real e imaginário (Baudrillard, 1991, [p. 9-10]) .

Here, simulacrum describes a mode of operation in which sign equivalence is enough to keep value circulating and the promise looking fulfilled.p. 296

PTAqui, simulacro descreve um funcionamento em que a equivalência sígnica basta para manter o valor circulando e a promessa parecendo cumprida.

The analytical gain is to separate the public continuity of value from the effective continuity of the purchased experience, making it possible to describe capture not as a “lie”, but as the administration of criteria of equivalence that operate on the symbolic plane and can conceal relevant losses without ostensible rupture of the object. With this, sign value can be treated as an economic dimension proper to consumption, and not as a mere by-product.p. 296

PTO ganho analítico é separar a continuidade pública do valor da continuidade efetiva da experiência comprada, tornando possível descrever a captura não como “mentira”, mas como administração de critérios de equivalência que operam no plano simbólico e que podem encobrir perdas relevantes sem ruptura ostensiva do objeto. Com isso, o valor-signo pode ser tratado como dimensão econômica própria do consumo, e não como simples subproduto.

The starting point is the rejection of consumption as appropriation of use value, since “one never consumes the object in itself” and objects “are always manipulated as signs that distinguish the individual”, whether by affiliation to the reference group or by demarcation from a higher status Baudrillard(Baudrillard, 1995, p. 60).p. 297

PTO ponto de partida é a recusa do consumo como apropriação do valor de uso, pois “nunca se consome o objecto em si” e os objetos “manipulam-se sempre como signos que distinguem o indivíduo”, seja por filiação ao grupo de referência, seja por demarcação perante um estatuto superior (Baudrillard, 1995, p. 60) .

Novelty, rarity, exclusivity, prestige, belonging, distinction and “edition” are not communicational adornments. They are classificatory categories that define what should be read as superior, desirable and “of the moment”, organizing hierarchy and comparison in real time.p. 297-298

The consequence for the offer is direct. Teasers, which are short pieces of advance communication, storefronts, previews, countdowns, suggestions of rarity, insinuated equivalences and promises of prestige, belonging or performance make up the program of the offer insofar as they take part in forming legitimate expectation. The basic rule is in Article 30 of the CDC4, under which all sufficiently precise information or advertising binds the supplier and becomes part of the contract. This holds with special force when the utility is mediated by interface, visual presentation and promises of progression, and the offer does not disappear after adhesion. Can advertising still persuade?

The first limit is that of persuasion. Economic activity has its own persuasive function, and control of the offer does not work as abstract repression of advertising, but as control of the promise in environments of governed enjoyment. The second is that of relevance. Informational adequacy is assessed by the elements that relevantly condition the formation of expectation, the preservation of enjoyment and the possibility of contestation, and there is a materially controllable offer wherever prior communication relevantly shapes the reading of the object, the perception of value and adhesion. The third is that of the symbolic. The contribution of the simulacrum does not lie in treating consumption as illusion, and the problem does not lie in desiring, fantasizing or attributing symbolic value to the object. The fourth is that of possibilities. The consequences of anticipation come with a modal, since unlawfulness may lie in the articulation and frustration may be a relevant inadequacy, so that a calendar, an event or a random draw does not become abusive merely by existing.

The basic rights of the consumer are: I - protection of life, health and safety against the risks caused by practices in the supply of products and services considered dangerous or harmful; III - adequate and clear information about the different products and services […]; IV - protection against misleading and abusive advertising, coercive or unfair commercial methods, as well as against abusive practices and clauses […]; VI - the effective prevention and reparation of patrimonial and moral damages […]; VIII - the facilitation of the defense of their rights, including the reversal of the burden of proof […].”

    PT“Art. 6º. São direitos básicos do consumidor: I - a proteção da vida, saúde e segurança contra os riscos provocados por práticas no fornecimento de produtos e serviços considerados perigosos ou nocivos; III - a informação adequada e clara sobre os diferentes produtos e serviços […]; IV - a proteção contra a publicidade enganosa e abusiva, métodos comerciais coercitivos ou desleais, bem como contra práticas e cláusulas abusivas […]; VI - a efetiva prevenção e reparação de danos patrimoniais e morais […]; VIII - a facilitação da defesa de seus direitos, inclusive com a inversão do ônus da prova […].”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 500, p. 511.

  3. Article 31 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 31. The offer and presentation of products or services must ensure correct, clear, precise, conspicuous information in the Portuguese language about their characteristics, qualities, quantity, composition, price, warranty, expiration dates and origin, among other data, as well as about the risks they present to the health and safety of consumers.”

    PT“Art. 31. A oferta e apresentação de produtos ou serviços devem assegurar informações corretas, claras, precisas, ostensivas e em língua portuguesa sobre suas características, qualidades, quantidade, composição, preço, garantia, prazos de validade e origem, entre outros dados, bem como sobre os riscos que apresentam à saúde e segurança dos consumidores.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 681, p. 601.

  4. Article 30 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 30. All sufficiently precise information or advertising, conveyed by any form or means of communication regarding products and services offered or presented, binds the supplier that conveys it or makes use of it and becomes part of the contract that may be entered into.”

    PT“Art. 30. Toda informação ou publicidade, suficientemente precisa, veiculada por qualquer forma ou meio de comunicação com relação a produtos e serviços oferecidos ou apresentados, obriga o fornecedor que a fizer veicular ou dela se utilizar e integra o contrato que vier a ser celebrado.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 442, p. 433.

  5. Article 46 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 46. Contracts governing consumer relationships shall not bind consumers if they are not given the opportunity to become aware of their content beforehand, or if the respective instruments are drafted in a way that hinders understanding of their meaning and scope.”

    PT“Art. 46. Os contratos que regulam as relações de consumo não obrigarão os consumidores, se não lhes for dada a oportunidade de tomar conhecimento prévio de seu conteúdo, ou se os respectivos instrumentos forem redigidos de modo a dificultar a compreensão de seu sentido e alcance.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 682, p. 601.

  6. Article 47 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 47. Contractual clauses shall be interpreted in the manner most favorable to the consumer.”

    PT“Art. 47. As cláusulas contratuais serão interpretadas de maneira mais favorável ao consumidor.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 498, p. 511.

  7. Article 38 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 38. The burden of proving the truthfulness and correctness of the advertising information or communication falls on whoever sponsors it.”

    PT“Art. 38. O ônus da prova da veracidade e correção da informação ou comunicação publicitária cabe a quem as patrocina.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 716, p. 627.

  8. Article 35 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 35. If the supplier of products or services refuses to honor the offer, presentation or advertising, the consumer may, alternatively and at their free choice: I - demand specific performance of the obligation […]; II - accept another equivalent product or provision of service; III - rescind the contract […], and to losses and damages.”

    PT“Art. 35. Se o fornecedor de produtos ou serviços recusar cumprimento à oferta, apresentação ou publicidade, o consumidor poderá, alternativamente e à sua livre escolha: I - exigir o cumprimento forçado da obrigação […]; II - aceitar outro produto ou prestação de serviço equivalente; III - rescindir o contrato […], e a perdas e danos.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 573, p. 540.
