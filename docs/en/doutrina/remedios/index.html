The jurist's path

The remedies

Remedies close the examination of a conflict over an Immaterial Object of Consumption, because they answer what to do with the injury once the object is qualified, the change reconstructed, the equivalence assessed and the proof organized. Primacy belongs to functional restoration and regime correction, and compensation comes after them, not in their place.

Why does a money award usually fail in the face of governed enjoyment?

The system's reflex in the face of a consumer injury is to convert it into compensation. That solution, often necessary, is not discarded, but it frequently proves insufficient when what is at stake is not only immediate economic loss but the degradation, interruption or abusive reconfiguration of a continuous enjoyment, governed and situated within an ecosystem. In these relationships, a later payment may leave the harmful mechanism untouched, preserve the architecture that produced the problem and normalize the repeated loss as an ordinary cost of the supply model.

Where does the primacy of restoration come from?

The rule is direct. Whenever technically and legally feasible, priority protection should seek the functional restoration of enjoyment and the correction of the regime that made it inadequate. The primacy does not derive from an abstract preference for specific performance nor from ritual attachment to restoration in natura, but from the structure of the bond. If the utility consumed is an immaterial experience maintained by continuous governance, the relevant injury tends to fall on access, continuity, integrity, equivalence, intelligibility, history, relational position or the consumer's insertion in the environment, and restoration corresponds better to the nature of the legal interest affected. In objects of high identity, historical or reputational density, setting a sum also runs the risk of treating as equivalent what, in practice, is not.

The regime of defects points in the same direction. The treatise by Benjamin, Marques and Bessa recalls that, in case of a defect, the consumer may demand another product of the same kind in perfect conditions of use, alongside restitution or a proportional reduction of the price. In the reading of the same treatise, Article 20 of the CDC4 allows, in the face of a quality defect that reduces the value of the service, rescission without penalty or a proportional reduction, without discussion of fault. What are the remedies and in what order?

Functional restoration is not a single remedy but a set of possible measures, calibrated to the structure of the impaired enjoyment. The main list runs from restoring access and reviewing sanctions to preserving history, reactivating functionalities, substituting a functionally equivalent position, reverting versions when technically possible and making procedural corrections, such as verifiable reasoning and intelligible contestation. The operation of auditability and equivalence adds re-performance of the obligation, restoration of ecosystemic or reputational attributes, mitigation through alternative versions, correction of parameters and proportional compensation when full restoration is not feasible. The conclusion adds reversal of relevant degradations, cessation of opaque or exploitative practices and minimum standards of verifiability. The list is open, and the three versions are read together.

What separates restoring the case from correcting the regime?

Restoring the case and correcting the regime answer different questions. Functional restoration looks at the injured consumer and seeks to reestablish the position of enjoyment the consumer had legitimately incorporated. Regime correction looks at the arrangement that produced the injury. It opens up when the injury is architectural, repeatable and built into ordinary supply, because then the problem is no longer only the loss of a particular consumer and comes to lie in the permanence of an arrangement capable of repeating the same inadequacy indefinitely. The remedies of this second plane address the continuous source of the problem, such as the reconfiguration of flows, procedural duties, review of sanction mechanisms, verifiable reasoning and intelligible contestation channels.

Where it comes from

The problem then ceases to be only the loss suffered by a particular consumer and comes to lie in the permanence of a harmful arrangement capable of reproducing the same inadequacy indefinitely.p. 741

PTO problema deixa então de ser apenas a perda sofrida por um consumidor determinado e passa a residir na permanência de um arranjo lesivo capaz de reproduzir indefinidamente a mesma inadequação.

The parameter therefore opens room for regime correction. This means admitting, when the case reveals a structural pattern of abusiveness, remedies aimed at the reconfiguration of flows, the imposition of procedural duties, the review of sanction mechanisms, the requirement of verifiable reasoning, the creation of intelligible contestation channels and other measures capable of reaching the continuous source of the problem.p. 741

PTO parâmetro, por isso, abre espaço para correção de regime. Isso significa admitir, quando o caso revelar padrão estrutural de abusividade, remédios voltados à reconfiguração de fluxos, à imposição de deveres procedimentais, à revisão de mecanismos de sanção, à exigência de motivação verificável, à criação de canais inteligíveis de contestação e a outras medidas aptas a atingir a fonte contínua do problema.

The difference is also one of time. Restoration repairs a loss already suffered, while correction is directed at the mode of operation that made the injury ordinary and at the future conditions of control of enjoyment. Treating the case only as an isolated event loses sight of the repeatability of the harm. Regime correction reconnects individual protection to the collective dimension, which may justify injunctive relief, structural measures, collective measures and even mechanisms of compulsory compliance, that is, compliance programs imposed on the supplier, compatible with open auditability.

Ela se desloca para plano mais delicado, no qual o fornecedor passa a deter meios de reconstrução psíquico-funcional do usuário que o próprio usuário não possui sobre si com grau equivalente de precisão operativa.

Semiotic vulnerability, from this perspective, reveals an even deeper reach than initially formulated.p. 799

PTA vulnerabilidade semiótica, sob esse prisma, revela alcance ainda mais profundo do que o inicialmente formulado.

The challenge facing Consumer Law, therefore, is not only to limit abusive mutations of enjoyment, but to confront, in future development, the possibility that continuous platform consumption becomes a private technique for producing intensive subjective legibility, with consequences still insufficiently examined for autonomy, for practical freedom and for the very integrity of personality in digitally governed environments.p. 800

PTO desafio que se anuncia ao Direito do Consumidor, portanto, não é apenas o de limitar mutações abusivas da fruição, mas o de enfrentar, em desenvolvimento futuro, a possibilidade de que o consumo contínuo em plataforma se converta em técnica privada de produção de legibilidade subjetiva intensiva, com consequências ainda insuficientemente tematizadas para a autonomia, para a liberdade prática e para a própria integridade da personalidade em ambientes digitalmente governados.

Therein lies the reason for the remedies. What presents itself as the simple functioning of the system can be reconstructed as choice, as program and, when necessary, as abuse, and only then does it become possible to restore degraded enjoyment, contest opaque decisions and rebuild trust.

The Brazilian constitutional order does not require the elimination of the private infrastructures that organize contemporary experience; it requires, rather, that the power inscribed in them let itself be seen, proven, limited and, when necessary, corrected.p. 802

PTA ordem constitucional brasileira não exige a eliminação das infraestruturas privadas que organizam a experiência contemporânea; exige, isto sim, que o poder nelas inscrito se deixe ver, provar, limitar e, quando necessário, corrigir.

Where it comes from

What presents itself as the simple functioning of the system can and must be reconstructed as choice, as program and, when necessary, as abuse.p. 801

PTO que se apresenta como simples funcionamento do sistema pode e deve ser reconstruído como escolha, como programa e, quando necessário, como abuso.

Only then will it be possible to restore degraded enjoyment, contest opaque decisions and rebuild trust without accepting that power organized by architecture should present itself as a mere, inevitable backdrop.p. 793

PTSó então será possível recompor fruições degradadas, contestar decisões opacas e reconstruir a confiança sem aceitar que poder organizado por arquitetura se apresente como simples cenário inevitável.
