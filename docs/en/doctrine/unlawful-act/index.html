The jurist's path

Abusive teleology and the unlawful act of architecture

Abusive teleology is the category of unlawful act of architecture that makes it possible to impute to the supplier a program of exploitation built into the design of the service, when no clause or isolated episode is enough to show the injury. It operates through five cumulative requirements, separates aggressive business from exploitative purpose and enters the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code (CDC) through doors the law has already opened.

What injury goes unseen when one looks only at the clause and the episode?

Change alone is not the problem. In a continuous digital service, altering, updating, rebalancing and reorganizing enjoyment is part of how the service is supplied, and mutability by itself does not amount to unlawfulness. The problem begins when change stops maintaining the consumed object and starts to work as an ordinary technique for exploiting vulnerability, obscuring understanding, concentrating proof in the supplier's hands and reducing the chance of reversing the bond.

Where it comes from

The mutability of the service, by itself, is not enough to characterize unlawfulness.p. 663

The legal problem begins when this mutability ceases to operate as a legitimate form of maintaining the consumed object and begins to function as an ordinary technique of exploiting vulnerability, obscuring understanding, unilaterally concentrating proof and systematically reducing the reversibility of the bond.p. 663

PTO problema jurídico começa quando essa mutabilidade deixa de operar como forma legítima de manutenção do objeto consumido e passa a funcionar como técnica ordinária de exploração da vulnerabilidade, de obscurecimento da compreensão, de concentração unilateral da prova e de redução sistemática da reversibilidade do vínculo.

This transition rarely leaves its mark in any one place. Many exploitative architectures produce not spectacular harm but ordinary wear, diffuse loss and selective difficulty, built from small pressures that, taken one by one, look like tolerable rough edges of the environment. The law was trained to react to delimited events more than to programs of operation, and two inadequate responses follow from this. The first dissolves the conflict in the language of innovation or of the platform's dynamic nature, as if continuous reorganization immunized any downgrading. The second reduces control to a hunt for isolated clauses, as if the supply could only be examined in its textual fragments.

Abusiveness shifts from the event to the regime. When governance repeatedly organizes informational noise, decisional friction, evidentiary opacity, mutation that cannot be audited and insufficient restoration, the unlawful act no longer lies only in the wording of the contract, because it lies in the architecture. The names call for clarification. Three expressions cover the same field, unlawful act of architecture, unlawful act of abusive teleology and teleological unlawful act of architecture, and they are not always kept apart. The clearest relation treats abusive teleology as a category of unlawful act of architecture, that is, as a mode of attribution that reaches exploitative purposes inscribed in the technical and organizational design of the service, and not only in contractual commands. Nor should it be confused with the law of the platform, which is a descriptive category of the setting in which power is exercised, while abusive teleology is a category of attribution. Its formulation depends on two prior categories, because no unlawful act can be captured without the semiotic vulnerability it exploits, or without the enjoyment bundle, the utility acquired and sustained over time, that it degrades.

Why can the design of the service be treated as conduct?

As long as the design is perceived as a neutral technical setting, it stays beyond the reach of attribution. The starting point is Julie Cohen, who describes governance by architecture as the capacity of protocols and centralized controls to actively define the space of possibilities for enjoyment, and modulation as the constant adjustment of the environment through information flows. The image of the Hogwarts staircases marks a difference. In fiction, the environment changes by itself, whereas on the platform change results from architectural choices, curation criteria and sanction mechanisms, even if it reaches the user as the world simply working.

Where it comes from

Governance by architecture can be described as the capacity of protocols and centralized controls to actively define the space of possibilities in which enjoyment takes place, including through structural choices that enable certain flexibilities and block others Cohen(Cohen, 2019, p. 42).p. 445

PTA governança por arquitetura pode ser descrita como a capacidade de protocolos e controles centralizados definirem, de forma ativa, o espaço de possibilidades em que a fruição ocorre, inclusive por meio de escolhas estruturais que habilitam certas flexibilidades e bloqueiam outras (Cohen, 2019, p. 42) .

On the platform, it is the result of choices of architecture, curation criteria, sanction mechanisms and layers of opacity that turn the mutation of the "ground" into an act of government, even if presented to the user as the simple working of the world.p. 446

PTNa plataforma, ela é resultado de escolhas de arquitetura, critérios de curadoria, mecanismos de sanção e camadas de opacidade que convertem a mutação do "chão" em ato de governo, ainda que apresentadas ao usuário como simples funcionamento do mundo.

Andrew Feenberg rejects the neutrality of technique, because projects that seem neutral may incorporate unconscious preferences, and technical development may follow different directions depending on the prevailing hegemony. Hence, architecture is the place where values and interests become operative as an organization of possibilities, costs, frictions and rewards. It organizes access, visibility, time, obstacles, irreversibility, contestation and the prominence of information, and this is what allows it to be understood as the supplier's way of acting. The platform appears as an agent that shapes, by design, the conditions in which choice occurs, loss becomes normal and reaction becomes harder.

Moving from diagnosis to the dogmatic category calls for methodological restraint, which three authors provide. Miguel Reale allows patterns to be read as legal facts, because the norm integrates facts and values and the formation of law presupposes the observation of regularities, though this does not license voluntarism. Tércio Sampaio Ferraz Jr. points out that dogmatics cuts out a piece of reality to make conflicts decidable, so the category works as a cutting device that turns diffuse suspicions into verifiable hypotheses, without requiring full transparency of the ecosystem or impossible proof from the consumer. Umberto Eco dispels the confusion between sign and private feeling, and so symbolic promises built into the bundle can be reconstructed as objective components of utility and legitimate expectation.

What does the five-requirement test consist of?

Abusive teleology is defined as the structural deviation of platform-governed supply in which architecture and governance come to operate under an identifiable exploitative purpose, with patterns of modulation that degrade, reconfigure or condition enjoyment beyond the ordinary risk of continued performance. Its object is the architectural program, the set of repeated choices that organizes possibilities, introduces frictions, manages visibility, distributes sanctions and stabilizes dependencies. To keep it from becoming an elastic label, the qualification takes the form of a test of cumulative elements, so that the absence of any one of them is enough to rule it out.

Where it comes from

With the premises and their respective limits established, abusive teleology can be defined, at this point, as the structural deviation of platform-governed supply in which the architecture and governance of the environment come to operate in a way oriented by an identifiable exploitative purpose, producing and reinforcing patterns of modulation that degrade, reconfigure or condition enjoyment beyond the ordinary risk of continued performance.p. 448

PTEstabelecidas as premissas e seus respectivos limites, a teleologia abusiva pode ser definida, neste ponto, como o desvio estrutural do fornecimento governado por plataforma no qual a arquitetura e a governança do ambiente passam a operar de maneira orientada por finalidade exploratória identificável, produzindo e reforçando padrões de modulação que degradam, reconfiguram ou condicionam a fruição para além do risco ordinário de uma prestação continuada.

For this qualification to remain controllable and not turn into an elastic label, it must take the form of a test made up of cumulative elements, able to bring the discussion back to verifiable requirements and to delimit the edges of application.p. 448

PTPara que essa qualificação permaneça controlável e não se converta em rótulo elástico, ela deve assumir a forma de um teste composto por elementos cumulativos, aptos a reconduzir a discussão a requisitos verificáveis e a delimitar bordas de aplicação.

The first requirement is inscribed teleology. The exploitative purpose must be reconstructable as something built into design choices, terms of use, authorization mechanisms and enforcement strategies, the term for the supplier's own application of its rules, and not as an occasional side effect. It is identified when the architecture systematically organizes possibilities and exclusions, introduces selective frictions, distributes visibility and sanctions asymmetrically or conditions features to steer behavior and reduce relevant alternatives.

Does it have to be proven that the company intended to exploit?

No. The category does not depend on imputing subjective psychological intent or on pinpointing a single act as a sufficient cause, because the focus is on the functional purpose objectively inscribed in the arrangement, revealed by replicability, internal coherence and repeated impact. Nor does the unlawful act require a confession of malice, and the program may have been conceived that way or come to be administered that way. Exploitation may lie in the design even when corporate discourse speaks of technical neutrality or operational inevitability, and proof may come from the supplier's own institutional material.

What is controlled is not an inner will, but a recognizable program of operation.p. 732

PTO que se controla não é uma vontade íntima, mas um programa reconhecível de funcionamento.

What do the Sanctum and the Exalted skins allow us to discuss?

The case arises from a trait of the supplier, an entertainment company that can extend the playful frame beyond the product, so that buying, converting currency, deciphering prices and tracking event windows themselves become game mechanics. The transposition is not symmetrical, and so the claim that it is all a game works as a symptom rather than a justification when the mechanic bears on contracting and enjoyment rather than on the content delivered.

Unlike a stage of the game, whose rules exist so that the player can, in theory, beat them, the game embedded in the consumer relationship is designed and refereed by the supplier itself, which defines the probabilities, sets the unit of account, recalibrates the internal exchange rate and establishes the deadlines, systematically reserving for itself the position of the house.p. 449

How does the exploitative program become visible?

Exploitation usually works by accumulation, and it can be described in a vocabulary of dogmatic and evidentiary interest, organized into five families. The first is artificial scarcity and manufactured urgency, with short windows, countdown timers, limited editions and ephemeral events, whose legal effect is to compress the time for reflection and induce hurried consent. The problem is not the use of time limits in the market, but how they combine with opacity and with the practical impossibility of comparing offers. The minimum evidentiary signs are screens showing time remaining, a history of repeated temporary campaigns and the programmed rotation of offers.

The exploitative program does not disappear just because it is spread over small repeated incidents.p. 670

PTO programa exploratório não desaparece só porque se distribui em pequenas incidências repetidas.

Where it comes from

PTO efeito juridicamente relevante não está apenas em tornar a compra mais atraente, mas em comprimir deliberadamente o tempo de reflexão e em induzir consentimento apressado, muitas vezes quando a informação essencial permanece escondida, dispersa ou hierarquicamente rebaixada.

The problem is not the isolated use of temporality in the market, but its articulation with opacity and with the practical impossibility of comparison.p. 670

PTO problema não é o uso isolado de temporalidade no mercado, mas sua articulação com opacidade e com impossibilidade prática de comparação.

How can the regime be examined without atomizing or inflaming the conflict?

The ten guidelines of control shift the center of gravity of the examination from clauses and terminal acts to the supply regime, and each follows from the one before. The first treats architecture and governance as constitutive elements of the supply rather than as a neutral setting, because they are what define paths, frictions and the conditions under which reaction will be possible. The second subjects relevant alterations to open auditability and functional value equivalence rather than to the generic rhetoric of updating, so what matters is whether the change was made intelligible and whether it preserved the function that justified the purchase. The third requires, for private sanctions with material impact, verifiable reasons, coherent decisions and an appeal process that is not an empty ritual.

Where it comes from

Doesn't the contract authorize the change, and isn't the category moralism?

The first objection is that the contract authorizes the change. Private autonomy, moreover, does not authorize organizing enjoyment on foundations structurally incompatible with good faith, balance and controllability.     In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 527, p. 520.

  6. Article 51, item XIII, of the CDC ↩

    Art. 51. The following contractual clauses relating to the supply of products and services, among others, are null and void by operation of law:

    PTArt. 51. São nulas de pleno direito, entre outras, as cláusulas contratuais relativas ao fornecimento de produtos e serviços que: IV - estabeleçam obrigações consideradas iníquas, abusivas, que coloquem o consumidor em desvantagem exagerada, ou sejam incompatíveis com a boa-fé ou a eqüidade; XIII - autorizem o fornecedor a modificar unilateralmente o conteúdo ou a qualidade do contrato, após sua celebração;

    IV - those that establish obligations considered iniquitous, abusive, that place the consumer at an exaggerated disadvantage, or are incompatible with good faith or equity;

    XIII - those that authorize the supplier to unilaterally modify the content or quality of the contract after its conclusion;

    Text in force copied from the Planalto website on 27/10/2026. Later addition, outside the thesis.

  7. Article 84 of the CDC ↩

    “Art. 84. In an action whose object is the performance of an obligation to do or not to do, the judge shall grant specific relief for the obligation or order measures that ensure a practical result equivalent to that of performance.”

    PT“Art. 84. Na ação que tenha por objeto o cumprimento da obrigação de fazer ou não fazer, o juiz concederá a tutela específica da obrigação ou determinará providências que assegurem o resultado prático equivalente ao do adimplemento.”

    In the wording the thesis transcribes in note 574, p. 540.
