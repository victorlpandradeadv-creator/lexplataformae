Follow the skin

This path follows your skin, from the click that buys it to the question of what the law can do for you. There are eleven parts. Each one answers a question, explains what lies behind it and leaves another question hanging, and at the end of each part you will find what you can demand.

Start with the purchase

  1. 1What did you buy, after all?The button says buy, the contract says license, and the difference shows up on the day something disappears.
  2. 2Why is this skin worth so much?It is a sign that everyone inside knows how to read, and the platform is the one that looks after the signs.
  3. 3Who profits from your click?Whoever sells the skin also owns the place where it has value, the currency and the clock.
  4. 4Who is in charge after the purchase?The rule that counts is not just the written one but the one that is running.
  5. 5Why does all this seem normal?Technology seems neutral, but it has an owner and a direction.
  6. 6What if your skin changes tomorrow?It is still in your account, but it is no longer the same.
  7. 7What if the loss is part of the plan?When loss becomes routine, the problem is in the design.
  8. 8Does the law already have an answer?More than it seems, because the space has been open since 1990.
  9. 9And when it goes wrong?Before money, give back what was taken.
  10. 10Does this only happen in games?No, the game just shows everything more clearly.
  11. 11Why give a name to all this?Because what has a name can be demanded.
