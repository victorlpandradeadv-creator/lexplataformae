Can you check what changed?Open auditability

An item was altered, a rule changed, an account was penalized. Most of the time, the user has no way of knowing what happened, when, why and with what effect. The platform knows.

It controls the design of the environment, the criteria for changes, the system records, the moment each thing changes and even the means by which you will notice the change. That is why it has more than the power to change things. It also has the power to tell the story of what happened on its own. You are left with its version, and any challenge becomes your word against theirs.

Where it comes from

Open auditability is thus born as a direct response to the functional opacity typical of IOCs.p. 636

PTA auditabilidade aberta nasce, assim, como resposta direta à opacidade funcional típica dos OICs.

If the supplier controls the architecture, the criteria for change, the relevant logs, the timing of the change and the very means by which the modification will be perceived, it concentrates not only the power of reconfiguration, but also the power to give a unilateral account of what occurred.p. 636

PTSe o fornecedor controla a arquitetura, os critérios de alteração, os logs relevantes, a temporalidade da mudança e os próprios meios pelos quais a modificação será percebida, ele concentra não apenas poder de reconfiguração, mas também poder de narrar unilateralmente o que ocorreu.

with structural asymmetries of challenge and proofp. 393

PTcom assimetrias estruturais de contestação e de prova

Open auditability

To audit is to check, and the concept describes a standard of transparency that actually serves a purpose. It is the requirement of minimum conditions so that judges, experts, consumer protection bodies and, to a certain extent, consumers themselves can reconstruct the story of what happened. This story connects what was promised, what changed, the penalty applied, the effect on use and the justification the company gave. It is worth understanding where it comes from. It is not a new law with its own name, nor a right that came out of nowhere. It is an offshoot of duties that consumer law already imposes, such as the duty to inform, good faith and making it easier for consumers to defend their rights. Its purpose is to keep information from fulfilling its role only on paper, hidden in enormous documents, notices and update histories that no one can find or connect to what happened.

Where it comes from

Open auditability means the existence of minimum conditions of verifiability regarding relevant aspects of supply, so that protection bodies, the Judiciary, experts and, to a certain extent, consumers themselves can reconstruct the relationship between promise, alteration, sanction, functional impact and the justification presented by the supplier.p. 727

PTAuditabilidade aberta significa a existência de condições mínimas de verificabilidade sobre aspectos relevantes do fornecimento, de modo que órgãos de tutela, Judiciário, peritos e, em certa medida, os próprios consumidores possam reconstruir a relação entre promessa, alteração, sanção, impacto funcional e justificação apresentada pelo fornecedor.

It is for this reason that open auditability must be understood as an internal unfolding of the duty to inform and not as an institution foreign to the microsystem.p. 641

PTÉ por essa razão que auditabilidade aberta deve ser entendida como desdobramento interno do dever de informação e não como instituto estranho ao microssistema.

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and trade secrets, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of performance; an unfolding of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of facilitating the consumer's defensep. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor

Information, under the CDC, does not fulfill its function merely because it exists somewhere, nor because it was deposited in lengthy documents, cross-references, FAQs or update histories that the consumer is unlikely to locate, understand and connect to the impactp. 641

PTA informação, no CDC, não cumpre sua função só porque existe em algum lugar, nem porque foi depositada em documentos extensos, remissões, FAQs ou históricos de atualização que o consumidor dificilmente localizará, compreenderá e conectará ao impacto

What if only one driver had the cameras?

Who tells the story of a traffic accident? If only one of the drivers had access to the cameras, the marks on the asphalt and the car's records, their version would be the only one that could be proven.

On a platform this is the default. Whoever controls the design, the criteria, the records and the timing of a change also controls the account of what happened. Without minimum trails, only one side's narrative exists. Open auditability is what gives the story more than one witness again.

Where it comes from

If the supplier controls the architecture, the criteria for change, the relevant logs, the timing of the change and the very means by which the modification will be perceived, it concentrates not only the power of reconfiguration, but also the power to give a unilateral account of what occurred.p. 636

PTSe o fornecedor controla a arquitetura, os critérios de alteração, os logs relevantes, a temporalidade da mudança e os próprios meios pelos quais a modificação será percebida, ele concentra não apenas poder de reconfiguração, mas também poder de narrar unilateralmente o que ocorreu.

It puts the conflict back on reconstructible ground and prevents the lack of access to evidence from turning into a practical impossibility of protectionp. 642

PTEla recoloca o conflito em terreno reconstruível e impede que a ausência de acesso aos elementos de prova se converta em impossibilidade prática de tutela

What needs to be within your reach?

What needs to be within reach is simple to say. What changed, when it changed, why it changed, what effect the change had and which records allow the case to be reconstructed.

No one is asking for the company's secrets or the game's code. What is asked for is a demonstrable minimum, enough to see whether a relevant change occurred, link it to what was promised and check whether it was justified and respected predictability, proportionality and the chance to challenge it. In a dispute, there are tools to reach this minimum, such as orders to preserve and disclose records, expert examination on defined points and, depending on the case, presumptions against the company.

Where it comes from

In other words, the platform cannot claim to alter the regime of enjoyment and, at the same time, keep in absolute secrecy what it actually altered, when it altered it, why it altered it, what relevant effects the change produces and which records make it possible to reconstruct the episode in case of conflictp. 636

PTEm outras palavras, a plataforma não pode pretender alterar o regime de fruição e, ao mesmo tempo, manter em sigilo absoluto o que de fato alterou, quando alterou, por que alterou, que efeitos relevantes a mudança produz e quais registros permitem reconstruir o episódio em caso de conflito

The standard does not require exposure of industrial secrets as a purifying fetish. It requires, rather, a demonstrable minimum capable of preventing information, good faith and transparency from turning into empty performances.p. 636

PTO standard não exige exposição do segredo industrial como fetiche purificador. Exige, isso sim, um mínimo demonstrável capaz de impedir que informação, boa-fé e transparência se convertam em performances vazias.

What is needed is sufficient intelligibility to identify the occurrence of a relevant alteration, relate it to the promised enjoyment, verify whether there was a justification compatible with the legal regime and ascertain whether the sanction, change or modulation respected minimum standards of predictability, proportionality and contestability.p. 728

PTO que precisa é de inteligibilidade suficiente para identificar a ocorrência de alteração relevante, relacioná-la com a fruição prometida, verificar se houve justificativa compatível com o regime jurídico e apurar se a sanção, a mudança ou a modulação respeitaram padrões mínimos de previsibilidade, proporcionalidade e contestabilidade.

The institutional consequence includes orders to preserve and disclose records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplierp. 745

PTA consequência institucional inclui ordens de preservação e exibição de registros, perícia delimitada, presunções desfavoráveis ao fornecedor

Who holds the records of a conflict?

In a dispute over an item or an account, almost everything that shows what happened is in the supplier's hands. The system logs, the screen trails, the change history, the penalty criteria and the internal support conversations all sit on the other side.

This is the normal situation in these disputes, not an exception. Whoever controls these traces largely controls what can be reconstructed later. Open auditability seeks to balance precisely this concentration.

Where it comes from

In disputes over IOCs, this is precisely the normal configuration: logs, interface trails, change histories, sanction criteria, internal support communications and relevant technical records are concentrated with the supplier.p. 659

PTEm litígios sobre OICs, essa é precisamente a configuração normal: logs, trilhas de interface, históricos de alteração, critérios de sanção, comunicações internas de suporte e registros técnicos relevantes ficam concentrados no fornecedor.

Whoever controls logs, histories, decision criteria, relevant versions, interface records, moderation flows, alteration parameters and traces of sanction controls, to a large extent, the conditions for the legal reconstruction of what happened.p. 734

PTQuem controla logs, históricos, critérios decisórios, versões relevantes, registros de interface, fluxos de moderação, parâmetros de alteração e vestígios de sanção controla, em larga medida, as condições de reconstrução jurídica do que ocorreu.

What becomes open to challenge?

As a result, what is decided inside closed systems becomes open to challenge. Another concept depends directly on this one, functional value equivalence. If you cannot reconstruct what changed, to what extent, why and when, you cannot show that you lost the equivalence of what you bought.

Where it comes from

[...] including with regard to access rules, delivery modulations and sanction mechanisms, so as to make contestable what is produced by opaque systemsp. 393

PT[...] inclusive no que diz respeito a regras de acesso, modulações de entrega e mecanismos de sanção, de modo a tornar contestável o que é produzido por sistemas opacos

Without auditability, functional equivalence becomes hard to demonstrate, because the consumer cannot reconstruct what was altered, to what extent, with what motivation and at what moment.p. 638

PTSem auditabilidade, a equivalência funcional fica difícil de demonstrar, porque o consumidor não consegue reconstruir o que foi alterado, em que extensão, com que motivação e em que momento.

Does the game's code have to be opened?

It does not mean opening the program's code, exposing all of the company's technology or doing away with trade secrets. It is not total transparency. It is sufficient transparency, minimum verifiability.

Nor does it apply to every small adjustment, purely cosmetic change or minor fluctuation. The duty begins when the alteration, the penalty or the rule change significantly affects what you use, your continued presence or your chance to prove and challenge.

Where it comes from

The expression does not designate unrestricted opening of source code, full exposure of the technological architecture or a generalized waiver of trade secrets.p. 727

PTA expressão não designa abertura irrestrita de código-fonte, exposição integral da arquitetura tecnológica ou renúncia generalizada a segredos empresariais.

Not total transparency, but sufficient transparency. Not absolute disclosure, but minimum verifiability.p. 728

PTNão transparência total, mas transparência suficiente. Não revelação absoluta, mas verificabilidade mínima.

Open auditability is not demandable in the face of any microscopic adjustment, any cosmetic change or any irrelevant fluctuation of the service.p. 642

PTAuditabilidade aberta não é exigível diante de qualquer ajuste microscópico, qualquer mudança cosmética ou qualquer oscilação irrelevante do serviço.

It may affect access and permanence, the functional integrity of the object, promised equivalences, the symbolic position of the IOC in the ecosystem, or even the consumer's conditions of proof and contestation.p. 642

PTPode atingir acesso e permanência, integridade funcional do objeto, equivalências prometidas, posição simbólica do OIC no ecossistema, ou mesmo as condições de prova e contestação do consumidor.

What can you demand?

A relevant change has to leave a trailA relevant change has to leave a trail

When does a change need to leave a trail?

When the platform changes something important about your access, the item's performance, its usefulness or the rules of the game, or when it hands out a punishment, and does so without keeping the minimum record needed to reconstruct what happened. That is the case with an item that loses strength in an update with no explanation you can check, or an account suspended without anyone knowing on what basis.

What trail must the platform keep?

A minimum of verifiability, which means making it possible to know what was changed, when, on what justification and with what effect on what you use.

This duty is not a new law with a name of its own. It comes from the duty to inform, from good faith and from making it easier for consumers to defend their rights, which the law already provides for. It does not require opening up all of the game's code or making the company's secrets public. The examination should be limited to what matters for the case, while keeping the necessary confidentiality.

Which records make it possible to understand the change or the punishment?

The essential system records, the history of the versions that matter, the records of the punishment and minimal justifications. Together with the context data, this should be enough for whoever decides the case to understand the problem. Almost all of it is usually in the platform's hands, not yours.

What can you ask for to reconstruct the change?

You can ask that these records be preserved and shown, and that an expert examination, meaning a review by a specialist, be carried out on specific points. You can also ask for a check, based on the minimum clarity the platform owes, of whether the change or the punishment was legitimate. Depending on the case, there may also be presumptions against the platform, which make certain facts count in your favor when the records only it had are missing.

Does every small adjustment require a record?

Not every small adjustment, purely cosmetic change or minor fluctuation calls for this trail. The duty begins when the change significantly affects what you use, your ability to stay or your chance to prove and contest what happened.

Where it comes from

Whenever relevant changes of access, performance, utility, operating rule or sanction affect enjoyment without the preservation of minimum trails for institutional reconstruction, there will be a violation of the open auditability parameter.p. 745

PTSempre que alterações relevantes de acesso, desempenho, utilidade, regra de funcionamento ou sanção incidirem sobre a fruição sem preservação de trilhas mínimas de reconstrução institucional, haverá violação do parâmetro de auditabilidade aberta.

The corresponding duty of supply is to maintain minimum verifiability over what was altered, when it was altered, on what grounds it was justified and what functionally relevant effects it produced.p. 745

PTO dever de fornecimento correspondente é o de manter verificabilidade mínima sobre o que foi alterado, quando o foi, por que motivo foi justificado e quais efeitos funcionalmente relevantes produziu.

It is for this reason that open auditability must be understood as an internal unfolding of the duty to inform and not as an institution foreign to the microsystem.p. 641

PTÉ por essa razão que auditabilidade aberta deve ser entendida como desdobramento interno do dever de informação e não como instituto estranho ao microssistema.

A standard of minimum verifiability, compatible with the protection of software and trade secrets, but incompatible with the opaque shielding of performance; an unfolding of the duty to inform, of objective good faith and of facilitating the consumer's defensep. 648

PTUm standard de verificabilidade mínima, compatível com a proteção do software e do segredo empresarial, mas incompatível com a blindagem opaca da execução; um desdobramento do dever de informação, da boa-fé objetiva e da facilitação da defesa do consumidor

The requirement of open auditability is not to be confused with full opening of the source code nor with suppression of the technical confidentiality of the software.p. 643

PTA exigência de auditabilidade aberta não se confunde com abertura integral do código-fonte nem com supressão do sigilo técnico do software.

The minimum required proof consists of essential logs, history of relevant versions, sanction records, minimum operational justifications, context data and delimited technical elements sufficient to make the controversy cognizable.p. 745

PTA prova mínima exigível consiste em logs essenciais, histórico de versões relevantes, registros de sanção, justificativas operacionais mínimas, dados de contexto e elementos técnicos delimitados suficientes para tornar a controvérsia cognoscível.

The institutional consequence includes orders for preservation and display of records, delimited expert examination, presumptions unfavorable to the supplier and review of the lawfulness of the alteration or sanction based on minimum institutional intelligibility.p. 745

PTA consequência institucional inclui ordens de preservação e exibição de registros, perícia delimitada, presunções desfavoráveis ao fornecedor e controle da licitude da alteração ou sanção com base em inteligibilidade institucional mínima.

Open auditability is not demandable in the face of any microscopic adjustment, any cosmetic change or any irrelevant fluctuation of the service.p. 642

PTAuditabilidade aberta não é exigível diante de qualquer ajuste microscópico, qualquer mudança cosmética ou qualquer oscilação irrelevante do serviço.

What does open auditability require?

It requires minimum conditions of verifiability over relevant aspects of supply. Protection bodies, the Judiciary, experts and, to a certain extent, consumers themselves need to be able to reconstruct the relation between promise, alteration, sanction, functional impact and the supplier's justification.

Where it comes from

Open auditability means the existence of minimum conditions of verifiability regarding relevant aspects of supply, so that protection bodies, the Judiciary, experts and, to a certain extent, consumers themselves can reconstruct the relationship between promise, alteration, sanction, functional impact and the justification presented by the supplier.p. 727

PTAuditabilidade aberta significa a existência de condições mínimas de verificabilidade sobre aspectos relevantes do fornecimento, de modo que órgãos de tutela, Judiciário, peritos e, em certa medida, os próprios consumidores possam reconstruir a relação entre promessa, alteração, sanção, impacto funcional e justificação apresentada pelo fornecedor.

What problem does it respond to?

It responds to the functional opacity typical of immaterial objects of consumption. Open auditability and functional equivalence operate on the concretely governed regime of enjoyment, not on an object taken in the abstract.

Where it comes from

Open auditability is thus born as a direct response to the functional opacity typical of IOCs.p. 636

PTA auditabilidade aberta nasce, assim, como resposta direta à opacidade funcional típica dos OICs.

For this reason, in IOCs, open auditability and functional equivalence do not operate on an object taken in abstraction, but on the concretely governed regime of enjoyment.p. 663

PTPor isso, em OICs, auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional não operam sobre um objeto tomado em abstração, mas sobre o regime de fruição concretamente governado.
