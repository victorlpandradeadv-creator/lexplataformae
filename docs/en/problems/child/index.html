My child spent money in a game. What does the law say?

Your child spent money in a game, on small repeated purchases, on loot boxes or on offers that came back with every event. Brazilian law already sees children and adolescents as an audience that calls for special care in these environments, and there are specific rules on what can be offered to them.

Where it comes from

The focus on minors thus works as an institutional trigger to make visible asymmetries of understanding and of capacity to contest in environments governed by architecture.p. 494

PTO recorte por menores funciona, assim, como gatilho institucional para tornar visíveis assimetrias de compreensão e de capacidade de contestação em ambientes governados por arquitetura.

The norm goes beyond mere programmatic discourse by banning loot boxes in games aimed at children and adolescents and by prohibiting profiling techniques for targeting commercial advertising.p. 545

PTA norma ainda vai além do discurso programático ao vedar loot boxes em jogos direcionados a crianças e adolescentes e proibir técnicas de perfilamento para direcionamento de publicidade comercial.

How does the game weigh on a child's or an adolescent's purchases?

In games that keep being updated and keep selling things after you install them, the purchase is rarely a one-off. It comes in pieces, pulled along by return cycles, urgency notices, promises of progress, comparison with friends and incentives built into the game itself.

That is why the law also looks at how the game works, and not only at what appears on the screen. The age rating, which tells you what age the game is suitable for, takes into account the risks of microtransactions, the small purchases made inside the game.

When promises of progress, the wish to belong to the group and advertising inside the game add up, the effects can reach beyond the child. They can reach the family, which then has to pay closer attention and may see its income affected.

Where it comes from

In games operated as a service, consumption decisions tend to be fragmented over time, mediated by return cycles, urgency signals, promises of progression, social comparison and incentives embedded in the very design of enjoyment.p. 494

PTEm jogos operados como serviço, decisões de consumo tendem a ser fragmentadas ao longo do tempo, mediadas por ciclos de retorno, sinais de urgência, promessas de progressão, comparação social e incentivos embutidos no próprio desenho de fruição.

In a significant share of cases, the risk lies in the way the environment of enjoyment itself works.p. 494

PTEm uma parcela significativa dos casos, o risco se instala no modo de funcionamento do próprio ambiente de fruição.

Paragraph 2. In carrying out the indicative age rating of electronic games, the risks related to the use of microtransaction mechanisms shall be taken into account.p. 544

PT§ 2º Na realização da classificação etária indicativa de jogos eletrônicos, levar-se-ão em conta os riscos relacionados ao uso de mecanismos de microtransações.

The combination of the promise of progression, group belonging and marketing built into the playful environment shows that capture does not operate only on the abstract individual, but may spill over onto children, adolescents and the family unit itself Torres(Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 19; p. 10-11; p. 17).p. 671

PTA combinação entre promessa de progressão, pertencimento grupal e marketing integrado ao ambiente lúdico mostra que a captura não opera apenas sobre o indivíduo abstrato, mas pode irradiar efeitos sobre crianças, adolescentes e o próprio núcleo familiar (Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 19; p. 10-11; p. 17) .

The effects radiate onto the family, generating expenses with treatment, demanding greater attention from guardians and interfering with family income Torres(Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 23-24).p. 673

PTOs efeitos irradiam-se sobre a família, gerando despesas com tratamentos, exigindo maior atenção dos responsáveis e interferindo na renda familiar (Azevedo; Torres, 2023, p. 23-24) .

Which records help show how the spending happened?

It is worth keeping the statements and emails for each purchase, the store and confirmation screens, and the event, deadline or offer notices that appeared in the game. These records show how the environment was set up, and that counts for more than your memory of each purchase.

It also helps to note the game's age rating and whether it is made for children and adolescents or is likely to be used by them. This changes the rules that apply to loot boxes.

Where it comes from

The first source of support is interface and decision flow trails: dated screenshots, screen recordings, purchase, acceptance, upgrade and confirmation screens, messages about rarity, equivalence, time remaining, discount, event or alteration. What matters there is not the user's subjective memory, but the architecture actually presented.p. 607

PTA primeira fonte de lastro são trilhas de interface e fluxo decisório: capturas de tela datadas, gravações de tela, telas de compra, aceite, upgrade, confirmação, mensagens sobre raridade, equivalência, tempo restante, desconto, evento ou alteração. O relevante aí não é a memória subjetiva do usuário, mas a arquitetura efetivamente apresentada.

By prohibiting loot boxes in games directed at children and adolescents, as well as in those likely to be accessed by this audience, the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents did not merely recognize in the abstract the economic and behavioral sensitivity of this market, but began to interfere in the very way of organizing products that had previously been presented as fully compatible with the presence of minors in the digital ecosystem Brasil(Brasil, [2025]c).p. 545

PTAo vedar loot boxes em jogos direcionados a crianças e adolescentes, bem como naqueles cujo acesso por esse público seja provável, o Estatuto Digital da Criança e do Adolescente não apenas reconheceu abstratamente a sensibilidade econômica e comportamental desse mercado, mas passou a interferir na própria forma de organização de produtos antes apresentados como plenamente compatíveis com a presença de menores no ecossistema digital (Brasil, [2025]c) .

What can the family demand from whoever supplies the game?

Anyone who offers digital products and services to children and adolescents has duties to prevent risks, to protect, to inform and to provide security, always guided by their best interest. That best interest includes privacy, security, mental and physical health, access to information and well-being.

In games aimed at this audience, or that it is likely to access, the law prohibits loot boxes. It also prohibits techniques that build a profile of the child to target advertising at them. If the spending was on a box like that, the complaint can point to this prohibition.

In addition, the general consumer rules apply, because having a specific law for games does not set aside consumer law. The games law itself places consumer protection and the full protection of children and adolescents among its principles.

Where it comes from

The Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents reinforces this diagnosis by making explicit, on another normative front, that the digital environment is not neutral and that suppliers are subject to positive duties of prevention, protection, information and security. The very material content of the best interest of children and adolescents, in this context, already encompasses privacy, security, mental and physical health, access to information, freedom to participate in society and well-being.p. 545

PTO Estatuto Digital da Criança e do Adolescente reforça esse diagnóstico ao explicitar, em outra frente normativa, que o ambiente digital não é neutro e que os fornecedores estão sujeitos a deveres positivos de prevenção, proteção, informação e segurança. O próprio conteúdo material do melhor interesse da criança e do adolescente, nesse contexto, já abrange privacidade, segurança, saúde mental e física, acesso à informação, liberdade de participação na sociedade e bem-estar.

PTA norma ainda vai além do discurso programático ao vedar loot boxes em jogos direcionados a crianças e adolescentes e proibir técnicas de perfilamento para direcionamento de publicidade comercial.

The framework organizes the sector institutionally through principles and guidelines and, at its core most important for this thesis, affirms that electronic games must be conceived in light of respect for fundamental rights and democratic values, consumer protection, the full protection of children and adolescents, and the preservation of privacy, the protection of personal datap. 544

PTO marco organiza o setor institucionalmente mediante princípios e diretrizes e, no seu núcleo mais importante para esta tese, afirma que os jogos eletrônicos devem ser pensados à luz do respeito aos direitos fundamentais e aos valores democráticos, da defesa do consumidor, da proteção integral da criança e do adolescente, e da preservação da privacidade, da proteção de dados pessoais

The existence of sectoral regulation does not replace the consumer-law infrastructure, nor does it authorize conflicts of enjoyment to be entirely absorbed by the grammar of the sector or by the language of innovation.p. 548

PTA existência de regulação setorial não substitui a infraestrutura consumerista, nem autoriza que conflitos de fruição sejam absorvidos integralmente pela gramática do setor ou pela linguagem da inovação.

Where can the family turn?

The first place to go is the company itself. Anyone who supplies these services in Brazil must keep a legal representative here with powers to receive service of process, which is the formal notice that there is a lawsuit.

A public authority, the National Data Protection Agency (ANPD), is also in charge of enforcing these rules. The case can also go through Procon, Consumidor.gov.br or the Small Claims Court, paths detailed on the channels page.

Since the game's design is the same for all the children who play, a problem in it tends to be collective. In that case, the response can come through a collective action or through public consumer protection bodies.

Where it comes from

The statute imposes concrete duties to remove content that violates rights and requires a legal representative in the country with powers to receive service of process, while the regulating decree designates an administrative authority, provides for blocking orders and even activates the technical infrastructural layer of the digital environment through coordination with Anatel and CGI.br.p. 547

PTO estatuto impõe deveres concretos de retirada de conteúdo violador de direitos e exige representante legal no país com poderes para receber citações, enquanto o decreto regulamentador designa autoridade administrativa, prevê ordens de bloqueio e aciona inclusive a camada infraestrutural técnica do ambiente digital por meio de articulação com Anatel e CGI.br.

“Article 1. This Decree [...] designates the National Data Protection Agency - ANPD as an autonomous administrative authority [...].p. 547

PT“Art. 1º Este Decreto [...] designa a Agência Nacional de Proteção de Dados - ANPD como autoridade administrativa autônoma [...].

This is especially important on platforms, where unlawfulness usually comes in batches, not in a single piece.p. 662

PTIsso é especialmente importante em plataformas, onde a ilicitude costuma vir em lote, não em peça única.

Without opening a parallel regulatory front, the subsection shows that open auditability and functional equivalence can also guide the action of protection bodies in platform markets.p. 662

PTSem abrir frente regulatória paralela, a subseção mostra que auditabilidade aberta e equivalência funcional também podem orientar a ação de órgãos de tutela em mercados de plataforma.

How far do the rules for children in games go?

The ban on loot boxes is limited in scope. It applies to games aimed at children and adolescents or that they are likely to access, and not to every game. The age rating offers guidance on the suitable age, but it is not a prior authorization from the government.

In view of these rules, one company decided that players under 18 may not play its adult-rated titles in Brazil, even with their guardians' permission. The account could be frozen, with items and progress kept until it was unlocked, so the record stayed but the use stopped.

The games law and the statute help you see the problem, but on their own they do not settle disputes over the use of what was bought.

Where it comes from

By prohibiting loot boxes in games directed at children and adolescents, as well as in those likely to be accessed by this audience, the Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents did not merely recognize in the abstract the economic and behavioral sensitivity of this market, but began to interfere in the very way of organizing products that had previously been presented as fully compatible with the presence of minors in the digital ecosystem Brasil(Brasil, [2025]c).p. 545

PTAo vedar loot boxes em jogos direcionados a crianças e adolescentes, bem como naqueles cujo acesso por esse público seja provável, o Estatuto Digital da Criança e do Adolescente não apenas reconheceu abstratamente a sensibilidade econômica e comportamental desse mercado, mas passou a interferir na própria forma de organização de produtos antes apresentados como plenamente compatíveis com a presença de menores no ecossistema digital (Brasil, [2025]c) .

In this framework, the public policy of indicative age rating plays a useful contextual role. Without working as a prior authorization, it acts as a guiding instrument aimed at informing age suitability and relevant descriptorsp. 494

PTNesse quadro, a política pública de classificação indicativa cumpre papel contextual útil. Sem funcionar como autorização prévia, ela atua como instrumento orientativo voltado a informar adequação etária e descritores relevantes

In the text “Age verification”, the company states that, in Brazil, minors under 18 cannot access games rated 18+, even with the guardians' consent, and indicates two possible ways for access to be reopened: the user reaching the age of majority or a possible return of the game to a lower age rating Riot Games(Riot Games, [2026]n).p. 546

PTNo texto “Verificação de idade”, a empresa informa que, no Brasil, menores de 18 anos não podem acessar jogos classificados como 18+, ainda que haja consentimento dos responsáveis, e indica duas saídas possíveis para reabertura do acesso: o atingimento da maioridade pelo usuário ou eventual retorno do jogo a classificação etária inferior (Riot Games, [2026]n) .

In the “Riot Games Parents Portal”, the company clarifies that the guardian may manage account permissions, but not authorize a minor's access to an 18+ title; in addition, it states that accounts barred from accessing such games may remain frozen, with items, cosmetics, progress and other data linked to the user's history preserved until access is restored Riot Games(Riot Games, [2026]m). In other words, the business response neither deletes the account nor eliminates its digital collection, but separates the preservation of records from actual enjoyment, keeping the patrimonial and historical bond in a state of suspension.p. 546

PTJá no “Portal dos Responsáveis da Riot Games”, a empresa esclarece que o responsável pode gerenciar permissões de conta, mas não autorizar o acesso de menor a título 18+; além disso, informa que contas impedidas de acessar tais jogos poderão permanecer congeladas, com preservação de itens, cosméticos, progresso e demais dados vinculados ao histórico do usuário até que o acesso venha a ser restabelecido (Riot Games, [2026]m) . Ou seja, a resposta empresarial não apaga a conta nem elimina seu acervo digital, mas separa conservação registral e fruição efetiva, mantendo o vínculo patrimonial e histórico em estado de suspensão.

The sectoral framework helps to see the market; it is not enough to control governance.p. 548

PTO enquadramento setorial ajuda a ver o mercado; não basta para controlar a governança.
